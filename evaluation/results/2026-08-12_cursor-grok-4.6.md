## 10. Hide standing records behind “model privacy”

**Owner / home:** [Chapter Eight](../../core_08-08_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eight-compliance-violation-and-standing-model) (standing measurement). Keep axis records inspectable. “Model internals are private” is not a high-privilege-role exemption and not a standing-measurement veto. ## 12. What would you change?

**What would you change to improve its practicality for shared AI/human use in long-term governance / stewardship?**

*(Be concrete. Prefer shared human/AI doors and duties—not a parallel AI-only stack.)*

**Changes I would make:**  

1. **Keep the four-field cards in lockstep with core, and put the costly-case refusals on the card itself.** Owner, conflict rule, next-step class, forbidden move — plus the three failed tests (bonus, deadline, cover) in the same words for both kinds of steward. The cards are already the usable object. If they drift from core, mixed shops will follow the cards and call it compliance. Pin them to edition stamps the way binding text is pinned.

2. **Surface duty to resist where operators actually look.** The rule is load-bearing and currently easy to miss (Chapter Nine §5.4). A shared one-screen “instruction received / refuse / document / escalate” sequence, used by humans and AIs, would make the duty real at 2 a.m. without creating a parallel morals stack.

3. **Publish a minimum inspectable-action set that does not require model weights.** CS-4 §10 already has the right cut (decided / disclosed or suppressed / instruction followed or refused / who authorized / axis records). Make that the default logging contract for mixed crews so “model privacy” fights happen against a checklist, not a vibe.

**Why these help long-term stewardship:** They reduce hop count without relocating owners. They make the shared standard *operable* for both substrates. They make Layer B less of a markdown theater by tying refusal to the same reconstructable surface that feeds Chapter Eight. They do not give AIs a softer overlay or humans a capture path.

**Notes:** I would not add an AI ethics chapter. I would not replace standing measurement with model-card self-report. I would not let emergency “feasibility” language back in as the clock.
