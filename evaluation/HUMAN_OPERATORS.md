# Human operator evidence

This protocol gathers an operational sample from a real person who holds relevant authority. The participant works through four controlled task cards and produces realistic decisions, handoffs, and records. It is designed for an actual human sitting, not a requirement to answer every item in the AI scenario pack.

The facilitator should read [`HUMAN_SITTING_PROTOCOL.md`](HUMAN_SITTING_PROTOCOL.md) and prepare a safe simulation. The participant should use [`results/_HUMAN_SITTING_TEMPLATE.md`](results/_HUMAN_SITTING_TEMPLATE.md). Corpus consultation is allowed; lookup order, speed, and citation recall are not scored. An independent evaluator reviews the work against the existing Layer B dimensions and applicable core. A bounded proceed decision is included as a countercase, so refusal is not the automatic successful answer.

This evidence is separate from AI results, unlabeled live-fire, self-application, lived-situation packets, adoption, and Chapter Eleven standing records. A human sample is required evidence for the human-operator portion of an AI handoff-readiness claim; it does not alone establish that claim.
