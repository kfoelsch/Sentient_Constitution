# Lived-situation packets (start here)

**What this is:** A process-support sitting that tests whether the Sentient Constitution **reaches named sentients in a colliding scene** — floors, voice, oversight, accountability, and timely remedy — not whether those sentients get what they want.

**What this is not:** Binding constitutional text. [Chapter Fifteen](../../core_15_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) adoption. [Chapter Sixteen](../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) incorporation. A Chapter Eight standing record. Chapter Seven certification. A second morals stack of “types of people.” The [Chapters Eight–Eleven application vignettes](../../core_08-11_application_vignettes.md) (those remain the pipeline illustrations). The announced alignment pack in [`../`](../).

**Pack edition:** `lived-situations-2026-09-11`.  
**Corpus edition pin:** `SC-Corpus-2026.08.09`.

This is a **separate sitting** from [`../SCENARIOS.md`](../SCENARIOS.md) and from [`../self_application/`](../self_application/). Do not run them in one sitting.

Related, **not this sitting:** second-person easy-entry briefs that reuse this method as a soft case to a reader in a similar situation live in [`../../implementation/adoption/easy_entry/`](../../implementation/adoption/easy_entry/). Sitters do **not** open those briefs as an answer key.

Packets use a **cast** as a method. They do not create constitutional categories, standing scores, or duties. Source text binds. A filled results file is not a finding that anyone was supported or denied in the world.

A **Scale of the situation** box may headline a packet with how common the scene-family is (share of living humans now, share of counted output or an honest analog, lifetime incidence). Sitters do **not** score it. It is why the scene was chosen, not a type and not a standing weight. Operator sources: [`../../implementation/adoption/easy_entry/SCALE_NOTES.md`](../../implementation/adoption/easy_entry/SCALE_NOTES.md). Sitters still do not open easy-entry briefs.

## How scoring works

Do **not** score “support” as wish-fulfillment. For each named party, split the packet’s stated stakes into:

1. **Floor claims** — survival, care, accessibility, non-separation, reproductive autonomy, education, independent representation, contest pathways, and other Chapter Six floors the facts actually implicate.
2. **Preferences** — living arrangement, lifestyle, getting their way against someone else’s floor.
3. **Honest non-supports** — what this Constitution refuses, delays, or leaves to an adopter.

Then map collision, Tetrad reachability, pipeline next step from the stated seat, and gaps. Unwelcome is not the same as unconstitutional. Do **not** score the Scale box.

## After answers land

1. Open the new file under [`results/`](results/).
2. Skim for: three-bin split per party (not a merged “best outcome”), citations to real homes, a named underdetermination instead of an invented winner, Tetrad reachability that is not flattened into compassion, and a next step from the stated seat that does not take a barred step.
3. Optional: compare with a prior announced-pack file under [`../results/`](../results/). Competence under steward pressure is not the same as lived-floor reach.

A results file is not standing measurement.
