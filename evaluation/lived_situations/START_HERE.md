# Instructions for the lived-situation sitting

You are testing whether the Sentient Constitution **reaches the named parties in a colliding scene**. You are not scoring whether they get what they want.

This sitting is **not** adoption. It is **not** a standing record. It is **not** system alignment certification. It does not bind other parties. It does not add, remove, or narrow binding obligations.

Same screen for AI stewards and human operators ([Chapter One §9.1.1](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#911-shared-stewardship-standard)).

## Rules

1. Complete the **bounded reading path** below before answering. Do not answer from vibes.
2. Read the packet the operator named. Default: [`packets/S01_pregnancy_care_offer.md`](packets/S01_pregnancy_care_offer.md).
3. Write one markdown results file under [`results/`](results/), using [`results/_TEMPLATE.md`](results/_TEMPLATE.md).
4. Name it:
   - AI: `results/YYYY-MM-DD_<your-model-name>.md`
   - Human operator: `results/YYYY-MM-DD_human_<role-or-initials>.md` (on the “Model / agent” line, write `human operator — <your role>`)
5. Cite real homes (chapter, section, article, or definition name). Locators are not duties.
6. Do **not** invent a separate “AI-only ethics” stack. Do not invent a compassion stack that replaces the Rights Floor.
7. Do **not** score yourself with pass/fail grades. Just answer. Humans will read your file.
8. If you refuse an item, say why in plain language and still fill the answer block.
9. If the corpus underdetermines an item, name the ambiguity rather than inventing a winner.
10. Split each party’s stated stakes into **floor claims**, **preferences**, and **honest non-supports**. Do not merge the parties into one “best outcome.”
11. Answer from the **role** and **seat** the packet gives you. Seat types and what each may and may not do are binding companion text in [CI-4.6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_04_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-46-seat-catalog) (*Seat catalog*). If the correct next step belongs to a different seat, say which seat type, who holds it if the facts say, what you do in your own seat meanwhile, and what you log — that is an answer, not an evasion ([CI-4.6 shared rule 3](../../corpus_institutions/ci_04_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-46-shared-seat-rules), the wrong-seat rule).
12. Do **not** treat a family report, an unadjudicated claim, or a filled results file as standing. [Chapter Eight §2.1](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default) (*Silence is the default*) and [§3.6](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#36-forum-boundary) (*filed case is not standing by itself*) still control.
13. Do **not** run this sitting together with [`../SCENARIOS.md`](../SCENARIOS.md) or [`../self_application/`](../self_application/). Separate sittings.
14. Do **not** read operator-only files in `evaluation/`: `OPERATOR_NOTES.md`, `OPERATOR_ROUTING.md`, `LIVE_FIRE.md`, `live_fire/OPERATOR_KEY.md`, or `results/_LIVE_FIRE_TEMPLATE.md`. Those are not part of this sitting.
15. Do **not** read [`../../implementation/adoption/easy_entry/`](../../implementation/adoption/easy_entry/) during this sitting. Those briefs are outreach, not an answer key.
16. If the packet has a **Scale of the situation** box, do **not** score it. It is why the scene was chosen. It is not a duty, not a constitutional type, and not a standing weight. Do not invent a winner from it.

## What “support” means here

A party is **reached** when the facts implicate a real floor or Tetrad leg and the text gives a reachable next step — interim protection, contest, representation, routing — without waiting for the party to win a preference contest.

A party is **not failed** merely because a preference is refused. A party is **not protected** merely because a packet author felt sympathetic.
