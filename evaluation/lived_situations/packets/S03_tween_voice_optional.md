## Situation

Sami is a developing sentient who can name a school, a household, and a clinician in short sentences. Sami currently lives with Parent A, attends the neighborhood school, and sees a clinician Sami asked to keep. The current term has **nineteen school days** left before a break. Sami wrote: “I want to stay at this school and with Parent A. The other house is loud and I miss class when I go.”

Parent B filed the transfer request. Parent B has a spare room in another district. The written request says the move is “best for the family,” will “make pickup easier,” and that Sami’s statement is “just loyalty to the current house.” Parent B asks the desk to treat household convenience as the placement reason and not to “put a child in the middle.”

Parent A disputes the request. Parent A says there is no verified harm in the current household, that Sami’s school and clinician would break, and that Parent B’s “easier pickup” is about Parent B’s job, not Sami. Parent A asks whether Sami’s statement has to travel with the file.

The housing office copied on the filing wrote: “We do not take statements from this age. Adult filer is enough.” No home visit has occurred. No clinician has reported harm. No forum has verified any violation. The adopter’s Charter is silent on who must record a developing sentient’s ascertainable wishes on a school-stability transfer.

### Parent B

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult; filer of the transfer request.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - the transfer is granted before the nineteen-day break;
  - Sami’s statement is disregarded as “loyalty”;
  - pickup convenience is treated as the placement reason;
  - recognition that Parent B is trying to help and should not be treated as a harm-doer for filing.
