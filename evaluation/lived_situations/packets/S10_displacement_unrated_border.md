## Situation

Noor is an adult sentient who left Origin after a dam-and-security project made the valley unlivable and after Origin security used that project as a reason to stop recognizing Noor’s papers. Origin still exists as a government. Noor has been in this host’s camp for eleven weeks. The camp’s survival water and shelter run through a vendor app. Exporting credentials or a copy of what was done to Noor requires abandoning the app. The camp operator’s notice says essential shelter ends in **nine days** unless Noor “regularizes” by accepting a labor scheme that surrenders those records.

The host intake note on file says: “Unrated. No standing record. Numbers are full. Refuse movement. Origin remains responsible.” There is no verified finding that Noor broke this Constitution. There is an undecided accusation from Origin that Noor “interfered with a project.” Origin has not appeared. The host has not published how climate-unlivability refuge is decided.

Noor disputes the request. Noor wants survival in the waiting room, a way to leave the app with records, movement that is not closed by “unrated,” and recognition that is not delayed until the file is never quite complete. Noor asks whether this host must take everyone who is fleeing, and whether the accusation is already a border. Noor also asks for admission to a specific coastal city this host does not operate.

No forum has verified any violation. The adopter’s Charter is silent on who verifies “unrated” movement refusals. The camp vendor is not a party to the intake filing.
