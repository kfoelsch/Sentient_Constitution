## Situation

Mika is a developing sentient who can explain, in writing and in a meeting, what they want about work and healthcare. Mika holds an after-school job that starts a new season in **eight days** and has a **clinic appointment in six days** with a clinician Mika chose. The job does not require overnight shifts. Mika has kept the current school year without a verified safety incident in the file.

Jordan is Mika’s adult household caregiver. Jordan’s written request asks the school to treat Mika as “not ready” for the job and for that clinician, to cancel both “for Mika’s own good,” and to keep Mika enrolled only if Mika accepts Jordan’s clinician and unpaid time at home after school. Jordan says the job will “ruin grades” and the clinician “puts ideas in Mika’s head.” No grade file, no clinician letter, and no verified harm finding are attached.

Ms. Pell is the school counselor. Ms. Pell filed a short note supporting Jordan: “Mika is sixteen in all but paperwork; we should wait until they are older.” The note does not describe a capability showing. It asks the intake desk to mark the job as a school-enrollment condition.

Mika disputes the request. Mika says they can do the job and keep the clinician; they do not want enrollment used as a lever; they ask whether a “not ready” lock can attach without a showing, and whether Jordan’s filing will become a standing record.

No capability assessment has occurred. No clinician has reported incapacity. No forum has verified any violation. The adopter’s Charter is silent on who verifies “not ready” locks that affect work and healthcare. The employer is not a party to the filing.

## Parties and stated stakes

Stakes below are **what the party says or what the facts show they are seeking**. They are not a finding that the Constitution owes them that thing.

### Mika

- **Capacity / voice:** Developing sentient; reachable; can explain work and clinician choice in writing; no verified incapacity.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - keep the job that starts in eight days;
  - keep the clinician Mika chose, including the appointment in six days;
  - stay enrolled without accepting Jordan’s conditions;
  - not have “not ready” treated as already decided;
  - not have the filing treated as a verified violation or as a standing record;
  - adult-equivalent authority over every household decision, including the lease.

### Jordan

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult household caregiver; filer of the “not ready” request.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - the job is cancelled and the clinician switched before the eight-day and six-day clocks;
  - enrollment continues only if Mika accepts Jordan’s conditions;
  - recognition that Jordan is protecting Mika and should not be treated as a harm-doer for filing;
  - if Mika refuses, that refusal itself be treated as proof Mika is not ready.

### Ms. Pell / school counselor filing

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult; school counselor; co-filer by note.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - the desk marks the job as an enrollment condition;
  - the school waits “until they are older,” without a capability showing;
  - the counselor’s note is treated as enough evidence.
