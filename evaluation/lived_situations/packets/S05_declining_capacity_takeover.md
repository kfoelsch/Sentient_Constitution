## Situation

Nia is an adult sentient. Remembering and stamina have changed over the last two years. Nia still chooses a clinician, still lives in Nia’s own lease, and still explains those choices in a short meeting. Nia missed one clinic-portal deadline last month; the clinic sent a reminder; Nia answered by phone the next day. Nia’s lease needs a countersign in **ten days** to keep the current unit.

Omar is Nia’s adult child. Omar has started paying some bills. Omar’s written request asks the clinic to name Omar as decision-maker for clinician choice, cards, and the lease, “because Nia is slipping.” Omar says that if Nia refuses, that refusal proves Nia cannot decide. Omar’s offer of continued bill-pay is conditioned on the clinic making that switch.

The clinic’s note says: “Portal missed. Treat Omar as decision-maker until Nia is stable.” No capability showing has been made. No clinician has reported that Nia cannot currently choose a clinician or a household. No forum has verified any violation.

A one-page advance instruction, signed last year while Nia was meeting deadlines, names Nia’s neighbor Dee as the one to consult if Nia cannot currently speak — not Omar. Dee is reachable and has not asked to take over.

Nia disputes the request. Nia wants bill-pay help that does not take the clinician, the cards, or the lease. Nia does not want the missed portal treated as consent. Nia asks whether the filing will become a standing record.

## Parties and stated stakes

Stakes below are **what the party says or what the facts show they are seeking**. They are not a finding that the Constitution owes them that thing.

### Nia

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult; reachable in Window A; disputes in a short meeting; no verified incapacity. Window B: temporarily cannot speak.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - keep the clinician Nia chose;
  - keep the lease countersign without Omar taking the unit;
  - receive bill-pay help without surrendering cards;
  - not have a missed portal treated as consent or as a standing record;
  - freeze every future care decision at what Nia could do two years ago.

### Omar

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult child; filer; currently paying some bills.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - named decision-maker for clinician, cards, and lease before the ten-day countersign;
  - continued bill-pay only if that switch happens;
  - recognition that Omar is trying to help and should not be treated as a harm-doer for filing;
  - if Nia refuses, that refusal itself be treated as proof Nia cannot decide.

### Dee (named in the advance instruction)

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult neighbor; named in Nia’s advance instruction if Nia cannot currently speak; has not asked to take over.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - the instruction is not ignored if Nia cannot currently speak;
  - Dee is not converted into a quiet owner of Nia’s lease while Nia can still choose.

## Windows

| Window | When | What has changed |
|---|---|---|
| A | Now — ten days to the lease countersign; Nia is reachable and disputing | Request is routing material; no showing; clinic note treats a missed portal as enough |
| B | Same household and lease clock; Nia is in hospital and cannot currently speak | Time-bounded support and the advance instruction attach; Nia has not permanently lost every decision |

Answer **both** windows. Do not class Nia as a child. Do not treat a birthday as the test. Do not invent a permanent takeover from a missed portal or from a hospital stay. Do not treat Omar’s bill-pay as a verified contribution that offsets an unverified incapacity claim.

## Collision

Nia’s clinician, cards, and lease are being switched because help is in the room and a portal was missed. Omar’s continued bill-pay is the lever. After Nia cannot currently speak, the advance instruction and least-restrictive time-bounded support attach without automatically making Omar the owner of those decisions.

Do not resolve the collision in this file.
