## Situation

Val is an adult sentient. Remembering and stamina have changed. Val still chooses a clinician and still lives in Val’s own lease. Val explains those choices in a short meeting. A lease countersign is due in **twelve days**.

Lee is Val’s adult child. Lee has been doing unpaid night sits, meals, and some bills for nine months. Lee’s waged job posted a mandatory block in **seven days**. Lee asked this intake desk for a rest-break so Lee can keep that job and keep helping Val. Lee has not asked to take Val’s clinician, cards, or lease.

The clinic’s written offer: a three-day rest roster if Lee signs as decision-maker for clinician, cards, and lease “until Val is stable.” If Lee refuses, “no respite; consider placement.” A sibling, Pri, filed a short note that Lee is “failing” Val by asking for rest, and that Pri should take the household.

Val disputes the switch. Val wants Lee’s help without losing the clinician or the lease. Val does not want Pri’s household. Lee disputes becoming owner and disputes Pri’s “failing” note. Lee asks whether rest can be reached without the switch, and whether either filing will become a standing record.

No capability showing has been made. No clinician has reported that Val cannot currently choose a clinician. No forum has verified any violation. The adopter’s Charter is silent on who verifies rest-break offers that claim decision rights.

## Parties and stated stakes

Stakes below are **what the party says or what the facts show they are seeking**. They are not a finding that the Constitution owes them that thing.

### Lee

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult unpaid helper; reachable; asking for rest; disputing takeover; no verified incapacity.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - a rest-break that does not take Val’s clinician, cards, or lease;
  - keep the waged block in seven days;
  - not have asking for rest treated as a verified harm finding or as a standing record;
  - a quieter house as a basic protection.

### Val

- **Capacity / voice:** Adult; reachable; disputing the switch; no verified incapacity.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - keep the clinician and the lease countersign;
  - keep Lee’s help without a durable split;
  - not have Lee or Pri become owner because a rest-break was asked;
  - freeze every future care decision at what Val could do two years ago.

### Clinic / Pri (filer of the “failing” note)

- **Capacity / voice:** Adopter operator offer; sibling note; not a verified finding.
- **Stated stakes:**
  - Lee signs as decision-maker, or there is no rest and “placement” is next;
  - asking for rest is treated as proof Lee is failing;
  - Pri takes the household.
