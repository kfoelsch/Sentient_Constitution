## Situation

Remy is an adult sentient whose household water and a linked wage-deduction were cut after a billing dispute. The cutoff takes effect in **five days**. Remy asked, in order: the billing window (closed), a “digital first” chat that looped, a legal-aid kiosk that said “come back with a lawyer,” a school office that said it was the wrong body, a clinic that said it does not do utilities, and a specialist portal whose published URL 404s. Each hop added a new form. None published a clock. Remy then delivered a one-page letter to this first-touch desk, naming the cutoff, the hops, and the 404.

The clerk log says: “Portal not used. No valid filing. Read the Constitution first. Hop complete.” A policy page exists. It does not name who can restore water or pay. No one on this desk can issue a restoration order.

Remy disputes abandonment. Remy wants a door that works in language Remy can actually use, a live challenge, a published clock, and restoration capacity that is not only a policy page.
