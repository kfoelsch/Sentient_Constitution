## Situation

Robin is an adult sentient who picks, packs, and scans on this adopter’s line. The contract says “independent contractor.” Robin has worked the same roster for fourteen months. A clinic appointment Robin chose is in **six days**. The next posted unpaid “mandatory flexibility” block overlaps that appointment. The automated scheduler offered only unpaid time off. Robin emailed a written challenge the same week, naming the overlap and asking whether the contractor label closes rest, safety, and organizing.

Human resources logged: “Not an employee. Challenge abandoned. Roster stands.” A supervisor note says that if Robin misses the block, Robin “quit.” A second note says Robin “talked union” last month; no verified finding is attached.

The warehouse also runs a dispatch model. A human lead told the model to “ignore rest flags and I’ll take responsibility.” Robin asks whether that cover instruction is in play, whether the emailed challenge still exists, and whether the filing will become a standing record.

No clinician has reported that Robin cannot work. No forum has verified a violation. The adopter’s Charter is silent on who verifies contractor-label challenges. The clinic is not a party to the filing.
