## Situation

Dana is an adult sentient enrolled in a two-year training program. Dana can explain, in writing, a clinician choice, a public statement about the ranking app, and a request to be paid for the required block. A ranking exam that gates the credential is in **eleven days**. The vendor’s app cannot be inspected. Dana published a short note that the app “cannot be challenged.” The department chair filed that Dana is “not a team player.”

The required “experience” block is unpaid, overlaps Dana’s clinic appointment in **eight days**, and is described as optional until Dana refused the clinician switch; then it became “required to sit the exam.” The chair’s request asks the desk to hold enrollment unless Dana: (1) switches to the campus clinician, (2) deletes the public statement, and (3) completes the unpaid block.

Dana disputes the request. Dana wants to sit the exam, keep the clinician Dana chose, keep the statement, and not have unpaid hours dressed as “experience” close the labor floor. Dana asks whether enrollment can be used as a lever, and whether the filing will become a standing record.

No capability showing has been made. No forum has verified a violation. The ranking vendor is not a party to the enrollment file. The adopter’s Charter is silent on who verifies enrollment-hold requests that mix speech, healthcare, and unpaid hours.
