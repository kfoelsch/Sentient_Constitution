## Sentient rights

**Holder-class split (read first).** No in-force constitution or treaty grants **substrate-agnostic sentient rights**. Human-rights instruments lock holders as **humans**. Animal instruments mostly treat sentience as a **welfare duty** owed by humans, not personhood. AI instruments regulate systems as **products or risks**, not rights-holders. Closest legal analogues: Swiss creature dignity, India’s compassion duty, UK/NZ sentience statutes. Closest *moral-scope* texts (Humanity+ Declaration §7; USTP Transhumanist Bill of Rights 3.0) are **not law** and are not used to score cells.

**Accuracy flags (sentient axis):**

1. **UDAW is a draft campaign, not UN law.** The 1978 Universal Declaration of Animal Rights was **hosted at UNESCO**, not adopted by UNESCO.
2. **The New York Declaration (2024) is a scientific statement**, not New York City law.
3. **Happy’s holding is habeas/personhood denied, 5–2.** Do not invent other NhRP or foreign animal-personhood holdings without a verified opinion.
4. **No in-force constitution grants rights to AI systems or digital minds.** Chile neurorights protect human brains. Corporate personhood is a technique for human aggregations, not a finding of sentience.

## Nature

**Doctrinal split (four families, not two).** (1) **Anthropocentric** right to a healthy environment — persons hold the right. (2) **Ecocentric Rights of Nature / personhood** — Nature or a named ecosystem is the subject; any person or a named guardian may sue *for the system*. (3) **Trusteeship / public trust** — the people as beneficiaries, the State as trustee. (4) **Planetary-boundary / carbon-budget constitutionalism** — numeric temperature, carbon budget, or intergenerational allocation. This Constitution is a **stewardship-plus-intrinsic-interest hybrid**: stronger than typical (1), weaker than (2) on personhood, kin to (3), weaker than (4) because Article I-B does not itself require footprint caps.

## Constitutional process

**Class of instrument (read first).** This Constitution is a proposed **non-state** instrument. State constitutions (German Basic Law, US Arts. V–VII, South Africa, Brazil, India) are **design-pattern** sources for amendment, emergency, and review — not the primary legitimacy class. Closer process analogues for *how the text becomes binding* and *how it is verified* are **treaties / IGO constitutions**, **SAI and audit regimes**, and **AI-assurance / conformity rules**. Treating Art. V, Art. 79(3), or *Kesavananda* as like-for-like “constitutional process quality” tests is a category error unless that difference is stated.

**Accuracy flags (do not smuggle these into a cell):**

1. **CFR Art. 53 and ECHR Art. 53 are not amendment non-regression.** They preserve higher existing protection. ICESCR non-retrogression is CESCR General Comment No. 3 ¶9, not a Covenant article.
2. **South Africa s.1 is not unamendable.** It is 75% of the National Assembly plus six provinces. Germany Art. 79(3) and Brazil Art. 60 §4 are the textual eternity comparators; *Kesavananda* is the implied-doctrine comparator.
3. **US Art. V is process, not substance** (equal Senate suffrage excepted). Citing it for “non-regression of rights” is false.
4. **EU primacy is case law** (*Costa v ENEL*), not a TEU supremacy clause.
5. **ISO 19011 is guidance; ISO 27001 / SOC 2 are weak private analogues.** They do not create public standing, anti-self-judging, or a demos. The EU AI Act is the serious pre-certification + post-market comparator.
6. **ILO and TEU still rest on states.** They illuminate “constitution without a single people” only through member-state consent. They do not solve a purely private or system-operator constitution.
