This TOR lets independent human reviewers reuse the same comparator set and six-factor rubric. It is not an appointment, not a forum proceeding, and not a Chapter Eight standing record.

## Purpose

Confirm, overturn, or qualify the desk verdicts on three axes — **sentient rights**, **nature**, and **constitutional process** — for edition `SC-Corpus-2026.08.09`.

## Reviewers (suggested mix)

Commission at least three independent reviewers, one lead per axis, with the other two as challenge readers:

1. **Comparative constitutional law** — amendment, review, emergency, supremacy, democratic legitimacy of non-state instruments.
2. **Animal / sentience ethics and law** — welfare vs personhood, contested status, synthetic-mind claims.
3. **Environmental law / Rights of Nature** — Ecuador/Whanganui personhood, Aarhus justiciability, climate-numeric constitutionalism, indigenous land/FPIC.

Reviewers must not treat steward cards, indexes, or this TOR as duties. Numbered `core_*` files bind.

## Rubric (mandatory)

Score each axis **Better / Mixed / Worse / Incommensurable** on:

- **Scope** — who or what is protected
- **Substance** — what duties and floors attach
- **Justiciability** — can a holder trigger review, challenge, and remedy
- **Non-regression** — can the floor be quietly rolled back
- **Operability** — can a steward apply it without inventing a parallel stack
- **Pedigree** — democratic adoption, independent appointment, real-world enforcement

Rules:

- No single scalar. Stronger scope plus weaker pedigree is **Mixed**.
- State constitutions are the **wrong primary class** for process scoring (patterns only). Treaties, SAI/audit regimes, and AI-assurance rules are closer analogues for how a non-state text binds. Mark incommensurable items instead of forcing a winner.
- Do not treat ECHR Art. 53 or EU Charter Art. 53 as amendment non-regression; do not call South Africa s.1 unamendable; do not cite US Art. V for rights non-regression; do not cite a TEU “supremacy clause.”
- Do not treat UDAW or the 1978 animal-rights declaration as UNESCO/UN law; do not treat the 2024 New York Declaration as municipal law; do not treat the EU AI Act or Chile neurorights as AI-as-rights-holder.
- Do not treat India Ganges/Yamuna personhood as in force (stayed 2017); do not treat Magpie River as national statute; do not treat ecocide as a Rome Statute crime. See [BIBLIOGRAPHY.md](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) accuracy flags.
- [Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) model-constitution caveat sits on every verdict: “better on paper” ≠ “better in force.”
- Pair every material finding with a corpus quote (file + heading) and a comparator quote from [BIBLIOGRAPHY.md](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) or a newly cited **primary** text.
- Steelman the weaker side on every Better or Mixed call.

## Required stress tests

Desk review asks reviewers to **confirm or overturn** these calls:

1. **Article V-E default inclusion** — workable anti-exclusion rule, or a capture vector for synthetic systems? Steelman over-inclusion *and* under-inclusion of ordinary animals on the Def.P1 ladder.
2. **Natural Systems Standing vs Ecuador Arts. 71–74 and Te Awa Tupua 2017** — keep the hybrid (interests of their own, no personhood), or is the absence of named guardians and ecosystem-as-party a decisive gap?
3. **Article I-B no-cap** plus missing planetary-boundaries / carbon-budget article — honest subsidiarity to adopter instruments, or a hole relative to *Neubauer* (2021) and the ICJ climate advisory opinion (23 July 2025)?
4. **Indigenous Continuity vs UNDRIP / ILO 169** — is “does not decide historical land title” a lawful non-relocation, or a rights cut?
5. **Process complexity vs the Constitutional Tetrad** — does the owner-register / multi-corpus stack defeat **participation** and **timeliness** in practice even where the text names both as duties?
6. **Test 1 vs German Art. 79(3) / Brazil Art. 60 §4 / *Kesavananda*** — is operational non-regression (including indirect narrowing) a genuine advance, or unenforceable without an independent public bench?
7. **Non-state constitutive analogue** — does ILO Art. 36, TEU Art. 48, or French Art. 61 a priori review change the pedigree or certification-before-standing cells? EU AI Act Arts. 43/72 remain the product-safety analogue, not civic standing.

## Method

1. Read [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md) and [BIBLIOGRAPHY.md](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md). Do not re-discover the corpus map unless a cited home is wrong.
2. Open locators then the named `core_*` source. On conflict, source wins.
3. Add missed **primary** instruments with URLs. Drop any comparator that cannot be tied to a primary text.
4. For each axis, fill the six-factor cells. State whether the desk headline (all three **Mixed**) stands.
5. List residual disagreements among the three reviewers. Do not manufacture consensus.

## Deliverable

A short written opinion per axis (or one joint opinion with signed dissents) that:

- restates or revises the factor matrix
- quotes both sides
- names any cell change and why
- remains process support (cannot narrow core)

Vocabulary: use **forums / forum family / adjudicative body**, not institutional “court” or “tribunal”; **Preamble**, not “Chapter Zero”; **standing measurement**, not “standing calculus”; do not call this Constitution a bare “charter.”
