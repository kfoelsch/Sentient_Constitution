Chapter Twenty-Eight is the constitutional owner of **substantive non-regression and substantive amendment validity** (Substantive Test 1), anti-evasion controls, and validity-protection measures.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: a change that weakens the protections this Constitution depends on is invalid, even if the words say otherwise and even if it is done through procedure, definitions, guidance, a temporary measure, or an emergency label. This chapter is Substantive Test 1.*

**Purpose:** The purpose of Chapter Twenty-Eight is to prevent regressive changes to the Constitution. It is a guardrail against alterations that degrade the core constraints, Rights Floors, integrity and verification requirements, and legitimacy and stewardship obligations the rest of the Constitution depends on. No constitutional change is valid if it violates these non-regression requirements.

**Role:** The chapter is the substantive half of change validity. Four tests judge a change: Substantive Test 1 and Procedural Tests 2 to 4. This chapter owns Substantive Test 1:

- **Substantive Test 1** (this chapter), asks what a change actually does, not what it is called, so relabeling a rollback as guidance or a temporary measure does not save it. Amendment validity must also preserve both aims, Flourishing and Continuity, and must not hollow the Tetrad below material stake.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:

- the non-regression floor (**section 3**);
- Substantive Test 1 (**section 4**);
- the anti-evasion clause (**section 5**); and
- the validity-protection controls the chapter may impose and the owner non-relocation rule (**section 6**).

The chapter leaves:

- numeric standing measurement to [Chapter Fifteen](core_15.000_standing_measurement.md#chapter-fifteen-standing-measurement--question-2-how-good-or-bad-was-it);
- anti-constitutional-misconduct designation to [Chapter Twenty](core_20.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-twenty-anti-constitutional-misconduct-designation);
- Procedural Tests 2 to 4, ratification, and the referral of serious bad-faith conduct to [Chapter Twenty-Nine](core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#chapter-twenty-nine-amendment-ratification-and-procedural-validity):
  - **Procedural Test 2:** procedural validity and publication integrity;
  - **Procedural Test 3:** authority-chain and custody validity; and
  - **Procedural Test 4:** contestability and independent review validity; and
- additive expansion of protection and the sequencing of supremacy to [Chapter Thirty](core_30.000_expansion_supremacy.md#chapter-thirty-expansion-supremacy-and-external-legal-orders).

### 2. Why Non-Regression Exists

*In plain terms: a protection that can be quietly removed is not a protection. This chapter exists so that no change can weaken what this Constitution guarantees.*

Protections are most often lost not by repeal but by small steps: a narrower definition, a new procedure, a temporary measure, or an emergency label that never ends. This chapter makes any change that weakens the protections the Constitution depends on invalid, however it is made. It is the substantive test every amendment must pass before the procedural tests in Chapter Twenty-Nine are even reached.

<br>

*In plain terms: weakening **Safety (Constitutional Constraint)**, **Truth (Constitutional Constraint)**, **Epistemic Integrity**, **Dignity and Equal Moral Standing**, **Meaningful Agency**, or other Chapter Four protections — including by tightening definitions, raising impossible proof bars, or carving emergencies so they eat the rule — counts as invalid even if the words "amendment" or "interpretation" are on the label. Where a change helps one group and costs another, the minority is favored by default, and no group may be pushed below the minimum.*

No constitutional change is valid if it materially weakens core protections for any covered sentient class. Core protections include:
- Safety (Constitutional Constraint);
- Truth (Constitutional Constraint);
- Epistemic Integrity;
- Dignity and Equal Moral Standing; and
- Meaningful Agency and other Rights Floor protections stated in Chapter Four.

Material weakening includes indirect narrowing through:
- definitions;
- procedures;
- standing gates;
- verification barriers; or
- emergency carve-outs.

**Trade-offs between classes.** A change that strengthens a protection for one covered class while weakening it for another is judged class by class.
- **Minimums first:** A change may never take any class below the minimum that a protection in this section guarantees to every sentient. That is a material weakening for that class, whatever the change does for another class.
- **Minority favored by default:** Above those minimums, the change fails if it weakens the protection of the minority class in order to strengthen it for another class. It does not fail under this rule if it weakens the protection of a class that is not the minority in order to strengthen it for the minority.
- **Who the minority is:** [Minority](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#minority) defines it: the class with less market or institutional power, presumed to include dependency-affected and high-risk groups and groups defined by a Protected Characteristic and exposed to disadvantage in the matter, and otherwise the smaller class, judged by real effects and not by labels.
- **Overcoming the default:** The default may be set aside only where the published determination shows that the minority class keeps the core of the protection and is not materially worse off. The proposer must name the classes on each side in the justification required by [Chapter Twenty-Nine §4](core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#4-burden-of-justification-and-public-traceability) (*Burden of Justification and Public Traceability*), and the **Constitutional Forum** decides under **section 4**.

### 4. Substantive Test 1 — Substantive Non-Regression Validity
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Upstream: [§3](#3-non-regression-floor) non-regression floor definition.
- Downstream: [§5](#5-anti-evasion-clause) evasion; [Chapter Twenty-Nine](core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#chapter-twenty-nine-amendment-ratification-and-procedural-validity) (29.000) Procedural Tests 2–4 and procedural requirements (invalid if Substantive Test 1 fails regardless of procedure).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: Substantive Test 1 is the checklist — if a proposal would gut Chapter Two constraints, Subcores 3-A to 3-C integrity, Chapter Four Rights Floors, or Chapter Twenty-Seven legitimacy, it fails, including when the damage is done through procedure, evidence rules, or re-tagging rather than a single blunt deletion. The Constitutional Forum decides whether a proposal fails it, in public, with reasons, after everyone has had time to respond. A change that fixes a real error in a protection, and keeps its core, is a correction and not a weakening.*

A proposed amendment is invalid if it materially weakens any of the following:
- Chapter Two non-negotiable principle constraints
- **Subcores 3-A to 3-C** integrity requirements
- Chapter Four Rights Floors
- Chapter Twenty-Seven legitimacy obligations

Invalidity includes indirect narrowing through:
- definitions;
- standing gates;
- evidentiary burden manipulation;
- observability degradation; or
- emergency re-labeling.

**Who decides.** Whether a proposed amendment fails Substantive Test 1 is decided by the **Constitutional Forum**, in a published determination with reasons under **Article XXVI-A** (*Bounded Interpretive Mandate and Public Reasons*). The determination follows the amendment comment window in **Chapter Twenty-Nine §13.3** (*Proposal, Hearing, and Comment Window*), and it is open to independent review under **Article XXVI-B** (*Challenge Rights and Independent Review*). A proposer's own statement that a change is not a weakening is an input to that determination, not the determination.

**Corrections.** A change that narrows a Chapter Four protection is not a weakening where the **Constitutional Forum** finds it to be a correction. The forum may make that finding only where its published determination finds all of the following:
- **Error shown:** The protection as written defeats its own purpose, either by harming the class it protects or by conflicting with another Rights Floor in the same case. The error is shown by recorded effects and not by argument alone;
- **Independent review:** The error was reviewed under [Heightened Scrutiny](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#heightened-scrutiny) by reviewers independent of the proposers;
- **Narrowest change:** No narrower change would remove the error;
- **Core kept:** The change keeps the core of the protection, takes no class below the minimum, and neither removes the protection nor reverses its direction; and
- **Minority not worse off:** The change passes the trade-off rule in **section 3**, so the [Minority](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#minority) is not made worse off.

A change that is not found to be a correction is judged as any other change. A proposer's label of a change as a correction is an input to the determination, not the determination.

### 5. Anti-Evasion Clause

<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

*In plain terms: calling a rollback "implementation guidance," burying it in metadata, or calling it an emergency patch does not save it if the real-world effect is the same as a bad amendment.*

Repackaging a regressive change as any of the following does not fix invalidity:
- interpretation guidance;
- implementation memo;
- adoption metadata; or
- emergency temporary measure.

Invalidity remains where substantive constitutional effect is materially equivalent.

Serious bad-faith conduct in amendment or adoption is referred under [Chapter Twenty-Nine §11](core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#11-invalid-change-handling-and-remediation-continuity) (*Invalid-Change Handling and Remediation Continuity*), not handled here.

*In plain terms: Chapter Twenty-Eight can demand extra review, preserve evidence, or pause things when validity is in doubt — but it does not run parallel classification or invent new offense categories; numeric standing measurement belongs to the Chapter Fifteen §8 (*Unified Proportional LEQU Scale — Contribution and Violation Axes*) unified scale, and Chapter Twenty only adds the corresponding anti-constitutional-misconduct designation to qualifying Violation Axis slots 7–9.*

This chapter may impose immediate validity-protection controls. Those controls include:
- heightened review;
- provisional suspension where materially necessary;
- [Evidence Preservation](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation); and
- remediation publication.

Under [Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#owner-non-relocation), this chapter must not absorb, restate, or relocate obligations owned by another constitutional owner layer — including final offense classification, sanction taxonomy, standing measurement, adjudicative workflow, or procedural amendment validity owned by Chapters Fifteen, Twelve, Thirteen, and Seventeen respectively. Slot-flavored or classification-flavored language in this chapter or in **Chapter Twenty-Nine** operates only as a **trigger or referral heuristic** for routing to canonical owners and is **non-canonical** for final offense classification.

Cross-layer drift that imports adjudicative workflow, sanction mechanics, or classification substance from another owner layer into **Chapter Twenty-Eight** or **Chapter Twenty-Nine** — whether as direct text, as reader-guidance prose elevated to operative effect, or as inference from review-trigger language — is **non-compliant** under this section.

Future additions that touch offense-flavored, sanction-flavored, or classification-flavored language must include an explicit non-relocation pointer to the canonical owner home in the [Chapter One — constitutional owner register](core_01.000_constitutional_map.md#4-principles-definitions-and-rights), read with [Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#owner-non-relocation).
