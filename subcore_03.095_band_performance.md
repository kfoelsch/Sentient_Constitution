### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Chapter Three, Constitutional Performance band, is the constitutional owner of the **Constitutional Performance measurement family** — questions about whether systems actually deliver constitutional results without pointless waste. The detailed definitions themselves are **not here**. They all live in the [Continuity band](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md).

- Why: those definitions belong with Continuity stewardship topics — lasting capacity, waste, and getting real results for the resources spent. See the [efficiency / capacity / burden topic group](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency) and the [proportionality–burden–efficiency cluster](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
- The questions still serve both constitutional aims ([**Flourishing**](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing) and [**Continuity**](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity)).

*In plain terms: this file is the home for questions about whether systems actually deliver constitutional results without pointless waste. The detailed definitions live in the Continuity band.*

### 2. Why Constitutional Performance Is Measured

*In plain terms: following every rule is not the goal; delivering the outcomes the rules exist for is. This file is the home for checking that, without waste.*

A system can satisfy each process requirement and still deliver little, or deliver it at a cost in sentient time and materials that defeats the purpose. Measuring constitutional performance asks whether the outcomes actually arrive, and whether the burden of getting them is justified. It keeps proxies and paperwork from standing in for results.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): both aims, which this file serves:
    - [Flourishing](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing): outcomes for sentients are what the rules exist to deliver;
    - [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity): measuring waste protects the time and resources shared systems need;
- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§9.1 Productive Capacity (Instrumental Good)](core_02.010_values_principles_continuity_aim.md#91-productive-capacity-instrumental-good);

### Measuring Constitutional Performance (Preamble Measurement Family)

*In plain terms: these questions ask whether constitutional outcomes are actually delivered without wasting sentient time, effort, or materials.*

*Chapter Three home for the Constitutional Performance measurement family. Preamble §2 (*Measurements Overview*) keeps the plain-language category question in the overview table; the family table, constitutional use, and definition routing live here.*

*Materiality* ([Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality)) is Integrative — not a separate measurement category; it scales how strongly this family applies under [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake).

| Measurement family | Plain question | Main constitutional use |
|---|---|---|
| [Constitutional Efficiency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency) | Are constitutional outcomes actually being delivered? | Prevents proxy metrics from replacing real protection |
| [Avoidable Burden](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) | Is the system wasting sentient time, effort, materials, infrastructure, or energy? | Reduces unnecessary friction |
| [Productive Capacity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#productive-capacity) | Can sentients and systems keep producing aligned outcomes? | Protects durable capability without ecological burden-shifting |

These cross-cutting measures ask whether systems deliver constitutional outcomes effectively, avoid pointless waste, and preserve the capacity to keep producing aligned results — instrumental to both [**Flourishing**](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing) and [**Continuity**](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity).

- Operative measurement layers live on Chapter Three definition homes — see [Constitutional Efficiency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency), [Avoidable Burden](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden), [Productive Capacity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#productive-capacity), and [Burden-Reduction Duty](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#burden-reduction-duty).
- Performance targets, rewards, and resource measures are valid only to the extent they remain traceable to constitutional outcomes.
- They cannot substitute for Constitutional Efficiency, [Productive Capacity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#productive-capacity), or Rights-Floor compliance.
- Read with [Subcore 8-B §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation) through [Subcore 8-B §6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) where whole-system performance is materially in scope.
