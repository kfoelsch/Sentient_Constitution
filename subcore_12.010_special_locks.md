### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 12-A is the constitutional owner of the **full terms of the eight special locks**: for each, its trigger, the gated pathway it closes, what it does not restrict, the risk and protected interests, the corrective conditions, and the review route and reassessment point. [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#chapter-twelve-violation-consequences--remedy-correction-and-locks) states that the special locks are mandatory once their trigger is met, that there are only eight, the rules common to all of them, and each lock's guarantees; where a line there and the text here seem to differ, the reading more protective of the affected sentient controls.

*In plain terms: Chapter Eighteen says which serious violations always close a particular door, and what each lock may never take away. This file sets out each lock in full: exactly when it applies, exactly what it closes and leaves open, and exactly what it takes to lift it.*

### 2. Why the Locks Are the Same for Every Adopter

*In plain terms: a lock that one adopter could set tighter or looser than another would follow the sentient differently from place to place, and would invite moving to wherever it is weakest.*

A special lock restricts a sentient's access to a gated pathway, so its terms must be exact, the same everywhere, and no wider than the risk. If adopters could rewrite a trigger, a gated pathway, or a corrective condition, the same verified violation finding could close a door in one place and leave it open in another, a subject could move to the weakest version, and a subject in a stricter place would carry a heavier burden for the same conduct. One set of terms for each lock, the same for every adopter, keeps the locks portable, predictable, and bounded.

<a id="3-the-eight-special-locks"></a>
### 3. The Eight Special Locks

*In plain terms: each lock in full, in the order Chapter Eighteen lists them.*

The rules common to every special lock are in [Chapter Twelve §4.5 Special Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks): each is mandatory once its verified trigger is met, is recorded with the attachment fields, attaches only to a verified violation finding, and substitutes for no other lock, designation, or remedy.

<a id="31-forum-service-standing-lock"></a>

#### 3.1 Forum-Service Standing Lock

*In plain terms: a forum member who failed to step aside when recusal was required, and whose bias materially affected the matter, is kept out of forum service roles until independent review finds the risk has been reduced.*

**Trigger:** a verified failure to recuse where recusal was required and impartiality was materially compromised. The lock applies whether or not the violation finding also reaches a Chapter Twenty designation track.

- **Gated pathway:**
  - merits;
  - appellate;
  - review;
  - emergency;
  - certifier;
  - contested panel-coordination;
  - reserve;
  - substitute; and
  - materially supporting forum roles across forum families.
- **Risk:** biased adjudication, forum-integrity injury, and recurrence of non-recusal in the same or related forum settings.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** parties to affected matters and public trust in forum impartiality.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - The lock must not be lifted, narrowed, or suspended unless the complete independent restoration record is satisfied and gives reasons showing that narrower protection is sufficient.
  - Reassessment requires:
    - full disclosure;
    - identification of affected matters after reasonable inquiry;
    - feasible correction, vacatur, rehearing, notice, and evidence preservation;
    - restitution or other practical repair;
    - cooperation with review;
    - role-separation and recusal requalification;
    - a demonstrated period of compliant lower-risk service where lawful; and
    - an independent finding that recurrence risk and public-trust injury have been materially reduced.
  - Expertise scarcity, staffing shortage, prior service, reputation, apology, or contribution cannot lift the lock by itself.
- **Review route:** independent review outside the compromised forum pathway.
- **Reassessment point:** the independent restoration finding above. Intentional, concealed, repeated, retaliatory, or outcome-affecting failure receives heightened scrutiny. Recurrence uncertainty and isolation from forum roles, networks, and environments that recreate the impartiality risk apply under [Chapter Twelve §4.1 Definition and Attachment](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#41-definition-and-attachment).

<a id="32-stakeholder-participation-standing-lock"></a>

#### 3.2 Stakeholder-Participation Standing Lock

*In plain terms: a sentient who corrupted, captured, or gamed the stake-weighted and binding stakeholder channels is closed out of them until independent review finds the risk has been reduced. It is separate from the governance-voting lock.*

**Trigger:** a verified violation finding that the subject corrupted, captured, coerced, gamed, or otherwise materially abused a **stakeholder-participation** pathway — including false or inflated stake claims, coercive participation-weight extraction, vote-buying or comparable influence-buying in binding stakeholder choice, concealment of material conflicts in stakeholder representation, or using stakeholder voice to capture the affected system, institution, or bounded decision domain.

- **Gated pathway:**
  - stake-weighted vote;
  - binding stakeholder choice participation;
  - stakeholder representation authority; and
  - comparable Stakeholder System Participation pathways within the affected system, institution, or bounded decision domain under [Stakeholder Weight](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder-weight) and [Subcore 3-N — Binding Stakeholder Choice](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp2-binding-stakeholder-choice).
- **Does not by itself restrict:**
  - [Subcore 3-N — Stakeholder](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder) status or recognition that the sentient is materially affected;
  - the **governance-voting** pathway, **Foundational Constitutional Choice**, or comparable legitimacy-mechanism binding collective choice under [Chapter Twenty-One §6.1 Entitlement and Eligibility](core_21.000_governance.md#61-entitlement-and-eligibility);
  - Rights-Floor minimums, survival-critical access, or constitutionally required audit, challenge, and remedy pathways.
- **Risk:** continued capture or distortion of stakeholder voice, false-stake dominance, coerced participation weight, and recurrence of stakeholder-pathway abuse in the same or related domains.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** affected stakeholders, integrity of binding stakeholder choice, and non-capture of the relevant system, institution, or bounded decision domain.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - The lock must not be lifted, narrowed, or suspended unless an independent restoration record is satisfied and gives reasons showing that narrower protection is sufficient.
  - Reassessment requires:
    - correction or withdrawal of false or inflated stake claims;
    - remedy for affected stakeholders where owed;
    - disclosure of conflicts and influence channels used in the abuse;
    - structural or procedural safeguards against recurrence in the affected domain;
    - cooperation with review; and
    - an independent finding that stakeholder-pathway recurrence risk has been materially reduced.
  - Prior contribution, reputation, stake size, operator indispensability, or apology cannot lift the lock by itself.
- **Review route:** independent review outside the captured stakeholder-participation pathway.
- **Reassessment point:** the independent restoration finding above. Intentional, concealed, repeated, retaliatory, or outcome-affecting abuse receives heightened scrutiny. Recurrence uncertainty and isolation from proxy networks that recreate the capture risk apply under [Chapter Twelve §4.1 Definition and Attachment](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#41-definition-and-attachment).
- **Non-substitution:** A Stakeholder-Participation Standing Lock is not a substitute for a **governance-voting** lock, and a **governance-voting** lock is not a substitute for this lock.

<a id="33-institutional-authority-standing-lock"></a>

#### 3.3 Institutional Authority Standing Lock

*In plain terms: a sentient who used an institution to carry out a violation loses governing, controlling, or owning authority in it and in its look-alikes. The sentient can still work there, be served by it, or form other institutions.*

**Trigger:** all three of the following must be true:

- a violation finding has been verified;
- the violation finding shows that the subject made real use of an institution, an enterprise, or an office in one to carry out the violation, as in any of the cases listed next; and
- the violation finding is still open, or the harm has not been fixed in a way that matters.

**Cases that count as that kind of use:**

- directing the institution or enterprise, or knowingly operating it, to carry out the violation or to get around a duty, check, remedy, certification, or forum order;
- hiding who controls, has an interest in, or owns the institution or enterprise, through a nominee, shell, front, or relabeling, either to hold authority the subject was barred from or to defeat the [formation check](core_05.000_charters.md#7-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity) (*Chapter Five §7 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity*);
- knowingly making, signing, or causing a materially false statement or piece of evidence, whether or not the falsehood hides who has control:
  - **What is false:**
    - a formation check statement; or
    - evidence in a System or Institutional Certification Record.
  - **What it is for:**
    - holding or keeping authority in the institution or enterprise; or
    - passing the formation check or a certification;
- retaliating, regardless of the means used, against a protected reporter or against a sentient who refused an unlawful or unconstitutional instruction, where the report or the refusal concerned that institution or enterprise;
- suppressing, burying, or refusing to receive a [Subcore 3-J — Protected Reporting (Whistleblowing)](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#protected-reporting-whistleblowing) report made through the institution or enterprise, including an insider's report of a crossing over the line of [Subcore 3-M — Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality); or
- knowingly keeping a misaligned reward received through the institution or enterprise after notice under [Chapter Twelve §4.4.2 Misaligned Rewards](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#442-misaligned-rewards).

- **Gated pathway:**
  - taking or exercising governing, controlling, or owning authority — including any covered office under [Chapter Five §7 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity](core_05.000_charters.md#7-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity) — in:
    - the institution or enterprise through which the wrongdoing occurred;
    - its successors, controlled entities, and any nominee-, shell-, or proxy-held entity through which the subject would hold the same authority; and
    - other institutions or enterprises with the same function or the same protected subjects, where the verified risk would recur and the lock record names them.
  - The lock follows the authority, not the label. A change of name, form, or seat does not take the authority outside it ([Chapter Thirteen §5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority)).
- **Does not by itself restrict:**
  - forming or leading an institution or enterprise outside the gated pathway;
  - joining, working in, being served by, or being a [Subcore 3-N — Stakeholder](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder) of any institution or enterprise, including the one concerned, in a role that carries no governing, controlling, or owning authority;
  - the existence or operation of the institution or enterprise itself, which another lawful holder may lead;
  - passive holdings that carry no control, no vote, and no say in appointments, although remedy owed from them stays collectable; or
  - wages for work outside the verified violation, the Rights-Floor minimums, survival-critical access, or constitutionally required audit, challenge, and remedy pathways.
- **Risk:** continued or renewed use of an institution or enterprise to carry out the same wrongdoing, evasion of the lock through nominees and successors, and recurrence in comparable institutions while the violation finding stays unresolved.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** the sentients the institution or enterprise affects, its workers and stakeholders, the integrity of the formation check and of alignment certification, and the independence of the seats that verify the institution.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - The lock must not be lifted, narrowed, or suspended unless an independent restoration record is satisfied and gives reasons showing that narrower protection is sufficient.
  - Reassessment requires:
    - correction, unwinding, or withdrawal of the wrongdoing that was carried out through the institution or enterprise;
    - remedy for affected sentients where owed;
    - disclosure of the control, interests, nominees, and rewards used;
    - a changed structure — an independent seat in place, and the control or reward arrangement that enabled the wrongdoing removed;
    - cooperation with review;
    - a demonstrated period of compliant service without governing, controlling, or owning authority, where lawful;
    - an independently verified authenticity finding on the four parts in [Chapter Five §7 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity](core_05.000_charters.md#7-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity), shown in conduct, control, and rewards and not in profession of belief, where a continuing statement that others deserve harm or discriminatory treatment counts against restoration under [Chapter Two §7.5 Expression and the Harm Line](core_02.000_values_principles.md#75-expression-and-the-harm-line); and
    - an independent finding that recurrence risk has been materially reduced.
  - Founder status, capital contributed, indispensability to the institution, prior contribution, reputation, or apology cannot lift the lock by itself.
- **Review route and reassessment:**
  - **Who reviews:** an independent review, run outside the institution or enterprise concerned and outside any body whose authority the lock limits.
  - **When the lock can change:** when the independent restoration finding above is made.
  - **Closer scrutiny:** use of an institution to carry out a violation gets a closer look when it was intentional, concealed, repeated, retaliatory, or changed the outcome of a matter.
  - **Risk qualifiers:** if it is unclear whether the same use will happen again, that doubt counts as risk, and the lock may need to keep the sentient away from nominee, shell, and proxy networks that would recreate the risk ([Chapter Twelve §4.1.3 Risk Qualifiers](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#413-risk-qualifiers)).
- **Gate effect:** While the lock is outstanding, the outstanding-lock gate in [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) applies to the gated pathway and no further. The institution or enterprise is not stopped. It must fill the authority again with another lawful holder. A body that knowingly seats the subject in the gated pathway, directly or through a nominee, commits evasion of the lock under [Chapter Thirteen §5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority). A body filling the gated pathway can ask whether the lock applies to it under [Chapter Thirteen §3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects). Where only the grounds for an Interim Hold are met, no lock attaches and the Interim Hold that **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) provides applies instead ([Chapter Twelve §4.6 Interim Holds and Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#46-interim-holds-and-locks)).
- **Honest error:** A statement that is wrong without the subject's knowledge is a defect to log and correct under [Chapter Five §8 When a Holder Fails the Formation Check](core_05.000_charters.md#8-when-a-holder-fails-the-formation-check). It is not a trigger. Knowledge is shown by verified facts, such as what the subject knew, controlled, or received, and is not presumed from the falsity alone.

<a id="34-perjury-standing-lock"></a>

#### 3.4 Perjury Standing Lock

*In plain terms: a sentient who knowingly lied on a material point under oath or attestation can still speak and be heard, but the sentient's own word counts only with independent corroboration, and the sentient cannot hold the seats that decide what is verified.*

**Trigger:** all of the following must be true:

- a violation finding has been verified;
- the violation finding is still open, or the harm has not been fixed in a way that matters;
- the violation finding shows that the subject knowingly gave a materially false statement:
  - **How it was given:**
    - under oath;
    - under affirmation;
    - under an equivalent truth-duty; or
    - as a stated attestation;
  - **Forms it can take, including:**
    - testimony;
    - a deposition or preserved testimony;
    - a sworn or attested declaration; and
    - an attestation given for another;
  - **Who it was given to:**
    - a forum;
    - a verifying authority;
    - a certifier; or
    - a record custodian; and
- a Standing Record or decision relied on the statement, or was put at risk by it.

A statement is material when it bears on a fact the forum, verifying authority, certifier, or custodian had to find.

The lock applies on the same terms as the Forum-Service Standing Lock, whether or not the violation finding also reaches a Chapter Twenty designation track. A knowingly false statement that was not given under oath, affirmation, an equivalent truth-duty, or a stated attestation does not trigger this lock; it goes to ordinary gated-pathway locks under [Chapter Twelve §3.2 Prevention — General Standing Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#32-prevention--general-standing-locks).

- **Gated pathway:**
  - the weight a Standing Record gives the subject's own attestation, declaration, or sworn or affirmed statement without independent corroboration;
  - the seats that decide what is verified: verifying authority, certifier, record custodian, and forum fact-finding roles; and
  - giving or signing an attestation for another on which a Standing Record, a certification, or a Standing Record relies.
- **Does not by itself restrict:**
  - testifying, giving evidence, answering, petition, defense, or protected reporting: the subject may still speak and be heard, and what the subject says is weighed with independent corroboration;
  - participation as an affected party;
  - governing, controlling, or owning authority in an institution or enterprise, which the Institutional Authority Standing Lock governs on its own integration record;
  - wages for work outside the verified violation, the Rights-Floor minimums, survival-critical access, or constitutionally required audit, challenge, and remedy pathways.
- **Risk:** the same subject's statements again defeating a Standing Record, a verification, or a forum decision, and the chilling of truthful witnesses while a false statement stands uncorrected.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** parties to the affected matters, those who rely on the Standing Record or certification, and the integrity of verification and forum fact-finding.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - The lock must not be lifted, narrowed, or suspended unless an independent restoration record is satisfied and gives reasons showing that narrower protection is sufficient.
  - Reassessment requires:
    - correction of the statement on the Standing Record where it was given, with disclosure of what was false and why;
    - identification of the affected matters and decisions after reasonable inquiry, and feasible correction, vacatur, rehearing, notice, and evidence preservation;
    - remedy for affected sentients where owed;
    - cooperation with review;
    - a demonstrated period of statements that independent corroboration confirms, where lawful; and
    - an independent finding that recurrence risk has been materially reduced.
  - Apology, a bare retraction, prior contribution, or reputation cannot lift the lock by itself.
- **Knowledge and correction:**
  - A statement that is wrong without the subject's knowledge, including an honest mistake of memory and a good-faith statement in a protected report or in testimony that proves wrong, is not a trigger.
  - Knowledge is shown by verified facts, such as what the subject knew, controlled, or received, and is not presumed from the falsity, or from a contradiction between two statements, alone.
  - A subject who corrects the statement on their own, fully, before the Standing Record or a decision relied on it and before the falsity was found, triggers no lock. A correction after reliance does not remove the trigger and counts toward restoration.
- **Review route:** independent review outside the forum, verifying authority, certifier, or record in which the statement was given.
- **Reassessment point:** the independent restoration finding above. Intentional, concealed, repeated, retaliatory, witness-influencing, or outcome-affecting falsity receives heightened scrutiny. Recurrence uncertainty and isolation from the networks and settings that recreate the risk apply under [Chapter Twelve §4.1 Definition and Attachment](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#41-definition-and-attachment).
- **No lock from doubt:** a statement that is doubted, contradicted, or unconvincing is weighed on the Standing Record and is not a violation finding ([Chapter Twelve §4.1.2 What a Lock Attaches To](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#412-what-a-lock-attaches-to)).

<a id="35-anti-constitutional-trust-lock"></a>

#### 3.5 Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock

*In plain terms: after a final anti-constitutional misconduct designation, the sentient is closed out of influence over the systems and gated pathways that keep the constitutional order honest. Restoration is exceptional.*

**Trigger:** a final Chapter Twenty designation for the responsible sentient, group, institution, successor entity, controlled entity, or materially coordinated proxy.

- **Gated pathway:**
  - Direct or indirect influence over Class A/B/C systems.
  - Constitutional Forums.
  - Alignment certification.
  - Critical-system stewardship.
  - Audit, evidence, safety, access-continuity, investigation, enforcement, restitution, and protected-reporting pathways.
  - Appointment, budget, ownership, voting, procurement, standards, ranking, access-allocation, model-governance, or policy authority affecting those functions.
  - Look-through coverage also reaches:
    - shells and nominees;
    - advisory capture and funding leverage;
    - contractual vetoes and information chokepoints;
    - data custody, privileged access, and comparable means of functional control.
  - The protections in [Chapter Twelve §4.1.4 What a Lock Does Not Itself Restrict](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#414-what-a-lock-does-not-itself-restrict) apply to this lock in full. In particular, this lock does not cut off the challenge, review, appeal, and remedy pathways that the Rights-Floor minimums require, and it adds no other limit on them.
- **Risk:** renewed anti-constitutional influence, capture, or proxy control while ACMD consequences remain unresolved.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** constitutional order, covered systems, the sentients who depend on those systems, forums, accountability pathways, and parties who would be exposed to renewed influence.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - Restoration is exceptional.
  - Restoration requires:
    - independently verified restitution or lawful substitute restoration;
    - structural correction of tainted governance, systems, records, and decisions;
    - disclosure and cooperation sufficient to map dependencies;
    - durable anti-recurrence and proxy-control safeguards;
    - required monitoring and requalification; and
    - a published finding that renewed influence is safer and constitutionally compatible.
  - For a Grave or Catastrophic designation, covered-system and forum influence remains barred unless an independent reviewer makes specific, published findings that reinstatement is:
    - necessary;
    - proportionate;
    - non-capturing; and
    - safer than continued exclusion for the protected constitutional interests.
- **Review route:** independent review with published findings.
- **Reassessment point:**
  - The published restoration or reinstatement finding above is the checkpoint for any narrowing or lifting.
  - Under [Chapter Twelve §4.1 Definition and Attachment](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#41-definition-and-attachment), keep the sentient isolated from proxy networks, successor entities, and settings shared with coordinated violators while the risk remains live.
- **Read with:** [Chapter Fourteen §7.2](core_14.000_misconduct_designation.md#72-prevention--anti-constitutional-locks) (*Prevention — Anti-Constitutional Locks*) for the extra rules on restricting liberty or imprisonment that apply only after a final anti-constitutional misconduct designation. The Trust Lock rules themselves stay in this section.

<a id="36-dissolution-reformation-lock"></a>

#### 3.6 Dissolution Reformation Lock

*In plain terms: once a forum has ordered a Chartered Party dissolved, it cannot come back under a new name or form. The lock binds the dissolved Party, its successors, and its controlled entities, not the sentients who belonged to it.*

**Trigger:** the lock is set by a final order of either of these kinds:

- a final dissolution order under **CI-12.210 §4** (*Dissolution*), including the wind-down order of a forum whose dissolution or disbandment threshold under **CF-16.445 §6** (*Performance Thresholds and Alerts*) is crossed; or
- a retirement order for a System under **CI-19.205 §5** (*Systems under Consequence Orders*).

**Who the lock is about:**

- the dissolved Chartered Party, or the dissolved Class P Operating Party that held no System Charter or Institutional Charter;
- not any sentient who belonged to the dissolved party; and
- not an Operating Party that is a single sentient, or a group of sentients acting together. For such a party:
  - it is never dissolved under **CI-12.210 §4** (*Dissolution*);
  - it is never the subject of this lock, and no lock under this entry reaches a sentient through it; and
  - the individual locks and **Chapter Twenty** apply to the sentients involved instead.

**How the lock is set:**

- the order sets the lock on the same two-key decision and records it; and
- the order records the permanence ground under [Chapter Thirteen §4 Restoration and Reassessment](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-restoration-and-reassessment), if the lock rests on one.

- **Gated pathway:**
  - re-forming, reviving, re-registering, or running the dissolved Chartered Party, under any name or form;
  - its successors, controlled entities, and any nominee-, shell-, or proxy-held entity through which the same activity would go on under the same centre of power; and
  - any Operating Party that is one operation with any of them under the common-control test in [Subcore 3-M — Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality).
  - The lock follows the activity and the control, not the label. A change of name, form, seat, or holder does not take an entity outside it ([Chapter Thirteen §5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority)).
- **Does not by itself restrict:**
  - any sentient forming or leading a different Operating Party, including one that does similar work and including a sentient who ran the dissolved Chartered Party alone or with others, so long as it is not one of the entities above and no lock on that sentient bars it;
  - a good-faith purchaser, an independent successor created by a structural-separation order under **CI-19.205 §3** (*Supervised Operation and Receivership*), a workers' cooperative formed by the workers, or an arm's-length buyer under the order;
  - members, workers, and dependents of the dissolved Chartered Party joining, working in, or being served by another Operating Party;
  - the receiver, liquidator, or successor that the dissolution order itself names to carry services, records, and challenge pathways forward;
  - the remedy, accounting, and records duties the order sets, which outlast the dissolution; or
  - the Rights-Floor minimums, survival-critical access, or constitutionally required audit, challenge, and remedy pathways.
- **Risk:** the same Operating Party, under the same control, coming back under a new name or form after a forum has found that nothing short of ending it protects others.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** the sentients the dissolved Chartered Party harmed or endangered, the dependents moved to receivers, and the integrity of the dissolution order and of the forum that made it.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - **Permanent form:** where the order records pervasive anti-constitutional conduct or unremedied Irreversible Harm under [Chapter Thirteen §4 Restoration and Reassessment](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-restoration-and-reassessment), nothing runs with time. Passage of time, apology, a resolved cause, prior contribution, capital, a change of holders, or a change of name or form cannot lift the lock by itself. It ends only on a published finding of independent review that the dissolution order was set aside under **CI-19.205 §6** (*Review, Fairness, and Enforcement*) or that material new verified facts show the violation finding it rested on was wrong.
  - **Restorable form:** where the order records neither permanent-lock ground, the lock ends on a published finding of independent review that the cause the order found is resolved, the remedy owed is delivered, the safeguards that would stop a repeat are in place, and a return is safer than continued exclusion for the protected interests. Apology, prior contribution, capital, or a change of name or form cannot satisfy that finding of independent review by itself.
- **Review route and reassessment:**
  - **Who reviews:** an independent review, run outside the dissolved Chartered Party, outside the issuing panel, and outside any body whose authority the lock limits.
  - **When the lock can change:**
    - for the permanent form, a set-aside of the order, or material new verified facts that bear on the violation finding the order rested on;
    - for the restorable form, the restoration finding above, or either of those same two events; and
    - never on time alone.
  - **The permanent form stays open to review:**
    - it is permanent as to the dissolved Chartered Party, and stays open to correction of an error, as [Chapter Twelve §4.1 Definition and Attachment](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#41-definition-and-attachment) requires; and
    - it is re-reviewed on the integration record at each review interval of the Tier that matches the dissolved Chartered Party's class.
  - **Who can ask for review:** any member, worker, harmed sentient, or good-faith successor, on material new verified facts, at any time.
- **Gate effect:** While the lock stands, the Operating Party lock gate in [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) applies to the dissolved Chartered Party and the entities named above, and to any sentient who would take or exercise governing, controlling, or owning authority in one of them. It does not apply to a different Operating Party. The party that says an entity is caught bears the burden of showing it, by look-through on verified facts, and an entity may keep operating while the forum decides unless the forum finds a specific risk.
- **Systems:** For a System, the lock bars re-launching or operating the retired System, or a successor that carries on the same operation under the same steward control. It does not reach state-bearing components held in archive or sealed custody under **CS-4.350 §8** (*Preservation during Adoption Transition*), and it restricts no sentient. Where a credible sentience indicator is on the Sentience-Status Adjudication Record, the lock bars only the steward's relaunch or operation of that System as a service to others, it is never a permanent bar on the System's own existence, and it is reviewed at each review interval of the Tier that matches the System's class.
- **Former members:** This lock is not a violation finding against any sentient who belonged to the Chartered Party. Where a sentient is separately found responsible, the other locks, [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-fourteen-anti-constitutional-misconduct-designation) designation, and remedy apply to that sentient on their own terms.

<a id="37-care-and-stewardship-lock"></a>

#### 3.7 Care and Stewardship Lock

*In plain terms: a sentient who abused someone in their care, was cruel to them, or left them in serious neglect loses the authority and the unsupervised access that made it possible, until an independent reviewer finds it safe to give them back. The rule is the same for a parent, a carer, an operator, and a parent system.*

**Trigger:** all of the following must be true:

- a violation finding has been verified;
- the violation finding is still open, or the harm has not been fixed in a way that matters;
- the violation finding shows abuse of, cruelty toward, or serious neglect of a developing or dependent sentient; and
- the subject held care, guardianship, stewardship, or parent-system authority over that sentient, or had unsupervised access to it through a role.

The lock is mandatory once its trigger is met, and it applies on the same terms to every substrate. The scope of the lock is calibrated under [Chapter Twelve §4.2 Proportionality and Calibration](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#42-proportionality-and-calibration). At the least it closes unsupervised care. Neglect short of this trigger follows [Chapter Twelve §4.4.1 Negligence and Neglect](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#441-negligence-and-neglect).

- **Gated pathway:**
  - care, guardianship, stewardship, or parent-system authority over a developing or dependent sentient, under **Article VIII-A** (*Family and Care Relationships*), **Article VIII-C** (*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*), and **Article VIII-D** (*Developing Sentients, Best-Interest, and Graduated Capability*);
  - roles that carry unsupervised access to developing or dependent sentients; and
  - consenting on a new sentient's behalf under Chapter Three *Instantiation Consent*.
- **Does not by itself restrict:**
  - supervised contact that the reviewing forum finds safe and in the dependent's best interest;
  - bodily reproduction, which stays under **Article VIII-B** (*Reproductive and Lineage Autonomy*), or other creation of sentients, which only the Creation Lock in [Chapter Twelve §4.4.7 Capacity Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#447-capacity-locks) may reach on its own integration record; or
  - the protections in [Chapter Twelve §4.1.4 What a Lock Does Not Itself Restrict](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#414-what-a-lock-does-not-itself-restrict).
- **A sentient born or derived later:** where a child is born to the subject, or a sentient is derived from the subject, while the lock stands, the lock applies to care authority over that sentient from the start.
- **Separation:** where the lock separates a protected care relationship, **Article VIII-E** (*Non-Separation*) and [Chapter Twelve §4.1.5 Separation Limits](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#415-separation-limits) apply, and the Chapter Three *Best-Interest Standard* governs for the dependent.
- **Body-and-mind limit:** the [body-and-mind limit](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#body-and-mind-limit) in Chapter Eighteen §4.4.7 Capacity Locks applies to this lock.
- **Risk:** renewed harm to the same sentient or to another developing or dependent sentient.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** the harmed sentient, and other developing or dependent sentients in the subject's care or likely to come into it.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - remedy to the harmed sentient is opened and is being delivered ([Chapter Twelve §3.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#34-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions));
  - correction offered on fair terms is completed and independently verified;
  - an independent reviewer finds that restored authority is safe and in the best interest of each dependent concerned; and
  - restoration proceeds in stages, beginning with supervised contact.
- **Review route:** independent review by the forum with jurisdiction. The dependent has an independent representative, and the dependent's own preferences are heard under Chapter Three *Graduated Capability*.
- **Reassessment point:** at the intervals stated on the integration record, and on new verified evidence. The lock does not lift on time alone.

<a id="38-financial-authority-standing-lock"></a>

#### 3.8 Financial Authority Standing Lock

*In plain terms: a sentient who took, hid, or misused money that was not theirs, or lied to get it, loses the positions that put other sentients' or the public's money in their hands, until an independent reviewer finds it safe to give them back. The lock does not touch their own pay, their own accounts, or what they need to live. Being unable to pay back does not keep it in place, and paying back does not lift it by itself.*

**Trigger:** all of the following must be true:

- a violation finding has been verified;
- the violation finding is still open, or the harm has not been fixed in a way that matters;
- the violation finding shows knowing or intentional conduct of one of the kinds listed next; and
- the conduct was material, as [Subcore 3-M — Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality) defines it.

**Kinds of conduct that count:**

- taking, diverting, or misusing money, funds, or assets held for other sentients or for the public, including self-dealing and use for a purpose other than the one they were held for;
- knowingly making, signing, or causing a materially false statement, record, account, or valuation to obtain money, funding, or a payment, or to hide a financial position, a loss, or where money went;
- concealing, moving, or disguising money or assets that came from a verified violation, or that are owed as remedy under [Chapter Twelve §3.1 Remedy and Correction](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#31-remedy-and-correction);
- withholding, delaying past what is owed, or diverting pay, benefits, refunds, or other payments owed to other sentients; and
- obtaining money from sentients by deception or coercion, or by exploiting a dependency or vulnerability the subject knew of.

The lock is mandatory once its trigger is met, and it applies on the same terms to every substrate. The scope of the lock is calibrated under [Chapter Twelve §4.2 Proportionality and Calibration](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#42-proportionality-and-calibration). At the least it closes unsupervised control of other sentients' or the public's money. A mistake or shortfall without that knowledge or intent follows [Chapter Twelve §4.4.1 Negligence and Neglect](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#441-negligence-and-neglect).

- **Gated pathway:**
  - authority to hold, control, approve, or pay out money, funds, or assets that belong to other sentients or to the public, including treasury, custody, trustee, payroll, procurement, grant-making, and signing authority;
  - roles that carry unsupervised access to that money or the power to alter the records of it; and
  - lending, taking deposits or investments, or managing investments for other sentients.
- **Does not by itself restrict:**
  - the sentient's own earning, holding, spending, saving, or borrowing;
  - working in or being paid by an institution or enterprise, including the one concerned, in a role that carries no financial authority;
  - supervised roles that the reviewing forum finds safe, such as a role with dual control or a set spending limit;
  - recovery of what is owed, which runs under [Chapter Twelve §3.1 Remedy and Correction](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#31-remedy-and-correction) on its own route: the lock does not freeze or seize the subject's own assets; or
  - the protections in [Chapter Twelve §4.1.4 What a Lock Does Not Itself Restrict](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#414-what-a-lock-does-not-itself-restrict).
- **Risk:** renewed taking, concealment, or misuse of money held for other sentients or the public, including through nominees, proxies, or successor accounts and entities.
- **Protected subjects or interests:** the sentients whose money, pay, or assets the subject could reach, the public purse, the members, workers, and beneficiaries of the institution or enterprise concerned, and the integrity of its financial records and funding decisions.
- **Corrective conditions:**
  - remedy to those harmed is opened and is being delivered ([Chapter Twelve §3.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#34-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions));
  - a full accounting of the money and assets concerned is disclosed and independently verified;
  - the control gap that allowed the conduct is closed, for example by an independent seat, dual control, or another safeguard;
  - an independent finding that recurrence risk has been materially reduced; and
  - restoration proceeds in stages, beginning with supervised authority or a set spending limit.
- **Debt and means:** Repayment alone does not lift the lock. Inability to repay alone does not keep it in place. Founder status, capital contributed, indispensability, prior contribution, reputation, or apology cannot lift it by itself.
- **Review route:** independent review, run outside the institution or enterprise concerned and outside any body whose authority the lock limits.
- **Reassessment point:** at the intervals stated on the integration record, and on new verified evidence. The lock does not lift on time alone.
- **Gate effect:** While the lock is outstanding, the gated pathway is closed to the subject and no further. The institution or enterprise is not stopped. It must fill the authority again with another lawful holder. A body that knowingly seats the subject in the gated pathway, directly or through a nominee, commits evasion of the lock under [Chapter Thirteen §5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority). A body filling the gated pathway can ask whether the lock applies to it under [Chapter Thirteen §3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects).
- **Honest error:** A statement or account that is wrong without the subject's knowledge is a defect to log and correct. It is not a trigger. Knowledge is shown by verified facts, such as what the subject knew, controlled, or received, and is not presumed from a shortfall alone.
