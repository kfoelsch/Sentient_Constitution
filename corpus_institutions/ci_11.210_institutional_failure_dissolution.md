<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Reader guidance (non-operative): Orientation</span></strong></summary>

> **Orientation**
> - **Who keeps the official record** — the authoritative consequence record is an **Integrity forum lead record** under **CF-13.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*), not an institution-only file controlled by the subject institution.
> - **What the institution still must do** — name a consequence liaison, submit required materials on deadline, mirror the forum-held record without rewrite, propose continuity transfer for forum approval, and publish the personal-accountability boundary.
> - **How the consequence ladder works** — **CI-11.210 §1** (*Opening a consequence matter*) through **CI-11.210 §3** (*Notice, response, and resolution*) say when a matter opens, what the forum must find before it orders anything, and how the Chartered Party is heard and can resolve the problem. **CI-11.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*) sets the steps short of dissolution: directed correction, constrained authority, a contraction order that returns a Chartered Party to below Class L, supervised operation, and receivership or structural separation, with emergency containment, a Systems counterpart, review, and enforcement. **CI-11.210 §4** (*Dissolution*) is the last step.
> - **What dissolution does** — ends the Chartered Party and carries a Dissolution Reformation Lock, so the dissolved Chartered Party and its successor vehicles cannot be re-formed. The lock is permanent where the order finds pervasive anti-constitutional conduct or unremedied Irreversible Harm, and otherwise it lifts on an independent restoration finding.
> - **What dissolution is not** — not a substitute for anti-constitutional-misconduct designation under **Chapter Twelve** or ordinary personal accountability under constitutional law.

Core home: 11

This file is the institutional implementation home for **CI-11.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*).

*In plain terms: **CI-11.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) is the institutions layer's "when has an institution failed, and when does that end it?" rulebook — when repeated misconduct or chronic misalignment opens a consequence matter, what the forum must find before it orders anything, how the institution is heard and can put things right, and how dissolution works as the last step, with rights, records, and services protected throughout. **CI-11.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*) holds the steps short of dissolution and how every order is reviewed and enforced. Institutions do not get infinite chances to stall reform — and they do not get to be the sole keeper of the official consequence record when they are the subject of review.*

**When review is mandatory**
- **Dissolution review** — institutions with repeated, verified anti-constitutional misconduct must enter mandatory dissolution review.
- **Alignment-supervision review** — institutions that operate, supervise, hold a [System Charter](../core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-charter) over, license, or materially control a chronically misaligned system enter review when an Integrity forum issues a final alignment ruling, withdraws recognition, imposes remediation conditions, or identifies sustained material misalignment under **Chapter Thirteen**.

*In plain terms: breaking constitutional obligations is not something an institution can paper over forever. Lighter steps come first when they can work. But if an institution resists fixing the problem, hides failures, or keeps harming sentients after a fair chance to put things right, the response escalates, and services, records, and the right to challenge stay protected along the way. An institution under review also cannot be the only place where the facts about it are written down.*

*Shared rules live elsewhere.* Apply **CJS-5.125 §15** (*Accountability: structural review, correction urgency, and disclosure terms*), **Realignment resistance and dissolution-review trigger minimum**, for the shared rules on repeated anti-constitutional misconduct, chronic misalignment, realignment resistance, escalation, graduated sanctions, continuity safeguards, and the order of dissolution or disbandment. Routing of integrity triggers and handling of verified misconduct are read with **CJS-5.110 §6** (*integrity assurance and resilience operations*). How the official Integrity forum record is kept is in **CF-13.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*). **CI-11.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) states institutional cooperation, implementation, and publication duties against a forum-held official record. The steps short of dissolution, emergency containment, Systems, review, and enforcement are in **CI-11.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*).

**Record-custody boundary**

The authoritative consequence record for dissolution review, alignment-supervision review, graduated-sanction sequencing, and realignment-resistance findings is an **Integrity forum lead record** under **CF-13.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*), not an institution-only file the subject institution may control, edit unilaterally, delay, or treat as the sole official version.

Where capture, self-review, record-custody dependency, or subject-forum conflict is material, custody must sit with one of these:

- an independent Integrity forum;
- backup forum authority under **CF-13.455** (*Fallback operation*); or
- another lawful non-subject custodian named on the record.

**What each institution must name and maintain locally**

These are cooperation and implementation duties only. Each institution must have:

- a designated **consequence liaison**, who is a contact and not the sole owner of the record or the final judge of the trigger facts;
- a duty to **open or join** dissolution review or alignment-supervision review within the **intake target** of the matching tier ([CF-13.445 §3.1](../corpus_forum/cf_13.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-13445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*)) when a lawful trigger fires, including when the matter is routed from **CI-8.210** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*), **CF-13.445** (*Forum performance, backlog requirements, publication timeliness, and accessibility*), or a **Chapter Thirteen** Integrity forum ruling;
- a duty to **submit** required materials, resolution reports, sanction responses, and continuity proposals to the lead Integrity forum by the published deadlines;
- a duty to **mirror publication** of the official record the forum holds, without leaving things out, delaying, or rewriting;
- a **continuity-transfer plan**, proposed for the forum to review, adopt, or amend before any receivership, supervised operation, or successor handoff goes ahead; and
- a published **personal-accountability boundary** saying that dissolving or disbanding an institution does not replace a designation under **Chapter Twelve**, or ordinary constitutional accountability for the sentients responsible.

**What the Integrity forum lead record must name**

The record kept under **CF-13.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*) must include at least:

- the order that opened dissolution review or alignment-supervision review, and the facts that triggered it;
- the verification status, the chances given to resolve the problem, and whether realignment resistance is found;
- any obstruction, concealment, retaliation, delay, or structural workaround found after a reasonable chance to resolve the problem;
- each sanction, authority restriction, supervised-operation step, or dissolution step that was ordered, attempted, or rejected, and whether it was completed;
- binding sequencing, stays, milestones, and coordination orders;
- for each rung ordered under **CI-11.215 §1** (*Correction and constrained authority*) through **CI-11.215 §5** (*Systems under consequence orders*), and for dissolution under **CI-11.210 §4** (*Dissolution*): the findings under **CI-11.210 §2** (*Findings before any order*), the order terms, the receiver, steward, or liquidator named, the verification result, the term and review dates, the watch period, and any defiance finding under **CI-11.215 §6** (*Review, fairness, and enforcement*);
- the approved continuity-transfer contacts, record custody, and service handoff milestones;
- referrals to **CI-8.210** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*), **CI-13.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*), **CI-6.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*), **CI-6.275** (*Transitional governance and institutional evolution*), **CF-13.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*), or constitutional forums where merits, enforcement, or structural remedy are involved; and
- notice and publication enough for affected stakeholders to understand the status and how to challenge it.

If verified realignment resistance meets the **Chapter Twelve** criteria, including material rollback effect, causally significant blocked correction, **Sustained High-Gravity Pattern**, or operative nullification, designation stays under **Chapter Twelve** and its safeguards.

## CI-11.210 §1: Opening a consequence matter

*In plain terms: this procedure opens only on a verified finding. A late filing, a failed first check, or a mismatch is a defect to fix, not a reason to start a consequence matter. When a matter does open, the lead Integrity forum works up a ladder of measures, using the lightest one that protects others, and the last rung is rarely reached.*

This section, **CI-11.210 §2** (*Findings before any order*), **CI-11.210 §3** (*Notice, response, and resolution*), **CI-11.210 §4** (*Dissolution*), and **CI-11.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*) state the procedure that **CJS-5.125 §15** (*structural review, correction urgency, and disclosure terms*) and [Chapter Nine Part D §13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](../core_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification.md#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) leave to these two files. Nothing in them is a licence or a gate. They are consequences of verified findings, aimed at the activity that puts others at risk, and each is time-bounded and reviewable under **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*).

The lead Integrity forum opens a consequence matter against a Chartered Party, or against a Class P Operating Party that has published no System Charter or Institutional Charter, when any of these is verified on the record. In **CI-11.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) and **CI-11.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*), "Chartered Party" reads to include that Class P Operating Party, which is a respondent on the same terms, with these limits. It is reached only on a ground in items 1 to 5 that carries a verified finding that its activity actually crossed a line in [Domain-Specific Materiality](../core_05.050_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) (for example a misclassification or a boundary game under **CI-11.215 §2** (*Contraction order*)), or on **Chapter Twelve** conduct under item 3, and **CI-11.210 §2** (*Findings before any order*) item 2 cannot be waived for it. Rung 6 and the Dissolution Reformation Lock do not reach an Operating Party that is a single sentient or a group of sentients acting together. For those, the individual locks and **Chapter Twelve** apply.

1. **Realignment resistance** under **CJS-5.125 §15** (*Accountability: structural review, correction urgency, and disclosure terms*): obstruction, concealment, retaliation, strategic delay, evidence destruction, metric gaming, dependency lock-in, or structural workaround that persists after a reasonable chance to resolve it.
2. **Chronic misalignment:** an alignment ruling, withdrawn recognition, a denial or revocation under [Chapter Nine Part D §13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](../core_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification.md#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution), or sustained material misalignment under **Chapter Thirteen**, left unresolved inside the resolution window.
3. **Repeated verified anti-constitutional misconduct** attributable to the Chartered Party, or a final **Chapter Twelve** designation that implicates the Chartered Party.
4. **A referral** under [Chapter Nine Part D §13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](../core_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification.md#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution), including a failed formation check not resolved inside the resolution window in [Chapter Seven §6 When a Holder Fails the Formation Check](../core_07.000_charters.md#6-when-a-holder-fails-the-formation-check).
5. **A forum performance trigger** under **CF-13.445** (*Forum performance, backlog requirements, publication timeliness, and accessibility*). A forum whose dissolution or disbandment threshold under **CF-13.445 §4** (*Performance thresholds and alerts*) is crossed follows that threshold, with these gates. The figure that crossed the threshold is first verified by a body outside that forum and outside the family that keeps it. Ordinary forum authority is then suspended at once, the suspended forum is heard by the lead Integrity forum inside the **prompt-review window**, and the lead Integrity forum names the forum that takes its live matters, Rights-Floor matters first. The dissolution or disbandment takes effect on the two keys in **CI-11.210 §4** (*Dissolution*), the lead Integrity forum's decision and the confirmation of a second independent forum, each due inside the Tier C outer bound of [Chapter Thirteen §6 Timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline](../core_13.000_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline). If the second key has not confirmed by then, there is no dissolution and the suspension lifts, unless the lead forum orders a lighter measure. A forum does not decide, or supply the only figure for, its own dissolution. The findings in **CI-11.210 §2** (*Findings before any order*) and the test in **CI-11.210 §4** (*Dissolution*) *When available* apply to a forum as to any other Chartered Party. The continuity, remedy, records, and accounting requirements of **CI-11.210 §4** (*Dissolution*) apply to that wind-down, and so does the bar on return in item 10 of that section: the wind-down order sets the Dissolution Reformation Lock, which is restorable unless the order records a permanence ground under [Chapter Eleven §8 Restoration and reassessment](../core_11.000_standing_integration.md#8-restoration-and-reassessment). The resolution window does not apply.
6. **Operating at Class L or higher in a way that harms or endangers others:** a correction, System Charter, Institutional Charter, or certification duty refused after notice and the resolution window. The refusal alone is a defect and supports only rung 1. It supports a higher rung only together with a risk finding under **CI-11.210 §2** (*Findings before any order*) item 2.

An allegation, a reputation, a label, a belief, or an [Interim Hold](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) is not a ground. Nothing here changes the Interim Hold, which stays directed at a named sentient and never reaches the existence or operation of a whole Chartered Party. The Chartered Party's consequence liaison is told at opening and cooperates under the duties above. Affected stakeholders get notice and the challenge pathways in the published record.


## CI-11.210 §2: Findings before any order

*In plain terms: before the forum orders anything, it writes down what is wrong, who is at risk, who caused it, and why a lighter step would not be enough. The side asking for a heavier step carries that burden.*

Before ordering any rung of the ladder, the lead forum records, with reasons:

1. **Ground:** which trigger in **CI-11.210 §1** (*Opening a consequence matter*) is verified, to the evidence tier the rung requires. Higher rungs require higher tiers under [Chapter Four](../core_04.000_burden_traceability_verification.md#chapter-four-burden-of-proof-traceability-and-verification) (*Burden of Proof, Traceability, and Verification*).
2. **Risk finding:** which of the nine areas in [Domain-Specific Materiality](../core_05.050_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) the Chartered Party's activity crosses, what specifically puts others at risk, who is exposed, and whether the harm can be undone. The lines sit lower for the vulnerable and for harm that cannot be undone.
3. **Cause:** whether the cause lies in the Chartered Party, a holder, an office, or a System it runs, so that orders land on the actor responsible ([Chapter Nine Part D §13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](../core_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification.md#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution)).
4. **Lesser measures:** what was tried at lower rungs and why a lighter rung would not protect others. The party seeking the rung carries this burden at each rung and for each joint requirement of **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*).
5. **Dependents:** who relies on the Chartered Party for survival, custody, income, information, or exit, and what protects them while the order runs.
6. **Protection first:** what interim steps keep affected sentients safe while the matter runs.


## CI-11.210 §3: Notice, response, and resolution

*In plain terms: the Chartered Party is told what is wrong and what a resolution looks like, gets a short window to answer and a longer one to fix it, both set by how critical it is, and a verified resolution ends the matter.*

The Chartered Party is told what is wrong, answers, and gets a bounded window to resolve it, on these terms:

1. **Notice** states the ground, the risk finding, the proposed rung, the evidence relied on, the route to respond, and what a resolution would look like.
2. **Response:** the Chartered Party has the response window to answer, contest the facts, or propose a resolution plan. The response window is the intake target of the Tier that matches the Chartered Party's class (Class A, B, C, L, or P takes Tier A, B, C, L, or P), counted from notice, and it is the same window as in [Chapter Seven §6 When a Holder Fails the Formation Check](../core_07.000_charters.md#6-when-a-holder-fails-the-formation-check).
3. **Resolution window:** the outer bound of the matching tier, counted from notice, with one good-faith extension of up to the length of the resolution window only on a showing of good-faith progress and no continuing harm. A sentient outside the Chartered Party's control line verifies the resolution.
4. **No credit for stalling:** time spent in obstruction extends no window. A pattern of late, partial, or cosmetic responses is itself a fact under **CI-11.210 §1** (*Opening a consequence matter*) item 1.
5. **Resolution ends the matter:** a verified resolution lifts the rung. The record stays open for a 12-month watch period only to see whether the resolution holds. The watch is not a penalty.
6. **Emergency skip:** where imminent irreversible harm is shown (Tier A under [Chapter Thirteen §6 Timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline](../core_13.000_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)), the forum may order emergency containment under **CI-11.215 §4** (*Emergency containment*) without waiting for the resolution window. The window then runs alongside the containment.
7. **Windows:** the forum decides each rung inside the tier outer bound for the matter in [Chapter Thirteen §6 Timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline](../core_13.000_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline). Resolution windows do not count against the outer bound, but they are themselves bounded as above. An adopting document may publish different figures with its reasons and may not exceed the tier outer bounds.


## CI-11.210 §4: Dissolution

*In plain terms: dissolution is for a Chartered Party that has had its chances, where nothing short of winding it down protects others, and where the wind-down can be done without leaving those who relied on it with nothing. The dissolved Chartered Party may not come back under any name or form while the lock on it stands, and for good where the harm is pervasive or cannot be undone, though the sentients in it stay free to start something different.*

**When available:** Only where the record shows that rungs 1 to 5 of the ladder in **CI-11.215 §1** (*Correction and constrained authority*) were tried and failed, or would plainly fail or cannot be carried out safely, and that the Chartered Party's continued existence is what keeps the risk alive, for example defiance of a contraction order or a structure that exists to evade. The forum names why contraction or separation would not protect others. The proposal for rung 5 or 6 comes from a body other than the deciding forum, such as the independent investigative service under **CF-13.435 §6** (*No self-investigation*) or another referring body, and the order records the lighter rung that was considered and rejected, with reasons.

**Two keys:** Dissolution needs the lead Integrity forum's decision and the concurrence of a second independent forum. The second key sits outside the lead forum's family, control line, funding, and docket benefit, and its independence is checked and published before it concurs. Both keys are due inside the Tier C outer bound; if the second has not concurred by then, there is no dissolution. Dissolution is certified under [Chapter Thirteen §5 Escalation and certification](../core_13.000_forum.md#5-escalation-and-certification) where a constitutional question is raised.

**Order contents:** A dissolution order:

1. sets an **effective date** after the wind-down plan is approved and the continuity transfer is complete for each dependent service;
2. appoints an independent **liquidator** who reports to the lead forum;
3. requires **continuity transfer first:** each service, record, and challenge pathway goes to a named receiver before the Chartered Party stops providing it, and records stay in non-subject custody for as long as any challenge pathway can still be used;
4. sets the **order of payment**, and nothing goes to owners or insiders until the last step:
   - remedy to those harmed (restitution, remediation, and compensation, [Chapter Twelve §4.1 Remedy and correction (anti-constitutional)](../core_12.005_misconduct_designation.md#41-remedy-and-correction-anti-constitutional));
   - wages and other pay owed to workers;
   - the costs of the wind-down, met under the ladder in **CI-11.215 §6** (*Review, fairness, and enforcement*) *Enforcement* item 7;
   - non-culpable creditors, dependents, and good-faith third parties, with the protections in [Article XXVIII-D](../core_06.025_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-d-non-compliant-property-and-systems-voluntary-turnover-incentives) (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*);
   - owners and members who were not at fault, in proportion to their stake; and
   - a share that would go to an owner or insider found at fault, or to a vehicle caught by the lock in item 10, goes instead to any unpaid remedy and then to the remedy system or receiver the forum names (**CI-11.205** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*)). Fault is found separately for each owner or insider named, on the standard of **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*), with notice and a hearing. An *insider* is an officer, a controlling holder, or a related party of the Chartered Party. An ordinary worker's wage claim is never excluded;
5. keeps **destructive disposition** to the higher bar in [Article XXVIII-D](../core_06.025_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-d-non-compliant-property-and-systems-voluntary-turnover-incentives) (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*): quarantine, hold, or transfer is the default, and taking something for good or destroying it needs stronger reasons and independent review;
6. bars **acquisitive enforcement:** no enforcer, beneficiary of the order, or competitor may take assets, staff, or users from the wind-down on better terms than any other buyer, and proceeds do not reward whoever sought the order;
7. preserves **the sentients inside the Chartered Party:** members, workers, and dependents keep their rights, wages owed, and essentials, and may re-associate. Dissolving a Chartered Party bars no sentient from forming a different one that is not one operation with it. The narrow gates in [Article XI-E](../core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) reach a named sentient's authority on that sentient's own lock, and reach the dissolved Chartered Party through the lock in item 10;
8. requires a **final accounting** to the lead forum, published on the **CF-13.445** (*Forum performance, backlog requirements, publication timeliness, and accessibility*) schedule;
9. restates the **personal-accountability boundary:** dissolution does not replace **Chapter Twelve** designation or ordinary accountability for responsible sentients, and it does not count as repair ([Chapter Twelve §4.1 Remedy and correction (anti-constitutional)](../core_12.005_misconduct_designation.md#41-remedy-and-correction-anti-constitutional), non-substitution rule); and
10. sets a **bar on return:** the order carries a Dissolution Reformation Lock under [Chapter Eleven §5.5 Special locks](../core_11.000_standing_integration.md#55-special-locks) on the dissolved Chartered Party, its successors, controlled entities, nominee- or shell-held vehicles, and any Operating Party that is one operation with them. None may be re-formed, revived, or run under any name or form while it stands. The order records whether it rests on pervasive anti-constitutional conduct, on a final Chapter Twelve designation, or on unremedied Irreversible Harm, and the permanence ground is confirmed at the final accounting ([Chapter Eleven §8 Restoration and reassessment](../core_11.000_standing_integration.md#8-restoration-and-reassessment)). If it does, the lock is permanent: time, a resolved cause, or a change of holders does not lift it, and it ends only if the order is set aside on review under **CI-11.215 §6** (*Review, fairness, and enforcement*) or material new verified facts show that its finding was wrong. If it does not, the lock ends on an independent finding that the cause is resolved, the remedy is delivered, and a return is safer than continued exclusion. It does not reach the receiver, liquidator, or successor that the order names to carry services forward, a good-faith purchaser, a successor created by a rung 5 order, or a workers' cooperative formed by the workers, and it bars no sentient from forming a different Operating Party. It does not reach a single sentient or a group of sentients acting together as such. The party that says a vehicle is caught carries the burden, on verified facts, and the vehicle may keep operating while the forum decides unless the forum finds a specific risk.

**Contest and stay:** The Chartered Party and affected stakeholders may contest. A contest does not stop rungs 1 to 5, but an order under contest is not a final order for **CI-11.215 §6** (*Review, fairness, and enforcement*) *Enforcement* item 6. It stays the effective date of a dissolution until the review is decided inside the tier outer bound, unless an emergency measure under **CI-11.215 §4** (*Emergency containment*) is in force.

**Systems:** For a System, the dissolution rung is retirement or decommissioning, on the terms in **CI-11.215 §5** (*Systems under consequence orders*), which also sets the bar on return for a retired System.
