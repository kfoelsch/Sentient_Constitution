Core home: 22

This file is the institutional implementation home for **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*).

*In plain terms: **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) is the institutions layer's overlap-and-deadlock rulebook — what happens when more than one body could handle the same matter, who coordinates handoffs, where to go when they disagree or stall, and how to reach an independent backup when the usual forum or investigator cannot act fairly. Shared coordination minimums live in **CJS-3.110 §6** (*integrity assurance and resilience operations*); forum routing and transfer detail lives in **CF-22.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*) and **CF-22.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*); independent investigative backup lives in **CF-22.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*). What this file adds is local: what each institution must publish and maintain locally.*

**Purpose and role:** This file sets the institutional publication, record, and escalation duties for coordination where responsibilities overlap.

**Quick orientation:**

- **When this applies** — two or more institutions share jurisdiction, evidence custody, review duty, enforcement support, investigative interface, or forum-adjacent routing on the same matter.
- **What the shared rules already cover** — conflict-order handling, a deadlock-breaker path, evidence-sharing boundaries, who is accountable for what, triggers for outside review when internal deadlock persists, independent investigative backup under **CF-22.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*), and cross-forum anti-self-judging backup under **Chapter Twenty-Two** (**CJS-3.110 §6** (*integrity assurance and resilience operations*)).
- **What each institution must publish** — who owns the coordination protocol; a role map; the official records that prove transfers and escalations happened lawfully; deadlines; escalation triggers; and how the public can see the plan.
- **Where sentients are routed when local review fails** — **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) is the named escalation route when an institution is conflicted, captured, unavailable, or stonewalling — including when it cannot credibly commission outside assurance under **CI-6.215 §2** (*External Assurance Triggers*).

*In plain terms: overlapping authority is not an excuse to improvise after a fight starts. Each affected institution must publish a usable coordination and escalation plan before conflict arises — with real owners, real deadlines, written transfer records, and backup routes that still work when independence is in doubt.*

*Shared rules live elsewhere.* Cross-institution coordination terms are in **CJS-3.110 §6** (*integrity assurance and resilience operations*), especially **Cross-institution coordination and escalation chain**. **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) keeps only what each institution must name and maintain locally.

Apply **CJS-3.110 §6** (*Oversight: Integrity Assurance and Resilience Operations*) **Cross-institution coordination and escalation chain** for the shared floor.

**What each institution must publish:**

- who owns the local coordination and escalation protocol;
- a role map showing which institution or office handles intake, evidence custody, review, enforcement support, investigative interface, transfer, and backup routing for overlapping matters;
- the official records required to review transfer, recusal, unavailability, backup designation, and lawful-panel formation;
- published deadlines for coordination steps and escalation;
- published escalation triggers — including when persistent internal deadlock, capture indicators, emergency incapacity, or independence failure requires outside review, **CF-22.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*) backup, or **Chapter Twenty-Two** backup routing; and
- cooperation duties with the independent investigative service interface under **CF-22.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*) and with forum backup-routing rules under **Chapter Twenty-Two** where materially applicable.

**What is not allowed:** moved on 2026-11-20 to [Subcore 22-A §8 Limits on Cross-Institution Coordination](../subcore_22.010_forum_intake_default_venue.md#8-limits-on-cross-institution-coordination), part of the Sentient Constitution, because the four limits are the same for every adopter.

**External-order boundary**

External-order interactions must remain consistent with constitutional change and supremacy rules in [Chapter Twenty-Seven](../core_27.000_non_regression.md), [Chapter Twenty-Eight](../core_28.000_amendment_ratification.md), and [Chapter Twenty-Nine](../core_29.000_expansion_supremacy.md).
