Core home: 06

This file is the institutional implementation home for **CI-6.260** (*Place-based stewardship, Indigenous continuity, and consultation routes*).

*In plain terms: **CI-6.260** (*Place-based stewardship, Indigenous continuity, and consultation routes*) is the local owner map for decisions that touch land, ecosystems, and communities with durable place-based connection — especially Indigenous continuity of language, cultural practice, governance, and knowledge transmission. Shared floors live in **CJS-5.120 §7** (*Participation: quorum and participatory legitimacy terms*); this file names who keeps consultation records, who routes indigenous-continuity impacts, and who escalates compliance theater.*

**Quick orientation**
- **The basic idea** — place-based stewardship decisions must identify affected communities, publish consultation pathways, and keep records that support accountable reconstruction — not symbolic listening sessions with no effect.
- **Indigenous continuity** — where community continuity of language, culture, governance practice, or knowledge transmission is materially implicated, institutions must route the response under:
  - **Chapter Five** *Indigenous Continuity*;
  - the owner floors in **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*) and **Article I-A** (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*).
- **Consultation records** — who was consulted, what was said, what changed, and what did not must be custodied and auditable.
- **Compliance theater** — funded projects that box-check consultation without meaningful risk response must escalate through **CI-8.215 §2** (*External assurance triggers*) and **CI-13.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*).
- **What this is not** — no unbounded territorial-restitution mandate; no constitutional-layer adjudication of historical territorial claims; adoption-scope and adopter-jurisdiction discipline stay in **Chapter Eighteen**.
- **Hard limits** — symbolic consultation cannot be presented as binding governance; binding governance cannot be hidden as optional consultation; participation records must support later challenge.
- **Read with** — **CI-8.215 §2** (*External assurance triggers*) and **CI-13.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) for external assurance and cross-institution escalation.

**Apply.** Apply **CJS-5.120 §7** (*Participation: quorum and participatory legitimacy terms*) **Stakeholder oversight and binding-governance pathway integrity**. **CI-6.260** (*Place-based stewardship, Indigenous continuity, and consultation routes*) states only the local owner duties below — without creating an unbounded territorial-restitution mandate or reopening adoption-scope choices reserved to **Chapter Eighteen**.

**Local place-based stewardship and consultation role map**
Each institution in scope must name the office or body responsible for:
- **consultation-record custody** — who receives, stores, versions, and publishes consultation notices, submissions, responses, and decision rationales so participation can be reconstructed and challenged later;
- **stakeholder and affected-community identification** — who determines which place-linked, Indigenous, tenure-holding, ecosystem-dependent, or comparable communities are materially affected, and so must receive notice and practical participation routes under **CJS-5.120 §7** (*quorum and participatory legitimacy terms*);
- **indigenous-continuity response** — who evaluates and routes decisions that materially implicate **Chapter Five** *Indigenous Continuity*, including impacts on language, cultural practice, governance practice, and knowledge transmission, under **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*), **Article I-A** (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*), and **Chapter Eighteen** discipline where territorial-continuity questions arise;
- **advisory-versus-binding pathway publication** — who publishes whether a consultation route is advisory, binding, or relied on for governance effect, and what challenge or correction paths follow; and
- **compliance-theater escalation** — who escalates funded projects or supervised programs where consultation occurs without meaningful risk response to **CI-8.215 §2** (*External assurance triggers*) external-assurance triggers and **CI-13.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) cross-institution escalation.
Read **Chapter Five** *Indigenous Continuity* alongside these articles where materially applicable:
- **Article I-A** (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*);
- **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*);
- **Article XXII-C** (*Non-Statelessness*).
