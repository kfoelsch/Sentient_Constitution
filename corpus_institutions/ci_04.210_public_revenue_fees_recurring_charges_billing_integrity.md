Core home: 04

This file is the institutional implementation home for **CI-4.210** (*Public revenue, fees, recurring charges, and billing integrity*).

*In plain terms: **CI-4.210** (*Public revenue, fees, recurring charges, and billing integrity*) is the institutions layer's money-and-access rulebook — how public revenue, user fees, subscriptions, and recurring charges must stay fair, transparent, and aligned with how essential the underlying system is. Institutions must not price sentients out of survival-critical access, trick them into paid renewals, or trap them in billing cycles they cannot exit. Shared fiscal and charge-exit minimums live in **CJS-3.125 §12** (*burden-of-justification and constraint terms*) and **CJS-3.130 §17** (*interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*); digital pathway detail lives in **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*). What this file adds is local: what each institution must publish and maintain locally.*

**Purpose and role:** This file sets institutional fiscal-map, charge-supervision, and reporting duties for revenue and billing pathways within its stated scope.

**Quick orientation:**

- **What this file covers** — class-aligned fiscal orientation and supervised recurring-charge duties for institutions that set, supervise, or authorize fee and billing rules.
- **What this file does not cover** — tax bases, rates, credits, enforcement mechanics, and cross-border tax law. Those remain governing law outside **CI-4.210** (*Public revenue, fees, recurring charges, and billing integrity*).
- **CI-4.210 §1** — public revenue and user fees: charges must align with system class and must not make essential access unaffordable or administratively unreachable.
- **CI-4.210 §2** — subscriptions, renewals, trials, and commercial billing: honest disclosure before commitment, usable cancel/downgrade paths, and supervision maps for recurring charges.
- **Read with** — **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*) and **CI-8.210 §4** (*Survival floors, voluntary exchange, and markets (Article III-A (Survival) interface)*) for classification and essential-access interfaces; **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*) where digital self-service billing pathways apply.

*In plain terms: if money touches access, the charge design must match the system's real importance — and sentients must be able to see what they owe, challenge unfair fees, and leave recurring charges through a published path that actually works.*

*Shared rules live elsewhere.* Class-aligned revenue and access-burden terms are in **CJS-3.125 §12** (*burden of justification and constraint terms*). Commitment, renewal, and charge-exit terms are in **CJS-3.130 §17** (*interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*). **CI-4.210** (*Public revenue, fees, recurring charges, and billing integrity*) keeps only institutional fiscal-map, supervision, and reporting duties.

## CI-4.210 §1: Public Revenue, User Fees, and Class-Aligned Burden
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CI-4.210 §2** (*Recurring Charges, Renewals, and Commercial Billing Integrity*); **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: fees and public charges must fit the system's risk class — survival-critical and normal-operation-critical access cannot be treated as the main place to extract money. Commercial or premium use may bear more cost; charges must not make protected baseline access practically unavailable.*

Apply **CJS-3.125 §12** (*Accountability: Burden-of-Justification and Constraint Terms*) **Class-aligned revenue and access-burden minimum** for the shared floor. **CI-4.210 §1** (*Public Revenue, User Fees, and Class-Aligned Burden*) keeps only what each institution must name and maintain locally:

- who owns the **fiscal map** linking revenue and charge choices to published class and stewardship-level assignments;
- who owns **constrained-capacity priority** rules when limited public capacity must be allocated fairly;
- the **CS-4.335** (*Resource allocation and funding stewardship*) reporting channel for resource-allocation and funding-stewardship alignment; and
- named owners for **CI-4.210 §2** (*Recurring Charges, Renewals, and Commercial Billing Integrity*) and **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*) where the institution supervises recurring charges or digital billing pathways.

## CI-4.210 §2: Recurring Charges, Renewals, and Commercial Billing Integrity
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with:
  - **CI-4.210 §1** (*Public Revenue, User Fees, and Class-Aligned Burden*);
  - **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*);
  - **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: subscriptions, memberships, trials, and auto-renewals must be honest before you sign up — clear price, clear timing, clear renewal rules — and cancellation or downgrade must work through a published path without dark patterns or surprise charges after exit.*

Apply **CJS-3.130 §17** (*Continuity: Interoperability, Portability, and Exit-Integrity Terms*) **Commitment, renewal, and charge-exit integrity** for the shared floor. **CI-4.210 §2** (*Recurring Charges, Renewals, and Commercial Billing Integrity*) keeps only what each institution must name and maintain locally:

- who owns the **charge-supervision map** for recurring and transaction-linked charges the institution supervises, authorizes, or sets compliance expectations for;
- a **stricter-law check** duty where governing law imposes stronger billing or consumer-protection requirements;
- **reclassification triggers** when essentiality or dependency changes and charge treatment must be reviewed with **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*) classification; and
- linkage to the **CI-4.210 §1** (*Public Revenue, User Fees, and Class-Aligned Burden*) fiscal interface where public revenue and supervised charges intersect.

Each supervised charge type must appear on the local map with:

- charge type and supervised scope;
- class and stewardship-level assignment under **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*);
- the accountable office;
- the evidence artifact showing compliance; and
- the billing and exit complaint route — including coordination with **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*) where digital self-service pathways apply.
