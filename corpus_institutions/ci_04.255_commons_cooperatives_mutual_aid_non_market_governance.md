Core home: 04

This file is the institutional implementation home for **CI-4.255** (*Commons, cooperatives, mutual aid, and non-market community governance*).

*In plain terms: commons, cooperatives, and mutual-aid groups need ways to manage shared resources that do not force them into a single institutional form. Shared floors live in **CJS-3.120 §7** (*Participation: Quorum and Participatory Legitimacy Terms*). This file names who recognizes records, maps fiscal rules, and routes independent review.*

## CI-4.255 §1: Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file covers the institutional interfaces that let commons and mutual-aid groups operate and seek review.*

This file assigns institutional ownership for commons and mutual-aid record recognition, contribution measures, fiscal and land-use interfaces, continuity, and independent review.

**Quick orientation:**

- **The basic idea** — commons and mutual-aid pathways must stay legible enough for funding, continuity, audit, fraud control, and participation review without forcing them into only state, corporate, or adversarial-forum forms.
- **Records and recognition** — stewardship rotation, mutual obligation, conflict handling, and resource-allocation records must count as valid inputs to institutional decisions where law permits.
- **Non-market reciprocity** — fiscal, tax, zoning, funding, and eligibility rules must be mapped so lawful solidarity is not accidentally disabled or criminalized.
- **Seats in common** — a local cooperative or association of small businesses may host the verify-or-authorize, record, or contest seats of its members under [Chapter Six §7 Proportional Scaling and Merged Hosting](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#7-proportional-scaling-and-merged-hosting), with one published map, an independent route outside the group, and no required membership.
- **Anti-romanticization** — informal community process must include independent review when exclusion, shunning, or local hierarchy harms sentients; read **CI-4.245** (*Relational coercive control, intimate power, and anti-domination routing*) and **CI-4.250** (*Community life, voluntary association, and non-instrumental time*) where coercive-control patterns are implicated.
- **Institutional interfaces** — due process, contestability, anti-capture, funding, zoning, tax, and continuity duties route through **CI-22.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*), **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*), and **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*).
- **Hard limits** — informality cannot hide exclusion, coercion, fraud, capture, or appeal denial; romanticized community process cannot block remedy for sentients harmed by informal power.
- **Read with** — **CI-22.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*), **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*), **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*), **CI-4.235** (*Collective public health, epidemic response, and addiction-informed care*), and **CI-4.250** (*Community life, voluntary association, and non-instrumental time*).

## CI-4.255 §2: Why Commons and Mutual-Aid Pathways Matter

*In plain terms: sentients need usable ways to organize and care for shared resources without being forced into one institutional form.*

Commons, cooperatives, and mutual-aid groups can support shared resources and community needs through different forms of organization. Clear institutional interfaces and independent review help those forms of organization remain usable while addressing exclusion, coercion, or capture.

**Local commons and mutual-aid application role map**

Each institution in scope must name the office or body responsible for:

- **commons-governance records recognition** — who accepts legible records of stewardship rotation, mutual obligation, conflict handling, and resource allocation as valid inputs to funding, zoning, tax, and continuity decisions where law permits, subject to local audit and fraud controls. Where that acceptance opens or corrects a [Chapter Fourteen](../core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fourteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) contribution Standing Record, this office, if it is an independent body with published authority to rely on the Standing Record, is the **record-opening authority** under [Chapter Fourteen §7.9](../core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#79-informal-and-small-scope-records) (*Informal and Small-Scope Records*) and [Chapter Six §3](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) (*Separation of Duties*): it is independent of the work, it verifies, and the commons steward who kept the rotation or obligation log is the recorder and a witness, never the verifying authority; co-participants in a collective record are claimants under [Chapter Fourteen §6.3](../core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#63-collective-and-actor-specific-records) (*Collective and Actor-Specific Records*). Formal-institution verification is not required where this office can verify;
- **standard contribution measures** — who publishes, and on what cadence revisits, any [Chapter Fifteen §9 Standard Contribution Measures](../core_15.000_standing_measurement.md#9-standard-contribution-measures) schedule of default contribution measures for recurring commons tasks (litter pickups, waterway-restoration work units, land-recovery plots, stewardship rotations), with the assumptions, evidence method, and contest path each standard rests on. This is the direction-and-policy seat under Chapter Fourteen §7.1 *Separation of duties*: it is not the recognition authority that verifies instances, and not a group the schedule measures. Standards are floors for ordinary performance, not caps — verified above-standard work is measured on its actual impact, and work that falls short is measured on what was verified. Re-baselining as efficiency improves applies forward only. Method and a worked schedule: [`implementation/LEQU_CALIBRATION_REFERENCE.md` §6.4 Standard contribution measures (adopter-set schedules)](../implementation/LEQU_CALIBRATION_REFERENCE.md#64-standard-contribution-measures-adopter-set-schedules) (*process support*);
- **funding and fiscal-interface mapping** — who maps cooperative and mutual-aid infrastructure to **CS-4.335** (*Resource allocation and funding stewardship*) and **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*) so fiscal rules do not accidentally criminalize or disable qualifying solidarity mechanisms that satisfy **Necessity**, **Proportionality**, and dependency-integrity requirements;
- **zoning and land-use interface** — who supervises zoning, licensing, land-use, and comparable spatial rules so lawful commons, cooperative, and community-managed resource pathways remain practically usable;
- **tax and eligibility interface** — who maintains the official mapping between non-market reciprocity, participatory budgeting, and comparable solidarity forms and local tax, benefits, and eligibility treatment;
- **continuity and succession routing** — who preserves governance records, handoff authority, and service continuity when commons stewards rotate, cooperatives reorganize, or mutual-aid networks change form; where a Standing Record exists, this office is its **record custodian** under Chapter Fourteen §7 (*Record Custody and Opening Authority*), and enters and holds what the separate records-recognition authority verified; the two may be hosted by the same relying body under its published lane map and independence safeguards, but the custodian never verifies the same Standing Record or acts as a claimant; and
- **independent review and anti-capture escalation** — who routes appeals when exclusion, informal power, fraud, or capture inside a commons or mutual-aid pathway harms sentients, including referral to **CI-4.245** (*Relational coercive control, intimate power, and anti-domination routing*) and **CI-4.250** (*Community life, voluntary association, and non-instrumental time*) where associational or coercive-control patterns are implicated.

Read **CI-22.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*), **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), **CI-22.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*), **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*), **CI-4.235** (*Collective public health, epidemic response, and addiction-informed care*), and **CI-4.250** (*Community life, voluntary association, and non-instrumental time*) for local procedure, scaling, participation, stewardship, and escalation interfaces.
