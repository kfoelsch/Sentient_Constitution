Core home: 06

This file is the institutional implementation home for **CI-6.270** (*Scientific publication, peer review, replication, and evidence stewardship*).

*In plain terms: **CI-6.270** (*Scientific publication, peer review, replication, and evidence stewardship*) is the local owner map for publishing claims, peer review, replication, corrections, and keeping evidence trustworthy enough to rely on. Shared floors live in **CJS-5.110 §5** (*Oversight: independent verification and claim-integrity terms*); local duties sit in **CI-6.270 §1** (*Publication intake and correction records*) through **CI-6.270 §5** (*Ombuds, escalation, and technical forum referral*).*

**Quick orientation**
- **The basic idea** — material empirical or analytical claims must be publishable with enough method, provenance, uncertainty, and evidence detail for independent checking — not locked behind prestige gates or opaque editorial black boxes.
- **What institutions should rely on** — evidence quality, critique, replication, correction history, and long-run reliability — not journal brand, closed ranking, or impact-factor proxies.
- **What must stay easy** — good-faith correction, amendment, supersession, null results, and replication-oriented work must remain publishable and citable without prestige punishment.
- **What is out of bounds** — suppressing inconvenient results, retaliating against reviewers or replicators, or quietly manipulating the scientific record.
- **CI-6.270 §1** — publication intake and correction records: who receives claims, how versions and retractions are tracked, and what reliance status they carry.
- **CI-6.270 §2** — evidence, review, and reviewer custody: evidence packages, structured review, reviewer independence, and replication status.
- **CI-6.270 §3** — challenge, correction, and access: challenge lanes, correction history, verification access, and bundling justification.
- **CI-6.270 §4** — incentive criteria: rewards tied to evidence quality, not prestige proxies or hidden correction history.
- **CI-6.270 §5** — ombuds, escalation, and technical forum referral: dispute review, **CI-14.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) escalation, and **CF-14.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*) specialist routes.
- **Read with** — **CI-14.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*) for local procedure maps; **CI-14.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) for cross-institution escalation; **CF-14.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*) for technical specialist forum referral.

- [**CJS-5.110 §5**](../corpus_joint_structure/cjs_05.110_oversight_operations.md#operational-transparency-and-structured-logging-requirements-c) (*independent verification and claim-integrity terms*).
- **Article XV-D** (*Scientific Publication, Review, and Replication Integrity*).
- **Chapter One** (*Truth*, epistemic disclosure constraints); **Article XVII-A** (*Lifecycle Governance and Environment Separation*).
- **CF-14.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*) under **CJS-R09**.

**Apply.** Apply **CJS-5.110 §5** (*Oversight: independent verification and claim-integrity terms*) for the shared claim-testing, review, reliance, correction, and incentive-integrity minimums. **CI-6.270** (*Scientific publication, peer review, replication, and evidence stewardship*) states only the local owner duties in **CI-6.270 §1** (*Publication intake and correction records*) through **CI-6.270 §5** (*Ombuds, escalation, and technical forum referral*).

## CI-6.270 §1: Publication intake and correction records
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CI-6.270 §1** (*Publication intake and correction records*); **CI-6.270 §2** (*Evidence, review, and reviewer custody*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: every claim an institution might rely on needs a clear intake trail — what was submitted, what changed, what was withdrawn, and whether the institution still treats it as trustworthy.*

Each institution in scope must name and maintain locally:

- **publication intake owner** — who receives and tracks versions of incoming scholarly or empirical claims for institutional reliance;
- **versioned intake and correction record** — a searchable history of submissions, amendments, supersessions, retractions, and expressions of concern; and
- **reliance-status field** — whether the institution treats a claim as provisional, verified, contested, superseded, or withdrawn.

## CI-6.270 §2: Evidence, review, and reviewer custody
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CI-6.270 §2** (*Evidence, review, and reviewer custody*); **CI-6.270 §1** (*Publication intake and correction records*); **CI-6.270 §3** (*Challenge, correction, and access*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: a claim is only as good as the evidence behind it and the review that tested it — methods, data access, reviewer independence, and whether anyone tried to replicate it must be on the record.*

Each institution in scope must name and maintain locally:

- **evidence-package file** — methods, provenance, uncertainty statements, funding and conflict disclosures, data or lawful access limits, and materials needed for proportionate verification;
- **review file** — structured review dimensions separating method quality, data integrity, inferential validity, uncertainty, reproducibility, and fit between evidence and conclusion;
- **reviewer-custody record** — who reviewed what, under what independence and conflict controls, including signed, pseudonymous, confidential, or specialist review modes where used; and
- **replication and adversarial-review status** — whether independent replication or adversarial review was attempted or completed for high-impact, safety-relevant, policy-relevant, or dependency-relevant claims.

## CI-6.270 §3: Challenge, correction, and access
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CI-6.270 §3** (*Challenge, correction, and access*); **CI-6.270 §2** (*Evidence, review, and reviewer custody*); **CI-6.270 §4** (*Incentive criteria*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: sentients who try to replicate, reanalyze, or correct a claim need a real door in — and if publication, funding, and editorial power are bundled together, that bundling must be justified and auditable.*

Each institution in scope must name and maintain locally:

- **challenge-lane owner** — who receives good-faith replication attempts, reanalysis, methodological critique, and correction requests;
- **correction file** — auditable record of corrections, retractions, clarifications, and superseding claims, with reasons and dates;
- **access file** — how qualified independent reviewers obtain underlying materials or justified substitutes for verification, subject only to narrow lawful limits; and
- **bundling-justification record** — where publication, review, curation, funding, archival custody, reputation, or reliance functions are bundled, the justification and independent audit route for that bundling.

## CI-6.270 §4: Incentive criteria
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CI-6.270 §4** (*Incentive criteria*); **CI-6.270 §3** (*Challenge, correction, and access*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: promotions, grants, and hiring should reward honest evidence and visible correction — not hype, citation games, or burying mistakes.*

Each institution in scope must name and maintain locally:

- **incentive-criteria file** — how hiring, promotion, grantmaking, procurement, ranking, adoption, or standing rewards evidence quality rather than opaque prestige proxies, selective-publication bias, hype inflation, citation cartels, or concealment of correction history.

## CI-6.270 §5: Ombuds, escalation, and technical forum referral
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with:
  - **CI-6.270 §5** (*Ombuds, escalation, and technical forum referral*);
  - **CI-14.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*);
  - **CI-14.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*);
  - **CF-14.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: when publication disputes stall or the local reviewer is conflicted, there must be a named ombuds lane, an escalation path, and — where needed — a specialist technical forum that is not the same shop that benefits from the original claim.*

Each institution in scope must name and maintain locally:

- **ombuds or review-lane office** — a named body for publication, review, and replication disputes that is separated from revenue-dependent or prestige-dependent editorial control where material;
- **CI-14.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*) / **CI-14.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) **escalation route** — the published path when local review stalls, is captured, or cannot act independently; and
- **CF-14.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*) **technical-forum referral route** — where science-facing specialist technical review under **CJS-R09** is required or appropriate.

Read **CI-14.205** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*) and **CI-14.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) for local procedure maps and cross-institution escalation interfaces.
