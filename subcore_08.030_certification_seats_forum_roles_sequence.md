### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 8-C is the constitutional owner of the **detail of who does what in a system certification**: how the four seats are placed, the bounded role of the Technical, Sentient, Institutional, and Constitutional and other regular forum families, and the default order in which forums supervise a System Certification Record. [Subcore 8-D](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#subcore-8-d-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes) states the forum process, the Integrity lead, the guarantees, and the outcomes; where a line there and the text here seem to differ, the reading more protective of the affected sentient controls.

*In plain terms: Chapter Eight says no one certifies their own system and no single kind of forum runs the whole review. This file says exactly who sits in each seat, what each kind of forum contributes, and the order the steps run in.*

### 2. Why the Detail Is the Same for Every Adopter

*In plain terms: if adopters could arrange the seats and the steps their own way, the easiest shortcut would be to let the operator pick its own reviewer or skip the step that was going to say no.*

A certification is trusted only if the sentients who rely on it know that the operator did not choose who checked it, that each kind of stake was looked at by the forum able to judge it, and that nothing was authorized while a required finding was still missing. One placement of seats, one division of roles, and one default order, the same for every adopter and scaled only by system class, make each certification comparable with every other and make a skipped step visible on the System Certification Record.

**Grounded in:**

<a id="3-four-seats-in-a-certification"></a>
### 3. Four Seats in a Certification

This section is how system alignment certification applies the [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement). [Chapter Six §10 Relationship to Later Processes](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#10-relationship-to-later-processes) requires every later process to say how it assigns and uses all four seats, and this section is that statement for certification. A certification is a [Subcore 3-J — Materially Binding Act](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), so every certification pass has the four seats Chapter Six defines, under the names Chapter Six gives them. This section adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Six. Other rules in Chapter Nine and in this subcore file may be stricter, never weaker.

For the certification act, the seats are held as follows:

- **[Subcore 3-J — Initiating Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#initiating-seat):** the system operator, steward, vendor, or other proponent seeking certification. It requests, operates, or claims alignment.
- **[Subcore 3-J — Verify-or-Authorize Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#verify-or-authorize-seat):** the forum or bounded component authority that checks the evidence and authority against the governing standard and issues its finding, within that authority.
- **[Subcore 3-J — Record Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-seat):** the office that opens, enters, amends, preserves, and publishes the coordinated System Certification Record. That System Certification Record is the Act Record for the certification act (see [Subcore 8-F §3.1 Minimum Record Contents](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#31-minimum-record-contents)).
- **[Subcore 3-J — Contest Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contest-seat):** the published record challenge path, together with the competent review or backup forum, for the challenged act (see [Subcore 8-F §5.2 Contestability Paths](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths)).

**Where the seats are placed:**

- The system's [Subcore 3-K — Charter Role Map](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) places the initiating, record, and contest seats for a certification. For each, it lists the eligible offices, the stewards now authorized to hold them, and the substitute.
- The record seat and the contest seat it names sit outside the [Subcore 3-J — Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line) of the operator and proponent.
- The map does not place the verify-or-authorize seat. The lead forum and the component authorities are assigned under [Subcore 8-D §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence) and Chapter Twenty-Two, so the operator cannot choose its own verifying authority.
- The System Certification Record names the Charter Role Map version in force for each certification pass (see [Subcore 8-F §3.1 Minimum Record Contents](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#31-minimum-record-contents)).
- A seat the map leaves unplaced is a System Charter defect to log and correct, and the certification pass routes as [Chapter Six §6 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#6-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution) provides.

**Outside the operator.** Everyone who holds a verify-or-authorize seat in a certification must be outside the [Subcore 3-J — Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line) of the operator and the proponent, and must have no interest that the outcome decides. In particular:

- The operator, steward, vendor, or other proponent cannot choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove the forum or component authority that checks it.
- No one who works for it, is owned by it, or answers to it may hold the seat for its certification.
- A sentient who cannot meet this must step aside from that pass (recusal under Chapter Sixteen).
- A seat placement that fails this is not valid, whatever any Charter Role Map says ([Chapter Six §4 Independence, Conflict, and Control Lines](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#4-independence-conflict-and-control-lines)).

A forum family may hold different seats on different acts. It may not use its family label to merge seats prohibited by [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) on the same act.

**Lead forum and record holder:**

- The lead forum holds the verify-or-authorize seat for the outcome, and each forum or authority that gives a component finding holds it for that finding.
- The lead forum directs the sequence, but it does not hold the record seat on the same act.
- The record holder named in the Charter Role Map opens, amends, preserves, and publishes the System Certification Record. It enters what the deciding forums determined and does not decide anything itself.
- The "one coordinated record" duty in Chapter Sixteen is a duty of the lead forum to coordinate the System Certification Record, not to keep it.

<a id="4-component-roles-of-the-forum-families"></a>
### 4. Component Roles of the Forum Families

*In plain terms: what the Technical, Sentient, Institutional, and Constitutional Forums each contribute to a system certification. The Integrity lead and the Environment role are stated in Chapter Eight itself.*

Each family's role is bounded as [Subcore 8-D §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) states; the Integrity role is in [Subcore 8-D §3.1.3 Integrity Forums](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#313-integrity-forums) and the Environment role in [Subcore 8-D §3.1.4 Environment Forums](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#314-environment-forums).

<a id="41-technical-forums"></a>

#### 4.1 Technical Forums

**The technical piece:**

- Maintain and apply technical specifications, measurement methods, testing protocols, expert-evidence standards, methods for reducing uncertainty, and technical adequacy findings, within their lawful scope;
- Supply **component findings** for the System Certification Record. These include findings on system-class-scaled assurance and data-handling infrastructure under [Subcore 8-B §3 System Class Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-system-class-evaluation) and [Subcore 8-B §4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation);
- Answer the technical questions the lead forum sends them for ruling, for the System Certification Record, as [Chapter Seventeen §3.2 Technical Forum Domains](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#32-technical-forum-domains) allows;
- Supply component findings on health and safety information where the system supplies, lists, recommends, or advises on products or services that can affect body, health, or safety. [Subcore 8-B §6.6 Trustworthiness and System-Reliance Integrity Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#66-trustworthiness-and-system-reliance-integrity-evaluation) tests this under **Article III-F** (*Safe Products and Health Information*). The technical finding is whether warnings, health claims, nutrition facts, ingredients, and allergen information are accurate, backed by evidence, and kept current, and whether they say plainly what is unknown or untested; and
- Hold the [Subcore 3-N — Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) component role (indicator evaluation, expert evidence, and bounded uncertainty):
  - This applies where certification materially affects who counts as a **sentient** for rights, standing, or protection purposes, consistent with [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*).
  - Technical Forums are the default lead under [Chapter Seventeen §3.2 Technical Forum Domains](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#32-technical-forum-domains) and [Chapter Seventeen §4 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) (*Sentience-status adjudication* hook).
  - Where Chapter Sixteen so requires, they refer the matter to Integrity, Institutional, or Constitutional special routes under that hook.

<a id="42-sentient-forums"></a>

#### 4.2 Sentient Forums

Where a system materially affects how a community governs itself, Sentient Forums:

- Supply **community-governance findings** on whether the system's decisions follow the communities' published processes, let affected members be heard and contest, and meet the Chapter Eight tests that apply ([Subcore 8-B §5.3 Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#53-assembly-collective-organization-and-institutional-formation), [Subcore 8-B §6.3 Proportionate Cross-System Support Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#63-proportionate-cross-system-support-evaluation), and [Subcore 8-B §6.4 Nondiscrimination Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#64-nondiscrimination-evaluation)). This covers membership, exclusion, moderation, access to shared community process, participatory inputs, and commons use, whoever operates the system ([Chapter Seventeen §3.1 Sentient Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#31-sentient-forums) describes these matters);
- Hold the Rights Floor component for community-level stakes, such as exclusion, access to shared community process, or commons use. Institutional Forums hold the broader Rights Floor questions under [§4.3 Institutional Forums](#43-institutional-forums). Where no family holds the component, the Integrity lead applies the Rights Floors, using the other component findings;
- Yield to the Institutional Forum if their Rights Floor findings conflict, since the Institutional Forum has the final say in that pass. Both findings stay on the System Certification Record under [Subcore 8-D §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence), and the conflict stays open to challenge under [Subcore 8-D §3.3 Contestability Chain](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-contestability-chain);
- Hear challenges confined to a community-governance finding (the component forum path under [Subcore 8-D §3.3 Contestability Chain](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-contestability-chain)), and receive community-level evidence and concerns from affected members during review, as part of the participation duty of the assigned forums under [Subcore 8-D §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence); and
- Take up remedy and restoration among sentients after a certification pass finds harm to community members, under [Chapter Seventeen §3.1 Sentient Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#31-sentient-forums). This follows the certification pass and creates no second merits track.

<a id="43-institutional-forums"></a>

#### 4.3 Institutional Forums

Where an institution is involved, Institutional Forums:

- Supply the component findings on what the institution is allowed and charged to do, the scope it is supervised under, how it is classified, and whether it is meeting its duties, when those questions arise in a certification; and
- Decide whether certification would preserve or defeat constitutionally protected floors. This applies when those floors are materially involved and any of these is true: an institution is a necessary party; the operator or steward is institutional; or institutional mandate or supervised compliance is the primary stake. The floors are the Chapter Four Rights Floors in the [Subcore 8-B §6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) table. The decision includes evaluation under the matching domain section there, where its trigger applies. The floors are:
  - **Article III-A** (*Survival*);
  - **Article IV-A** (*Equal Educational Access*);
  - **Article IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*);
  - **Article V** (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*);
  - **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*);
  - **Article VI-D** (*Accessibility*);
  - **Article XIV** (*Right to Reliable and Trustworthy Systems*); and
  - **Article XIV-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*), with [Subcore 3-K — **Safe Conditions**](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safe-conditions).

<a id="44-constitutional-and-other-regular-forum-families"></a>

#### 4.4 Constitutional and Other Regular Forum Families

**Constitutional and residual stakes:**

- Keep the questions Chapter Sixteen gives them: whether constitutional text is valid, what it means, and whether a class-wide fix must restructure governance, along with any other question whose main stake Chapter Sixteen gives to a family not named above;
- Answer the constitutional questions the lead forum sends them for ruling under [Chapter Seventeen §4 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling): a question of validity, a structural question about restructuring governance, or a conflict among lower panels on a constitutional point. Until they answer, the lead forum cannot treat that point as finally resolved ([§5 Default Sequence](#5-default-sequence), step 5); and
- Technical expertise and Integrity coordination must not take those questions over.

<a id="5-default-sequence"></a>
### 5. Default Sequence

The **supervisory sequence** is the step-by-step order in which forums supervise a System Certification Record. Chapter Sixteen may require a different lawful order when primary stakes, anti-self-judging protection, or certification routing applies. Otherwise, the default sequence is:

1. **Lead coordination:** An **Integrity** forum leads each pass of official constitutional alignment certification, validation, or recertification, including a certification pass that ends in withdrawal or non-certification. It directs the record seat holder (see [Subcore 8-D §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles)) to open the one coordinated System Certification Record for that pass, and the record seat holder maintains it. The System Certification Record opened here is completed in Phase V (see step 6). The exception is where Chapter Twenty-Two assigns a different lawful lead for the primary stake.
2. **Component findings:** Technical, Environment, Institutional, Sentient, Constitutional, and other assigned forum families each produce bounded component findings within their lawful jurisdiction:
   - **Technical Forums** supply specifications, methods, tests, and technical adequacy findings. That includes [Subcore 3-N — Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) component findings where [Subcore 8-D §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) assigns that stake;
   - **Environment Forums** supply required environmental-alignment component findings where there is material ecological exposure. That includes [Subcore 3-K — Ecological Footprint](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#ecological-footprint) evaluation under [Subcore 8-B §6.1 Ecological Footprint Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#61-ecological-footprint-evaluation) where attributable environmental burdens are material;
   - **Institutional Forums** supply required institutional-mandate and Rights Floor component findings where [Subcore 8-D §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) assigns those stakes;
   - **Sentient Forums** supply community-governance findings where the system materially affects how a community governs itself, and the Rights Floor component for community-level stakes, with Institutional Forums having the final say where the two conflict ([§4.2 Sentient Forums](#42-sentient-forums)); and
   - **Constitutional** and other assigned forum families supply component findings for stakes that Chapter Sixteen assigns to them.
3. **Referral, sending for ruling, and stay:** The lead forum must refer, send for ruling, or [Subcore 3-J — stay](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#stay) any component question that belongs mainly to another forum family. Until the required component work behind an authorization is finished, the lead forum cannot authorize the affected scope, and it cannot remove or significantly loosen the certification conditions of the affected scope. Required component work includes:
   - any finding a component forum is required to give;
   - a decision on any material objection (one that could affect the outcome);
   - verification that any certification condition required before authorization has been met; and
   - the answer to any question sent to another forum (a question for ruling), if the authorization depends on that answer.

   Two rules keep this from being bypassed or from stalling the process:
   - **Limited operation needs a completed finding:** A component authority may issue a completed finding that supports operation within set limits, under stated continuing certification conditions or corrective work (see [Subcore 8-D §4.1 Go-Live Gate](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate)). The finding must say why the permitted scope still protects the applicable Rights Floors, and what remains to be verified.
   - **Silence is not approval:** An unanswered objection, a missing finding, or a component's silence never counts as authorization.
4. **Integration for decision:** The lead forum integrates the component findings for decision. The record seat holder enters them in the one System Certification Record opened at step 1. The System Certification Record then states, as things stand when the decision is made: which forum or authority gave each component finding, what each finding says, every unresolved item, every certification condition, and every reliance limit.
5. **Constitutional disposition:** If constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy is materially at issue, the lead forum must send the question for ruling or escalate under Chapter Sixteen before treating the matter as finally resolved on that point.
6. **Hand-off to Phase IV and Phase V:** The lead forum states the outcome under [Subcore 8-D §4 Certification Outcomes](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) (Phase IV, Decide). The record seat holder then completes the System Certification Record under [Subcore 8-F §3 System Certification Record](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-system-certification-record) (Phase V, Record) by entering the outcome and the concerns raised in reaching it. No second record is opened for the decision.
