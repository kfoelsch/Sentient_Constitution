# Joint Structure

This page is the non-operative navigation home for the Joint Structure layer. The substantive rules are in the linked Joint Structure files.

## Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: the shared glue that tells the systems, institutions, and forum implementation files how to work together when more than one of them applies to the same situation.*

**Scope:**

- How the systems, institutions, and forum layers fit together when more than one applies to the same facts
- How shared contracts set authority, readability, identifiers, and “who owns this topic”
- How topic routing and mandatory read-with order work across implementation files
- How joint obligations and interlocks must be satisfied together — not restated as domain doctrine
- Shared operational definitions (**oDef**) across the operational cluster chapters
- Interface and drafting homes for shared work across layers

The operational cluster chapters are **CJS-3.105** (*operational cluster library*), **CJS-3.110 §2** (*Oversight: Reflexive Transparency and Accountability Terms*), **CJS-3.115 §3** (*Audit process home*), **CJS-3.120 §7** (*Participation: Quorum and Participatory Legitimacy Terms*), **CJS-3.125 §11** (*Accountability: Distributed and Proportional Authority Terms*), **CJS-3.130 §16** (*Continuity: Dependency Integrity and Disclosure Terms*), and **CJS-3.135 §22** (*Integrative: Constrained-Secrecy and Protected-Investigation Terms*). The [CJS-3.115 §3 audit process home](corpus_joint_structure/cjs_03.115_audit_process.md#cjs-3115-3-audit-process-home-odef3) explains the shared audit process.

The main interface and drafting homes are **CJS-0.110** (*Scope, purpose, and boundary interface*), **CJS-30.105 §7** (*Intentional Overlap (Non-Duplication Discipline)*), **CJS-3.140 §13** (*Operational Structure and Subsection-Local Terminology*), and **CJS-6.105 §9** (*Boundary between Support Roles and Merits Decisions*). Expand **Joint structure index** below or open [CJS-0.105 §1 Cross-File Routing](corpus_joint_structure/cjs_00.105_registry_and_reading_rules.md#cjs-0105-1-cross-file-routing) for a specific file.

**Does not:**

- Replace day-to-day domain rules in [Systems and Data](corpus_systems.md), [Institutions](corpus_institutions.md), or [Forums](corpus_forum.md)
- Redefine Rights Floors or Chapter Three terms
- Require most readers to read this folder front to back

**Implements from the core files:**

- [Chapter Two](core_02.000_values_principles.md) / [Preamble](core_00.000_preamble.md) — [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) and [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims) provide the compass for **oDef** and the shared operational clusters.
- [Chapter Three](core_03.000_definitions_home.md) definition stack and [Corpus](subcore_03.045_band_integrative.md#corpus) designation — joint interfaces and **oDef** clusters that must track constitutional **Def.*** meaning without redefining it
- Cross-layer support for [Chapter Four](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md) through [Chapter Twenty-Six](core_26.000_governance.md) where systems, institutions, and forums must satisfy shared integrity terms together
- [Chapter Thirty](core_30.000_incorporation.md) incorporation discipline for how implementation text binds when adopted
