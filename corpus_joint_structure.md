# Joint structure

*In plain terms: the shared glue that tells the systems, institutions, and forum implementation files how to work together when more than one of them applies to the same situation.*

**Scope:**
- How the systems, institutions, and forum layers fit together when more than one applies to the same facts
- How shared contracts set authority, readability, identifiers, and “who owns this topic”
- How topic routing and mandatory read-with order work across implementation files
- How joint obligations and interlocks must be satisfied together — not restated as domain doctrine
- Shared operational definitions (**oDef**) are organized across the operational cluster chapters: **CJS-5.105** (*operational cluster library*), **CJS-5.110 §2** (*Oversight: reflexive transparency and accountability terms*), **CJS-5.115 §3** (*Audit process home*), **CJS-5.120 §7** (*Participation: quorum and participatory legitimacy terms*), **CJS-5.125 §11** (*Accountability: distributed and proportional authority terms*), **CJS-5.130 §16** (*Continuity: dependency integrity and disclosure terms*), and **CJS-5.135 §22** (*Integrative: constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*). The [CJS-5.115 §3 audit process home](corpus_joint_structure/cjs_05.115_audit_process.md#cjs-5115-3--odef3-audit-process-home) explains the shared audit process.
- The main interface and drafting homes are **CJS-0.110** (*Scope, purpose, and boundary interface*), **CJS-18.105 §7** (*Drafting contracts*), **CJS-5.140 §13** (*oDef parse mechanics*), and **CJS-8.105 §9** (*Support roles, hosting independence, and classification alignment*). Open the [Joint structure index](corpus_joint_structure.md) or the [CJS-0.105 §1 Cross-file routing](corpus_joint_structure/cjs_00.105_registry_and_reading_rules.md#cjs-0105-1-cross-file-routing) for a specific file.

**Does not:**
- Replace day-to-day domain rules in [Systems and data](corpus_systems.md), [Institutions](corpus_institutions.md), or [Forums](corpus_forum.md)
- Redefine Rights Floors or Chapter Five terms
- Require most readers to read this folder front to back

**Implements from the core files:**
- [Chapter One](core_01.000_values_principles.md) / [Preamble](core_00.000_preamble.md) — [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) and [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims) provide the compass for **oDef** and the shared operational clusters.
- [Chapter Five](core_05.000_definitions_home.md) definition stack and [Corpus](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#corpus) designation — joint interfaces and **oDef** clusters that must track constitutional **Def.*** meaning without redefining it
- Cross-layer support for [Chapter Six](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md) through [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_governance.md) where systems, institutions, and forums must satisfy shared integrity terms together
- [Chapter Eighteen](core_18.000_incorporation.md) incorporation discipline for how implementation text binds when adopted
