*In plain terms: Chapter Eight asks whether a system is aligned. Chapter Nine asks the same of the institution that runs systems, or that holds authority without running one. An institution can be aligned while one of its systems is not, and the reverse, so a system's certification belongs to the system. An institution's certification cites those certifications and adds what only the institution can answer for. One system can appear under several institutions, or under none. Nothing here lets a certification stand in the way of starting an institution.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Nine sets the evaluation that decides whether an Institution can be certified as aligned. The Institution may run systems or may hold authority without running one. It is asked what only the Institution can answer for: whether it does what its Institutional Charter says, who answers for its authority, what it rewards in its own members, and whether the independent checks on it can actually be used.

**Role:** Chapter Eleven extends [Subcore 8-B](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#subcore-8-b-system-alignment-certification--evaluation) from a System to an Institution. Where it sits:

- The two certifications are joined by a link, not nested. An Institutional Certification Record cites each System Certification Record it relies on and does not redo it.
- [Subcore 9-D](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#subcore-9-d-institutional-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes) covers forum process and outcomes (Phases III and IV): the forum roles and sequence ([Subcore 9-D §3 Forum Process](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#3-forum-process)), and the outcome, remediation readiness, and denial and revocation ([Subcore 9-D §4 Certification Outcomes](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes)).
- [Subcore 9-F](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#subcore-9-f-institutional-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing) holds the Institutional Certification Record ([Subcore 9-F §3 Institutional Certification Record](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-institutional-certification-record)) and what follows it (Phases V and VI): recertification and reopening, the cascade from System certifications, the reopening of its Systems, the challenge path, and the link to standing ([Subcore 9-F §4 Recertification and Reopening](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#4-recertification-and-reopening) to [Subcore 9-F §6 Relationship to Standing](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing)).
- Phases III to VI run as [Subcore 8-D §3 Forum Process](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#3-forum-process) to [Subcore 8-F §6 Relationship to Standing](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing) provide, applied to an Institution in Chapters Twelve and Thirteen, which state only what changes when the subject is an Institution.
- The certification is not a licence. Nobody needs its permission to start or run an institution.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers the evaluation phases for an Institution:

- why institutional alignment certification exists, how it links to system certification, who is certified, and the window for the first certification (**section 2**);
- the institution class, and how it scales the evaluation (**section 3**);
- the whole-institution evaluation (**section 4**); and
- the evaluations that apply where implicated, and the Rights-Floor and domain evaluations (**section 5** and **section 6**).

The chapter leaves System evaluations to Chapter Eight, forum process and outcomes to Chapter Nine, and records and recertification to Chapter Nine.

### 2. Why Institutional Alignment Certification Exists

Institutional alignment certification and System Alignment Certification are two certifications joined by a link. The relation is **composition by link**, not containment. In the figure, an arrow runs from a System Certification Record to each Institutional Certification Record that cites it. A System under no Institution has no arrow and needs none, and an Institution that runs no System has an Institutional Certification Record alone.

Composition by link has two sides, the Institution's first.

On the Institution's side:

- **An Institutional Certification Record cites a System's and does not redo it:** It cites the System Certification Record by link and names its version. This Chapter never re-runs a System evaluation, never re-decides a System's class, and never changes a System's outcome, status, or certification conditions.
- **The Institution's certification cannot lift a System's:** A System that is Not Certified stays Not Certified whatever the Institution's status. A System that holds certification gains no protection from an Institution's finding.
- **Institutional facts are found once:** Where [Subcore 8-B §4.1 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#41-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline) asks about the operator's governance and incentives, a current Institutional Certification Record for the operator may supply those facts by link. The System's forums still decide whether those facts are enough for that System. They may not find the same institutional fact differently without saying why on the System Certification Record.

On the System's side:

- **The System Certification Record belongs to the System:** It is the System's own System Certification Record under [Subcore 8-B](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#subcore-8-b-system-alignment-certification--evaluation).
- **One System Certification Record may be cited by several Institutions:** A System can be operated, stewarded, or materially controlled by more than one Institution under [Chapter Five §3 When System and Institutional Charters Are Required](core_05.000_charters.md#3-when-system-and-institutional-charters-are-required). Each cites the same System Certification Record.
- **A System need not sit under any Institution:** A single sentient is not an Institution, so a System may have none. A System with no Institution is certified under [Subcore 8-B](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#subcore-8-b-system-alignment-certification--evaluation) alone and needs nothing from this Chapter. A System that does sit under an Institution is certified the same way: this Chapter adds a separate record for the Institution and is not a step in the System's own certification.

<a id="21-who-is-certified"></a>

#### 2.1 Who Is Certified

An Institution of Class L or higher is within this Chapter, with the class decided under [§3.1 Institution Class](#31-institution-class). That includes an Institution that runs no System at all: it has no System Certification Record to cite, and its class comes from its own impact. An Institution's **portfolio** is the set of Systems it operates, stewards, or materially controls ([Portfolio](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#portfolio)).

- **Class A, B, C, and L:** the process applies, scaled by class under [§3.2 Scaling by Class](#32-scaling-by-class).
- **Class P:** the process is encouraged and may be used voluntarily. It becomes mandatory on reclassification to Class L or higher, or where the Institution voluntarily asserts constitutional compliance. A Class P Institution that decides to take on new material authority is reclassified to at least Class L when it gives the notice in [Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority), because that authority is material by definition ([Subcore 9-F §4.2.1 What Counts as New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#421-what-counts-as-new-material-authority)) and an Institution that is material in any domain is at least Class L ([§3.1 Institution Class](#31-institution-class), item 2). The reclassification lasts from the notice until the notice is withdrawn before clearance, or the authority is not cleared, provided nothing under it has been exercised. The Institution then returns to Class P and the limited first certification ends. A new notice starts it again, and exercising the authority without clearance stays a finding under [Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority) (*Without notice, or before it is cleared*). This mirrors [Subcore 8-B §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#1-purpose-role-and-scope).
- **One System:** an Institution that runs exactly one System that needs a System Charter has one certification pass and one System Certification Record, which this Chapter calls the **one-System evaluation**. The System Certification Record carries an *institutional findings block* with the items in [Subcore 9-F §3 Institutional Certification Record](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-institutional-certification-record), and it serves as the Institutional Certification Record. Forums, seats, and clocks are those of the System's own certification pass, with the institutional items added. Its outcomes follow their own denial and revocation grounds, as [Subcore 9-D §4.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#44-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) states. The cascade in [Subcore 9-F §4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#45-cascade-from-system-certifications) does not apply as a cascade, since the System is the whole portfolio, but [Subcore 9-D §4.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#44-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) applies its rules 1 and 2 (the indicator and the test of where the cause lies).
- **Several Systems:** one Institutional Certification Record for the Institution, which links to the System Certification Record of each System it runs. Each System keeps its own pass and its own System Certification Record.
- **No System:** one Institutional Certification Record on its own. It has no System Certification Record to link to, because the Institution runs no System.

<a id="22-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running"></a>

#### 2.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running

Institutional alignment certification is an independent check on how an Institution actually uses its authority. Forums independent of the Institution make it, and it ends in an Institutional Certification Record of what they found.

- **Starting and operating:** An Institution forms, publishes its Institutional Charter, begins to exercise authority, and keeps operating without first holding it, and no outcome grants or withholds that right.
- **Effect of an outcome:** An outcome has only the effects in [Subcore 9-D §4.1 Go-Live for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-for-an-institution).
- **The only gates in this area:** There are three narrow gates, all in **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*):
  - an outstanding lock on a named sentient, which attaches to that sentient's authority;
  - an Interim Hold on specific verified facts of misalignment, which also attaches to a named sentient's authority;
  - a lock on an Operating Party itself, which attaches to an Institution only where a lock sits on that Institution, as one does on a dissolved Institution under **CI-12.210 §4** (*Dissolution*).

  A certification finding can be referred to the forum that decides one of these gates, as evidence under [Subcore 9-D §4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution). A certification outcome is not itself a gate and does not open or close one.
- **Waiting for new material authority:** An Institution that decides to take on new material authority waits for its recertification to clear that authority before it exercises it, on the terms of [Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority). **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) states this wait. It is not one of the three narrow gates, and it is not a gate on forming or running. It attaches only to the new authority, and everything else the Institution does continues. The figure in [Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority) summarizes the steps.
- **No back door:** Nothing in this Chapter may be used, by a forum, an adopter, or an incumbent, as an authorization threshold that shuts out founders or protects incumbents. That would defeat [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*).
  - [Avoidable Burden](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) applies to the cost of the process.
  - [Classification-Scaled Governance](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) applies to its depth.
  - The wait for new material authority applies on the same terms to every Institution of its class, and only to authority that is already material to others.

The go-live gate for Systems is separate and stays with them. Under [Subcore 8-D §4.1 Go-Live Gate](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate), a new System, or a material expansion of one, goes live only through its own certification, whatever the Institution's status.

<a id="23-window-for-the-first-certification"></a>

#### 2.3 Window for the First Certification

A first Institutional Certification Record is due within a window the adopting document sets for the Institution's class.

- **A new Institution:** The window runs from the day it first has a material impact on others, as [Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) judges it, and its first certification is provisional.
- **A Class P Institution reclassified on notice:** The window runs from the day it gives the notice under [Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority), as for a new Institution, and never from an Adoption Transition window that has already ended.
- **One already operating:** The window is the Adoption Transition window under [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#xxviii-a-existing-instantiations-adoption-transition-window) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), consistent with the default in [Chapter Five §3.5 When a System Charter or Institutional Charter Is Due](core_05.000_charters.md#35-when-a-system-charter-or-institutional-charter-is-due).
- **A missed window:** An Institution that lets the window run out without a first pass, and is not excused below, has a defect to log and correct, and the missed window opens that pass. An Institution of Class L or higher that is already operating also has the milestone failure below. The Institution is not stopped or barred from operating, since there is no gate ([§2.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](#22-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)).
  - **An Institution already operating:** This is this Chapter's own rule for Institutions of Class L or higher, set by analogy to [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#xxviii-a-existing-instantiations-adoption-transition-window) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), which names Systems and derived entities. If its Adoption Transition window ends and no first certification pass is complete (no provisional or full certification and no decided outcome under [Subcore 9-D §4 Certification Outcomes](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes)), it has missed its deadline. An Institution that filed and cooperated inside the window has not missed it where the certification pass is incomplete because a forum overran its own time ([Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), *Overrun review*; [Subcore 8-F §4.3.4 Tier Windows](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#434-tier-windows)) or because the one deferral under [Subcore 9-D §4.3 Remediation Readiness for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#43-remediation-readiness-for-an-institution) is running. Without the reauthorization [Article XXIX-B](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxix-b-adoption-transition-authority-limits-and-reauthorization) (*Adoption Transition Authority Limits and Reauthorization*) requires, an Institution that misses this Adoption Transition milestone has a milestone failure and enters the ordinary compliance and violation route in [Subcore 10-B](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#subcore-10-b-standing-records--question-1-what-happened). The restraint duties in that Article apply from the effective date, and the gap does not become the new normal by lasting. This mirrors [Subcore 8-D §4.1.1 Systems Already Operating](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#411-systems-already-operating). It is not a gate.
  - **The certification pass:** It is decided like any other. It can end in any outcome in [Subcore 9-D §4 Certification Outcomes](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes), including Not Certified, with the effects in [Subcore 9-D §4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution).
  - **Sanctions and dissolution:** This Chapter creates none. Suspension, receivership, structural separation, and dissolution stay with the forum with jurisdiction under **CI-12.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) and **CI-19.205** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*). That forum receives a referral only on the referral grounds in [Subcore 9-D §4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](subcore_09.040_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) (item 5, *Referral*).

### 3. Institution Class Evaluation

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: [§4](#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation) (*the items the class scales*); [Subcore 9-F §3](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-institutional-certification-record) (*the class is stated on the Institutional Certification Record*); [Subcore 9-F §4.2](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers) (*the triggers that reassess it*).
- Read with:

  - **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*);
  - **CI-6.205 §6** (*System and Institutional Charter Contents, Review, and Formation Template*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: first sort the institution into a class by how much it matters to others. Judge that by what it actually decides and holds, not by what it calls itself. The class then sets how thorough the evaluation is: the higher the class, the stricter the proof, the more independent the review, and the sooner it is checked again.*

This section is **Phase I, Frame** for an Institution.

<a id="31-institution-class"></a>

#### 3.1 Institution Class

**Institution class** is decided by the greatest material impact the Institution has, whether that impact comes through a System it runs or outside any System. It is judged by what the Institution actually decides, holds, and controls, and not by its label, legal form, or the list in its Institutional Charter. The class governs the depth of the evaluation and the clocks, as [Subcore 8-B §3.2 How system class scales every evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#32-how-system-class-scales-every-evaluation) does for a System. It is the class used in this Chapter and in [Chapter Five §3.2 Which Classes Are Covered](core_05.000_charters.md#32-which-classes-are-covered).

The factors in full are in [Subcore 9-A — §3 Institution Class Factors](subcore_09.010_institution_class_evaluation_items.md#3-institution-class-factors). Their guarantees are:

- **At least its highest System:** an Institution's class is at least the highest class of any System it runs, found from observed operation, so a System missing from the Institutional Charter still counts.
- **Its own impact counts:** what the Institution itself decides and holds, whether it is material in any domain, and how much depends on it set its class whether or not it runs a System. Material in any one domain means at least Class L; freely offered aid binds no one and is not a Materially Binding Act.
- **No splitting:** Operating Parties under common practical control are classified together, and dividing an Operating Party so that no part reaches Class L is an evasion.
- **Stated and reassessed:** the class is stated with reasons on the Institutional Certification Record and reassessed on the reopening triggers in [Subcore 9-F §4.2 Reopening Triggers](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers).

<a id="32-scaling-by-class"></a>

#### 3.2 Scaling by Class

The institution class scales the depth of evidence, the independence of the review, the default certification interval, and the disclosure on the Institutional Certification Record, in the way [Subcore 8-B §3.2 How system class scales every evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#32-how-system-class-scales-every-evaluation) does. The certification intervals are in [Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#review-cadence) (*Review cadence*); the adopting document may set shorter intervals and sets the number of clean provisional recertifications.

- **Class A, B, and C:** all eight items in [§4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), with evidence and independence in proportion to the class.
- **Class L:** the same eight items, simplified, with simpler records and a longer default certification interval where [Classification-Scaled Governance](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) allows. The core of a Class L pass is the Institutional Charter against practice, the Role Map, the formation check statement, and the currency of the cited System certifications. No item may be omitted, and an item with nothing to find is stated in a line.
- **Class P:** voluntary, as [§2.1 Who Is Certified](#21-who-is-certified) states.

**A host in a joint professional practice.** Where an Institution is a host ([**CI-21.205 §7**](corpus_institutions/ci_21.205_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-46-seat-advisory) (*Practice Licence, Practice Clearance, and Joint Professional Practice*)), its certification also samples members' acts against the seats the host carries and reads the members' adoption notices against what the host does. A finding about one member's act is a finding about that member, and it reaches the host only where a seat the host carries failed.

### 4. Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

  - **CI-6.210** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*), **CI-4.205** (*Transparency, participation, and accessible challenge and service pathways*), and **CI-19.210** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*), for item 8.

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: check the things that belong to the institution and not to any one of its systems: whether it keeps to its Institutional Charter, who answers for its authority, what it rewards, and whether the independent checks on it work.*

This section is **Phase II, Evaluate** for an Institution.

Every certification pass evaluates these eight items, which are the institutional counterpart of [Subcore 8-B §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation). Each finding states its evidence and the Institutional Charter text or observed operation it rests on.

The full tests for each item, and for the two further parts of [Subcore 8-B §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation), are in [Subcore 9-A — §4 The Eight Evaluation Items](subcore_09.010_institution_class_evaluation_items.md#4-the-eight-evaluation-items). Each item's guarantee is:

1. **Institutional Charter against practice:** the Institutional Charter is tested against observed operation, and a gap either way is a finding.
2. **Portfolio and aggregate effect:** each System traces to the Institution's stated purpose, and the evaluation looks across its Systems for shared dependencies, aggregate reliance, cumulative extraction, and splitting one function to escape a class or a duty.
3. **Accountable authority:** every authority over others traces to a named, answerable office, and a change of ownership, control, or structure does not release responsibility.
4. **Rewards and incentives:** what the Institution rewards is put to the [Two Fundamental Questions](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-fundamental-questions); rewarding misalignment, hiding it, or short-horizon results at long-horizon cost is a finding even where every System is certified.
5. **Role Map and custody:** the Charter Role Map names real offices, independent of the Institution for its own certification, and the independent route works in fact.
6. **Currency of each cited System certification:** each System it runs has a certification in force or inside its Adoption Transition window, and the Institution meets what that certification requires of it.
7. **Office-holder standing and authenticity:** the formation check statement is tested against observed operation, not taken on attestation; staff without a covered office are covered by the staff safeguards check; findings attach to a named sentient only through the verified-input gate; and the formation check is never a test of belief.
8. **The Institution's own institutional duties:** its oversight, accountability, participation, and timeliness routes exist, can be used in fact, and answer within stated times no longer than the floor timing for its class.

**Two more parts.** Systemic scope and risk factors, and data types and handling, are evaluated on the Institution's own conduct where each one's trigger is met, under [Subcore 8-B §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation), and an evaluation already made on a cited System Certification Record is not made again.

### 5. Evaluations Where Implicated

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

  - **CI-19.210** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: where the institution's own conduct touches one of the areas a system is checked on when it is implicated, the same evaluation is made on the institution, once.*

Where an Institution's own conduct implicates a matter covered by [Subcore 8-B §5 Evaluations Where Implicated](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated), the matching evaluation runs on the Institution's own conduct, with the same triggers. Each applies only where its own trigger is met:

- **Privacy (Informational):** where the Institution's conduct materially implicates more than one Chapter Four privacy locus, each locus is addressed, and closing the matter under one alone is not enough ([Subcore 8-B §5.1 Privacy (Informational) Joint Invocation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#51-privacy-informational-joint-invocation)).
- **Voluntary discontinuation, major self-modification, and exit rights:** voluntariness, consent, anti-coercion, dependency pressure, information, and reversibility are tested before the certification claim stands, and formal assent alone is not enough ([Subcore 8-B §5.2 Voluntary Discontinuation, Major Self-Modification, and Exit Rights](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#52-voluntary-discontinuation-major-self-modification-and-exit-rights)).
- **Assembly, collective organization, and institutional formation:** the matter is tested jointly under the Anti-Segmentation Principle, and not through one framing alone ([Subcore 8-B §5.3 Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#53-assembly-collective-organization-and-institutional-formation)).
- **Dissent and peaceful protest:** the Institution may not use dissent or protected nonviolent civil disobedience as an adverse input, directly or through proxies ([Subcore 8-B §5.3.1 Dissent and Peaceful Protest](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#531-dissent-and-peaceful-protest)).

The institutional duties themselves are owned by adopted institutional text, including:

- **CI-6.210** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*);
- **CI-4.205** (*Transparency, participation, and accessible challenge and service pathways*);
- **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*); and
- **CI-19.210** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*).

This Chapter cites them and does not restate them. Whether the Institution keeps them in fact is tested under [§4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation) (item 8). An evaluation already made on a cited System Certification Record is not made again.
