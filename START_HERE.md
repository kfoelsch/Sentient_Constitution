# Using or adopting the Sentient Constitution — start here

**Who this page is for:** people who run, found, or build systems, and are thinking about using this Constitution in their own work or formally adopting it. That includes AI labs, platform operators, cooperatives, commons, research bodies, and their human and AI stewards.

**Not sure this is you?** If you want to understand what the Constitution is, what it protects, or what it would mean for you, begin at the **[README](README.md)**. That is the front door for all readers, and it has [timed reading paths](README.md#reading-paths). If you have been harmed and cannot find where to turn, go straight to [*I can't find the door*](implementation/adoption/easy_entry/E15_cannot_find_the_door.md).

> This page is reader support, not constitutional text. It cannot change or narrow what the numbered `core_*` files say. Reading it is not adoption under [Chapter Sixteen §10](core_16_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption).

<a id="what-it-is"></a>
## 1. What adoption means

This is a **model constitution**. It becomes **enforceable** for a particular body only after that body validly adopts it under [Chapter Sixteen](core_16_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) and [Chapter Seventeen §4](core_17_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority). Until then it may guide, inform, or serve as a reference; it has no power to enforce against anyone who has not adopted it. It does not supersede local, national, or international law. Where it is used inside an existing legal framework, its processes must stay consistent with that framework ([Chapter Fifteen §5](core_15_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)).

Four duties (**participation**, **oversight**, **accountability**, and **timeliness**) grow stronger as a system's [material stake](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) grows ([Preamble §1](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad)). Human and AI stewards are held to one shared standard ([Chapter One §9.1.1](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#101-shared-stewardship-standard)): there is no AI-only rulebook, and no exemption for humans.

<a id="pre-release-status"></a><a id="core-vs-operational"></a>
### Pre-release status, and which parts are mature

Edition, status, and license are kept in one place: the [release details](README.md#edition) at the top of the README; what pre-release means is under [*Is this in effect?*](README.md#is-this-in-effect). Two points matter most for anyone planning to use the text:

- The numbered `core_*` files (the Preamble and Chapters One–Seventeen) are the binding constitutional source and are in **much better shape** than anything else.
- The implementation files (`corpus_systems`, `corpus_institutions`, `corpus_forum`, `corpus_joint_structure`) and the process-support pages (steward doors, adoption kits) are **not yet mature**. They spell out *how* to carry out what the core already requires; they may not change what its terms mean, shrink its duties, or add a second set of rights. If one of them disagrees with a `core_*` file, the `core_*` file wins. Do not treat them as a finished operator manual.

<a id="two-modes"></a>
## 2. Two ways to use it

Named in [PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md §3.1](implementation/PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md) and [FIT_SITUATIONS.md §2](implementation/adoption/FIT_SITUATIONS.md#2-two-modes). This page does not add a third.

| Mode | What it is | When you can honestly say "we adopted it" |
|---|---|---|
| **Operations-guide use** | Using parts of the text as a practical guide for real problems, without claiming full constitutional adoption | Never. Attach the [non-adoption banner](implementation/adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#6-non-adoption-banner) when this is your situation |
| **Full adoption** | A qualifying body records a Chapter Sixteen instrument and keeps it in its own custody under Chapter Seventeen | Only after that instrument exists in **that body's** custody |

<a id="fit-tests"></a>
## 3. Does it fit your situation?

A situation is a good fit when **several** of these are true. The full table lives in [FIT_SITUATIONS.md §1](implementation/adoption/FIT_SITUATIONS.md#1-fit-tests); this is the short version, and passing it is not adoption.

- Your body can actually **bind the systems** it runs.
- Humans and AIs share the **costly duties**, with no exemptions for either.
- You are **founding or operating a body**, not retrofitting a national constitution.
- The **material stake** (impact, dependence, risk) is high enough to justify the effort.
- **Who may govern** is documented separately from giving people a voice inside a system that is already authorized.
- **Independent review** is available from someone other than the founding crew.

If you cannot bind the systems, or cannot name independent review that is not the founding crew, stop at operations-guide use plus a declaration about your own conduct. Do not move on to building oversight, certification, or other constitutional-function bodies (what the institution kit calls "Track B"; see [FIT_SITUATIONS.md §3](implementation/adoption/FIT_SITUATIONS.md#3-how-to-route)).

<a id="honest-non-fits"></a>
## 5. Where it does not fit

Same list as [FIT_SITUATIONS.md §5](implementation/adoption/FIT_SITUATIONS.md#5-honest-non-fits):

- Retrofitting an existing national constitution as drop-in law.
- Claiming this Constitution supersedes local, national, or international law, or that operations-guide use displaces a regulator ([Chapter Fifteen §5](core_15_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)).
- Marketing "we follow it" while dodging the matching obligations ([Chapter Seventeen §4](core_17_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)).
- Two chat partners, or a markdown working group, claiming adoption or calling themselves the oversight body ([Chapter One §9.5](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#104-aligned-self-organization)).
- Binding host products, vendors, or platforms the crew does not control.
- An AI-only rulebook ([§9.1.1](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#101-shared-stewardship-standard)), or exempting humans from the costly cases ([§9.1.2](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#102-alignment-under-pressure)).
- Treating a participation vote, token vote, or standing score as authorization, or as a finding about sentience ([Preamble §3.3](core_00_preamble.md#33-governance-layers); [Article XVIII-A](core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xviii-a-standing-distinction)).
- Staffing Chapter Twelve review forums from the cooperating crew and calling that independent review ([FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md](implementation/adoption/FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md)).

If any "cannot bind" row in the first-adopter kit's [scope honesty](implementation/adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#3-scope-honesty) block describes your situation, do not claim Chapter Sixteen adoption.
