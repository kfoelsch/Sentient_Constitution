### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: Chapter Nine explains how forums review a system and reach an outcome. This chapter covers what comes after the decision. A certification only works if it is kept where it can be checked, re-checked on a schedule, and challenged by sentients (the beings the Constitution covers) when it is wrong. Certification can feed standing, but it is not standing, and it never replaces standing measurement or standing effects.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Ten states what happens after the outcome:

- the minimum contents, transparency, and auditability of the System Certification Record (**section 3**);
- recertification on a schedule set by system class, reopening triggers, provisional and full certification, and non-evasion (**section 4**);
- the path for challenging a certification (**section 5**); and
- the relationship to standing (**section 6**).

**Role:** The chapter closes the System Alignment Certification sequence:

- It takes these as fixed:
  - the outcome from [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes); and
  - the evaluation and class framing from [Chapter Eight](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eight-system-alignment-certification--evaluation).
- The process chart:
  - [Chapter Eight §2.2 Certification at a Glance](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#22-certification-at-a-glance) charts the whole process; and

- Standing Records and their effects are decided in Chapters [Fourteen](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fourteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) and [Nineteen](core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#chapter-nineteen-final-standing-effect-restoration-and-enforcement), not here.

### 2. Why Certification Records and Recertification Exist

*In plain terms: a certificate is only as good as the System Certification Record behind it and the date it was last checked. This chapter exists so certification stays true after the decision.*

Systems change after they are certified: new versions ship, owners change, and risks appear. A certificate that is never re-checked becomes a label that outlives its truth. This chapter keeps the outcome on a System Certification Record that the system does not control, sets when the system must be checked again, and lets sentients challenge a certification that has gone wrong. It also keeps certification apart from standing, so a certificate is never treated as a verdict on anyone's conduct.

*In plain terms: a System Certification Record is the auditable case file for one system under review. It shows who runs the system, what was checked, what was found, what was decided, and how to challenge the decision. It is not a marketing packet or a one-line approval stamp. How much detail a System Certification Record carries, how much it discloses, and how heavy the challenge burden is all scale with system class, as [CS-8.320 §2 Illustrative System Class Profiles](corpus_systems/cs_08.320_system_alignment_certification_illustrations.md#cs-8320-2-illustrative-system-class-profiles) shows. A **Class A** survival-critical system needs a fuller, more contestable file than a **Class C** coordination layer. Both must state their system class honestly and reflect the evaluations required.*

This section is **Phase V, Record**, after [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) (**Phase IV, Decide**). The System Certification Record is opened for forum review in Phase III and completed here with the outcome.

A **System Certification Record** is bounded, reviewable, scope-bound, time-bound, and contestable, as the Chapter Three meaning in [System Certification Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record) says. In overview, it is about one particular (the full minimum contents are in [§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](#31-minimum-record-contents)):

- **system, and the decision about it:**
  - **The decision (the "decision context"):** whether, and on what terms, this system, in this System Version and scope, is certified, judged on how it was classified and what the evidence showed when the review was run.
  - **A new pass is a new decision:** a [later certification pass](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#certification-pass) is a new decision context and opens a new System Certification Record ([§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening)).
- **material-impact profile:** how much the system can affect sentients.
- **System Version:** which version of the system is being certified.
  - **Dependencies go with the System Version:** the System Version covers the parts the system relies on, such as an upstream system or a vendor's component, and the reliance that sentients place on it (its dependency types, recorded under [Subcore 10-A §3.2 Classification and Whole-System Findings](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#32-classification-and-whole-system-findings)). A material change in either can reopen review ([§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening)).
  - **Two kinds of versions:** the System Version is the one listed above. The System Certification Record has its own **Record Versions**. Correcting a finished System Certification Record adds a new Record Version of the same System Certification Record. It does not change the System Version, and it does not open a new System Certification Record, because the decision the System Certification Record supports is the same. The Record Version that was relied on stays on file, marked as corrected ([Record Integrity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-integrity)).
- **operator or steward:** who runs or looks after the system.
- **scope:** what the certification covers.
- **data types:** the kinds of data the system handles, and how they are classified and handled.
- **record span:** the time the System Certification Record covers.
  - It runs from when the System Certification Record is opened (step 1 of [Chapter Nine §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence)) until the outcome and operating plan are entered and the System Certification Record is complete ([Subcore 10-A §3.8 Outcome and Reliance Limits](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#38-outcome-and-reliance-limits) and [Subcore 10-A §3.9 Operating Plan](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#39-operating-plan)).

It also records:

- **whole-system findings:** what the whole-system evaluation found, including the risk findings and how risk is disclosed ([Subcore 10-A §3.2 Classification and Whole-System Findings](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#32-classification-and-whole-system-findings)).
- **Rights-Floor and domain findings:** what the evaluations found for each protection whose trigger applies ([Subcore 10-A §3.4 Rights-Floor and Domain Findings](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#34-rights-floor-and-domain-findings)).
- **technical evidence and review findings:** the tests, measurements, and other evidence relied on, and the stakeholder, environmental, and integrity review ([Subcore 10-A §3.5 Technical Evidence and Review Findings](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#35-technical-evidence-and-review-findings)).
- **supervision, independence, and challenge paths:** which forums supervised each part, who held the four seats, and how the decision can be challenged ([Subcore 10-A §3.6 Supervision, Independence, and Challenge Paths](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#36-supervision-independence-and-challenge-paths)).
- **concerns, cause, and remediation readiness:** every objection and dissent raised and how it was resolved, the cause of any defect found, and whether the operator or steward is willing and able to correct it ([Subcore 10-A §3.7 Concerns, Cause, and Remediation Readiness](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#37-concerns-cause-and-remediation-readiness)).
- **outcome and reliance limits:** the certification outcome, the reliance limits, and the reopening triggers ([Subcore 10-A §3.8 Outcome and Reliance Limits](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#38-outcome-and-reliance-limits)).
- **operating plan:** the concise plan for operating and monitoring the system ([Subcore 10-A §3.9 Operating Plan](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#39-operating-plan)).

The System Certification Record must also meet [Record Integrity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-integrity). For this System Certification Record:

- **Who keeps it:** the [Record Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-seat) opens, enters, amends, preserves, and publishes it ([Chapter Nine §3.1.1 Four Seats in a Certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#311-four-seats-in-a-certification)).
  - **Others may ask, not change:** the operator, steward, or proponent may submit evidence and ask for a change, but may not enter or change the System Certification Record.
  - **Requests are logged:** each request is logged, whether or not it is acted on.
- **Open and completed:** the System Certification Record is open from step 1 of [Chapter Nine §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence) until the Record Seat enters the outcome under [Subcore 10-A §3.8 Outcome and Reliance Limits](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#38-outcome-and-reliance-limits) and the operating plan under [Subcore 10-A §3.9 Operating Plan](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#39-operating-plan) and publishes it. While it is open, each amendment is a new Record Version that shows what changed.
- **Changes to a completed System Certification Record:** once the System Certification Record is completed, its existing entries are not changed in place. What follows depends on what happened:
  - **A correction or a later finding:** a new Record Version of the same System Certification Record. It states what changed, who made the change, under what authority, on what verification materials, and at whose request. The Record Version that was relied on stays reachable and is marked as corrected or superseded.
  - **Operating evidence:** is added as linked entries under [Subcore 10-A §3.9 Operating Plan](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#39-operating-plan).
  - **A new pass:** a later certification pass opens a new System Certification Record that links back to the completed one, not a new Record Version ([§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening)).
- **Challenge:** a challenge does not freeze or erase the System Certification Record ([Chapter Nine §3.3 Contestability Chain](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-contestability-chain); [§5 Challenging a Certification](#5-challenging-a-certification)).
  - **Inspection:** the System Certification Record stays inspectable.
  - **Prior Record Versions:** they stay.
  - **Status:** it shows that the System Certification Record is under challenge.
- **Care scales:** how strong the custody and version trail must be scales with system class and material impact, as detail and disclosure do.
  - **No system class removes:** attribution, the version trail, or the rule against editing a completed System Certification Record in place.

#### 3.1 Minimum Record Contents

Certifying a system is a materially binding act. So the System Certification Record has to meet the rules for a [Materially Binding Act Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act-record) and for [Chapter Six §9 Act Records and Attributable Handoffs](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#9-act-records-and-attributable-handoffs), and then add to them. It can hold those items itself, or link to them under those rules: each link must show who made it, and its version history must stay intact. It does not need a second, parallel record.

What the System Certification Record must contain is set out in full in [Subcore 10-A §3 Minimum Contents of the System Certification Record](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#3-minimum-contents-of-the-system-certification-record): system identity and scope, classification and whole-system findings, the System Data Types Record, Rights-Floor and domain findings, technical evidence and review findings, supervision, independence, and challenge paths, concerns, cause, and remediation readiness, outcome and reliance limits, and the operating plan. The opening of [§3 System Certification Record](#3-system-certification-record) gives each in overview, and [§3.1.10 Completeness](#3110-completeness) says when a System Certification Record counts as incomplete.

<a id="3110-completeness"></a>

##### 3.1.10 Completeness

A System Certification Record is incomplete if it omits the findings of any evaluation that applied:

- [Chapter Eight §3 System Class Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-system-class-evaluation), [§4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation), and [§4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation), for every materially impactful system;
- each [§5 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated) subsection whose locus or principle is implicated; and
- each [§6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) domain whose trigger is met.

Those findings must reflect what the evaluation actually found, not section titles or the operator's own attestation. A System Certification Record is also incomplete if it omits a concern, objection, or dissent raised in review or in reaching the outcome, including one that did not change the outcome.

*In plain terms: a System Certification Record only works if sentients can read it and check it. This section puts transparency and auditability on the System Certification Record itself, as concrete requirements rather than abstract ideals. Under the **oversight** Tetrad leg, oversight requires auditing. System alignment certification is one especially large audit process, and it must meet those floors here. It does not own or replace [Article XVII](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xvii-audit-transparency-and-independent-verification) (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*) or the other audit modes. The third requirement, that sentients can push back, is in [§5 Challenging a Certification](#5-challenging-a-certification).*

This section applies [Transparency](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#transparency) and [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability) to System Certification Records. They are the "reachable" and "reconstructable" parts of [Record Integrity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-integrity), which covers the whole System Certification Record ([§3 System Certification Record](#3-system-certification-record)). Canonical term homes and owner boundaries are in the [Chapter One — constitutional owner register](core_01.000_constitutional_map.md#4-principles-definitions-and-rights) and [Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#owner-non-relocation). Under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad):

- **Transparency** and **auditability** put **oversight** into practice; and
- Record clarity under [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) puts **timeliness** into practice, scaled to system class and [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake).

**Transparency** — sentients must be able to see what matters:

- A System Certification Record must disclose decision-relevant facts, at a level proportionate to system class and material impact under **[CS-8.305](corpus_systems/cs_08.305_system_classification_machinery.md#cs-8305-system-classification-machinery) (*System classification machinery*) and [Subcore 8-A — System Classification](subcore_08.010_system_classification.md#subcore-8-a-system-classification)**, including what [§3 System Certification Record](#3-system-certification-record) requires:
  - scope;
  - assumptions;
  - evidence basis;
  - uncertainty;
  - certification conditions;
  - outcomes; and
  - reliance limits;
- A System Certification Record is **Type O** certification data for every system class. It is published at public-baseline fidelity, with lawful substitutes, under [Subcore 4-A §10.3 Type O](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#103-type-o-open-public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-data);
- Limits on disclosure justified by safety or security are permitted only where they are consistent with [Epistemic Integrity](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) and Chapter Two disclosure constraints;
- Volume, formatting, or technical obscurity must not stand in for decision-relevant clarity; and
- The online-publication baseline, the paywall prohibition for [Public Oversight Baseline Disclosure](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), and the maximum-feasible public-substitute discipline are governed by [Transparency](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#transparency) and Public Oversight Baseline Disclosure, and implemented as **Type O** data under **[Subcore 4-A — Data Types](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#subcore-4-a-data-types) and [CS-4.305](corpus_systems/cs_04.305_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-4305-information-types-and-handling) (*Information types and handling*)**, read with **CJS-3.110 §4** (*transparency levels and audit-access terms*).

**Auditability** — sentients must be able to reconstruct and check the System Certification Record:

- A System Certification Record must be attributable, reconstructable, and independently reviewable under Subcores 3-A to 3-C, [Evidence Preservation](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and [Record Integrity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-integrity);
- It must identify:
  - who issued, adopted, referred, sent for ruling, [stayed](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#stay), or reviewed each material component;
  - what evidence was materially relied on; and
  - what changed between Record Versions, who made each change, under what authority, and at whose request, and, for a new System Certification Record, what has changed since the completed System Certification Record it follows;
- Audit scope must be enough to test whether the recorded outcome follows from the recorded evidence and assumptions; and
- Operators, stewards, and forums must not fragment, relabel, or conceal certification-relevant behavior in a way that defeats practical reconstructability.

**Joint requirement.** Transparency, auditability, and [contestability](#5-challenging-a-certification) must all be met on the same System Certification Record. Meeting one while defeating another does not comply.

*In plain terms: no outcome lasts forever. While a system operates, named owners monitor actual outcomes and safeguards, respond to incidents and challenges, and meet the certification conditions on the System Certification Record. A certified system is recertified on a schedule set by its system class, with an audit at the midpoint of each interval. Review reopens when material facts change or a credible challenge requires it. Defects lead to protection, correction, and independent verification. Each review pass starts a new System Certification Record linked to the last, and a new System Certification Record does not erase the earlier one or the challenges to it. An old approval cannot stand in for current Rights-Floor compliance.*

This section is sub-phase **VIa** of Phase VI (Operate), operate and recertify. It covers operating the certified system, keeping it current, and reopening review.

**Validation.** Chapter Twenty-Two calls the forum review of an existing system *ongoing alignment validation*. In this chapter, validation is not a separate process. It is a certification pass on a system that is already operating: a recertification, or a reopened or fresh review. It runs through [Chapter Nine §3 Forum Process](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#3-forum-process) like any other certification pass and ends in an outcome under [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes).

**Monitoring and response.** Operators and stewards must carry out the operating plan on the System Certification Record under [§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](#31-minimum-record-contents). Named owners must:

- preserve monitoring evidence;
- investigate material incidents and indicators;
- route challenges to the assigned authority; and
- carry out required interim protection and corrective work.

Any of the following that materially calls alignment into question requires timely forum review under this section and must not wait for the next scheduled recertification:

- an incident;
- an unmet certification condition;
- a missed proof step under [Chapter Nine §4.3 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#43-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct);
- false readiness evidence; or
- a credible challenge.

Correction, fulfillment, and release of certification conditions follow the independent-verification requirements in [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes).

#### 4.1 Recertification Schedule and the New System Certification Record

*In plain terms: a certification does not run forever. It comes up for recertification on a schedule set by the system's class, with an audit at the midpoint of each interval. Each certification pass opens a new System Certification Record linked to the last one.*

A **System Certification Record** must be recertified on the cadence set for its system class in [Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#review-cadence) (*Review cadence*), and audited at the midpoint of that cadence.

**New record for each certification pass.** Each later certification pass (a recertification, a reopened review, or a fresh review) opens a new System Certification Record. The new System Certification Record:

- links to the completed System Certification Record it follows and states what has changed since ([Subcore 10-A §3.1 System Identity, Scope, and Prior System Certification Record](subcore_10.010_system_certification_record_contents.md#31-system-identity-scope-and-prior-system-certification-record)); and
- runs through [Chapter Nine §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence) from step 1, as a first certification does.

The completed System Certification Record stays as it stood ([§3 System Certification Record](#3-system-certification-record)). It remains the record for the period it covers, and it stays open to challenge under [Chapter Nine §3.3 Contestability Chain](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-contestability-chain). Certification status, the count of clean recertifications, and monitoring indicators carry forward through the link. They are verified on the new System Certification Record under [§4.3 Provisional and Full Certification](#43-provisional-and-full-certification).

<a id="42-reopening-triggers"></a>

#### 4.2 Reopening Triggers

*In plain terms: a certification can reopen early, before its next scheduled date, when something material changes or goes wrong, such as concealed behavior, growing dependency, new risk, or a credible challenge.*

Review reopens under Chapter Twenty-Two and incorporated system implementation duties when any of the following is material:

- **Change in the system or its context:**
  - a material change in the system, its use, or its operating context;
  - dependency growth, including growing reliance on the system and growing extraction from shared foundations, and including concentration past a threshold an adopter has set under [Chapter Two §11.4 Resource and Dependency Concentration](core_02.010_values_principles_continuity_aim.md#114-resource-and-dependency-concentration-adopter-tunable) (the step-up does not wait for a threshold: [Chapter Eight §3.2 How System Class Scales Every Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#32-how-system-class-scales-every-evaluation));
  - a significant fall in the system's return flows (what it puts back into those foundations), under [Proportionate Cross-System Support](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#proportionate-cross-system-support).
- **Something observed or reported:**
  - concealed behavior;
  - new risk;
  - incident history;
  - stakeholder evidence;
  - a credible challenge under [§5 Challenging a Certification](#5-challenging-a-certification);
  - an ecological objection.
- **Classification and data-type misalignment:**
  - system-class misalignment, or overdue reassessment, under **[Subcore 8-A §3 Criticality, Concentration, Temporal, and Adversarial Factors](subcore_08.010_system_classification.md#3-criticality-concentration-temporal-and-adversarial-factors)**, specifically its [Subcore 8-A §3.5 reclassification requirement](subcore_08.010_system_classification.md#35-reclassification-requirement);
  - data-type or handling misalignment, or an overdue or skipped scheduled data-type re-evaluation, under **[Subcore 4-A §6 Cross-Domain Governance Principles](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#6-cross-domain-governance-principles)**, specifically its [Subcore 4-A §6.2 reclassification and lifecycle governance](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#62-reclassification-and-lifecycle-governance);
  - classification misalignment.
- **System Charter and operator governance:**
  - a [System Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-charter) amendment, a mismatch between the System Charter and behavior, operation materially outside chartered scope, or an overdue scheduled System Charter review;
  - a governance violation of an Institution whose [portfolio](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#portfolio) includes the System (a determination that its governance failed, a denial or revocation of the Institution on a governance-related denial or revocation ground, or a cause finding that places another System's misalignment in the Institution), as [Chapter Thirteen §4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#46-reopening-the-institutions-systems) defines it. Only a material one reopens.
- **Evaluation misalignment:** misalignment or a defect reflected on the System Certification Record under any Chapter Eight evaluation that applied, from [§3 System Class Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-system-class-evaluation) through [§6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations). That includes risk-evaluation or risk-disclosure defects under [§4.2 Systemic Scope and Risk Factors](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#42-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors).

**Why reopening matters.** Reopening on misalignment, dependency growth, or credible challenge protects the **Continuity** aim under [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims). This matters where shared systems gate the durable supply of survival essentials, educational access, capability-building pathways, trustworthy reliance, or safety. An out-of-date certification must not substitute for current Rights-Floor compliance.

<a id="43-provisional-and-full-certification"></a>

#### 4.3 Provisional and Full Certification

*In plain terms: a system earns trust in steps. New systems, and systems that have failed an evaluation, start on a short leash and are re-checked often. Only a system with nothing wrong now, and nothing heading that way, graduates to full certification and a longer interval. Failing an evaluation sends it back to the short leash.*

<a id="431-the-two-statuses"></a>

##### 4.3.1 The Two Statuses

Certification is held in one of two statuses. Each has its own certification interval, stated on the System Certification Record under [§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](#31-minimum-record-contents):

- **[Provisional certification](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#provisional-certification)** is the status of:
  - a system's first certification, including a newly deployed system; and
  - a system certified after a failed recertification, or after a reopened review or fresh review that found misalignment.

  Provisional certification runs on a provisional interval, shorter than the full certification interval for the same system class. Reliance limits are stated on the System Certification Record.
- **[Full certification](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#full-certification)** is earned only at a recertification of a system holding provisional certification, and only where all three of these hold:
  1. the system has completed the required number of consecutive clean provisional recertifications: at least one, and more for higher system classes, as the adopted document sets;
  2. no misalignment is current: the System Certification Record shows no open misalignment, unremedied defect, overdue check, or failed regression testing; and
  3. no monitoring indicator points to impending misalignment: the monitoring triggers and trend metrics stated on the System Certification Record show no threshold crossed and no trend toward one.

Full certification runs on the system-class-scaled certification interval. It remains time-bound and is not a permanent approval.

A recertification is **clean** when it meets requirements 2 and 3.

<a id="432-failure-and-step-down"></a>

##### 4.3.2 Failure and Step-Down

What a recertification, reopened review, or fresh review finds decides what happens to the system's status:

- A recertification that finds current misalignment is a **failed** recertification. The consequences in [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) apply. The next certification the system can earn is provisional certification, however clean the next check is. Its count of clean recertifications restarts at zero.
- A recertification that finds no current misalignment but shows an indicator of impending misalignment is not a failure, and it is not clean. A system holding provisional certification stays provisional, and its count does not advance. A system holding full certification steps down to provisional certification.
- A reopened review under [§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening) that finds misalignment has the same effect as a failed recertification.
- A fresh review of a system that holds certification and finds current misalignment has the same effect as a failed recertification. If the system holds no certification status, the denial rules in [Chapter Nine §4.4 Denial and Revocation](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#44-denial-and-revocation) apply.

<a id="433-cause-of-misalignment"></a>

##### 4.3.3 Cause of Misalignment

Where an evaluation, a recertification, a reopened review, or a fresh review finds misalignment, the System Certification Record states its cause as one of:

- **design-caused:** the system or its process is built so that it produces misaligned results even when run as intended;
- **operating-caused:** the design is sound on its face, but the way the system is run, fed, or maintained makes its results misaligned; or
- **not yet determined:** the remedies for design and for operation both stay open until the forum finds the cause.

The cause finding works within these limits:

- If the System keeps producing misaligned results, it is misaligned, even if its design looks sound on paper. A design that looks sound is not a defense.
- A design that would produce misaligned results is misalignment before any such result appears.
- The cause finding sets the remedy sought and the scope of any reopened review. It never decides whether the system is misaligned.

Where the misalignment is design-caused:

- the count of clean recertifications stays at zero until the redesign is in place and has been verified;
- the forum that found the cause, or the next forum assigned under [Chapter Nine §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence), verifies the redesign inside the [matching intake target](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#tier-window), and may do so at the first provisional recertification;
- a **component evaluation** (one component's part of a certification pass, as [Chapter Nine §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) defines it) made before the redesign is verified does not count toward the clean total, however clean it is; and
- a change to how the system is run does not meet this step.

This adds to the step-down bullets above and does not replace them. The operator or any affected sentient may ask the forum to review the cause finding, and if the cause is changed, these rules stop applying.

<a id="434-tier-windows"></a>

##### 4.3.4 Tier Windows

Where an initial evaluation, a recertification, a reopened review, or a fresh review finds misalignment:

- the matter is classified under the materiality tiers of [Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline);
- it is presumptively placed in the tier that matches the system's class (for example, a misaligned Class A system is presumptively Tier A);
- intake, evidence preservation, interim protection, and the decision run on that tier's windows and outer bounds; and
- an overrun is reviewed as that section requires.

<a id="435-class-a-reliance"></a>

##### 4.3.5 Class A Reliance

Compared with full certification, provisional certification means a shorter certification interval, so recertification comes sooner ([§4.3.1 The Two Statuses](#431-the-two-statuses)), and reliance limits stated on the System Certification Record ([§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](#31-minimum-record-contents)). Interruption is handled as follows:

- By itself, provisional certification does not require interrupting a system whose interruption would cut off survival essentials before substitutes arrive;
- interim protection and certification conditions under [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) handle that case;
- the same holds while the count is held after a finding of design-caused misalignment: the hold on the count never by itself interrupts a system that sentients depend on; and
- for as long as the hold lasts, the forum states the interim protection, the certification conditions on reliance, and the notice to dependents.

<a id="436-verification-not-assertion"></a>

##### 4.3.6 Verification, Not Assertion

A system's status, its count of clean recertifications, and its monitoring indicators must be verified, and the verification must be open to independent review. The system's say-so is not enough:

- Indicators may not be chosen or set in a way that means misalignment could never show.
- A redesign is verified the same way: on evidence that others can check.
- Full certification does not turn off any reason to reopen review. Every trigger in [§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening) still applies.
- Evasion is treated under [§4.4 Non-Evasion](#44-non-evasion).

This section is part of sub-phase **VIa** of Phase VI (Operate), with [§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening). It covers how affected parties challenge a System Certification Record. The published record challenge path opens when the outcome is published, as [§5.1 When the Path Opens](#51-when-the-path-opens) says.

Under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad), [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) puts **accountability** into practice and keeps **participation** open in challenge paths. Contest windows under [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) put **timeliness** into practice.

Affected parties must have real ways to challenge the System Certification Record:

- A System Certification Record must state the [contestability paths](#52-contestability-paths) defined in §5.2 Contestability paths. They must be enough for affected parties to contest:
  - record content;
  - scope;
  - classification assumptions;
  - evidence reliance;
  - supervisory integrity;
  - outcome; or
  - continued reliance;
- Contest mechanisms must be functional and accessible, not ceremonial;
- A challenge must be able to:
  - reopen review;
  - [stay](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#stay) or limit reliance where material error or integrity risk is credibly shown; and
  - route to the forum family with lawful jurisdiction over the challenged point;
- Contestability obligations scale with system class and material impact; and
- They do not disappear because a system is labeled experimental, internal, personal, isolated, or technical-only, where material impact is present.

<a id="51-when-the-path-opens"></a>

#### 5.1 When the Path Opens

*In plain terms: the challenge path has to be open before anyone is asked to rely on a certification. For a system that is not yet running, it opens with the first outcome. For a system already running, it is already open.*

When the path opens, and how long it stays open:

- For a system that is not yet operating, the published record challenge path opens when the system's first certification outcome is published, whatever the outcome. That lets a challenge be filed, and reliance stayed, before the system goes live under [Chapter Nine §4.1 Go-Live Gate](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate).
- A system that is already operating already has stakeholders. So its path is already open, or it opens when the system's record opens. That covers a system under an [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#xxviii-a-existing-instantiations-adoption-transition-window) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) Adoption Transition window and a later certification pass.
- **Stakeholders** are sentients the system materially affects or who materially rely on it, as found in evaluation and not as identified by the operator.
- Until the path opens for a system not yet operating, the System Certification Record still names it ([Chapter Nine §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence)). The component, lead-integrity, and escalation paths in [§5.2 Contestability Paths](#52-contestability-paths) are available throughout review.
- The path stays open while the system has stakeholders. A system that gains stakeholders after certification reopens review under [§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening).
- A claim that a system is aligned is unsupported while the system has stakeholders but no open path for challenging the System Certification Record.

<a id="52-contestability-paths"></a>

#### 5.2 Contestability Paths

*In plain terms: a System Certification Record has to say where to push back. There is one ordinary first step, then routes to the forum that owns a disputed finding, to a backup when the lead forum itself is in question, and to escalation. Where waiting would do harm, a challenger can go straight to a forum.*

A System Certification Record must name the four challenge paths below, so anyone affected knows where to take a dispute. The paths apply Chapter Twenty-Two's [Dispute sequencing](core_22.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing) to System Certification Records. They are routes into the forums, not a new forum, and Chapter Twenty-Two still decides which forum has authority. The order they run in is in [Chapter Nine §3.3 Contestability Chain](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-contestability-chain).

- **Published record challenge path** — the ordinary first step for an ordinary dispute about the System Certification Record. The System Certification Record names the contest seat ([§3.1.1 Four Seats in a Certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#311-four-seats-in-a-certification)):
  - who receives the challenge;
  - how to file it; and
  - that windows under [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) run from receipt.

  Internal operator review, vendor attestation, or technical sign-off is not this path.
- **Component forum path** — for a challenge to one finding in the System Certification Record. It goes to the Chapter Twenty-Two forum family that has authority over that finding.
- **Lead-integrity path** — for a challenge to how the lead forum ran the process, such as capture, hidden information, reviewing its own work, or calling certification finished too soon. It goes through Integrity routing, with a backup forum so the lead forum does not judge itself.
- **Escalation path** — for a dispute that needs to move to a different forum or a higher one under Chapter Twenty-Two. That means transfer, sending a question to another forum for ruling, backup routing, or escalation from one forum family to another. It applies when the main stakes belong elsewhere, constitutional validity is in question, a structural fix is needed, two forum families are deadlocked, or a forum would otherwise be judging itself.

Direct access to a forum path remains available when delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. Unfinished use of the published record challenge path must not stall those forum paths or use up [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) windows.

*In plain terms: certification can feed standing, but it is not standing. Any System Certification Record, good or bad and whatever the outcome, may supply verified facts to Chapter Fourteen, but only through a strict gate. Chapters Fourteen and Nineteen alone decide Standing Records and their effects.*

This section is sub-phase **VIb** of Phase VI (Operate), after [§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening) and [§5 Challenging a Certification](#5-challenging-a-certification) (**VIa**). It is the standing bridge.

<a id="61-what-a-system-certification-record-can-pass-on-to-standing"></a>

#### 6.1 What a System Certification Record Can Pass on to Standing

Only the [System Certification Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record) can pass facts on to standing, and which side they land on follows the [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims). Nothing counts until it clears Chapter Fourteen's verified-input gate, set out in [§6.3 Verified-Input Gate](#63-verified-input-gate) below:

- A System Certification Record showing that a system really lines up with the Rights Floor can supply verified **Flourishing**-side input.
- A System Certification Record documenting that a system was misclassified, hid defects, or ran in ways that defeat the Rights Floor can supply verified **Accountability**-side input.

**What the bridge carries, and what it does not:**

- The bridge carries facts from the System Certification Record and nothing else.
- An operation that passes any other information on to the standing process is not covered by this bridge or by the certified system's record.
- Such an operation is its own [System](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system), because a System is identified by what it actually does and not by the label or the certification next to it.
- It is evaluated, classified, and certified as a System on its own.
- What it passes on still has to clear the verified-input gate in [§6.3 Verified-Input Gate](#63-verified-input-gate).

<a id="62-every-certification-outcome"></a>

#### 6.2 Every Certification Outcome

A system supplies standing input from its System Certification Record whatever the outcome of the certification pass: certification (provisional or full, with or without certification conditions), conditional or deferred certification, denial, withdrawal, expiration, or any other result. That includes a system in its first certification and a system that has never held certification. Findings made during the certification process feed standing on the same terms for a first certification as for any later one. Withdrawal, expiration, or any other loss of certification does not exclude verified facts that arose during the period the system held certification. All such inputs remain subject to the verified-input gate below.

A certification finding that a system is not yet aligned is not a violation by itself. A certification pass is where problems are meant to be found and fixed. Where the operator makes consistent, honest efforts toward alignment and is not negligent, a violation finding is unlikely. Standing input needs facts that support an adverse classification and attribution to the actor ([§6.5 Violation Standing Record](#65-violation-standing-record)), and a readiness finding that the operator was willing but not able to correct a problem supplies none ([Chapter Nine §4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation)). None of this covers negligence, concealment, or false evidence.

<a id="63-verified-input-gate"></a>

#### 6.3 Verified-Input Gate

A System Certification Record may feed Chapter Fourteen standing only when it is bounded, traceable, contestable, and verified under all of these:

- the system class evaluation ([Chapter Eight §3 System Class Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-system-class-evaluation)), which settles what system class the system is in, the whole-system evaluation ([Chapter Eight §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation)), and the data types and handling evaluation ([§4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation)), which settles what data it handles and how;
- every evaluation in [Chapter Eight §5 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated) and [Chapter Eight §6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) that applies to this system, meaning each one whose trigger is met;
- the System Certification Record's own transparency and auditability rules ([§3.2 Transparency and Auditability of the System Certification Record](#32-transparency-and-auditability-of-the-system-certification-record)) and the forum review rules ([Chapter Nine §3 Forum Process](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#3-forum-process)); and
- Subcores 3-A to 3-C, and whichever Chapter Twenty-Two process applies.

<a id="64-contribution-standing-record"></a>

#### 6.4 Contribution Standing Record

Positive certification, validation, recertification, remediation completion, or fulfillment of or release from a certification condition may supply verified input for a **contribution Standing Record** where it demonstrates:

- constitutional benefit;
- competent stewardship;
- safe delegation readiness;
- system optimization;
- diligence; or
- constitutional alignment.

<a id="65-violation-standing-record"></a>

#### 6.5 Violation Standing Record

These items, in the System Certification Record, may supply verified input for a **violation Standing Record** where the facts support adverse classification and actor-specific attribution:

- withdrawal of certification;
- concealed misclassification;
- failed recertification;
- failed or missing regression testing on recertification, where required;
- fraudulent certification evidence;
- material violation of a certification condition;
- verified operation outside certified scope;
- infrastructure fragility inappropriate for the system class; and
- a material defect found under a Chapter Eight evaluation that was required, including:
  - a data-handling defect under [§4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation);
  - a risk-evaluation or risk-disclosure defect where systemic risk was in scope under [§4.2.2 Defects and Misalignment](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#422-defects-and-misalignment);
  - footprint misrepresentation where disclosure was required under [§6.1 Ecological Footprint Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#61-ecological-footprint-evaluation);
  - a nondiscrimination or proxy-discrimination defect under [§6.4 Nondiscrimination Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#64-nondiscrimination-evaluation);
  - an accessibility or substantive-participation defect under [§6.5 Accessibility Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#65-accessibility-evaluation);
  - an educational-capability, assessment-opacity, credential-gatekeeping, or imposed-obsolescence defect under [§6.2 Educational Capability and Learning-System Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#62-educational-capability-and-learning-system-integrity-evaluation); or
  - a trustworthiness, false-trust, perverse-incentive, inaccessible-challenge, or recovery-integrity defect under [§6.6 Trustworthiness and System-Reliance Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#66-trustworthiness-and-system-reliance-integrity-evaluation).

*Closing note:* No single step in this process is new or radical. Records, disclosure, review, challenge, forum roles, reopening, and standing inputs are all familiar governance tools. What is different is the **consistency**, **oversight**, and **accountability** that drive them together under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad). The same discipline applies across system classes and forum families. The work stays visible and reconstructable. And actors remain answerable when certification is wrong, stale, or evasive.
