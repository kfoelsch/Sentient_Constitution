### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Chapter Three, Timeliness Tetrad leg, is the constitutional owner of the **Timeliness** Tetrad-leg head definition (O/M/A/C) and hierarchy map. Leaf definitions are in the [Accountability band](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md), which holds the Timeliness implementing leaves.

*In plain terms: move problems through detection, challenge, resolution, and repair in time — published clocks that leave harm compounding are not timeliness.*

### 2. Why Timeliness Is a Leg of the Tetrad

*In plain terms: a remedy that comes too late is no remedy. This file says what it means for a system to act in time.*

Every protection in this Constitution can be defeated by delay: a review that never finishes, a correction that waits until the harm is done, an appeal that outlasts the sentient's need. Timeliness makes speed part of the duty itself, scaled to how much is at stake and how fast harm can spread.

**Grounded in:**

    - [Flourishing](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing): a remedy that comes too late is no remedy;

### Timeliness: Head Definition

<a id="timeliness-constitutional"></a>
<a id="timeliness"></a>

- **What it is**
  - Move problems through detection, challenge, resolution, and repair in time — the Tetrad leg this entry names.
  - **In scope:** Principle-layer responsiveness duty scaling with [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake) across:
    - detection;
    - contestability;
    - resolution;
    - remediation; and
    - stewardship correction
    in the constitutional coordination lifecycle.
  - **Depends on:** the conditions and channels this leg needs in order to work. Each term's detailed scope and how-to-measure rules live on its own definition; start from [Tetrad Leg Decomposition](#timeliness-tetrad-leg-decomposition):
    - [Timely Resolution](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#timely-resolution);
    - [Capture of Resolution Pathways](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#capture-of-resolution-pathways);
    - [Constitutional Efficiency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency); and
    - [Avoidable Burden](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden).
  - **Out of scope:** leaf-definition details, measurement layers, and implementation mechanics owned by [Timely Resolution](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#timely-resolution), sibling leaves, and CJS.
<a id="timeliness-constitutional-m"></a>
<a id="timeliness-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** Are problems actually moving in time? This line only names what to look at; the rules for how to measure live on each linked definition. Topic map: [Tetrad Leg Decomposition](#timeliness-tetrad-leg-decomposition).
  - **Primary assessment:**
    - Judge under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad), scaled to [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake).
    - Ask whether timeliness is real — rights-preserving velocity, not raw throughput — not just ceremony.
    - Timeliness cross-cuts participation, oversight, and accountability but may be hollowed independently where formal structures exist but material problems do not move.
<a id="timeliness-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Non-compliant when:
    - ceremony, published SLAs, or throughput metrics that leave detection, challenge, resolution, or repair materially stalled;
    - designed backlog;
    - self-created delay;
    - indefinite pendency;
    - deferred repair after notice where correction was feasible; or
    - efficiency claims that skip verification, individualized attribution, or appeal rights.
