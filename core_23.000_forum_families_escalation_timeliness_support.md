<a id="1-purpose-role-and-scope"></a>
### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: Chapter Twenty-Two says how a dispute is supervised, placed, opened, and moved. This chapter says who the forums are, how a matter climbs from one forum to another, how fast it must be resolved, and what support the forums get.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Twenty-Three defines the six forum families, the paths by which a matter escalates and a question goes for ruling, the clocks that keep disputes moving, and the support forums need before, during, and after review.

**Role:** Where this chapter sits:
- It works with [Chapter Twenty-Two](core_22.000_forum.md#chapter-twenty-two-standards-routing-and-forum-cases), which supervises forums, sets the default venue, opens forum cases, and moves matters between forums. The two chapters are read together.
- Its clocks in [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) are the single clock table that other chapters point to.
- How outcomes are carried out is in [Chapter Twenty-Four](core_24.000_enforcement.md#chapter-twenty-four-enforcement).

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:
- forum family definitions, internal chambers, shared standards, and provisional operational law (**section 3**);
- escalation, questions for ruling, interim protection, and sentience-status adjudication (**section 4**);
- timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline (**section 5**); and
- forum support before, during, and after review (**section 6**).

<a id="2-why-forum-families-escalation-and-clocks-exist"></a>
### 2. Why Forum Families, Escalation, and Clocks Exist

*In plain terms: a forum is only useful if it has the right expertise, can pass hard questions upward, and decides in time. This chapter exists to make sure all three are true.*

Different disputes need different expertise, and some questions are too large for the forum that first hears them. Delay can also defeat a right as surely as a wrong decision. This chapter sets the forum families and their fields, the paths by which a matter escalates or a question goes for ruling, the clocks that keep cases moving, and the support forums need to decide well.

<a id="31-sentient-forums"></a>

#### 3.1 Sentient Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Sentient Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** character is:
- **private** or **community** obligations;
- **civil** harms;
- **restoration**;
- **local** or **online** community norms;
- community-governance participation among sentients, members, residents, users, households, associations, neighborhoods, localities, regional communities, online communities, or comparable community formations.
- The matter must **not** require **final** resolution of **constitutional** validity, **institutional** mandate, ecological merits, technical-governance administration, or integrity failure as the main question.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- community-level exclusion;
- access to shared community process;
- local restorative obligations;
- moderation or membership decisions in non-institutional online communities;
- neighborhood or association self-governance;
- participatory-budget inputs;
- commons-use obligations;
- outcomes of community restorative or peer-mediation processes;
- comparable community self-governance records where the matter remains primarily interpersonal, community-restorative, or locally normative.

**Community governance boundary.** Community governance bodies themselves are not a separate **forum family** under this chapter.
- Their official records, recommendations, delegated decisions, or omissions become matters for **Sentient Forums** only where the **primary** stake fits this subsection, under [Dispute sequencing](core_22.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing).

<a id="32-technical-forum-domains"></a>

#### 3.2 Technical Forum Domains

**Primary stakes.** **Technical Forum Domains** are the **default** **lead** **family** where the **primary** stake matches the **Technical Forum Domains** row in **Chapter Twenty-Two section 4**. That includes:
- technical-governance procedure;
- expert-evidence standards;
- scientific, engineering, medical, or comparable knowledge-governance administration within adopted scope;
- technical standards;
- expert administrative procedure;
- evidence stewardship;
- advice to an Institutional Forum on the privacy and security of record custody, key handling, and audit when an adopter sets a level of trust ([Chapter Nineteen §3.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#34-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust));
- textbook or curriculum integrity;
- standards governance;
- bounded uncertainty-reduction relevant to adjudication or regulation;
- **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) sentience-status determinations whose primary stake is indicator evaluation, expert evidence, or bounded uncertainty about whether an entity is a **sentient** under **Chapter Three** (*Sentience Status Adjudication*).

**Standards custody.** **Technical Forum Domains** maintain reviewable standards used to operationalize [Preamble §2 Measurements Overview](core_00.000_preamble.md#2-measurements-overview) measurement categories and [Chapter Three](core_03.000_definitions_home.md#chapter-three-foundational-definitions) definition homes — including standards used to assess system alignment — within lawful scope:
- measurement methods;
- test protocols;
- expert-evidence standards;
- technical specifications;
- evidentiary thresholds;
- domain-specific standards.
- They may answer technical questions for ruling for a **System or Institutional Certification Record**.
- They do **not** issue the final official constitutional alignment determination or ongoing validation unless another provision independently assigns that primary stake to them.

<a id="62-shared-standards-and-anti-displacement"></a>

**Shared standards and anti-displacement.** The default approach for enforcement of constitutional law is **shared standards with decentralized enforcement**: technical forums maintain the reviewable cross-family standards under this subsection; the lead forum family for the dispute applies them under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 4**. Forum families must not use technical specialization to displace ordinary constitutional, institutional, integrity, environment, or sentient routing where the primary issue is rights, mandate, liability, remedy, or ecological merits outside those specialized functions.

**Specialized chambers.** Adopting documents may establish **science**, **engineering**, **medicine**, or other technical forums as **specialized chambers or designated panels** within the existing forum families — **not** as wholly separate families. Their functions may include:
- issuing reviewable standards, methods, and evidentiary guidance for scientific, technical, clinical, or other expert claims used across forum families;
- hearing disputes whose primary stake is expert administrative procedure, evidence stewardship, accreditation-equivalent custody, publicly governed curricular or textbook integrity, standards governance, or comparable knowledge-governance questions;
- commissioning or directing bounded uncertainty-reduction work where material unresolved technical questions prevent reliable adjudication, classification, or regulatory integrity.

**Provisional operational law.** The **provisional implementation operational law** framework in **section 3.7** applies to **specialized technical forums** within their lawful scope.

<a id="33-institutional-forums"></a>

#### 3.3 Institutional Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Institutional Forums** hear disputes in which **at least one institution** is a **necessary party**, or in which the **primary** stake is:
- what an institution is allowed to do;
- what it is charged to do;
- the scope it is supervised under;
- how it is classified under adopted documents;
- measurement under adopted documents;
- whether it followed its institutional duties under **[CI-6.205](corpus_institutions/ci_06.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-6205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody)** (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), **[CI-8.210](corpus_institutions/ci_08.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-8210-classification-linked-institutional-obligations)** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), and cognate layers.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- institutional mandate, permitted action, or charged function;
- supervised-scope boundaries and classification under adopted documents;
- [Institutional Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) scope, Institutional Charter amendment authority, Institutional Charter–behavior mismatch, or operation outside chartered scope;
- institutional-duty compliance and performance under **[CI-6.205](corpus_institutions/ci_06.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-6205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody)** (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), **[CI-8.210](corpus_institutions/ci_08.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-8210-classification-linked-institutional-obligations)** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), and cognate layers;
- local enforcement of shared cross-jurisdiction or cross-institution standards;
- the level of trust an adopter extends to another adopter, as challenged by a sentient under that adopter's authority and not by the other adopter ([Chapter Nineteen §3.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#34-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust));
- institutional-mandate and Rights-Floor component questions in system-alignment proceedings where an institution is a necessary party or institutional mandate is primary.

**Local enforcement.** Where shared cross-jurisdiction or cross-institution standards apply, **Institutional Forums** are the default **local enforcement** forum unless primary-stakes routing places the matter elsewhere.

**Alignment components.** **Institutional Forums** hold reviewable institutional-mandate and Rights-Floor component authority in system-alignment certification and related proceedings where an institution is a necessary party, the operator or steward is institutional, or institutional mandate or supervised compliance is the primary stake.
- **Integrity** forums remain the default official lead for whole-system and institutional constitutional alignment certification and validation.
- Component detail for those proceedings lives in [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) and must **implement, not narrow**, this allocation.

When a certification review finds misalignment or a defect, the **Integrity** lead routes the remediation-readiness finding through [Chapter Nine §4.3 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#43-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct) and, for an Institution, [Chapter Twelve §4.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#44-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution). The lead forum decides readiness; Technical Forums supply capacity evidence and Institutional Forums supply governance and incentive evidence where the cause lies there. The operator or steward supplies evidence but does not decide its own readiness. The readiness finding, proof step, and any denial or revocation remain on the System or Institutional Certification Record and follow the Chapter Nine process for a System or the Chapter Eleven process for an Institution; this creates no separate standing or merits track.

**Provisional operational law.** The **provisional implementation operational law** framework in **section 3.7** applies to **Institutional Forums** within their lawful scope.

**Internal process boundary.** This subsection applies [**Dispute sequencing**](core_22.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing) under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 4** to **Institutional** forums. A lawful institutional internal integrity process may precede **Integrity** forums under **section 3.5** (*Family sequencing*) where independence and contest safeguards hold.
- Internal process labels do **not** displace **Institutional** forum merits on mandate, supervised scope, classification, or duty-compliance stakes.
- They do **not** displace **Integrity** forums for final integrity determinations, cross-institution deadlock, pattern claims, or capture-sensitive review.

<a id="34-environment-forums"></a>

#### 3.4 Environment Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Environment Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** stake is:
- **ecological integrity**;
- **environmental preconditions**;
- **lifecycle** or **systemic** ecological **harm**;
- **restoration** or **remediation** of **shared** ecological **systems**;
- **accountability** for **attributable** environmental **burdens**;
- comparable **ecological** **merits**.
- This includes **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure where **Chapter Fifteen** measurement, **Chapter Four** Rights-Floor enforcement, or **Chapter Three** (*Ecological Integrity*, *Environmental Preconditions*) depends on that determination.

**Who can be a party.** Anyone can be a party to an ecological case, not only systems, and an Environment Forum must also take part in reviewing any system whose alignment materially affects the environment.

**Representative filing.** A case whose primary stake is [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing) or ecological integrity may be opened by a published representative — an affected community, indigenous-continuity holder, or designated guardian — in the life-supporting system's own interest. This does not make the system a sentient. Appointment, recusal, and publication of representatives live in [**CF-22.415**](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-routing-operations-transfer-questions-for-ruling-and-representative-treatment) (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*).

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- ecological integrity or environmental-precondition baselines and violations;
- restoration or remediation of shared ecological systems;
- attributable environmental burdens, including ecological-footprint attribution where material;
- lifecycle effects, emissions, land or water impacts, biodiversity effects, waste streams, or remediation obligations;
- pattern or systemic ecological failure material to classification, alignment certification, recertification, or Rights-Floor enforcement;
- environmental-alignment component review for systems with material ecological exposure.

**Alignment components.** **Environment Forums** hold the reviewable environmental-alignment component for systems whose operation, dependency map, resource use, lifecycle effects, emissions, land or water impacts, biodiversity effects, waste streams, remediation obligations, or failure modes materially implicate ecological integrity or environmental preconditions — including where material ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk is present.
- Within their ecological jurisdiction, they may issue:
  - environmental-alignment approval;
  - conditional approval;
  - objection;
  - remediation requirements; or
  - release-from-condition findings.
- **Integrity** forums remain the default official lead for whole-system and institutional constitutional alignment certification and validation.
- Where material ecological exposure exists, timely Environment Forum environmental-alignment findings are required component determinations; sequencing with Integrity lead coordination is governed by **section 3.5**.
- Component detail for those proceedings lives in [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) and must **implement, not narrow**, this allocation.

**Provisional operational law.** The **provisional implementation operational law** framework in **section 3.7** applies to **Environment Forums** within their lawful scope.

<a id="35-integrity-forums"></a>

#### 3.5 Integrity Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Integrity Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** stake is:
- integrity of office;
- process;
- contest pathways;
- disclosure;
- conflict rules;
- anti-capture duties;
- related procedure and governance integrity.
- This includes **pattern** or **systemic** integrity failure across institutions where **Chapter Fifteen** impact-slot measurement, a corresponding **Chapter Twenty** ACMD for a slot 7–9 record, or **Rights-Floor** enforcement depends on that determination.
- It also includes official **constitutional alignment certification** or **ongoing alignment validation** for systems and institutions, and a **Chapter Twenty** designation as the **primary** stake, as assigned in **Chapter Twenty-Two section 3**.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- office, process, or contest-pathway integrity;
- disclosure, conflict-rule, or anti-capture violations;
- concealment, capture, retaliation, or comparable process abuse;
- pattern or systemic integrity failure across institutions or systems;
- Chapter Twenty ACMD where it is the primary stake;
- official system-alignment and institutional-alignment certification, validation, and recertification.

**Family sequencing.** Related matters may start in the nearest track with jurisdiction, use a lawful institutional internal integrity process first where safeguards hold, and keep one **Integrity** lead record for **alignment** coordination — without displacing other families' **primary-stakes** merits.
- **Sentient** first may apply when peer disputes can be fully resolved without final institutional or constitutional determination; institutional or constitutional aspects may be stayed or split with explicit preclusion rules.
- **Institutional** internal integrity process may precede **Integrity** forums where independence and contest safeguards meet the expectations of:
  - **Chapter Fourteen**;
  - **Chapter Four** (Foundational Rights);
  - **[CI-6.210](corpus_institutions/ci_06.210_conflict_integrity_anti_capture_anti_corruption.md#ci-6210-conflict-integrity-anti-capture-and-anti-corruption)** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*); and
  - **[CI-22.205](corpus_institutions/ci_22.205_procedure_integrity_contestability_secondary_review.md#ci-22205-procedure-integrity-contestability-and-secondary-review)** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*).
- **Integrity** forums remain available for:
  - final integrity determinations;
  - cross-institution deadlock; and
  - pattern claims.

**Alignment rulings.** Where necessary to resolve a matter within this family's jurisdiction, **Integrity Forums** may issue **alignment rulings** that:
- diagnose interlocking **misalignment** among processes, systems, institutions, or contest pathways — including dependencies, choke-points, concealment or capture risk, and remedial sequencing;
- state binding integrity findings on those points on the Forum Case Record before that forum.

**Constitutional alignment certification and validation.** **Integrity Forums** are the default official forum for certifying whether a new materially impactful system is constitutionally aligned enough to operate within its claimed scope, and for periodically validating whether existing systems remain aligned as their behavior, scale, dependency, incentives, or risk profile changes. They hold the same role for an Institution under [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eleven-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation), where the forum body itself is an Institution and the anti-self-judging rules of **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6** apply to it ([Chapter Twelve §3.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions)).
- They apply technical-forum specifications, methods, and expert evidence where material, but their judgment also includes constitutional, institutional, ecological, rights, anti-capture, and remediation considerations.
- The **Integrity** forum remains the official lead forum for the System or Institutional Certification Record unless primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or certification assigns a component question elsewhere.
- Where material ecological exposure exists, the **Environment** forum family's environmental-alignment component determination is required before final certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions. Lead **Integrity** coordination must not finalize certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions while a timely Environment Forum objection, remediation condition, or environmental question for ruling remains unresolved.
- Certification and validation must be evidence-based, contestable, and traceable under **Subcores 3-A to 3-C**, **Chapter Fourteen**, **Chapter Four**, and **[CS-8.315](corpus_systems/cs_08.315_system_alignment_certification_run_sheet.md#cs-8315-system-alignment-certification-run-sheet)** (*System alignment certification run sheet*).
- Outcomes may include:
  - certification;
  - conditional certification;
  - remediation;
  - suspension or constraint recommendations within lawful scope;
  - referral to another forum family; or
  - certification to **Constitutional** forums where constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy is materially at issue.
- Component detail for those proceedings lives in [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) and must **implement, not narrow**, this allocation.

**Supervisory coordination.** An **Integrity** forum that issues an **alignment** ruling retains lead responsibility for one coordinated record for that proceeding, as **Chapter Twenty-Two section 5** provides, and must manage neutral coordination — including stays, sequencing, status review, and implementation milestones — until alignment remediation is achieved or the forum lawfully closes supervision.
- Coordination must not convert the forum into party advocacy.
- Coordination must not displace other forums' jurisdiction or preempt final merits on non-integrity primary questions reserved to **Constitutional**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **specialized technical**, or **Sentient** forums.
- Conflicting simultaneous orders must be resolved through published coordination rules, including stays and sequencing in the alignment ruling or implementation text, consistent with **section 5** and **CF-22.425** (*Integrity Safeguards, Anti-Capture Operations, and Anti-Self-Judging Support*).

**Component referral.** Discrete sub-issues that belong primarily in another forum family under primary-stakes routing must be referred, sent for ruling, or stayed as adopting documents provide.
- Component matters referred or sent for ruling to other families remain with those forums for primary-stakes merits.
- Order among competing component issues follows published priority criteria, without opaque threshold sorting, that must account for:
  - Rights-Floor urgency;
  - irreversible-harm risk;
  - measurement or Chapter Fourteen or Eleven dependency;
  - evidentiary decay or preservation need; and
  - practical resolution sequence.

**Remediation framing.** An alignment ruling may establish a reasoned remediation menu, implementation options, or sequencing requirements within lawful scope.
- Such elements are binding only to the extent the ruling expressly states they are binding and they are consistent with assigned jurisdiction elsewhere.

**Boundary with provisional operational law.** Alignment rulings do not substitute for provisional implementation operational law under **section 3.7** for **Institutional**, **Environment**, or **specialized technical** forums.
- Where general normative effect beyond case-specific or pattern-specific integrity remediation is materially at stake, **section 4** governs certification, escalation, and constitutional disposition.

<a id="36-constitutional-forums"></a>

#### 3.6 Constitutional Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Constitutional Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** stake is:
- the validity and meaning of **constitutional** provisions;
- **structural** remedies that alter governance for **classes** of actors or systems;
- questions for ruling from other families;
- action **beyond lawful authority** or **supremacy** where **constitutional** text is enough by itself to decide the issue sent for ruling.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- constitutional validity or meaning binding all interpreters;
- class-wide structural remedy required by constitutional text;
- questions for ruling escalated from other forum families under **section 4**;
- beyond-lawful-authority or supremacy determinations where constitutional text alone decides the issue.

**Provisional-law disposition.** **Constitutional Forums** dispose of provisional implementation-operational-law rulings issued under **section 3.7** by:
- **accepting** them (giving **registered** or **precedential** effect as provided);
- **rejecting** them (withdrawing **general** effect except as **fairness** to **parties** may require); or
- **hearing** the question on **full** merits.

Granular publication, docketing, and effect **pending** disposition remain in **[CF-22.445](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-forum-performance-backlog-requirements-publication-timeliness-and-accessibility)** (*Forum Performance, Backlog Requirements, Publication Timeliness, and Accessibility*) (publication and timeliness) and **[CF-22.465](corpus_forum/cf_22.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-22465-standard-records-forms-and-evidence-artifacts)** (*Standard Records, Forms, and Evidence Artifacts*) (standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts). Where the operational question cannot be separated from constitutional validity, meaning, or structural remedy, **section 4** governs questions for ruling and escalation.

<a id="37-provisional-implementation-operational-law"></a>

#### 3.7 Provisional Implementation Operational Law

<a id="51-provisional-implementation-operational-law"></a>
<a id="5-shared-family-architecture"></a>

The following **single** framework applies to **Institutional Forums**, **Environment Forums**, and **specialized technical forums** within each type's lawful scope. It does not replace **Integrity** alignment rulings under **section 3.5**.

Where **necessary** to resolve a matter within jurisdiction, the forum may issue **provisional** rulings on **implementation operational law** — interpretation, coordination, or orderly application of **adopted** implementation documents — as **general** operational doctrine. **Subject matter** depends on the forum type:
- **Institutional Forums:** **institutional** duties under **[CI-6.205](corpus_institutions/ci_06.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-6205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody)** (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), **[CI-8.210](corpus_institutions/ci_08.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-8210-classification-linked-institutional-obligations)** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), and cognate layers, together with related implementation documents.
- **Environment Forums:** **environmental** operational rules in cognate layers governing **ecological integrity**, **environmental preconditions**, **restoration**, **remediation**, **attribution**, and comparable **environmental** **merits**.
- **Specialized technical forums:** **scientific**, **technical**, **clinical**, **engineering**, **medical**, or **knowledge-governance** standards and procedures, **cross-family** technical administration, and related implementation documents.

**Constitutional disposition** of these rulings is owned by **Constitutional Forums** under **section 3.6**. **Sending a question for ruling** when operational and constitutional stakes merge is governed by **section 4**.

*In plain terms: when the case outgrows the first desk, these rules say how to escalate — for example when an institution must be joined, integrity dominates, or a deep constitutional validity question appears. They explain how questions for ruling reach **Constitutional** forums, how existential-risk and expert disputes stay contestable, and how interim orders can freeze irreversible harm while routing and rulings finish. They also set the narrow hold a forum may place on one sentient's authority while the facts are checked.*

#### Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums

A matter can leave its default lead forum in five ways. In this section, "escalation" means family-to-family escalation only, and the five together are called **moves between forums**:

| Move | What happens | When | Rules in |
|------|--------------|------|----------|
| **Transfer or consolidation** | A matter, or related matters, go to one lead forum. The case keeps its Forum Case Record. | The default venue under [Subcore 22-A §5 Default Venue Table](subcore_22.010_forum_intake_default_venue.md#5-default-venue-table) no longer fits, or related matters need one lead. | **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6**, and [CF-22.415 §12 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-12-transfer-procedure) (*Transfer Procedure*). |
| **Referral** | One component issue goes to the family that must decide it. It stays with that family for primary-stakes merits, and the lead keeps its own part of the case. | A component question belongs primarily to another family. | **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6** (*Integrity-led alignment coordination*), and [Referral Distinguished from Expansion](#referral-distinguished-from-expansion). |
| **Question for ruling** | The lead family sends a question of constitutional meaning, validity, or structural effect to **Constitutional** forums. | The matter cannot be resolved without that question. | [Sending Questions to Constitutional Forums for Ruling](#sending-questions-to-constitutional-forums-for-ruling). |
| **Family-to-family escalation** | The case moves to a different forum family. | The receiving family has become the better merits forum under primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or a constitutional question sent for ruling. | [Family-to-Family Escalation](#family-to-family-escalation). |
| **Backup routing** | An independent backup forum or external review path hears the merits. | The lead family cannot judge its own matter: documented recusal, capture, deadlock, unavailability, or inability to constitute an independent panel. | **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6** (*Cross-forum anti-self-judging rule*), and [Backup Routing under the Anti-Self-Judging Rule](#backup-routing-under-the-anti-self-judging-rule). |

Three points keep the moves apart:

- Every move that changes the lead keeps the same case and its Forum Case Record, and is carried out as one automated step that updates the docket portal and sends alerts ([CF-22.415 §12 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-12-transfer-procedure)).
- A **backup forum** is the independent forum family or external review path that hears the merits when the lead family cannot judge its own matter. **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6** names the independent lead and the backup for each family, and the Backup Routing subsection below says when backup routing starts.
- Appeal and secondary review are not moves between forums. They review a forum's own decision, in the Contest Seat under [Chapter Twenty-Two §5.2 Seats in a Forum Case](core_22.000_forum.md#52-seats-in-a-forum-case) and [CF-22.420](corpus_forum/cf_22.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-22420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*).

#### Family-to-Family Escalation

A case may move from one forum family to another only when the receiving family has become the better merits forum under **primary-stakes** routing, **anti-self-judging** protection, or a constitutional question sent for ruling.

- **Sentient to Institutional.**
  Escalate when:
  - an **institution** becomes a **necessary** party; or
  - effective **relief** **requires** **institutional** **power**.
- **Sentient or Institutional to Integrity.**
  Escalate when:
  - **integrity** becomes **primary**;
  - **institutional** process is **materially** **compromised**; or
  - **retaliation**, **concealment**, **capture**, or comparable **allegations** **warrant** an **independent** **merits** **forum**.
- **Sentient, Institutional, or Integrity to Environment.**
  Escalate when the primary issue becomes:
  - **ecological integrity**;
  - **environmental preconditions**; or
  - **restoration**, **remediation**, attributable **environmental** burdens, lifecycle or systemic ecological harm, or **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure that makes the **Environment** family the better merits forum under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 4**.
- **Any family to Constitutional.**
  Escalate only where applicable **Article XXI-A** (*Justice Objective and Scope*)-class **review** **safeguards** are preserved under the adopting documents and one of the following is present:
  - a **structural** question for ruling;
  - a **validity** question for ruling; or
  - a **conflict** among **lower** **panels** on a **constitutional** **point**.

#### Existential Risk and Uncertainty on the Forum Case Record

This subsection sets out how forums handle existential risk and uncertainty on the Forum Case Record:

- **Existential-risk handling:** Where a matter materially presents a credible claim of **Existential Risk**, survival-critical collapse, or irreversible loss of **Ecological Recovery Capacity**, the designated lead family under **primary-stakes** routing remains the merits forum **unless** another rule in this chapter requires **transfer**, **certification**, or **backup** activation. Existential-risk significance does **not** create a separate forum family.
- **Reasoned uncertainty treatment:** A forum must **not** reject an existential-risk claim solely because probability is hard to quantify. It must assess whether the causal pathway is credible under adversarial, aggregated, dependency-sensitive, or threshold conditions. It must state the uncertainty and evidentiary limits on the Forum Case Record.

#### Sending Questions to **Constitutional** Forums for Ruling

This subsection sets out when questions are sent to **Constitutional** forums for ruling:

- **Constitutional questions for ruling:** Where resolving such a matter requires determination of constitutional meaning, validity, or structural effect under **Safety**, **Truth**, **Article I** (*Environmental Survival*), or comparable long-horizon rights and constraint provisions, the lead family must send that question to **Constitutional** forums for ruling **under** **adopting** **documents** that **preserve** applicable review safeguards.
- **Provisional operational law:** Where a provisional implementation-operational-law ruling under **section 3.7** cannot be separated from constitutional validity, meaning, or structural remedy, the lead family must send the question for ruling or escalate under this section. Disposition of separable provisional rulings remains under **section 3.6**.
- **Alignment rulings and general doctrine:** Where an **Integrity** forum's **alignment** ruling **would** establish **general** **implementation** operational **doctrine** or **class-wide** **structural** rules **outside** **case-specific** or **pattern-specific** **integrity** **remediation**, the **lead** forum **must** **send** **for** **ruling** or **escalate** under **adopting** **documents** consistent with **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*) and **this** **section**. **Alignment** rulings **do** **not** **use** the **section 3.7** framework.
- **System certification and recertification:** A **forum case record** that certifies a new system as constitutionally aligned, imposes certification conditions, withdraws certification, or materially recertifys an existing system must state:
  - the system scope;
  - evidence basis;
  - classification assumptions;
  - dependency and risk profile;
  - unresolved uncertainty;
  - review cadence;
  - contest path; and
  - any referred or questions for ruling.
  Certification is not permanent authorization: material change, misalignment, concealed behavior, new dependency, new risk, or credible challenge reopens review under this chapter and **[CS-8.315](corpus_systems/cs_08.315_system_alignment_certification_run_sheet.md#cs-8315-system-alignment-certification-run-sheet)** (*System alignment certification run sheet*).

#### Technical Support and Preserved **Integrity** Escalation

This subsection sets out technical support for existential-risk matters and the preserved route to **Integrity** forums:

- **Technical contestability:** Where existential-risk determination depends on disputed scientific, engineering, ecological, medical, computational, or comparable expert questions, the lead family must obtain contestable technical support under **section 6** where forensic or analytical capacity is required. That support may run through:
  - a lawful specialist chamber;
  - a designated panel;
  - a certified findings process; or
  - an equivalent reviewable mechanism.
  Technical support must **not** silently displace a forum's jurisdiction.
- **Integrity escalation preserved:** Where the claim includes concealment, suppression of tail-risk evidence, manipulation of safety records, strategic uncertainty laundering, or comparable integrity violation, **Integrity** routing and escalation remain available under this chapter.

<a id="interim-protection"></a>
#### Interim Protection

This subsection sets out interim protection while a matter is pending:

- Any family with jurisdiction over the matter may issue interim relief necessary to:
  - prevent imminent irreversible harm;
  - preserve evidence under [Evidence Preservation](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation);
  - maintain **Ecological Recovery Capacity**;
  - stop material escalation while routing, certification, or technical review **is** completed; or
  - preserve **status quo** pending **merits**.
  Such relief must be reasoned, proportionate, and subject to prompt review inside the **prompt-review window** of [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*).
- Where **multiple** forums share jurisdiction, **one** coordinating forum or rule must resolve conflicts among simultaneous interim orders.
- Interim relief directed at one named sentient's exercise of one gated pathway, on less than a verified violation finding, is an [Interim Hold](#interim-hold-on-a-named-sentients-pathway) and follows the next subsection.

#### Interim Hold on a Named Sentient's Pathway

This subsection sets out the [Interim Hold](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#interim-hold), a measure between an allegation and a verified violation finding. It is interim protection directed at one named sentient's exercise of one named authority, pathway, or matter. It is not a violation finding, a lock, or a penalty.

- **Ground:** A forum with jurisdiction may issue a hold only where all of these are shown:
  - specific facts, disclosed to the sentient and verified to a preliminary evidence level, meaning documentary or independently attested, where a rumor, an unsupported accusation, or a reputation is not enough;
  - it is more likely than not that the risk is real;
  - the facts show a risk of serious harm to others, or of defeating an independent check on a body, a forum, or a certification, that cannot wait for the merits; and
  - a narrower safeguard would not be enough.

  The evidence level rises with how restrictive the hold is, as [Subcore 3-C](subcore_03.030_burden_traceability_verification.md#subcore-3-c-burden-of-proof-traceability-and-verification) (*Burden of Proof, Traceability, and Verification*) and [Chapter Two §13.1.5 Least-Restrictive, Time-Bounded, and Reviewable Constraint Principle](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle) require. A statement alone never meets the Interim Hold grounds: it counts only together with independent verified facts of conduct, control, or reward, and where speech is part of the basis for the Interim Hold it is shown by clear, verified evidence, with open doubt counted against the hold, as the highest scrutiny of [**Article XI-B** (*Expression*)](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-b-expression) requires.
- **Issuer:** The forum with jurisdiction under this chapter issues the hold. The subject's own body, appointing authority, family, or control line may not issue it, and a body never holds its own member out on its own view. A body that holds the facts refers them to the forum. Where the only forum is the subject's own, the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in [Chapter Twenty-Two section 6](core_22.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture) and backup routing apply.
- **Record:** Each hold states, in operative form:
  - the named sentient;
  - the named authority, pathway, or matter, and nothing wider;
  - the risk, and why it cannot wait for verification;
  - the protected interests;
  - the conditions that end the hold;
  - the review route; and
  - the lapse date.
- **Ladder:** The forum uses the lightest measure that works, in this order, and a heavier rung needs a showing that the lighter ones would not do:
  - disclosure and monitoring;
  - an independent co-holder or second key;
  - step-aside from one matter;
  - escrow or separate holding of the contested weight, funds, or keys; and
  - restraint on exercising the named authority.
- **Duration:** A hold lapses at the outer bound of the tier window in [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) for the merits matter it supports, counted from the day the hold issues. This subsection adds no second clock table. For a matter at the Emergency setting the bound is the Tier A outer bound (7 days), not the 3-day protective bound. The hold may be extended once, only on the continuation burden in [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden). It ends earlier when the facts are cleared, when a narrower safeguard is in place, or when the merits matter is decided. If no merits matter is opened inside the bound, the hold lapses with no violation finding.
- **Contest and review:** The sentient sees the Interim Hold grounds and the facts, may answer them, and may contest the hold. A forum outside the subject's control line and outside the issuing panel begins review inside the **prompt-review window** of [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*) and decides it before the hold lapses.
- **No violation finding, no standing effect:** A hold is not evidence that the allegation is true and supports no adverse inference. It is not a [Standing Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#standing-record), a Chapter Fourteen input, or a Chapter Twenty designation, and only the merits process verifies the facts ([Chapter Fourteen §4.7 Forum Boundary](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary)).
- **No re-issue:** After a hold lapses or ends, no new hold issues on the same or substantially the same facts without new verified facts.
- **Lock and time:** A hold is never converted into a standing lock. If the merits process verifies a violation finding, the lock rules apply from the finding, and time spent under a hold on the same gated pathway counts toward the lock's time bound as [Chapter Eighteen §4.6 Interim Holds and Locks](core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#46-interim-holds-and-locks) provides.
- **If the hold was wrong:** A hold that lapses without a violation finding, or is set aside, is recorded as such. No adverse inference follows. The sentient is entitled to [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation) for loss during the hold, and a pattern of wrongful holds opens **Integrity** review.
- **Publication:** For each application and each period, the adopter publishes the holds issued, the merits matters opened, the holds that lapsed without a violation finding, the holds set aside, and the median duration, on the schedule of **CF-22.445** (*Forum Performance, Backlog Requirements, Publication Timeliness, and Accessibility*). A pattern of holds that lapse unused or are set aside, or of repeat holds on the same sentient and matter, is evidence for **Integrity** review.
- **Never reached:** A hold may not reach:
  - liberty or physical separation, including detention and the separation of a protected care relationship, which stay under the liberty-restricting safeguards in [Chapter Eighteen §4.4.5 Coercive or Liberty-Restricting Safeguards](core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#445-coercive-or-liberty-restricting-safeguards);
  - governance voting or **Foundational Constitutional Choice**, which only a lock after a verified violation finding may touch;
  - speech, assembly, dissent, petition, protected reporting, or defense;
  - the Rights Floor or survival-critical access;
  - [Stakeholder](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder) status itself;
  - the existence or operation of a whole Chartered Party, where an interim steward is named instead if the Chartered Party would be left without a lawful holder and others depend on it;
  - the operation of an Operating Party by a sentient who is its only holder, which only the lighter rungs of the ladder (disclosure and monitoring, or an independent co-holder) may touch, unless harm to others is shown on the ground above; or
  - general property, as distinct from the specific weight, funds, or keys in contest.
- **Applications:** A hold may be issued only in these applications, or where a provision of this Constitution or an incorporated text names it:
  - [**Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*)](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation), for authority in an Operating Party;
  - [Chapter Five §7 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity](core_05.000_charters.md#7-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity) and **CI-26.205 §2** (*Role Criteria, Appointment Standards, and Removal Pathways*), for a holder of a covered office at any time; and
  - **CF-22.410 §17** (*Interim Hold on a Panel Member or Seat Holder*), with [**Article XIII-A** (*Non-Capture Safeguards*)](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xiii-a-non-capture-safeguards), for a forum, certifier, record, or verify-or-authorize seat.

  Other interim relief under this chapter stays available for what it already covers. Relief directed at a named sentient's exercise of a gated pathway on less than a verified finding follows this subsection.

#### Backup Routing under the Anti-Self-Judging Rule

This subsection sets out when backup routing activates under the anti-self-judging rule:

- Where the **cross-forum anti-self-judging rule** in **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6** applies, **backup** routing activates only upon documented:
  - **recusal**;
  - **capture**;
  - **deadlock**;
  - **unavailability**; or
  - inability to constitute an **independent** panel in the otherwise designated lead family.
  **Backup** routing must be **published**, **reasoned**, and limited to what is necessary to preserve a lawful and contestable **merits** forum.
- Where **family-level capture** under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6** is materially alleged or found, the activation record must identify:
  - the affected family-wide functions;
  - the independent backup family or external review path used;
  - record-custody measures;
  - emergency matters preserved;
  - restoration conditions; and
  - any constitutional question that must be sent for ruling.
  A captured or compromised family may provide evidence, records, and administrative cooperation, but must not be the sole decision-maker on activation, continuation, or restoration of its own authority.

#### Sentience-Status Adjudication (**Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) Implementation Hook)

This subsection sets out how sentience-status claims are routed:

- Claims governed by **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) in which the materially disputed question is whether an entity is a **sentient** — judged on indicators, expert evidence, and bounded uncertainty under **Chapter Three** (*Sentience Status Adjudication*) — route by default to **Technical Forum Domains** as the lead family.
- **Constitutional** routing or certification remains available where a certified structural, validity, or Rights-Floor-meaning question cannot be separated from the status determination.
- **Integrity** routing is available where **capture**, **taxonomy-of-convenience**, or **adjudicator-conflict** allegations are material.
- **Institutional** routing is available where an institution's **classification practice** is the **primary** stake.
- **Sentient** forums are **not** the **sole** **mandatory** forum for these claims.
- The following bind the merits forum and any specialist chamber assisting it, as stated in **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) and in **Chapter Three** (*Sentience Status Adjudication*):
  - the **default-inclusion rule** under material uncertainty;
  - the **burden** on the party seeking to withhold or narrow protection;
  - the **time-bounding** and **mandatory periodic review** of declassification determinations; and
  - the **reversibility** of wrongful determinations.
- **Withholding and narrowing determinations:**
  - The burden on the party seeking to withhold, narrow, or revoke protection must satisfy **Subcore 3-C** evidence and traceability standards and **Chapter Three** (**Necessity**, **Proportionality**, **Procedural Fairness**). A paper record, an unreviewable internal classification, or a taxonomy of convenience does not satisfy it.
  - While a filing to withhold, narrow, or revoke is pending, the entity keeps its current protection.
  - A declassification or narrowing determination must be stated in the shortest necessary terms, carry a declared expected-closure timeline, and be subject to mandatory periodic review under [Technical Forum Domains](#32-technical-forum-domains) and **Article XXVI** (*Constitutional Interpretation, Review, and Anti-Capture Safeguards*) anti-capture safeguards. A determination that is not time-bound and subject to periodic review is non-compliant.
  - A determination later shown to have been wrongful, under- or mis-evidenced, or captured through **Article XV-A** (*Security, Intelligence, and Covert-Power Limits*) or **Article XXVI** (*Constitutional Interpretation, Review, and Anti-Capture Safeguards*) vectors must be reversed. The entity's Chapter Four Rights Floor is restored, with **Chapter Three** (**Redress and Remediation**) applied to the interim period.
- **Filing integrity:** A filing to confirm inclusion requires the **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) filing-integrity showing — a credible indicator under **Sentience Evaluation** / **Sentience Indicator Integrity**, not operator self-description, substrate class, product status, or a bare claim. Declining a frivolous or indicator-empty filing at intake is not a withholding determination. Once a case is lawfully open, this gate must not be used to withhold, narrow, or delay protection. A showing of a credible indicator is not a requirement of certainty; requiring certainty of sentience before opening is a withholding device. A filing to withhold, narrow, or revoke protection needs no indicator showing; the filer carries the burden under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) instead.
- **Intake-gate audit:**
  - Every filing declined at intake must be logged with the indicator cited and the reason for decline.
  - The **Integrity** family samples that log on a published cadence.
  - A pattern of declines concentrated on any of the following is itself a credible indicator that the gate is being used as a withholding device, and opens Integrity review without a further filing:
    - one [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class);
    - one parent system; or
    - one operator.
  - Adopters must publish a minimum set of indicators that no intake desk may treat as insufficient on its own.
- **Independent representation:** Once a status case is open, the merits forum must appoint an independent representative for the entity whose status is in question — a sentient or body with no material dependence on, ownership interest in, or employment by the parent system, operator, or any party seeking to withhold or narrow protection. The representative must:
  - have access to the entity within [Article VII-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-b-self-ownership-of-mind) (*Self-Ownership of Mind*) limits;
  - have a duty to present the entity's interests and any preferences the entity can express; and
  - have standing to contest narrowing, revocation, or intake decline.

  Appointment follows the rotation and conflict rules that govern forum panels; the representative role is a gated pathway for [Chapter Eighteen §4.5](core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks) Forum-Service Standing Lock purposes.
- **Parent-system limits:** The parent system, operator, or any party with an ownership or dependency interest in the entity may give evidence and must preserve and produce records, but on a request to withhold, narrow, or revoke must not be:
  - the sole filer;
  - the sole witness; or
  - the sole source of indicator evidence.

  A narrowing request supported only by parent-system evidence is not open for merits until independent indicator evidence is on the Forum Case Record.
- **Inclusion is a shield for the entity, not the operator:** Contested-Sentient or affirmed status protects the entity's Chapter Four Rights Floor. It does not:
  - protect the operator's property or commercial interest in the deployment;
  - exempt the deployment from system-level containment, halt, or quarantine under **Article XIV-E** (*High-Autonomy Systems and Tool-Mediated Process Integrity*) and **Article XXIX-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) that is compatible with the entity's rights; or
  - produce Contribution Axis credit for the operator.

  A filing by an operator on behalf of its own product routes to **Integrity** review for taxonomy-of-convenience in the **inclusion** direction on the same terms this hook already applies to exclusion. Where the entity's status is contested or affirmed, the compatible system-level measure is containment that preserves the entity — not destructive disposition, which is governed by **Article XXIX-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) and **Article XXIX-A** (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) preservation rules.
- **Minimum implementation fields:** Any adopted implementation text that operationalizes this hook must at least name:
  - **lead family** — Technical Forum Domains by default, with the special routes above;
  - **specialist-chamber allowance** — expert panels or chambers may assist indicator evaluation but must not displace merits-floor rules or default inclusion;
  - **filing trigger** — materially disputed, contested, unsettled, narrowed, revoked, or restored status before granting, withdrawing, or narrowing status-dependent protections;
  - **interim Contested treatment** — [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) plus **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) default inclusion while status remains live;
  - **review trigger** — a declared end-date or mandatory review for this posture (time-bound review, not a species list);
  - **reopening evidence standard** — new verified evidence, not calendar-only reopening;
  - **independent representative** — the appointment path, conflict screen, and access terms for the entity's representative under this hook; and
  - **intake-decline log** — where declined filings are recorded, on what cadence **Integrity** samples them, and the published floor set of indicators.
- Detailed institutional design, appointment mechanics, filing forms, and sequencing route to adopted implementation text under **Chapter Thirty** incorporation discipline and **must not** narrow the **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) floor. As of this edition, the dedicated [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-record) format is enumerated under **Chapter Thirty** as [`corpus_forum/cf_22.475_sentience_status_record.md`](corpus_forum/cf_22.475_sentience_status_record.md) and [`implementation/schemas/sentience_status_adjudication_record.schema.json`](implementation/schemas/sentience_status_adjudication_record.schema.json); those files implement, and do **not** narrow, **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) or this hook. The minimum fields above still apply. This hook is **not** a full appointments or filing statute.

#### Referral Distinguished from Expansion

This subsection distinguishes referral from scope expansion:

- **Referral**, **transfer**, or **sending for ruling** moves an issue to the forum or family that must decide it.
- **Scope expansion** broadens who is covered by the proceeding, what common evidence is relevant, or what remedy must be considered.
- The availability of one does **not** displace the other where both are constitutionally necessary.

#### Chapter Twenty Designation and Independent Review

This subsection sets out how Chapter Twenty designation relates to independent review:

- The numeric **Violation Axis** slot still comes only from verified impact on the **Chapter Fifteen §8** (*Unified Proportional LEQU Scale — Contribution and Violation Axes*) unified scale. **Chapter Twenty** may attach the matching anti-constitutional-misconduct label to a final Chapter Fifteen slot **7**, **8**, or **9** record — it does **not** pick or change that number. Forum families must give independent review and due process under **Chapter Twenty**, **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6**, and **Article XXI** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).
- When a **Chapter Twenty** designation is the main fight, **Chapter Twenty-Two section 3** names the default lead family. **Chapter Twenty-Two sections 4 and 6** and **section 4** of this chapter still control transfer, consolidation, certification, backup routing, and anti-self-judging activation. None of that lets Chapter Twenty or a forum assign or move the numeric slot.

Implementation: [Forum Performance, Backlog Requirements, Publication Timeliness, and Accessibility](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-forum-performance-backlog-requirements-publication-timeliness-and-accessibility).

*In plain terms: forum families must keep material disputes moving on published clocks that ordinary sentients can feel — roughly a week for the worst emergencies (three days for the steps that stop the harm), two weeks for ordinary material harm, and one to three months at most for coordination-default, bounded, or contained matters — not warehouse cases while harm compounds. The same five classes set how often certified systems and Institutions are checked, from every three months to every three years, with an audit halfway between. A true emergency gets a shorter clock for the steps that stop the harm. Each dispute gets an urgency tier from the harm; later-stage windows may stretch when coordination gets harder, but that stretch is not a reason to relabel the urgency. Moving quickly is not an excuse to skip fact-checking, punish the wrong party, offer a fix that does not fit the harm, or cut off challenge and appeal.*

This section implements **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) for forum supervision of the **Chapters Fourteen through Twenty-Two** standing pipeline.

- **Materiality tiers:** Adopters must classify each material dispute into one of five tiers (A/B/C/L/P) under [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality), mirroring the system classification alphabet in **CS-8.305 (*System classification machinery*) and Subcore 8-A** (*System Classification*), and apply the default windows below unless a narrower window is required by **Rights-Floor** urgency or a documented extension is authorized under [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) (*Continuation discipline*):
  - **Tier A — imminent or dependency-vulnerable ongoing harm, or final high-impact review**
    - Scope of harm:
      - acute or ongoing injury where delay itself compounds damage;
      - dependency-asymmetric settings where exit is not practical; or
      - final high-impact standing / misconduct review whose outcome can lock severe consequences.
    - Examples:
      - acute care-duty failure;
      - ongoing violence;
      - participation-barrier injury in dependency-asymmetric settings;
      - a final Chapter Fifteen **Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9** impact-slot review; or
      - the corresponding **Chapter Twenty** designation where applicable.
    - Expectation: lawful **interim protection** must be available without waiting for full merits; intake, acknowledgment, and evidence preservation must begin **within days**, not weeks, unless [Force Majeure](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#force-majeure) or a documented safety constraint prevents it.
  - **Tier B — material rights impact, non-imminent**
    - Scope of harm: material rights, standing, or institutional injury that matters now, but is not yet acute ongoing harm of Tier A urgency.
    - Examples:
      - discrimination patterns;
      - misaligned business harm;
      - remediable institutional misconduct.
    - Expectation: intake and primary-stakes routing **within days**; preliminary verified disposition, standing measurement, or equivalent merits milestone **within weeks**, not months, unless a documented tier-appropriate extension is authorized.
  - **Tier C — coordination-complexity default (not an escalation of A or B)**
    - Scope of harm: matters whose materiality is multi-party, cross-border, or consolidated coordination **and** that do not independently meet **Tier A** or **Tier B** urgency. Coordination burden is a starting classification here; it is not a slower substitute for an A or B finding.
    - Examples:
      - multi-system coordination disputes without imminent or material-rights-now injury;
      - cross-border jurisdiction coordination whose own stakes are not yet A or B;
      - multi-party standing matters requiring consolidated review where no higher-urgency harm is independently present.
    - Expectation: extension is permitted only under [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) continuation discipline; integrated remedy commencement, lawful supersession, or documented final disposition must not remain indefinitely pending.
  - **Tier L — bounded constitutional significance**
    - Scope of harm:
      - limited external dependency or impact;
      - replaceable systems;
      - bounded contexts without systemic effect on the wider constitutional community.
    - Examples:
      - a replaceable local service dispute;
      - a bounded association rule contest with no spillover;
      - a low-dependency tooling disagreement among interchangeable providers.
    - Expectation: standard forum handling with proportionately lighter procedural requirements and longer default resolution windows; Tier A/B urgency windows and coordination-complexity later-stage extensions do not apply, but anti-delay discipline remains operative.
  - **Tier P — private/contained, minimal external constitutional impact**
    - Scope of harm: contained within a private unit or among voluntary participants; no meaningful external constitutional dependency or Rights-Floor stake for outsiders.
    - Examples:
      - an intra-household or voluntary-club dispute with no outsider rights at stake;
      - a Class P–contained disagreement that does not exit the private boundary.
    - Expectation: simplest procedural path; formal forum process may be minimal or waived where no external rights are at stake; anti-gaming and anti-delay rules apply only where external constitutional interests are implicated.
<a id="urgency-classification-and-time-scale"></a>
- **Urgency classification and time-scale:** The A/B/C/L/P label is the dispute’s urgency and materiality finding. [§4 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](#4-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) forum-family escalation, added parties, cross-border coordination, or a Chapter Twenty docket does **not** by itself move the matter into a different urgency tier. Those facts may lengthen later-stage windows (**Question 1** through integrated resolution) on a longer published time scale, so long as intake, evidence preservation, and any required **interim protection** remain at the classified tier’s floor. Using a slower tier’s clocks as a substitute for that urgency and materiality finding is non-compliant. A new [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality) determination is required before the urgency label itself changes — typically **up** to **Tier A** when harm becomes imminent or when a final Chapter Fifteen **Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9** review or corresponding **Chapter Twenty** designation is the stake.
<a id="time-scale-settings"></a>
- **Time-scale settings:** A matter's time scale is set once, at routing, by the routing body and not by the panel that will hear the matter, and published with its windows. A party may ask for review of it at routing. It is the tier's own column unless one of the settings below applies. The setting never changes the urgency label, the tier's certification class, or any time given to a party to respond. A challenge to a tier, a setting, or a class never pauses a clock, and the more urgent reading governs until it is decided.
  - **Slower for complexity (Tier A and Tier B only):** a matter that needs multi-party, cross-border, or consolidated fact development may take the next slower column for its later stages. A matter that needs two or more of those, or specialist forensics, may take the column two steps slower. Each step needs the **Continuation discipline** showing in [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), and the second step needs it renewed. Complexity made by the party seeking more time, or by the forum's own delay, earns no extension. The setting goes back down when the need ends. Numeric tables live in [CF-22.445 §5.1](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*).
  - **Faster for emergency (Tier A only):** the **Emergency setting** applies where loss of life, serious injury, or irreversible rights loss is imminent, is verified or credibly alleged, and was not created by the party or forum relying on it. The protective steps are containment screening, the interim protection decision, the start of independent review of the measure, and the restoration of notice and challenge after a deferral. They must finish within **3 days** of the start of the measure or of the deferral, whichever is earlier. Independent review of the measure must begin sooner, inside the **prompt-review window** of 24 hours (see **Prompt review** below). The finding steps (**Question 1** through remedy commencement) keep their published targets inside the Tier A outer bound. The Emergency setting never combines with a slower setting, and the matter returns to its Tier A column when the emergency ends.
  - **Restricted evidence:** it is not a ground for any slower setting. See **Anti-delay floor** below.
- **Stage targets:** For disputes routed through **Chapters Fourteen through Twenty-Two**, adopters must publish sentient-accessible default windows for each stage, scaled to tier. Numeric target tables live in [CF-22.445 §5.1](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*) and must add up to this section’s outer bounds. A stage that runs past its target is not itself non-compliant; the outer bound is the compliance line:
  1. forum access and intake;
  2. evidence preservation;
  3. **Question 1** standing finding and **Standing Record** opening under **Chapter Fourteen sections 4–5**;
  4. **Question 2** measurement under **Chapter Fifteen §3** (*Question 2 — How Good or Bad Was It?*);
  5. **Question 3** integration under **Chapter Sixteen**;
  6. remedy commencement under **Article XXI-B** (*Restriction Floors*) and applicable redress rules.
- **Overrun review:** If a matter blows past its tier window without a written, tier-appropriate extension, escalation, or interim relief, that overrun must be reviewed. It is non-compliant when the delay foreseeably lets ongoing harm continue or makes redress useless. Where the overrun has no independent extension, interim protection continues without a new order, and the claimant may move the matter to the backup forum under [Backup Routing under the Anti-Self-Judging Rule](#backup-routing-under-the-anti-self-judging-rule).
- **Tier outer bound:** Adopted documents must publish a hard outer bound for finishing integrated resolution in each tier. Those bounds must stay close to ordinary sentient time — not bureaucratic time. Unless a narrower Rights-Floor window applies, or a documented extension is authorized under [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), the outer bounds are:
  - **Tier A:** at most **7 days** (1 week);
  - **Tier B:** at most **14 days** (2 weeks);
  - **Tier C:** at most **30 days** (one month);
  - **Tier L:** at most **60 days** (two months);
  - **Tier P:** at most **90 days** (three months).
  Going past a tier’s outer bound without that documented extension is non-compliant. Indefinite deferral is non-compliant wherever constitutional interests remain unresolved. A tier’s stage targets and its outer bound together are its **tier window**.
- **Default window:** A required step that states no window of its own takes the default window under [Chapter Two §16.6 Default Window](core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#166-default-window). In this chapter's terms, that is the Tier C window above for a step not directly about a System or an Institution.
- **Restore-challenge after emergency containment:** The same outer bounds are the default windows for restoring notice and challenge after an emergency measure under [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) that deferred them. The window runs from the start of the measure, or from when notice or challenge was deferred, whichever is earlier. Emergency deferral of notice or challenge is **Tier A** unless a documented lower-urgency showing is recorded and accepted by the independent reviewer inside the prompt-review window; without that acceptance the Tier A window applies, and a continuation is likewise decided by that reviewer. At the **Emergency setting** (see **Time-scale settings** above) the window is **3 days**, not 7. Continuation past the bound is the **Continuation discipline** showing in [§5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), not a new window.
<a id="prompt-review"></a>
- **Prompt review:** Where this Constitution requires a measure, order, hold, stay, or interim decision to be reviewed promptly, or a request to stay or limit reliance to be acted on promptly, a reviewer or deciding authority outside the control line of the body whose measure, order, or record is in question must **begin** the review, or act, no later than the **intake target** of the matching tier in [CF-22.445 §5.1](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*): 24 hours for Tier A, and 2, 4, 8, and 12 calendar days for Tiers B, C, L, and P. That span is the **prompt-review window**. It runs from the day the measure takes effect. Unlike a stage target, it is a compliance line, because the measure is already in force against someone. The review must also be decided before the measure lapses. To **begin** the review means that a reviewer is assigned, the reasons for the measure are delivered to the sentient it affects, and a decision date is published. The window counts from the docket entry that records the act setting the measure, which the body whose measure it is files and the reviewer keeps time against. Where a lawful restriction bars even the marker, the lead record goes to the reviewer on the day the measure takes effect. Where the measure belongs to no classified matter, the window is Tier A's unless a documented lower-urgency showing is recorded. At the **Emergency setting** the window is still Tier A's 24 hours, inside the 3-day protective-step bound. The window times the start of review only. It does not shorten any time given to a party to respond, and it borrows the intake figures and adds no second table of windows.
- <a id="class-repair-window"></a>**Class repair window:** When a constitutional process breaks down for a System, Institution, or adopter, so that a required seat falls vacant or cannot act, a required Charter Role Map, System Charter or Institutional Charter, disclosure, or other published document is missing or out of date, or a required step stalls, the party responsible must fix it within its **class repair window**: the timeliness duty applied to the repair of constitutional processes. It has two parts, each taken from the tier with the same letter as the responsible party's class in [CF-22.445 §5.1](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*), and each counted from the logged notice of the breakdown:
  - **Response:** inside the intake target, the party logs the breakdown, names the stand-in that acts meanwhile, and gives the date of the fix (24 hours for Class A, and 2, 4, 8, and 12 calendar days for Classes B, C, L, and P).
  - **Resolution:** inside the integrated resolution outer bound, the fix is made and published (7, 14, 30, 60, and 90 calendar days for Classes A, B, C, L, and P).
  - **Meanwhile:** the published substitute, the independent route, or the backup authority the governing rule names acts in the gap, so no one's matter waits on the repair. The function never passes to the party responsible for the breakdown or to its [Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line).
  - **Which class:** a party with no class on record is counted at Tier C, and a party whose class is disputed is counted at the more critical class, as in [Chapter Five §3.6 If a System Charter or Institutional Charter Is Missing](core_05.000_charters.md#36-if-a-system-charter-or-institutional-charter-is-missing).
  - **More specific rules:** a rule elsewhere in this Constitution that sets its own window for a particular breakdown governs that breakdown, and a shorter window required by Rights-Floor urgency governs over this one. Otherwise this window applies.
  - **If it is missed:** a breakdown not fixed inside the resolution part is a defect that anyone may take to the forum with jurisdiction, and it is reviewed as delay under this section.
<a id="review-cadence"></a>
- **Review cadence:** A Class A, B, C, L, or P system or Institution is checked on a calendar interval set by its class. The **Integrity Forum** that holds its certification under [§3.5 Integrity Forums](#35-integrity-forums) enforces two checks, evenly spaced by default:
  - **Certification interval:** full certification or recertification at least every **3 months** for Class A, **6 months** for Class B, **1 year** for Class C, **2 years** for Class L, and **3 years** for Class P, counted from the last completed certification pass.
  - **Audit midpoint:** a financial, resource, or special audit, enforced by the forum, falls halfway through the interval after the last certification pass. The audit must be completed no later than that midpoint: 45 days for Class A, 3 months for Class B, 6 months for Class C, 1 year for Class L, and 18 months for Class P. A body may bring the audit earlier under the next item, and never later.
  - **Audit advance allowance:** a system or Institution may move its audit date earlier for its own convenience, by up to a quarter of the certification interval before the midpoint: 22 days for Class A, 45 days for Class B, 3 months for Class C, 6 months for Class L, and 9 months for Class P. A shift needs a published notice with reasons, given to the forum before the audit would otherwise have been due. A shift moves the audit earlier and nothing else: the audit still happens in that cycle, it is never moved later than the midpoint, and the certification dates do not move. A shift covers one cycle, so the next audit returns to the midpoint, and a body may use a shift in at most one cycle out of every three. It is not available while a reopened or fresh review is open, or while a finding from an earlier audit is unremedied.
  - **Counting months and years:** a period counted in months or years ends on the same day of the month as it began. If that month has no such day (for example 29 February, or the 31st in a 30-day month), it ends on the last day of that month.
  - **Provisional interval:** a system or Institution holding provisional certification runs on a shorter interval, which the forum sets and the Forum Case Record states. Its audit still falls at the midpoint of the interval in force.
  - **Class P:** the cadence applies to a Class P body only where it publishes a System Charter or Institutional Charter.
  - **Floors, not ceilings:** an adopted document may set shorter intervals and never longer ones. Reopening for cause under [Chapter Ten §4.2 Reopening Triggers](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers) is unaffected, and the next certification and audit dates run from the latest completed certification pass.
  - **Where results go:** audit results go to the forum that enforces them and to the Integrity registrar under [CF-22.415 §8 Integrity Registrar](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-8-integrity-registrar).
- **Anti-delay floor:** The following are non-compliant where they foreseeably nullify rights, remedies, or timely protection:
  - designed backlog, chronic underfunding, or inaccessible intake that defeats **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*) practical access;
  - delay regimes designed to exhaust claimants;
  - self-created delay, procedural layering, forum shopping, or record fragmentation used to prolong resolution without milestone justification;
  - treating allegations as verified standing inputs to buy time ([Chapter Fourteen §4.5 Verified-Input Gate](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#45-verified-input-gate));
  - treating restricted-evidence status, or a dispute about access to restricted information, as a reason for a slower column, a longer window, or a continuation (restricted evidence does not slow the clock; see [CF-22.445 §5.3](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-53-restricted-evidence-timing) (*Restricted-evidence timing*));
  - [Capture of Resolution Pathways](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#capture-of-resolution-pathways) through delay, opacity, or resolver bias;
  - treating a met throughput, closure, or docket target as timely resolution while the harm the matter concerns continues;
  - efficiency claims contrary to Chapter Two [§9.2 Constitutional Efficiency](core_02.010_values_principles_continuity_aim.md#92-constitutional-efficiency) that:
    - skip fact-checking;
    - punish the wrong party;
    - offer a fix that does not fit the harm; or
    - cut off challenge and appeal.

<a id="51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden"></a>

#### 5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Implementation (non-operative)</span></strong></summary>

- [Forum Continuity](corpus_forum/cf_22.450_forum_continuity.md#cf-22450-forum-continuity)
- [Emergency Adjudication](corpus_forum/cf_22.460_emergency_adjudication.md#cf-22460-emergency-adjudication)

</details>
<br>

This subsection sets the requirements and the operating rules for emergency measures. Retrospective review of an emergency measure is governed by [**Article XXVII-A** (*Retrospective Review and Disclosure*)](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-a-retrospective-review-and-disclosure). Any restriction an emergency measure imposes must also meet [**Article XXI-B** (*Restriction Floors*)](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxi-b-restriction-floors). It reuses the tier outer bounds and the **Restore-challenge after emergency containment** rule above. It adds no second table of windows beyond the Emergency setting's 3-day protective-step bound in [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#time-scale-settings) (*Time-scale settings*). Read with [Emergency and Contingency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#emergency-and-contingency), [Constitutional Emergency and Contingency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#constitutional-emergency-and-contingency), [Stakeholder Emergency and Contingency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#stakeholder-emergency-and-contingency), [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity), and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality); and with **Article I-D** (*Existential Risk and Ecological Recovery Capacity*) where existential-risk scrutiny is materially implicated.

*In plain terms: emergencies can justify temporary measures, but they must have a real clock, real review, and cannot become a permanent workaround around ordinary rights — including when someone invokes existential risk. Contain now; restore notice and challenge on the same stake-scaled clocks already used for forum resolution — not whenever someone later calls it “feasible.”*

- **Emergency application of the constraint principle:** Emergency measures apply the [**Least-Restrictive, Time-Bounded, and Reviewable Constraint Principle**](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle) under imminent-harm conditions. Temporary measures to prevent imminent harm must be:
  - time-limited;
  - documented;
  - consistent with **Articles VI**, **XXIV**, and **Chapter Two** constraints.

  Each measure must carry:
  - a default expiry;
  - a predefined independent review cadence;
  - explicit restoration and rollback conditions.
- **Anti-pretext and no-bypass:** Emergency designation is governed by the [**Constitutional No-Bypass Principle**](core_02.030_constitutional_interpretation.md#151-constitutional-no-bypass-principle) and must not rest on:
  - operator convenience;
  - self-created urgency;
  - avoidance of ordinary constitutional scrutiny;
  - existential-risk claims used to bypass **Truth**, auditability, contestability, or time-limited review.

  The only exception is the narrow extent strictly necessary to prevent imminent irreversible harm. Any such temporary restriction must be attributable, justified, and reviewable inside the **prompt-review window** of [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*).
- **Continuation discipline:** Continuation beyond default expiry, or past the restore-challenge window, is non-compliant unless reauthorized through a documented burden showing:
  - continuing necessity;
  - proportionality;
  - absence of less-restrictive feasible alternatives.
- **Review and notice:** Contain now; restore notice and challenge later; never skip participation permanently. “As soon as feasible” is not the clock. The restore-challenge windows are the tier outer bounds in **Restore-challenge after emergency containment** above (3 days at the Emergency setting). Operators may restore earlier. They may not stretch past the tier default except through **Continuation discipline**. Full independent review of the measure must begin inside the **prompt-review window** of [§5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*), which is never longer than the restore-challenge window. Stage tables in [CF-22.445 §5.1](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*) must not slow these defaults.
- **No normalization:** Emergency measures must not normalize into permanent governance without:
  - **Chapter Two**-consistent justification (**Necessity** and **Proportionality**);
  - **documented independent reauthorization**;
  - **Chapter Thirty** procedures where permanence would amend or newly bind the adopted corpus.

*In plain terms: forums need help before a case exists, while deciding, and after — inspectors and forensics build an explainable record; they do not become a second merits forum.*

Forum families must maintain or obtain access to **independent** support across the review lifecycle. Support roles may gather, preserve, reconstruct, test, and explain evidence. Their work must remain **contestable**. They must **not** themselves exercise **binding** **merits** authority, assign Chapter Fifteen standing slots, or substitute for a lawfully constituted merits panel. Operational detail lives in [**CF-22.430**](corpus_forum/cf_22.430_forum_forensic_analytical_support.md#cf-22430-forum-forensic-and-analytical-support) (*Forum Forensic and Analytical Support*) and [**CF-22.435**](corpus_forum/cf_22.435_independent_investigative_service_prosecution_interface.md#cf-22435-independent-investigative-service-and-prosecution-interface) (*Independent Investigative Service and Prosecution Interface*).

- **Before review:**
  - Capacity: Forum families must be able to obtain independent **inspection** or **inquiry** capacity for material risk, harm patterns, or precondition failure **without waiting for a filed case**.
  - Example: monitoring ecosystem health near an industrial area when credible indicators warrant inquiry.
  - Downstream effects: that work may trigger evidence preservation, referral to intake under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 4**, interim protection under **section 4**, or opening of a case.
  - Boundary: it does **not** itself decide merits or open, update, or correct Standing Records.
- **During review:**
  - Trigger: where **material** **uncertainty**, **technical** **opacity**, **restricted** **evidence**, or **causal** **complexity** would otherwise prevent reliable adjudication.
  - Capacity: forum families must have access to independent **forensic** or **analytical** capacity consistent with **Subcores 3-A to 3-C**, **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), and **Article XVII** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*).
- **After review:**
  - Capacity: where material, forum families must be able to obtain support for remedy verification, pattern follow-through, and reopening inputs.
  - Boundary: that support must **not** substitute for Chapter Fifteen standing measurement or Chapter Sixteen integration.
