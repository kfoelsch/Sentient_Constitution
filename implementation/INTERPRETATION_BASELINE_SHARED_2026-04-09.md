# Shared Interpretation Baseline (Sentient Constitution / CP / CS)

## Purpose
Provide one reusable interpretation baseline for companion-file front matter so repeated guidance can be referenced from a single location.

This note is editorial support. It does not create constitutional obligations beyond the corpus files.

## Baseline clauses

- **Constitutional source order:** the numbered `core_*` Sentient Constitution files, `core_00-01_principles.md` through `core_15-15_incorporation.md`, are the binding constitutional source layer.
- **Companion incorporation:** `corpus_joint_structure.md` (including **Cross-domain implementation layer**) and designated portions of `corpus_systems.md` are binding where incorporated through valid adoption scope.
- **Definition authority:** O/E/C definition mechanics are owned by Sentient Constitution Chapters Two through Four; constitutional term definitions are owned by Chapter Five.
- **Rights authority:** Rights language is owned by Sentient Constitution Chapter Ten; companion files implement and must not restate weaker rights floors.
- **Compliance-classification authority:** Canonical compliance/violation/standing classification policy is owned by Sentient Constitution Chapter Six.
- **Operational taxonomy authority:** Data types, system classes, dependency types, and steward tiers are owned by [corpus_systems.md](../corpus_systems.md) Chapters S1 through S3.
- **Layer distinction:** PRIM8 and PROT2 remain distinct architecture-layer vs governance-layer intervention authorities.
- **Conflict handling:** Sentient Constitution constitutional constraints and rights floors control meaning; operational layers may be stricter but may not narrow constitutional requirements.
- **Cross-companion integration (CP / CS / CI / CC):** default read order, read-with routing, and **stricter-wins** among companions are stated in **`corpus_joint_structure.md` CJS-2** (binding where that file is incorporated).
- **Adoption and custody:** Incorporated companion text is operative only for adopted editions with auditable custody-chain metadata.
- **No silent constitutional drift:** Post-adoption companion-file revisions are non-operative until valid Chapter Thirteen adoption and Chapter Fifteen incorporation custody.

## Migration guidance

- Keep local interpretation deltas in CP/CS only where file-specific context is needed.
- Replace duplicated shared clauses with pointers to this baseline in future cleanup passes.
- For **hybrid authority composition** shared by institutions and forums, use **`corpus_joint_structure.md` CJS-3.7** in the authoritative corpus rather than parallel paraphrases.
