# Governance disclosure template

**Maps to:** `corpus_institutions.md` **CI-6.205** (*Transparency, participation, accessible challenge and service pathways*), **CI-6.205 §4** (*Governance disclosure schema*), **CI-0.215** (*Compliance mapping and stable registry*).  
**Non-authoritative** unless adopted.

## Publication cadence

- Annual / quarterly / event-triggered: (pick; justify by class/stewardship level)

## Required fields (recurring report)

1. **Authority** — legal or policy basis for the decision body.
2. **Decision scope** — what is and is not within the governing [Institutional Charter](../core_05.040_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) (**CI-8.205 §6** minimum fields).
3. **Participation gates** — who may participate under which rules.
4. **Dissent and challenge** — routes, time limits, escalation.
5. **Conflicts** — disclosed interests for decision participants (link to **CI-8.210**).
6. **Assurance results** — internal / external assurance outcomes.
7. **Unresolved risks** — open items, mitigations, owners.
8. **Plain-language summary** — 1–2 pages max; must stand alone for non-specialists (**Chapter One §5.2** stewardship alignment).
9. **Accessibility** — formats, languages, reasonable accommodations for access.

## Comparison fields (period-over-period)

- Participation rate, contest outcomes, backlog against **CF-14.445** targets where forums are in scope.
- Integrity incidents and closure status (**CI-8.210** / **CI-12.210**).

## Model flexibility

Adopters may use different governance brands; this template requires **disclosure content**, not a specific organizational chart.
