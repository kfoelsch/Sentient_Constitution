*In plain terms: they should not be able to take your food, water, soil, or home by making next season’s seed, a loan, or a “progress” project the price of staying — and filming a meeting is not the same as you saying yes.*

## A living tied to land is ordinary — and barely counted

These figures describe a recognizable situation. They do not change the Rights Floor that applies to you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Alive in it now** | agriculture is about **26 in 100** jobs worldwide. Small farms support about **2 billion** living humans in developing countries (about **24 in 100**). About **500 million** smallholder farms. |
| **Counted in world output** | about **4%** of what the books count as production — while farms under two hectares grow about **35%** of the world’s food. |
| **Meet it in a full life** | everyone eats. Many will farm, or be fed by this work. |

The interesting fact is the gap: a quarter of jobs and of food, a sliver of counted output. Sources: [`SCALE_NOTES.md`](SCALE_NOTES.md).

## Your situation

You grow food mainly to eat, and you sell what is left. The public safety net is thin.

If a body that actually runs the systems in your life adopted this Constitution:

**Survival is not a purchase.**
- **Today:** Water, seed, fertilizer, or a buyer app now comes as a bundle: take their loan and their sensor, and agree to sell your harvest only to them — or you do not get seed, fertilizer, or water for next season. Hunger is priced as a market.
- **With this Constitution:** You cannot be made to work a particular scheme, buy extra services, or win a lottery just to eat, drink, or stay housed. They cannot throw you off the land or out of the shelter you need to live without a real reason.

See: [Article III-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-a-survival); [Occupancy Continuity](../../../core_05_band_continuity.md#occupancy-continuity-constitutional); [CI-9.4](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_09_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md).

**The living system is not a leftover.**
- **Today:** This season’s harvest is the whole score. Someone says the way your family has always used this land does not count on paper, so moving you off it is called progress.
- **With this Constitution:** Air, water, soil, and the living systems that keep you alive count. Harm to them can fail the rule even if this year’s yield looks good.

See: [Article I-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity); [Article I-C](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-i-c-intergenerational-responsibility).

**Asking has to change something, or it is theater.**
- **Today:** A company or ministry that wants the land or the water calls one village meeting, films the room to look like they asked, and then builds anyway — even if you said no.
- **With this Constitution:** Decisions about a place have to name who is affected, keep a record of what was asked and what changed, and escalate projects that only go through the motions.

See: [CI-23](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_23_place_based_stewardship_indigenous_continuity_consultation.md). Bringing this Constitution into a body's own rules must not shrink land, consultation, or free, prior, and informed consent duties that body already bears ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority); [FIT §4.9](../FIT_SITUATIONS.md#49-fpic-bearing-communities)).

**Shared work and shared land can stay shared.**
- **Today:** Only a company or a ministry form “counts.”
- **With this Constitution:** Commons, cooperatives, and mutual aid are supposed to stay recognizable without being forced into only those forms. Informal arrangements cannot be used to hide shut-out.

See: [CI-22](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_22_commons_cooperatives_mutual_aid_non_market_governance.md).

**A tool you depend on should still be fixable.**
- **Today:** The pump, radio, or drone is a subscription that dies with the vendor.
- **With this Constitution:** Repair and independent servicing have a floor for covered products.

See: [Article II-B](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-ii-b-repair-maintenance-and-independent-servicing).

## What you might reasonably object to

- **“This will not stop a bulldozer.”** Until a body that can bind the project records a formal agreement, this text has no independent power against someone who did not agree ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)). The case is what an operator, lender, or platform that agreed would be forbidden to do with survival and consultation — not a claim that your national land law has been replaced.

- **“You will take our land in the name of ecology.”** CI-23 does not reopen every old land case, and it does not turn this Constitution into a land-title fight. Protecting air, water, and soil is a basic protection; it is not a spare deed for an outside project.

- **“We already have our own rules.”** This Constitution does not override applicable local, national, or international law ([Chapter Fourteen §5](../../../core_14_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)). Where used inside that framework, processes have to stay consistent with it. It is a companion measurement and challenge frame for systems that affect you, not a second state.

- **“A sensor loan is just how we get seed now.”** Making next season’s food depend on giving up your data, selling only to them, or being unable to leave a buyer app is the lock-in pattern [Article XIX-C](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xix-c-anti-lock-in-rule) names. Ordinary trade with a real way to leave is not that pattern.

- **“I am not ‘Indigenous’ as a form.”** You do not have to take that label to use this page. This brief is for someone whose food and living come from land and water. It does not change the Rights Floor that applies to you. A project that touches your plot still has to ask in a way that can change the plan. When a community’s language, knowledge, or lasting tie to a place is actually at stake, those rules are located in the section on [Indigenous Continuity](../../../core_05_band_continuity.md#indigenous-continuity-constitutional).

## What this will not pretend to give you

- A new land title, a crop-price guarantee, or a rebuilt water table on this page alone.
- A replacement for your state’s constitution, or a claim of supremacy over it.
- Unbounded reopening of historical territorial claims.
- Enforcement against a trader, ministry, or platform that has not adopted it.
