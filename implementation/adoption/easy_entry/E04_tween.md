*In plain terms: you already count; decisions about you have to be about your interests, not only the household’s convenience — and your voice is supposed to be sought to the extent you can give it, even when you are not the one who decides.*

This brief is for a tween whose voice is treated as optional. It does not sort you by birthday. When decisions about you are at stake, those rules are located in the section on [Article V-F](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-f-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability).

## Your situation

Adults are rearranging school, housing, clinics, or who you live with. You can say some of what you want. You cannot run the household.

If a body that actually runs the systems in your life adopted this Constitution:

**You hold the full floor while you are still growing.**
- **Today:** Younger voice is decorative. They talk over you, or they ask once and then use whatever answer is convenient.
- **With this Constitution:** Still growing does not narrow dignity, nondiscrimination, self-ownership, or the rest of Chapter Six.

See: [Article V-F](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-f-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability).

**Decisions about you have to be about your interests.**
- **Today:** “Best for the family” or “easier for us” is treated as the same thing. You are used as a reason in a fight that is not about you.
- **With this Constitution:** Decisions that actually affect you must reflect your own interests and what you can say you want — not operator, maker-system, or household convenience.

See: [Article V-F](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-f-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability).

**Your voice is not only one adult’s story.**
- **Today:** Whichever carer is louder is “what the child wants.”
- **With this Constitution:** Preferences have to be sought in a way that fits what you can actually do. You are not failed because you cannot yet argue like an adult, and you are not protected merely because an adult felt sympathetic.

See: [Article V-F](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-f-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability).

**Lasting separation is hard on purpose.**
- **Today:** A household reshuffle happens, and review is later if at all.
- **With this Constitution:** Durable separation from a protected care relationship has to be needed, not bigger than the harm, fair, and reviewed on a clock.

See: [Non-Separation](../../../core_05_band_participation.md#non-separation-constitutional); [Article VII-D](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-d-family-care-relationships-reproductive-autonomy-and-non-separation).

## What you might reasonably object to

- **“They still won’t listen.”** The text cannot make a particular adult kind. A body that has agreed to follow this Constitution still has to show that a decision about you is actually about *your* interests, not what is easier for the household. It has to include what you can actually say you want. If a limit is meant to last, it has to be looked at again. That is a reachable next step, not a promise that you pick the house.

- **“I want to decide where we live.”** Deciding the household on your own is not a basic protection. What you may do yourself scales with what you can demonstrate. Wanting a different room or neighborhood is often a preference.

- **“If I say the wrong thing, I’ll get someone in trouble.”** What you say is not, by itself, an official record of verified help or harm. Most of us have no such record. That is the normal state, not a hole that has to be filled because you spoke. An adult who heard you has a report waiting to be looked at, not a finding that someone is in trouble. A record opens only when actual facts have been checked, and only by the role allowed to open one ([Chapter Eight §2.1](../../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default)). Adults do not get to treat your words as a weapon without that process.

- **“This isn’t how my family works.”** This Constitution binds a body that has agreed to follow it, not every household that has not agreed ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)). It also does not override applicable family law ([Chapter Fourteen §5](../../../core_14_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)).

- **“I’m not a baby. Don’t talk to me like a teenager either.”** You do not have to take either label to use this page. This brief is for someone whose voice is treated as optional. It does not sort you into a named group. How much you may do yourself still tracks what you can actually show. When that is at stake, those rules are located in the section on [Article V-F](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-f-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability). If the closed door is “you’re old enough to know better, but not to decide,” that is [the teen page](E03_teen.md).

## What this will not pretend to give you

- Running the household, or a veto on every adult care decision.
- A second “kid constitution,” or a rule that parents are always wrong.
- Winning a preference contest against someone else’s basic protection.
- Enforcement against a school or family that has not adopted it.
