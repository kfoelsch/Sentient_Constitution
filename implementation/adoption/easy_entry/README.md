This page is the index for short briefs in the “you” voice: what this Constitution would change for someone in a recognizable situation, what they might reasonably object to, and what it will not pretend to give them.

*In plain terms: pick the brief that sounds like your situation. It is a soft case, not a promise that anyone has adopted this text, and not a new legal type.*

<a id="purpose-and-role"></a>
## Purpose and role

Each brief uses the same method as the [lived-situation packets](../../../evaluation/lived_situations/README.md): a colliding scene, basic protections rather than wish-fulfillment, and honest limits. The packets **test** whether those protections reach named parties. These briefs **speak to** a reader in a similar situation.

The roster names **pages for situations**. It does not create legal types, trust scores, or duties. It does not change the Rights Floor that applies to you. Source text binds. Teen and tween briefs do not sort you by birthday; those rules are located in the section on [Article V-F](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-f-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability) (*what you can actually do, not calendar age*). The mental-health brief does not sort you by diagnosis. Shop, capital, and operator briefs are roles, not a second morals stack.

<a id="what-this-is-not"></a>
## What this is not

These briefs are **not**:

- The Constitution itself, or text written into a body's own rules
- [Adopting this Constitution](../../../core_15_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) or [bringing it into a body's own rules](../../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)
- A claim that this Constitution overrides applicable law, or that a hospital, platform, lab, or host product has adopted it
- An official record of verified help or harm, a Chapter Seven check, or a finding that anyone was supported or denied in the world
- A second morals stack that sorts readers into types
- The lived-situation sitting, the announced evaluation pack, or an answer key for either

If you were invited to use the [lived-situation packets](../../../evaluation/lived_situations/), open [`evaluation/lived_situations/START_HERE.md`](../../../evaluation/lived_situations/START_HERE.md) and do **not** use these briefs as answers.

<a id="do-not-use-in-these-briefs"></a>
## Do not use in these briefs

These pages are for a first-time reader. Keep house terms out of the body. Official names may appear **inside markdown links**. Keep **Sentient Constitution** and **Rights Floor** as names. Do not apply this table to numbered `core_*` files.

| Do not use | Use instead |
|---|---|
| standing record / standing score / routing material | official record of verified help or harm; a score of how trusted you are; a report waiting to be looked at, not already a verdict |
| adopter / operations-guide / incorporation | a body that has agreed to follow this Constitution; using the ideas as a handbook; bringing this Constitution into a body's own rules |
| substrate / parent-system / derived sentient / overlay | body or form; the system that made you; a copy or fork; a separate extra rulebook |
| forum / Class A / material stake / steward | independent review; the highest-impact kind of system; how much is actually on the line; those running the system |
| load-bearing / reader door / age-proxy / lineage | the real rule this page points to; a page for this situation; using birthday instead of what you can do; whether to have a child |
| offtake / customary use / ecological integrity | you may sell your crop only to them; the way your family has always used this land; air, water, soil, and the living systems that keep you alive |

## How to read a brief

Every brief has the same five blocks:

1. **A thesis headline** — a one-line claim that this situation is ordinary, large, or already in everyone's path (not a generic “how common” label), then three figures (now / counted output or analog / full life), plus sources. Not a legal type.
2. **Your situation** — a short who-you-are line, then 3–5 points. Each point has **Today** and **With this Constitution** sub-bullets, then a **See:** home
3. What you might reasonably object to — each item is a bullet: the objection in the reader’s voice, then the answer
4. What this will not pretend to give you
5. If you want to look further

Put colliding facts in the **Today** line. Do not write a second scene. Cite the named homes if you want the real text. Locators are not duties. If a brief and core disagree, core wins.

### How to read a process guide

Guides **G01–G05** are for a first-time reader who is **affected** by a system, not for those running it. Each has: what is happening; what a body that agreed to follow this Constitution would owe; what to open next; what this will not pretend to give you. They point at boxed operative statements. They do not restate duties. Day-to-day cards for those running the system remain [`STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md`](../../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md).
