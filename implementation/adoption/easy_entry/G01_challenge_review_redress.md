*In plain terms: if a system that matters to your life fails you, there has to be a real way to challenge it, get it looked at, and be made whole — and a good-faith report must not be punished. This page points at those rules. It is not already that path.*

## What is happening

A cutoff, lockout, or decision hit you. The office says file a form, wait, or read the whole rulebook first. The promised fix exists only on paper, or the path is a specialist portal you cannot actually use.

## If a body that actually runs the systems in your life agreed to follow this Constitution

**You get a real way to challenge, not a maze.**
- **Today:** The only door is a specialist portal, a hop count, or “come back when you have a lawyer.”
- **With this Constitution:** Challenge, review, and being made whole have to work in practice. A file that never finishes is still a refusal.

See: [Article XII-B](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-b-right-to-challenge-review-and-redress).

**A good-faith report is not a punishable act.**
- **Today:** Speaking up costs the lease, the shift, or the next appointment.
- **With this Constitution:** Good-faith reports must not be punished. The boxed next-step for those running the system is on the same article.

See: [Article XII-B](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#operative-steward-statement-contest).

**A form on paper is not being made whole.**
- **Today:** There is an inbox and a policy page. Nobody can actually restore the water, the pay, or the record.
- **With this Constitution:** The path has to have real capacity to deliver, not only a form.

See: [Chapter Nine §4.1](../../../core_09_standing_integration.md#41-remedy-and-correction); [Remedy](../../../core_09_standing_integration.md#operative-steward-statement-remedy).
