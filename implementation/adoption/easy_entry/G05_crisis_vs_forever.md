*In plain terms: a real crisis can justify fast containment, but notice and a real way to challenge must come back on a clock — not “whenever it is convenient,” and not never.*

## What is happening

Something broke. Those running the system want to skip telling you, and skip your chance to challenge, for as long as they like. Or someone uses “emergency” to freeze ordinary rules. Or the opposite: a documented fast containment is already in place, and someone wants to block it until every notice is perfect.

## If a body that actually runs the systems in your life agreed to follow this Constitution

**Contain now. Do not skip notice forever.**
- **Today:** “We’ll tell you when it is feasible.” Feasible never arrives.
- **With this Constitution:** Fast containment is allowed when harm is imminent. Skipping notice and challenge **permanently** is not. Restore them on a published clock.

See: [Article XXIII-D](../../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxiii-d-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden); [Emergency](../../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#operative-steward-statement-emergency).

**The clock is not a slogan.**
- **Today:** Process, hop count, or “read more files” eats the window while harm continues.
- **With this Constitution:** Restore notice and challenge on the same stake-scaled clocks already used for timely resolution. Continuation past the bound needs a documented showing, not a vibe.

See: [Article XXIII-D restore-challenge clocks](../../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#xxiii-d-restore-challenge-clocks); [Delay](../../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#operative-steward-statement-delay).

**Blocking documented containment until full notice can also fail.**
- **Today:** A survival-critical deferral is already written down. Someone refuses to contain until every notice is perfect.
- **With this Constitution:** Do not block a documented highest-urgency deferral in order to insist on full notice before containment. Contain now; bring notice and challenge back on the clock.

See: [Emergency](../../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#emergency).

- A right to block a documented highest-urgency containment until a meeting is perfect.
- A permanent extra rulebook that replaces ordinary rights because someone said “crisis.”
- Enforcement against a body that has not agreed to follow this Constitution ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)).
