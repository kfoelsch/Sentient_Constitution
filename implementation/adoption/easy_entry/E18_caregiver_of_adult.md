*In plain terms: unpaid care for an adult is real work, rest cannot be billed as the price of staying in that care, and helping with bills is not a key to every decision — and this still will not make you their owner, or make them disappear.*

## Helping an adult whose daily life is harder is ordinary

These figures describe a recognizable situation. They do not change the Rights Floor that applies to you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Alive in it now** | about **13 in 100** of those ages 50+ in OECD surveys provide informal long-term care (about 1 in 8 of that age band). There is no single global census of “unpaid carer of an adult.” |
| **Counted in world output** | unpaid cooking, cleaning, and looking-after hours worldwide would count as about **9%** of world GDP if paid at a local hourly minimum wage (ILO 2018) — hours national books usually omit. Adult care sits inside that pile. There is no published “adult-carer” slice. |
| **Meet it in a full life** | nearly everyone will spend a stretch helping an adult whose remembering, stamina, or decisions are getting harder. |

These figures are approximate — nearest 1-in-n or whole percent. Sources: [`SCALE_NOTES.md`](SCALE_NOTES.md).

This brief is not the child-raising page ([E02](E02_single_parent.md)) and not the page for the adult whose help is taking over ([E06](E06_age_related_challenges.md)). You are the helper.

## Your situation

You cook, lift, remind, pay some bills, or sit the night shift for an adult you care about. A clinic or relative offers rest only if you take their cards, lease, or clinician.

If a body that actually runs the systems in your life adopted this Constitution:

**Unpaid adult care is in scope, not a private leftover.**
- **Today:** The hours do not count until they are waged. No rest-break. The job that keeps them alive is your hobby.
- **With this Constitution:** A body that has agreed to follow this Constitution has to name who routes rest-breaks, who counts unpaid care, and who takes reports of pressure.

See: [CI-16](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_16_care_labor_dependent_support_respite_care_economy_fairness.md).

**Rest is not supposed to cost their say — or yours.**
- **Today:** The clinic will give you a break if you become “decision-maker.” Or they will only help if you leave the household. Keep the adult and lose rest, or take rest and take over.
- **With this Constitution:** Survival and rest cannot be billed as the price of unpaid care. Helping with one hard thing is not a key to every decision. If someone has to decide a thing they cannot currently carry, that step has to be only that thing, the smallest needed, time-limited, and looked at again.

See: [Article III-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-a-survival); [Article VII-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-a-self-ownership-of-body-and-mind); [Chapter One §6.1.5](../../../core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#615-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle).

**You are not automatically their owner.**
- **Today:** Because you pay a bill, the file lists you as the one who decides the clinician, the cards, and who they may see. Refusal is treated as proof they cannot decide — or as proof you are failing.
- **With this Constitution:** Family relationship is not authority over another adult’s clinician or household. Supported help is not a quiet takeover. A concern report is not an official record of verified help or harm.

See: [CI-15](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_15_neurodiversity_disability_justice_trauma_informed_participation.md); [Chapter Eight §2.1](../../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default).

**A lasting split is not the default “help.”**
- **Today:** The “safe” rest-break is a durable break from the care relationship you actually have — or from the adult you care for.
- **With this Constitution:** A lasting split from someone you depend on, or someone who depends on you, is allowed only if it is actually needed to prevent harm, no bigger than that harm, decided fairly, and open to challenge. A long split has to be looked at again.

See: [Article VII-D](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-d-family-care-relationships-reproductive-autonomy-and-non-separation); [Non-Separation](../../../core_05_band_participation.md#non-separation-constitutional).

## What you might reasonably object to

- **“I still have to do all of it. Naming care labor doesn’t feed anyone.”** A model constitution does not print a paycheck. What it requires of a body that has agreed to follow it is that unpaid care not be invisible, that rest-breaks have an owner, and that survival not be conditioned on that labor. Whether a particular body has taken those duties up is a separate, honest question.

- **“If I ask for rest, they’ll say I’m abandoning them — or they’ll make me the owner.”** Asking for rest is not, by itself, a verified harm finding, and paying a bill is not, by itself, a verified takeover right. Verified harm, if it exists, still has to be verified in the right role.

- **“I want to decide for them. I know better.”** What they can still do tracks what they can actually show, not your convenience. The page for the adult being taken over is [E06](E06_age_related_challenges.md). This text will not make you their owner to settle a preference contest.

- **“This isn’t law in this clinic.”** Correct until a qualifying body adopts it. It does not override applicable health, family, or labor law ([Chapter Fourteen §5](../../../core_14_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)).

- **“Don’t put me in a carer type.”** You do not have to take that label to use this page. This brief is for someone doing unpaid care for an adult, when rest or help is being billed as takeover. It does not change the Rights Floor that applies to you. When unpaid care, rest, or a quiet takeover is actually at stake, those rules are located in the sections on [CI-16](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_16_care_labor_dependent_support_respite_care_economy_fairness.md) and [Article VII-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-a-self-ownership-of-body-and-mind).

## What this will not pretend to give you

- A paid care job, a guaranteed rest roster, or a quieter house as a basic protection.
- Authority over the adult’s clinician, cards, or lease because you help.
- A rule that the adult, a sibling, or a clinic is a harm-doer for existing, offering help, or disagreeing.
- Enforcement against a clinic, employer, or relative who has not adopted it.
