*In plain terms: if a system that matters to your life harmed you, you should be able to find a real way to challenge it without reading the whole rulebook first. A specialist portal, a hop count, or “come back with a lawyer” is not that door. This page points at the rules. It is not already that path.*

This page is for you if something went wrong and you cannot tell where to object, or the only next step is a maze. You do not have to take a label to use it. When the path itself is unusable, those rules are located in the section on [Article XX](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xx-comprehensibility-and-complexity-stewardship) and [Article XII-B](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-b-right-to-challenge-review-and-redress).

## Lost in the maze is ordinary — not a type they get to invent

These figures describe a recognizable situation. They do not change the Rights Floor that applies to you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Alive in it now** | **none** as a census type. Being shut out of a usable challenge path is not a job, a diagnosis, or a household form. |
| **Counted in world output** | **reach, not a production line:** schools, clinics, pay, housing, and the path of about **68 in 100** living humans online already run through systems someone else designed. |
| **Meet it in a full life** | nearly everyone who is cut off, delayed, or sent to a specialist portal they cannot actually use. |

These figures are approximate — nearest 1-in-n or whole percent. Sources: [`SCALE_NOTES.md`](SCALE_NOTES.md).

## Your situation

A cutoff, lockout, or delay hit you. The office says the fix exists. The link 404s, the form never finishes, or the next step is “read this Constitution first.” You are not asking to be sorted into a type. You are asking for a door that works.

If a body that actually runs the systems in your life agreed to follow this Constitution:

**You should not need the whole rulebook to object.**
- **Today:** The only path is a specialist portal, a hop count, or “come back when you have a lawyer.”
- **With this Constitution:** Challenge, review, and being made whole have to work in practice, in language you can actually use. Hiding the home is still a refusal.

See: [Article XX](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xx-comprehensibility-and-complexity-stewardship); [Article XX-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xx-a-proportional-comprehensibility-right); [Article XII-B](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-b-right-to-challenge-review-and-redress); [plain challenge](../../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#plain-challenge).

**Delay is not a neutral wait.**
- **Today:** The inbox is open. The clock is not. Harm keeps compounding while the file sits.
- **With this Constitution:** Published clocks have to be real. A file that never finishes is still a refusal.

See: [G05 — Crisis vs forever](G05_crisis_vs_forever.md); [Delay](../../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#delay).

**A form on paper is not being made whole.**
- **Today:** There is a policy page. Nobody can restore the water, the pay, or the record.
- **With this Constitution:** The path has to have real capacity to deliver, not only a form.

See: [G01 — Challenge, review, and being made whole](G01_challenge_review_redress.md); [Remedy](../../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#remedy).

## What you might reasonably object to

- **“Just read the Constitution first.”** Finding the home is part of the duty, not extra homework they get to assign you. See [Comprehensibility](../../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#comprehensibility).

- **“Don’t put me in a type.”** You do not have to take that label to use this page. This brief is for someone harmed who cannot find the door. It does not change the Rights Floor that applies to you.

## What this will not pretend to give you

- Enforcement against a hospital, platform, lab, or host that has not agreed to follow this Constitution ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)).
- A substitute for ordinary local, national, or international law ([Chapter Fourteen §5](../../../core_14_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)).
- An instant win, or a promise that every challenge is correct.
