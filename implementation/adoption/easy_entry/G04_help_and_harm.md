*In plain terms: when help or harm is serious enough to matter here, verified facts go into official records on two separate tracks — help toward flourishing, and harm plus failures to answer for it. They never fold into one net score. Rumors, reputations, and a filed case are not that record.*

## What is happening

Someone claims you are trusted, or unsafe, because of a story, a flag, or a single number. Good deeds are said to cancel harm. Nothing verified has been written down. Or a filing is treated as if it already decided the case.

## If a body that actually runs the systems in your life agreed to follow this Constitution

**Two tracks, never one net score.**
- **Today:** A dashboard folds praise and harm into one number. The “good” side wipes the harm.
- **With this Constitution:** Help and harm stay separate. Verified good conduct does not erase verified harm.

See: [Chapter Eight](../../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eight-compliance-violation-and-standing-model).

**Rumors are not the record. A filed case is not the record either.**
- **Today:** Gossip, a relative’s letter, or “we filed” is treated as the finding.
- **With this Constitution:** Only verified facts enter the official record of verified help or harm. A report waiting to be looked at is not already a verdict. Filing a case, by itself, does not change how trusted you are.

See: [Chapter Eight §2.1](../../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default); [Chapter Eight §3.6](../../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#36-forum-boundary).

**What follows has to be real on each track.**
- **Today:** A badge with no extra authority, or a punishment with no repair for the one who was hurt.
- **With this Constitution:** Verified help can open real clearance to take on more. Verified harm can limit what you may do, require correction, and require those harmed to be made whole — on its own track.

See: [Chapter Nine](../../../core_09_standing_integration.md#chapter-nine-standing-effects-and-integration).

- A score of how trusted you are that you can spend like money, or that decides whether you count ([Article XVIII-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xviii-a-standing-distinction)).
