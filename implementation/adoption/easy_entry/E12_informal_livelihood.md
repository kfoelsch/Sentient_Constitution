*In plain terms: cash work, unpaid care, and platform gigs still count as work; identity and payment systems are not supposed to trap you; how poor or rich you are cannot be used to close the survival floor — and this is not a geography class.*

## Most work is already informal

These figures describe a recognizable situation. They do not sort you into a named group.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Alive in it now** | about **58 in 100** workers hold informal jobs — about **2 billion**, or **25 in 100** of everyone counted worldwide. |
| **Counted in world output** | the uncounted / “shadow” economy is about **12%** of world output when large economies dominate the average, and about **19%** if every country counts the same. |
| **Meet it in a full life** | in much of the world, most of a working life is already informal. |

The interesting fact is the gap: most workers, a much smaller share of what the books count. Sources: [`SCALE_NOTES.md`](SCALE_NOTES.md).

## Your situation

You sell in a market, cook, build, drive, care, or take platform tasks. Public protections are thin.

If a body that actually runs the systems in your life adopted this Constitution:

**Work is work.**
- **Today:** There is no formal contract, or the contract is a waiver. Someone says informality means you opted out of rights. No contract, no floor.
- **With this Constitution:** Anyone who contributes productive work — wages, contracts, platforms, cooperatives, or comparable arrangements — holds fair compensation, collective organization, safe conditions, and rest. Classification tricks designed to defeat that are out.

See: [Article III-D](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-d-labor-and-economic-floor).

**Poverty is not a lawful gate on survival.**
- **Today:** Being poor is treated as proof you can be priced out of water, shelter, or a clinic. “Unbanked” is used as a lesser tier.
- **With this Constitution:** How poor or rich you are, what you own, or your social standing cannot be used to close the Rights Floor minimums.

See: [Protected Characteristics](../../../core_05_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics-constitutional); [Article III-A](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-a-survival).

**The new app is not allowed to be the only door.**
- **Today:** A new ID wallet, mobile lender, or NGO app is now the gate to water, a stall permit, a remittance, or a clinic queue. A fee or a data-share is the price of staying visible. Modernization means one vendor, opaque formats, and a fee to exit.
- **With this Constitution:** Leaving, taking your records with you, and systems that can actually work with each other have to work in practice. Tricks that make leaving costly or confusing are violations.

See: [Article XIX](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xix-interoperability-portability-and-exit-integrity); [Article XIX-C](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xix-c-anti-lock-in-rule).

**Unpaid care still cannot be the price of staying alive.**
- **Today:** The household absorbs the clinic queue and the water walk, then the earner is told to “just work more.”
- **With this Constitution:** Survival floors cannot be conditioned on care labor.

See: [CI-16](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_16_care_labor_dependent_support_respite_care_economy_fairness.md); [E02](E02_single_parent.md).

**Shared arrangements can stay shared.**
- **Today:** A savings circle or stall cooperative is invisible until it becomes a company.
- **With this Constitution:** Commons and mutual aid are supposed to remain legible without being forced into only state or corporate form, and cannot hide exclusion.

See: [CI-22](../../../corpus_institutions/ci_22_commons_cooperatives_mutual_aid_non_market_governance.md).

## What you might reasonably object to

- **“There is no inspector here. Words on a foreign site do nothing.”** Honest. This Constitution binds a body that has agreed to follow it, not a stall, a ministry, or a telco that did not agree. The case is what a payment system, identity stack, or aid platform that agreed would owe you — not a claim that your city has adopted it.

- **“Formalization is how they tax us out.”** Business creation and collective organization are floors against incumbent freeze-out, not a mandate that every stall become a corporation. CI-22 warns against accidentally criminalizing lawful solidarity. It does not hide a tax holiday.

- **“If I challenge the app, they will shut the SIM.”** Retaliation against good-faith reports is in the challenge floor ([Article XII-B](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-b-right-to-challenge-review-and-redress)). Reachability still depends on a body that actually runs that system and has agreed to follow this Constitution.

- **“This isn’t our law.”** Correct. It does not override applicable law ([Chapter Fourteen §5](../../../core_14_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)). Use inside an existing framework has to stay consistent with that framework.

- **“Call this ‘developing world’ and you have already ranked us.”** You do not have to take that label to use this page. This brief is for someone who sells in a market, cooks, builds, drives, cares, or takes platform tasks, with thin public protections and a newly arrived ID, payment, or aid gate. It does not sort you into a named group. The same lock-in and labor patterns appear in rich cities. When work or leaving an app is actually at stake, those rules are located in the sections on [Article III-D](../../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-d-labor-and-economic-floor) and [Article XIX](../../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xix-interoperability-portability-and-exit-integrity).

## What this will not pretend to give you

- A formal job, a visa, or a public utility this page cannot staff.
- A second, softer rights stack because the state is thin.
- Enforcement against a lender, telco, or NGO that has not adopted it.
- Winning every preference about which app is convenient.
