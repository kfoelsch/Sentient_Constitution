This page is the process-support home for founding institutions while sentients cooperate under this Constitution.

*In plain terms: cooperating sentients get a fill-in path to publish a real Chapter Five Charter and staff real lanes — not a markdown working group that calls itself an oversight body, and not a substitute for a Chapter Fifteen instrument.*

<a id="purpose-and-role"></a>
## Purpose and role

*In plain terms: this page sequences institution foundation. It does not form the institution, and it does not adopt the Constitution.*

This page gives cooperating sentients a fill-in path from operations-guide cooperation toward a published [Chapter Five Charter](../../core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) and the CI duties that Charter triggers. Binding formation fields stay in [CI-3.6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_03_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-36-charter-contents-review-and-formation-template). Formation burden scales with impact under [CI-9.1](../../corpus_institutions/ci_09_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-91-formation-proportionality) and **CJS-3.11**.

This page, the Charter fill-in, and the non-institution banner **cannot narrow core text**. A filled template that diverges from CI-3.6, Chapter Fifteen §10, or Chapter Sixteen §4 is not a compliance path.

---

## 0. What this is not

*In plain terms: this page is a how-to. Filling it out does not found an institution, adopt the Constitution, or give anyone power over people who did not agree.*

Go to the owner files for the real rules. This page does not replace them:

- **What a Charter must say** — [CI-3.6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_03_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-36-charter-contents-review-and-formation-template) is the rulebook for a published [Chapter Five Charter](../../core_05_band_continuity.md#charter): purpose, limits, who is affected, how it can change, and how often it is re-checked. This page helps you fill those fields. It does not rewrite them.
- **Moving systems over later** — [CS-10](../../corpus_systems/cs_10_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-10-1-purpose-and-role) sequences that move **after** a body records a Chapter Fifteen adoption instrument. A Charter draft is not that move.
- **Dispute forums** — [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md) is the process path; [CF-3](../../corpus_forum/cf_03_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md) is how forum families are formed. A forum hears disputes. It is not the institution you are founding here. Name the forum route on the Charter; do not found the forum with this kit.

This kit also does **not**:

- **Adopt the Constitution**
  - Using this page is not [Chapter Fifteen §10](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) adoption.
  - It is not [Chapter Sixteen](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) incorporation (pulling the how-to files into the adopter’s binding set).
- **Create a real institution**
  - The form in [§5](#5-chapter-five-charter-fill-in-not-a-live-charter) is a draft. It is not a live Charter until the body that will actually run the institution publishes it in **its** records.
  - A shared note, group chat, or “we are the oversight body now” file does not make you one.
  - Starting useful work does not make the founding group the boss of other people ([Chapter One §9.5](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#95-aligned-self-organization)).
- **Bind people who did not agree, or mint official files**
  - Host products, labs, vendors, platforms, and other sentients who did not consent are not bound.
  - This page does not create a Chapter Eight standing record (the formal file of contribution or harm), a Chapter Seven system-alignment certificate, or a final decision on the merits.
- **Stand in for a forum, or mix up two different “charter” words**
  - Do not staff a dispute forum from the founding group and call that independent review.
  - A standing score is not a verdict on whether someone is a sentient ([Article XVIII-A](../../core_06-06_rights_part_c.md#article-xviii-a-standing-distinction)).
  - A Chapter Five Charter says what an institution is for and what it may do. A Chapter Twelve **treaty, compact, or charter** is one way a community authorizes who may govern ([Chapter Twelve §1.1](../../core_12-12_governance.md#11-mechanism-families-auditability-and-pluralism)). Filling one is not filling the other.
  - Having a voice inside a body that already exists is not the same as being authorized to govern that body — and authorization does not wipe the voice, challenge, and fair-process duties owed to people the body affects. [Preamble §3.3](../../core_00_preamble.md#governance-layer-discipline) names those two jobs **Constitutional Contract Layer** and **Stakeholder System Participation**.
- **Finish the public edition**
  - This corpus is still **pre-release**. Using this kit does not publish it ([PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md](../PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md)).

## 1. Two tracks (do not merge)

| Track | What you are founding | What it still is not | When it can go live |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Ordinary formation** | A cooperative, mutual-aid network, research body, commons body, or other non-commercial institution under [System Creation](../../core_05_band_participation.md#system-creation-constitutional); or a commercial form under [Business Creation](../../core_05_band_participation.md#business-creation-constitutional). Cooperatives and commons also read [CI-22](../../corpus_institutions/ci_22_commons_cooperatives_mutual_aid_non_market_governance.md). | Not governing authority over non-consenters. Not a forum family. Not CS-10 phase entry. | A qualifying body can bind the named scope, publishes a Chapter Five Charter meeting [CI-3.6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_03_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-36-charter-contents-review-and-formation-template) in **its** custody, and passes the [§4](#4-formation-cliffs-not-a-slope) cliffs. A Chapter Fifteen instrument is **not** required to exist as an ordinary institution. |
| **B. Constitutional-function machinery** | Oversight, standing and challenge routes, amendment and oversight machinery, and other ordinary constitutional operations named in [Article XXVI-B](../../core_06-06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-b-transitional-authority-limits-and-reauthorization). Name the function in [§1.1](#11-track-b-function-pointers-same-kit). | Not headcount of cooperating parties. Not interim authority dressed up as maturity. Not forums (those are [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md) / [CF-3](../../corpus_forum/cf_03_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md) / [Chapter Eleven](../../core_11-11_forum.md#chapter-eleven-forums-and-jurisdiction)). Not a new kit per function. | A qualifying adopter already holds a recorded [§10.1–§10.2](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#101-deliberate-adoption) instrument. Then the same Charter / lane / cliff path as Track A, scaled to the function’s class. Drafting Track B before an instrument is scaffolding only. |

**Same duties for both kinds of steward.** [Chapter One §9.1.1](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#911-shared-stewardship-standard). Do not invent an AI-only overlay. Do not exempt humans.

**Layer check.** Founding who may govern, by what legitimacy mechanism, on what durable terms, is [Constitutional Contract Layer](../../core_00_preamble.md#governance-layer-discipline) work. Giving affected sentients voice, contest, and due process *inside* an already-authorized body is Stakeholder System Participation. Do not use a participation vote as a substitute for authorization, and do not treat authorization as wiping participation duties.

**Two “charter” words.** A [Chapter Five Charter](../../core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) is the published scope instrument for a system, institution, or business. A Chapter Twelve **treaty, compact, or charter** is a legitimacy-mechanism family for authorizing governing authority ([Chapter Twelve §1.1](../../core_12-12_governance.md#11-mechanism-families-auditability-and-pluralism)). A Track A body often needs the first. A Track B body, and any body that will govern, also needs a documented legitimacy mechanism — which may be the second, or another §1.1 family. Filling one is not filling the other.

```text
Track A (ordinary institution)
        fitness + own-conduct (existing kits; still not adoption)
                ↓
        qualifying body can bind the named scope
                ↓
        Charter + cliffs (this kit) → live Chapter Five Charter in that body's custody

Track B (constitutional-function machinery)
        recorded Chapter Fifteen instrument (first-adopter / scaling kits)
                ↓
        name the function (§1.1) — still this kit
                ↓
        Charter + cliffs for the function (this kit)
                ↓
        CS-10 for systems under that instrument
        CI-14 for institutional transition
        Article XXVI-B: shrink interim authority as the machinery actually works
```

## 3. Formation sequence

Use this after the parties know which track they are on. Two parties start from [`FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md). More than two start from [`COOPERATION_SCALING_KIT.md`](COOPERATION_SCALING_KIT.md).

1. **Pin the edition.** Name `SC-Corpus-2026.08.09` unless a later publication cut exists. Do not mix editions.
2. **Name the body and the track.** Ordinary formation (Track A) or constitutional-function machinery (Track B). If Track B, name the function in [§1.1](#11-track-b-function-pointers-same-kit) and read that owner. Do not open a new kit. If Track B and there is no recorded instrument, stop at scaffolding. Attach the [non-institution banner](#9-non-institution-banner).
3. **Name classification honestly.** [CI-9.1](../../corpus_institutions/ci_09_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-91-formation-proportionality): low-impact scopes stay simple; high-dependency **Class A** / **Class B** scopes take deeper diligence. Do not pick a lower class to dodge burden. If class is not yet known, say so; auditability still scales with material impact.
4. **Fill the Chapter Five Charter fields** in [§5](#5-chapter-five-charter-fill-in-not-a-live-charter). Copy the completed block into the body’s custody. Leaving a filled copy in this repo is not publication.
5. **Apply the [§4](#4-formation-cliffs-not-a-slope) cliffs** before claiming a live institution. Fail any cliff → scaffolding only.
6. **Staff lanes, appointment, contest, and remedy** as [§6](#6-lanes-appointment-contest-remedy) requires. A paper Charter with the founding crew in every lane is not formation.
7. **If the work is a forum family, leave this kit.** Open [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). Binding formation stays in [CF-3](../../corpus_forum/cf_03_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md). See [§7](#7-forums-are-not-this-kit).
8. **After a live Charter**, use [§8](#8-after-a-live-charter) for CS-10, CI-14, and the Article XXVI-B progress measure.

[Chapter One §9.5](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#95-aligned-self-organization) still lets sentients *start* low-risk work without incumbent permission. Starting that work is not appointment to governing authority.

## 4. Formation cliffs (not a slope)

Advancement from a draft to a live institution must **not** rest on elapsed time, convenience, or headcount alone. Party count is not a legitimacy mechanism.

**Constitutional Tetrad.** Participation upgrades when more sentients are affected. Oversight stays independent of the founding crew. Accountability still needs a body that can bind the named scope. Timeliness uses these gates, not “we have cooperated long enough.”

| Gate | Pass | Fail → scaffolding only |
|---|---|---|
| **Track honesty** | Track A, or Track B with a recorded Chapter Fifteen instrument. | Track B claimed with no instrument. A draft Charter labeled “constitutional oversight.” |
| **Qualifying body** | A body that can bind the named scope ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) when the claim is adoption-backed; otherwise the body that will actually operate the institution). That body holds the Charter in **its** custody. | Stewards listing themselves as the institution. *n* declarations stapled together. |
| **Chapter Five Charter** | Published fields matching [CI-3.6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_03_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-36-charter-contents-review-and-formation-template). If this kit and CI-3.6 disagree, CI-3.6 wins. | Mission slogan, marketing copy, or a filled template left in `implementation/adoption/`. |
| **Classification / burden** | Class or “not yet classified” is stated; formation burden matches [CI-9.1](../../corpus_institutions/ci_09_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-91-formation-proportionality). | Lower-class label used to skip high-impact duties. |
| **Lanes** | Direction, execution, challenge, assurance, and publication are hosted and published ([CI-3.2](../../corpus_institutions/ci_03_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-32-functional-separation-lanes)). Merged hosting has named independence safeguards. | Founding crew holds every lane with no safeguard. |
| **Independent review** | A published backup, external reviewer, or forum family the founding crew does not solely staff. [Chapter Eleven §3](../../core_11-11_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination); [Chapter Fifteen Test 4](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#6-test-4-contestability-and-independent-review-validity). Keep [Article XII-B](../../core_06-06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-b-right-to-challenge-review-and-redress) reachable. | The only merits path is the founding crew. |
| **Legitimacy (if governing)** | If the body will exercise governing authority, a documented [Chapter Twelve §1.1](../../core_12-12_governance.md#11-mechanism-families-auditability-and-pluralism) mechanism is published. A polity also needs the [§10.1](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#101-deliberate-adoption) affected-sentient participation check. | Silence, usage, marketing alignment, or “more of us now.” A Chapter Five Charter offered as that mechanism. |
| **Remedy capacity** | Practical capacity to deliver challenge and redress ([Chapter Nine §9](../../core_09-09_standing_integration.md#9-enforcement-realism)). | Named routes with no capacity. |
| **No self-appointment** | Effects that bind others, certify, or decide merits use the separate lawful authority assigned by this Constitution ([Chapter One §9.5](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#95-aligned-self-organization)). | “We founded it, so we govern it.” |

Fail any row: attach the [non-institution banner](#9-non-institution-banner). Ordinary cooperation may continue as operations-guide use plus declarations.

```text
FORMATION CHECK (complete before claiming a live institution)
[ ] Track A, or Track B with a recorded Chapter Fifteen instrument.
[ ] A body can bind the named scope and will hold the Charter in its custody.
[ ] Chapter Five Charter fields match CI-3.6 (CI-3.6 wins on conflict).
[ ] Class / burden is honest under CI-9.1.
[ ] Lanes are published; merged hosting has independence safeguards.
[ ] Independent review is not solely the founding crew.
[ ] If this body will govern, a Chapter Twelve §1.1 mechanism is published
    (not the Chapter Five Charter standing in for it).
[ ] Remedy capacity is practical, not paper-only.
[ ] Nobody is treating founding-crew self-organization as appointment.

If any box is unchecked, stop. Scaffolding + non-institution banner only.
```

## 7. Forums are not this kit

[Chapter Eleven](../../core_11-11_forum.md#chapter-eleven-forums-and-jurisdiction) and [CF-3](../../corpus_forum/cf_03_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md) own forum-family formation. The process-support path is [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). [Chapter Eleven §4.3](../../core_11-11_forum.md#43-institutional-forums) hears institutional-mandate and supervised-scope disputes; it is not the institution itself.

Do not staff a forum family from the cooperating crew and call that independent review. Do not invent a multi-party forum as ceremony. If the work is actually a forum family, stop here and open the forum kit. Institutional forums remain a *route* named in CI-3.6 field 9 — they are not founded by filling this kit.

## 8. After a live Charter

A live Chapter Five Charter is still not Chapter Fifteen adoption, not CS-10 phase entry, and not certification.

- **Systems under a recorded instrument** migrate under [CS-10](../../corpus_systems/cs_10_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-10-1-purpose-and-role). Do not reuse CS-10 phase names (preparation, limited adoption, expanded adoption, steady state) as a metaphor for “we founded a body.” Do not use a 2026-04 planning annex as a CS-10 substitute.
- **Institutional redesign, merger, or handoff** uses [CI-14](../../corpus_institutions/ci_14_transitional_governance_institutional_evolution.md). “We are in transition” is not a license to skip named interim authority, sunset, or contest paths.
- **Progress** under [Article XXVI-B](../../core_06-06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-b-transitional-authority-limits-and-reauthorization) is shrinking interim authority and increasing functionality of constitutional institutions and processes — forums, standing and challenge routes, amendment and oversight machinery — not headcount of cooperating parties, and not the number of filled templates.

## 10. Honest statements (copy if they are true)

1. **Scaffolding.** We are drafting a Chapter Five Charter for a named body. We will not treat this markdown as that Charter, as a forum family, or as appointment.
2. **Track A.** We are founding an ordinary institution under System Creation / Business Creation / CI-22. We can bind the named scope. We will publish the Charter in that body’s custody and pass the formation cliffs.
3. **Track B.** A qualifying adopter already holds a recorded Chapter Fifteen instrument. We named the function in §1.1. We are founding that machinery under the instrument — not as a substitute for it, and not by opening a new kit.
4. **Stop.** We cannot bind the named scope, or we cannot name independent review outside the founding crew. Scaffolding plus banners only.
