<a id="purpose-and-role"></a>
## Purpose and role

*In plain terms: this page supplies words and routing. It does not found, adopt, or certify anything.*

This Constitution is a **model constitution** for systems that materially affect sentients ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)). It becomes enforceable for a particular body only after that body records a [Chapter Fifteen §10](../../core_15_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) instrument in its own custody. Until then the honest public invitation is: read it, use it as an operations guide, and say so plainly.

Public readers land at [`../../START_HERE.md`](../../START_HERE.md). Fit ranking stays in [`FIT_SITUATIONS.md`](FIT_SITUATIONS.md). This page does not add adopter types, duties, or a compliance path.

<a id="1-the-announcement"></a>
## 1. The announcement

*In plain terms: this is the copy. Post it as written, or shorten it. Do not lengthen it into a second constitution.*

```text
The Sentient Constitution — pre-release

The Sentient Constitution is a draft model constitution for systems
that materially affect sentient beings. It is written for the people
and AI systems that build, run, and oversee those systems.

It sets out four duties — participation, oversight, accountability,
and timeliness — that grow with how much is at stake. Human and AI
stewards are held to the same standard: no separate rulebook for AI,
no exemption for humans.

How it works

The Constitution defines every term it relies on — more than two
hundred — once, in one place. Each definition says what the term
covers, what it does not, and how to check whether it is being met.
Other parts of the text may apply a term, but may not quietly redefine
it. (Chapter Five)

On that base, three processes carry most of the weight:

- System alignment certification. Before people rely on a system at
  scale, it is checked against what the Constitution requires, under
  the supervision of an independent forum. The check is as demanding
  as the system's real impact, is written down, is time-limited, and
  must be repeated on a schedule that matches the risk. (Chapter Seven)
- Standing records. What a person, organization, or system has done —
  verified help and verified harm — is kept on the record as separate
  entries, so neither erases the other. Consequences follow from the
  record, not from reputation or a popularity score. (Chapters Eight
  and Nine)
- Contestability. Anyone affected by a system or a decision can
  challenge it, have it reviewed by people who did not make it, and
  get it corrected within set time limits. (Chapter Eleven)

What "pre-release" means

- Edition SC-Corpus-2026.08.09. Section links may change in later cuts.
- The core text (the Preamble and Chapters One through Sixteen) is
  the binding source and the most complete part.
- The operational text around it (companion implementation files,
  steward doors, adoption kits) is still a work in progress. If they
  disagree with the core, the core wins. Those files say how to carry
  out what the core already requires; they may not change what it means.

What it is not

- It is not law. It binds no one who has not adopted it.
- Reading it, forking it, or saying "we follow it" is not adoption.
  Adoption happens only when a body that can bind its own systems
  records a formal instrument under Chapter Fifteen.
- It does not supersede local, national, or international law.
  Adoption binds only the adopting body, and only as far as that
  body already has lawful authority to bind itself. Where the
  Constitution is used inside an existing legal framework, its
  processes and participation must stay consistent with that
  framework. (Chapter Fourteen)
- It does not bind vendors, platforms, or anyone else who did not
  agree to it.

Start: START_HERE.md
Where it fits: implementation/adoption/FIT_SITUATIONS.md
License: CC BY 4.0
```

Short form (repository description, social preview):

```text
Pre-release model constitution for systems that materially affect
sentient beings. Four duties, one standard for human and AI stewards.
Core text is binding and most complete; operational text is still in
progress. Reading or forking this is not adoption. Does not supersede
applicable law. CC BY 4.0.
```

Any shorter version must still say **pre-release**, **not adoption**, **does not supersede applicable law**, and **core text controls**.

## 2. Say and do not say

*In plain terms: every public note carries the left-hand column. Do not trade a line away to sound finished.*

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| **Pre-release.** Edition `SC-Corpus-2026.08.09`; section links may change. | “This is the launch.” “This is the publication cut.” |
| **Model constitution.** It may guide now. It binds a body only after that body records a Chapter Fifteen instrument ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)). | “We launched it as law.” “Our org / city / protocol has adopted it” — unless that instrument exists. |
| **Does not supersede applicable law.** Publication is not a claim of supremacy over local, national, or international law. Adoption binds only the adopting body, within its lawful authority and recorded scope ([Chapter Fourteen §5](../../core_14_expansion_supremacy.md#5-relation-to-applicable-external-law)). Where used inside an external legal framework, processes and participation must stay consistent with that framework. | “This Constitution now overrides city / national / treaty law.” “Using it as an operations guide displaces our regulator.” |
| **Core text controls.** If an operational file and a numbered `core_*` file disagree, the `core_*` file wins. Companion files spell out how to carry out what the core already requires; they may not change what those terms mean, shrink those duties, or invent a second set of rights ([Chapter Sixteen](../../core_16_incorporation.md#chapter-sixteen-incorporation-bridge)). | “The whole corpus is ready to implement.” “The companion files are unofficial.” |
| **Operations-guide use** is the near-term invitation ([FIT §2](FIT_SITUATIONS.md#2-two-modes)). Attach the [non-adoption banner](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#6-non-adoption-banner) to any shared file that is not a completed instrument. | “We follow it” as a marketing line ([FIT §5](FIT_SITUATIONS.md#5-honest-non-fits)). |
| **Binds only what you run.** Vendors, host products, and non-consenting parties stay unbound. | “Labs and vendors in our stack are now bound.” |
| **Same standard for both kinds of steward** ([Chapter One §9.1.1](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#911-shared-stewardship-standard)). | “AI stewards follow a separate ethics overlay.” |
| **Who may govern is a documented legitimacy mechanism** ([Preamble §3.3](../../core_00_preamble.md#33-governance-layers)). | “A token vote / standing score authorized this.” |
| **Independent review** is an external reviewer or published backup the founding crew does not solely staff ([FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md) Track A). | “Our Slack / markdown panel is independent review.” |
| **AI handoff trials remain open** ([PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md](../PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md) §6.5). | “AI handoff is ready.” |
| **Describe the working parts** — definitions with a way to check them ([Chapter Five](../../core_05__definitions_home.md)); certification ([Chapter Seven](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md)); standing records ([Chapter Eight](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md)); contestability ([Chapter Eleven](../../core_11_forum.md)) — as verified, written down, time-limited, and challengeable. | “Simple, proven, and effective.” No adopter has run them yet. |
| **License:** [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE). Attribution, not a second constitution. | — |

Quote the Preamble in short authentic spans. Do not paraphrase it into marketing.

## 4. Publishing checklist

*In plain terms: one or two existing venues per audience. Keep the contact list outside the corpus.*

- Post into an existing list or forum for that audience, preferably with a personal introduction. Do not spray.
- Keep a dated outreach log **outside** this corpus. A contact list is not a Chapter Eight standing record.
- After a publication cut, refresh the edition pin and status lines. That is a second announcement, not this one.

GitHub settings once the repository is public (none of these drop **pre-release**):

| Surface | Set to |
|---|---|
| **Description / social preview** | The short form in [§1](#1-the-announcement); link [`../../START_HERE.md`](../../START_HERE.md) |
| **Topics** | `constitution`, `ai-governance`, `stewardship`, `commons` |
