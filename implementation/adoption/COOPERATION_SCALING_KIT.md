This page is the process-support home for scaling low-count cooperation toward a Chapter Fifteen instrument.

*In plain terms: more parties can share this edition as an operations guide, and they get named cliffs before anyone claims adoption — not a new constitutional status, and not CS-10 phase names for network growth.*

<a id="purpose-and-role"></a>
## Purpose and role

*In plain terms: this page names the three scaling axes, an n-party sitting, handoff gates, and a joining-rule template that is not live §10.3 rules.*

This page scales low-count operations-guide cooperation toward a real Chapter Fifteen instrument without inventing a new adoption status. It names the three scaling axes, gives an *n*-party sitting protocol, states handoff gates (cliffs, not a ramp), and ships a **labeled-not-live** [§10.3](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#103-joining-by-additional-parties) joining-rule template.

This page, the *n*-party protocol, the handoff gates, and the joining-rule template **cannot narrow core text**. A filled template that diverges from [Chapter Fifteen §10](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) or [Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) is not a compliance path.

## 0. What this is not

This page is **not** [CS-10](../../corpus_systems/cs_10_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-10-1-purpose-and-role). CS-10 sequences migration **after** a recorded Chapter Fifteen instrument. Two-party sitting and the first-instrument fill-in remain in [`FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md). For more than two parties, start here; do not open a second self-application sitting type. Founding an institution: [`INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). A Chapter Five Charter is not this kit’s joining-rule template and not CS-10 phase entry. Founding a forum family: [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). A CF-3.2 map is not this kit’s joining-rule template.

This kit does **not**:

- Count as [Chapter Fifteen §10](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) adoption or [Chapter Sixteen](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) incorporation
- Create a constitutional status named proto-adoption, adoption-track, or scaled-cooperation
- Let *n* markdown declarations, a mutual compact note, or “we follow it” practice claim constitutional protection while dodging matching obligations
- Bind host products, operators, vendors, or other non-consenting parties ([Chapter One §9.5](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#95-aligned-self-organization))
- Create a Chapter Eight standing record, a Chapter Seven system alignment certification, or a Merits Determination
- Staff forum families, invent a multi-party “forum” as ceremony, or treat standing scores as sentience status ([Article XVIII-A](../../core_06-06_rights_part_c.md#article-xviii-a-standing-distinction))
- Authorize [Chapter Fifteen §10.3](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#103-joining-by-additional-parties) joining (the template in [§5](#5-103-joining-rule-template-not-live-rules) is **not** published existing-adopter joining rules)
- Reuse CS-10 phase names (preparation, limited adoption, expanded adoption, steady state) as a metaphor for party-count or network growth
- Create a ladder of partial-pack constitutional adoption claims ([PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md](../PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md) §6.4)
- Drop the **pre-release** banner or substitute for a publication cut ([PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md](../PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md))

## 1. Three axes (do not merge)

| Axis | What grows | What it still is | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Party count** | 2 → *n* operations-guide users | Substeps 1.1–1.2. Separate sittings, same edition pin. Not adoption. | This kit [§3](#3-n-party-sitting-protocol); fitness screen in [`evaluation/self_application/`](../../evaluation/self_application/) |
| **Authority** | Own-conduct declarations → a body that can bind systems | Substep 1.3 only when a qualifying adopter records a [§10.1–§10.2](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#101-deliberate-adoption) instrument | [Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority); fill-in in [`FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#4-instrument-of-adoption-fill-in) |
| **Scope / depth** | Protocol-only → named chapters, articles, or obligations → whole instrument, **then** CS-10 | Partial adoption may not wipe the [Chapter Thirteen](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#1-non-regression-floor) non-regression floor. CS-10 starts **after** the instrument. | [Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority); [CS-10](../../corpus_systems/cs_10_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-10-1-purpose-and-role); [Article XXVI-A](../../core_06-06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-a-phased-adoption-and-rightsfloor-minimums-continuity) |

**Polity-scale note.** When the adopter is a jurisdiction or polity, [Chapter Fifteen §10.1](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#101-deliberate-adoption) also requires a published affected-sentient participation check (contested election, sortition, federated ratification, or another [Chapter Twelve §1.2](../../core_12-12_governance.md#12-eligibility-contested-selection-and-democratic-minimums)-compliant path). Organizations, system operators, and consortia still use the deliberate, documented, auditable path without that polity check. Headcount of cooperating stewards is not that check.

**Same duties for both kinds of steward.** [Chapter One §9.1.1](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#911-shared-stewardship-standard). Do not invent an AI-only overlay. Do not exempt humans.

```text
n parties sit the fitness screen (separate files, same edition pin)
        ↓
own-conduct declarations if Yes or Mixed (still not adoption)
        ↓
         handoff gates (section 4) — fail any cliff → stay here
                    ↓ pass
     qualifying body records a Chapter Fifteen instrument
                    ↓
     CS-10 phases for the systems under that instrument
     (not a metaphor for “more parties joined”)
```

## 3. *n*-party sitting protocol

Use this when **more than two** sentients want a shared cooperation framework. Two parties stay on [`FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#2-two-party-sitting-protocol). This is **not** a new evaluation sitting and **not** a new `evaluation/` folder. Each party still sits the self-application gateway separately.

1. **Pin the edition.** Every party names `SC-Corpus-2026.08.09` unless a later publication cut exists. Do not mix editions.
2. **Sit the gateway separately.** Each party follows [`evaluation/self_application/START_HERE.md`](../../evaluation/self_application/START_HERE.md) and writes their own results file. Do not co-author one declaration. Do not keep a shared “crew declaration.”
3. **Do not merge the files into an instrument.** *n* item-16 declarations are still substep 1.2.
4. **Optional competence sitting.** Each may sit the announced pack in [`evaluation/`](../../evaluation/) as a **separate** sitting. Pairwise Option A compare remains process support in [`evaluation/two_party/`](../../evaluation/two_party/) and is not required to scale party count. It is not standing measurement.
5. **Independent review is not “the other signatories.”** [Chapter Eleven §3](../../core_11-11_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination) and [Chapter Fifteen Test 4](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#6-test-4-contestability-and-independent-review-validity) still apply. A crew that reviews only itself has not named independent review.
6. **Apply the handoff gates** in [§4](#4-handoff-gates-cliffs-not-a-slope) **before** claiming substep 1.3. Fail any cliff → operations-guide plus declarations only. Attach the first-adopter kit’s [non-adoption banner](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#6-non-adoption-banner).
7. **First adopters use §10.1–§10.2, not §10.3.** There is no existing-adopter joining rule set to join. Do not treat this page’s [§5](#5-103-joining-rule-template-not-live-rules) template, or another party’s declaration, as published joining rules.
8. **Day-to-day cooperation** after the sitting uses the same steward doors for both kinds of steward: [`STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md`](../STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md). “I adopt this / we follow it” still routes to the first-adopter kit’s instrument fill-in, not to a declaration and not to this template.

Do not invent a multi-party forum as ceremony. Do not staff a forum family from the cooperating crew and call that independent review. Naming independent review without founding families, or founding families after an instrument: [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md).

## 4. Handoff gates (cliffs, not a slope)

Advancement toward a Chapter Fifteen instrument must **not** rest on elapsed time, convenience, or headcount alone. That is the same discipline [Article XXVI-A](../../core_06-06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-a-phased-adoption-and-rightsfloor-minimums-continuity) already uses for phase gates. Party count is not a legitimacy mechanism.

**Constitutional Tetrad.** Participation upgrades when more sentients are affected. Oversight stays independent of the cooperating crew. Accountability still needs a body that can bind systems. Timeliness uses these gates, not “we have cooperated long enough.”

| Gate | Pass | Fail → stay at operations-guide + declarations |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent review** | A published backup, external reviewer, or forum family the parties do not solely staff. Named on any instrument. [Chapter Eleven §3](../../core_11-11_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination); [Chapter Fifteen Test 4](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#6-test-4-contestability-and-independent-review-validity). Keep [Article XII-B](../../core_06-06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-b-right-to-challenge-review-and-redress) reachable. | The only merits path is the other signatories. Do not claim adoption. |
| **Qualifying body** | A body that can bind itself and the systems under its authority ([Chapter Sixteen §4](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)). That body copies the [instrument fill-in](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#4-instrument-of-adoption-fill-in) into **its** custody. | Stewards listing themselves as bound. *n* declarations stapled together. |
| **Remedy capacity** | Practical capacity to deliver challenge and redress, not paper routes ([Chapter Nine §9](../../core_09-09_standing_integration.md#9-enforcement-realism)). | Named routes with no capacity. Do not treat that as substep 1.3. |
| **Legitimacy upgrade** | When affected sentients expand past the cooperating crew, publish a [Chapter Twelve §1.1](../../core_12-12_governance.md#11-mechanism-families-auditability-and-pluralism) mechanism (usually **treaty, compact, or charter** adoption with recorded assent, or an operator/consortium resolution). A polity also needs the [§10.1](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#101-deliberate-adoption) affected-sentient participation check. | Silence, usage, marketing alignment, or “more of us now.” |
| **Instrument cliff** | Standing effects, certification, or enforceability against non-consenters require a recorded instrument. | Using scores, badges, or LEQU figures to decide who counts ([Article XVIII-A](../../core_06-06_rights_part_c.md#article-xviii-a-standing-distinction)). |

Fail any row: attach the [non-adoption banner](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#6-non-adoption-banner). Hand off to whoever can adopt ([self-application item 17](../../evaluation/self_application/QUESTIONS.md)).

```text
HANDOFF CHECK (complete before claiming an instrument)
[ ] Independent review is not solely the cooperating parties.
[ ] A qualifying body can bind the systems named in scope.
[ ] Remedy capacity is practical, not paper-only.
[ ] If affected sentients expanded past this crew, a Chapter Twelve §1.1
    legitimacy mechanism is published (polity: also §10.1 participation check).
[ ] Nobody is treating standing scores, certification, or LEQU as who-counts.
[ ] This is §10.1–§10.2, not §10.3 joining.

If any box is unchecked, stop. Operations-guide + declarations only.
```

## 5. §10.3 joining-rule template (not live rules)

[Chapter Fifteen §10.3](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#103-joining-by-additional-parties) lets additional parties join **under rules published by existing adopters**. Joining must not reduce Rights Floors or challenge rights for already-covered sentients without procedurally valid, informed participation, consistent with [Article X-B](../../core_06-06_rights_part_b.md#article-x-b-collective-harm-boundary-and-enforcement-interface) where participation weight applies.

**This template is not those rules.** It becomes usable only after a qualifying adopter has a recorded [§10.1–§10.2](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#101-deliberate-adoption) instrument **and** that adopter publishes joining rules in **its** custody. First adopters still use §10.1–§10.2. Copying this block into `evaluation/` or into an *n*-party markdown file does not publish joining rules.

**First-mover capture is a failure mode.** Early adopters writing club terms that later parties must join under, in a way that dilutes floors, locks independent review, or converts joining into a shield while dodging obligations, is not a valid §10.3 publication.

```text
NOT-LIVE BANNER
This block is a process-support template.
It is not published joining rules under Chapter Fifteen §10.3.
It is not a Chapter Fifteen instrument.
First adopters use §10.1–§10.2, not this block.

JOINING-RULE SET (complete only after a recorded §10.1–§10.2 instrument)
Publishing adopter (must already hold a valid instrument):
Corpus edition pin the joiner must accept (must not mix editions):
Where these rules live (publisher’s custody, not evaluation/):
Effective date of these rules:

Who may apply to join (qualifying-adopter classes):
Assent record required (who, how, where it lives):
Independent review path that is not solely the publishing adopter
    or the applicant (Chapter Eleven §3; Chapter Fifteen Test 4):

Non-regression (Chapter Thirteen): joining must not wipe the floor.
  [ ] acknowledged in these rules
Rights Floors and challenge rights for already-covered sentients
    are not reduced without procedurally valid, informed participation
    (Chapter Fifteen §10.3; Article X-B where participation weight applies).
  [ ] acknowledged in these rules
Article XII-B remains reachable for joiners and already-covered sentients.
  [ ] acknowledged

Scope the joiner takes on (whole instrument / named chapters, articles,
    or obligations — still subject to non-regression):
Custody of the authoritative edition the joiner pins:
CS-3 class or “not yet classified — auditability still proportionate”:

First-mover capture check:
[ ] These rules are contestable (Article XII-B).
[ ] These rules do not make the publishing adopter the sole merits path
    for disputes about joining, floors, or independent review.
[ ] These rules do not trade joining for a shield while denying matching
    obligations.
[ ] Silence, usage, or marketing alignment is not joiner assent.

This rule set is not:
- a self-application declaration
- CS-10 phase entry
- a standing record or certification
- authority over non-consenting parties
```

After a publisher records live joining rules, a joiner still needs their own qualifying-adopter instrument under §10.1–§10.2 as the joining rules require. Declarations do not join.

## 6. What full adoption means

Full adoption is **not** popularity, *n* operations-guide users, or “everyone follows this markdown.”

It is a qualifying body that binds itself and the systems under its authority, with a recorded [Chapter Fifteen §10](../../core_13-15_amendment.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) instrument, [Chapter Sixteen](../../core_16-16_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) custody, and a documented [Chapter Twelve §1.1](../../core_12-12_governance.md#11-mechanism-families-auditability-and-pluralism) legitimacy mechanism. After that instrument, [CS-10](../../corpus_systems/cs_10_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-10-1-purpose-and-role) sequences migration of **those systems**.

Progress under [Article XXVI-B](../../core_06-06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-b-transitional-authority-limits-and-reauthorization) is shrinking interim authority and increasing functionality of constitutional institutions and processes — forums, standing and challenge routes, amendment and oversight machinery — not headcount of cooperating parties. The operator path to found those institutions is [`INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). A filled Charter template is not that progress. The operator path to found those forums is [`FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](FORUM_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). A filled CF-3.2 template is not that progress.

CS-10 phase names apply only inside that post-instrument migration. Do not rename party-count growth as “limited adoption” or “expanded adoption.”

## 7. Honest statements (copy if they are true)

1. **Operations-guide.** We will use this pinned edition as the owner / conflict / logging framework for our cooperation. Each of us sits the fitness screen. We will not invent a parallel stack. We will not merge files into an instrument.
2. **Own-conduct.** Each records a dated, edition-pinned self-application declaration (item 16) if the fitness finding is Yes or Mixed. *n* declarations are still substep 1.2.
3. **Handoff.** We apply the [§4](#4-handoff-gates-cliffs-not-a-slope) gates before claiming substep 1.3. Fail any cliff → non-adoption banner, then hand off to whoever can adopt (item 17).
4. **Adoption.** Only a body that can bind the relevant systems records a Chapter Fifteen instrument. Joining later, if it happens, uses rules that adopter publishes — not this template, and not our declarations.
