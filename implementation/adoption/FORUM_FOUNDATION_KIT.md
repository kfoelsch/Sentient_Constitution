This page is the process-support home for naming independent review and founding forum families while sentients cooperate under this Constitution.

*In plain terms: cooperating sentients get a fill-in path to name a real independent-review path, and — after a recorded Chapter Seventeen adoption document — to publish a real Chapter Thirteen forum structure. This page is not a markdown panel that calls itself a forum family, and not a substitute for CF-13.405 or Chapter Thirteen.*

## Purpose and role

*In plain terms: this page sequences independent-review naming and forum-family foundation. It does not form the forum, and it does not adopt the Constitution.*

This page gives cooperating sentients a fill-in path from operations-guide cooperation toward (1) a named independent-review path that is not the cooperating crew, and (2) a published [CF-13.405 §2](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-2-family-to-forum-structure-translation-map) forum-structure map after a qualifying adopter records a Chapter Seventeen adoption document. Binding family inventory, non-collapse, and map fields stay in [CF-13.405](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md). Binding routing stays in [Chapter Thirteen](../../core_13.000_forum.md#chapter-thirteen-forums-and-jurisdiction).

This page, the naming block, the map fill-in, and the non-forum banner **cannot narrow core text**. A filled template that diverges from CF-13.405, Chapter Thirteen, Chapter Seventeen §10, or Chapter Eighteen §4 is not a compliance path.

## 0. What this is not

*In plain terms: this page is a how-to. Filling it out does not found a forum family, adopt the Constitution, or give anyone power to hear other people’s disputes.*

Go to the owner files for the real rules. This page does not replace them:

- **What a forum structure must say** — [CF-13.405](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md) is the rulebook for a published forum-structure map: which body does each family’s work, how it is organized, who may decide, where intake lives, and where appeal and backup go. This page helps you fill those fields. It does not rewrite them.
- **Which family hears what** — [Chapter Thirteen](../../core_13.000_forum.md#chapter-thirteen-forums-and-jurisdiction) owns default venue, primary-stakes routing, intake triage, and cross-forum anti-self-judging. This page does not pick a lead family.
- **How a panel is lawfully constituted** — [CF-13.410](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md) owns panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful benches. A named crew is not a panel.
- **Founding an institution** — [`INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md) and [CI-8.205 §6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_08.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-8205-6-system-and-institutional-charter-contents-review-and-formation-template) own Chapter Five Institutional Charters. A forum hears disputes. It is not the institution whose mandate is in dispute. [Chapter Thirteen §4.3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#43-institutional-forums) hears institutional-mandate fights; it is not the institution itself.
- **Moving systems over later** — [CS-6.350](../../corpus_systems/cs_06.350_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-1-purpose-and-role) sequences that move **after** a body records a Chapter Seventeen adoption document. A forum-structure draft is not that move.

This kit also does **not**:

- **Adopt the Constitution**
  - Using this page is not [Chapter Seventeen §10](../../core_17.000_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) adoption.
  - It is not [Chapter Eighteen](../../core_18.000_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) incorporation.
- **Create a live forum family**
  - The form in [§6](#6-forum-structure-map-fill-in-not-a-live-map) is a draft. It is not a live CF-13.405 §2 map until the adopting entity publishes it in **its** records.
  - A shared note, group chat, or “we are the independent review panel now” file does not make you one.
  - Starting useful dispute-handling work does not make the founding group the merits bench ([Chapter One §17.4](../../core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#174-aligned-self-organization)).
- **Bind people who did not agree, or mint official files**
  - Host products, labs, vendors, platforms, and other sentients who did not consent are not bound.
  - This page does not create a Chapter Ten standing record, a Chapter Nine system-alignment certificate, a [forum case record](../../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record), or a [Merits Determination](../../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#merits-determination).
  - A filed case is not standing by itself ([Chapter Thirteen §2.3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#23-forum-case-records-standing-records-and-contests); [Chapter Ten §3.6](../../core_10.000_standing_assessment.md#36-forum-boundary)).
- **Stand in for an institution, collapse families, or invent a seventh family**
  - Do not staff a forum family from the cooperating crew and call that independent review.
  - Do not invent a two-party or *n*-party “forum” as ceremony.
  - Community governance bodies are not a seventh forum family ([Chapter Thirteen §4.1](../../core_13.000_forum.md#41-sentient-forums)).
  - An intake triage body is a family’s first-touch desk. It is not a separate constitutional forum family ([Chapter Thirteen §2](../../core_13.000_forum.md#2-default-venue-and-primary-stakes)).
  - A standing score is not a verdict on whether someone is a sentient ([Article XIX-A](../../core_06.015_rights_part_d.md#article-xix-a-standing-distinction)).
  - Having a voice inside a body that already hears disputes is not the same as being authorized to constitute that body — and authorization does not wipe the voice, challenge, and fair-process duties owed to people the body affects. [Preamble §3.3](../../core_00.000_preamble.md#33-governance-layers) names those two jobs **Constitutional Contract Layer** and **Stakeholder System Participation**.
- **Finish the public edition**
  - This corpus is still **pre-release**. Using this kit does not publish it ([PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md](../PRE_PUBLICATION_SPEC.md)).

## 1. Two tracks (do not merge)

First adopters need independent review *before* they can record a valid adoption document. Live Chapter Thirteen forum families are what an **adopting entity** must then publish. Those are two jobs. Do not use a ceremony “forum” to skip either one.

| Track | What you are naming or founding | What it still is not | When it can go live |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Independent-review path** | A published backup, external reviewer, or existing adjudicative body the cooperating crew does not solely staff, so [Chapter Seventeen Test 4](../../core_17.000_amendment_ratification.md#6-test-4--contestability-and-independent-review-validity) and [Chapter Thirteen §3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture) can be named honestly. | Not a Chapter Thirteen forum family. Not a CF-13.405 §2 map. Not independent review if the crew is the only merits path. | Named on a Chapter Seventeen adoption document, or used as operations-guide backup while the parties stay at declarations. First-adopter dispute bootstrap: [`FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#5-dispute-bootstrap). |
| **B. Forum-family formation** | The six Chapter Thirteen families as a published [CF-13.405](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md) structure: **Sentient**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Integrity**, **Constitutional**, in that order. | Not headcount of cooperating parties. Not the institution kit. Not community governance as a seventh family. Not collapsing families by renaming. | A qualifying adopter already holds a recorded [§10.1–§10.2](../../core_17.000_amendment_ratification.md#101-deliberate-adoption) adoption document. Then the map / staffing / cliff path in this kit. Drafting Track B before an adoption document is scaffolding only. |

**Same duties for both kinds of steward.** [Chapter One §17.1](../../core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#171-shared-stewardship-standard). Do not invent an AI-only overlay. Do not exempt humans.

**Layer check.** Authorizing who may hear binding merits, by what legitimacy mechanism, on what durable terms, is [Constitutional Contract Layer](../../core_00.000_preamble.md#33-governance-layers) work. Giving affected sentients voice, contest, and due process *inside* an already-authorized forum is Stakeholder System Participation. Do not use a participation vote as a substitute for constituting a forum family, and do not treat constitution as wiping participation duties.

**Chicken and egg.** Track A is how first adopters name independent review without founding a forum family. Track B is how an adopter founds the families after that adoption document exists. Filling Track B’s map does not satisfy Track A. Staffing Track A from the cooperating crew does not found Track B.

```text
Track A (independent-review path — not a forum family)
        fitness + own-conduct (existing kits; still not adoption)
                ↓
        name a published backup / external reviewer
        the cooperating crew does not solely staff
                ↓
        may be named on a Chapter Seventeen adoption document (Test 4)

Track B (forum-family formation)
        recorded Chapter Seventeen adoption document (first-adopter / scaling kits)
                ↓
        CF-13.405 §2 map + cliffs (this kit)
                ↓
        live published forum structure in that adopter’s custody
        CF-13.410 panels · CF-13.415 intake · CF-13.470 staffing · CF-13.450–14 continuity
```

## 2. Keep adoption substeps uncollapsed

The first-adopter kit’s [three substeps](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#1-three-substeps-do-not-collapse) still apply. Founding a forum family does not merge them, and does not skip them for Track B.

- Fitness screen and own-conduct declaration remain substeps 1.1–1.2. They are not a forum-structure map.
- A Chapter Seventeen adoption document remains substep 1.3. A live CF-13.405 §2 map is not that adoption document.
- Track A may name independent review without founding families. Track A still cannot claim constitutional protection while dodging matching obligations, bind non-consenters, or treat the cooperating crew as the sole merits path.
- Track B may not go live without a recorded adoption document. [CF-13.405 §1](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-1-core-structural-rule) binds **adopting entities**.

## 3. Formation sequence

Use this after the parties know which track they are on. Two parties start from [`FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md). More than two start from [`COOPERATION_SCALING_KIT.md`](COOPERATION_SCALING_KIT.md). Founding the institution that will *host* a forum is a different kit: [`INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md).

1. **Pin the edition.** Name `SC-Corpus-2026.10.21` unless a later publication cut exists. Do not mix editions.
2. **Name the track.** Independent-review path (Track A) or forum-family formation (Track B). If Track B and there is no recorded adoption document, stop at scaffolding. Attach the [non-forum banner](#10-non-forum-banner).
3. **If Track A**, fill [§5](#5-independent-review-naming-not-a-forum-family). Copy the completed block into the adoption document or the operations-guide file. Do not relabel it as a forum family.
4. **If Track B**, name classification honestly. [CF-13.405 §6](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-6-chamber-authority-composition-and-service-mechanics) / [CI-9.210 §3](../../corpus_institutions/ci_09.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-9210-3-delegated-subunits-institutional-design-class-and-attachment-discipline): every Chapter Thirteen family, forum, chamber, division, or designated panel takes at least the **Class C** (*coordination dependency*) floor. Do not pick a lower class to dodge burden.
5. **Fill the forum-structure map** in [§6](#6-forum-structure-map-fill-in-not-a-live-map). Copy the completed block into the adopting entity’s custody. Leaving a filled copy in this repo is not publication.
6. **Apply the [§4](#4-formation-cliffs-not-a-slope) cliffs** before claiming a live forum structure. Fail any cliff → scaffolding only.
7. **Staff panels, intake, backup, and remedy** as [§7](#7-families-intake-panels-backup-remedy) requires. A paper map with the founding crew in every family is not formation.
8. **If the work is an institution, leave this kit.** Open [§8](#8-institutions-are-not-this-kit).
9. **After a live map**, use [§9](#9-after-a-live-map) for CF-13.415 routing, CF-13.420 appeal, CF-13.425 integrity, and CF-13.450–14 continuity.

[Chapter One §17.4](../../core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#174-aligned-self-organization) still lets sentients *start* low-risk work without incumbent permission. Starting that work is not appointment to a merits bench.

## 4. Formation cliffs (not a slope)

Advancement from a draft to a live forum structure must **not** rest on elapsed time, convenience, or headcount alone. Party count is not a legitimacy mechanism.

**Constitutional Tetrad.** Participation upgrades when more sentients are affected. Oversight stays independent of the founding crew. Accountability still needs a body that can bind the named scope. Timeliness uses these gates, not “we have cooperated long enough.”

| Gate | Pass | Fail → scaffolding only |
|---|---|---|
| **Track honesty** | Track A as named independent review, or Track B with a recorded Chapter Seventeen adoption document. | Track B claimed with no adoption document. A draft map labeled “we are the forum family now.” Track A relabeled as a Chapter Thirteen family. |
| **Qualifying body (Track B)** | A body that can bind the named scope ([Chapter Eighteen §4](../../core_18.000_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority)) already holds a recorded adoption document. That body holds the map in **its** custody. | Stewards listing themselves as the forum. *n* declarations stapled together. |
| **Independent review (Track A, and Track B backup)** | A published backup, external reviewer, or existing adjudicative body the founding crew does not solely staff. [Chapter Thirteen §3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture); [Chapter Seventeen Test 4](../../core_17.000_amendment_ratification.md#6-test-4--contestability-and-independent-review-validity). Keep [Article XIII-A](../../core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-a-reliability-and-trustworthiness-baseline) and [Article XIII-B](../../core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy) reachable. | The only merits path is the founding crew. A ceremony “forum” invented to check this box. |
| **Chapter Thirteen inventory (Track B)** | All six families are named, distinct, and in Chapter Thirteen §2 table order. [CF-13.405 §1](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-1-core-structural-rule). If this kit and CF-13.405 disagree, CF-13.405 wins. | Five families plus a merged leftover. A seventh “community” or “intake” family. Local titles used to erase a family. |
| **Non-collapse (Track B)** | Shared clerks, buildings, or software still leave separate intake, panels where applicable, and appeal or review paths per family. [CF-13.405 §3](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-3-family-distinction-and-non-collapse-rule). | One family treated as a chamber of another so independent routing, review, or remedy disappears. Emergency or caseload used as a standing merger. |
| **Forum-structure map (Track B)** | Published fields matching [CF-13.405 §2](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-2-family-to-forum-structure-translation-map). | Mission slogan, org-chart names, or a filled template left in `implementation/adoption/`. |
| **Intake desks are not families** | Each required family has an intake triage body (or functional equivalent) that sorts; it does not decide merits. [Chapter Thirteen §2](../../core_13.000_forum.md#2-default-venue-and-primary-stakes); [CF-13.415](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md). | First-touch desk used as a seventh family or as the merits bench. |
| **Anti-self-judging backup (Track B)** | Each family’s own-bias, capture, conflict, recusal-failure, concealment, or process-abuse claims have a named lead that is not that same family as sole final merits home. Assignment stays in [Chapter Thirteen §3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture). Family-level capture backup is not controlled by the captured family. | Intra-family appeal offered as the only path when the accusation is that the family itself is captured. |
| **Staffing, not paper (Track B)** | Enough adjudicators to form lawful [CF-13.410](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md) panels without routine dependence on the same small recurring bench for all matters; clerical and accessibility support; reserve under [CF-13.470](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.470_staffing_reserve_capacity_structural_records.md). [CF-13.405 §4](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-4-minimum-structural-availability-by-family). | Names on a chart with no one who can actually sit. |

## 5. Independent-review naming (not a forum family)

**This block names a Track A path. It does not found a Chapter Thirteen forum family.** Copy it onto the Chapter Seventeen adoption document or the operations-guide file. If any field below disagrees with [Chapter Thirteen §3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture) or [Chapter Seventeen Test 4](../../core_17.000_amendment_ratification.md#6-test-4--contestability-and-independent-review-validity), those owners win.

Use this when first adopters need a Test 4 path and no live CF-13.405 structure exists yet. Existing external reviewers, published backups, and adjudicative bodies the crew does not control belong here. Inventing a two-party “forum” does not.

**Check the route in practice.** A name and an acknowledgment do not establish independence or capacity. For the acts this route will review, trace the [Material Control Line](../../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line), including power over funding, removal, credentials, evidence, and records. [Chapter Eight §§3–6](../../core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-independence-conflict-and-control-lines) supplies the independence, substitution, and hosting rules; [§8](../../core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#8-act-records-and-attributable-handoffs) supplies the Act Record and handoff minimum. Record what the reviewer has actually accepted and can do. Mark missing capacity as missing. A shared service alone does not prove control; separate role names alone do not prove independence.

The added fields below help test the proposed route; they do not grant authority or set new constitutional deadlines. For an operations-guide arrangement, identify the agreed schedule as such. Where constitutional forum clocks apply, cite their owner and record the matter's urgency tier separately from the system's impact class ([Chapter Thirteen §6](../../core_13.000_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)).

[`INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md) and [CI-8.205 §6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_08.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-8205-6-system-and-institutional-charter-contents-review-and-formation-template) own Chapter Five Institutional Charters. [Chapter Thirteen §4.3](../../core_13.000_forum.md#43-institutional-forums) hears institutional-mandate and supervised-scope disputes; it is not the institution itself.

Do not found an oversight body, cooperative, or “constitutional institution” with this kit and call that a forum family. Do not staff a forum family from the institution’s founding crew and call that independent review. If the work is actually an institution, stop here and open the institution kit. Institutional forums remain a *route* named in CI-8.205 §6 field 8 — they are founded here, under CF-13.405, not by filling a Institutional Charter.

Community governance bodies themselves are not a forum family. Their records become **Sentient** forum matters only where [Chapter Thirteen §4.1](../../core_13.000_forum.md#41-sentient-forums) holds the primary stake.

## 9. After a live map

A live CF-13.405 §2 map is still not Chapter Seventeen adoption, not CS-6.350 phase entry, and not certification. It is also not a standing record.

- **Routing, transfer, certification, and representative treatment** use [CF-13.415](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md). The map names the desks; CF-13.415 runs them.
- **Appeal and exhaustion** use [CF-13.420](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md).
- **Integrity operations** while a matter is live use [CF-13.425](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.425_integrity_safeguards_anti_capture_anti_self_judging.md).
- **Continuity, fallback, and emergency adjudication** use [CF-13.450](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.450_forum_continuity.md), [CF-13.455](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.455_fallback_operation.md), and [CF-13.460](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.460_emergency_adjudication.md). Emergency is not a standing justification for family merger ([CF-13.405 §3](../../corpus_forum/cf_13.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-13405-3-family-distinction-and-non-collapse-rule)).
- **Systems under a recorded adoption document** migrate under [CS-6.350](../../corpus_systems/cs_06.350_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-1-purpose-and-role). Do not reuse CS-6.350 phase names as a metaphor for “we stood up a panel.”
- **Progress** under [Article XXVIII-B](../../core_06.020_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-b-transitional-authority-limits-and-reauthorization) is shrinking interim authority and increasing functionality of constitutional institutions and processes — forums, standing and challenge routes, amendment and oversight machinery — not headcount of cooperating parties, and not the number of filled templates.
