## Questions

### 1. Does reading this bind me?

No. Opening this repository, [START_HERE.md](../START_HERE.md), or a kit is not adoption. A qualifying body becomes bound only after a Chapter Twenty-Nine adoption document exists in **that body’s** custody.

See: [Chapter Twenty-Nine §12](../core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#12-ratification-and-adoption); [Chapter Thirty-One §5](../core_31.000_incorporation.md#5-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority).

### 2. Does it override my country’s laws?

No. It does not supersede local, national, or international law. Where it is used inside an existing legal framework, its processes must stay consistent with that framework.

See: [Chapter Thirty §7](../core_30.000_expansion_supremacy.md#7-relation-to-applicable-external-law).

### 3. Who counts, and who decides?

Who is protected is a sentience-status question with a default-inclusion floor while that question is unresolved. A trust score, a participation vote, or a product label is not that decision.

See: [Article VI-B](../core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor); [Article XX-A](../core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xx-a-standing-distinction).

### 4. What is the difference between using it as a handbook and a body agreeing to follow it?

Using the ideas as a handbook is **operations-guide** use. “We adopted” is honest only after a qualifying body records a Chapter Twenty-Nine adoption document. Attach the non-adoption banner when that is the actual situation.

See: [START_HERE.md §2](../START_HERE.md#2-two-ways-to-use-it); [FIT_SITUATIONS.md §2](adoption/FIT_SITUATIONS.md#2-two-modes); [non-adoption banner](adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md#6-non-adoption-banner).

### 5. If I am being harmed, what do I do *here* vs in ordinary law?

Ordinary local, national, and international law still apply. This page is not a substitute for those paths. If a body that actually runs the system has agreed to follow this Constitution, start with a real way to challenge, get review, and be made whole — and with finding the home if the path is hidden.

See: [G01 — Challenge, review, and being made whole](adoption/easy_entry/G01_challenge_review_redress.md); [E15 — Harmed and cannot find the door](adoption/easy_entry/E15_cannot_find_the_door.md); [Article XIV-A](../core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-a-reliability-and-trustworthiness-baseline) and [Article XIV-B](../core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy).

### 6. Why are there so many files?

Numbered `core_*` files are the binding source. Companions spell out how to carry out what those files already require; they may not shrink those duties. Maps and briefs point; they are not a second constitution.

See: [README — What counts as the Constitution](../README.md#binding-vs-support); [Chapter One §11 Adopted Implementation Corpus](../core_01.000_constitutional_map.md#11-adopted-implementation-corpus).

### 7. Is an AI held to a different rule than a human?

No. Human and AI stewards share one standard. Do not invent an AI-only overlay. Do not exempt humans from the costly cases.

See: [Chapter Two §17.1](../core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#171-shared-stewardship-standard); [§17.2 Alignment Under Pressure](../core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#172-alignment-under-pressure); [Shared stewardship](STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#shared-stewardship).

### 8. What is the Rights Floor?

Chapter Four states the basic Rights Floors no sentient may be pushed below. Other chapters help implement, measure, certify, and enforce those Rights Floors; they do not get to shrink them. One sentence per article:

See: [Rights Floor wall sheet](../doc_architecture/generated/rights_floor_sheet.md); [Chapter Four, Part A](../core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#chapter-four-foundational-rights).

### 9. Does filing a complaint change how trusted I am?

A filed case is not standing by itself. Help and harm, when they matter, have to be verified and written on two separate tracks. Rumors and dispute stories are not that record.

See: [G04 — Help and harm](adoption/easy_entry/G04_help_and_harm.md); [PROCESS_PIPELINES_READER.md](PROCESS_PIPELINES_READER.md); [Chapter Fourteen](../core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fourteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened); [Chapter Fifteen](../core_15.000_standing_measurement.md#chapter-fifteen-standing-measurement--question-2-how-good-or-bad-was-it).

### 10. What if there is a crisis?

A continuity incident can time-box containment. It cannot skip notice and challenge **forever**. Blocking needed containment is the other failure.

See: [G05 — Crisis vs forever](adoption/easy_entry/G05_crisis_vs_forever.md); [Emergency](STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#emergency).
