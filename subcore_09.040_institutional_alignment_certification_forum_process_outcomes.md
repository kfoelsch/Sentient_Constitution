Chapter Twelve is the constitutional owner of **forum process and outcomes of institutional alignment certification**.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: the same independent forums, in the same order, review the institution, and the institution never chooses its own reviewer. A published outcome never decides whether the institution may exist or operate.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Twelve states how forums supervise institutional alignment certification, and what the outcomes are:

- the four seats in an institutional certification, forum roles and sequence, and forums, certifiers, and record offices treated as institutions (**section 3**); and
- the outcomes: go-live for an Institution, defects and correction, remediation readiness, denial and revocation, and their effects (**section 4**).

An outcome decides what the Institution may say about itself, who is warned, and how much the outcome counts when the Institution's Systems are reviewed. The forums can also limit the specific practice or authority the review found at issue.

**Role:** Institutional certification and System Alignment Certification are two certifications joined by a link, not one inside the other (**Chapter Eleven section 1**):

- It takes the evaluation from [Subcore 9-B](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#subcore-9-b-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation) as fixed.
- It runs the same forum process as [Subcore 8-D](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#subcore-8-d-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes), applied to an Institution.
- It cites System Certification Records by link and does not redo them.
- [Subcore 9-F](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#subcore-9-f-institutional-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing) takes over from here, with the Institutional Certification Record, recertification, challenge, and the standing bridge.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:

- the forum process (**section 3**); and
- the outcomes (**section 4**).

The chapter leaves:

- evaluation to Chapter Eleven;
- the Institutional Certification Record, recertification, challenge, and standing bridge to Chapter Thirteen; and
- every System-level decision to [Subcore 8-B — Chapters Eight through Ten](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#subcore-8-b-system-alignment-certification--evaluation).

### 2. Why Forum Review of Institutional Certification Exists

*In plain terms: an institution must never choose its own reviewer. This chapter exists so institutional certification is decided by forums the institution does not control.*

Chapter Eleven sets what an institution is evaluated on. The institution has every reason to want a favourable result, and often the power to shape who reviews it. This chapter sends the evaluation to the same independent forums, in the same order, as system certification, and publishes the outcome. It also states that no outcome decides whether the institution may exist or operate, so certification can never be used to shut down lawful association.

<a id="31-four-seats-in-an-institutional-certification"></a>

#### 3.1 Four Seats in an Institutional Certification

*In plain terms: the same four seats as in any certification, and the Institution never picks or controls the seats that judge, keep, or hear challenges to its own certification.*

An institutional certification is a [Subcore 3-J — Materially Binding Act](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), and this subsection is the statement [Chapter Six §10 Relationship to Later Processes](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#10-relationship-to-later-processes) requires of this process. The seat placements in full are in [Subcore 9-C §3 Four Seats in an Institutional Certification](subcore_09.030_institutional_certification_seats_sequence_readiness.md#3-four-seats-in-an-institutional-certification). Its guarantees are:

- **Four seats, no relaxation:** each certification pass carries all four seats that [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) requires, and Chapter Twelve neither adds a seat nor loosens any Chapter Six rule.
- **Independent verifying authority:** the Institution may not choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove its own verifying authority, and no one who works for it, is owned by it, or answers to it may hold that seat.
- **Record and contest seats outside its control:** the record seat and the contest seat sit outside the Institution's [Subcore 3-J — Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line). A seat placement inside it is invalid whatever the map says, the work routes to the independent route the Charter Role Map names, and the defective placement is itself a finding.
- **Merged hosting unchanged:** [Chapter Six §7 Proportional Scaling and Merged Hosting](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#7-proportional-scaling-and-merged-hosting) applies unchanged.

<a id="32-forum-roles-and-sequence"></a>

#### 3.2 Forum Roles and Sequence

*In plain terms: an Integrity Forum leads, the Institutional Forums judge the core parts, a System's own questions stay in the System's own review, and the sentients the Institution affects can speak, anonymously if they need to.*

The supervisory sequence of [Subcore 8-D §3.2 Supervisory Sequence](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-supervisory-sequence) and [Subcore 8-D §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) apply, with the changes set out in full in [Subcore 9-C §4 Forum Roles and Sequence](subcore_09.030_institutional_certification_seats_sequence_readiness.md#4-forum-roles-and-sequence). Its guarantees are:

- **Integrity leads:** an Integrity Forum leads each certification pass, unless Chapter Twenty-Two assigns a different lawful lead for the primary stake, and the lead forum never holds the record seat on the same act.
- **The Institution is always a party:** it is a necessary party to its own certification, and Institutional Forums hold component authority over its mandate, scope, class, duties, and its Rights Floor conduct.
- **Systems are referred, not decided:** a component question that belongs to one System is decided in that System's own certification pass, never in the Institution's.
- **Affected sentients are heard:** the Institution's affected members, workers, users, and communities have an accessible way to submit evidence, and a submitter, staff included, may stay anonymous until the lead forum decides.
- **Protection, contest, and anti-bypass apply in full:** urgent protection, the contestability chain, and the anti-bypass rule of Chapter Nine apply to an Institution's certification pass.

<a id="33-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions"></a>

#### 3.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions

*In plain terms: the bodies that do the certifying are institutions too, so they cannot certify each other in a circle. A body that is itself Not Certified gives up its seats in other certifications.*

A forum host, a certifying body, an Institutional Certification Record office, a verifying authority, or any other body that takes part in certifications is an [Subcore 3-K — Institution](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institution) where it meets that definition, and this Chapter applies to it as to any other Institution of its class.

- **No self-judging:** Its own pass is led and reviewed by a forum outside it and outside its [Subcore 3-J — Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line). Where the question is the forum family's own bias, capture, conflict, or process abuse, the backup routing of the [Chapter Sixteen §6 Transfer, consolidation, and anti-self-judging](core_16.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture) cross-forum anti-self-judging rule and **CF-16.435 §7** (*no self-investigation*) applies.
- **No circle:** A body does not hold a seat in the certification of a body that holds a seat in its own, directly or through a chain of bodies that returns to the first within one recertification cycle. A certification pass that would close a circle routes to a body outside it.
- **Seats stay apart:** Taking part in another body's certification, including by holding a seat in it, gives a body no advantage of its own.
  - **No hold over the other body:** it gives no power over how that body responds to its certification, such as its answer to findings, certification conditions, or challenges.
  - **No preference in its own certification:** it gives no favorable treatment in the body's own certification.
- **No seat while Not Certified:** While an Institution is Not Certified ([§4 Certification Outcomes](#4-certification-outcomes)), it may not hold the verify-or-authorize, record, or contest seat in another body's certification, and it may not host a forum or certifying body. Any seat placements it holds are moved elsewhere in a new, dated version of the Role Map.

*In plain terms: a certification review ends with a published outcome for the Institution. The outcome never decides whether the Institution may exist or operate. It decides what the Institution may say about itself, who is warned, and how much the outcome counts when the Institution's Systems are reviewed. The forums can also limit the specific practice or authority the review found wrong, and a Not Certified Institution may not hold a seat in another body's certification. They cannot bar the Institution from operating or from forming Systems.*

This section is **Phase IV, Decide** for an Institution: the forums reach the outcome here, after the review in [§3 Forum Process](#3-forum-process).

An Institution can receive the same outcomes a System can. [Subcore 8-D §4 Certification Outcomes](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) defines them:

- provisional certification and full certification, which are the two **statuses** (a provisional certification is reviewed again sooner);
- conditional certification (certification that carries certification conditions);
- deferred certification (the decision is put off);
- non-certification, which is a **denial** (certification is refused);
- withdrawal, which is a **revocation** (a certification already held is taken back);
- expiration (the certification runs out);
- fulfillment of certification conditions (the certification conditions have been met); and
- release from certification conditions (the certification conditions no longer apply, so they are lifted without having been met).

An Institution that holds neither provisional nor full certification is **Not Certified**. The rules for statuses and certification conditions are those of Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes and [Subcore 8-F §4.3 Provisional and Full Certification](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#43-provisional-and-full-certification). Under them, a certification condition ends, by fulfillment or by release, only after independent verification.

<a id="41-go-live-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 4.1 Go-Live for an Institution

*In plain terms: a System cannot go live until it is certified. An Institution can form and run without a certificate, but it waits for its recertification before it exercises new material authority. An outcome for an Institution does three narrower things: it sets the Institution's public status, it attaches certification conditions to the specific problem found, and it counts as evidence when the Institution's Systems are reviewed.*

[Subcore 8-D §4.1 Go-Live Gate](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate) says a System does not go live until it is certified, the outcome has been published with the challenge path open, the notice interval has run, and every required certification condition has been independently verified.

An Institution has no such gate on forming or running ([Subcore 9-B §2.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#22-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)). It forms, publishes its Institutional Charter, begins to exercise authority, and keeps operating without holding a certification, and no outcome grants or withholds that right. New material authority is the one exception. The Institution waits for its recertification to clear that authority before it exercises it ([Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority)). Everything else it does continues, and any certification conditions required before the new authority is exercised are verified first ([§4.1.1 Certification Conditions](#411-certification-conditions)).

**Go-live still gates the Systems.** A new System, or a material expansion of one, goes live only through its own certification, whatever the Institution's status. The Institution's status can inform that decision, but it can never make it:

- **Evidence, not an outcome for the System:** When one of the Institution's Systems is up for certification, the Institution's status is one piece of evidence about how that System is run, what drives it, and whether sentients can challenge it ([Subcore 8-B §4.1 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#41-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline)). On its own it never certifies a System and never blocks one.
- **A higher bar after a denial or revocation:** If the Institution has been denied or has lost its certification, anyone asking to launch a new System or make a major expansion that depends on it has more to prove. They have to show that what they are relying on about the Institution is still solid enough, even with that finding on record ([§4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution), effect item 3).

<a id="411-certification-conditions"></a>

##### 4.1.1 Certification Conditions

A conditional certification of an Institution is written as [Subcore 8-D §4.1 Go-Live Gate](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate) (*Certification conditions and bounded authorization*) requires, with these limits:

- **Tied to the problem found:** A certification condition or reliance limit attaches to the authority, practice, or structure the finding is about.
- **Fully specified:** Every certification condition spells out five things:
  - Who is responsible for it.
  - When it is due, or how often it repeats if it is ongoing.
  - What proof will show it was met.
  - Who independently checks that proof.
  - What happens if it is missed.
- **No reaching past the problem:** It cannot require the Institution to stop forming Systems, to ask leave to keep operating, or to change anything the findings do not touch.
- **Certification conditions before new authority:** The certification conditions that [Subcore 8-D §4.1 Go-Live Gate](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate) requires before go-live apply to an Institution only for new material authority. They must be independently verified before the Institution exercises that authority. They never make its existing operation wait.
- **How it ends:** A certification condition ends by fulfillment or by release, as Chapter Nine §4.1 Go-Live Gate (*How a certification condition ends*) sets out, and only after independent verification.

<a id="412-other-effects-of-an-outcome"></a>

##### 4.1.2 Other Effects of an Outcome

Apart from the Systems and the certification conditions, an outcome has only these effects:

- **Status on the Institutional Certification Record:** The Institution's status is published, and anyone affected can challenge it. An Institution that is Not Certified may not describe itself as certified.
- **A warning to those who rely on the Institution:** Where a finding means their reliance is not safe, they are told.
- **How soon the next review comes:** A provisional certification has a shorter certification interval than a full certification.
- **Denial and revocation:** The effects are in [§4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution).
- **Reopening and the cascade:** A review can be reopened, and a failed System certification can affect the Institution. See [Subcore 9-F §4 Recertification and Reopening](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#4-recertification-and-reopening).
- **Standing:** An outcome can touch a sentient's standing only through [Subcore 9-F §6 Relationship to Standing](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing).

Sanctions, dissolution, and institutional-failure consequences stay with **CI-12.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) and **CI-19.205** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*). This Chapter creates none of them.

<a id="42-certification-defects-and-correction"></a>

#### 4.2 Certification Defects and Correction

*In plain terms: a certification defect is a real problem the evaluation turns up, not a paperwork slip. When one is found in an Institution, the repair rules are the same as for a System.*

[Subcore 8-D §4.2 Certification Defects and Correction](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#42-certification-defects-and-correction) applies when a certification pass finds a defect in an Institution. The findings under [Subcore 9-B §3 Institution Class Evaluation](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-institution-class-evaluation), [Subcore 9-B §4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), [Subcore 9-B §5 Evaluations Where Implicated](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated), and [Subcore 9-B §6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) take the place of the Chapter Eight evaluations that Chapter Nine lists. Chapter Nine §4.2 Certification Defects and Correction sets:

- the choices open to the forums;
- the statement of what is affected, the interim protection and reliance limits, and the corrective work required; and
- the rule that the correction is independently verified before it supports fulfillment of certification conditions.

Two points are specific to an Institution:

- **No reaching past the defect:** A certification condition cannot reach beyond the defect ([§4.1 Go-Live for an Institution](#41-go-live-for-an-institution)).
- **No ban on operating:** The last paragraph of Chapter Nine §4.2 Certification Defects and Correction says a System that is Not Certified cannot begin new operation. That is a rule for Systems. It does not apply to an Institution ([Subcore 9-B §2.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#22-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)).

<a id="43-remediation-readiness-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 4.3 Remediation Readiness for an Institution

*In plain terms: when a review finds a problem in an Institution, the forums ask the same question they ask about a System: is it both willing and able to fix the problem? If the honest answer is no, its certification is refused or taken back. By itself, that cannot stop the Institution from existing or operating. It takes away the Institution's claim to be certified, warns those who rely on it, weakens the support its Systems can draw from its good status, and keeps it out of the seats that certify others.*

[Subcore 8-D §4.3 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#43-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct) applies to an Institution, with the roles and added factors set out in full in [Subcore 9-C §5 Remediation Readiness Factors](subcore_09.030_institutional_certification_seats_sequence_readiness.md#5-remediation-readiness-factors). Its guarantees are:

- **No judging its own readiness:** the Institution's governing authority carries the case and supplies the evidence; the lead Integrity Forum decides the finding.
- **Institution-specific factors:** willingness and capacity are judged on Chapter Nine's factors and on what only an Institution can do, such as changing its own reward structure, replacing an office-holder found not authentic, restoring independent seat placements, and having the authority, funds, staff, and succession plan for the work.
- **Verifiable correction only:** a correction that no independent verifying authority can test, with the access and time it is actually given, is not capacity.
- **One System, one readiness check:** an Institution that runs exactly one System gets one readiness check, which states its denial grounds separately for the System and the Institution.

<a id="44-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 4.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution

*In plain terms: denial means certification is refused, and revocation means a certification already held is taken back. Both are real consequences. Neither shuts the Institution down.*

[Subcore 8-D §4.4 Denial and Revocation](subcore_08.040_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#44-denial-and-revocation) applies to an Institution, with the changes and the one-System rule set out in full in [Subcore 9-C §6 Denial and Revocation Grounds and the One-System Rule](subcore_09.030_institutional_certification_seats_sequence_readiness.md#6-denial-and-revocation-grounds-and-the-one-system-rule). Its guarantees are:

- **Grounds bind:** Chapter Nine's grounds apply unchanged, and when one is met the forum must deny or revoke. It may not grant or continue certification with certification conditions in place of that outcome.
- **Protection first, status only:** the forum states interim protection and reliance limits before either takes effect, and denial or revocation acts on certification status, not on the Institution's existence.
- **Fair process:** the Institution gets notice of the finding and the evidence, a window to answer, the contestability chain, and reasons on the Institutional Certification Record; urgent protection may be ordered at once and the process runs afterward.
- **Not a permanent bar:** after a denial or revocation, the Institution may seek certification again.
- **One System treated no worse:** where an Institution runs exactly one System, each outcome follows only the denial or revocation ground that supports it, and the Institutional Certification Record states which denial or revocation ground supports which outcome.
- **Effects:** the effects are those in [§4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution).

<a id="45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution

*In plain terms: a denial or revocation changes what the Institution may say about itself, who is told, how much its status counts in its Systems' reviews, and where the matter goes next. By itself it does not shut the Institution down.*

**Effects:** Because there is no gate ([Subcore 9-B §2.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](subcore_09.020_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#22-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)), a denial or revocation has only these effects:

1. **Status:** The Institution is published as Not Certified and may not describe itself as certified.
2. **Notice:** Those who rely on the Institution are told, and so are the forums of each System in its portfolio. The lead forum that publishes the outcome sees that this is done. Where [Subcore 9-F §4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#46-reopening-the-institutions-systems) applies, the same notice carries the request to reopen and runs on the giver, clock, and defaults of Chapter Thirteen §4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems, and the *Systems notified* entry of the Institutional Certification Record covers both.
3. **Weight in its Systems' certifications:**
   - The status is adverse evidence under [Subcore 8-B §4.1 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](subcore_08.020_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#41-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline) when each System in its portfolio is certified.
   - For a new System's go-live or a material expansion, the proponent must show that the institutional facts it relies on are enough despite the finding.
   - The status does not certify or block any System by itself. The System's own certification pass decides.
4. **Seats in other bodies' certifications:** The Institution gives them up while it is Not Certified, as [§3.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions](#33-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions) provides.
5. **Referral:** Route each disputed institutional certification issue to the forum family with jurisdiction over that question. There are three routes:
   - **To the forum that decides failure and sanctions:** The lead forum refers the matter to the forum with jurisdiction under **CI-12.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) and **CI-19.205** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*) where:
     - the finding is able and not willing;
     - a revocation rests on concealment or fraudulent evidence; or
     - a failed formation check was not resolved inside the resolution window in [Chapter Five §8 When a Holder Fails the Formation Check](core_05.000_charters.md#8-when-a-holder-fails-the-formation-check) (*Resolution window*).

     That forum alone decides any suspension, receivership, structural separation, or dissolution. This Chapter creates none of them.
   - **To the forum over a named sentient:** Where the certification finding rests on a holder who is not authentic, or on a sentient's use of the Institution to carry out a violation, the lead forum also refers the facts to the forum with jurisdiction over that sentient.
   - **To a lock or an Interim Hold:** For a verified finding of violation, the matter goes to the Institutional Authority Standing Lock under [Chapter Twelve §4.5 Special Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks). If an institutional case meets only the [Interim Hold](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) grounds, use the procedure in [Chapter Seventeen §4 Interim Hold on a Named Sentient's Pathway](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#interim-hold-on-a-named-sentients-pathway). The hold rests on that sentient's authority, never on the Institution.
6. **Standing:** Only through [Subcore 9-F §6 Relationship to Standing](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing). This holds for a denial of a first certification too: the findings behind it may supply input under [Subcore 9-F §6.2 Every Certification Outcome](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#62-every-certification-outcome), on the same terms as any other.

An Institution that is Not Certified may continue to exist, publish its Institutional Charter, operate, and apply again on the terms of Chapter Nine §4.4 (*Not a Permanent Bar*). It may not exercise new material authority until a recertification clears that authority ([Subcore 9-F §4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority)).

Not Certified status does not open any of the three narrow gates in **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*):

- a lock on a named sentient's authority;
- an Interim Hold on a named sentient's authority; and
- a lock on an Operating Party itself. This binds an Institution only where a lock actually sits on it, such as the Dissolution Reformation Lock under **CI-12.210 §4** (*Dissolution*). Not Certified status is not such a lock.

Whether an Operating Party falls outside the right to form, under the Limits in [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*), is for the forums to decide on that Article's terms. This Chapter does not decide it.
