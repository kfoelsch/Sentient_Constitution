# Chapter Twenty-Five sections 2 to 7: draft for the custodian (2026-11-18)

Draft only; not entered in `core_25.000_enforcement.md`. Decision: option B, draft sections 2 onward and cite the existing enforcement rules in Chapters Nineteen, Twenty and Twenty-Two rather than moving them. Everything below either points to an existing rule or is marked as new in this list.

New duties in this draft (each needs the custodian's approval):

1. §3: an interim or emergency measure binds on issue; any other outcome binds from its stated date or, failing one, from finality.
2. §3: a pending challenge, appeal or secondary review does not suspend an outcome unless the forum orders a stay with reasons and an end date (generalizes the rule already in Chapter Twenty-Three §4.6).
3. §4: every outcome names the bound actor, the body that carries it out or verifies it, and the time limit; where no body is named, the issuing forum names one on request inside the tier intake target.
4. §5: on a missed time limit the issuing forum may order completion by a new date, order substitute performance by another body at the bound actor's cost, and refer the failure.
5. §5: delay in carrying out is never counted against the sentient the outcome protects.
6. §7: carrying out may not reach further, last longer or cost more than the outcome requires; no outcome is carried out by intruding into a body or a mind (extends the lock limit to every outcome); over-enforcement can be challenged and is remedied like any other harm; carrying out stops when the outcome ends.

---

<a id="2-why-enforcement-exists"></a>
### 2. Why Enforcement Exists

*In plain terms: a remedy that is ordered but never delivered teaches everyone that harm pays. This chapter exists so that an outcome is real only when it has actually happened.*

A system gets more of whatever it rewards ([What Systems Reward](../../core_00.000_preamble.md#what-systems-reward)). If an outcome can be ignored, delayed until it no longer matters, or dodged by moving money, offices, or borders, then the system rewards ignoring, delaying, and dodging, whatever the outcome says. The chapters before this one decide what happened and what follows. This chapter carries those decisions to the point where they change something, and stops there: it adds no new kind of consequence, and it never lets carrying out an outcome become a second punishment.

<a id="3-what-must-be-carried-out-and-from-when"></a>
### 3. What Must Be Carried Out, and From When

*In plain terms: anything a forum or the standing pipeline orders someone to do must be done, starting from a stated moment. Asking for review does not pause it unless a forum says so.*

- **What counts as an outcome:** an outcome is any of the following that requires someone to act or to stop acting:
  - a final standing effect ([Chapter Twenty §2 Final Standing Effect](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#2-final-standing-effect));
  - a remedy or correction order ([Chapter Nineteen §2.1 Remedy and Correction](../../core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#21-remedy-and-correction));
  - a standing lock or special lock ([Chapter Nineteen §3 Lock Design and Enforcement](../../core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#3-lock-design-and-enforcement));
  - a forum order, including an interim or emergency measure ([Chapter Twenty-Four §4.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](../../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#41-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden)); and
  - a certification outcome that requires a system or institution to act.
- **From when:** an interim or emergency measure binds when it is issued. Any other outcome binds from the date it states, or, where it states none, from the date it becomes final.
- **Review does not pause it:** a pending challenge, appeal, or secondary review does not suspend an outcome unless the forum hearing it orders a stay, with stated reasons and an end date.

<a id="4-who-carries-outcomes-out"></a>
### 4. Who Carries Outcomes Out

*In plain terms: every outcome names who must act and by when. The one who carries it out is not the one who decided it, and nobody may hide behind not having been named.*

- **Named in the outcome:** every outcome names the actor bound by it and the body that carries it out or verifies that it was carried out, with the time limit under [§5 Time Limits and Missed Deadlines](#5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines).
- **When no one is named:** where an outcome names no body to carry it out, the forum that issued it names one on the request of any party, inside the intake target of the matter's tier ([Chapter Twenty-Four §4 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](../../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)). The gap does not excuse anyone bound in the meantime.
- **Separate seats:** the body that carries an outcome out acts within the outcome. It is not the body that decided it, and it does not judge a challenge to its own performance ([Chapter Eight §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](../../core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement)).
- **Institutional detail:** how institutions are sanctioned and supervised when they are the bound actor is set out in [CI-25.205](../../corpus_institutions/ci_25.205_institutional_accountability_sanctions.md) (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*); remedy-system capacity is set out in [CI-25.210](../../corpus_institutions/ci_25.210_remedy_systems_institutional_redress_capacity.md) (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*).

<a id="5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines"></a>
### 5. Time Limits and Missed Deadlines

*In plain terms: outcomes run on the same published clocks as everything else, and a missed deadline is itself something a forum can act on.*

- **The clocks:** remedy commencement, correction milestones, lock review, and reassessment follow [Chapter Twenty §4.5 Timely Implementation](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#45-timely-implementation), the tier windows of [Chapter Twenty-Four §4 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](../../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), and **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*). This chapter adds no clock table.
- **A missed time limit is non-performance:** any sentient affected by the outcome, or the body that verifies it, may bring the missed limit to the forum that issued the outcome. The forum may:
  - order completion by a new date;
  - order another body to carry the outcome out, at the cost of the actor bound by it where that actor can lawfully bear it ([Chapter Twenty §4.1 Remediation Capacity and Funding](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#41-remediation-capacity-and-funding)); and
  - refer the failure for a violation finding, and, where an authority refuses or suppresses enforcement, under [Chapter Twenty-Two §5.2 Enforcement Refusal, Suppression, or Non-Prosecution Conduct](../../core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#52-enforcement-refusal-suppression-or-non-prosecution-conduct).
- **Not carried by the sentient harmed:** delay in carrying an outcome out is never counted against the sentient it protects, and does not shorten any window that sentient has.

<a id="6-refusal-stalling-and-evasion"></a>
### 6. Refusal, Stalling, and Evasion

*In plain terms: you cannot escape an outcome by refusing, stalling, going broke, moving, or changing your name. The rules that stop this already exist; this section gathers them.*

- **Refusal and stalling:** refusing or stalling an outcome is non-performance under [§5 Time Limits and Missed Deadlines](#5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines), whoever does it, including the institution meant to carry the outcome out. Where an enforcement, oversight, or referral authority does it knowingly, recklessly, corruptly, selectively, or by pretext, it may be anti-constitutional misconduct under [Chapter Twenty-Two §5.2 Enforcement Refusal, Suppression, or Non-Prosecution Conduct](../../core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#52-enforcement-refusal-suppression-or-non-prosecution-conduct).
- **Cost and insolvency:** expense, insolvency, restructuring, or asset transfer does not by itself defeat a verified obligation, and successors inherit proportionate duties ([Chapter Twenty §4.1 Remediation Capacity and Funding](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#41-remediation-capacity-and-funding)).
- **Borders:** obligations stay actionable across boundaries wherever applicable law permits ([Chapter Twenty §4.3 Cross-Jurisdiction Continuity](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#43-cross-jurisdiction-continuity)).
- **Change of form or identity:** forums look through formal structure to functional control ([Chapter Twenty §4.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#44-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority)), read with [Chapter Twenty-Eight §4 Anti-Evasion Clause](../../core_28.000_non_regression.md#4-anti-evasion-clause), which this chapter does not narrow.
- **Capacity to deliver:** adopters keep the capacity to deliver outcomes, including remedy parity ([Chapter Twenty §4.2 Remedy-System Durability](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#42-remedy-system-durability)).

<a id="7-delivered-as-found-and-no-further"></a>
### 7. Delivered as Found, and No Further

*In plain terms: an outcome is carried out exactly as decided. Carrying it out harder, wider, or longer than decided is itself a wrong that can be challenged and repaired.*

- **No wider than the outcome:** carrying an outcome out may not reach further, last longer, or cost the bound actor more than the outcome requires.
- **No reach into body or mind:** no outcome is carried out by intruding into a body or a mind, the limit [Chapter Nineteen §3.4.7 Capacity Locks](../../core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#347-capacity-locks) and the Care and Stewardship Lock already set for those locks. A suspension or a health-crisis intervention follows its own floor under **Article XXI-B** (*Restriction Floors*) or **Article VII-C** (*Health Crisis and Involuntary-Intervention Floor*), not this section.
- **Over-enforcement can be challenged:** the bound actor, or any sentient affected, may challenge carrying out that goes beyond the outcome before the forum that issued it, and over-enforcement found on that challenge is remedied under [Chapter Nineteen §2.1 Remedy and Correction](../../core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#21-remedy-and-correction) on the same terms as any other harm.
- **The outcome ends when it ends:** when an outcome is complete, or a lock ends or is lifted, carrying it out stops at once, and the record says so ([Chapter Twenty §3 Restoration and Reassessment](../../core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#3-restoration-and-reassessment)).
