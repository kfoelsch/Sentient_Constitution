# Process principles: a reader guide (draft) — 2026-11-23

**Status:** draft for the custodian, **non-normative**. It is a reading aid, not constitutional text, and it changes, narrows, or ranks nothing. It answers the TODO item *Decide whether to gather scattered Process principles in a non-normative reader guide* by showing what such a guide would look like. Publishing it, and where, is the custodian's decision.

**Where it comes from.** The [process, system, and institution crosswalk](../PROCESS_SYSTEM_INSTITUTION_CROSSWALK.md) tagged each Chapter Two principle and Chapter Six duty by what it governs. This guide gathers the items tagged **Process** first (how a duty is carried out: steps, records, clocks, handoffs), using current section numbers. The crosswalk's Chapter Six numbers predate the 2026-11-20 reorganization and are matched here by title. Many tags are still provisional pending independent review, so a reader should treat the grouping as a convenience.

**How to use it.** Each line names the principle and says in a few words what it asks of whoever carries out a process. The linked section is what binds.
