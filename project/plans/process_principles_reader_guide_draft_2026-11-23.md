# Constitutional processes and institutions: a reader guide (draft) — 2026-11-23

**Status:** draft for the custodian, **non-normative**. It is a reading aid, not constitutional text, and it changes, narrows, or ranks nothing. It answers the TODO item *Decide whether to gather scattered Process principles in a non-normative reader guide*. Publishing it, and where, is the custodian's decision.

**How it is organized.** Revised the same day at the custodian's request ("broken down by constitutional process and institution"). Part 1 takes each constitutional process in the order the chapters run, says where it lives and what happens in one line, and names the Process principles that bear on it most. Part 2 takes each institution and lists the processes it runs or answers to. Part 3 keeps all 36 Process principles from the [process, system, and institution crosswalk](../PROCESS_SYSTEM_INSTITUTION_CROSSWALK.md) in one list. Which principles bear most on a process is a reading choice, not a ranking; every principle binds every process it reaches, and the linked sections are what bind.

## Part 1. By constitutional process

### 1.1 Publishing a System or Institutional Charter

- **Where:** [Chapter Five](../../core_05.000_charters.md).
- **What happens:** A Chartered Party writes down what it is for, what it does and does not do, who relies on it, who holds each seat, and where its binding rules are.
- **Principles that bear most:**
  - [Chapter Two §5.2](../../core_02.000_values_principles.md#52-plain-language-accessibility-participation-and-stewardship-duty) (rules, decisions, and notices in plain language);
