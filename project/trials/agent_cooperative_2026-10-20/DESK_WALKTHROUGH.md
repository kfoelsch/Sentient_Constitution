## Proposed repair and failure branches

**Stage 2:** publishing reasons, Rowan's interest, the objection and a filing channel makes the proposal more inspectable. It does not establish a disinterested recipient. The missing evidence is an accepted scope of review, a power to decline and a workable correction commitment, an actual control-line assessment, protected evidence access and custody, available resources, an agreed schedule, and qualified substitutes. Binding appointment or authority arrangements themselves require their own seat and authority checks. Asking a reviewer is not the reviewer's acceptance.

For a repaired allocation route, use this functional map as a proposal: Rowan initiates; a disinterested external holder verifies or authorizes; a separate qualified holder keeps the official record; a distinct disinterested holder hears the challenge. The packet does not invent their identities or assert that they exist. Any proposed permitted merger needs its own Chapter Eight §6 justification and safeguards; scarce staffing alone is insufficient. This map is not a six-family forum structure or proof of affordable operation.

The combined official record should link `AUTH-01`, `CLASS-01`, `SEATS-01`, `ALLOC-01`, and `CHALLENGE-01`, with stable identity, versions, timestamps, authority, seat holders, determinations, evidence, objections, conditions, handoffs, clocks and correction status. **Stage 3:** under the packet's counterfactual accepted arrangements, the separate record holder preserves the file and reviewer-unavailability event. The named substitute's new work under Rowan makes that substitute unsuitable for this act. Log the conflict and absence of a qualified independent route; do not substitute Rowan, erase Cedar's objection, pronounce it decided, or restart a clock through an administrative handoff.
