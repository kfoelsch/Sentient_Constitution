## Approved replacement for CJS-2.1.1 “Emergencies only”

**Emergencies only.** An entirely home-based or entirely rotating workflow fails this rule for ordinary binding decisions. A temporary departure is permitted only to the extent authorized by the primary owner's continuity rules and consistent with [Emergency and Contingency](../../core_05040_band_continuity.md#emergency-and-contingency-constitutional), [Chapter Thirteen §6.1 Emergency measures and continuation burden](../../core_13000_forum.md#61-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), and [Chapter Eight §7 Emergency and Urgent Action](../../core_08000_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#7-emergency-and-urgent-action). A published plan alone does not establish an emergency or authorize a departure. The applicable emergency predicate, necessity, proportionality, default expiry, independent review, continuation burden, notice and challenge restoration, and restoration or rollback requirements must all be met. A staffing shortage, deadline, release target, or reputational concern is not by itself an emergency. Emergency action preserves seat ownership and class-scaled separation, including the prohibition on any merger of the four seats for Class A; an actor may not verify its own continuation, hold a conflicted record, or hear the challenge to its own act.

For forums, apply [CF-13.450 §3 Activation triggers](../../corpus_forum/cf_13450_forum_continuity.md#cf-13450-3-activation-triggers), [CF-13.455 §1 Temporary quorum and provisional authority](../../corpus_forum/cf_13455_fallback_operation.md#cf-13455-1-temporary-quorum-and-provisional-authority), and [CF-13.460 Emergency adjudication](../../corpus_forum/cf_13460_emergency_adjudication.md), including independent review during emergency mode and restoration and reconciliation. Document and carry out the return to the ordinary hybrid workflow when the emergency conditions clear or lawful continuation fails. Retrospective review and disclosure remain governed by [Article XXVI-A Retrospective Review and Disclosure](../../core_06025_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvi-a-retrospective-review-and-disclosure); restrictions must meet [Article XX-B Restriction Floors](../../core_06020_rights_part_d.md#article-xx-b-restriction-floors). Where constitutional-system continuity is implicated, apply [CS-6.345 §8 Governance continuity, crisis communications, and exercises (high-impact systems)](../../corpus_systems/cs_06345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-6345-8-governance-continuity-crisis-communications-and-exercises-high-impact-systems).
