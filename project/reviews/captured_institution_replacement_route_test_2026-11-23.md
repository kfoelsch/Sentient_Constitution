**Numbering note.** The item comes from the [conceptual overview review](../../evidence/2026-09-16/conceptual_overview_corpus_alignment_review.md) ("Revisability depends on independent judgments of regression"), written before the core reorganization. Its Article XXV (*Constitutional Evolution and Non-Entrenchment*) is now [Article XXVIII](../../core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-constitutional-evolution-and-non-entrenchment), and its Chapter Thirteen (non-regression) is now [Chapter Twenty-Eight](../../core_28.000_non_regression.md). The test therefore reads Article XXVIII and Chapters Twenty-Seven and Twenty-Eight together, with the routes they lead to.

## 1. Question and pass condition

**Question (from the review).** Can opponents replace a failed or captured institution through protected channels, without agreement from the office whose power is at issue, when that office says the replacement would weaken the protections it serves?

**Pass.** For each step a replacement needs (starting it, deciding it, running the vote, answering the regression objection, and handling a captured decider), the text names a route that the incumbent does not control.

## 2. Scenario

A community's Public Stewardship Office, appointed for a set term, is captured by the operators it supervises. A coalition of residents wants it replaced by a new body with the same duties and stronger independence rules. The office says the replacement "weakens the Rights-Floor protections it administers" and is a regression under Chapter Twenty-Eight.

## 4. Gaps found

**Gap A: accelerated revalidation has no independent owner.** Article XXVIII-A is the one route aimed directly at a mechanism that is failing, and it is the one route the incumbent could hold: "as implemented in implementation protocols and governance safeguards" leaves the trigger and the review to whoever writes or runs those protocols, which may be the captured body. Chapter Twenty-Two §6.5 already solves the same problem for capacity failure (the starved body gives evidence but does not find its own failure).

*Options.* (a) Add to Article XXVIII-A: any affected party, protected reporter, or self-organized group may file evidence of an accelerating condition; the body whose mechanism is under review may give evidence but must not control the trigger finding, the revalidation, or the continuation-or-replacement decision; the Integrity family leads, with the Chapter Twenty-Two §6.3 backup pairs. Recommended. (b) Leave the Article as a principle and put the same rule in an implementation file. (c) Leave as is.

**Gap B: no plain statement that replacing a mechanism is not, by itself, a weakening.** Chapter Twenty-Eight §4 tests protections, and a proposer's own statement is only an input. But the objection here comes from the body being replaced, not from the proposer, and the text never says that replacing a mechanism, office, or body is not a weakening where the protection it served is kept at least as strong. That leaves room for exactly the interpretive capture the review warned about.

*Option.* Add to Chapter Twenty-Eight §4: replacing a mechanism, office, or body is not a weakening where the protection it served is carried at least as strongly by the replacement or by another mechanism; the affected body's own claim that its replacement weakens a protection is an input to the determination, not the determination. Recommended, and smaller than Gap A.

## 5. Resolution

The custodian approved both recommendations ("please do so"), and the 963rd move made them: Article XXVIII-A gains *Who starts and decides accelerated review* (Gap A, option a), and Chapter Twenty-Eight §4 gains *Replacing a mechanism* (Gap B). Step 7 and step 4 now **hold**.
