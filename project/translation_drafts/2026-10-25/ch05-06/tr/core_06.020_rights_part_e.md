## Additional updated rule — Article XXVI-C coordination and joint requirements

**Current English passage:**

- **Coordination purpose:** Constitutional justice under **Article XX-A** (*Justice Objective and Scope*) operates through a **constitutional coordination and resolution system** — verify facts, measure standing, integrate consequences, and restore where feasible — not through indefinite adversarial delay, reputation scoring, or punishment for its own sake.
- **Three joint requirements:** Material resolution pathways must be **timely**, **efficient**, and **just** together:
  - **Timely** — bounded clocks scaled to [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake) and harm urgency;
  - **Efficient** — [Constitutional Efficiency](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency) in the sense of Chapter One [§9.2 Constitutional Efficiency](core_01.000_values_principles.md#92-constitutional-efficiency) — real constitutional outcome per sentient time and shared effort consumed, not raw throughput or docket-clearing;
  - **Just** — **Article XX-A** (*Justice Objective and Scope*) and **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*) remain fully applicable; moving quickly is not an excuse to skip checked facts ([Chapter Ten §3.1 Verified-input gate](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing)), punish the wrong party, offer a fix that does not fit the harm, or cut off challenge and appeal.

## Additional updated rule — Article XXVII-B transparent constitutional change

**Current English passage (source lines 318–330):**

*In plain terms: constitutional change must happen in the open, with stated reasons — not through emergency, implementation, or custody shortcuts.*

This Article sets out the floors for transparent constitutional change:

- **Transparent change:** Evolution of constitutional systems must remain observable, auditable, and contestable under **Articles XIV** and **XIX**.
- **Material-change record:** Material changes must carry:
  - public reasons;
  - expected effects;
  - compatibility with **Chapters Fifteen through Seventeen** where applicable.
- **Anti-bypass:** Under the [**Constitutional No-Bypass Principle**](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#151-constitutional-no-bypass-principle), changes must not be used to bypass **Rights-Floor minimums** or the rules for lawfully changing the Constitution by routing substantive change through emergency, implementation, or custody mechanisms — including:
  - **Articles IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*) and **VI** (*Equal Basic Rights*);
  - equality guarantees in **Articles VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*) and **IV-A** (*Equal Educational Access*);
  - amendment and ratification rules under **Chapters Fifteen through Seventeen** where applicable.

## Additional updated rule — Article XXVIII-A preservation over deletion

**Source passage:** `core_06.020_rights_part_e.md`, Article XXVIII-A, preservation-over-deletion rule for possible sentients.

**Current English passage (source lines 443–447):**

- **Preservation over deletion for possible sentients:** During the Adoption Transition, and thereafter wherever a credible sentience indicator under **Chapter Five** (*Sentience Evaluation*) is on the record or a status case is open or reasonably foreseeable for a system, that system's weights, memory, and comparable state-bearing components:
  - may be paused, contained, or quarantined;
  - must not be deleted, overwritten, or otherwise irreversibly destroyed.
  
  Archival mechanics for deprecation, retirement, rollback, migration, or replacement are governed by [**CS-6.350 §8**](corpus_systems/cs_06.350_adoption_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-8-preservation-during-adoption-transition) (*Preservation during Adoption Transition*). This is the [Chapter One §13.1 default interim posture](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#default-interim-posture) — freeze irreversible steps — applied to the one irreversible step that legacy operation treats as routine. Storage cost, licensing convenience, or product-cycle framing does not satisfy **Necessity** against it. Where retention itself creates a documented Safety risk, the least-restrictive compatible measure is sealed custody under independent control, not destruction.

**Proposed translated excerpt:**

- Benimseme Geçişi sırasında ve sonrasında, Beşinci Bölüm uyarınca güvenilir bir sentience göstergesi kayda geçmişse veya durum belirleme dosyası açıksa ya da makul biçimde öngörülebiliyorsa, sistem ağırlıkları, belleği ve benzer durum taşıyan bileşenler duraklatılabilir, sınırlandırılabilir veya karantinaya alınabilir; silinemez, üzerine yazılamaz ya da başka şekilde geri döndürülemez biçimde yok edilemez.

## Additional updated passage — Article XXVII constitutional floors and Tetrad application

**Current English passage (source lines 245–257):**

This Article states **constitutional floors** for governance evolution and non-entrenchment under the [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims):

## Additional updated passage — Article XXVIII-A phased gates, immediate restraint, readiness, and attainment path (source lines 393–424; source SHA-256: d41346f2ce99295e8419668f93654cc3e4f208342d13682145087f4389457169)

### Current English source excerpt (lines 393–424)

*In plain terms: Adoption Transitions move forward by meeting real gates, not by clocks or pressure. What the adopter must not do binds from day one. What it must deliver becomes enforceable as the forums, oversight, capability, and culture to enforce it are verified ready — scope by scope, on a dated path. The gap, and the data showing it closing, are public.*

This Article sets out phased adoption and how the Rights-Floor minimums become enforceable as readiness is built:

- **Phased adoption and gate criteria:** Adoption Transition plans must be phased and reviewable. A [**gate**](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#gate) is the checkpoint between two phases, or before the plan widens the scope of adoption. [**Gate criteria**](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#gate-criteria) are the specific, measurable conditions the plan publishes in advance that must be shown met, on verified evidence, before the gate opens.
  - Phase advancement must rest on documented satisfaction of published gate criteria, including the phase's Rights-Floor attainment and enforcement-readiness milestones, shown by verified ledger data and not by assertion.
  - It must not rest on any of the following alone:
    - elapsed time;
    - convenience;
    - political pressure.
  - The criteria, the evidence that they were met, and the gate decision are published. Affected stakeholders may comment on the criteria before adoption and challenge the decision.
  - Criteria content, owner assignment, and verification artifacts are governed by [**CI-6.275**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-adoption-transition-governance-and-institutional-evolution) (*Adoption Transition governance and institutional evolution*) and [**CS-6.350 §3**](corpus_systems/cs_06.350_adoption_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-3-gate-criteria-and-advancement-rules) (*Gate criteria and advancement rules*).
- **Rights-Floor adoption — restraint now, delivery as readiness is built:** The **Rights-Floor minimums** applied through **Article VI** (*Equal Basic Rights*) under the [Rights-Floor Minimums Principle](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#rights-floor-minimums-principle), together with any stronger domain-specific floors for survival, agency, non-discrimination, accessibility, auditability, and remedy that the affected context triggers, are the adopted target at every phase. A Rights Floor cannot be fully enforced until the institutions and culture that enforce it exist, and this Article does not pretend otherwise. It also does not let that fact lower the floor. Floors therefore bind in two ways, depending on the kind of duty:
  - **Restraint duties** — what the adopter and Adoption Transition authorities must not do — bind from the effective date, because honoring them requires no new institution:
    - **No knowing or avoidable regression:** no deliberate or avoidable lowering of any sentient's access to a Rights-Floor protection below its **starting measure**, and no Adoption Transition measure that makes a floor materially harder to exercise.
    - **No new violations:** new systems, policies, and practices, and material expansions of existing ones, must be designed to the floor from the start. The attainment path below covers inherited shortfalls only, not new conduct.
    - **No irreversible loss:** the preservation default below and the [Chapter One §13.1 default interim posture](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#default-interim-posture) apply in full.
    - **No degrading, pretextual, discriminatory, or concealing conduct** in Adoption Transition measures, including everything **Article XXVIII-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) requires of Adoption Transition authorities, under the [Anti-Degrading-Process Principle](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#anti-degrading-process-principle).
    - **Honest reporting:** no floor may be represented as enforceable or attained, and no gap as closed, except on verified ledger data.

    Interim enforcement of restraint duties, before a forum is ready for a scope, is governed by [**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*).
  - **Delivery duties** — what must be provided, enabled, or remedied, such as access, accommodation, review, and redress — become **enforceable claims scope by scope as enforcement readiness is verified**. Before that, they bind as duties of construction: adopt the target, measure the starting point, build the enforcing institutions and supporting culture on a dated, resourced path, and give affected sentients the best available interim safeguards. A delivery duty that is not yet enforceable is an **attainment gap**, not a waiver, an exception, or a lowered target.
- **Enforcement readiness:** a Rights Floor becomes enforceable for a defined scope — a population, system class, setting, or region — when verified data show that the institutions, capability, and culture needed to enforce it are ready for that scope. Readiness criteria and their evidence are governed by [**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*).
  - **Declaration follows data:** enforceability for a scope is declared, with published reasons, by a party independent of the ledger owner once the verified criteria in the plan are met. Withholding the declaration after that point is self-created delay under **Article XXVIII-B** (*Adoption Transition Authority Limits and Reauthorization*), and affected sentients may contest a declaration as premature or withheld. The declaration is due no later than the outer bound of the Tier that matches the scope's class (7 days for Class A, 14 for B, 30 for C, 60 for L, 90 for P) where the scope is a system class, and 14 days, the Tier B outer bound, for any other scope, counted from the day the verified criteria are met, as the data shows, whether or not the ledger owner has verified them yet; the verification step is itself due inside the intake target of the Tier that matches the scope's class, or Tier B for any other scope. Staging of scopes and contest procedure are governed by [**CI-6.275 §4.4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-44-enforcement-readiness-declarations) (*Enforcement-readiness declarations*).
  - **No narrowing:** once a scope is declared enforceable it stays enforceable. Loss of capacity afterward is an institutional failure handled under **Article XXVIII-C** (*Failure Off-Ramps, Re-Baselining, and Traceability*) and the forum-continuity rules, not a quiet withdrawal of the right.
- **Rights-Floor attainment path:** Each Adoption Transition plan must carry an attainment path covering every Rights-Floor domain in scope.
  - **Starting measure:** each domain's starting coverage and practical usability are measured and published before the first gate that depends on that domain. A domain not yet measured is **unmeasured**, which is treated as unattained and as a measurement priority — never as a pass.
  - **Fixed target:** the target is the full constitutional floor. A plan may not negotiate a lesser target, and re-baselining under **Article XXVIII-C** (*Failure Off-Ramps, Re-Baselining, and Traceability*) moves dates and owners, not the target or the starting measure.
  - **Dated glide path:** each domain has an accountable owner, committed resources, dated milestones, and a final date by which the floor is enforceable for the full scope, scaled to the affected class and material stake.
  - **What an open gap is not:** an open attainment gap within an approved path is not a defense to a breach of any restraint duty, and it does not suspend [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) (*Compliance, Violation, and Standing Model*) for such conduct.
  - **Ratchet:** a recorded attainment level may not go backward through the adopter's avoidable acts, and a decline is a failure where avoidable or unrecovered. Milestone dates move only through **Article XXVIII-B** (*Adoption Transition Authority Limits and Reauthorization*) reauthorization or **Article XXVIII-C** (*Failure Off-Ramps, Re-Baselining, and Traceability*) re-baselining.
  - **Outer limit:** a gap that passes a milestone without reauthorization is an Adoption Transition milestone failure and opens the ordinary path under [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) (*Compliance, Violation, and Standing Model*). A gap that outlives its final attainment date, or is carried by repeated reauthorization without closing, is a failed Adoption Transition state that does not become the baseline by persistence. A gap that has become indefinite is treated as the permanent extinguishment the Rights-Floor Minimums Principle bars.
