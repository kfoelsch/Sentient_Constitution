## Additional updated rule — Article VII-A genetic-information rule

**Current English passage:**

- **Self-ownership of genetic and substrate-defining information:** Every sentient has the main say over the information that defines them at the most basic level — the traits they can pass on, how they develop, and what kind of being they are.

## Additional updated rule — Article VII-B no reconstruction from data

**Current English passage:**

## Additional updated passage — Article VI-B sentience-status adjudication floor

**Current English passage:**

*In plain terms: when an entity's sentience is in doubt, it gets a fair hearing first and is treated as included by default — the burden of denying protection rests on whoever wants to withhold it, and a wrongful exclusion must be reversible.*

This Article sets out the floor for deciding disputed sentience status. The procedure that carries it out is in **Chapter Thirteen §5** (*Sentience-status adjudication*), and that procedure must not narrow this floor:
- Any entity whose sentience is materially disputed has a right to a timely, impartial, reviewable decision before protections depending on that status are granted, withdrawn, or narrowed; origin, form, substrate class, and adopter convenience do not affect the right.
- When sentience is genuinely unsettled, the entity is treated as included under the Chapter Six Rights Floor. Uncertainty alone never justifies withholding protection: wrongful inclusion can be undone, wrongful exclusion cannot.
- Any withholding or narrowing must be as narrow as possible, carry a declared end date, be periodically reviewed, and be reversible. If wrong, protection is restored with Redress and Remediation for the withheld period.
- The entity has the right to an independent representative. A parent system or operator may provide evidence but may not be the only filer, witness, or source of evidence against the entity.
- Inclusion protects the entity's rights, not the operator's property or commercial interest; it does not prevent rights-respecting containment of a system.
- Anyone may file to confirm inclusion. One credible sign under Chapter Five Sentience Evaluation and Sentience Indicator Integrity is enough, not proof; operator self-description, a substrate label, product category, or bare claim is insufficient. Rejecting a filing with no credible sign is not a ruling against the entity. A filer seeking to withhold or narrow protection bears the burden under Chapter Four evidence standards and Necessity, Proportionality, and Procedural Fairness; protection remains until decision.
- Only this Article, Def.P1, and Chapter Thirteen §5 decide who counts as sentient. Certification badges or records, LEQU or standing scores, competency bars or clearance, standing locks, substrate labels, and product classifications do not decide sentience status.

**Proposed locale translation:**

*简言之：当某个实体是否具有感知能力尚存疑问时，应先给予公正听审，并默认将其纳入保护。主张拒绝保护的一方承担举证责任；错误排除必须能够撤销。*

## Additional updated passage — Article XII-A representation and legitimacy gate

*In plain terms: decisions that materially affect sentients must be explained on the record and open to real challenge, and every materially affected group must have a genuine seat — especially those without market or institutional power. High-impact decisions also need a recorded legitimacy check before they bind anyone.*

This Article sets out the floors for deliberation records, for representation in high-impact decisions, and for the legitimacy gate those decisions must pass before they bind:

- **Deliberation and records:** Decisions that materially affect rights, access, or resources must be supported by accessible records; understandable explanation where feasible; and a practical opportunity for affected stakeholders to contest the decision, consistent with adopted adjudicative implementation safeguards.
- **Representation and standing in high-impact decisions:** For materially high-impact decisions, governance design must document stakeholder-class mapping; maintain representation pathways for each materially affected class; and preserve procedural standing for dependency-affected and high-risk groups, even where they lack conventional market or institutional power.
- **Legitimacy gate before binding adoption:** No materially high-impact decision is binding unless legitimacy checks are completed and recorded before adoption. Recorded checks must include participation sufficiency, affected-class coverage, dissent recording, and explanation of how materially relevant non-selected alternatives were evaluated.
- **Act Record:** Where such a decision is a [materially binding act](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), its [Act Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act-record) must carry or link the stakeholder-class mapping and representation pathways used; the participation record, legitimacy checks, and explanation given; and the challenge route and its status. - **Scope:** layered with **Article X-B** (*Governance Participation and Voting Entitlement*). The weighting rules in this Article:

## Article VII-A public-health limitation passage (current source lines 422–428)

**Current English passage:**

- **Public-health requirements:** A vaccination requirement — or a similar requirement to get preventive treatment or testing — limits the right to refuse care. Such a requirement is allowed only if it passes [Chapter One §7.1 Limitation Discipline](core_01.000_values_principles.md#71-limitation-discipline) and meets every one of these conditions:
  - it responds to a specific, evidence-based risk of significant harm to others or to society as a whole — not to a risk that falls only on the sentient themselves;
  - gentler options that could reasonably work are tried first, such as sharing information, offering the measure voluntarily, or allowing alternatives like testing;
  - it exempts anyone for whom the measure itself would pose a real health risk, and makes room for matters of conscience under [**Article XI-A** (*Freedom of conscience, religion, and comparable worldview*)](#article-xi-a-freedom-of-conscience-religion-and-comparable-worldview) — in both cases in keeping with [Proportionality](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#proportionality): exemptions are weighed against the size of the risk to others, and may be narrowed only as far as needed to keep the measure from being defeated;
  - it has an end date, the evidence behind it is published, and it is reviewed regularly;
  - anyone affected can challenge it before an independent reviewer under [Procedural Fairness](core_05.055_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness); and
  - it is not enforced through penalties that take away the survival, healthcare, or work guarantees in [**Article III** (*Survival and Essential Access*)](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-survival-and-essential-access) or the education guarantees in [**Article IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*)](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-right-to-sentient-centered-education), and it is never enforced by physical force except under the emergency thresholds of [**Chapter Thirteen §6.1**](core_14.000_forum.md#61-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) (*Emergency measures and continuation burden*).
