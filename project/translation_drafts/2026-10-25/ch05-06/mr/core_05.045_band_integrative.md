### Source line 384; section: No-Bypass; anchor `no-bypass`

**English source line:**   - **In scope:** A constitutional requirement attaches to the substantive act. It cannot be avoided by changing the label, route, owner (including a supplier, partner, or other system that performs or bears the act), forum, document, or timing of that same act, and packaging the act as a special process does not skip it. Whether a requirement was met is judged on what the act substantively is and does, shown by evidence that can be checked, not on the actor's own label, self-report, or paperwork.
