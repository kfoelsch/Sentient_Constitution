An Operating Party that is below every line stays Class P, where a System Charter or Institutional Charter is voluntary. A member acting alone does not make the Operating Party covered.`
- `  - <a id="domain-specific-materiality-private-unit"></a>**In scope:** The meaning of private unit in this entry and in **CS-9.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-9.310** (*System classification machinery*): a single sentient, a household, or a tightly bounded private circle whose members all chose to be in it, and whose acts and effects stay among them. Effects that reach others, or that others rely on, are beyond the private unit. Ordinary community activity open to others is not a private unit; the nine questions test it.`

- `      - Material when: the Operating Party is a major source of others' income (as employer, payer, buyer, or platform), sets prices or terms for something others need and cannot easily replace, or holds market power as [Chapter One §11 Market Structure](core_01.005_values_principles.md#11-market-structure) describes it.`
- `    - **Ordinary community activity is the expected case, and the Operating Party shows it:** Giving, making, teaching, caring, hosting, and sharing are expected to fall below every line unless a domain's own test is met. For ordinary community activity, the short written statement in the primary assessment is that showing. A challenge to the statement must name a specific crossing in a named domain, and the Operating Party then shows it is below that line. Each case a domain lists under its "Material when" test, such as the care of dependents under Safety, is a named crossing: a challenger who shows the Operating Party doing one has named it. Size, headcount, regular meetings, and being valued by those served are not crossings. Where a named crossing is still in doubt after the Operating Party's showing, [Materiality Under Uncertainty](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#materiality-under-uncertainty) applies.
