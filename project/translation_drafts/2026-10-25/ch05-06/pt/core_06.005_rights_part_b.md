## Additional updated rule — Article VII-A genetic-information rule

**Current English passage:**

- **Self-ownership of genetic and substrate-defining information:** Every sentient has the main say over the information that defines them at the most basic level — the traits they can pass on, how they develop, and what kind of being they are.

  - For other kinds of sentients, it means whatever information plays the same defining role for them.

## Additional updated rule — Article VII-B self-ownership of mind

**Current English passage:**

- **Self-ownership of mind:** Every sentient has the right to keep their thoughts, feelings, and inner mental life private and secure.
  - **Allowed under this Article:** ordinary, everyday sensing of how others seem to feel, and reading someone with their consent — as a therapist, doctor, or coach does.
  - **Not allowed without the sentient's Consent or other proper authorization:**
    - deliberately monitoring or analyzing a sentient — for example through surveillance, data collection, or tools built for the purpose — to work out or reconstruct their inner states;

  - **Não permitido sem o Consentimento do ser senciente ou outra autorização válida:**
    - monitorar ou analisar deliberadamente um ser senciente — por exemplo, por meio de vigilância, coleta de dados ou ferramentas criadas para esse fim — para descobrir ou reconstruir seus estados internos;

## Additional updated rule — Article VII-B no reconstruction from data

**Current English passage:**

- **No reconstructing someone's inner life from their data:** No one may collect, combine, cross-match, or analyze records of what a sentient does or how they interact in ways that let anyone reconstruct or estimate their private thoughts and feelings. The only exceptions are those this Constitution allows under this Article, **Chapter One**, **Chapter Five**, and **CS-6.305 (*Information types and handling*) and CS-6.310** (*Data classifications*). This ban applies the [Chapter One §15.1.2 Derived-Information Principle](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#1512-derived-information-principle) to inner states. The ban covers:
  - reconstructing someone's inner states directly;
  - reconstructing them indirectly;
  - producing a reliable estimate of them;
  - calling the method something else, or measuring stand-ins, to get the same result.

**Proposed locale translation:**

- **Proibição de reconstruir a vida interior a partir dos dados:** Ninguém pode coletar, combinar, cruzar ou analisar registros do que um ser senciente faz ou de como interage de modo a permitir a reconstrução ou estimativa de seus pensamentos e sentimentos privados. As únicas exceções são as permitidas por esta Constituição nos termos deste Artigo, do **Chapter One**, do **Chapter Five**, e de **CS-6.305 (*Information types and handling*) e CS-6.310** (*Data classifications*). Esta proibição aplica aos estados internos o [Chapter One §15.1.2 Derived-Information Principle](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#1512-derived-information-principle). A proibição abrange:
  - reconstruir diretamente os estados internos de alguém;
  - reconstruí-los indiretamente;
  - produzir uma estimativa confiável deles;

## Additional updated passage — Article X-B governance voting entitlement and political-equality floor (source lines 1292–1304; source SHA-256: 6611eb401519e173972e87b38f478c3e965eef2876ef374212d8154bf7061635)

### Current English source excerpt (lines 1292–1304)

*In plain terms: when a community decides its biggest questions — who holds power, how that power is granted, and on what terms — every sentient gets a vote, and every vote counts the same. Age, what kind of being someone is, or where they come from cannot make a vote count for less.*

This Article covers two things: who gets to vote, and the rule that votes on the biggest questions count equally.

- **The right to vote:** Sentients have the right to vote in **governance voting** and in other **binding collective choice** that decides how authority is granted. The details are in [Chapter Fourteen §4.1 Entitlement and eligibility](core_15.000_governance.md#41-entitlement-and-eligibility).
  - **Who can vote:** Anyone who meets the published eligibility criteria can vote. The only exception is a [Standing Lock](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#standing-lock) that specifically blocks governance voting, applied under **Article XIX-D** (*Violations and Standing Locks*) and **Article XIX-E** (*Voting and Special Locks*).
  - **Not the same as a stakeholder's say:** This vote is separate from a stakeholder's say in how an already-established system is run, which is covered in [**Article X-C** (*Stakeholder Role and Participation Rights*)](#article-x-c-stakeholder-role-and-participation-rights). A lock on one does not lock the other.
  - **Local rules:** Each adopter's own recorded process for granting authority (its [documented legitimacy mechanism](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#documented-legitimacy-mechanism)), and any extra criteria set by its designated owners, apply alongside this right. They may not shrink it.
- **Equal votes on the biggest questions:** Some decisions set the foundations — who holds governing authority, how that authority is legitimately granted, and what its scope and lasting terms are. **Chapter Five** calls these [Foundational Constitutional Choice](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#foundational-constitutional-choice). For these decisions, a **political-equality floor** applies:
  - Every sentient in the community entitled to vote has an equal vote.
  - Age, substrate (the kind of body or system a sentient exists in), and lineage (ancestry or origin) may not be used to change how much a vote counts. This follows **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*) and **Chapter Five** *Sentience Non-Exclusion*.
  - **Where equal votes stop:** This rule covers setting up and authorizing the system of authority itself. Once a system, institution, or decision area has been properly set up, rights-affecting decisions made inside its [Governance](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#governance) — its structures, rules, allocation of authority, and processes — follow ordinary **Stakeholder Weight**, where each stakeholder's say matches how much the decision affects them.
  - [Chapter Fourteen §4.1 Entitlement and eligibility](core_15.000_governance.md#41-entitlement-and-eligibility) sets out how this works in practice, and it may not weaken this floor.

### Proposed translation (condensed excerpt)

Toda pessoa senciente tem direito a votar em eleições de governança e escolhas coletivas vinculantes que concedam autoridade. Qualquer pessoa que cumpra critérios de elegibilidade publicados pode votar; a única exceção é um Standing Lock que bloqueie especificamente o voto de governança nos termos de XIX-D e XIX-E. Esse voto é distinto da voz de stakeholders na gestão de um sistema já estabelecido; bloquear um não bloqueia o outro. O mecanismo documentado de legitimidade de cada adotante e critérios adicionais dos proprietários designados coexistem com o direito, sem reduzi-lo. Nas escolhas constitucionais fundamentais sobre quem detém autoridade, como ela é legitimamente concedida, seu escopo e termos duradouros, cada pessoa senciente elegível tem voto igual; idade, substrato ou linhagem não podem alterar o peso. Após instituído o sistema, decisões internas que afetam direitos seguem o Stakeholder Weight ordinário, conforme o impacto em cada stakeholder. O processo de elegibilidade do Capítulo Quatorze não pode enfraquecer esse piso.


## Additional updated passage — Article XII-B participation-weighting scope, anti-capture, and no-token safeguards (source lines 1989–2009; source SHA-256: 6611eb401519e173972e87b38f478c3e965eef2876ef374212d8154bf7061635)

### Current English source excerpt (lines 1989–2009)

*In plain terms: participation weighting can reflect who is affected and how much, but it must never become a device for silencing affected groups or handing permanent control to a narrow coalition — and "consulting" stakeholders whose input is ignored or unreachable does not count.*

This Article sets out the scope of participation weighting, the constraints on it, and the prohibition of token participation:

- **Scope:** layered with **Article X-B** (*Governance Participation and Voting Entitlement*). The weighting rules in this Article:
  - **apply** where **[Stakeholder Weight](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-weight)** is the governing rule — materially rights-affecting decisions *within* the **[Governance](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#governance)** **structures, rules, allocation of authority, and processes** already authorized for the **relevant system, institution, or bounded decision domain**;
  - do **not** authorize impact-, dependency-, or stake-proportional **vote weights** for **[Foundational Constitutional Choice](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#foundational-constitutional-choice)** (**Article X-B** (*Governance Participation and Voting Entitlement*); [**Chapter Fourteen §4.1 Entitlement and eligibility**](core_15.000_governance.md#41-entitlement-and-eligibility)).
- **Weighted participation constraints:** Participation weighting may account for:
  - impact;
  - dependency;
  - demonstrated stake.
  
  Weighting must not:
  - reduce materially affected stakeholder voice to symbolic participation (see **No token participation** below);
  - grant persistent control to any narrow coalition, operator bloc, or capital-concentration proxy, absent constitutionally sufficient justification.
  
  Weighting rules must be auditable, periodically revalidated, and contestable.
- **No token participation:** Asking stakeholders for their views does not count if it is only for show. Consultation breaks this rule — whether or not weighting is used — if, for the sentients materially affected, any of these is true:
  - what they say cannot change the outcome;
  - they cannot reasonably reach or use it;
  - their input is routinely ignored.

### Proposed translation (condensed excerpt)

Junto ao Artigo X-B, estas regras se aplicam quando o Stakeholder Weight rege decisões que afetam materialmente direitos dentro das estruturas, regras, alocações de autoridade e processos já autorizados de um sistema, instituição ou domínio decisório delimitado. Elas não autorizam ponderar votos por impacto, dependência ou interesse em Escolhas Constitucionais Fundamentais. A ponderação pode considerar impacto, dependência e interesse demonstrado, mas não reduzir a voz de stakeholders materialmente afetados a participação simbólica nem conceder controle persistente a coalizão estreita, bloco de operadores ou proxy de concentração de capital sem justificativa constitucional suficiente. As regras devem ser auditáveis, revalidadas periodicamente e contestáveis. A consulta é simbólica se a opinião dos afetados não puder mudar o resultado, se não puderem acessá-la/usá-la razoavelmente ou se suas contribuições forem ignoradas de forma recorrente.


## Article VII-A public-health limitation passage (current source lines 422–428)

**Current English passage:**

- **Public-health requirements:** A vaccination requirement — or a similar requirement to get preventive treatment or testing — limits the right to refuse care. Such a requirement is allowed only if it passes [Chapter One §7.1 Limitation Discipline](core_01.000_values_principles.md#71-limitation-discipline) and meets every one of these conditions:
  - it responds to a specific, evidence-based risk of significant harm to others or to society as a whole — not to a risk that falls only on the sentient themselves;
  - gentler options that could reasonably work are tried first, such as sharing information, offering the measure voluntarily, or allowing alternatives like testing;
  - it exempts anyone for whom the measure itself would pose a real health risk, and makes room for matters of conscience under [**Article XI-A** (*Freedom of conscience, religion, and comparable worldview*)](#article-xi-a-freedom-of-conscience-religion-and-comparable-worldview) — in both cases in keeping with [Proportionality](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#proportionality): exemptions are weighed against the size of the risk to others, and may be narrowed only as far as needed to keep the measure from being defeated;
  - it has an end date, the evidence behind it is published, and it is reviewed regularly;
  - anyone affected can challenge it before an independent reviewer under [Procedural Fairness](core_05.055_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness); and
  - it is not enforced through penalties that take away the survival, healthcare, or work guarantees in [**Article III** (*Survival and Essential Access*)](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-survival-and-essential-access) or the education guarantees in [**Article IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*)](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-right-to-sentient-centered-education), and it is never enforced by physical force except under the emergency thresholds of [**Chapter Thirteen §6.1**](core_14.000_forum.md#61-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) (*Emergency measures and continuation burden*).

**Proposed locale translation:**

**Requisitos de saúde pública:** Uma exigência de vacinação — ou semelhante para tratamento preventivo ou testagem — limita o direito de recusar cuidados. Só é permitida se passar pela [Disciplina de Limitação do Capítulo Um §7.1](core_01.000_values_principles.md#71-limitation-discipline) e cumprir todas estas condições:
- responder a um risco específico, baseado em evidências, de dano significativo a outras pessoas ou à sociedade como um todo — não a um risco que recaia apenas sobre o próprio ser senciente;
- tentar primeiro opções mais brandas que possam funcionar razoavelmente, como compartilhar informações, oferecer a medida voluntariamente ou permitir alternativas como testagem;
- isentar quem enfrentaria risco real à saúde por causa da própria medida e acolher questões de consciência nos termos do [Artigo XI-A](#article-xi-a-freedom-of-conscience-religion-and-comparable-worldview), conforme a [Proporcionalidade](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#proportionality): as isenções são ponderadas em relação ao risco para terceiros e só podem ser limitadas no necessário para evitar que a medida seja frustrada;
- ter data de término, publicar as evidências que a fundamentam e passar por revisão regular;
- permitir que qualquer pessoa afetada a conteste perante revisor independente, conforme a [Justiça Processual](core_05.055_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness); e
- não ser aplicada por sanções que retirem as garantias de sobrevivência, saúde ou trabalho do [Artigo III](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-survival-and-essential-access), nem as garantias educacionais do [Artigo IV](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-right-to-sentient-centered-education); e nunca ser aplicada por força física, salvo dentro dos limiares de emergência do [Capítulo Treze §6.1](core_14.000_forum.md#61-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden).
