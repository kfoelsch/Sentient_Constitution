## Additional updated passage — Article V-B cross-system fairness and sustainability

**Current English passage:**

*In plain terms: systems that lean heavily on shared foundations must put enough back to keep them running, in real and traceable resources, not gestures. Shared money and capacity should be split with attention to who is truly dependent, who has real alternatives, environmental burden, and whether use can last, not mainly to whoever wins in the short run. [System Alignment Certification](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification) is where this is checked: certification compares what a system takes from shared infrastructure with what it gives back, using the maps and flow records that **Article V-A** (*Dependency Mapping and Resource-Flow Transparency*) requires. Where arrangements concentrate wealth, power, control, or opportunity in ways that harm others, Chapter One non-concentration rules add further scrutiny on top.*

This Article sets the minimum rules for sharing the load fairly between systems. It also covers [Cross-System Extraction](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#cross-system-extraction): when a system uses shared infrastructure that others depend on but does not put enough back to keep it running. [System Alignment Certification](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification) under [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) is where that floor is verified.

- **What the floor requires:**
  - **Fair allocation:** Funding and allocation of shared infrastructure and foundational dependencies must account for:
    - criticality, shown by the shared infrastructure's [system class](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#2-system-class-evaluation) where one is assigned;
    - dependency asymmetry;
    - substitutability;
    - availability of alternatives;
    - ecological footprint, including upstream and downstream burden;
    - long-term sustainability.
  - **No persistent extraction:** Systems must not persistently extract. A system that draws on shared foundations must put [Proportionate Cross-System Support](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#proportionate-cross-system-support) back; that entry sets what the support must cover and how its adequacy is judged.
  - **What does not count:** Do not treat a press release, a one-time grant, an opaque transfer, an off-map transfer, or the fact that a system meets the [survival floor](#article-iii-a-survival) or stays within [market-concentration limits](core_01.000_values_principles.md#11-market-structure) as Proportionate Cross-System Support.
  - **No formula precondition:** Adequacy is judged on the flows mapped now under **Article V-A** (*Dependency Mapping and Resource-Flow Transparency*). No allocation formula or numeric target is a precondition.
- **How certification checks it:** Certification evaluates this floor under [Chapter Nine §5.3 Proportionate Cross-System Support Evaluation](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#53-proportionate-cross-system-support-evaluation), which sets what it must verify, how its depth scales with system class, what goes on the **System Certification Record**, and when it runs.
- **When support falls short:** Shortfalls are certification defects, with the consequences set in [Chapter Nine §5.3 Proportionate Cross-System Support Evaluation](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#53-proportionate-cross-system-support-evaluation) and [Chapter Nine Part B §7 Certification Outcomes](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). Persistent imbalances also remain open to audit under **Article XVI-A** (*Auditability and Observable Evidence*) and challenge under [Article XIII-A](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-a-reliability-and-trustworthiness-baseline) (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*), whether or not a certification is pending.
- **Between certifications:** These duties do not pause between reviews, and they also bind systems below the certification trigger. Keeping support adequate and flows mapped is what lets the next certification, audit, or challenge rely on them.

**Proposed locale translation:**

*Em termos simples: sistemas muito dependentes de bases compartilhadas devem devolver recursos reais, rastreáveis e suficientes para mantê-las, não meros gestos. Fundos e capacidade comuns devem ser alocados considerando a dependência efetiva, alternativas reais, ônus ambiental e sustentabilidade do uso, não apenas o vencedor de curto prazo. A Certificação de Alinhamento do Sistema compara o que um sistema retira da infraestrutura compartilhada com o que devolve, usando os mapas e registros de fluxo exigidos pelo Artigo V-A. Arranjos que concentrem riqueza, poder, controle ou oportunidade em prejuízo de outros recebem escrutínio adicional segundo as regras de não concentração do Capítulo Um.*

Este Artigo estabelece regras mínimas para dividir justamente o ônus entre sistemas e inclui a **Extração entre Sistemas**: usar infraestrutura compartilhada da qual outros dependem sem contribuir o bastante para mantê-la.
- **Alocação justa:** O financiamento e a alocação de infraestrutura compartilhada e dependências fundamentais devem considerar criticidade (classe do sistema, quando atribuída), assimetria de dependência, substituibilidade, alternativas disponíveis, pegada ecológica a montante e a jusante e sustentabilidade de longo prazo.
- **Sem extração persistente:** Sistemas não devem extrair persistentemente. Quem usa bases compartilhadas deve oferecer **Apoio Proporcional entre Sistemas**, conforme seu escopo e critério de suficiência.
- **O que não conta:** Comunicado à imprensa, doação única, transferência opaca ou fora do mapa, ou apenas cumprir o piso de sobrevivência ou limites de concentração de mercado não constitui apoio proporcional.
- **Sem fórmula prévia obrigatória:** A suficiência é avaliada pelos fluxos atualmente mapeados sob o Artigo V-A; fórmula de alocação ou meta numérica não é pré-requisito.
- **Verificação da certificação:** A avaliação do Capítulo Nove §5.3 define o que verificar e registrar, a profundidade conforme a classe do sistema e quando fazê-lo.
- **Apoio insuficiente:** A insuficiência é defeito de certificação, com consequências previstas no Capítulo Nove §5.3 e Parte B §7. Desequilíbrios persistentes continuam sujeitos a auditoria pelo Artigo XVI-A e a contestação pelo XIII-A, com ou sem certificação pendente.
- **Entre certificações:** Os deveres não cessam entre revisões e também vinculam sistemas abaixo do gatilho de certificação. Apoio adequado e fluxos mapeados permitem que a próxima certificação, auditoria ou contestação se baseie neles.

## Additional updated passage — Article IV-C lifelong/adaptive learning and contestable learning systems

**Current English passage:**

*In plain terms: when the world and the tools change, sentients are not left quietly obsolete — they get fair paths to retrain.*

This Article sets out the floors for lifelong learning and for transparent, contestable learning systems:
- **Lifelong and adaptive learning:** Where system evolution materially changes required competencies, sentients must have practical and reasonably accessible opportunities for continuing education, retraining, and transition support. Those opportunities must prevent agency from being nullified by imposed obsolescence or lock-in. They must also include equitable stewardship-development pathways into constitutionally relevant stewardship responsibilities under **Chapter Fourteen, section 5** — *Authorized Roles, Competency Development, and Contribution*.
- **Transparency and contestability in learning systems:** When a school, platform, or training system can materially affect a sentient's future through grades, rankings, recommendations, or placement, sentients must be able to see how those decisions are made, have them checked, and challenge them if they are wrong. Those systems cannot hide behind secret rules that cannot be reviewed, or pressure or trick sentients into choices they would not make freely.

**Proposed locale translation:**

*Em termos simples: quando o mundo e as ferramentas mudam, os seres sencientes não são deixados silenciosamente obsoletos — recebem caminhos justos de requalificação.*

Este Artigo estabelece pisos para a aprendizagem ao longo da vida e para sistemas de aprendizagem transparentes e contestáveis:
- **Aprendizagem contínua e adaptativa:** Quando a evolução do sistema alterar materialmente as competências exigidas, os seres sencientes devem ter oportunidades práticas e razoavelmente acessíveis de:
  - educação continuada;
  - requalificação;
  - apoio à transição.

  Essas oportunidades devem impedir que a agência seja anulada por obsolescência imposta ou bloqueio. Também devem incluir caminhos equitativos de desenvolvimento de stewardship que levem a responsabilidades de stewardship constitucionalmente relevantes, conforme o **Capítulo Quatorze, seção 5** — *Funções autorizadas, desenvolvimento de competências e contribuição*.

  - mitigation and interruption measures are proportionate to the scale of possible harm;
  - monitoring can detect the causal pathway early enough to interrupt it **before harm becomes irreversible**, and a named actor holds both the authority and the practical capacity to interrupt it.
  
  The burden is continuing: it must be met again on new material evidence, on expansion, and at intervals proportionate to the risk. Where it is not met, the activity is non-compliant, and consequences follow [Chapter Three §3 — Non-Compliance Finding Profiles](core_03.000_definition_integrity.md#3-non-compliance-finding-profiles). Each authorization, continuation, or expansion determination under this Article is a [materially binding act](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act). Its [Act Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act-record) must link the actor's showing and the reviewer's assessment, and must openly state uncertainty, assumptions, evidence limits, disagreement, and material gaps.
