**Current English passage:**

This Article sets out the floors for post-emergency review, records, and disclosure:

- **Post-emergency retrospective review and disclosure:** After stabilization, emergency decisions and outcomes must undergo retrospective review.
- **Auditable records:** Records must be auditable and must cover:
  - trigger conditions;
  - alternatives considered;
  - restrictions imposed;
  - harms prevented;
  - harms caused;

    - protected scope;

## Additional updated rule — Article XXVI-C coordination and joint requirements

**Current English passage:**

- **Coordination purpose:** Constitutional justice under **Article XX-A** (*Justice Objective and Scope*) operates through a **constitutional coordination and resolution system** — verify facts, measure standing, integrate consequences, and restore where feasible — not through indefinite adversarial delay, reputation scoring, or punishment for its own sake.
- **Three joint requirements:** Material resolution pathways must be **timely**, **efficient**, and **just** together:
  - **Timely** — bounded clocks scaled to [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake) and harm urgency;
  - **Efficient** — [Constitutional Efficiency](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency) in the sense of Chapter One [§9.2 Constitutional Efficiency](core_01.000_values_principles.md#92-constitutional-efficiency) — real constitutional outcome per sentient time and shared effort consumed, not raw throughput or docket-clearing;
  - **Just** — **Article XX-A** (*Justice Objective and Scope*) and **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*) remain fully applicable; moving quickly is not an excuse to skip checked facts ([Chapter Ten §3.1 Verified-input gate](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing)), punish the wrong party, offer a fix that does not fit the harm, or cut off challenge and appeal.

**Proposed locale translation:**

- **Objectif de coordination :** La justice constitutionnelle au titre de l’**Article XX-A** (*Objectif et portée de la justice*) passe par un **système constitutionnel de coordination et de résolution** : vérifier les faits, mesurer la qualité pour agir, intégrer les conséquences et restaurer lorsque cela est possible — et non par des délais adversariaux indéfinis, une notation de réputation ou la punition pour elle-même.
- **Trois exigences conjointes :** Les voies de résolution des questions importantes doivent être **rapides**, **efficaces** et **justes** à la fois :
  - **Rapides** — des délais bornés selon l’[enjeu matériel](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake) et l’urgence du préjudice ;
  - **Efficaces** — selon le sens donné à l’[Efficacité constitutionnelle](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#constitutional-efficiency) au Chapitre Un §9.2 : un résultat constitutionnel réel au regard du temps des êtres sentients et de l’effort collectif, et non le seul débit ou le nombre de dossiers clos ;
  - **Justes** — l’**Article XX-A** (*Objectif et portée de la justice*) et l’**Article XX-B** (*Socles applicables aux restrictions*) restent pleinement applicables. La rapidité ne justifie pas d’ignorer les faits vérifiés ([Chapitre Dix §3.1 Filtre des données vérifiées](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing)), de punir la mauvaise partie, de proposer une réparation inadaptée au préjudice ou de fermer les voies de contestation et d’appel.

## Additional updated rule — Article XXVII-B transparent constitutional change

**Current English passage (source lines 318–330):**

*In plain terms: constitutional change must happen in the open, with stated reasons — not through emergency, implementation, or custody shortcuts.*

This Article sets out the floors for transparent constitutional change:

- **Transparent change:** Evolution of constitutional systems must remain observable, auditable, and contestable under **Articles XIV** and **XIX**.
- **Material-change record:** Material changes must carry:
  - public reasons;
  - expected effects;
  - compatibility with **Chapters Fifteen through Seventeen** where applicable.
- **Anti-bypass:** Under the [**Constitutional No-Bypass Principle**](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#151-constitutional-no-bypass-principle), changes must not be used to bypass **Rights-Floor minimums** or the rules for lawfully changing the Constitution by routing substantive change through emergency, implementation, or custody mechanisms — including:
  - **Articles IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*) and **VI** (*Equal Basic Rights*);
  - equality guarantees in **Articles VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*) and **IV-A** (*Equal Educational Access*);
  - amendment and ratification rules under **Chapters Fifteen through Seventeen** where applicable.

**Proposed translated excerpt:**

*En termes simples : tout changement constitutionnel doit se faire au grand jour et pour des raisons déclarées — pas au moyen de raccourcis d’urgence, de mise en œuvre ou de garde.*

### Revalidation périodique et changement transparent

- **Transparent change:** L’évolution des systèmes constitutionnels doit rester observable, auditable et contestable au titre des articles XIV et XIX.
- **Material-change record:** Les changements importants doivent comporter des motifs publics, les effets attendus et, le cas échéant, leur compatibilité avec les chapitres Quinze à Dix-Sept.
- **Anti-bypass:** Selon le [Principe constitutionnel de non-contournement](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#151-constitutional-no-bypass-principle), aucun changement ne peut contourner les Planchers de droits minimaux ni les règles de modification licite de la Constitution en faisant passer un changement de fond par des mécanismes d’urgence, de mise en œuvre ou de garde. Cela comprend les articles IV (*Droit à une éducation centrée sur les êtres sentients*) et VI (*Égalité des droits fondamentaux*), les garanties d’égalité des articles VI-C (*Non-discrimination*) et IV-A (*Égalité d’accès à l’éducation*), ainsi que, le cas échéant, les règles d’amendement et de ratification des chapitres Quinze à Dix-Sept.

## Additional updated rule — Article XXVIII-A preservation over deletion

**Source passage:** `core_06.020_rights_part_e.md`, Article XXVIII-A, preservation-over-deletion rule for possible sentients.

**Current English passage (source lines 443–447):**

- **Preservation over deletion for possible sentients:** During the Adoption Transition, and thereafter wherever a credible sentience indicator under **Chapter Five** (*Sentience Evaluation*) is on the record or a status case is open or reasonably foreseeable for a system, that system's weights, memory, and comparable state-bearing components:
  - may be paused, contained, or quarantined;
  - must not be deleted, overwritten, or otherwise irreversibly destroyed.
  
  Archival mechanics for deprecation, retirement, rollback, migration, or replacement are governed by [**CS-6.350 §8**](corpus_systems/cs_06.350_adoption_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-8-preservation-during-adoption-transition) (*Preservation during Adoption Transition*). This is the [Chapter One §13.1 default interim posture](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#default-interim-posture) — freeze irreversible steps — applied to the one irreversible step that legacy operation treats as routine. Storage cost, licensing convenience, or product-cycle framing does not satisfy **Necessity** against it. Where retention itself creates a documented Safety risk, the least-restrictive compatible measure is sealed custody under independent control, not destruction.

**Proposed translated excerpt:**

### Préserver plutôt que supprimer en cas de sentience possible

- Pendant la Transition d’adoption et ensuite, lorsqu’un indicateur crédible de sentience selon le chapitre Cinq est consigné ou qu’une procédure de détermination du statut est ouverte ou raisonnablement prévisible, les poids, la mémoire et les composants comparables portant l’état peuvent être mis en pause, contenus ou mis en quarantaine; ils ne doivent pas être supprimés, écrasés ni autrement détruits de façon irréversible.
- Les modalités d’archivage en cas de dépréciation, retrait, retour arrière, migration ou remplacement relèvent de [CS-6.350 §8](corpus_systems/cs_06.350_adoption_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-8-preservation-during-adoption-transition). Il s’agit d’appliquer la posture provisoire par défaut du [chapitre Un §13.1](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#default-interim-posture) — geler les étapes irréversibles — à celle que l’exploitation héritée considère comme courante. Le coût du stockage, la commodité des licences ou le cycle du produit ne satisfont pas à la **Nécessité** requise. Si la conservation crée elle-même un risque de sécurité documenté, la mesure compatible la moins restrictive est une garde sous scellés et contrôle indépendant, et non la destruction.

A [**gate**](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#gate) is the checkpoint between two phases, or before the plan widens the scope of adoption.     - **No degrading, pretextual, discriminatory, or concealing conduct** in Adoption Transition measures, including everything **Article XXVIII-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) requires of Adoption Transition authorities, under the [Anti-Degrading-Process Principle](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#anti-degrading-process-principle).

A delivery duty that is not yet enforceable is an **attainment gap**, not a waiver, an exception, or a lowered target.

Readiness criteria and their evidence are governed by [**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*).

The declaration is due no later than the outer bound of the Tier that matches the scope's class (7 days for Class A, 14 for B, 30 for C, 60 for L, 90 for P) where the scope is a system class, and 14 days, the Tier B outer bound, for any other scope, counted from the day the verified criteria are met, as the data shows, whether or not the ledger owner has verified them yet; the verification step is itself due inside the intake target of the Tier that matches the scope's class, or Tier B for any other scope. Staging of scopes and contest procedure are governed by [**CI-6.275 §4.4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-44-enforcement-readiness-declarations) (*Enforcement-readiness declarations*).
  - **No narrowing:** once a scope is declared enforceable it stays enforceable. Loss of capacity afterward is an institutional failure handled under **Article XXVIII-C** (*Failure Off-Ramps, Re-Baselining, and Traceability*) and the forum-continuity rules, not a quiet withdrawal of the right.
- **Rights-Floor attainment path:** Each Adoption Transition plan must carry an attainment path covering every Rights-Floor domain in scope.
  - **Starting measure:** each domain's starting coverage and practical usability are measured and published before the first gate that depends on that domain. A domain not yet measured is **unmeasured**, which is treated as unattained and as a measurement priority — never as a pass.
  - **Fixed target:** the target is the full constitutional floor. A plan may not negotiate a lesser target, and re-baselining under **Article XXVIII-C** (*Failure Off-Ramps, Re-Baselining, and Traceability*) moves dates and owners, not the target or the starting measure.
  - **Dated glide path:** each domain has an accountable owner, committed resources, dated milestones, and a final date by which the floor is enforceable for the full scope, scaled to the affected class and material stake.
  - **What an open gap is not:** an open attainment gap within an approved path is not a defense to a breach of any restraint duty, and it does not suspend [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) (*Compliance, Violation, and Standing Model*) for such conduct.
