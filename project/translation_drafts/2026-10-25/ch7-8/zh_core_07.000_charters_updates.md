## Earlier update 12 — Class P applicability boundary

**Current English passage:**

The requirement applies from the day the Operating Party takes a [Materially Binding Act](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act) that others must follow or may rely on as settled, which crosses the line in the authority-over-others domain and makes it at least Class L.

**Proposed locale translation:**

## Earlier update 15 — charter response and resolution windows

**Current English passage:**

- **Response window:** The Chartered Party responds inside the response window, which is the intake target of the matching tier in **CF-13.445 §3.1** (*Stage targets and outer bounds*), counted from the request. A Chartered Party with no independent seat yet files the statement as a declaration with its evidence, as that section provides.

**Proposed locale translation:**

## Earlier update 18 — right to form and publication duty

**Current English passage:**

Anyone may form a new system, institution, or business. That right is set out in [Article XI-E](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*). This chapter adds the duty that comes next. Once what an Operating Party does is material to others in any one of the nine areas of [Domain-Specific Materiality](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality), it must say in public, ahead of time, what it claims authority to do ([§1 When System and Institutional Charters Are Required](#1-when-system-and-institutional-charters-are-required)). Adopting a System Charter or Institutional Charter must not be read to narrow that right, or any Rights Floor protection.

**Proposed locale translation:**

任何人都可以设立新的系统、机构或企业。[Article XI-E](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation)（*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*）规定了这项权利。本章增加了随后适用的义务：一旦 Operating Party 的行为在 [Domain-Specific Materiality](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) 九个领域中的任何一个对他人具有实质意义，就必须提前公开说明其声称拥有何种权限（[§1 When System and Institutional Charters Are Required](#1-when-system-and-institutional-charters-are-required)）。采纳 System Charter 或 Institutional Charter 不得被解释为缩小此项权利或 Rights Floor 保护。

## Earlier update 55 — certification review after mismatch

**Current English passage:**

- **Certification review:** A material mismatch also sets off a new forum review of that System's or Institution's certification. The review does not wait for the next scheduled one. Certification reads the stated scope and compares it with the real boundaries.
