## Updated passage 5 — class-scaled seat placement

**Current English passage:**

The separation-of-duties requirement applies at every class, and the four seats are how it is met. The class-scaled question is whether those seats may be merged or share a holder. For an institution or system operating within:

- Class C scope, full four-seat separation is always recommended;
- Class B scope, full four-seat separation should almost always be required; and
- Class A scope, full four-seat separation is always required.

## Updated passage 10 — routing cannot assign seats

**Current English passage:**

Wrong-seat routing should be built into the process wherever practicable. An intake, queue, workflow, or interface should send each step to its published seat from the start, so a step is not offered to a seat that may not take it. Automated routing:

- follows the placement published under [§5 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](#5-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution) and routes on seat, not on the merits of the act;
- logs each route as item 3 below requires;
- follows the published substitute or independent path under [§5.3 A seat the map leaves unplaced, or a holder who cannot serve](#53-a-seat-the-map-leaves-unplaced-or-a-holder-who-cannot-serve) when a holder cannot serve, and holds the matter and logs the gap when no seat, substitute, or independent path is placed, rather than sending it to the nearest, fastest, or most senior seat; and
- is not itself a seat-holder and cannot change who holds a seat; only the published placement does.

**Proposed locale translation:**

Jika memungkinkan, pengalihan ke kursi yang tepat harus tertanam dalam proses. Penerimaan, antrean, alur kerja, atau antarmuka harus mengirim setiap langkah sejak awal ke kursi yang dipublikasikan, sehingga langkah itu tidak ditawarkan kepada kursi yang tidak berwenang mengambilnya. Pengalihan otomatis:

- mengikuti penempatan yang dipublikasikan berdasarkan [§5 Penempatan yang Dipublikasikan, Kekosongan, dan Penggantian](#5-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution) serta mengarahkan berdasarkan kursi, bukan pokok tindakan;
- mencatat setiap rute sebagaimana diwajibkan butir 3 di bawah;
- ketika pemegang tidak dapat bertugas, mengikuti pengganti yang dipublikasikan atau jalur independen berdasarkan [§5.3 Kursi yang tidak ditempatkan dalam peta atau pemegang yang tidak dapat bertugas](#53-a-seat-the-map-leaves-unplaced-or-a-holder-who-cannot-serve); jika tidak ada kursi, pengganti, atau jalur independen yang ditetapkan, perkara ditahan dan kesenjangan dicatat, bukan dikirim ke kursi terdekat, tercepat, atau paling senior; dan
- bukan pemegang kursi dan tidak dapat mengubah siapa yang memegang kursi; hanya penempatan yang dipublikasikan yang menentukan hal itu.

## Updated passage 11 — steward response to wrong-seat requests

**Current English passage:**

Where routing is not automated, or fails, the duty below applies to the steward. A steward asked to take a step outside the steward's seat must:

1. decline that step without purporting to decide its merits;
2. name the seat that may take it and, where published, the holder or substitute;
3. log the request, the seat held, the gap or conflict, and the route used;
4. preserve evidence and the integrity of any record already received; and
5. route without avoidable delay.

**Proposed locale translation:**

Jika perutean tidak otomatis atau gagal, kewajiban berikut berlaku bagi steward. Steward yang diminta mengambil langkah di luar kursinya harus:

1. menolak langkah tersebut tanpa berpura-pura memutus pokok perkaranya;
2. menyebutkan kursi yang berwenang mengambil langkah itu dan, jika dipublikasikan, pemegang atau penggantinya;
3. mencatat permintaan, kursi yang dipegang, kesenjangan atau konflik, serta rute yang digunakan;
4. menjaga bukti dan integritas setiap catatan yang telah diterima; dan
5. meneruskan perkara tanpa penundaan yang dapat dihindari.

## Updated passage 13 — unplaced seats and unavailable holders

**Current English passage:**

An unplaced seat, or a holder who is absent, excluded, captured, or conflicted, is a governance defect to log and correct.

- The seat does not pass automatically to the initiating seat, an interested party, an operator, or a superior in their [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line).
- Until corrected, the act routes to the published substitute or independent path, however urgent the act.
- For a certification seat that the Charter Role Map leaves unplaced, the correction must meet the response and resolution windows, counted from the logged notice of the gap, as set out in [Chapter Seven §4.7.4 Certification seats](core_07.000_charters.md#474-certification-seats).

**Proposed locale translation:**

Kursi yang belum ditempatkan, atau pemegang yang tidak hadir, dikecualikan, dikuasai, atau berkonflik, merupakan cacat tata kelola yang harus dicatat dan diperbaiki.

- Kursi tersebut tidak otomatis beralih kepada kursi pemrakarsa, pihak berkepentingan, operator, atau atasan dalam [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line) mereka.

## Updated passage 14 — required Charter Role Map contents

**Current English passage:**

Every adopter of Class L or higher that takes materially binding acts must maintain a published, auditable, and contestable [Charter Role Map](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) that identifies:

- which lane, office, role, or process hosts each seat;
- the acts and scope for which that placement applies;
- every permitted merged-hosting arrangement and its independence safeguard;
- each deputy or substitute who may act in a seat, including for an absent, excluded, captured, or conflicted holder;
- the independent route when no qualified substitute is available; and
- its version and effective date, so that an act is judged against the map in force when it was taken.

**Proposed locale translation:**

Setiap adopter Kelas L atau lebih tinggi yang melakukan tindakan yang mengikat secara material harus memelihara [Charter Role Map](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) yang dipublikasikan, dapat diaudit, dan dapat digugat, yang mengidentifikasi:

- jalur, kantor, peran, atau proses yang menampung setiap kursi;

- setiap pengaturan penggabungan penempatan yang diizinkan beserta perlindungan independensinya;

## Updated passage 16 — delegation preserves seat boundaries

**Current English passage:**

A seat holder may delegate the seat only to a deputy or substitute that the [Charter Role Map](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) names. A delegation to anyone else leaves the seat unplaced, and [§5.3 A seat the map leaves unplaced, or a holder who cannot serve](#53-a-seat-the-map-leaves-unplaced-or-a-holder-who-cannot-serve) applies. Naming a new deputy or substitute changes the map ([Chapter Seven §4.5 Changing seat rules and seat holders](core_07.000_charters.md#45-changing-seat-rules-and-seat-holders)). A delegation cannot do it.

A delegation keeps the same seat boundary, evidence duties, record, and clock, and the delegate stays subject to the [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line). It does not create a new seat, merge seats, or allow a delegate to do what the delegating seat could not do. A delegate who holds a second seat on the same act is a merger, and only the merged-hosting arrangements the map lists under [§6 Proportional Scaling and Merged Hosting](#6-proportional-scaling-and-merged-hosting) allow one.

**Proposed locale translation:**

Pemegang kursi hanya boleh mendelegasikan kursinya kepada wakil atau pengganti yang disebut dalam [Charter Role Map](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map). Delegasi kepada pihak lain membuat kursi tidak ditempatkan, sehingga [§5.3 A seat the map leaves unplaced, or a holder who cannot serve](#53-a-seat-the-map-leaves-unplaced-or-a-holder-who-cannot-serve) berlaku. Penamaan wakil atau pengganti baru mengubah peta ([Chapter Seven §4.5 Changing seat rules and seat holders](core_07.000_charters.md#45-changing-seat-rules-and-seat-holders)); delegasi tidak dapat melakukannya.

Delegasi mempertahankan batas kursi, kewajiban bukti, catatan, dan tenggat yang sama, serta penerima delegasi tetap tunduk pada [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line). Delegasi tidak menciptakan kursi baru, menggabungkan kursi, atau mengizinkan penerima delegasi melakukan hal yang tidak dapat dilakukan kursi pemberi delegasi. Jika penerima delegasi memegang kursi kedua untuk tindakan yang sama, itu merupakan penggabungan; hanya pengaturan penempatan gabungan yang tercantum dalam peta di bawah [§6 Proportional Scaling and Merged Hosting](#6-proportional-scaling-and-merged-hosting) yang mengizinkannya.

## Updated passage 18 — outside seat-holder independence

**Current English passage:**

**Independent of the business:** The holder is [independent](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#independence) of the initiating business. It is outside the business's [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line), and nothing the act decides gives it a stake. ## Updated passage 21 — group acts require external review

**Current English passage:**

## Updated passage 23 — group admission limits and grounds

**Current English passage:**

**Admission limits:** A group may refuse or defer an applicant for a reason tied to what the group can serve, so that it is never forced to take on more than it can carry. Such reasons include:

- **Capacity:** the applicant's expected volume of material acts would use up capacity that existing members rely on. The group may cap the volume it takes from any one member or keep a waiting list;
- **Fit:** the applicant's kind of acts or class is outside what the group's seat holders are qualified and staffed to serve, such as work that needs more competency redundancy than the group has ([§6.6 Stake, class, and what does not justify a merger](#66-stake-class-and-what-does-not-justify-a-merger));
- **Independence:** the group's holders could not serve the applicant's acts independently, because the applicant is inside the [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line) of one of them; and
- **Conduct:** the applicant has broken the group's published terms before, or will not accept them.

**Proposed locale translation:**

**Batas penerimaan:** Kelompok boleh menolak atau menunda pemohon karena alasan yang terkait dengan kemampuan layanan kelompok, agar kelompok tidak dipaksa menanggung lebih dari kapasitasnya. Alasan tersebut mencakup:

- **Kapasitas:** volume tindakan yang mengikat secara material dan diperkirakan dari pemohon akan menghabiskan kapasitas yang diandalkan anggota saat ini. Kelompok boleh membatasi volume yang diterima dari anggota mana pun atau membuat daftar tunggu;
- **Kecocokan:** jenis atau kelas tindakan pemohon berada di luar kualifikasi dan kapasitas staf pemegang kursi kelompok, misalnya pekerjaan yang membutuhkan redundansi kompetensi melebihi kemampuan kelompok ([§6.6 Stake, class, and what does not justify a merger](#66-stake-class-and-what-does-not-justify-a-merger));
- **Independensi:** pemegang kursi kelompok tidak dapat menangani tindakan pemohon secara independen karena pemohon berada dalam [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line) salah satu dari mereka; dan

## Updated passage 36 — stake and merger presumption

**Current English passage:**

The higher the stakes, the stronger the starting expectation that each seat sits in its own office and that each job has a qualified backup, meaning more than one sentient able to do it (competency redundancy). Sharing a seat through a group under [§6.3 Seats held in common](#63-seats-held-in-common) does not lower that expectation.

Being short of staff, in a hurry, or wanting convenience is not enough to justify putting two seats in one office. Neither is having one expert who knows more than anyone else. The class rules in [§2 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](#2-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) apply:

- **Class A:** the four seats are never merged.
- **Class B or Class C:** a merger is allowed only if it meets every condition in this section.

The more harm that could follow and the more others depend on the work, the harder a merger is to justify, and the more the work needs separate offices, backup holders for each job, and independent stand-ins.

**Proposed locale translation:**

Semakin tinggi taruhannya, semakin kuat ekspektasi awal bahwa setiap kursi berada di kantor tersendiri dan setiap tugas memiliki cadangan berkualifikasi, yakni lebih dari satu sentien mampu melakukannya (redundansi kompetensi). Berbagi kursi melalui kelompok berdasarkan [§6.3 Seats held in common](#63-seats-held-in-common) tidak mengurangi ekspektasi tersebut.

Kekurangan staf, keadaan terburu-buru, atau keinginan akan kemudahan tidak cukup untuk membenarkan dua kursi ditempatkan di satu kantor. Memiliki satu ahli yang lebih tahu daripada siapa pun juga tidak cukup. Aturan kelas dalam [§2 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](#2-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) berlaku:

- **Kelas A:** keempat kursi tidak pernah digabungkan.
- **Kelas B atau Kelas C:** penggabungan hanya diizinkan jika memenuhi setiap syarat dalam bagian ini.

Semakin besar potensi bahaya dan ketergantungan pihak lain pada pekerjaan tersebut, semakin sulit penggabungan dibenarkan dan semakin besar kebutuhan akan kantor terpisah, pemegang cadangan untuk setiap tugas, dan pengganti independen.

## Updated passage 44 — later processes and seat allocation

- A separate duplicate record is not required when the official record path remains complete and traceable.
- Giving a process a different name does not exempt it from [§4 Wrong-Seat Routing](#4-wrong-seat-routing) or [§8 Act Records and Attributable Handoffs](#8-act-records-and-attributable-handoffs), including their handoff and wrong-seat rules.

## Updated passage 48 — materially binding acts within scope

**Current English passage:**

This chapter applies to every [Materially Binding Act](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), as Chapter Five defines it, including:

- a decision;
- an authorization or certification;
- a finding;
- a record entry or material version change;
- a release, deployment, continuation, or withdrawal;
- a disbursement or allocation; and
- a disposition of a challenge.

**Proposed locale translation:**

### Cakupan tindakan yang mengikat secara material

Bab ini berlaku untuk setiap [tindakan yang mengikat secara material](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), sebagaimana didefinisikan Bab Lima, termasuk:

- keputusan;
- otorisasi atau sertifikasi;
- temuan;
- entri catatan atau perubahan versi yang material;
- peluncuran, penerapan, kelanjutan, atau penarikan;
- pencairan atau alokasi; dan
- penyelesaian suatu gugatan.

## Updated passage 49 — seat roles are act-specific, not job titles

**Current English passage:**

The seats in this chapter define who may do what for a particular act; they are not job titles. The same sentient, office, or system may hold different seats for different acts. A title, delegation, technical capability, or ability to perform a step does not enlarge the seat held for that act.

**Proposed locale translation:**

### Kursi ditetapkan untuk tiap tindakan

Kursi dalam bab ini menentukan siapa boleh melakukan apa untuk tindakan tertentu; kursi bukanlah jabatan kerja. Sentient, kantor, atau sistem yang sama dapat memegang kursi berbeda untuk tindakan berbeda. Gelar, delegasi, kemampuan teknis, atau kemampuan melakukan suatu langkah tidak memperluas kursi yang dipegang untuk tindakan tersebut.
