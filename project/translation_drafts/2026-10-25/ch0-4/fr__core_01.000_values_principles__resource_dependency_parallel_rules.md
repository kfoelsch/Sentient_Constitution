# Chapter One §11.4 — Parallel Application of Resource Rights Floors

## English source passage

Where the same arrangement also falls short of [Article V-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-a-dependency-mapping-and-resource-flow-transparency) (*Dependency Mapping and Resource-Flow Transparency*) or [Article V-B](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-b-cross-system-fairness-and-sustainability) (*Cross-System Fairness and Sustainability*), both apply: concentration scrutiny is added on top of **Article V** (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*) and does not replace it.


## Draft translation (fr)

Si le même arrangement ne respecte pas non plus l’[Article V-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-a-dependency-mapping-and-resource-flow-transparency) (*Cartographie des dépendances et transparence des flux de ressources*) ou l’[Article V-B](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-b-cross-system-fairness-and-sustainability) (*Équité et durabilité entre systèmes*), les deux s’appliquent : l’examen de la concentration s’ajoute à l’[Article V](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-resource-allocation-dependencies-and-ecosystem-funding) (*Allocation des ressources, dépendances et financement de l’écosystème*) sans le remplacer.
