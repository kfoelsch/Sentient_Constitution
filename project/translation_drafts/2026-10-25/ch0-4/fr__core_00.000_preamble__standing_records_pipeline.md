- **Standing records** ([Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement)) — when conduct or harm matters constitutionally, place verified facts in formal contribution or violation case files; rumors and reputation are not enough.
