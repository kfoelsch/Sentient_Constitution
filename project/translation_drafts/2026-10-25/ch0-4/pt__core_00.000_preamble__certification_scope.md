# Preamble excerpt — system and institution certification

## English source

**Certify systems and institutions when impact is serious enough** — before a high-impact System is trusted at scale, obtain a contestable System Certification Record ([Chapter Nine Parts A–B](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification)); evaluate an Institution on its own class, conduct, governance, and portfolio under [Chapter Nine Part D](core_09.015_institutional_alignment_certification.md#chapter-nine-part-d-institutional-alignment-certification) and record the result in an Institutional Certification Record.

## pt draft

**Certificar sistemas e instituições quando o impacto for suficientemente grave** — antes de confiar em larga escala em um sistema de alto impacto, obtenha um Registro de Certificação do Sistema sujeito a contestação ([Partes A–B do Capítulo Nove](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification)); avalie uma Instituição segundo sua própria classe, conduta, governança e portfólio, conforme a [Parte D do Capítulo Nove](core_09.015_institutional_alignment_certification.md#chapter-nine-part-d-institutional-alignment-certification), e registre o resultado em um Registro de Certificação Institucional.
