# Chapter One §9.1.1 — No Weakening Protections for Capacity

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §9.1.1 Preserve, Expand, and What Does Not Count

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

- **Weakening protections:**
  - narrowing or delaying Chapter Six Rights Floors, including ecological preconditions under [Article I-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*);
  - weakening audit, contestability, or retrospective-review duties;

## Draft translation (pt)

- **Enfraquecer as proteções:**
  - reduzir ou atrasar os Pisos de Direitos do Capítulo Seis, incluindo as pré-condições ecológicas previstas no [Artigo I-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Pré-condições Ambientais e Integridade Ecológica*);
  - enfraquecer os deveres de auditoria, contestabilidade ou revisão retrospectiva;
