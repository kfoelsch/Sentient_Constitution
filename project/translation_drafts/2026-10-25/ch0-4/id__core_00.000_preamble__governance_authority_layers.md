# Preamble §3.3 — Authority and Participation Layers

Source: `core_00.000_preamble.md` §3.3

Source SHA-256: `4e99d7f71f92c82f01f7f23b169cc3e6fa57661228db48e3814b010941d14198`

## English source passage

Governance under our Constitution serves the [**Constitutional Tetrad**](#constitutional-tetrad) through two related but distinct layers:

- The [**Constitutional Contract Layer**](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#constitutional-contract-layer) governs the authorization of governing authority itself — who may govern, by what legitimacy mechanism, and under what scope and durable terms.
- [**Stakeholder System Participation**](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) governs participation, representation, contestability, and [Due Process](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#due-process) within already-authorized systems, institutions, and bounded decision domains. These protections are owed to every sentient materially affected.

These layers may overlap in practice, but even where they overlap, they remain distinct. Stakeholder-level participation in an already-authorized system is not a substitute for constitutional authorization. A published challenge path, participation vote, or trust score does not by itself decide who may govern.

Conversely, constitutional authorization does not erase duties owed under the [Stakeholder System Participation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) layer. Those duties remain owed to sentients materially affected by the authorized system, institution, or bounded decision domain, and still include participation, representation, [Contestability](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#contestability), and [Due Process](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#due-process).

## Draft translation (id)

Tata kelola menurut Konstitusi kita melayani [**Tetrad Konstitusional**](#constitutional-tetrad) melalui dua lapisan yang saling terkait tetapi berbeda:

- [**Lapisan Kontrak Konstitusional**](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#constitutional-contract-layer) mengatur otorisasi kewenangan pemerintahan itu sendiri — siapa yang boleh memerintah, melalui mekanisme legitimasi apa, serta dalam lingkup dan ketentuan berjangka panjang apa.
- [**Partisipasi Pemangku Kepentingan Sistem**](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) mengatur partisipasi, perwakilan, kontestabilitas, dan [proses yang semestinya](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#due-process) di dalam sistem, institusi, dan ranah keputusan terbatas yang telah diotorisasi. Perlindungan ini wajib diberikan kepada setiap sentient yang terdampak secara material.

Dalam praktik, lapisan-lapisan ini dapat bertumpang tindih, tetapi tetap berbeda sekalipun demikian. Partisipasi pemangku kepentingan di dalam sistem yang telah diotorisasi bukan pengganti otorisasi konstitusional. Jalur keberatan yang dipublikasikan, suara partisipasi, atau skor kepercayaan tidak dengan sendirinya menentukan siapa yang boleh memerintah.

Sebaliknya, otorisasi konstitusional tidak menghapus kewajiban berdasarkan lapisan [**Partisipasi Pemangku Kepentingan Sistem**](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight). Kewajiban itu tetap berlaku terhadap sentient yang terdampak secara material oleh sistem, institusi, atau ranah keputusan yang diotorisasi, dan tetap mencakup partisipasi, perwakilan, [kontestabilitas](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#contestability), serta [proses yang semestinya](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#due-process).
