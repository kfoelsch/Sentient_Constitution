# Preamble excerpt — Chapter Eight’s cross-process requirement

## English source

**Chapter Eight — Functional independence and separation of duties**

- **What it owns:** The cross-process requirement that every [Materially Binding Act](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act) assign four functions separately:
  - initiation;
  - verification or authorization;
  - official record custody; and
  - review of challenges.

- **याची जबाबदारी:** प्रत्येक [भौतिकदृष्ट्या बंधनकारक कृती](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act)मध्ये चार कार्ये स्वतंत्रपणे नेमण्याची सर्व प्रक्रियांना लागू होणारी अट:

  - आव्हानांचे पुनरावलोकन.
