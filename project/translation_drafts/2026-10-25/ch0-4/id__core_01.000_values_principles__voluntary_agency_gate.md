# Chapter One §7.4 — Agency and Consent Gate

## English source passage

A high-stakes life-direction matter may not be treated as voluntary through formal assent where substantive agency, consent, or anti-coercion conditions fail. Consent here presupposes [Meaningful Agency](core_05.055_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency); it does not replace it.

## Draft translation (id)

Perkara arah hidup berisiko tinggi tidak boleh dianggap sukarela hanya berdasarkan persetujuan formal ketika syarat agensi substantif, persetujuan, atau anti-pemaksaan tidak terpenuhi. Persetujuan di sini mensyaratkan [Agensi Bermakna](core_05.055_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency); persetujuan tidak menggantikannya.
