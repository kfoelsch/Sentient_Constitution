## English source passage

**Which rights articles still control.** **§7.2** (*Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation*) is a Chapter One principle. It does not replace the Chapter Six Rights Floor. Inside **§7.2** (*Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation*), those articles still decide what the right is and how it may be limited:
- **[Article XI-D](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-d-assembly-dissent-and-peaceful-protest) (*Assembly, Dissent, and Peaceful Protest*)** — gathering, associating, and acting together in physical, digital, or shared-compute spaces for expression, politics, culture, community, and similar purposes
- **[Article III-C](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-c-labor-and-economic-floor) (*Labor and Economic Floor*)** — collective organization in productive and economic activity (unions, cooperatives, guilds, worker councils, and comparable forms used to shape the terms of work)
- **[Article XI-E](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*)** — [System Creation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#system-creation) (forming and running non-commercial institutions) and [Business Creation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#business-creation) (forming and running commercial enterprises)
- **[Article X-C](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-x-c-stakeholder-role-and-participation-rights) (*Stakeholder Role and Participation Rights*)** and **[Article XII](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xii-stakeholder-system-participation-representation-and-due-process) (*Stakeholder System Participation, Representation, and Due Process*)** — stakeholder rights once a created institution or enterprise materially affects others

## Draft translation (id)

**Pasal hak mana yang tetap mengatur.** **§7.2** (*Berkumpul, Organisasi Kolektif, dan Pembentukan Institusi*) adalah prinsip Bab Satu. Bagian ini tidak menggantikan Lantai Hak Bab Enam. Di dalam **§7.2**, pasal-pasal berikut tetap menentukan apa haknya dan bagaimana hak itu dapat dibatasi:
- **[Pasal XI-D](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-d-assembly-dissent-and-peaceful-protest) (*Berkumpul, Perbedaan Pendapat, dan Protes Damai*)** — berkumpul, berasosiasi, dan bertindak bersama di ruang fisik, digital, atau komputasi bersama untuk tujuan ekspresi, politik, budaya, komunitas, dan tujuan serupa.
- **[Pasal III-C](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-c-labor-and-economic-floor) (*Lantai Ketenagakerjaan dan Ekonomi*)** — organisasi kolektif dalam kegiatan produktif dan ekonomi (serikat pekerja, koperasi, gilda, dewan pekerja, dan bentuk sebanding untuk membentuk syarat kerja).
- **[Pasal XI-E](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Pembentukan Institusi dan Penciptaan Usaha*)** — [Penciptaan Sistem](core_05.055_band_participation.md#system-creation) (membentuk dan menjalankan institusi nonkomersial) dan [Penciptaan Usaha](core_05.055_band_participation.md#business-creation) (membentuk dan menjalankan perusahaan komersial).
- **[Pasal X-C](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-x-c-stakeholder-role-and-participation-rights) (*Hak Peran dan Partisipasi Pemangku Kepentingan*)** dan **[Pasal XII](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-xii-stakeholder-system-participation-representation-and-due-process) (*Partisipasi, Representasi, dan Proses Hukum yang Patut dalam Sistem bagi Pemangku Kepentingan*)** — hak pemangku kepentingan ketika institusi atau usaha yang dibentuk berdampak material terhadap orang lain.
