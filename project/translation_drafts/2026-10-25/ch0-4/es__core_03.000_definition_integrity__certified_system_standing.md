# Chapter Three excerpt — standing effects for an already-certified system

## English source

If a system is already running under a [system alignment certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) — including certification, conditional certification, or a recertification that has not expired — material verified non-compliance on that system's functional scope **must** go into [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) standing measurement for **that system**. Only facts that pass the [verified-input gate](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing) may enter. Measure standing under [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement); apply its integration and effects under [Chapter Eleven](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). Reopening, withdrawal, or other certification consequences stay under [Chapter Nine](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). They do not replace that system's standing records.

## es draft

Si un sistema ya funciona bajo una [certificación de alineación del sistema](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) —incluidas la certificación condicional o una recertificación que no haya caducado—, el incumplimiento material verificado dentro del alcance funcional de ese sistema **debe** incorporarse a la medición de posición de [Capítulo Diez](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) para **ese sistema**. Solo pueden admitirse los hechos que superen la [puerta de datos verificados](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing). Mídase la posición conforme al [Capítulo Diez](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) y aplíquense su integración y efectos conforme al [Capítulo Once](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). La reapertura, retirada u otras consecuencias de la certificación corresponden al [Capítulo Nueve](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). No sustituyen los registros de posición de ese sistema.
