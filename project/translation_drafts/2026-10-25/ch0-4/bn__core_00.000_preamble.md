# Preamble §3.3 — Authority and Participation Layers

## English source passage

Governance under our Constitution serves the [**Constitutional Tetrad**](#constitutional-tetrad) through two related but distinct layers:

- The [**Constitutional Contract Layer**](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#constitutional-contract-layer) governs the authorization of governing authority itself — who may govern, by what legitimacy mechanism, and under what scope and durable terms.
- [**Stakeholder System Participation**](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) governs participation, representation, contestability, and [Due Process](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#due-process) within already-authorized systems, institutions, and bounded decision domains. These layers may overlap in practice, but even where they overlap, they remain distinct. Stakeholder-level participation in an already-authorized system is not a substitute for constitutional authorization. A published challenge path, participation vote, or trust score does not by itself decide who may govern.

Conversely, constitutional authorization does not erase duties owed under the [Stakeholder System Participation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) layer. Those duties remain owed to sentients materially affected by the authorized system, institution, or bounded decision domain, and still include participation, representation, [Contestability](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#contestability), and [Due Process](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#due-process).

# Preamble §1 — Model and Four Scaled Duties

Source: `core_00.000_preamble.md` §1 The Model

Source SHA-256: `4e99d7f71f92c82f01f7f23b169cc3e6fa57661228db48e3814b010941d14198`

## English source passage

*In plain terms: four duties — participation, oversight, accountability, and timeliness — scale with how much impact, dependence, and risk are on the line, and they serve two aims together: Flourishing and Continuity, inside a Rights Floor that does not move.*

Critical systems that support sentient life — whether biologically or materially — must be governed and operated under four duties, together called the [**Constitutional Tetrad**](#constitutional-tetrad), which set the general standard those systems must meet. Their strength depends on the [**material stake**](#material-stake): how much impact, dependence, and risk are involved. The higher the stake, the stronger the duties must be.

- **participation** — affected sentients get a real voice, fair representation, a fair chance to challenge decisions, and access to consequential roles in proportion to the stake
- **oversight** — responsible actors watch, check, verify, and keep records so problems can be found; independent reviewers can constrain bad choices
- **accountability** — responsibility traces to the right actors; those actors must answer for their choices; those harmed get redress; bad outcomes trigger real correction
- **timeliness** — problems must be detected, challenged, resolved, and fixed within time limits that match the stake. Delay is not legitimate governance when it effectively erases rights, remedies, or the chance of repair

দুটি লক্ষ্য একসঙ্গে অনুসরণ করতে হবে, সর্বদা এই সংবিধানের আপসহীন নীতিগত সীমা ও অধিকার-সীমার মধ্যে। সেই অনুসরণ কীভাবে বৈধ থাকবে তা [**সাংবিধানিক চতুষ্টয়**](#constitutional-tetrad) পরিচালনা করে; চারটি কর্তব্যই [**বস্তুগত স্বার্থের**](#material-stake) অনুপাতে বাড়ে বা কমে। চতুষ্টয়ের অঙ্গ ও লক্ষ্যগুলোর বাধ্যতামূলক সংজ্ঞা পঞ্চম অধ্যায়ে রয়েছে: [অংশগ্রহণ](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [তদারকি](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [জবাবদিহি](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [সময়ানুবর্তিতা](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional), [সমৃদ্ধি](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional), এবং [ধারাবাহিকতা](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim)।

## English source passage

*In plain terms: first name the problem and the stake. Then choose the matching measurement category and use shared technical standards — not convenience numbers — as evidence.*

When the Constitution requires concrete measurement rules, **Technical Forum Domains** under [Chapter Thirteen](core_14.000_forum.md#42-technical-forum-domains) develop and maintain shared standards for measurement, testing, and reliable evidence. The forum responsible for a dispute then applies those standards when deciding the case under [Chapter Thirteen §4.2 (*Technical Forum Domains*)](core_14.000_forum.md#42-shared-standards-and-anti-displacement).

Constitutional stewardship starts by naming the problem and the [**material stake**](#material-stake) — the impact, dependence, and risk involved. Next, choose the relevant [**measurement**](#2-measurements-overview) category and subcategory from the [overview](#2-measurements-overview). Apply the standards above to test real-world effects, not convenience metrics. Require traceable evidence.
