# Chapter One §9.1 — Productive Capacity Definition

## English source passage

**[Productive Capacity](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#productive-capacity)** is one aspect of **[Shared-System Capacity](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#shared-system-capacity)**. It names the lasting ability of sentients and shared systems to:
- support real participation, contribution, and skill-building; and
- turn time, attention, effort, coordination, materials, infrastructure, and energy into outcomes this Constitution actually requires.

It is an **instrumental good** — a means, not a trump value. Its job is to raise, sustain, and spread quality of life under the **Flourishing** aim, consistent with [Wellbeing](#3-foundational-objective-wellbeing-flourishing-aim), [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](core_05.055_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), the Chapter Six Rights Floor, and the ecological and intergenerational limits of the **Continuity** aim under the [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims).

## Draft translation (id)

**[Kapasitas Produktif](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#productive-capacity)** adalah salah satu aspek **[Kapasitas Sistem Bersama](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#shared-system-capacity)**. Istilah ini menamai kemampuan bertahan makhluk berkesadaran dan sistem bersama untuk:
- mendukung partisipasi, kontribusi, dan pengembangan keterampilan yang nyata; serta
- mengubah waktu, perhatian, upaya, koordinasi, bahan, infrastruktur, dan energi menjadi hasil yang benar-benar diwajibkan Konstitusi ini.

Ini adalah **kebaikan instrumental** — sarana, bukan nilai tertinggi. Tugasnya meningkatkan, mempertahankan, dan menyebarkan kualitas hidup di bawah tujuan **Kemajuan**, selaras dengan [Kesejahteraan](#3-foundational-objective-wellbeing-flourishing-aim), [Martabat dan Kedudukan Moral Setara](core_05.055_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), Lantai Hak Bab Enam, serta batas ekologis dan antargenerasi bagi tujuan **Kontinuitas** dalam [Dua Tujuan Konstitusional](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims).
