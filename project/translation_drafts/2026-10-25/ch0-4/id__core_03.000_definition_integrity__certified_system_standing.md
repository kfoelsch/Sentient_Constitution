# Chapter Three excerpt — standing effects for an already-certified system

## English source

If a system is already running under a [system alignment certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) — including certification, conditional certification, or a recertification that has not expired — material verified non-compliance on that system's functional scope **must** go into [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) standing measurement for **that system**. Only facts that pass the [verified-input gate](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing) may enter. Measure standing under [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement); apply its integration and effects under [Chapter Eleven](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). Reopening, withdrawal, or other certification consequences stay under [Chapter Nine](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). They do not replace that system's standing records.

Jika suatu sistem sudah beroperasi di bawah [sertifikasi keselarasan sistem](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) — termasuk sertifikasi bersyarat atau sertifikasi ulang yang belum kedaluwarsa — ketidakpatuhan material terverifikasi dalam lingkup fungsional sistem itu **harus** dimasukkan ke pengukuran standing menurut [Bab Sepuluh](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) untuk **sistem tersebut**. Hanya fakta yang lolos [gerbang masukan terverifikasi](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing) yang boleh masuk. Ukur standing menurut [Bab Sepuluh](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement); terapkan integrasi dan dampaknya menurut [Bab Sebelas](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). Pembukaan kembali, penarikan, atau konsekuensi sertifikasi lainnya tetap berada di bawah [Bab Sembilan](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). Hal-hal tersebut tidak menggantikan catatan standing sistem itu.
