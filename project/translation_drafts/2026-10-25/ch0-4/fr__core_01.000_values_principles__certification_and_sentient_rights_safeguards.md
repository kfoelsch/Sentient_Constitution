# §6 Trust — Complementary Certification and Rights Safeguards

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §6

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

Certification is one of two safeguards that work together: it makes a system worthy of trust, and the rights of sentients to [challenge it](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-a-reliability-and-trustworthiness-baseline) and [seek redress](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy) and to [have it audited](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xvi-audit-transparency-and-independent-verification) keep it honest. Neither replaces the other — [Article XIII](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-right-to-reliable-and-trustworthy-systems) (*Right to Reliable and Trustworthy Systems*) states how the two combine.

## Draft translation (fr)

La certification est l’une des deux garanties qui agissent de concert : elle rend le système digne de confiance, tandis que les droits des sentients à le [contester](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-a-reliability-and-trustworthiness-baseline), à [demander réparation](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy) et à le [faire auditer](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xvi-audit-transparency-and-independent-verification) le maintiennent honnête. Aucune ne remplace l’autre ; l’[Article XIII](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-right-to-reliable-and-trustworthy-systems) (*Droit à des systèmes fiables et dignes de confiance*) précise comment elles se combinent.
