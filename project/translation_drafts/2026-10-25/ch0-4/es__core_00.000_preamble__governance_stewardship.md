## English source

Durable legitimacy depends on [Safety](core_01.000_values_principles.md#4-safety-harm-constraint), [Truth](core_01.000_values_principles.md#5-truth-epistemic-integrity-constraint), and [Trust](core_01.000_values_principles.md#6-trust-and-trustworthiness-coordination-integrity). It also depends on proportionate recognition of lawful stewardship, truthful cooperation, and bounded aspiration — not solely on sanction and restraint. Chapter One states this recognition dimension in [Recognition, Reinforcement, and Aspiration](core_01.000_values_principles.md#32-recognition-reinforcement-and-aspiration).

## es draft

La legitimidad duradera depende de la [Seguridad](core_01.000_values_principles.md#4-safety-harm-constraint), la [Verdad](core_01.000_values_principles.md#5-truth-epistemic-integrity-constraint) y la [Confianza](core_01.000_values_principles.md#6-trust-and-trustworthiness-coordination-integrity). También requiere reconocer de manera proporcionada la custodia legítima, la cooperación veraz y la aspiración acotada, no depender solo de sanciones y restricciones. El Capítulo Uno expone esta dimensión del reconocimiento en [Reconocimiento, refuerzo y aspiración](core_01.000_values_principles.md#32-recognition-reinforcement-and-aspiration).
