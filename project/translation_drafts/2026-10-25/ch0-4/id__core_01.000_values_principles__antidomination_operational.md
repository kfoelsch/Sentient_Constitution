# Chapter One §11.2.2 — Durable-Power Prohibition

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §11.2.2 Anti-Domination Prohibitions (Don'ts)

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

None of the following:
- sentient(s);
- institution(s);
- platform(s);
- enterprise(s);
- state organ(s);
- steward(s);
- coordinated group(s)

may:
- create;
- maintain;
- acquire;
- exploit;
- conceal;
- restructure around

durable power of any of the following kinds:
- market;
- platform;
- infrastructure;
- labor;
- supplier;
- data;
- compute;
- credentialing;
- capital-access;
- resource-control.

where that power foreseeably degrades:
- wellbeing;
- meaningful agency;
- fair compensation;
- innovation;
- access;
- ecological integrity;
- contestability;
- constitutional review.

- institusi;
- platform;
- perusahaan;
- organ negara;

- menciptakan;
- mempertahankan;
- memperoleh;
- mengeksploitasi;
- menyembunyikan;
- menyusun ulang di sekeliling

kekuasaan yang bertahan lama dalam jenis apa pun berikut:
- pasar;
- platform;
- infrastruktur;
- tenaga kerja;
- pemasok;
- data;
- komputasi;
- kredensial;
- akses modal;
- kendali sumber daya.

jika kekuasaan itu dapat diperkirakan akan merusak:

- kompensasi yang adil;
- inovasi;
- akses;
- integritas ekologis;
