# Chapter One excerpt — Continuity principle hierarchy

## English source

**Principle hierarchy (Continuity).** At principle layer, the Continuity aim is developed in this order, opening with the capacity that bridges the two aims:

6. **[Shared-System Capacity](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#shared-system-capacity)** is what good stewardship, governance, and incentives should add up to over time — real, challengeable ability for sentients and shared systems to get constitutionally required work done. It straddles the two aims: it is a means toward **Flourishing** and the substance of **Continuity**, and it is not a trump card over everything else. **[§9.1](#91-productive-capacity-instrumental-good)** and **[§9.2](#92-constitutional-efficiency)** explain its two main aspects.
7. **[Resilience and Self-Healing Design](#10-resilience-and-self-healing-design)** states how systems that sentients depend on detect trouble early, contain it, fail along disclosed paths, and recover honestly (**[Self-Healing](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#self-healing)**), so the capacity from item 6 stays durable and stability does not depend on emergency intervention.
8. **[Market Structure](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#market-structure)** at [§11](#11-market-structure) supplies the anti-concentration discipline that keeps that capacity contestable in practice.
9. **[Systemic Evaluation Requirement](#12-systemic-evaluation-requirement)** verifies whole-system scope, dependency, and incentive alignment before compliance or governance claims stand — under the **oversight** Tetrad leg as principle-layer orientation for auditing, including [System Alignment Certification](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification) as one especially large audit process among others.

**Hierarki prinsip (Continuity).** Pada tingkat prinsip, arah Continuity dikembangkan dengan urutan berikut, dimulai dari kapasitas yang menjembatani kedua arah:

6. **[Kapasitas Sistem Bersama](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#shared-system-capacity)** adalah hasil yang seharusnya terbentuk dari penatalayanan, tata kelola, dan insentif yang baik dari waktu ke waktu: kemampuan nyata dan dapat digugat bagi para sentien serta sistem bersama untuk melaksanakan pekerjaan yang diwajibkan konstitusi. Kapasitas ini menjembatani kedua arah: sarana menuju Flourishing dan substansi Continuity, tetapi bukan kartu truf atas segalanya. Dua aspeknya dijelaskan dalam [§9.1](#91-productive-capacity-instrumental-good) dan [§9.2](#92-constitutional-efficiency).
7. **[Desain Ketahanan dan Pemulihan Mandiri](#10-resilience-and-self-healing-design)** menjelaskan bagaimana sistem yang diandalkan sentien mendeteksi masalah lebih awal, membatasinya, gagal melalui jalur yang diungkapkan, dan pulih secara jujur (**[Pemulihan Mandiri](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#self-healing)**), agar kapasitas butir 6 bertahan dan stabilitas tidak bergantung pada intervensi darurat.
8. **[Struktur Pasar](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#market-structure)** pada [§11](#11-market-structure) menyediakan disiplin antikonsentrasi agar kapasitas itu tetap dapat digugat dalam praktik.
9. **[Persyaratan Evaluasi Sistemik](#12-systemic-evaluation-requirement)** memeriksa ruang lingkup seluruh sistem, ketergantungan, dan keselarasan insentif sebelum klaim kepatuhan atau tata kelola diterima—sebagai orientasi prinsip untuk audit di bawah kaki **pengawasan** Tetrad, termasuk [Sertifikasi Keselarasan Sistem](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification) sebagai salah satu proses audit besar di antara proses lainnya.
