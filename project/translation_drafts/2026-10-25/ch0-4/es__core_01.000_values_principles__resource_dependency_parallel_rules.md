# Chapter One §11.4 — Parallel Application of Resource Rights Floors

## English source passage

Where the same arrangement also falls short of [Article V-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-a-dependency-mapping-and-resource-flow-transparency) (*Dependency Mapping and Resource-Flow Transparency*) or [Article V-B](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-b-cross-system-fairness-and-sustainability) (*Cross-System Fairness and Sustainability*), both apply: concentration scrutiny is added on top of **Article V** (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*) and does not replace it.


## Draft translation (es)

Si el mismo arreglo también incumple el [Artículo V-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-a-dependency-mapping-and-resource-flow-transparency) (*Mapeo de dependencias y transparencia de los flujos de recursos*) o el [Artículo V-B](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-b-cross-system-fairness-and-sustainability) (*Equidad y sostenibilidad entre sistemas*), se aplican ambos: el examen de concentración se añade al [Artículo V](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-resource-allocation-dependencies-and-ecosystem-funding) (*Asignación de recursos, dependencias y financiación del ecosistema*), no lo sustituye.
