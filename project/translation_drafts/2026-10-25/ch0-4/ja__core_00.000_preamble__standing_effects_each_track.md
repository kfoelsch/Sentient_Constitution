# Preamble excerpt — apply standing effects on each track

## English source passage

4. **Apply standing effects on each track** ([Chapter Eleven](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration)) — verified contribution may grant competency clearance and must support proportionate recognition and material rewards; verified violation may trigger standing locks, correction, and [remedy for those harmed](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#41-remedy-and-correction).
