## English source passage

- **In the open:** the levels, and the reasons for them, must be written down and open to challenge, in line with [Article V](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-resource-allocation-dependencies-and-ecosystem-funding) (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*), which requires resource flows to be visible, checkable, and open to challenge.
