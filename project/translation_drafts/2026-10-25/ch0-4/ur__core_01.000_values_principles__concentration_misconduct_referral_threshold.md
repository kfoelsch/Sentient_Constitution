# Chapter One excerpt — concentration-breach referral threshold

## English source passage

- **Creating, maintaining, or exploiting concentration that breaches the requirements:** this can go to Chapter Twelve for misconduct designation review, but only in two steps. First, [Chapter Ten §4 Question 2 — how good or bad was it?](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#4-question-2--how-good-or-bad-was-it) must already have scored the verified harm among the three most serious levels. Then Chapter Twelve checks whether the concentration meets criterion 3 (a workaround that rolls back protections), criterion 4 (damage to Rights Floor protections or blocked challenges), or criterion 6 (constitutional process made unusable in practice).
