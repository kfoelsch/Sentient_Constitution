# Chapter One Part B excerpt — Rights-Floor and temporary-restriction safeguards

## English source passage

- No constitutional process, measure, Adoption Transition, amendment, standing consequence, emergency action, or justice-related outcome may permanently extinguish or waive the **Rights-Floor minimums** of baseline dignity protections under [Article VI-A](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-a-dignity-and-equal-moral-standing) (*Dignity and Equal Moral Standing*) and the [Anti-Degrading-Process Principle](#anti-degrading-process-principle), minimum subsistence access, or core challenge, review, and appeal rights.
- Temporary restriction of specific rights is permitted only when it satisfies the [Least-Restrictive, Time-Bounded, and Reviewable Constraint Principle](#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle) and the emergency rules in [Chapter Thirteen §6.1](core_14.000_forum.md#61-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) (*Emergency measures and continuation burden*) — meaning any restriction must be justified, minimal, documented, time-limited, and independently reviewable. Specific articles may add stronger safeguards, but they may not narrow this principle or use convenience, efficiency, classification, emergency, Adoption Transition, standing, amendment, contract, or implementation labels to bypass it.

## Draft translation (id)

- Tidak ada proses konstitusional, tindakan, Transisi Adopsi, amendemen, konsekuensi status, tindakan darurat, atau hasil terkait keadilan yang boleh menghapus secara permanen atau melepaskan **minimum Lantai Hak** dari perlindungan martabat dasar berdasarkan [Artikel VI-A](core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-a-dignity-and-equal-moral-standing) (*Martabat dan Kedudukan Moral yang Setara*) dan [Prinsip Anti-Proses yang Merendahkan](#anti-degrading-process-principle), akses minimum untuk kebutuhan hidup, atau hak inti untuk menggugat, meninjau, dan mengajukan banding.
- Pembatasan sementara atas hak tertentu hanya diperbolehkan jika memenuhi [Prinsip Pembatasan Paling Tidak Membatasi, Berbatas Waktu, dan Dapat Ditinjau](#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle) serta aturan darurat dalam [Bab Tiga Belas §6.1](core_14.000_forum.md#61-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) (*Tindakan darurat dan beban untuk melanjutkannya*). Artinya, setiap pembatasan harus beralasan, minimal, terdokumentasi, dibatasi waktu, dan dapat ditinjau secara independen. Pasal tertentu boleh menambahkan perlindungan yang lebih kuat, tetapi tidak boleh mempersempit prinsip ini atau menghindarinya dengan label kemudahan, efisiensi, klasifikasi, keadaan darurat, Transisi Adopsi, status, amendemen, kontrak, atau implementasi.
