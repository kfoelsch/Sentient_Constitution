# Chapter One §9.2 — Efficiency Is Not a Proxy Goal

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §9.2 Constitutional Efficiency

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

Efficiency can drive broadly shared improvement, but only inside constitutional bounds. By itself, it is **not**:
- raw speed;
- administrative convenience;
- utilization targets;
- revenue growth;
- market share;
- headcount; or
- cost-cutting for its own sake.

## Draft translation (id)

Efisiensi dapat mendorong perbaikan yang manfaatnya dibagi luas, tetapi hanya dalam batas-batas konstitusional. Efisiensi saja **bukanlah**:
- kecepatan semata;
- kemudahan administratif;
- target pemanfaatan;
- pertumbuhan pendapatan;
- pangsa pasar;
- jumlah tenaga kerja; atau
- pemangkasan biaya demi pemangkasan itu sendiri.
