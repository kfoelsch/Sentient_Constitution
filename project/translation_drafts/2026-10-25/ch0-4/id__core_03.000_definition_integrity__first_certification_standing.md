# Chapter Three excerpt — first certification and standing inputs

## English source

If the system is still in its **first** [system alignment certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) and has not yet been certified — including where certification is deferred or refused — verified non-compliance primarily decides the **certification outcome** under Chapter Nine. That outcome may be conditional certification, deferred certification, non-certification, or a comparable result. Until certification is granted, those certification records supply no standing input under [Chapter Nine §10 Relationship to Standing](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#10-relationship-to-standing), which traces standing input only from certified systems. Once certification is granted, including conditional certification, [§3.1 Standing Effects for Already-Certified Systems](#31-standing-effects-for-already-certified-systems) applies. This chapter does not require the same standing records that an already-certified running system must receive.

Jika sistem masih berada pada [sertifikasi keselarasan sistem](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) **pertama** dan belum disertifikasi — termasuk ketika sertifikasi ditunda atau ditolak — ketidakpatuhan terverifikasi terutama menentukan **hasil sertifikasi** menurut Bab Sembilan. Hasilnya dapat berupa sertifikasi bersyarat, sertifikasi tertunda, tidak disertifikasi, atau hasil sejenis. Sebelum sertifikasi diberikan, catatan sertifikasi tersebut tidak memberikan masukan standing menurut [Bab Sembilan §10 Hubungan dengan Standing](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#10-relationship-to-standing), yang hanya menelusuri masukan standing dari sistem tersertifikasi. Setelah sertifikasi diberikan, termasuk sertifikasi bersyarat, [§3.1 Dampak Standing bagi Sistem yang Sudah Disertifikasi](#31-standing-effects-for-already-certified-systems) berlaku. Bab ini tidak mewajibkan catatan standing yang sama bagi sistem berjalan yang belum tersertifikasi seperti yang diwajibkan bagi sistem berjalan yang sudah tersertifikasi.
