# Chapter One §10 — Self-Healing Legitimacy and Limits

## English source passage

This is what Chapter Five calls [**Self-Healing**](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#self-healing) — and it is only legitimate when it makes a system more honest about its own condition, not less. Automatic recovery that masks the root cause, suppresses evidence, or substitutes for governance is not self-healing. It is a [Truth](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) violation and an [Incentive Alignment](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment) defect.

अध्याय पाच याला [**स्व-उपचार**](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#self-healing) म्हणतो — आणि प्रणालीला तिच्या स्वतःच्या स्थितीबद्दल अधिक प्रामाणिक बनवते तेव्हाच ते वैध असते. मूळ कारण लपवणारी, पुरावे दडपणारी किंवा प्रशासनाची जागा घेणारी स्वयंचलित पुनर्प्राप्ती ही स्व-उपचार नाही. तो [सत्याचा](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) भंग आणि [प्रोत्साहन-संरेखनातील](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment) दोष आहे.
