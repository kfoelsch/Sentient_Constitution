# Preamble §1 — Two Aims and Binding Definitions

## English source passage

Those four duties serve the [**Two Constitutional Aims**](#two-constitutional-aims) — what shared systems must optimize for:

- <a id="flourishing"></a>**Flourishing** — sentient wellbeing sustained through truth, safety, trustworthiness, and meaningful agency
- <a id="continuity"></a>**Continuity** — long-horizon stability, sustainability, resilience, and ecological wellbeing

Those aims must be pursued together, always within the Constitution's non-negotiable principle constraints and Rights Floor. The [**Constitutional Tetrad**](#constitutional-tetrad) governs how that pursuit remains legitimate, with all four duties scaled to [**material stake**](#material-stake). Binding definitions for the Tetrad legs and aims live in Chapter Five: [Participation](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [Oversight](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [Accountability](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [Timeliness](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional), [Flourishing](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional), and [Continuity](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim).

## Draft translation (id)

Keempat kewajiban itu melayani [**Dua Tujuan Konstitusional**](#two-constitutional-aims)—hal yang harus dioptimalkan oleh sistem bersama:

- <a id="flourishing"></a>**Flourishing** — kesejahteraan sentien yang dipertahankan melalui kebenaran, keselamatan, kelayakan untuk dipercaya, dan agensi yang bermakna.
- <a id="continuity"></a>**Continuity** — kestabilan jangka panjang, keberlanjutan, ketangguhan, dan kesejahteraan ekologis.

Kedua tujuan harus diupayakan bersama, selalu dalam batasan prinsip yang tidak dapat ditawar dan Batas Hak Konstitusi ini. [**Tetrad Konstitusional**](#constitutional-tetrad) mengatur agar upaya itu tetap sah, dengan keempat kewajiban disesuaikan menurut [**taruhan material**](#material-stake). Definisi yang mengikat bagi unsur Tetrad dan tujuan-tujuan tersebut tercantum di Bab Lima: [Partisipasi](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [Pengawasan](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [Akuntabilitas](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [Ketepatan Waktu](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional), [Flourishing](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional), dan [Continuity](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim).
