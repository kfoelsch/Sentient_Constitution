## English source passage

**Where the full rules live.** Chapter Five groups related definitions that must be read together when the questions they cover travel together. That grouping is a **definition cluster**. It is not a separate right, and it is not a substitute for the Chapter Six articles below. The definitions for this topic live in [Chapter Five — Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#assembly-collective-organization-and-institutional-formation):
- [Assembly](core_05.055_band_participation.md#assembly)
- [Collective Organization](core_05.055_band_participation.md#collective-organization)
- [System Creation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#system-creation)
- [Business Creation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#business-creation)

The cluster's own reading rules live there. **§7.2** (*Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation*) applies the [Anti-Segmentation Principle](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#1511-anti-segmentation-principle) to this topic; it does not restate those Chapter Five mechanics.

## Draft translation (id)

**Di mana aturan lengkapnya.** Bab Lima mengelompokkan definisi terkait yang harus dibaca bersama ketika pertanyaan yang dicakupnya muncul secara bersamaan. Pengelompokan itu adalah **klaster definisi**. Klaster ini bukan hak yang terpisah dan bukan pengganti Pasal-Pasal Bab Enam di bawah. Definisi untuk topik ini terdapat dalam [Bab Lima — Berkumpul, Organisasi Kolektif, dan Pembentukan Institusi](core_05.055_band_participation.md#assembly-collective-organization-and-institutional-formation):
- [Berkumpul](core_05.055_band_participation.md#assembly)
- [Organisasi Kolektif](core_05.055_band_participation.md#collective-organization)
- [Penciptaan Sistem](core_05.055_band_participation.md#system-creation)
- [Penciptaan Usaha](core_05.055_band_participation.md#business-creation)

Aturan untuk membaca klaster itu sendiri juga terdapat di sana. **§7.2** (*Berkumpul, Organisasi Kolektif, dan Pembentukan Institusi*) menerapkan [Prinsip Anti-Segmentasi](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#1511-anti-segmentation-principle) pada topik ini; bagian tersebut tidak mengulang mekanisme Bab Lima.
