# Chapter One §10 — Resilience Design Requirements

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §10 Resilience and Self-Healing Design

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

Systems that sentients depend on should be built to:
- detect problems early;
- contain them before they spread;
- fail along paths that were planned and disclosed, not hidden ones;
- recover in ways consistent with [Reversibility](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#reversibility) and the Chapter Six Rights Floor.

## Draft translation (id)

Sistem yang menjadi sandaran makhluk berakal harus dirancang untuk:
- mendeteksi masalah sejak dini;
- membatasinya sebelum menyebar;
- mengalami kegagalan melalui jalur yang direncanakan dan diungkapkan, bukan jalur tersembunyi;
- pulih dengan cara yang selaras dengan [reversibilitas](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#reversibility) dan Batas Minimum Hak Bab Enam.
