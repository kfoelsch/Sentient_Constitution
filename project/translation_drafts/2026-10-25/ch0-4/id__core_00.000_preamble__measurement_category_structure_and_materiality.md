## English source passage

The overview below lists the measurement **categories**, mapping each to the [Two Constitutional Aims](#two-constitutional-aims) and the [Constitutional Tetrad](#constitutional-tetrad). Each category poses one plain-language question and names its **subcategories**, and links to its **Chapter Five measurement-family home**, where the family table, constitutional use, and definition routing live. Each subcategory links to its canonical Chapter Five definition.

**Materiality** is not a separate measurement category. It sets how strongly every category applies, based on [**material stake**](#material-stake) — how much impact, dependence, and risk are involved. **Constitutional performance** is cross-cutting and instrumental to both aims. Use the [Chapter Five measurement crosswalk](core_05.000_definitions_home.md#chapter-five-measurement-crosswalk) to trace each category to its canonical home.

## Draft translation (id)

Ikhtisar di bawah mencantumkan **kategori** pengukuran dan memetakan masing-masing ke [Dua Tujuan Konstitusional](#two-constitutional-aims) serta [Tetrad Konstitusional](#constitutional-tetrad). Setiap kategori mengajukan satu pertanyaan dalam bahasa sederhana, menyebutkan **subkategori**, dan menautkan ke **rumah keluarga pengukuran di Bab Lima**, tempat tabel keluarga, penggunaan konstitusional, dan rute definisi berada. Setiap subkategori menautkan ke definisi kanonisnya di Bab Lima.

**Materialitas** bukan kategori pengukuran tersendiri. Materialitas menentukan seberapa kuat setiap kategori berlaku berdasarkan [**taruhan material**](#material-stake)—besarnya dampak, ketergantungan, dan risiko. **Kinerja konstitusional** berlaku lintas kategori dan menjadi sarana bagi kedua tujuan. Gunakan [peta silang pengukuran Bab Lima](core_05.000_definitions_home.md#chapter-five-measurement-crosswalk) untuk menelusuri setiap kategori ke rumah kanonisnya.
