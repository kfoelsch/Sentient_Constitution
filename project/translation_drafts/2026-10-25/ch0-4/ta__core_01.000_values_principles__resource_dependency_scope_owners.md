# Chapter One §11.4 — Resource-Concentration Scope and Ownership

## English source passage

Concentration is easy to miss in shared infrastructure. A few systems can come to control how resources are allocated, how many others rely on them with no real alternative, and who pays to keep the infrastructure going. [Article V](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-resource-allocation-dependencies-and-ecosystem-funding) (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*) sets the Rights Floor for sharing resources. This subsection applies the [§11.1 Market Concentration Threshold Mechanism (Adopter-Tunable)](#111-market-concentration-threshold-mechanism-adopter-tunable) concentration thresholds to that ground. Operative threshold-setting rules live in **CJS-5.125 §11.1** (*Market concentration threshold-setting discipline*); the detail on resource maps, flows, allocation, and ecosystem funding lives in [**CS-6.335**](corpus_systems/cs_06.335_resource_allocation_funding_stewardship.md) (*Resource allocation and funding stewardship*) and [**CS-6.325**](corpus_systems/cs_06.325_adaptive_sustainability_ecosystem_resilience.md) (*Adaptive sustainability and ecosystem resilience*).
