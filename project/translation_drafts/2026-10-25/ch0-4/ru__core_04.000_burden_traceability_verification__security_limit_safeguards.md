# Chapter Four excerpt — limits on security constraints

## English source passage

- ensure any limit is **materially necessary**, **proportionate**, **narrowly scoped**, **auditable**, and **non-pretextual**; its justification and scope must be **independently verifiable**; it must be **time-limited** where feasible
