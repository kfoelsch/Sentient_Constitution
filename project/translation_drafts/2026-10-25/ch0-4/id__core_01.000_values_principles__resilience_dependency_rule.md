# Chapter One §10 — Recovery Capacity Scaled to Dependency and Impact

## English source passage

The more sentients depend on a system and the greater its impact, the less that system should rely on emergency intervention. It should instead invest in tested, audited, bounded self-recovery — reducing [Avoidable Burden](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) and supporting long-term [**Continuity**](#continuity).

## Draft translation (id)

Semakin besar ketergantungan makhluk berakal pada suatu sistem dan semakin besar dampaknya, semakin kecil sistem itu seharusnya bergantung pada intervensi darurat. Sistem sebaiknya berinvestasi pada pemulihan mandiri yang teruji, diaudit, dan dibatasi—untuk mengurangi [Beban yang Dapat Dihindari](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) serta mendukung [**Kontinuitas**](#continuity) jangka panjang.
