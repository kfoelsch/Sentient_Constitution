# Preamble excerpt — system and institution certification

## English source

**Certify systems and institutions when impact is serious enough** — before a high-impact System is trusted at scale, obtain a contestable System Certification Record ([Chapter Nine Parts A–B](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification)); evaluate an Institution on its own class, conduct, governance, and portfolio under [Chapter Nine Part D](core_09.015_institutional_alignment_certification.md#chapter-nine-part-d-institutional-alignment-certification) and record the result in an Institutional Certification Record.

## fr draft

**Certifier les systèmes et les institutions lorsque l’impact est suffisamment grave** — avant de faire confiance à grande échelle à un système à fort impact, obtenez un dossier de certification du système susceptible de contestation ([parties A–B du chapitre neuf](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) ; évaluez une institution selon sa propre classe, sa conduite, sa gouvernance et son portefeuille conformément à la [partie D du chapitre neuf](core_09.015_institutional_alignment_certification.md#chapter-nine-part-d-institutional-alignment-certification), puis consignez le résultat dans un dossier de certification institutionnelle.
