# Chapter One excerpt — how the constitutional layers connect

## English source

**How the layers connect.** Each layer answers a different question, and every Chapter One principle points to the other three:

- **Aims and Tetrad** ([Preamble §1](core_00.000_preamble.md#the-model)): what shared systems are for ([Flourishing](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional) and [Continuity](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim)), and the four duties that keep that pursuit legitimate ([participation](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [oversight](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [accountability](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), and [timeliness](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional)).
- **Principles** (this chapter): how those aims and duties become values, constraints, and rules for weighing them against each other.
- **Articles** ([Chapter Six](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#chapter-six-foundational-rights)): the Rights Floor protections that stay usable while the aims are pursued. A section's **Trace** lists them under *Especially*.
- **Definitions** ([Chapters Two through Five](core_02.000_definition_structure.md)): the shared meanings used to test whether a claim is true. A section's **Definitions · Assessment · Compliance** widget lists them.

## pt draft

**Como as camadas se conectam.** Cada camada responde a uma pergunta diferente, e cada princípio do Capítulo Um aponta para as outras três:

- **Objetivos e Tetrad** ([§1 do Preâmbulo](core_00.000_preamble.md#the-model)): para que servem os sistemas compartilhados ([Flourishing](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional) e [Continuity](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim)) e os quatro deveres que mantêm legítima essa busca ([participação](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [supervisão](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [responsabilização](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability) e [pontualidade](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional)).
- **Princípios** (este capítulo): como esses objetivos e deveres se tornam valores, restrições e regras para ponderá-los entre si.
- **Artigos** ([Capítulo Seis](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#chapter-six-foundational-rights)): as proteções do Piso de Direitos que permanecem utilizáveis durante a busca dos objetivos. O **Trace** de cada seção lista os artigos correspondentes em *Especialmente*.
- **Definições** ([Capítulos Dois a Cinco](core_02.000_definition_structure.md)): os significados compartilhados usados para verificar se uma afirmação é verdadeira. O widget **Definitions · Assessment · Compliance** de cada seção as lista.
