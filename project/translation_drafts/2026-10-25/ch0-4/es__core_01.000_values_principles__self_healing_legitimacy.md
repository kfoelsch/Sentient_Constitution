# Chapter One §10 — Self-Healing Legitimacy and Limits

## English source passage

This is what Chapter Five calls [**Self-Healing**](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#self-healing) — and it is only legitimate when it makes a system more honest about its own condition, not less. Automatic recovery that masks the root cause, suppresses evidence, or substitutes for governance is not self-healing. It is a [Truth](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) violation and an [Incentive Alignment](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment) defect.

## Draft translation (es)

Esto es lo que el Capítulo Cinco llama [**Autorreparación**](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#self-healing), y solo es legítima cuando hace que el sistema sea más honesto sobre su propio estado, no menos. La recuperación automática que oculta la causa raíz, suprime pruebas o sustituye a la gobernanza no es autorreparación. Es una infracción de la [Verdad](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) y un defecto de [Alineación de Incentivos](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment).
