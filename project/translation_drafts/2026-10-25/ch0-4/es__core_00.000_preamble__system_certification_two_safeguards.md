# Preamble excerpt — certification and challenge/audit safeguards

## English source

- **System alignment certification** ([Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification)) — before a high-impact system is trusted at scale, gather and review evidence to determine whether it is constitutionally safe to rely on right now. Certification is one of two safeguards that work together: it makes a system worthy of trust, and the rights of sentients to challenge and audit it ([Article XIII](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-right-to-reliable-and-trustworthy-systems) (*Right to Reliable and Trustworthy Systems*)) keep it honest. Neither replaces the other.

## es draft

**Certificación de alineación del sistema** ([Capítulo Nueve](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification)) — antes de confiar a gran escala en un sistema de alto impacto, reúna y examine pruebas para determinar si es constitucionalmente seguro depender de él ahora. La certificación es una de dos salvaguardas que funcionan conjuntamente: hace que el sistema merezca confianza, y el derecho de las personas sentientes a impugnarlo y auditarlo ([Artículo XIII](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-right-to-reliable-and-trustworthy-systems) (*Derecho a sistemas fiables y dignos de confianza*)) lo mantiene honesto. Ninguna sustituye a la otra.
