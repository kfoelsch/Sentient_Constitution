## English source passage

**Chapter Four — Burden, traceability, and verification**

- **What it owns:**
  - puts the proof burden on whoever claims compliance; and
  - requires traceable evidence, observability, and verification that scale to [**material stake**](#material-stake) and remain practically challengeable.
- **What it produces:** The verification pipeline that feeds **verified inputs** in [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) and system-alignment evidence in [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification). It does not replace standing measurement itself.

## Draft translation (id)

**Bab Empat — Beban pembuktian, keterlacakan, dan verifikasi**

- **Ruang lingkupnya:**
  - membebankan pembuktian kepada pihak yang menyatakan kepatuhan; dan
  - mewajibkan bukti yang dapat dilacak, keteramatan, serta verifikasi yang sebanding dengan [**besarnya kepentingan material**](#material-stake) dan tetap dapat dipersoalkan secara praktis.
- **Hasilnya:** Alur verifikasi yang memasok [**masukan terverifikasi**](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) bagi Bab Sepuluh dan bukti keselarasan sistem bagi [Bab Sembilan](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification). Alur ini tidak menggantikan pengukuran status itu sendiri.
