# Chapter Three excerpt — standing effects for sentients and institutions

## English source passage

A sentient's standing is affected only when a verified link to that sentient is shown. Being associated with the non-compliant system is not enough. The verified link must be to:

- causal role
- duty
- authority
- control
- foreseeability
- benefit
- concealment
- feasible prevention capacity

An institution's standing is affected when the institution is the subject being assessed, or when it is verified as the vehicle of the non-compliance pattern. Institution records remain separately traceable from individual sentient records. Apply those cases under [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) and [Chapter Eleven](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). The finding profile helps route and audit. It does not itself set standing outcomes, slots, locks, gates, or remedies.

## Draft translation (id)

Kedudukan makhluk sentien hanya terpengaruh jika hubungan terverifikasi dengan makhluk tersebut ditunjukkan. Keterkaitan dengan sistem yang tidak patuh saja tidak cukup. Hubungan terverifikasi itu harus berupa:

- peran kausal
- kewajiban
- kewenangan
- kendali
- keterdugaan
- manfaat
- penyembunyian
- kapasitas pencegahan yang layak

Kedudukan institusi terpengaruh jika institusi tersebut menjadi subjek penilaian, atau jika terverifikasi bahwa institusi itu menjadi sarana pola ketidakpatuhan. Catatan institusi tetap dapat ditelusuri secara terpisah dari catatan setiap makhluk sentien. Terapkan kasus-kasus tersebut menurut [Bab Sepuluh](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) dan [Bab Sebelas](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). Profil temuan membantu perutean dan audit. Profil itu sendiri tidak menetapkan hasil kedudukan, slot, penguncian, gerbang, atau pemulihan.
