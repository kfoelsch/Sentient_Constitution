# Preamble excerpt — system-certification review coverage

## English source

  - The review must cover:
    - whether the system respects survival essentials;
    - **Article V** (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*) resource allocation and dependency stewardship — including [Proportionate Cross-System Support](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#proportionate-cross-system-support) where shared-infrastructure reliance is materially at issue;
    - safety;
    - participation; and
    - other constitutional floors.
