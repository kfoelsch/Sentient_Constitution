# Preamble excerpt — designation review step

## English source

5. **Conduct anti-constitutional designation review** ([Chapter Twelve](core_13.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-thirteen-anti-constitutional-misconduct)) — if a highest-impact violation finding may also satisfy anti-constitutional criteria, Chapter Twelve must decide whether the corresponding designation attaches. Designation does not change how serious Chapter Ten already found the harm to be. Ordinary Chapter Eleven effects continue in parallel until a final designation triggers the Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock.
