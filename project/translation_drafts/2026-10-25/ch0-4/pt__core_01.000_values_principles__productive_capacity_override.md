# Chapter One §9.1.1 — No Bypassing Principle-Weighing Rules

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §9.1.1 Preserve, Expand, and What Does Not Count

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

- **Bypassing the rules for weighing principles:**
  - bypassing [§13 Constitutional Collision Resolution Process](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#13-constitutional-collision-resolution-process), including the [Constitutional Collision Record](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#constitutional-collision-record) requirements; or
  - the other prohibited override paths in [§14 Prohibition on Absolute Override](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#14-prohibition-on-absolute-override), including shifting ecological, intergenerational, or distributional harm off the books that **Chapters Two through Four** require stay visible.

## Draft translation (pt)

- **Contornar as regras de ponderação de princípios:**
  - contornar o [§13 Processo de Resolução de Colisões Constitucionais](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#13-constitutional-collision-resolution-process), incluindo os requisitos do [Registro de Colisão Constitucional](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#constitutional-collision-record); ou
  - recorrer a outros caminhos de sobreposição proibidos pelo [§14 Proibição de Sobreposição Absoluta](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#14-prohibition-on-absolute-override), inclusive retirar da contabilidade danos ecológicos, intergeracionais ou distributivos que os **Capítulos Dois a Quatro** exigem que permaneçam visíveis;
