# Chapter One §10 — Recovery Capacity Scaled to Dependency and Impact

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §10 Resilience and Self-Healing Design

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

The more sentients depend on a system and the greater its impact, the less that system should rely on emergency intervention. It should instead invest in tested, audited, bounded self-recovery — reducing [Avoidable Burden](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) and supporting long-term [**Continuity**](#continuity).

## Draft translation (es)

Cuanto más dependan los seres sintientes de un sistema y mayor sea su impacto, menos debe depender ese sistema de la intervención de emergencia. En su lugar, debe invertir en una autorrecuperación probada, auditada y acotada, que reduzca la [Carga Evitable](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) y sostenga la [**Continuidad**](#continuity) a largo plazo.
