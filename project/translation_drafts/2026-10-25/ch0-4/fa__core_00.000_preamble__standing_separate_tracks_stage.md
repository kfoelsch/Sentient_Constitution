# Preamble excerpt — measure standing on separate tracks

## English source passage

3. **Measure standing on separate tracks** ([Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement)) — when help or harm is serious enough to matter constitutionally, Chapter Ten must open formal case files, admit only **verified inputs** (including system-alignment certification evidence from Chapter Nine when that evidence is material), and classify what was verified.
