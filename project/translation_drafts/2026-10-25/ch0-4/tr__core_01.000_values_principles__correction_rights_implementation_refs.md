# §6.1 Trust — Right to Correction and Implementation References

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §6.1

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

The right to this correction is stated in [Article XIII-B](core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy) (*Right to Redress and Remedy*) in Chapter Six. [Chapter Eleven §9](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#9-enforcement-realism-and-remedy-systems) (*Enforcement realism and remedy systems*) applies this principle to standing consequences, and [CI-11.205](corpus_institutions/ci_12.205_remedy_systems_institutional_redress_capacity.md) (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*) sets institutional implementation.
