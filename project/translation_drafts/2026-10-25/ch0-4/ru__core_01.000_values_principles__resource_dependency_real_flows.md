# Chapter One §11.4 — Thresholds Based on Real Flows

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §11.4 Resource and Dependency Concentration

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

- **From real flows:** base the levels on the dependency maps and resource-flow records that [Article V-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-a-dependency-mapping-and-resource-flow-transparency) (*Dependency Mapping and Resource-Flow Transparency*) requires, not on operator labels or one-off transfers.


## Draft translation (ru)

- **На основе реальных потоков:** уровни должны опираться на карты зависимостей и записи о потоках ресурсов, требуемые [Статьёй V-A](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-v-a-dependency-mapping-and-resource-flow-transparency), а не на обозначения операторов или разовые передачи.
