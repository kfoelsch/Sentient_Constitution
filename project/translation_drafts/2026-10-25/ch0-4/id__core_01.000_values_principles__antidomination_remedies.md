# Chapter One §11.2.3 — Remedies for Substantiated Domination

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §11.2.3 Remedies

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

*In plain terms: when domination is substantiated, the response must fit the harm, restore real choice, and not punish size for its own sake.*

The following live in **CJS-5.125 §11.2 — Anti-domination conduct and remediation catalog**:
- illustrative prohibited conduct patterns;
- proportionate remedy tools;
- cross-domain evaluation routing.

Other owners:
- labor-mobility categorical prohibitions: [Article III-C](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-c-labor-and-economic-floor) (*Labor and Economic Floor*);
- interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity operational terms: **CJS-5.130 §17** (*Continuity: interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*);
- horizontal and vertical consolidation risk patterns: **§11.3** (*Consolidation Ceiling*).

Remedies must:
- be proportionate to:
  - concentration;
  - dependency;
  - conduct;
  - constitutional harm;
- restore contestability where domination is substantiated;
- preserve the Chapter Six Rights Floor.

Remedies may not punish scale alone.

Operative remedy selection follows **CJS-5.125 §11.2** (*Anti-domination conduct and remediation catalog*).


## Draft translation (id)

*Secara sederhana: ketika dominasi terbukti, respons harus sebanding dengan kerugian, memulihkan pilihan yang nyata, dan tidak menghukum ukuran semata.*

Hal berikut diatur dalam **CJS-5.125 §11.2 — Katalog Perilaku Dominasi dan Pemulihan**:
- pola perilaku terlarang yang bersifat ilustratif;
- perangkat pemulihan yang proporsional;
- perutean evaluasi lintas bidang.

Pemilik aturan lainnya:
- larangan kategoris mengenai mobilitas tenaga kerja: [Pasal III-C](core_06.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-c-labor-and-economic-floor) (*Batas Minimum Hak Buruh dan Ekonomi*);
- ketentuan operasional interoperabilitas, portabilitas, dan integritas keluar: **CJS-5.130 §17** (*Kontinuitas: ketentuan interoperabilitas, portabilitas, dan integritas keluar*);
- pola risiko konsolidasi horizontal dan vertikal: **§11.3** (*Batas Konsolidasi*).

Pemulihan harus:
- sebanding dengan:
  - pemusatan;
  - ketergantungan;
  - perilaku;
  - kerugian konstitusional;
- memulihkan daya untuk menggugat ketika dominasi terbukti;
- mempertahankan Batas Minimum Hak Bab Enam.

Pemulihan tidak boleh menghukum skala semata.

Pemilihan pemulihan operasional mengikuti **CJS-5.125 §11.2**.
