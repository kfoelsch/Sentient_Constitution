# Chapter One §9.2 — Claim Criteria and Limits on Efficiency Gains

## English source passage

**When an efficiency claim counts:**

- **Traces to:** real constitutional outcomes.
- **Stays consistent with:**
  - Safety;
  - Truth;
  - the Chapter Six Rights Floor;
  - ecological integrity;
  - dignity;
  - meaningful agency; and
  - fair distribution.

**Efficiency gains must not:**

- hollow the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad); or
- substitute dashboard metrics for progress toward the [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims).


## Draft translation (id)

**Kapan klaim efisiensi dapat dihitung:**

- **Harus dapat ditelusuri ke:** hasil konstitusional yang nyata.
- **Harus tetap selaras dengan:**
  - Keselamatan;
  - Kebenaran;
  - Batas Minimum Hak Bab Enam;
  - integritas ekologis;
  - martabat;
  - agensi yang bermakna; dan
  - distribusi yang adil.

**Peningkatan efisiensi tidak boleh:**

- mengosongkan makna [Tetrad Konstitusional](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad); atau
- mengganti kemajuan menuju [Dua Tujuan Konstitusional](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims) dengan metrik dasbor.
