# Chapter One §9.1.1 — Power Concentration and Shifted Costs

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §9.1.1 Preserve, Expand, and What Does Not Count

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

- **Concentrating power or pushing costs onto others:**
  - concentrating wealth, power, control, or opportunity in ways that harm other sentients' wellbeing, agency, dignity, or ecological conditions — now or later;
  - degrading life-supporting natural systems, or pushing ecological or intergenerational costs onto others without mitigation, disclosure, and representation;

## Draft translation (zh)

- **集中权力或将成本转嫁给他人：**
  - 以损害其他有感知者当下或未来的福祉、能动性、尊严或生态条件的方式，集中财富、权力、控制权或机会；
  - 破坏维持生命的自然系统，或在没有减缓、披露和代表机制的情况下，将生态或代际成本转嫁给他人；
