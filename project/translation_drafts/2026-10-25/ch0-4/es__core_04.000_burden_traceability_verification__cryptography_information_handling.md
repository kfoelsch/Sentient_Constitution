# Chapter Four excerpt — cryptography and information-type handling

## English source

Cryptography and information-type handling must align with the Constitution and **[CS-6.305](corpus_systems/cs_06.305_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-6305-part-a-information-types-and-handling) (*Information types and handling*) and [CS-6.310 — Data classifications](corpus_systems/cs_06.310_data_classifications.md#cs-6310-part-b-data-classifications)**; where materially relevant, they must still permit full compliance verification under this chapter. Cryptographic and information-type handling must not replace [Truth (Constitutional Constraint)](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) or [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint), or the duties in **sections 2**, **3**, or **5**.

## es draft

La criptografía y el tratamiento de tipos de información deben ajustarse a la Constitución y a **[CS-6.305](corpus_systems/cs_06.305_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-6305-part-a-information-types-and-handling) (*Tipos y tratamiento de la información*) y [CS-6.310 — Clasificaciones de datos](corpus_systems/cs_06.310_data_classifications.md#cs-6310-part-b-data-classifications)**; cuando sean materialmente pertinentes, deben seguir permitiendo la verificación plena del cumplimiento conforme a este capítulo. La criptografía y el tratamiento de tipos de información no deben sustituir a [Verdad (restricción constitucional)](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) ni a [Seguridad (restricción constitucional)](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint), ni a las obligaciones de las **secciones 2**, **3** o **5**.
