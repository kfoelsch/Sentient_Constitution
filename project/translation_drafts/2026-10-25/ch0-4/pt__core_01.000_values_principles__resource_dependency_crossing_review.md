# Chapter One §11.4 — Threshold Crossing Requires Review

## English source passage

Crossing a level calls for heightened review and mitigation under [§11.1.1 Concentration Threshold Triggers (Adopter-Tunable)](#1111-concentration-threshold-triggers-adopter-tunable). It is not, by itself, a finding of misconduct ([§11.1 Market Concentration Threshold Mechanism (Adopter-Tunable)](#111-market-concentration-threshold-mechanism-adopter-tunable)).


## Draft translation (pt)

A superação de um limite exige análise reforçada e mitigação nos termos de [§11.1.1 Acionadores dos Limites de Concentração (ajustáveis pelos adotantes)](#1111-concentration-threshold-triggers-adopter-tunable). Por si só, a superação não constitui constatação de má conduta ([§11.1 Mecanismo de Limite de Concentração de Mercado (ajustável pelos adotantes)](#111-market-concentration-threshold-mechanism-adopter-tunable)).
