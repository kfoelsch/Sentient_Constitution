# Preamble §1 — Two Aims and Binding Definitions

## English source passage

Those four duties serve the [**Two Constitutional Aims**](#two-constitutional-aims) — what shared systems must optimize for:

- <a id="flourishing"></a>**Flourishing** — sentient wellbeing sustained through truth, safety, trustworthiness, and meaningful agency
- <a id="continuity"></a>**Continuity** — long-horizon stability, sustainability, resilience, and ecological wellbeing

Those aims must be pursued together, always within the Constitution's non-negotiable principle constraints and Rights Floor. The [**Constitutional Tetrad**](#constitutional-tetrad) governs how that pursuit remains legitimate, with all four duties scaled to [**material stake**](#material-stake). Binding definitions for the Tetrad legs and aims live in Chapter Five: [Participation](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [Oversight](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [Accountability](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [Timeliness](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional), [Flourishing](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional), and [Continuity](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim).

## Draft translation (pt)

Esses quatro deveres servem aos [**Dois Objetivos Constitucionais**](#two-constitutional-aims), aquilo que os sistemas compartilhados devem otimizar:

- <a id="flourishing"></a>**Flourishing** — bem-estar senciente sustentado por verdade, segurança, confiabilidade e agência significativa.
- <a id="continuity"></a>**Continuity** — estabilidade de longo prazo, sustentabilidade, resiliência e bem-estar ecológico.

Os objetivos devem ser perseguidos em conjunto, sempre dentro das restrições de princípios inegociáveis e do Piso de Direitos desta Constituição. O [**Tetrad Constitucional**](#constitutional-tetrad) rege como essa busca permanece legítima, com os quatro deveres proporcionais à [**relevância material**](#material-stake). As definições vinculantes dos eixos do Tetrad e dos objetivos constam do Capítulo Cinco: [Participação](core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional), [Supervisão](core_05.020_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), [Responsabilização](core_05.005_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [Tempestividade](core_05.030_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional), [Flourishing](core_05.015_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional) e [Continuity](core_05.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-five-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim).
