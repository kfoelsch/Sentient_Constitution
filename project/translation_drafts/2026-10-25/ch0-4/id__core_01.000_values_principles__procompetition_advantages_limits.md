# Chapter One §11.2.1 — Allowed Advantages and Boundaries

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §11.2.1 Pro-Competition Duties (Dos)

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

The following is not prohibited by itself:
- scale;
- integration;
- intellectual-property protection;
- temporary advantage from genuine innovation;
- efficiency from lawful coordination.

Those advantages stay valid only while they do not become the following, and therefore do not undermine the [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims) or the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad):
- durable domination;
- coercive dependency;
- Rights-Floor degradation;
- ecological burden shifting;
- capture of accountability pathways.

The following justifications must satisfy the traceability and evidence duties in [13. Constitutional Collision Resolution Process](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#13-constitutional-collision-resolution-process) and Chapter Four:
- efficiency;
- competitiveness;
- emergency;
- security;
- productive-capacity.

## Draft translation (id)

Hal-hal berikut tidak dilarang dengan sendirinya:
- skala;
- integrasi;
- perlindungan kekayaan intelektual;
- keunggulan sementara dari inovasi yang sungguh-sungguh;
- efisiensi dari koordinasi yang sah.

Keunggulan tersebut tetap berlaku hanya selama tidak berubah menjadi hal-hal berikut sehingga merusak [Dua Tujuan Konstitusional](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims) atau [Tetrad Konstitusional](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad):
- dominasi yang bertahan lama;
- ketergantungan yang memaksa;
- penurunan Batas Minimum Hak;
- pengalihan beban ekologis;
- penguasaan jalur akuntabilitas.

Pembenaran berikut harus memenuhi kewajiban keterlacakan dan bukti dalam [§13 Proses Penyelesaian Benturan Konstitusional](core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#13-constitutional-collision-resolution-process) dan Bab Empat:
- efisiensi;
- daya saing;
- keadaan darurat;
- keamanan;
- kapasitas produktif.
