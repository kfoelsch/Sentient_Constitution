## English source passage

**Chapter Four — Burden, traceability, and verification**

- **What it owns:**
  - puts the proof burden on whoever claims compliance; and
  - requires traceable evidence, observability, and verification that scale to [**material stake**](#material-stake) and remain practically challengeable.
- **What it produces:** The verification pipeline that feeds **verified inputs** in [Chapter Ten](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) and system-alignment evidence in [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification). It does not replace standing measurement itself.
