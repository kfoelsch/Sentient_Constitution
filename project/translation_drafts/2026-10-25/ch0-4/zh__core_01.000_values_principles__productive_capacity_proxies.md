# Chapter One §9.1.1 — Proxy Measures That No Longer Track Outcomes

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §9.1.1 Preserve, Expand, and What Does Not Count

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

- **Counting the wrong things:**
  - raw throughput, output volume, utilization, headcount, revenue, asset growth, market share, or similar proxies that no longer track real outcomes — including proxies that show "growth" while harm is exported to sentients, future generations, or the environment;

## Draft translation (zh)

- **计算错误的指标：**
  - 不再追踪真实结果的原始吞吐量、产出量、利用率、员工人数、收入、资产增长、市场份额或类似代理指标；也包括那些显示“增长”却把损害转嫁给有感知者、后代或环境的指标；
