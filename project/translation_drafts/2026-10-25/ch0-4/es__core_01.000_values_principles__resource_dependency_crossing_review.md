# Chapter One §11.4 — Threshold Crossing Requires Review

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §11.4 Resource and Dependency Concentration

Source SHA-256: `6b6f07eff0faf4249c4ef3844f8e6ee4b24041768de3ffd4262240bc68854c17`

## English source passage

Crossing a level calls for heightened review and mitigation under [§11.1.1 Concentration Threshold Triggers (Adopter-Tunable)](#1111-concentration-threshold-triggers-adopter-tunable). It is not, by itself, a finding of misconduct ([§11.1 Market Concentration Threshold Mechanism (Adopter-Tunable)](#111-market-concentration-threshold-mechanism-adopter-tunable)).


## Draft translation (es)

Superar un umbral exige una revisión reforzada y medidas de mitigación conforme a [§11.1.1 Activadores de los umbrales de concentración (ajustables por quienes adoptan)](#1111-concentration-threshold-triggers-adopter-tunable). Por sí solo, el cruce no constituye una declaración de conducta indebida ([§11.1 Mecanismo de umbral de concentración del mercado (ajustable por quienes adoptan)](#111-market-concentration-threshold-mechanism-adopter-tunable).)
