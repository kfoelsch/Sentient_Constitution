# Chapter One Part A excerpt — fair treatment

## English source passage

This subsection sets out what fair treatment requires:

- Sentients in similar situations should be treated by the same basic rules.
- What sentients receive, owe, or risk should fit what they contributed, what they need, or what burdens they actually face.
- Different treatment must have a real reason, be proportionate to that reason, respect dignity, and avoid discrimination.
- The detailed rules are carried through Chapter Five, including [Protected Characteristics](core_05.055_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics) and [Protected Characteristic Proxying and Disparate Impact](core_05.055_band_participation.md#protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact).
- When a decision materially affects someone, or when they challenge it, the review path must satisfy [Procedural Fairness](core_05.055_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) wherever Chapter Six or the governing document requires notice, hearing, explanation, or review.

Subbagian ini menjelaskan tuntutan perlakuan yang adil:

- Makhluk sentien dalam situasi serupa harus diperlakukan menurut aturan dasar yang sama.
- Apa yang diterima, wajib ditanggung, atau dipertaruhkan oleh makhluk sentien harus sesuai dengan kontribusi, kebutuhan, atau beban yang benar-benar mereka hadapi.
- Perlakuan berbeda harus memiliki alasan nyata, sebanding dengan alasan tersebut, menghormati martabat, dan menghindari diskriminasi.
- Aturan terperinci diterapkan melalui Bab Lima, termasuk [Karakteristik yang Dilindungi](core_05.055_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics) dan [proksi Karakteristik yang Dilindungi serta Dampak Berbeda](core_05.055_band_participation.md#protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact).
- Jika suatu keputusan berdampak material pada seseorang, atau orang itu menggugatnya, jalur peninjauan harus memenuhi [Keadilan Prosedural](core_05.055_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) setiap kali Bab Enam atau dokumen yang mengatur mensyaratkan pemberitahuan, sidang, penjelasan, atau peninjauan.
