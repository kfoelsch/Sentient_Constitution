# Chapter One excerpt — links to fairness and capability rules

## English source

This builds on [§3.1.1 Access and Opportunity](#311-access-and-opportunity) and [§3.1.3 Fair Treatment](#313-fair-treatment), and it applies [§16 Pillar 2 — Proactive stewardship](core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#16-pillar-2-proactive-stewardship) to the sentients a system affects. For developing sentients it works with [Graduated Capability](core_05.055_band_participation.md#graduated-capability), which judges growth by what a sentient can actually do.

## es draft

Esto se apoya en [§3.1.1 Acceso y oportunidades](#311-access-and-opportunity) y [§3.1.3 Trato justo](#313-fair-treatment), y aplica [§16 Pilar 2 — Custodia proactiva](core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#16-pillar-2-proactive-stewardship) a los seres sintientes afectados por un sistema. Para los seres sintientes en desarrollo, se articula con la [Capacidad gradual](core_05.055_band_participation.md#graduated-capability), que evalúa el crecimiento según lo que cada ser sintiente puede hacer realmente.
