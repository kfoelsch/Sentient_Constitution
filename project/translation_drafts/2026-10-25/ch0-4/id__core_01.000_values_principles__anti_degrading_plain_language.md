# §3.3 Anti-Degrading Process — Plain-Language Explanation

Source: `core_01.000_values_principles.md` §3.3

## English source passage

*In plain terms: however you govern, enforce, judge, restrict, or remedy — you don't run sentients through humiliation, public spectacle, retaliation, or "because it's easier for us" cruelty. Fair consequences, public accountability, and firm restrictions can still be lawful even when they hurt or embarrass someone. What crosses the line is when the process itself is the punishment — designed to degrade, shame, or lash out rather than to protect, correct, restore, or prevent. This applies everywhere constitutional authority runs, not only during rights tradeoffs.*

## Draft translation (id)

*Sederhananya: apa pun cara Anda memerintah, menegakkan aturan, mengadili, membatasi, atau memberi pemulihan, jangan membuat sentien mengalami penghinaan, tontonan publik, pembalasan, atau kekejaman “karena itu lebih mudah bagi kami.” Konsekuensi yang adil, akuntabilitas publik, dan pembatasan tegas tetap dapat sah meskipun menyakiti atau mempermalukan seseorang. Batasnya dilanggar ketika proses itu sendiri menjadi hukuman—dirancang untuk merendahkan, mempermalukan, atau melampiaskan amarah, bukan untuk melindungi, mengoreksi, memulihkan, atau mencegah. Prinsip ini berlaku di mana pun kewenangan konstitusional dijalankan, bukan hanya saat menimbang hak.*
