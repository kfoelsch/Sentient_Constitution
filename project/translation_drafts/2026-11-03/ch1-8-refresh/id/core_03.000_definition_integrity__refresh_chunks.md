## §3.1 Standing effects for already-certified systems

**Current English passage:**

If a system is already running under a [system alignment certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) — including certification, conditional certification, or a recertification that has not expired — material verified non-compliance on that system's functional scope **must** go into [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-compliance-violation-and-standing-model) standing measurement for **that system**. Only facts that pass the [verified-input gate](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing) may enter. Measure standing under [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-compliance-violation-and-standing-model); apply its integration and effects under [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). Reopening, withdrawal, or other certification consequences stay under [Chapter Nine](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). They do not replace that system's standing records.

**id draft:**

Jika suatu sistem telah berjalan berdasarkan [sertifikasi keselarasan sistem](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification)—termasuk sertifikasi, sertifikasi bersyarat, atau sertifikasi ulang yang belum kedaluwarsa—ketidakpatuhan material yang terverifikasi dalam lingkup fungsional sistem tersebut **wajib** dimasukkan ke dalam pengukuran status [Bab Sebelas](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-compliance-violation-and-standing-model) untuk **sistem itu**. Hanya fakta yang lolos [gerbang masukan terverifikasi](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#verified-inputs-for-standing) yang boleh dimasukkan. Ukur status berdasarkan [Bab Sebelas](core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-eleven-compliance-violation-and-standing-model); terapkan integrasi dan dampaknya berdasarkan [Bab Dua Belas](core_12.000_standing_integration.md#chapter-twelve-standing-effects-and-integration). Pembukaan kembali, pencabutan, atau konsekuensi sertifikasi lainnya tetap diatur oleh [Bab Sembilan](core_09.005_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes). Hal-hal tersebut tidak menggantikan catatan status sistem itu.

## §3.2 Standing effects at first certification

**Current English passage:**

If the system is still in its **first** [system alignment certification](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) and has not yet been certified — including where certification is deferred or refused — verified non-compliance first decides the **certification outcome** under Chapter Nine. That outcome may be conditional certification, deferred certification, non-certification, or a comparable result. The outcome does not stop standing input: those System Certification Records supply it under [Chapter Nine §11.2 Every certification outcome](core_09.010_system_alignment_certification_record_recertification.md#112-every-certification-outcome), on the same terms as [§3.1 Standing Effects for Already-Certified Systems](#31-standing-effects-for-already-certified-systems) and only through the verified-input gate. A certification finding that a system is not yet aligned is not a violation by itself. Where the operator makes consistent, honest efforts toward alignment and is not negligent, a violation finding is unlikely.

**id draft:**

Jika sistem masih menjalani [sertifikasi keselarasan sistem](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification) **pertamanya** dan belum disertifikasi—termasuk ketika sertifikasi ditunda atau ditolak—ketidakpatuhan terverifikasi terlebih dahulu menentukan **hasil sertifikasi** berdasarkan Bab Sembilan. Hasil itu dapat berupa sertifikasi bersyarat, sertifikasi tertunda, tidak disertifikasi, atau hasil sebanding. Hasil tersebut tidak menghentikan masukan status: Catatan Sertifikasi Sistem menyediakan masukan itu berdasarkan [Bab Sembilan §11.2 Setiap hasil sertifikasi](core_09.010_system_alignment_certification_record_recertification.md#112-every-certification-outcome), dengan ketentuan yang sama seperti [§3.1 Dampak Status untuk Sistem yang Telah Disertifikasi](#31-standing-effects-for-already-certified-systems) dan hanya melalui gerbang masukan terverifikasi. Temuan sertifikasi bahwa suatu sistem belum selaras bukanlah pelanggaran dengan sendirinya. Jika operator berupaya secara konsisten dan jujur mencapai keselarasan serta tidak lalai, temuan pelanggaran kecil kemungkinannya.
