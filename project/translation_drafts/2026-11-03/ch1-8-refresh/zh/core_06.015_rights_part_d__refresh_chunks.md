
## Justice Rights Floor

**Current English passage:**

Article XX is the justice Rights Floor. Once a violation is verified, the response is a fair process to stop harm, repair damage, and prevent recurrence, scaled to the stakes. Serious restrictions require a proven safety need, a time limit, and a way back; killing is never a justice measure. Disputes and timely review are in Article XXVI; emergency measures are in Chapter Fourteen §6.1.

**zh draft:**
