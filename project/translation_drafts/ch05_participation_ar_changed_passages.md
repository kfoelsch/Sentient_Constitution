## `#creation-rights-limits`

الحق مكفول لكائن واعٍ يتوافق فعليًا مع الغرض المعلن للطرف التشغيلي. ولا يشمل الحق إنشاء طرف تشغيلي أو إدارته بقصد تضليل الآخرين بشأن غرضه أو سيطرته أو مصالحه، أو بقصد إلحاق الضرر بالآخرين، كما تقرر [المادة XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*التأسيس المؤسسي وإنشاء الأعمال*). والشرطان الوحيدان لإنشاء مؤسسة أو مشروع أو إدارته هما الشرطان الضيّقان المتعلقان بصلاحية كائن واعٍ مسمّى في [المادة XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation): وجود قيد قائم، أو فرض تعليق مؤقت عند وجود سبب مرجّح للاعتقاد بعدم التوافق أو بقصد التضليل أو الإضرار. وهذان الشرطان ليسا حدّين لحق الإنشاء، ولا يضيّقان فئة من يجوز لهم إنشاء طرف تشغيلي. أما ترخيص الممارسة أو تصريحها لفعل محدد بموجب [المادة XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*التأسيس المؤسسي وإنشاء الأعمال*) فهو أمر منفصل: إذ يتعلق بأداء الفعل، لا بإنشاء طرف تشغيلي أو إدارته.

وتُحال الآليات التشغيلية — التسجيل، واعتماد [ميثاق النظام](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-charter) أو [الميثاق المؤسسي](core_05_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) وحدود النطاق الميثاقي، والمعاملة المالية، وأطر المسؤولية، وتنظيم الأوراق المالية، ومسارات الحل — إلى [corpus_institutions.md](corpus_institutions.md) وفق ضوابط التأسيس في **الفصل الثامن عشر**، ويجب ألا تنتقص من هذه الحدود الدنيا.

### `#lifespan-equivalent-unit-lequ`

> *In plain terms: a shared unit for expressing full-life-equivalent benefit or loss when Chapter Ten standing measurement calibrates Contribution and Violation records on one scale — not ordinary calendar years or species-only averages.*
>
> - **In scope:** Shorthand for a **Lifespan Equivalent Unit** — the numeric or narrative unit adopted implementation uses to express **full-life-equivalent constitutional benefit** or comparable loss when calibrating the separate Contribution Axis and Violation Axis records against the shared five-times LEQU bands of the [Chapter Ten §7 unified scale](core_10_standing_assessment.md#7-unified-proportional-lequ-scale--contribution-and-violation-axes). Chapter Five pointer; canonical mechanics live in Chapter Ten **§7 Unified proportional LEQU scale — Contribution and Violation Axes**.
> - **Out of scope:** ordinary calendar time, informal "years of life" talk, or species-bound lifespan averages used outside the Chapter Ten unified scale.
> - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](core_05_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether LEQU is applied only as Chapter Ten's unified scale requires — with contribution and violation kept separate and auditable.
> - **Primary assessment:** Apply **only** as implemented consistently with the Chapter Ten **§7** (*Unified proportional LEQU scale — Contribution and Violation Axes*) unified scale, preserving separate contribution and violation records. Read with [Materiality](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality) where magnitude disputes are material.
> - **Primary failure:** undisclosed, un-auditable, or **species-bound** **LEQU** uses that displace, narrow, split, or substitute for the Chapter Ten **§7** (*Unified proportional LEQU scale — Contribution and Violation Axes*) unified-scale calibration rules.

### `#creation-rights-limits`

> The right is for a sentient who is authentically aligned with the purpose stated for the Operating Party, and forming or running one with intent to mislead others about its purpose, control, or interests, or with intent to harm others, is outside it, as [Article XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) states. The only gates on forming or running an institution or enterprise are the two narrow gates on a named sentient's authority in [Article XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*): an outstanding lock, and an Interim Hold on probable cause of misalignment or of intent to mislead or to harm. They are not creation-rights limits and they do not narrow who may form an Operating Party. A practice licence or practice clearance for a named act under [Article XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) is separate: it attaches to performing the act and not to forming or running an Operating Party.
>
> Operational mechanics — registration, [System Charter](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-charter) or [Institutional Charter](core_05_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) adoption and chartered-scope limitation, fiscal treatment, liability frameworks, securities regulation, and dissolution pathways — route to [corpus_institutions.md](corpus_institutions.md) under **Chapter Eighteen** incorporation discipline and must not narrow these floors.

Intimate, health, belief, or comparable inferences that reveal internal states are also [**Type N**](corpus_systems/cs_02_b_data_classifications.md#96-type-n-neurocognitive-and-internal-data). > - **Data types:** Intimate media, sexual-history signals, and intimate-status inferences are [**Type I**](corpus_systems/cs_02_b_data_classifications.md#95-type-i-identity-and-attribution-data) where they attach to an identifiable sentient, and [**Type N**](corpus_systems/cs_02_b_data_classifications.md#96-type-n-neurocognitive-and-internal-data) where they infer intimate or internal states. The more protective handling governs ([CS-2 Part A §2](corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-22-determination-of-classification) (*Determination of classification*)).

Dead Charter Pointers, or System Charter or Institutional Charter text offered as a substitute for those rules, fail both the System Charter or Institutional Charter duty and this cluster.
>
> - [Chapter Fourteen §4.3 Decision-resolution requirements for binding stakeholder choice](core_14_governance.md#43-decision-resolution-requirements-for-binding-stakeholder-choice), as the governance owner floor for the compound requirement.
