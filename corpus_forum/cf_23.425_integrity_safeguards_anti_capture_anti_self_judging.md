This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-23.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*).

*In plain terms: **CF-23.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*) stops a body from judging its own conduct, and stops an interested party from quietly capturing the body that reviews it. It covers alignment rulings, when a matter must be routed away from a compromised venue, and what happens when a whole forum family is compromised rather than a single member.*

**Chapter Twenty-Three linkage.** This file implements the Chapter Twenty-Three interfaces that Integrity Forums need to run alignment work without becoming the general owner of constitutional routing:
- **Section 4** authorizes Integrity Forum alignment rulings and official constitutional alignment certification or review for new and existing systems.
- **Section 4** also authorizes Environment Forum environmental-alignment component review where material ecological exposure exists, together with supervisory coordination.
- **Section 6** provides for integrity-led alignment coordination with other forum families.
- **Section 7** limits alignment rulings that would establish general doctrine and requires records for system certification and recertification.

*In plain terms: How an Integrity Forum handles a tangled, multi-part problem: keep one lead record, send each component to the body that properly owns it, and coordinate without absorbing everyone else's jurisdiction.*

**Purpose.** Give adopting entities practical, reviewable mechanics for Integrity Forums. Those mechanics must let the forum diagnose interlocking misalignment, keep one lead record, refer component issues to the constitutionally proper merits forum, and coordinate neutral implementation. They must remain consistent with **CF-23.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*) (forensic support), **CF-23.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*) (independent investigation interfaces where enforcement fact-development is implicated), and **CF-23.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*) (routing, certification, and publication).

### CF-23.425 §1.1 Alignment Ruling Record

An alignment ruling must be attributable and reviewable. At minimum, the Alignment Ruling Record must identify:
- the integrity findings that bind the matter before the Integrity Forum;
- the system, process, institutional, or contest-pathway dependencies the forum treated as material;
- each component matter referred, sent for ruling, or stayed, including the assigned lead family or forum;
- the priority basis for sequencing where multiple components compete;
- any stays, sequencing requirements, milestones, or coordination orders the Integrity Forum keeps on the lead record; and
- where applicable, the remediation options or menus, including which elements are binding and which are advisory, consistent with **Chapter Twenty-Three**, **section 4**; and
- where a consequence matter is open, the findings, rung, order terms, verification result, term and review dates, and any defiance finding that **CI-19.210 §1** (*Opening a Consequence Matter*) through **CI-19.210 §4** (*Dissolution*) and **CI-19.215 §1** (*Correction and Constrained Authority*) through **CI-19.215 §6** (*Review, Fairness, and Enforcement*) require the lead record to carry.

### CF-23.425 §1.2 Component Referral and Priority Publication

Adopting documents must publish tie-break rules for ordering component referrals and overlapping urgency. Those rules must not displace the primary-stakes assignment of merits. Published criteria must account for Rights-Floor urgency, irreversible-harm risk, classification or compliance dependency where **Chapter Fifteen** is implicated, evidentiary decay or preservation need, and practical resolution sequence. Opaque or caption-driven sorting at the referral threshold is presumptively non-compliant.

### CF-23.425 §1.4 Merits Boundaries and Referred-Forum Authority

Integrity Forums must not issue final merits determinations reserved to another family's primary question unless **Chapter Twenty-Three** assigns that primary stake to Integrity Forums. Reserved questions include constitutional validity and class-wide structural remedy. Forums receiving referrals exercise their own lawful jurisdiction. They must treat binding integrity findings and coordination orders on the lead Integrity record as lawful inputs under certification, issue preclusion, stay, and sequencing rules. They must not permit collateral re-litigation outside published review paths.

### CF-23.425 §2.1 Technical-Specification and Environmental-Component Separation
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-23.425 §2.1** (*Technical-Specification and Environmental-Component Separation*); **CF-23.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*).

<br>

Technical specialist forums under **CF-23.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*) maintain the specifications, measurement methods, test protocols, expert-evidence standards, and domain-specific criteria. Those materials support system-alignment assessment. Environment Forums hold environmental-alignment component review where material ecological exposure exists. The same review applies where there is environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk.

Integrity Forums make the official constitutional alignment certification, validation, recertification, and remediation-supervision record. They must use technical-forum standards where materially applicable. They may send technical questions to the proper technical forum for ruling, but must not rewrite technical specifications as a substitute for referral. Where material ecological exposure exists, Integrity Forums must obtain and record the Environment Forum's environmental-alignment approval, conditional approval, objection, remediation requirement, or release-from-condition finding. Technical Forums must not treat specification maintenance or technical adequacy findings as final constitutional alignment certification unless `core_23.000_forum.md` independently assigns that primary stake to them.

### CF-23.425 §2.2 Triggering Events

Alignment certification or review must be available when any of the following materially applies:
- a new system seeks constitutional certification, incorporation, deployment approval, reliance by a public or critical steward, or other official status within an adopting order;
- an existing system undergoes material change in capability, scale, dependency, stakeholder exposure, integration, autonomy, incentive structure, or risk profile;
- a system has material ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk that requires Environment Forum environmental-alignment review;
- periodic review is due under the system's class, stewardship level, or prior forum order;
- credible evidence indicates misalignment, concealed behavior, under-classification, misclassification, capture, unresolved residual risk, Rights-Floor exposure, ecological exposure, or info-sphere integrity risk;
- a stakeholder, steward, affected institution, technical specialist forum, or lawful oversight body makes a good-faith request for review with a non-frivolous evidentiary basis.

- the contest, appeal, question-for-ruling, or referral path, including any restricted-evidence handling.

### CF-23.425 §2.4 Permitted Dispositions

An Integrity Forum may issue any of the following within lawful scope:
- **certification** for the stated scope and cadence;
- **conditional certification** with remediation milestones, monitoring, limits, or sunset;
- **deferred certification** pending specified evidence, testing, correction, or component determination;
- **non-certification** where the Forum Case Record does not support constitutional alignment;
- **withdrawal or constraint recommendation** where an existing system materially drifts, conceals risk, defeats contestability, or no longer satisfies its certified scope.

### CF-23.425 §2.5 Environmental Sign-Off Effect

Where Environment Forum review is required, final certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions must not issue while a timely Environment Forum objection remains pending. The same bar applies while an unresolved remediation condition or environmental question for ruling remains pending. An Integrity Forum may issue deferred or conditional certification only if the Forum Case Record explains why interim operation remains compatible with **Article I** (*Environmental Survival*). The Forum Case Record must also identify environmental limits and monitoring, and preserve Environment Forum review and reopening authority.

### CF-23.425 §2.6 Component Referrals and Questions for Ruling

Technical adequacy, institutional mandate, ecological merits, environmental-alignment component review, sentience-status, rights restriction, and constitutional-validity questions must be referred, sent for ruling, or stayed under `core_23.000_forum.md` **Chapter Twenty-Three** and this file's routing rules. Alignment certification does not let an Integrity Forum displace another forum's assigned role. That includes a specialized technical panel's standards function, an Institutional Forum's mandate determination, an Environment Forum's ecological merits or environmental-alignment authority, and a Constitutional Forum's final authority over constitutional meaning and structural remedy.

### CF-23.425 §2.7 No Permanent Certificate

Certification is scope-bound and time-bound by its stated cadence and reopening triggers. Material change reopens review. So does newly discovered evidence, stakeholder challenge, incident history, dependency growth, or classification drift. Reopening review does not require proof that the original certification was bad faith.

## CF-23.425 §3 Anti-Self-Judging Activation, Capture-Sensitive Routing, Contest-Integrity Interfaces, and Family-Level Capture

<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with:
  - **CF-23.425 §3** (*Anti-Self-Judging Activation, Capture-Sensitive Routing, Contest-Integrity Interfaces, and Family-Level Capture*);
  - **CF-23.410 §9** (*Inability to Form an Independent Panel*);
  - **CF-23.410 §10** (*Certifier for Inability-to-Form Findings*);

  - **CF-23.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*);

  - [**CI-8.210**](../corpus_institutions/ci_08.210_conflict_integrity_anti_capture_anti_corruption.md#ci-8210-conflict-integrity-anti-capture-and-anti-corruption) (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*),
  - **CI-8.215 §3** (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*), and
  - **CI-6.205** (*Transparency, participation, and accessible challenge and service pathways*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: The machinery for moving a matter away from a body that cannot judge it impartially — including the severe case where an entire forum family, not just one member, has been compromised.*

**Purpose.** Provide the forum-side mechanics for the Chapter Twenty-Three cross-forum anti-self-judging rule, capture-sensitive routing, contest-integrity interfaces, and family-level capture activation. This subsection does not replace **CF-23.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*) panel-formation rules, **CF-23.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*) routing operations, **CF-23.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*) forensic support, **CF-23.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*) investigative-service boundaries, **CF-23.450** (*Forum continuity*) continuity rules, **CF-23.455** (*Fallback operation*) fallback rules, **CF-23.460** (*Emergency adjudication*) emergency-adjudication rules, or [**CI-8.215 §3**](../corpus_institutions/ci_08.215_oversight_assurance_controls_evidence.md#ci-8215-3-contest-integrity-monitoring-class-a-and-class-b) (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*) contest-integrity monitoring. It tells those mechanisms what Forum Case Record, trigger, and boundary conditions must exist when the risk is that a forum, institution, system steward, or integrity process would otherwise judge its own capture, conflict, concealment, recusal failure, retaliation, or comparable process abuse.

### CF-23.425 §3.1 Activation Threshold
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-23.425 §3.1** (*Activation Threshold*); **CF-23.410 §9** (*Inability to Form an Independent Panel*).

</details>

<br>

Anti-self-judging activation is available only where the Forum Case Record contains a non-frivolous, material basis to believe that ordinary routing would make the challenged forum family, institutional chain, or integrity process the sole final merits judge of its own integrity violation. The threshold may be met by:
- credible allegations of capture, conflict, concealed dependency, retaliatory routing, recusal failure, evidence suppression, process abuse, or comparable integrity violation;
- a documented inability to form an independent panel under **CF-23.410 §9** (*Inability to Form an Independent Panel*);
- a recusal or disclosure pattern that indicates coordinated manipulation, selective unavailability, or repeated failure to constitute a lawful bench;
- contest-integrity findings under [**CI-8.215 §3**](../corpus_institutions/ci_08.215_oversight_assurance_controls_evidence.md#ci-8215-3-contest-integrity-monitoring-class-a-and-class-b) (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*) showing contest-pathway failure, chill, backlog abuse, evidence unavailability, or protected-escalation obstruction;
- system-alignment certification or recertification facts showing that the same steward, operator, forum chain, or oversight line materially controls the evidence, classification, review cadence, and contest pathway being challenged.

Mere dissatisfaction with a ruling, captioning a claim against a forum, or naming a forum actor as a witness does not activate backup routing. The activation finding must explain why independent merits review is materially necessary to preserve independence, contestability, or public trust under Chapter Twenty-Three.

### CF-23.425 §3.2 Anti-Self-Judging Activation Record

Every activation or refusal to activate must produce a reviewable Forum Case Record. At minimum, the Forum Case Record must identify:
- the ordinary lead family, panel, institution, steward, or integrity process that would otherwise handle the matter;
- the alleged or verified self-judging risk, including whether it arises from bias, capture, conflict, recusal failure, concealment, process abuse, retaliation, evidence-control dependency, deadlock, unavailability, or inability to form an independent panel;
- the evidence considered at the threshold, including disclosure, recusal, panel-formation, routing, backlog, protected-escalation, forensic, restricted-evidence, and contest-integrity materials where applicable;
- the Chapter Twenty-Three backup family or question-for-ruling path selected, with reasons for using or rejecting each available backup;
- any interim relief, evidence-preservation order, stay, sequencing order, or restricted-evidence handling needed while the routing question is resolved;
- any [Interim Hold](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) on an individual member or seat holder under **CF-23.410 §15** (*Interim Hold on a Panel Member or Seat Holder*), recorded apart from the family-level activation;
- the contest, appeal, publication, and later-review path for the activation decision.

The Forum Case Record must preserve allegations and verified violation findings separately. An activation record may support routing, preservation, interim protection, or referral, but it must not by itself create a Chapter Fifteen standing effect, final Chapter Twenty-One anti-constitutional-misconduct designation, system non-certification, disciplinary finding, or merits judgment unless the governing merits process independently verifies the necessary facts.

### CF-23.425 §3.3 Backup Family Map and Limits

When the Chapter Twenty-Three cross-forum anti-self-judging rule applies, the forum system must use the Chapter Twenty-Three backup map unless a more specific constitutional rule controls:
- claims against **Constitutional** forums route to **Integrity** first and **Institutional** as backup;
- claims against **Institutional** forums route to **Constitutional** first and **Integrity** as backup;
- claims against **Integrity** forums route to **Institutional** first and **Constitutional** as backup;
- claims against **Environment** forums route to **Institutional** first and **Integrity** as backup.

Backup routing is limited to what is necessary to preserve a lawful and contestable merits forum. It does not give the backup family general supervisory power over the challenged family, does not erase component referrals required by primary-stakes routing, and does not displace Constitutional certification where constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy is materially at issue.

### CF-23.425 §3.4 Capture-Sensitive System-Alignment Review
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-23.425 §3.4** (*Capture-Sensitive System-Alignment Review*); **CF-23.425 §2** (*Constitutional Alignment Certification and Review*).

</details>

<br>

For constitutional alignment certification, validation, recertification, withdrawal, or material condition-release under **CF-23.425 §2** (*Constitutional Alignment Certification and Review*), the Integrity Forum must make a capture-sensitive routing check when capture, misalignment, concealed behavior, evidence-control dependency, self-review, or contest-pathway failure is material. The routing check must state:
- who controls the technical evidence, incident history, audit access, environmental component record, classification assumptions, and contest pathway;
- whether the operator, steward, institutional sponsor, or forum-adjacent unit has a material incentive or dependency that could distort certification or recertification;
- whether technical, Environment, Institutional, Sentient, or Constitutional component questions require referral, sending for ruling, or stay;
- whether contest-integrity monitoring, independent forensic support, external assurance, or **CI-23.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) escalation is needed to make the Forum Case Record reviewable.

Where the same institution or system steward materially controls both the system under review and the ordinary contest pathway, the Forum Case Record must explain why the contest pathway remains independent enough to proceed or must activate backup routing, external assurance, or both. Conditional certification may not be used to defer unresolved capture or self-review defects that would defeat practical contestability.

### CF-23.425 §3.5 Internal Integrity Process and Forum Access

Institutional internal integrity process may precede Integrity Forum adjudication only when the process provides independence, disclosure, protected escalation, practical contest, and review safeguards sufficient for the matter's class and urgency. It must not be used to exhaust, delay, chill, or narrow access to a lawful Integrity Forum where:
- the internal chain is itself materially implicated;
- protected escalation or evidence access is obstructed;
- retaliation, concealment, or capture allegations require independent merits review;
- cross-institution deadlock or dependency makes local resolution unreliable;
- a Chapter Twenty-One designation, Rights-Floor, system-certification, or public-trust consequence requires forum-level verification.

Where internal process remains available, the [forum case record](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) must state what issues are stayed, what issues proceed, what evidence must be preserved, and what deadline or trigger returns the matter to the forum if the internal process fails.

### CF-23.425 §3.6 Contest-Integrity Interface

<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with:
  - **CF-23.425 §3.6** (*Contest-Integrity Interface*);
  - **CF-23.410 §10** (*Certifier for Inability-to-Form Findings*);
  - **CF-23.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*);
  - **CF-23.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*).

</details>

<br>

Contest-integrity monitors under [**CI-8.215 §3**](../corpus_institutions/ci_08.215_oversight_assurance_controls_evidence.md#ci-8215-3-contest-integrity-monitoring-class-a-and-class-b) (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*) assess whether contest, secondary review, audit access, and protected escalation pathways function in practice. They may provide pattern findings, contest-pathway records, independence observations, backlog data, escalation history, and evidence-availability information to support anti-self-judging activation, refusal, transfer, or remediation. They must not silently become the merits forum for the underlying dispute.

Where feasible in cross-forum anti-self-judging cases, the **CF-23.410 §10** (*Certifier for Inability-to-Form Findings*) certifier should include or obtain review from contest-integrity or equivalent independent assurance capacity. The resulting monitor material must be kept distinct from case-specific forensic support under **CF-23.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*) and from independent investigative-service work under **CF-23.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*). If monitor findings indicate material contest-pathway failure beyond the case, the forum may refer the structural matter to remediation, external assurance, or **CI-23.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) escalation while preserving the assigned merits forum for the dispute.

### CF-23.425 §3.7 Recusal-Abuse and Routing-Abuse Detection
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-23.425 §3.7** (*Recusal-Abuse and Routing-Abuse Detection*); **CF-23.450** (*Forum continuity*); **CF-23.460** (*Emergency adjudication*).

</details>

<br>

Forum systems must track recusal, reassignment, transfer, threshold dismissal, backlog, and emergency-routing patterns sufficient to detect whether routing tools are being used to manipulate outcomes or avoid independent review. Non-compliant patterns include:
- repeated recusals or substitutions that predictably remove independent members from a disfavored class of cases;
- sudden backlog, publication, or evidence-access delays concentrated in protected-escalation or anti-capture matters;
- routing integrity challenges back to the same materially implicated panel, office, steward, or operational chain as sole final merits judge;
- using emergency, continuity, or restricted-evidence procedures to bypass recusal, publication, contest, or backup-forum obligations;
- treating contest-integrity monitoring, forensic support, or internal integrity review as a substitute for the forum family assigned by Chapter Twenty-Three.

When such a pattern is material, the Forum Case Record must identify whether immediate backup routing, panel reconstitution, external assurance, **CI-23.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*) escalation, or continuity, fallback, or emergency-adjudication measures under **CF-23.450** (*Forum continuity*) through **CF-23.460** (*Emergency adjudication*) are required.

### CF-23.425 §3.8 Family-Level Capture Activation
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-23.425 §3.8** (*Family-Level Capture Activation*); **CF-23.460 §3** (*Restoration and Reconciliation*).

</details>

<br>

Family-level capture activation applies where the Forum Case Record contains a non-frivolous, material basis to believe that capture, compromise, coercive control, coordinated obstruction, concealed dependency, or structural conflict affects a forum family as a whole rather than only a single panel, office, docket, or decision-maker. The threshold may be met by:
- repeated inability to form independent panels across a material class of cases;
- coordinated recusal, reassignment, intake, backlog, publication, evidence-access, or emergency-routing patterns that protect the same interest, steward, institution, faction, or operational chain;
- credible evidence that family leadership, intake, records, questions for ruling, appeal, or restoration functions are controlled by the actor or interest whose conduct is at issue;
- contest-integrity, forensic, audit, or investigative records showing that ordinary intra-family review cannot practically correct the compromise before remedies become ineffective;
- evidence that the family would be deciding for itself whether it should be removed from control, keeping or limiting access to the key records, deciding urgent relief, reviewing its own restoration, or making the final decision that it is independent again.

The activation record must identify:
- the affected family-wide functions, including intake, panel formation, merits adjudication, questions for ruling, appeal, publication, record custody, emergency relief, or restoration;
- the evidence supporting family-level treatment rather than ordinary recusal or panel reconstitution;
- the backup family, external review path, or Constitutional certification path selected, including reasons for rejecting alternatives;
- immediate record-custody, evidence-preservation, publication, stay, and emergency-relief measures;
- which pending matters continue, pause, transfer, or receive emergency-only handling;
- the temporary scope and duration of backup authority;
- the restoration conditions, independent reviewer, review cadence, and contest path.

During activation, the captured or compromised family may provide records, logistical support, witness testimony, and technical cooperation only under safeguards set by the backup or external review authority. It must not control activation, continuation, record custody, merits transfer, restoration review, or the final independence-restoration finding.

Backup authority under this subsection is remedial and temporary. It must preserve primary-stakes routing as far as feasible, must not become permanent control over the displaced family's subject matter, and must send the question to Constitutional Forums for ruling where constitutional meaning, validity, structural remedy, or class-wide redesign is materially at issue. When the trigger clears, restoration follows **CF-23.460 §3** (*Restoration and Reconciliation*) rules, with an independent record explaining what was displaced, what decisions require later review, what Forum Case Records were reconciled, and what hardening measures are required.
