Core home: 23

This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-23.450** (*Forum continuity*).

*In plain terms: **CF-23.450** (*Forum continuity*) states what forums must keep doing when normal operation breaks down — emergency conditions, missing key staff, no lawful panel available, or infrastructure failure. Procedure may be simplified; the right to contest a decision may not be erased.*

This section governs forum continuity when ordinary forum operation is materially impaired by:
- emergency conditions;
- incapacity of key personnel;
- inability to form lawful panels;
- deadlock across adjudicative bodies;
- capture or compromise indicators, including family-level capture under **CF-23.425 §3.8** (*Family-Level Capture Activation*);
- network or infrastructure disruption that impairs constitutional adjudication.
It governs continuity of adjudication. Fallback substitutions are governed by **CF-23.455** (*Fallback operation*), and emergency merits handling, review, restoration, and prohibited emergency patterns are governed by **CF-23.460** (*Emergency adjudication*). This section does not authorize permanent alteration of constitutional forum-family boundaries.
Where forum continuity materially depends on constitutional systems, this section must be read with [**CS-6.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-6345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*) on governance continuity, crisis communications, and exercises. CS-6.345 (*System lifecycle*) states the shared emergency lifecycle, crisis-communication, exercise, restoration-evidence, and audit-trail mechanics; **CF-23.450** (*Forum continuity*) supplies the forum-specific continuity modes, activation triggers, minimum functions, record continuity, communications, coordination, and exercise requirements. **CF-23.455** (*Fallback operation*) and **CF-23.460** (*Emergency adjudication*) supply fallback-operation and emergency-adjudication requirements.

## CF-23.450 §1 Rights-Floor Continuity Rule

*In plain terms: Emergencies may simplify procedure; they may never erase the right to contest, the route to urgent relief, or the Forum Case Record that makes review possible.*

Emergency forum operation must preserve, to the greatest feasible extent:
- baseline challenge rights;
- access to emergency relief;
- reviewability of coercive or materially rights-affecting action;
- auditability of emergency decisions;
- a path back to ordinary independent adjudication.

Emergency simplification must not become a pretext to extinguish contestability.

## CF-23.450 §2 Continuity Modes

*In plain terms: The named states a forum can be in, from Normal through Emergency and Fallback to Restoration. Moving between them must be triggered, recorded, and reversible.*

Each adopting entity must publish at least these forum continuity modes:
- Normal
- Degraded
- Emergency limited-capacity
- Fallback / backup-forum operation
- Family-capture continuity
- Restoration and reconciliation

Mode transitions must be trigger-defined, attributable, auditable, and reversible when triggering conditions clear.

## CF-23.450 §3 Activation Triggers

*In plain terms: A continuity mode switches on only from a documented finding — no lawful panel can be formed, infrastructure is down, key staff are unavailable — not from general unease.*

Continuity mode may activate only on documented findings such as:
- inability to constitute lawful independent panels at ordinary scale;
- physical or digital infrastructure outage materially impairing filing, record access, hearing, or publication;
- emergency incapacity or absence of required judicial or administrative personnel;
- credible capture or compromise indicators affecting ordinary routing or decision security;
- family-level capture activation under **CF-23.425 §3.8** (*Family-Level Capture Activation*);
- overlapping crisis conditions that make ordinary scheduling constitutionally inadequate for emergency matters.

Activation records must state:
- the trigger condition;
- affected forum family or families;
- expected scope and duration;
- what rights-protective functions must continue without interruption;
- who authorized activation;
- when independent review will occur.

Shared expiry, reauthorization, restoration, and closure-evidence requirements follow [**CS-6.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-6345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*), where constitutional-system continuity is implicated. Forum activation records add the **Chapter Twenty-Three** routing and family-specific adjudication details listed here.

## CF-23.450 §4 Continuity Minimum Functions

*In plain terms: The minimum that survives any degraded mode: urgent intake, emergency relief, enough triage to spot Rights-Floor cases, and preservation of the Forum Case Record.*

In any degraded or emergency mode, the system must preserve at minimum:
- intake for urgent matters;
- emergency interim-relief authority;
- routing or triage sufficient to identify Rights-Floor, [Evidence Preservation](../core_05.050_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and safety-critical cases;
- record preservation and chain of custody under [Evidence Preservation](../core_05.050_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation);
- a way to challenge emergency orders;
- a path to restore ordinary forum review when feasible.

## CF-23.450 §5 Record Continuity and Custody

*In plain terms: Even in emergency operation the docket must stay identifiable and the evidence chain unbroken, so the period can be reconstructed afterwards.*

Forum emergency adjudication requires continuous preservation of:
- docket identity;
- filings and timestamps;
- panel-formation and recusal records;
- evidence custody records under [Evidence Preservation](../core_05.050_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation);
- emergency orders and reasons;
- publication-delay records;
- restoration and rollback decisions.

Where digital systems are impaired, institutions must maintain a lawful fallback for record capture and later reconciliation. Generic crisis audit-trail, communication-log, and closure-evidence requirements remain in [**CS-6.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-6345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*); this subsection identifies the forum records that cannot be lost or silently merged during emergency operation.

Long-term archival and retrieval systems that materially support record access, restoration, precedent or publication continuity, audit, challenge, or lawful reconstruction must be treated as forum-continuity dependencies under **CF-23.465 §5** (*Publication, Retention, and Reconciliation*) and classified under [**CS-9.305**](../corpus_systems/cs_09.305_system_classification_machinery.md#cs-9305-part-a-system-classification-machinery) (*System classification machinery*) according to their continuity function.

## CF-23.450 §6 Communication Duties

*In plain terms: Parties must be told which mode is active, what still works, and what has been suspended. Silence during a crisis is itself a failure.*

During continuity-mode operation, forum-facing communications must apply [**CS-6.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-6345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*), crisis-communication discipline and [Chapter One §16.4 Document at Action](../core_01.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#164-document-at-action). The activation record under **CF-23.450 §3** (*Activation Triggers*) is made by the activation itself. The adjudication-specific facts parties need are published or otherwise communicated from that Forum Case Record when the mode takes effect, except for any part a lawful restriction holds back, and a held-back part still leaves a visible marker (**CF-23.445 §3.2** (*Publication timeliness*)). Where digital systems are impaired, the lawful fallback under **CF-23.450 §5** (*Record Continuity and Custody*) carries both the Forum Case Record and the communication. The facts are:
- the active mode;
- what functions remain available;
- where urgent filings should go;
- what publication or hearing delays to expect;
- how parties can seek emergency review;
- when the next continuity-status review will occur.

## CF-23.450 §7 Cross-System and Cross-Institution Coordination

*In plain terms: When several bodies are affected at once, the plan must name who decides, how evidence is shared, who breaks a deadlock, and in what order normal operation resumes.*

Where multiple institutions or forum families are affected, the continuity protocol must define:
- accountability for the continuity decision;
- evidence-sharing boundaries;
- transfer authority;
- deadlock-breaker path;
- conditions for external participation or assurance;
- restoration sequencing across affected bodies.

## CF-23.450 §8 Exercises and Testing

*In plain terms: Continuity plans must be rehearsed against realistic scenarios — no independent panel available, a backlog surge, an outage — because an untested plan is an assumption.*

Forum continuity protocols must participate in the exercise cadence required by [**CS-6.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-6345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*), where constitutional-system continuity is implicated. Forum-specific exercise coverage should include at least:

- inability to form an independent panel;
- major backlog surge;
- digital outage affecting filings or records;
- emergency relief under reduced staffing;
- capture-sensitive routing and backup activation;
- family-level capture activation and restoration.

Findings must be recorded and tied to remediation under **CS-6.345** (*System lifecycle*) **§8** (*Governance continuity, crisis communications, and exercises*), with docket, routing, quorum, and backup-forum failures separately identified.
