This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-22.405** (*Forum formation, forum-structure mapping, and chamber structure*).

*In plain terms: adopting entities turn the constitutional forum families into working benches, chambers, and support structures. This file sets the minimum staffing and structure, keeps family boundaries distinct, and states what chambers may decide.*

## CF-22.405 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file covers how forum families become working benches and chambers without losing their distinct roles.*

This file sets the institutional structure, availability, and chamber boundaries for the constitutional forum families.

This file governs how adopting entities turn the forum families defined in [Chapter Twenty-Three §3](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#3-forum-family-definitions--accountability-through-adjudication) (*Forum Family Definitions — Accountability through Adjudication*) into adjudicative bodies, benches, chambers, and support structures. [Chapter Twenty-Two §4](../core_22.000_forum.md#4-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) (*Default Venue and Primary Stakes*) governs the default venue and routing.

It sets structural requirements for:

- forum-structure mapping and public adjudicative architecture;
- minimum availability of adjudicators and support sufficient to form lawful panels and preserve traceability;
- chamber specialization and anti-collapse limits.

Published intake priority classes, the intake triage body, and day-one filing routing expectations are governed by **CF-22.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*). Staffing composition frameworks, reserve and surge capacity, shared administration and objective assignment safeguards, periodic structural review, prohibited staffing and capacity patterns, and the structural governance record set are governed by **CF-22.470** (*Forum staffing, reserve capacity, shared administration, structural review, and structural records*).

This section does not itself govern panel assignment, recusal, lawful bench formation, backlog requirements, or emergency continuity. Those subjects are governed by **CF-22.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*), **CF-22.445** (*Forum performance, backlog requirements, publication timeliness, and accessibility*), and **CF-22.450** (*Forum continuity*), **CF-22.455** (*Fallback operation*), and **CF-22.460** (*Emergency adjudication*).

## CF-22.405 §2 Why Forum Structure Exists

*In plain terms: forum families need workable benches and chambers to hear matters while keeping their distinct jobs and independent review.*

The forum families established by the Constitution need practical structures before they can hear cases. Mapping families to forums, maintaining available benches, and defining chamber boundaries help keep the proper forum available without collapsing separate functions or placing review under the control of the body being reviewed.

**Grounded in:**

- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§18.3 Separation of Duties](../core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#183-separation-of-duties).
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XIII-A: Non-Capture Safeguards](../core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xiii-a-non-capture-safeguards);
  - [Article XXVII-C: Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor](../core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor).

## CF-22.405 §3 Core Structural Rule
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-22.405 §3** (*Core Structural Rule*); **CF-22.470** (*Forum staffing, reserve capacity, shared administration, structural review, and structural records*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: Every adopting entity must publish a real, working structure for each forum family — not a name on an organization chart. A filer must be able to tell which body handles what.*

Every adopting entity must establish and maintain a published forum structure that:

- gives operational effect to each forum family defined in [Chapter Twenty-Three §3](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#3-forum-family-definitions--accountability-through-adjudication) (*Forum Family Definitions — Accountability through Adjudication*);
- makes family boundaries intelligible to filers, affected parties, and review bodies;
- preserves independence, contestability, and traceability in actual forum design rather than by labels alone;
- maintains enough qualified personnel and, as specified in **CF-22.470** (*Forum staffing, reserve capacity, shared administration, structural review, and structural records*), reserve and substitute capacity to keep the forum families functional in ordinary and stress conditions.

**Minimum family inventory.** Adopting documents must maintain at least the following distinct forum families, in the same order as the default venue table in [Chapter Twenty-Two §4](../core_22.000_forum.md#4-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) (*Default Venue and Primary Stakes*). This inventory implements that table and the family definitions in [Chapter Twenty-Three §3](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#3-forum-family-definitions--accountability-through-adjudication) (*Forum Family Definitions — Accountability through Adjudication*); it does not create additional families.

- **Sentient** forums.
- **Technical Forum Domains** (including specialized technical chambers or panels within other families, as [Chapter Twenty-Three §3.2](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#32-technical-forum-domains) (*Technical Forum Domains*) describes).
- **Institutional** forums.
- **Environment** forums.
- **Integrity** forums.
- **Constitutional** forums.

**Practical flexibility:** Local forum names, internal organization, and administrative detail may differ from any model structure, as long as filers and review bodies can still see and use each required forum family: separate intake, panels where applicable, and appeal or review paths that match that family's role. Cosmetic changes, such as renaming bodies or redrawing chamber lines, cannot satisfy this rule if they merge or erase constitutionally distinct family boundaries. **CF-22.405 §5** (*Family Distinction and Non-Collapse Rule*) states the non-compliant patterns.

## CF-22.405 §4 Family-to-Forum-Structure Translation Map
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-22.405 §4** (*Family-to-Forum-Structure Translation Map*); **CF-22.415 §4** (*Intake Triage Body*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: A published map showing which actual body does the work of each constitutional forum family, and how it is organized — one standing body, several regional ones, or several levels.*

Each adopting entity must publish a forum-structure map that states, in plain language:

- which forum, bench, panel, or set of adjudicative bodies performs the work of each constitutional forum family;
- whether each family is organized as a single standing forum, multiple regional forums, a multi-level set of forums, or another lawful arrangement;
- which chambers, divisions, or designated panels exist within each family;
- which matters are routed directly to a family-level forum and which may begin in a chamber or lower-level bench;
- which adjudicators are authorized to decide matters for each forum family, chamber, division, designated panel, or bench, distinguishing adjudicators from intake, coordination, clerical, forensic, technical, analytical, and other administrative or support roles;
- how each family's published intake priority classes and **intake triage body** (or equivalent under **CF-22.415 §4** (*Intake Triage Body*)) connect initial filings to forums, chambers, or benches;
- the appeal or review path for each family and chamber configuration;
- the backup or fallback forum used when an ordinary forum cannot lawfully act.

The map must be current, publicly accessible, and cross-referenced to the primary-stakes routing rules in `core_22.000_forum.md` **Chapter Twenty-Two**.

## CF-22.405 §5 Family Distinction and Non-Collapse Rule

*In plain terms: Families may share staff, buildings, and software and still must stay constitutionally distinct. Shared plumbing is not a reason to merge functions that are meant to check one another.*

Adopting documents must preserve the distinct constitutional functions of the **Sentient**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Integrity**, and **Constitutional** forum families even where:

- personnel overlap;
- facilities, clerks, or digital systems are shared;
- a single chief administrator or budgeting authority serves multiple families;
- a jurisdiction uses a unified judiciary for ordinary administration.

The following patterns are non-compliant unless adopted through constitutional amendment that lawfully changes **Chapter Twenty-Two** itself:

- treating one family as a mere chamber of another where the result removes independent routing, review, or remedy authority;
- giving one family routine authority to decide another family's primary questions merely by internal reassignment;
- using emergency, specialization, efficiency, or caseload pressures as a standing justification for family merger;
- creating technical, commercial, security, or other specialist forums that operate as de facto sovereign families outside the **Chapter Twenty-Two** structure.

## CF-22.405 §6 Minimum Structural Availability by Family
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-22.405 §6** (*Minimum Structural Availability by Family*); **CF-22.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: Each family needs enough adjudicators and clerical support to actually function, so the same small group is not hearing everything.*

Each required forum family must maintain, at minimum:

- enough adjudicators to form lawful panels under **CF-22.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*) without routine dependence on the same small recurring bench for all matters;
- clerical, records, and scheduling support sufficient to preserve traceability and contestability;
- access to translation, accessibility, and public-notice support where needed for meaningful use;
- access to independent forensic, analytical, or technical support where that family's ordinary workload foreseeably requires it.

No family may exist only on paper. If a family's work is assigned to a broader judiciary, the adopting entity must still identify the concrete forums, benches, or judicial officers responsible for that family's functions.

*In plain terms: A family may create specialized chambers, but the document creating one must say which family it sits in and what it covers. A chamber never outranks its family boundary.*

Forum families may create internal chambers, divisions, or designated panels to improve competence, speed, or consistency, but chamber structure must remain subordinate to family boundaries.

Each chamber-creation document must state:

- the family within which the chamber operates;
- the chamber's subject-matter scope;
- the competence profile expected for service in the chamber.

Chambers must not be used to relabel one forum family as another, to erase family-level intake or review paths, or to create a parallel constitutional forum outside Chapter Twenty-Two.

**Forum-specific rule.** This chamber-authority rule remains operative for **Chapter Twenty-Two** forum families and chambers. Where general delegated-subunit rules in **CI-8.210 §3** (*Delegated Subunits, Institutional Design Class, and Attachment Discipline*) and forum-specific chamber-authority duties in this section overlap, the rule with the [Fullest Protective Effect](../subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#fullest-protective-effect) governs. Forum-specific continuity, fallback, and emergency-adjudication controls remain in **CF-22.450** (*Forum continuity*), **CF-22.455** (*Fallback operation*), and **CF-22.460** (*Emergency adjudication*).

*In plain terms: Each chamber must state up front how its rulings can be reviewed. A chamber may not rewrite routing by habit or quietly take work that belongs to another family.*

Each chamber-creation document must state whether chamber rulings are appealable within the same family, directly to another family only on questions for ruling, or through another published review path consistent with **Chapter Twenty-Two**.

Chambers must not:

- rewrite family-level routing by internal practice;
- make sending a question for ruling or transfer unnecessary where **Chapter Twenty-Two** would otherwise require it;
- hold themselves out as a separate constitutional forum family;
- use specialization to reduce challenge rights, publication duties, or independence safeguards.

*In plain terms: Creating a specialist chamber requires explaining why ordinary family-level handling will not do. Specialization must be justified, not assumed.*

Where adopting documents create technical, scientific, engineering, medical, security-sensitive, commercial, electoral, environmental, or other specialist chambers, the creating document must explain why chamber-level specialization is needed and why family-level reassignment is not constitutionally required.

Specialist chambers must:

- remain reviewable and contestable within the constitutional family structure;
- use published entry criteria rather than ad hoc prestige, patronage, or opaque expertise claims;
- preserve generalist participation or review where exclusive specialist control would create capture or methodological monoculture;
- coordinate with **CF-22.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*) where the chamber exercises technical-forum functions.

Specialization must add competence without creating an insulated guild-like adjudicative body.
