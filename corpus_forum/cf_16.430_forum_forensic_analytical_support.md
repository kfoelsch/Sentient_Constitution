This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-16.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*).

*In plain terms: forums need qualified technical help when evidence is too specialized, hidden, or tangled to assess unaided. That help explains the evidence; it does not decide or prosecute the case or become a second forum.*

## CF-16.430 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file covers independent technical help with evidence and its separation from the forum’s decision.*

This file governs access to forensic and analytical support, its independence and evidence handling, and its boundary with forum decisions.

Where a **constitutional community** establishes, adopts, or relies on forum families, it must ensure they maintain or can obtain access to **independent forensic and analytical support**. [Chapter Seventeen §6](../core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#6-forum-support-before-during-and-after-review--oversight-architecture) (*Forum Support Before, during, and After Review — Oversight Architecture*) requires support before, during, and after review, including inquiry before a case is filed and, where material, remedy verification and reopening inputs after review. A forum must have qualified help when facts are too technical, hidden, fragmented, or causally tangled for the parties and panel to handle fairly on their own.

This support exists to help the forum understand evidence. It does not create another merits forum, another prosecutor, or another decision-maker.

## CF-16.430 §2 Why Forensic and Analytical Support Exists

*In plain terms: specialized evidence needs qualified, reviewable help so that a forum and the parties can understand and test it.*

Complex, hidden, or fragmented evidence can be difficult to assess without specialist assistance. Independent support helps make methods and findings understandable and open to challenge while leaving decisions with the authorized forum.

## CF-16.430 §3 When Forensic or Analytical Support Is Required

<br>

*In plain terms: The situations where a forum must be able to call for technical help: the evidence is fragmented, restricted, highly technical, or the uncertainty could change the outcome.*

Forensic or analytical support must be available when a dispute materially involves any of the following:

- uncertainty that could change the outcome, remedy, or protected status of a sentient, community, institution, system, or environment;
- evidence that is restricted, fragmented, technically complex, difficult to interpret, or held across more than one system or institution;
- disputed causation, event reconstruction, technical failure, model behavior, record integrity, or comparable fact questions;
- suspected concealment, tampering, deletion, selective disclosure, or chain-of-custody failure;
- evidence dependencies that ordinary party presentation, ordinary audit records, or judicial notice cannot fairly resolve.

The support requirement is practical, not decorative. If a case cannot be fairly decided without competent help preserving, testing, reconstructing, or explaining material evidence, the forum must have a lawful way to obtain that help.

## CF-16.430 §4 What Support Personnel May Do

<br>

*In plain terms: Titles vary — inspector, analyst, special master — but the scope of the appointment must be written down, and the Forum Case Record must make clear to the parties what the appointee was asked to do.*

Support personnel may be called **forum inspectors**, **forensic analysts**, **special masters**, technical reviewers, evidence custodians, or comparable titles. Whatever title is used, the Forum Case Record must make the support role understandable to the parties and to later reviewers.

Within a **published** or **reasoned** scope order, support personnel may:

- identify and preserve relevant evidence;
- reconstruct events, technical states, transactions, communications, model behavior, or other material facts;
- test records, artifacts, systems, samples, logs, methods, or claimed causal explanations;
- explain technical or specialized material in a form the forum and affected parties can understand;
- state uncertainty, limits, assumptions, and alternative explanations.

In their support role, personnel must not impose sanctions, decide liability, make final credibility determinations reserved to the forum, or issue binding merits rulings. Any separately authorized role remains subject to the independence and seat-separation requirements in [Chapter Six §3](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) (*Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement*) and the support boundary in [Chapter Seventeen §6](../core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#6-forum-support-before-during-and-after-review--oversight-architecture) (*Forum Support Before, during, and After Review — Oversight Architecture*). Support makes evidence more reliable and understandable; the panel decides the merits.

<br>

*In plain terms: Technical helpers face the same conflict screening as decision-makers, and parties must have a real way to challenge who was appointed.*

Appointment, conflict disclosure, recusal, and challenge pathways must satisfy [**CI-21.205**](../corpus_institutions/ci_21.205_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-21205-appointment-competency-rotation-and-removal) (*Appointment, competency, rotation, and removal*), **CI-6.210** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*), and `core_16.000_forum.md` **Chapter Twenty-Two**.

Parties and materially affected sentients must have a meaningful opportunity, consistent with lawful restrictions, to challenge:

- the scope of the assignment;
- the support actor's qualifications, independence, conflicts, or methods;
- material assumptions, omitted evidence, uncertainty statements, and conclusions;
- any restriction that prevents a fair chance to understand or answer the support record.

Evidence showing that a sentient is not at fault, is less at fault, or is at fault, and evidence casting doubt on a witness, must be preserved under the same good-faith standard. A support process is defective if it helps one side develop technical facts while burying material evidence that would fairly assist another side or an affected sentient.

*In plain terms: The trail a later reviewer needs: what was tested, how, by whom, what was uncertain, and where the gaps are.*

Forensic and analytical support must leave a Forum Case Record that later reviewers can follow. At minimum, proportionate to impact and sensitivity, the support record must include:

- chain-of-custody documentation;
- preservation steps and any known gaps;
- method logs showing what was tested, how it was tested, and by whom;
- uncertainty statements, assumptions, and limits;
- access, retention, isolation, and disclosure handling for restricted or sensitive material.

Evidence handling must satisfy Chapter Three [Evidence Preservation](../subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation). Access to restricted evidence must also satisfy **Chapter Three** (*Auditability*, *Verifiability*, *Transparency*), `subcore_03.010_definition_structure.md` **Subcores 3-A to 3-C** security-constrained observability requirements, **[Subcore 4-A — Data Types](../subcore_04.050_data_types.md#subcore-4-a-data-types) and [CS-4.305](../corpus_systems/cs_04.305_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-4305-information-types-and-handling) (*Information types and handling*)** restricted-data rules, and `core_04.020_rights_part_c.md` **Article XVII-A** (*Auditability and Observable Evidence*) plus **Article XVII** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*)'s verification-access provisions.

*In plain terms: Findings must be understandable to an ordinary affected sentient without dumbing down the technical substance. Acting without notice is allowed only when delay would destroy the evidence.*

Findings, reports, technical explanations, and preservation records must be reasoned, attributable, reviewable, and written or presented in a form an ordinary affected sentient can understand without losing necessary technical precision.

Emergency ex parte preservation steps may be permitted only when delay would foreseeably destroy, hide, alter, or materially corrupt evidence. Those steps must be narrowly scoped, recorded at action under [Chapter Two §15.4](../core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#154-document-at-action) (*Document at Action*) and inside the **intake target** of the matching tier (6 hours at the Emergency setting; [CF-16.445 §5.1](cf_16.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-16445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*)), and made contestable after the immediate preservation need passes. Emergency preservation may secure evidence; it must not become an unreviewable merits shortcut.

<br>

*In plain terms: These are related jobs with different purposes. Forensic support develops evidence and analysis before, during, or after review; contest-integrity monitoring checks whether contest pathways work in practice.*

Forum forensic support and contest-integrity monitoring are related but different.

[**CI-6.215 §3**](../corpus_institutions/ci_06.215_oversight_assurance_controls_evidence.md#ci-6215-3-contest-integrity-monitoring-class-a-and-class-b) (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*) asks whether contest pathways, procedures, and institutions function in practice. **CF-16.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*) develops evidence and analysis for the review lifecycle, including case-specific work. Institutions must not use forensic support to silently replace structural monitoring, and must not use contest-integrity monitoring to quietly decide case-specific facts that belong in the [forum case record](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record).
