This file is the designated implementation text for the [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](../core_05.055_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-record) format.

*In plain terms: this is the inspectable status-file format — who or what was reviewed, what status was found or left contested, on what evidence, under which forum route, with what end-dates or review triggers, who represents the entity, how declined filings are logged, and how the case can be reopened. It does not decide who counts, and it is not a Standing Record.*

**Purpose and role.** This protocol specifies the Sentience-Status Adjudication Record used to document and review a status determination or contested posture. It connects the constitutional minimum fields to the machine-checkable format and the independent-representative and intake-decline interfaces.

This protocol implements, and does **not** narrow, **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*). The Rights Floor remains in Chapter Six. Process meaning remains in Chapter Five. Venue and minimum fields remain in the [Chapter Twenty-Four §3 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#3-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) (*Sentience-status adjudication* hook).

**Record format.** Machine-checkable form: [`implementation/schemas/sentience_status_adjudication_record.schema.json`](../implementation/schemas/sentience_status_adjudication_record.schema.json). Worked examples live under [`implementation/schemas/examples/`](../implementation/schemas/examples/). That schema is validating form, **not** who-counts and **not** a [Standing Record](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#standing-record).

When [Sentience Status Adjudication](../core_05.055_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) runs, the Sentience-Status Adjudication Record must state at least:

- the subject entity;
- the status determination or live contested posture (including affirmed, narrowed, revoked, restored, or still contested);
- the lead forum family and any Chapter Twenty-Three–warranted special route;
- the indicators and expert evidence relied on, with material uncertainty noted;
- the subject count where it was in question, the basis for it, and any branches treated as part of a subject, with the facts relied on (Chapter Five [*Sentient*](../core_05.055_band_participation.md#subject-count), *Subject Count*);
- interim [Contested-Sentient Life](../core_05.055_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) treatment while status remains live;
- the declared end-date or mandatory review trigger for this posture;
- any narrowing's declared expected-closure timeline and mandatory periodic-review trigger;
- the reopening evidence standard — new verified evidence, not calendar-only reopening;
- the independent representative — appointment path, conflict screen, and access terms under the [Chapter Twenty-Four §3 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#3-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) hook, or that the filing was declined before a case opened; and
- the intake-decline log — where declined filings are recorded, the published cadence on which **Integrity** samples them, and the published floor set of indicators. Each declined filing is logged with the indicator cited and the reason for decline.

**Out of scope.** This file does **not**:

- decide who counts as a sentient;
- open, update, or substitute for a [Standing Record](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#standing-record) or standing measurement;
- replace the whole [Forum Case Record](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record); or
- hold appointment schedules, filing-form libraries, or sequencing manuals — appointment mechanics are in [CF-23.410 §14](cf_23.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md#cf-23410-14-independent-representative-appointment) (*Independent Representative Appointment*); this file holds the appointment result fields and intake-decline log fields required by [Chapter Twenty-Four §3](../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#3-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) (*Escalation and Questions for Ruling*).
