This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-16.465** (*Standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts*).

*In plain terms: a Forum decision must remain checkable after it is made. This file gathers the minimum Forum documentation: what is captured, which fields it carries, who may see it, and how it is preserved.*

## CF-16.465 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file covers the forms, access rules, and evidence handling that keep forum decisions checkable.*

This file sets minimum Forum record forms, fields, access classes, evidence handling, retention, and non-compliant record patterns.

This section is the forum-operational home for standard forum records, forms, and evidence artifacts. It consolidates the record duties created in **CF-16.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*) through **CF-16.450** (*Forum continuity*), **CF-16.455** (*Fallback operation*), and **CF-16.460** (*Emergency adjudication*) and supplies a common minimum set for adopting entities. It does not create a second traceability or verification standard; traceability and verification discipline remain governed by `subcore_03.010_definition_structure.md` **Subcores 3-A to 3-C**, **Article XVII-A** (*Auditability and Observable Evidence*), and **CJS-R12** (*Standard forum records, forms, and evidence artifacts*). Where forum record systems, forms, or evidence artifacts are maintained by **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** systems, or materially affect those systems' portability, audit, continuity, migration, or cross-implementation operation, structured-record formats and exchange protocols must also satisfy **CJS-3.130 §17** (*interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*).

## CF-16.465 §2 Why Standard Forum Records Exist

*In plain terms: a forum decision must leave enough reliable information for someone to understand, review, and challenge it later.*

Forum decisions and the evidence behind them need to remain intelligible and available for review, correction, and continuity. Common fields and preserved evidence make it possible to follow who acted, under what authority, and what review remains, while access classes protect information that cannot lawfully be made public.

**Grounded in:**

Each adopting entity must maintain standard templates or equivalent structured records for at least:

- filing intake and routing decision;
- panel-formation record;
- member disclosure record;

- recusal challenge and decision;

- forensic or analytical support scope order under **CF-16.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*);
- restricted-evidence review order, naming the issuing body (the restricted information authority under **CF-16.415 §10** (*Restricted Information Authority*) or the appeal forum), the asserting party, and the time taken;
- investigative scope, referral, or closure record under **CF-16.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*) where the forum interface is used;
- technical-standard departure, variance, or recognition record under **CF-16.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*);
- backlog-threshold remediation record under **CF-16.445** (*Forum performance, backlog requirements, publication timeliness, and accessibility*);
- publication-delay log entry;
- continuity activation record;
- restoration and reconciliation record;
- post-incident review record; and
- [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](cf_16.475_sentience_status_record.md) when **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) status adjudication runs — format in that protocol, not restated here.

Adopting systems may combine compatible forms, but must not merge records in a way that hides who acted, what authority was used, what facts or constraints mattered, what review path remains, or which forum family is responsible.

## CF-16.465 §4 Required Fields

*In plain terms: What each Forum Case Record must carry — which body, which authority, when, who was responsible, and who was affected — so it remains interpretable years later.*

Each standard record must include, proportionate to the action:

- forum family, chamber or panel, and docket identity;
- authority invoked, including **Chapter Twenty-Two** routing basis where material;
- date, time, responsible role, and any approving or reviewing role;
- affected parties or affected class, with lawful anonymization where required;
- scope of action and duration where temporary or restricted;
- reasons, material uncertainty, and evidence relied on;
- conflict, recusal, or independence disclosures where material;
- publication status, restriction basis, and later publication or reconstruction path;
- challenge, appeal, question-for-ruling, or review path still available;
- retention class and audit-access handling.

## CF-16.465 §5 Access Classes

*In plain terms: Records are tagged by who may see them: the parties, the public, or restricted review only. Tagging is decided by design rather than case by case.*

Records must be tagged, at minimum, for the following access classes:

- **party-facing** — available to parties or affected sentients subject to lawful privacy, safety, and evidence-protection limits;
- **public-facing** — published or summarized where needed for routing intelligibility, precedent, shared standards, or governance trust;
- **audit-facing** — available for lawful audit, reconstruction, and **Article XVII-A** (*Auditability and Observable Evidence*) verification;
- **contest-integrity-facing** — available to [**CI-6.215 §3**](../corpus_institutions/ci_06.215_oversight_assurance_controls_evidence.md#ci-6215-3-contest-integrity-monitoring-class-a-and-class-b) (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*) monitors where contest-pathway performance, backlog, recusal, routing, or publication integrity is at issue;
- **external-assurance-facing** — available when **CI-6.215 §2** (*External Assurance Triggers*), **CI-16.210** (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*), or equivalent lawful assurance triggers apply;
- **restricted or sealed** — limited only as justified by lawful secrecy, safety, privacy, protected internal-state, or evidence-integrity constraints, with a deferred review or reconstruction path wherever feasible.

Access tagging must support meaningful review without converting lawful restriction into concealment. If a Forum Case Record is withheld or summarized, the forum must preserve enough attributable detail for later lawful reconstruction.

## CF-16.465 §6 Evidence Artifact Handling

*In plain terms: Evidence must keep its origin, handling history, and transformations attached, whether it is a physical object, a digital file, or a system log.*

Forum evidence artifacts must preserve chain of custody, source, method, transformation history, uncertainty, and access restrictions proportionate to impact and sensitivity under Chapter Three [Subcore 3-M — Evidence Preservation](../subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation). This applies to physical evidence, digital records, model or system logs, expert analyses, summaries of restricted evidence, forensic workpapers, and technical-standard materials.

Where an artifact is transformed, summarized, redacted, compressed, or represented through a derived exhibit, the Forum Case Record must preserve enough information to test reliability, contest material assumptions, and reconstruct the original evidentiary path where lawful.

### CF-16.465 §7.1 Retention Classes and Physical Backup

*In plain terms: every entry in a Forum Case Record is tagged routine or non-routine when it is made. When a matter closes, each non-routine entry gets a physical backup kept offline and apart from the forum's systems, and the most critical entries are kept in more than one region.*

The system tags each Forum Case Record entry with a retention class when the entry is made, as the **retention class** field of **CF-16.465 §4** (*Required Fields*) requires. The class is set by rule, not by an official's choice, under [Chapter Two §16.5 Automation](../core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#165-automation). It may be raised and never lowered, and each change is logged.

**Routine.** An entry is routine only if its loss would change no one's rights, remedy, or ability to contest or audit, and the forum can rebuild it in full from other preserved entries. Examples are acknowledgements, scheduling and reminder notices, status alerts, and duplicate routing logs.

**Non-routine.** Every other entry is non-routine, and doubt is resolved as non-routine. Whatever the tier, these are non-routine:

- a decision, order, or reasoned record that ends or shapes the merits of a matter;
- a matter that opens or corrects a [Subcore 3-J — Standing Record](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#standing-record), or that confirms, corrects, or revokes a System or Institutional Certification Record;
- a Forum Case Record entry with binding or precedential reach beyond the parties, such as a question for ruling, a constitutional holding, an alignment ruling, or a class-wide or structural remedy;
- an emergency, interim, hold, or restricting order, and any matter touching liberty, safety, or irreversible loss, including Existential Risk and Ecological Recovery Capacity;
- restricted-evidence matters and Forum Case Record entries about protected sentients;
- integrity matters: capture, recusal failure, backup routing, family-level capture, continuity activation, and capacity failure;
- Sentience-Status adjudication records;
- records made on paper or offline during degraded operation under **CF-16.465 §7** (*Publication, Retention, and Reconciliation*); and
- an entry recording an earlier disposition or closure while contest, appeal, or secondary review remains pending; the matter closes for physical backup only at final disposition with no review pending.

**Physical backup.** A physical backup is a durable copy held offline and apart from the forum's own systems, on paper or write-once offline media, so that a capture, outage, or ransomware event cannot reach it.
- **When:** at closing, which is final disposition with no review pending, the system makes one physical backup of each non-routine entry in the Forum Case Record. No one makes a separate request. If a matter reopens, the entries added later are backed up at the next closing.
- **Early copy:** If the forum's digital systems are impaired, or continuity is activated under **CF-16.450** (*Forum continuity*), before the matter closes, the non-routine entries made so far are backed up at once.
- **Tie to the digital copy:** Each physical backup carries a hash that ties it to the digital entry, and restoring from it is tested in the exercises under **CF-16.450 §10** (*Exercises and Testing*).
- **Where it is held:** The public part goes to the [Subcore 3-J — Constitutional Library](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#constitutional-library), and the restricted part goes to the [Subcore 3-J — Constitutional Archive](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#constitutional-archive), as **CF-16.465 §7.2** (*Constitutional Library and Constitutional Archive*) sets out.
- **On a move:** The retention class travels with the Forum Case Record on a transfer under **CF-16.415 §12** (*Transfer Procedure*), and the matter closes once, in its last lead forum.

**Essential Records.** The following records are [Subcore 3-J — Essential Records](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#essential-record), because the constitutional order could not be rebuilt without them:

- constitutional holdings and general doctrine, including alignment rulings that set doctrine and questions for ruling decided by **Constitutional** forums;
- forum decisions that confirm, correct, or revoke a System or Institutional Certification Record;
- Sentience-Status adjudication records and rulings on Existential Risk;
- capture, continuity-activation, restoration, and reconciliation records;
- the registers and indexes of the Constitutional Library and the Constitutional Archive, including the hashes that tie each physical backup to its digital entry;
- adoption records in the adoption chain and custody records required by [Chapter Twenty-Five §3.2 Custody Chain](../core_25.000_incorporation.md#32-custody-chain); and
- each [Subcore 3-J — Standing Record](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#standing-record) at slot 5 or above on the Contribution Axis or the Violation Axis, whose physical backup follows [Subcore 10-B §8.5 Physical Backup and Certified Copies](../subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#85-physical-backup-and-certified-copies).

An Essential Record's physical backup is held in at least three separate places in different regions, with different custodians, so that no one disaster, seizure, legal order, or capture can reach every copy. Where there is more than one adopter, adopters are encouraged to hold more copies by exchanging sealed copies with one another, and each exchange keeps the access limits that held the original.

### CF-16.465 §7.2 Constitutional Library and Constitutional Archive

*In plain terms: the Library is the public shelf and the Archive is the sealed shelf. Both are kept by someone outside the forum that made the entries. A sealed item leaves a visible marker unless a specific lawful order temporarily bars it.*

Each forum system keeps one [Subcore 3-J — Constitutional Library](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#constitutional-library) and one [Subcore 3-J — Constitutional Archive](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#constitutional-archive), under the access classes of **CF-16.465 §5** (*Access Classes*).
- **Library:** It holds every entry tagged public-facing. Entries are published when the action is taken, under **CF-16.445 §5.2** (*Publication Timeliness*). It also holds a status marker for each item in the Archive: the fact of action taken, the restriction basis, and the later publication or reconstruction path. Where a specific lawful order bars even the marker, the forum records the order and its basis, and the marker appears when the bar ends, under **CF-16.445 §5.2** (*Publication Timeliness*).
- **Archive:** It holds every entry and evidence artifact tagged restricted or sealed, whole and hashed. Access follows the published terms or a lawful order, under **CF-16.415 §10** (*Restricted Information Authority*), and the audit-facing, contest-integrity-facing, and external-assurance-facing access classes reach it as **CF-16.465 §5** (*Access Classes*) allows. Sealing never ends the review or reconstruction path, and an item is released to the Library when its restriction ends.
- **Custodian:** A custodian independent of the forum family that made the entries keeps each, with the separations in **CF-16.435 §6** (*Required Separations*). The forum family that made an entry may not choose, appoint, fund, or instruct the custodian that holds it.
- **Physical backups:** The Library holds the physical backups of public non-routine entries, and the Archive holds the physical backups of restricted non-routine entries, each sealed and hashed.
- **No second system:** The Library and the Archive do not replace the forum's own dockets. They are where the closed and non-routine entries are kept safe and findable.

## CF-16.465 §8 Non-Compliant Record Patterns

*In plain terms: The failures that make later review impossible: action with no attributable Forum Case Record, records omitting the review path, and forms that obscure how a matter was routed.*

The following patterns are non-compliant:

- material forum action with no attributable Forum Case Record;
- records that omit review path or publication status for rights-affecting actions;
- form designs that obscure forum-family routing or backup activation;
- restricted-evidence handling that prevents lawful later reconstruction;
- emergency or continuity records that cannot be reconciled to ordinary dockets;
- private or vendor-controlled evidence artifacts that cannot be audited or contested within lawful limits;
- closing a matter without the physical backup of each non-routine entry, or classing a non-routine entry as routine to avoid it;
- keeping every copy of an Essential Record in one place or under one control; and
- sealing an item in the Constitutional Archive without leaving its marker in the Constitutional Library, except while a specific lawful order bars the marker under **CF-16.445 §5.2** (*Publication Timeliness*).
