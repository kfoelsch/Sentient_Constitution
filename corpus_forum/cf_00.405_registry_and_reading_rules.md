## CF-0.405 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this annex explains forum identifiers and helps readers find the applicable implementation files.*

This annex explains Forum identifiers, points to the stable family list, and directs integrators to the applicable cross-layer owners and read-with rules.

## CF-0.405 §2 Why the Forum Registry Exists

*In plain terms: stable labels and routing pointers help a reader find the right forum rules and their constitutional homes.*

A forum implementation layer spans many files and cross-layer interfaces. Consistent identifiers and a family map help readers follow those connections, so that the operational paths supporting challenge and remedy can be located without treating a label or routing pointer as a separate source of authority.
