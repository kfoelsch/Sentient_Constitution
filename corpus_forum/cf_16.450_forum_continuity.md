Core home: 16
This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-16.450** (*Forum continuity*).

*In plain terms: forums need a way to keep working through emergencies, missing staff, panel failures, or infrastructure outages. Procedure may be simplified, but contestability cannot be extinguished.*

## CF-16.450 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file covers how forums continue essential functions when ordinary operation is impaired.*

This file governs Forum continuity modes, activation, minimum functions, custody, communications, coordination, and exercises.

This section governs forum continuity when ordinary forum operation is materially impaired by:

- emergency conditions;
- incapacity of key personnel;
- inability to form lawful panels;
- deadlock across adjudicative bodies;
- capture or compromise indicators, including family-level capture under **CF-16.425 §5.8** (*Family-Level Capture Activation*);
- network or infrastructure disruption that impairs constitutional adjudication.

It governs continuity of adjudication. Fallback substitutions are governed by **CF-16.455** (*Fallback operation*), and emergency merits handling, review, restoration, and prohibited emergency patterns are governed by **CF-16.460** (*Emergency adjudication*). This section does not authorize permanent alteration of constitutional forum-family boundaries.

Where forum continuity materially depends on constitutional systems, this section must be read with [**CS-4.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-4345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*) on governance continuity, crisis communications, and exercises. CS-4.345 (*System lifecycle*) states the shared emergency lifecycle, crisis-communication, exercise, restoration-evidence, and audit-trail mechanics; **CF-16.450** (*Forum continuity*) supplies the forum-specific continuity modes, activation triggers, minimum functions, record continuity, communications, coordination, and exercise requirements. **CF-16.455** (*Fallback operation*) and **CF-16.460** (*Emergency adjudication*) supply fallback-operation and emergency-adjudication requirements.

## CF-16.450 §2 Why Forum Continuity Exists

*In plain terms: when a forum is disrupted, urgent relief and the ability to challenge decisions still need a way to continue.*

Outages, missing staff, compromised authority, and other disruptions can interrupt adjudication just when sentients need it. Continuity arrangements preserve essential forum functions and the means to review decisions while ordinary operation is impaired, with a path back to ordinary operation.

**Grounded in:**

- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§8 Continuity Aim: Introduction](../core_02.010_values_principles_continuity_aim.md#8-continuity-aim-introduction).
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XXVII-C: Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor](../core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor).

## CF-16.450 §3 Rights-Floor Continuity Rule

*In plain terms: Emergencies may simplify procedure; they may never erase the right to contest, the route to urgent relief, or the Forum Case Record that makes review possible.*

Emergency forum operation must preserve, to the greatest feasible extent:

- baseline challenge rights;
- access to emergency relief;
- reviewability of coercive or materially rights-affecting action;
- auditability of emergency decisions;
- a path back to ordinary independent adjudication.

Emergency simplification must not become a pretext to extinguish contestability.

## CF-16.450 §4 Continuity Modes

*In plain terms: The named states a forum can be in, from Normal through Emergency and Fallback to Restoration. Moving between them must be triggered, recorded, and reversible.*

Each adopting entity must publish at least these forum continuity modes:

- Normal
- Degraded
- Emergency limited-capacity
- Fallback / backup-forum operation
- Family-capture continuity
- Restoration and reconciliation

Mode transitions must be trigger-defined, attributable, auditable, and reversible when triggering conditions clear.

## CF-16.450 §5 Activation Triggers

*In plain terms: A continuity mode switches on only from a documented finding — no lawful panel can be formed, infrastructure is down, key staff are unavailable — not from general unease.*

Continuity mode may activate only on documented findings such as:

- inability to constitute lawful independent panels at ordinary scale;
- physical or digital infrastructure outage materially impairing filing, record access, hearing, or publication;
- emergency incapacity or absence of required judicial or administrative personnel;
- credible capture or compromise indicators affecting ordinary routing or decision security;
- family-level capture activation under **CF-16.425 §5.8** (*Family-Level Capture Activation*);
- overlapping crisis conditions that make ordinary scheduling constitutionally inadequate for emergency matters.

Activation records must state:

- the trigger condition;
- affected forum family or families;
- expected scope and duration;
- what rights-protective functions must continue without interruption;
- who authorized activation;
- when independent review will occur.

Shared expiry, reauthorization, restoration, and closure-evidence requirements follow [**CS-4.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-4345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*), where constitutional-system continuity is implicated. Forum activation records add the **Chapter Sixteen** routing and family-specific adjudication details listed here.

## CF-16.450 §6 Continuity Minimum Functions

*In plain terms: The minimum that survives any degraded mode: urgent intake, emergency relief, enough triage to spot Rights-Floor cases, and preservation of the Forum Case Record.*

In any degraded or emergency mode, the system must preserve at minimum:

- intake for urgent matters;
- emergency interim-relief authority;
- routing or triage sufficient to identify Rights-Floor, [Evidence Preservation](../subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and safety-critical cases;
- record preservation and chain of custody under [Evidence Preservation](../subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation);
- a way to challenge emergency orders;
- a path to restore ordinary forum review when feasible.

## CF-16.450 §7 Record Continuity and Custody

*In plain terms: Even in emergency operation the docket must stay identifiable and the evidence chain unbroken, so the period can be reconstructed afterwards.*

Forum emergency adjudication requires continuous preservation of:

- docket identity;
- filings and timestamps;
- panel-formation and recusal records;
- evidence custody records under [Evidence Preservation](../subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation);
- emergency orders and reasons;
- publication-delay records;
- restoration and rollback decisions.

Where digital systems are impaired, institutions must maintain a lawful fallback for record capture and later reconciliation. If digital systems are impaired or continuity is activated before the matter closes, non-routine entries made so far are also backed up in physical form at once, under [CF-16.465 §7.1 Retention Classes and Physical Backup](cf_16.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-16465-71-retention-classes-and-physical-backup). Generic crisis audit-trail, communication-log, and closure-evidence requirements remain in [**CS-4.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-4345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*); this subsection identifies the forum records that cannot be lost or silently merged during emergency operation.

Long-term archival and retrieval systems that materially support record access, restoration, precedent or publication continuity, audit, challenge, or lawful reconstruction must be treated as forum-continuity dependencies under **CF-16.465 §7** (*Publication, Retention, and Reconciliation*) and classified under [**CS-8.305**](../corpus_systems/cs_08.305_system_classification_machinery.md#cs-8305-system-classification-machinery) (*System classification machinery*) according to their continuity function.

## CF-16.450 §8 Communication Duties

*In plain terms: Parties must be told which mode is active, what still works, and what has been suspended. Silence during a crisis is itself a failure.*

During continuity-mode operation, forum-facing communications must apply [**CS-4.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-4345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*), crisis-communication discipline and [Chapter Two §16.4 Document at Action](../core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#164-document-at-action). The activation record under **CF-16.450 §5** (*Activation Triggers*) is made by the activation itself. The adjudication-specific facts parties need are published or otherwise communicated from that Forum Case Record when the mode takes effect, except for any part a lawful restriction holds back, and a held-back part still leaves a visible marker unless a specific lawful order bars it (**CF-16.445 §5.2** (*Publication timeliness*)). Where an order bars the marker, the forum records the order and its basis, and the marker appears when the bar ends. Where digital systems are impaired, the lawful fallback under **CF-16.450 §7** (*Record Continuity and Custody*) carries both the Forum Case Record and the communication. The facts are:

- the active mode;
- what functions remain available;
- where urgent filings should go;
- what publication or hearing delays to expect;
- how parties can seek emergency review;
- when the next continuity-status review will occur.

## CF-16.450 §9 Cross-System and Cross-Institution Coordination

*In plain terms: When several bodies are affected at once, the plan must name who decides, how evidence is shared, who breaks a deadlock, and in what order normal operation resumes.*

Where multiple institutions or forum families are affected, the continuity protocol must define:

- accountability for the continuity decision;
- evidence-sharing boundaries;
- transfer authority;
- deadlock-breaker path;
- conditions for external participation or assurance;
- restoration sequencing across affected bodies.

## CF-16.450 §10 Exercises and Testing

*In plain terms: Continuity plans must be rehearsed against realistic scenarios — no independent panel available, a backlog surge, an outage — because an untested plan is an assumption.*

Forum continuity protocols must participate in the exercise cadence required by [**CS-4.345**](../corpus_systems/cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-4345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*), where constitutional-system continuity is implicated. Forum-specific exercise coverage should include at least:

- inability to form an independent panel;
- major backlog surge;
- digital outage affecting filings or records;
- emergency relief under reduced staffing;
- capture-sensitive routing and backup activation;
- family-level capture activation and restoration.

Findings must be recorded and tied to remediation under **CS-4.345** (*System lifecycle*) **§8** (*Governance continuity, crisis communications, and exercises*), with docket, routing, quorum, and backup-forum failures separately identified.
