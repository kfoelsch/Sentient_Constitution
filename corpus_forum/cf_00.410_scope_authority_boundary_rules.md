This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-0.410** (*Scope, authority, and boundary rules*).

*In plain terms: **CF** is the forums operations rulebook — how panels form, cases route, appeals work, and hearings stay independent under stress.*

## CF-0.410 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file explains what the forum implementation layer covers and where its authority ends.*

This file states the Forum layer’s operational scope, the subjects that remain with the core or other layers, and the limits on its authority.

## CF-0.410 §2 Why the Forum-Layer Boundary Exists

*In plain terms: forum procedures need a clear home without changing the constitutional rules or another layer’s work.*

Forum operations depend on constitutional rights, definitions, and family authority as well as shared and local implementation rules. A visible boundary helps readers combine the applicable requirements while keeping constitutional meaning and jurisdiction in their established homes.

**Grounded in:**

- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§15.4.4 Combined Satisfaction of Jointly Applicable Incorporated Obligations](../core_02.030_constitutional_interpretation.md#1544-combined-satisfaction-of-jointly-applicable-incorporated-obligations).
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XIV-B: Right to Redress and Remedy](../core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy).

## CF-0.410 §3 Scope and Authority Boundaries

**What this layer owns**

- forum formation, chamber structure, panel formation, disclosure, and recusal (**CF-22.405** (*Forum formation, forum-structure mapping, and chamber structure*), **CF-22.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*));
- routing, transfer, questions for ruling, appeal, and exhaustion operations (**CF-22.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*), **CF-22.420** (*Appeal, secondary review, and exhaustion pathways*));
- integrity, anti-capture, and anti-self-judging support (**CF-22.425** (*Integrity safeguards, anti-capture operations, and anti-self-judging support*));
- forensic support, investigative interfaces, and specialist chambers (**CF-22.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*) through **CF-22.440** (*Technical specialist forums and specialist chambers*));
- performance, continuity, fallback, emergency adjudication, records, and staffing (**CF-22.445** (*Forum performance, backlog requirements, publication timeliness, and accessibility*) through **CF-22.470** (*Forum staffing, reserve capacity, shared administration, structural review, and structural records*)); and
- the dedicated [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](cf_22.475_sentience_status_record.md) format listed in [Chapter Thirty](../core_30.000_incorporation.md#chapter-thirty-incorporation-bridge), which implements and does not narrow **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*).

**What this layer does not own**

- which forum family decides which primary question, and why — that remains in Chapter Twenty-Two;
- constitutional term meaning and O/M/A/C definition mechanics — those remain in the Sentient Constitution (Chapter Three, including its subcore);
- joint operational definitions, and joint cross-layer interfaces — those belong to **CJS** (reusable joint operational definitions in **CJS-3.105 (*Operational cluster library (oDef)*) through CJS-3.135 (*Integrative cross-leg operations (oDef.22–oDef.23)*)**);
- Rights Floors — those remain in Chapter Four;
- system-class and steward taxonomies — those belong to **CS**;
- institutional formation, authorization, oversight, correction, and dissolution — those belong to **CI**.

This layer must **not**:

- create new forum families beyond those authorized in Chapter Twenty-Two;
- silently expand constitutional-forum jurisdiction beyond Chapter Twenty-Two;
- replace general constitutional due-process, traceability, or rights rules with weaker local procedure;
- create a parallel offense taxonomy.
