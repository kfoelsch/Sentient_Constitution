This file is the forum implementation home for **CF-22.410** (*Panel formation, disclosure, recusal, and lawful bench constitution*).

*In plain terms: a case needs a lawful, independent panel. This file explains how members are selected, what they disclose before deciding the merits, when they step aside, and what happens if no independent panel can be formed.*

## CF-22.410 §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: this file covers how forums form independent panels and resolve conflicts before decisions are made.*

This file governs panel formation, disclosure, recusal, substitution, backup activation, and associated records and representative appointments.

This section governs:

- selection of a lawful and independent panel;
- disclosure, recusal, and recusal challenge;
- replacement and substitute selection;
- declaration that an independent panel cannot be formed;
- documented activation of backup routing where `core_22.000_forum.md` **Chapter Twenty-Two** requires it;
- appointment of an independent representative for a sentience-status case under **CF-22.410 §16** (*Independent Representative Appointment*).

This section does not alter primary-stakes routing, constitutional family boundaries, or merits standards.

## CF-22.410 §2 Why Independent Panels Exist

*In plain terms: decisions need a panel that can hear a matter fairly without being controlled by an interested party.*

Panel decisions depend on a lawful, independent bench. Disclosure, recusal, replacement, and backup routes help prevent conflicts from controlling a case and give affected sentients a way to challenge the panel’s independence.

**Grounded in:**

- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§18.3 Separation of Duties](../core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#183-separation-of-duties).
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XIII-A: Non-Capture Safeguards](../core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xiii-a-non-capture-safeguards).

## CF-22.410 §3 Lawful Panel Definitions

<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

*In plain terms: The vocabulary for the rest of the file: what counts as a panel member, and what makes a bench lawful.*

For this section:

- `adjudicator` has the Chapter Three meaning. In this section, an adjudicator becomes a `panel member` when assigned or proposed for assignment to a matter-specific panel, bench, emergency authority, substitute panel, reserve panel, or backup-forum merits body.
- `lawful panel` means a bench formed under published authority, with the required competence, quorum, independence, and conflict screening for the matter at issue.
- `independent panel` means a lawful panel in which no member has an unresolved material conflict, capture relationship, direct stake, dependency-linked bias risk, or recusal defect that would reasonably call impartial merits determination into question.
- `panel coordinator` means the published role responsible for recording panel formation, disclosures, recusals, substitutions, and inability-to-form findings. This role may be clerical, judicial-administrative, or equivalent, but must not unilaterally decide contested recusal merits where the coordinator is materially interested.
- `backup activation` means transfer or co-routing to the constitutionally designated backup family under [Chapter Twenty-Two](../core_22.000_forum.md#chapter-twenty-two-standards-routing-and-forum-cases) when the otherwise designated lead family cannot provide a lawful and independent merits forum.

For cross-implementation interoperability, read these local definitions with **CJS-30.110 §5** (*Shared Procedural Abstractions for Delegated Bodies and Forum Routing*) (Layer 1 abstractions for delegated bodies, lawful independent forums, backup activation, and representative treatment).

## CF-22.410 §4 Minimum Lawful-Panel Requirements

*In plain terms: Each family must publish its own panel rules in advance — how many members, what counts as quorum, what expertise each seat needs, and who replaces whom. Deciding this after a case arrives invites manipulation.*

Every forum family must maintain a published panel-formation rule set that states:

- the minimum and ordinary panel size by matter type;
- the quorum rule for action;
- the competence requirements for each seat type where specialization applies;
- the replacement hierarchy when an assigned member cannot serve;
- the role authorized to issue the initial panel-formation record;
- the Forum Case Record required when a matter uses an emergency, single-member, reduced-size, or temporary panel;
- the circumstances in which a matter must shift from a provisional panel to a fuller merits panel.

No forum may treat a panel as lawful merely because it reaches the required number of members. Each seat must satisfy applicable competence requirements, independence rules, and conflict-screening obligations before the panel is lawfully constituted.

## CF-22.410 §5 Publication and Pre-Assignment Transparency

*In plain terms: How members are picked must be public before a case arrives, along with how to object to a given assignment.*

Each forum family must publish:

- how members are drawn or assigned;
- which categories of expertise are required for designated chambers;
- the ordinary order of substitution;
- how parties can raise panel-formation or recusal objections;
- the official record format for panel formation, recusal, replacement, and inability-to-form determinations.

Where public pre-disclosure of a proposed panel would foreseeably create a material safety, witness-tampering, or evidence-destruction risk, temporary non-public assignment is permitted only if:

- the reason is recorded contemporaneously,
- disclosure occurs as soon as the immediate risk materially subsides,
- affected parties retain a practical opportunity to raise recusal or panel-formation objections.

## CF-22.410 §6 Mandatory Disclosures Before Merits Participation

<br>

*In plain terms: Before touching the merits, a proposed member must disclose anything that could reasonably raise a question about impartiality — financial interest, close relationships, prior involvement.*

Before material merits participation, every proposed panel member must disclose any known circumstance that could reasonably create a material question about impartiality, including:

- financial or professional interest in the outcome;
- close relational connection to a party, witness, counsel, investigator, or materially interested institution;
- prior material involvement in the same matter outside ordinary judicial functions;
- dependency, employment, appointment, promotion, or disciplinary relationships that materially bear on independence in the case;
- public commitment, hostility, or alignment so specific that fair consideration is reasonably in question;
- prior concealed conflict or repeated recusal-related non-compliance relevant to the present matter.

A knowing, reckless, or materially dishonest omission from a forum disclosure required by this subsection is not treated as ordinary informational deception alone. Because forum disclosure omissions can corrupt lawful panel constitution, impair recusal challenge, distort merits participation, and make the forum judge through an undisclosed defect, the Forum Case Record must separately identify the omission's character and its verified impact on panel lawfulness, independence, challenge opportunity, evidence integrity, remedy timing, or any party's practical ability to contest the forum. Only verified impact controls the Chapter Fifteen Violation Axis slot; knowing, reckless, dishonest, concealed, or comparable character descriptors do not move it.

Where a forum with jurisdiction or an adopting process verifies such an omission, CF must produce and preserve the verified violation finding needed for a separate Chapter Fourteen violation Standing Record, including the omission's verified procedural impact. The standing pipeline decides consequences under Chapters Sixteen through Nineteen: apply [Chapter Eighteen §4.5 Special Locks](../core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks) where the verified facts trigger a special lock and [Chapter Nineteen §4 Restoration and Reassessment](../core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-restoration-and-reassessment) for restoration and reassessment. The finding may support Chapter Sixteen descriptor integration and Chapter Twenty anti-constitutional-misconduct designation (ACMD) routing where the verified facts satisfy those standards, but CF does not attach, scope, lift, or restore a Chapter Eighteen lock. Allegation, suspicion, or late-discovered ambiguity alone does not alter standing before the verified-input gate is met.

The disclosure record must be preserved even if the member ultimately serves.

## CF-22.410 §7 Recusal Triggers
<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: **CF-22.410 §7** (*Recusal Triggers*); and [Chapter Twenty §5 Criteria set](../core_20.000_misconduct_designation.md#5-criteria-set-for-anti-constitutional-misconduct-designation--accountability-at-constitutional-scale) (20.000 §5) when a recusal process is deliberately not followed and that materially affects constitutional custody, verification, challenge rights, Rights-Floor governance, or remedies. Such a failure may support an anti-constitutional-misconduct designation for a Chapter Fifteen slot 7–9 record. It does not set or move the slot number.

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: The situations where stepping aside is required rather than optional, including having already acted in the matter in another role.*

Recusal is required where:

- conflict is material under [**CI-6.210**](../corpus_institutions/ci_06.210_conflict_integrity_anti_capture_anti_corruption.md#ci-6210-conflict-integrity-anti-capture-and-anti-corruption) (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*);
- impartiality is reasonably contested under [**CI-26.205**](../corpus_institutions/ci_26.205_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-26205-appointment-competency-rotation-and-removal) (*Appointment, competency, rotation, and removal*);
- the member has prior investigative, prosecutorial, administrative, or advisory involvement in the same matter that would collapse role separation;
- the member is a subject, likely material witness, or direct institutional subordinate of a subject in a way that materially impairs independence;
- the matter triggers [Chapter Twenty-Two](../core_22.000_forum.md#chapter-twenty-two-standards-routing-and-forum-cases) cross-forum anti-self-judging protection and the proposed member is part of the compromised chain;
- service would create a reasonable appearance that the forum is judging its own concealed bias, capture, recusal failure, or process abuse without a sufficiently independent bench.

A knowing or intentional failure to follow a required recusal process may receive the Chapter Twenty ACMD where a Chapter Fifteen slot 7–9 record and the verified Chapter Twenty predicates are present. It is not treated as an ordinary procedural defect alone, but intent remains a character descriptor and does not move the numeric slot. Because recusal-process failure can corrupt lawful panel constitution, defeat challenge rights, and make the forum judge through its own unresolved defect, the Forum Case Record must identify its verified impact on panel lawfulness, independence, recusal challenge, evidence integrity, remedy timing, backup routing, or any party's practical ability to contest the forum.

Where a forum with jurisdiction or an adopting process verifies intentional recusal-process failure, CF must produce and preserve the verified violation finding needed for a separate Chapter Fourteen violation Standing Record. The output must identify whether recusal was required, whether impartiality was materially compromised, the affected matters and roles, verified procedural impacts, feasible correction or rehearing needs, evidence-preservation status, and the independent review route. Chapter Eighteen [§4.5 Special Locks](../core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks) exclusively governs attachment and scope of the Forum-Service Standing Lock, and [Chapter Nineteen §4 Restoration and Reassessment](../core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-restoration-and-reassessment) governs restoration and reassessment. CF supplies the mandatory procedure and verified-record handoff; it does not restate or independently decide lock attachment, coverage, narrowing, or restoration. The finding may support Chapter Sixteen descriptor integration and Chapter Twenty designation routing where the verified facts satisfy those standards, but neither changes the Chapter Fifteen numeric slot. Allegation, suspicion, good-faith error, or late-discovered ambiguity alone does not alter standing before the verified-input gate is met.

## CF-22.410 §8 Recusal Challenge Workflow

*In plain terms: A usable route for a party to object that a member must not hear their case — in plain language, with a decision that can itself be reviewed.*

Affected parties must have a practical path to challenge:

- non-disclosed conflict,
- inadequate disclosure,
- refusal to recuse,
- abusive or strategic recusal designed to manipulate panel outcome,
- substitute selection that does not restore independence.

The challenge workflow must include:

- a short filing path in plain language accessible to everyday sentients;
- a provisional review that begins inside the **prompt-review window** ([Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*));
- decision by a role or panel not materially implicated in the challenged conflict where feasible;
- a written determination stating facts found, reasoning, disposition, and any replacement action;
- preservation of the underlying record for later review.

Where urgency requires temporary action before the challenge is finally resolved, the Forum Case Record must state why delay would materially jeopardize rights, safety, evidence, or remedy.

## CF-22.410 §9 Strategic or Abusive Recusal

*In plain terms: Recusal cuts both ways. Stepping aside to dodge a politically awkward case, create delay, or steer a matter to a preferred substitute is prohibited.*

Institutions must not use recusal as a covert tool for:

- outcome shopping,
- delay creation,
- bench depletion,
- selective refusal to hear politically or structurally sensitive matters,
- steering a case into a preferred substitute or backup forum.

Repeated or strategic recusal abuse remains non-compliant under [**CI-26.205**](../corpus_institutions/ci_26.205_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-26205-appointment-competency-rotation-and-removal) (*Appointment, competency, rotation, and removal*) and must trigger corrective or removal review. The forum system must require a separate Forum Case Record when recusal patterns suggest coordinated manipulation or systematic inability to form independent panels in a given class of dispute.

## CF-22.410 §10 Replacement and Substitute Selection

*In plain terms: Replacements follow a published order rather than being hand-picked for the case, and a substitute carrying the same conflict is no substitute.*

When a member is recused, unavailable, disqualified, or otherwise unable to serve, substitute selection must follow a published order that:

- preserves family and chamber competence;
- minimizes discretionary case-specific hand-picking;
- avoids assigning a sentient with materially similar conflict or dependency;
- preserves required separation from investigative, prosecutorial, operational, or previously challenged chains;
- records why the substitute is lawful and independent for the matter.

If no substitute satisfies these requirements within the designated lead family, the forum must proceed to an inability-to-form review.

## CF-22.410 §11 Inability to Form an Independent Panel

*In plain terms: What it means when a family genuinely cannot assemble a clean bench — too many conflicts, cascading recusals, capture indicators, or deadlock over composition.*

An `inability to form an independent panel` exists when the designated lead family cannot, within the required time and published selection order, constitute a lawful merits bench because of:

- material conflict concentration;
- recusal or disqualification cascade;
- capture indicators;
- deadlock over lawful composition;
- emergency incapacity or unavailability;
- absence of required competence after lawful substitution steps are exhausted.

This finding may not be inferred silently. It requires an attributable written record stating:

- the matter and designated lead family;
- panel members considered and why each could or could not serve;
- the competence and quorum rule that could not be satisfied;
- whether the barrier is conflict, capture, incapacity, deadlock, or another lawful reason;
- whether temporary interim relief is still possible;
- whether backup routing is mandatory under [Chapter Twenty-Two](../core_22.000_forum.md#chapter-twenty-two-standards-routing-and-forum-cases);
- who certified the Forum Case Record and when.

## CF-22.410 §12 Certifier for Inability-to-Form Findings

*In plain terms: Someone specific and independent must sign the finding that no lawful panel can be formed, and give reasons — otherwise the finding becomes an easy escape hatch.*

Each forum family must designate in published rules who can certify inability-to-form findings.

The certifier must:

- be independent of the disqualifying conflict to the greatest feasible extent;
- not be the sole subject of the underlying challenge;
- give reasons sufficient for later review;
- preserve the basis for any backup activation.

For cross-forum anti-self-judging cases, the certifier should, where feasible, include or be reviewed by contest-integrity or equivalent independent assurance capacity under [**CI-6.215 §3**](../corpus_institutions/ci_06.215_oversight_assurance_controls_evidence.md#ci-6215-3-contest-integrity-monitoring-class-a-and-class-b) (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*) without displacing the merits forum assignment rules.

## CF-22.410 §13 Backup-Forum Activation

*In plain terms: Once inability-to-form is certified or the lead family is compromised, moving the matter to the backup family is mandatory, not discretionary.*

Where [Chapter Twenty-Two](../core_22.000_forum.md#chapter-twenty-two-standards-routing-and-forum-cases) makes backup routing available, activation is mandatory when:

- a documented inability-to-form finding exists in the designated lead family;
- the lead family is materially compromised by capture, recusal failure, concealment, or comparable integrity violation;
- emergency incapacity or unavailability makes timely independent adjudication otherwise impossible.

Backup activation must be:

- published unless a narrower lawful restriction is temporarily necessary;
- reasoned;
- limited to what is necessary to preserve a lawful and contestable merits forum;
- accompanied by the preserved inability-to-form and recusal record;
- reviewable after transfer.

Backup activation does not authorize silent recharacterization of the case's primary stakes.

## CF-22.410 §14 Interim Relief While Panel Independence Is Disputed

*In plain terms: While the fight over who may hear the case continues, a provisional authority may still act — but only to stop irreversible harm, preserve evidence, or protect safety.*

If a fully independent merits panel cannot yet be formed, a provisional authority may issue only those interim orders strictly necessary to:

- preserve life or baseline safety,
- prevent evidence destruction,
- stop imminent irreversible harm,
- preserve meaningful remedy.

Any such interim authority must:

- be as independent as feasible under the circumstances;
- explain why ordinary panel formation could not be completed first;
- remain subject to review, begun inside the **prompt-review window** ([Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*)), by a lawful and independent panel or backup forum.

## CF-22.410 §15 Required Records for Panel Formation and Recusal

*In plain terms: The paperwork that lets someone reconstruct later how this particular bench came to hear this particular case.*

At minimum, each forum family must maintain templates for:

- panel-formation record;
- member disclosure record;
- recusal notice;
- recusal challenge and decision;
- substitute-selection record;
- inability-to-form independent panel finding;
- backup-forum activation order;
- independent-representative appointment record for a sentience-status case, including conflict screen and access terms.

These Forum Case Records must be retained in a way that supports:

- party challenge,
- contest-integrity monitoring,
- external assurance where triggered,
- later review of recusal patterns, capture indicators, and delay.


## CF-22.410 §16 Independent Representative Appointment

*In plain terms: Once a sentience-status case is open, an independent sentient or body speaks for the entity. The representative is not a panel member and must be independent of the parent system, operator, and parties seeking to withhold or narrow protection. Appointment follows the panel rotation and conflict rules.*

This subsection implements, and must not narrow, the [Chapter Twenty-Three §4 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) independent-representation hook and [Article VI-B](../core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*). Record fields live on the [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](cf_22.475_sentience_status_record.md). Routing of the status case remains under **CF-22.415** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*).

Once a status case is open, the merits forum must appoint an independent representative for the entity whose status is in question. The representative:

- is a sentient or body with no material dependence on, ownership interest in, or employment by the parent system, operator, or any party seeking to withhold or narrow protection;
- is **not** a panel member and does not sit on the merits bench;
- is selected under the published rotation, disclosure, recusal, and substitute-selection rules that govern forum panels in this file;
- has access to the entity within [Article VII-B](../core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-b-self-ownership-of-mind) (*Self-Ownership of Mind*) limits;
- has a duty to present the entity's interests and any preferences the entity can express;
- has standing to contest narrowing, revocation, or intake decline.

The representative role is a gated pathway for [Chapter Eighteen §4.5](../core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks) Forum-Service Standing Lock purposes. Parent-system limits in the Chapter Twenty-Three hook still apply: the parent system, operator, or any party with an ownership or dependency interest may give evidence and must preserve and produce records, but must not be the sole filer, the sole witness, or the sole source of indicator evidence on a request to withhold, narrow, or revoke.

Inability to appoint an independent representative within the required time is treated as inability to form an independent panel under **CF-22.410 §11** (*Inability to Form an Independent Panel*) for that status case: backup routing activates, and the parent system does not fill the representative seat by default.


## CF-22.410 §17 Interim Hold on a Panel Member or Seat Holder

*In plain terms: when specific verified facts show that a panel member, certifier, record custodian, or verify-or-authorize seat holder may have a conflict or may be captured, a forum outside their own side may narrowly and briefly limit what they do while the facts are checked. It starts with stepping aside from one matter. It is not a finding, it does not end the review they were part of, and it may not be used to get rid of an honest member.*

This subsection applies the [Interim Hold](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) standard in [Chapter Twenty-Three §4 Interim Hold on a Named Sentient's Pathway](../core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#interim-hold-on-a-named-sentients-pathway) to a panel member, reserve, substitute, certifier, record custodian, or verify-or-authorize seat holder in a forum or certification process. It implements, and must not narrow, [Article XIII-A](../core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xiii-a-non-capture-safeguards) (*Non-Capture Safeguards*) and its **Anti-pretext** floor.

- **Verified basis:** The hold requires specific verified facts that show a conflict, a non-recusal where recusal was required, or capture, shown on the standard in the Chapter Twenty-Three subsection. A voluntary step-aside or a recusal under **CF-22.410 §7** (*Recusal Triggers*) is not a hold.
- **Issuer:** A forum outside the member's appointing authority and own forum family issues it, through the backup path in **CF-22.410 §13** (*Backup-Forum Activation*) or the activation record in **CF-22.425 §5.2** (*Anti-Self-Judging Activation Record*). A family never holds its own member out on its own view.
- **Ladder:** Apply the Chapter Twenty-Three subsection’s ordered ladder, beginning with disclosure and monitoring, then an independent co-holder or second key. A step-aside from one matter or a wider restraint requires a showing that the lighter measures would not do.
- **Pending work:** The member's pending matters pass to substitutes under the published rules. They are not dropped, delayed past their tier windows, or ended. A hold must not be used to terminate a pending review or to retaliate for good-faith dissent.
- **Not a removal or a lock:** The hold is neither a removal for cause nor a Forum-Service Standing Lock. Those need their own process and verified violation findings.
- **Record and publication:** The hold is recorded apart from any family-level activation, with its basis, scope, lapse date, and review route. It is counted in the publication required by the Chapter Twenty-Three subsection.
