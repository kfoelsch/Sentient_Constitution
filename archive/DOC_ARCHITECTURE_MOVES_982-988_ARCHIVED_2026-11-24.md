Chapter Twenty-Nine’s scope and traces and the references in Chapter Twenty-Eight and the Authority Stack entry are aligned.

**Nine hundred and eighty-seventh move (place the amendment procedure before its tests; 2026-11-24; structural and citation alignment; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (existing uncommitted work; custodian authorized inclusion in the merge): Chapter Twenty-Nine places the amendment procedure at §3, followed by heightened review and the justification burden, Procedural Tests 2–4, review and remediation, and adoption. The amendment procedure and substantive tests remain in force; see also moves 982–984 for subsequent changes to the hearing gate and owner non-relocation section.

**Nine hundred and eighty-eighth move (publication integrity and independent amendment evaluation; 2026-11-24; rules and definitions; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (existing uncommitted work; custodian authorized inclusion in the merge): add [Publication Integrity](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#publication-integrity), governing publication of proposals for materially binding acts where the deciding process is open to response, including content, timing, recorded changes, and response opportunities. Chapter Twenty-Nine §6 applies it to amendments; related Record Integrity and Contestability entries and measurement routing are aligned. [Subcore 7-B §4.7](../subcore_07.020_public_policy_office.md#47-proposed-amendments) provides for independent Public Policy Office evaluation of amendments meeting the materiality threshold or requested for evaluation; a restriction or condition on a Chapter Four right always meets the threshold. The proposal's justification serves as the sponsor's statement; the office evaluates evidence and constitutional alignment against the text the amendment would leave in force, while validity remains for the constitutional decision-makers. Chapter Twenty-Nine §5 and Subcore 7-B §6 connect the evaluation and publication record. These additions impose the stated publication and evaluation duties; they are not merely wording changes.
