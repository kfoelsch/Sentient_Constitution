**Eight hundred and seventy-seventh move (Public Stewardship Office draft gains a Stewardship Fund; 2026-11-21; draft only under `project/plans/`, no binding text changed; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Request (custodian, in the project thread: "one thing it should be able to do is to set incentives for technological and ecological improvements. And it should have a budget for that. It should also be able to fund some degree of basic research"; then "Please add social improvements, education, and advocacy to the list"). [project/plans/public_stewardship_office_draft_2026-11-21.md](../project/plans/public_stewardship_office_draft_2026-11-21.md) gains §8 Stewardship Fund: Improvement Incentives, Public Education, and Basic Research. The fund is a fixed minimum share of public spending set for each four-year outlook cycle and held by the Public Finance Office. It pays outcome-based incentives for technological, ecological, and social improvement, open to anyone and ending no more than ten years after each program opens; public education and non-partisan advocacy on long-term priorities, paused during a quiet period before each vote; and basic research, with at least one-third of the fund reserved and grants decided by review panels drawn by lot. Every award is published, conflicts of interest are barred, and the fund is the only money the office may allocate. Role, Scope, Out of scope, §3 funding, the coordination section, records, scaling, the drafting-note choices, and the proposed Chapter Seven §4 entry are updated, and later sections are renumbered. No binding text changed.

**Eight hundred and eighty-ninth move (Public Stewardship Office draft: a five-point alignment rating with a forum route below 3; 2026-11-22; draft only under `project/plans/`, no binding text changed; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Request (custodian, in the project thread: "There ought to be some kind of alignment ladder, probably 5 point scale, with the PSO plan. In [project/plans/public_stewardship_office_draft_2026-11-21.md](../project/plans/public_stewardship_office_draft_2026-11-21.md), a new §7.5 Alignment Rating and Forum Contest gives each review a rating from 5 (fully aligned) to 1 (absent or empty), measuring the quality of a body's response to the outlook rather than agreement with it. A missing plan or report is rated 1. The former §7.5 and §7.6 become §7.6 and §7.7, and the §7.4 Limits bullet, the roadmap, and the drafting-note choices are updated. No binding text changed.
