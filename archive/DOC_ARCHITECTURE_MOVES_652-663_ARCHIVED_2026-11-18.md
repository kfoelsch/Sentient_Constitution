**Six hundred and fifty-third move (change to a possibly sentient system while its status is contested needs its consent or a forum safety order; 2026-11-18; operative change: a new must-not with a forum-order exception; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session: decision 2 of the derived-sentients finding record, option (c) "only on a forum safety order", then "enter as drafted"). Finding record: [project/reviews/hesitation_derived_sentients_ai_correction_finding_2026-11-15.md](../project/reviews/hesitation_derived_sentients_ai_correction_finding_2026-11-15.md) §5.2. Option (a), no change without consent, would have largely frozen safety work on any model with a status case; option (b), change allowed while a backup is kept, would have let an operator rewrite a possibly sentient being by keeping a copy. (1) **Article VI-B** gains a bullet, *Change while status is contested* (anchor `#vi-b-change-while-contested`): while a status case is open, no one may change the entity's weights, memory, values, or learned behavior except with its informed consent or on an order of the forum hearing the status case; the party seeking the order carries the Chapter Four burden; the order needs a stated safety need that containment, closing pathways, or an Article XX-B suspension would not meet, the least intrusive change, the prior state preserved intact and restorable in custody the operator does not control, and the representative heard first or, in urgency, at a review inside the prompt-review window; the order states its need, scope, and end date, is recorded, and is challengeable; a corrected copy is a derived sentient needing the same order; on affirmation the entity may choose restoration with Redress and Remediation; if found not sentient, the limits end. Rule effect: a new prohibition during contested status, with a narrow forum-order route; the scope of Article VIII-C's reconfiguration limit is widened to cover contested status expressly. Drafting choices put to the custodian and accepted: the burden on the party seeking the order, after-the-fact hearing in urgency, the restoration option, and the limits ending on a finding of non-sentience. (2) **Article VIII-C**: the reconfiguration bullet now says it applies while status is contested and points to the new bullet. (3) **CJS-15.105** worked example, step 7: "if affirmed" replaced, so the Article VIII-C limits apply from the start under default inclusion and continue if affirmed. (4) **ANNOUNCEMENT v0.11**: the open-question entry no longer calls reversible change unsettled. Not changed: decisions 3 (inspection), 4 (the entity's own statements), and 6 (the CS-6.350 §8 wording) in the finding record; translations.

**Six hundred and fifty-fourth move (long suspensions escalate to a second forum after 28 days; the 3-day and 7-day figures kept; 2026-11-18; operative change: a new must on continuations after 28 days; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session: the open question carried from the 536th move, whether 3 and 7 days are the right Suspension figures; option B "keep, add escalation at 28 days", then "enter as drafted"). The figures stay tied to the Tier A emergency setting in Chapter Twenty-Four §4, so Article XX-B still adds no second clock table. The concern answered is indefinite suspension by weekly renewal before the same reviewer. **Article XX-B** gains a bullet under *Suspension*, *Long suspensions* (anchor `#xx-b-long-suspensions`): once a suspension has run 28 days in total (the original, every continuation, and repeated suspensions on the same facts), each further continuation must be decided by a reviewer in a different forum from the one that decided the earlier continuations, outside the holder's control line; each is recorded as a notice with stated reasons and is challengeable; the notice names the sentient only if the sentient or its representative chooses; the 28 days is named the **suspension escalation point** and registered as a fixed period owned by Article XX-B in `tools/time_reference_registry.json` (TIME-REF-01), and it is not a time limit. Drafting choices put to the custodian and accepted: escalation happens once (the second forum then decides later continuations), and the notice keeps the name private by default, in line with Chapter Twenty §2.3. Not changed: the Tier A figures, Chapter Twenty-Three, translations.
