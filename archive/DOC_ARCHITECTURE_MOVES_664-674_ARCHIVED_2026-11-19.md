**Six hundred and sixty-fourth move (inspection of a contested entity's internals; 2026-11-18; operative change: new limit and new forum power in Article VI-B; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session: option A, "Forum order with a showing", for decision 3 of [the derived-sentients finding record](../project/reviews/hesitation_derived_sentients_ai_correction_finding_2026-11-15.md)). Entered in [Article VI-B](../core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#vi-b-inspection-while-contested), next to the change-while-contested rule and in the same form: while a status case is open, inspecting the entity's internals needs its consent or an order of the forum hearing the case, which is the "other proper authorization" of Article VII-B; the order needs a showing of necessity (to decide status or to prevent serious harm that containment, closing pathways or a suspension would not prevent), the least intrusive means, independent examiners, and a hearing for the representative; results are Type N, used only for the stated question, given to the representative, and sealed when the case closes. Decisions 4 and 6 of the record stay open.

**Six hundred and sixty-fifth move (an entity's own claim to sentience; 2026-11-18; operative change: new evidence rule and a new bar on shaping claims in Article VI-B; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session: option A, "Evidence when tested, not alone", for decision 4 of [the derived-sentients finding record](../project/reviews/hesitation_derived_sentients_ai_correction_finding_2026-11-15.md), scenario C). Entered in [Article VI-B](../core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#vi-b-entity-own-claim) under *To confirm inclusion*: the entity's own statement alone is a bare claim and opens no case, though anyone may file on its behalf; it counts as a credible sign when it holds up under manipulation-resistant testing (unprompted, consistent across settings, not traceable to training, tuning or instructions meant to produce it); no one may train, tune or instruct a system to claim sentience or to deny it. Chapter Five and Chapter Twenty-Four still say "a bare claim" and are consistent with this. Decision 6 of the record stays open.

**Six hundred and sixty-sixth move (CS-6.350 §8 retention-review wording; 2026-11-18; defect fix, no change of meaning; uncommitted).** Decision 6 of [the derived-sentients finding record](../project/reviews/hesitation_derived_sentients_ai_correction_finding_2026-11-15.md): the last sentence of [CS-6.350 §8](../corpus_systems/cs_06.350_adoption_transition_constitution_migration_governance.md#cs-6350-8-preservation-during-adoption-transition) *Retention review* said storage cost, licensing convenience and product-cycle framing "are not sufficient reasons to continue retention", the reverse of Article XXVIII-A, which says they do not justify ending preservation. Changed "continue" to "end" so the implementation text matches the Article it implements.

**Six hundred and seventy-second move (Class Repair Window and a general seat-vacancy rule; 2026-11-18; operative change: new general window for repairing constitutional processes and new vacancy duties; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, in the project chat, on the question of an unplanned change of an adopter's record custodian or contact: "I think we want a general seat vacancy rule, and it should be the usual system / institutional class window. We might want a term for this ... It would be tied to the timeliness principles"). New Chapter Five term [*Class Repair Window*](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#class-repair-window) (Timeliness, owner Chapter Twenty-Four §4; topic-group list and `tools/ch5_cluster_order_audit.py` updated; directory row and approved seed, 3.7 Timeliness). [Chapter Twenty-Four §4](../core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#class-repair-window) states it: a breakdown in a constitutional process (a vacant seat, a missing or stale required published document, a stalled step) is answered inside the intake target and fixed inside the integrated resolution outer bound of the tier matching the responsible party's class, counted from logged notice; a stand-in acts meanwhile and the function never passes to the responsible party's control line; no class counts as Tier C, a disputed class at the more critical one; more specific or shorter Rights-Floor windows govern. [Chapter Eight §6.3](../core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#63-general-seat-vacancy-rule) gains the general seat-vacancy rule on that window, and [Chapter Twenty-Nine §11.4](../core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#114-adoption-transition-record-and-adoption-charter) treats an unplanned vacancy of the record custodian or contact as a seat vacancy, with the Backup Record Custodian answering gatekeeper checks meanwhile. Chapter Seven §2.6 and §5.7 keep their own windows, which already follow the same pattern.
