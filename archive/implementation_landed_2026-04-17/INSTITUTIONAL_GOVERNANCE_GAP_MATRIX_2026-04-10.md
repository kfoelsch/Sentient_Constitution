# Institutional governance gap matrix (research pass)

Status: Planning/review artifact (non-authoritative).  
Date: 2026-04-10.  
Scope baseline: `implementation/CORPUS_INSTITUTIONS_OUTLINE_DRAFT_2026-04-10.md` (latest in-session draft state).

## Purpose

Map current outline coverage against widely used governance frameworks and identify:
- what is already present;
- what is partial and needs stronger specification;
- what is missing and should be added before normative drafting.

## Method

- Compared outline chapters/protocols against public benchmark themes from:
  - OECD/G20 Principles of Corporate Governance (2023),
  - ISO 37000:2021 (governance of organizations),
  - COSO Internal Control Integrated Framework,
  - IIA Three Lines Model (2020),
  - OECD Public Integrity (2017),
  - UK Corporate Governance Code 2024 (material controls declaration direction),
  - public/nonprofit governance best-practice sets (independence/conflict/transparency emphasis).
- Classified each item as:
  - `Present` — explicit in outline with clear ownership.
  - `Partial` — implied or named but lacking operational specificity.
  - `Missing` — absent from outline.
