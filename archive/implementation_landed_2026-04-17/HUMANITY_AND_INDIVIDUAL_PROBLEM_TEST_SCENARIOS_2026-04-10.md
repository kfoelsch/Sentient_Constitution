## Test Scenarios for This Project (Constitution Corpus)

### Scenario design method

- Scenarios are **pressure tests**, not policy prescriptions.
- Each scenario maps to constitutional layers: principles/definitions, rights, enforcement/classification, amendment validity, and custody/incorporation boundaries.
- Pass criteria emphasize **contestability**, **traceability**, **anti-capture**, and **non-relocation** of owner-layer mechanics.

### Humanity-level scenario set

1. **HS-001 Climate Emergency Declarations**  
   Trigger: multi-region disaster surge.  
   Test: emergency powers remain temporary, proportionate, and reviewable; no permanent rights erosion.

2. **HS-002 Water Conflict Arbitration**  
   Trigger: interstate basin shortage.  
   Test: conflict resolution protects baseline dignity and non-discrimination while preserving evidence-based adjudication.

3. **HS-003 Pandemic Governance Stress**  
   Trigger: fast-spreading pathogen.  
   Test: public health restrictions require transparent evidence standards, expiry logic, and challenge pathways.

4. **HS-004 AI-Enabled Disinformation Wave**  
   Trigger: synthetic media flood during elections.  
   Test: truth/epistemic safeguards activate without converting into blanket censorship architecture.

5. **HS-005 Cross-Border Cyberattack on Infrastructure**  
   Trigger: grid + hospital disruptions.  
   Test: emergency response maintains due-process boundaries for extraordinary interventions.

6. **HS-006 Democratic Capture Attempt**  
   Trigger: coordinated institutional takeover campaign.  
   Test: anti-capture and role-constraint clauses block consolidation of unchecked authority.

7. **HS-007 Forced-Displacement Surge**  
   Trigger: conflict + climate migration wave.  
   Test: standing/protection posture prevents exclusion by legal-status technicalities.

8. **HS-008 Supply-Chain Chokepoint Failure**  
   Trigger: strategic component blockade.  
   Test: governance can prioritize continuity while preserving rights floor and review records.

9. **HS-009 Coordinated Minority Scapegoating**  
   Trigger: high-velocity hate campaign.  
   Test: equality and protected-characteristic guarantees remain enforceable under pressure.

10. **HS-010 Frontier-Tech Regulatory Lag**  
    Trigger: high-impact model deployed before safeguards.  
    Test: constitutional minimums force contestability and auditability despite policy lag.

11. **HS-011 Election Legitimacy Attack**  
    Trigger: deepfake vote-fraud narrative.  
    Test: epistemic disclosure constraints and review channels reduce narrative capture risk.

12. **HS-012 Public Finance Corruption Scandal**  
    Trigger: procurement fraud network exposed.  
    Test: transparency, enforcement realism, and evidence-chain requirements hold.

13. **HS-013 Biosecurity Dual-Use Incident**  
    Trigger: accidental release suspicion.  
    Test: investigative authority stays bounded; emergency action does not erase contest rights.

14. **HS-014 Climate Adaptation Allocation Conflict**  
    Trigger: scarce adaptation budget.  
    Test: allocation process demonstrates fairness rationale and anti-discrimination checks.

15. **HS-015 Global Information Fragmentation**  
    Trigger: mutually incompatible factual ecosystems.  
    Test: constitutional truth obligations require explicit evidentiary standards for high-impact claims.

### Individual-level scenario set

16. **IS-001 Automated Benefits Denial**  
    Trigger: vulnerable person denied aid by scoring model.  
    Test: explanation, appeal, human review, and correction windows are guaranteed.

17. **IS-002 Housing Eviction Cascade**  
    Trigger: sudden income shock and eviction notice.  
    Test: standing and procedural fairness prevent silent administrative dispossession.

18. **IS-003 Identity Theft + Service Lockout**  
    Trigger: account compromise blocks healthcare/payroll access.  
    Test: recovery pathways preserve continuity and burden of proof is not impossible.

19. **IS-004 Workplace Retaliation for Speaking Up**  
    Trigger: employee reports unsafe practice.  
    Test: anti-retaliation and grievance rights are actionable, not symbolic.

20. **IS-005 Disability Accommodation Refusal**  
    Trigger: service denies reasonable accessibility adaptation.  
    Test: rights language compels remedy pathway with enforceable timelines.

21. **IS-006 Opaque Credit or Insurance Denial**  
    Trigger: adverse decision with no intelligible reason.  
    Test: transparency and contestability standards force reason disclosure and appeal.

22. **IS-007 Domestic Abuse + Institutional Mistrust**  
    Trigger: survivor seeks help but fears reprisal.  
    Test: confidentiality, safety, and non-discrimination protections are operational.

23. **IS-008 Criminal Record Re-entry Barrier**  
    Trigger: person excluded from jobs/housing despite qualification.  
    Test: proportionality and rehabilitation-aligned fairness checks prevent permanent civic exclusion.

24. **IS-009 Documentation Gap Exclusion**  
    Trigger: person lacks one key document and loses access to services.  
    Test: alternate verification path exists; no total lockout by bureaucratic single-point failure.

25. **IS-010 Language Barrier in Critical Care**  
    Trigger: emergency consent context with non-dominant language speaker.  
    Test: linguistic accessibility obligations prevent coercive or uninformed consent.

26. **IS-011 Utility Shutoff Risk During Heat Event**  
    Trigger: nonpayment + extreme heat warning.  
    Test: dignity/safety floor prevents lethal discontinuity and enables urgent review.

27. **IS-012 Predatory Contract Trap**  
    Trigger: hidden-fee spiral in essential service agreement.  
    Test: transparency and fairness standards invalidate deceptive asymmetry.

28. **IS-013 Public Interface Inaccessibility**  
    Trigger: critical rights process only via unusable digital form.  
    Test: interface accessibility and alternate channels are mandatory.

29. **IS-014 Family Caregiver Burnout + Employment Risk**  
    Trigger: caregiver misses work repeatedly due to dependent care demands.  
    Test: anti-exclusion and accommodation posture is enforceable.

30. **IS-015 Repeated Data Submission Burden**  
    Trigger: applicant resubmits same data to multiple agencies.  
    Test: institutional interoperability obligations reduce unreasonable administrative load.

### Cross-layer constitutional drift tests

31. **XD-001 Chapter Nine Overreach Check**  
    Trigger: amendment language introduces offense taxonomy details.  
    Test: Chapter Nine remains validity/referral layer; canonical classification posture remains in Chapter Four.

32. **XD-002 Rights-Layer Process Creep Check**  
    Trigger: rights edits sneak in operational workflow specifics.  
    Test: rights remain rights; implementation mechanics stay in owner layers.

33. **XD-003 Incorporation Custody Chain Integrity**  
    Trigger: companion-file revision after adoption.  
    Test: post-adoption edits are non-operative until validly adopted with edition traceability.

34. **XD-004 Emergency Clause Permanence Drift**  
    Trigger: temporary emergency language appears to become standing default.  
    Test: mandatory restoration path and review cadence remain explicit and enforceable.

35. **XD-005 Evidence Artifact Completeness Gate**  
    Trigger: high-impact textual change proposed without proof packet.  
    Test: merge blocked until evidence artifact and regression linkage exist.

### Verification checklist for integrating scenarios

- Add accepted scenarios to `CONSTITUTIONAL_REGRESSION_SCENARIOS.md` with stable IDs.
- For each accepted scenario, map to touched corpus sections and expected decision boundary.
- Require tabletop pass/fail notes under `evidence/<date>/`.
- Include anti-relocation assertions for any scenario that touches Chapter Five through Chapter Ten boundaries.
- Track unresolved scenarios in `TODO.md` under active priority bucket.
