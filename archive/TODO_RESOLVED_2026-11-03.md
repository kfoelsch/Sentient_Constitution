## Chapter Nine and Ten fresh review and Institution terms

At the custodian's request, the narrowed *Fresh Review* meaning was confirmed against Chapter Nine Part B §7.2. A fresh review is a later certification pass ordered in response to a certification defect.

The Chapter Nine Part C §§9.3.1–9.3.4 rules now apply to fresh reviews of a System: a fresh review that finds misalignment in a System holding certification has the same failure and step-down effect as a failed recertification or reopened review; its record states a cause; and the matter runs on the applicable tier windows. Chapter Nine Part B §7.4 now includes fresh review among the passes where revocation may be required when its conditions hold. Chapter Ten §9.5 treats a System record that fails at fresh review like other failed cited System certifications.

Where the same material certification defect both leads to a fresh-review order under Part B §7.2 and qualifies as a material reopening trigger under Part C §9.2, the fresh review is the single later pass opened for that defect. A separate reopened review does not open solely for that same defect; a later material change or independent trigger may still reopen review.

Chapter Five now defines *Institution Class* and *Institutional Data Types and Handling*. The latter covers materially in-scope data an Institution handles outside any System and clarifies that those findings are part of the Institutional Certification Record, not a separate record. Data handled inside a System remains evaluated once in that System's record and cited institutionally. Existing *Urgent Protection* and *Tier Window* definitions remain in Chapter Five.
