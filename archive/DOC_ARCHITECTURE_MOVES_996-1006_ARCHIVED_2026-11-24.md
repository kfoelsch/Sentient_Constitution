# Archived doc_architecture move history: moves 996–1006

Archived on 2026-11-24 from the move log in `doc_architecture.md`, at base commit `fc8a3ba21`. Moves 996–1006 were merged into main in pull requests #280 and #281. Links are relative to this folder.

**Nine hundred and ninety-sixth move (selection and dual roles in Independent Bodies; 2026-11-24; new rule; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): extend the Electoral Office's selection-related dual-role rule to every Independent Body. [Chapter Seven §3](../core_07.000_independent_bodies.md#3-what-makes-a-body-independent) bars a sentient from simultaneously holding a seat in an Independent Body and a seat in an assembly, executive, or forum whose members that body elects or draws. [Subcore 7-A §3](../subcore_07.010_electoral_office.md#3-independence-and-placement) identifies the Electoral Office rule as an application of the general rule. Effect on a rule: widens the existing Electoral Office restriction to all Independent Bodies; the existing restrictions on decision seats in bodies they fund, check, or measure and on material stakes remain.

**Nine hundred and ninety-seventh move (Electoral Office protected funding; 2026-11-24; clarification; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): [Subcore 7-A §3](../subcore_07.010_electoral_office.md#3-independence-and-placement) explicitly points to the Independent Body protected funding requirements, including the reserve and shortfall rules, alongside the Electoral Office's election-specific floor protections. Effect on a rule: no change; Chapter Seven §3 already applies those requirements to the office.

**Nine hundred and ninety-eighth move (group the electorate roll rules; 2026-11-24; layout only; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): organize the list in [Subcore 7-A §4](../subcore_07.010_electoral_office.md#4-the-electorate-roll) under eligibility and entries, access and corrections, and voting and safeguards. The existing rules and wording are unchanged.

**Nine hundred and ninety-ninth move (group the voting and counting rules; 2026-11-24; layout only; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): organize the list in [Subcore 7-A §5](../subcore_07.010_electoral_office.md#5-running-and-counting-votes) under access and equal treatment, and counting, results, and verification, with emergencies kept as a separate item. The existing rules and wording are unchanged.

**One thousandth move (general scaling for shared Independent Bodies; 2026-11-24; clarification; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): generalize the sharing and safeguards in [Subcore 7-A §9](../subcore_07.010_electoral_office.md#9-proportional-scaling). [Chapter Seven §3](../core_07.000_independent_bodies.md#3-what-makes-a-body-independent) expressly permits adopters to share an Independent Body by published agreement and states that applicable duties and protections remain in force for each adopter and its sentients, including access to records, challenge routes, and required audits. Effect on a rule: clarifies shared provision and continued application of existing duties; the Electoral Office's separate-roll and post-vote audit safeguards remain in place.

**One thousand and first move (link Electoral Office scaling to the general rule; 2026-11-24; wording only; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): make the existing reference in [Subcore 7-A §9](../subcore_07.010_electoral_office.md#9-proportional-scaling) a direct link to Chapter Seven §3 *What Makes a Body Independent*. Effect on a rule: none; the general scaling provision already applies.

**One thousand and second move (archive merged moves 989–995; 2026-11-24; organization only; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session to archive and merge): moves 989–995, already merged in pull request #279, move to [archive/DOC_ARCHITECTURE_MOVES_989-995_ARCHIVED_2026-11-24.md](../archive/DOC_ARCHITECTURE_MOVES_989-995_ARCHIVED_2026-11-24.md), with a pointer in the historical-moves line. Moves 996–1001 remain in the live log until merged. No rule changed.

**One thousand and third move (continuity during amendment moved to Chapter Twenty-Nine; 2026-11-24; layout only; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): move the continuity of supremacy and challenge rights during amendment from Chapter Thirty §4 to [Chapter Twenty-Nine §10.1](../core_29.000_amendment_ratification.md#101-continuity-of-supremacy-and-challenge-rights-during-amendment), alongside the provisional effect rule. Chapter Thirty §4 retains a non-operative pointer and its existing numbering; chapter scope statements, trace references, and the lexical guidance point to the new home. Effect on a rule: none; the operative paragraph and its plain-terms explanation are unchanged.


**One thousand and fourth move (consolidate Chapter Thirty hierarchy and execution references; 2026-11-24; wording only; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): shorten [Chapter Thirty §5.1](../core_30.000_expansion_supremacy.md#51-internal-hierarchy-for-adopters) to point to the canonical Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy, retaining its adopted-implementation scope and non-regression reference; combine the repeated execution references in §5.3 and correct their stale section number from §10 to §9. Effect on a rule: none. The stricter-external-law rule and conflict duties remain; §7.2 retains its anti-evasion language because it also applies to uses outside adoption.


**One thousand and fifth move (external prohibitions on protected characteristics and conduct; 2026-11-24; new rule; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session, accepted the recommended lawful-challenge baseline): add an explicit rule to [Chapter Thirty §5.3](../core_30.000_expansion_supremacy.md#53-conflict-disclosure-and-mitigation) for external prohibitions or penalties targeting protected characteristics or constitutionally protected conduct, including sexual orientation and consensual same-sex relationships where protected. Effect on a rule: adopters must not voluntarily discriminate, punish, report, or assist enforcement on that ground; when a binding demand conflicts with those protections, they must document and review it, exhaust available lawful challenges and narrowing means, protect sensitive information, and pursue the least harmful available course. Existing review and remedy pathways remain available; the rule does not itself displace external law or guarantee protection from external authorities. No categorical duty to disobey external law is added.


**One thousand and sixth move (protect data in transit and against exposure under external prohibitions; 2026-11-24; new rules; uncommitted).** Decision (custodian, asked in session, accepted the recommended general transmission safeguard and targeted exposure protection): [Subcore 4-A §6](../subcore_04.050_data_types.md#6-cross-domain-governance-principles) requires encryption in transit between systems or components for data in the restricted-by-default and non-accessible-by-default bands, alongside existing storage, key-custody, owner-copy, audit, and export safeguards. [Chapter Thirty §5.3](../core_30.000_expansion_supremacy.md#53-conflict-disclosure-and-mitigation) requires adopters to assess and reduce exposure risk where external prohibitions could lead to punishment or discrimination, including identifying, combined, inferred, and conflict-record data, with justified collection and access and applicable Subcore 4-A protections. Effect on rules: adds a general transmission encryption duty and a targeted exposure-risk duty; does not make all data about sentients confidential or displace audit, review, or remedy pathways.
