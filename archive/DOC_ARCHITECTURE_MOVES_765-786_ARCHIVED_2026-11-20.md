**Seven hundred and eighty-second move (Chapter Eight §11 protected funding as a general rule; 2026-11-20; operative change).** Decision (custodian, asked in session: "Section 9. The Office's Own Funding and the Information Office's Funding should be a general rule. We'll want to point to it once it is written from multiple institutions."). In `core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md` §11 *Independent Bodies*, the *Funding* requirement becomes **Protected funding**: an Independent Body's floor is set on its own published track by a published formula; no body it funds, checks, measures, or decides about may set, reduce, delay, or condition its funding, and two bodies that check or fund each other may not set each other's floors; the floor holds through a funding cycle except under a shared published shortfall rule, and formula changes wait for the next cycle; the Article XIII-D funding-integrity floor still applies to every funder; and a self-funded body qualifies where no one may seize, redirect, or condition its operating income except by a published rule set in advance. The plain-terms line adds that whoever pays a body can steer it. The Public Finance Office draft §9 now applies this rule to the two offices instead of stating it, and the Public Information Office and Public Monetary Office drafts cite it in their §3. Effect on a rule: new musts for every Independent Body (own funding track, no funding control by checked bodies, cycle protection).

**Seven hundred and eighty-fourth move (a reserve for every Independent Body; 2026-11-20; operative change).** Decision (custodian, asked in session: "section 11. Reserve Fund should apply to all independent institutions to isolate them from political funding problems. If you agree, please implement"). Chapter Eight §11 *Protected funding* gains a **Reserve** requirement: each Independent Body has a reserve in its name, held by itself or by the office that pays it, with a published target (default one quarter of its floor for a funding cycle); drawn only to keep its floor paid during a shortfall, a workload surge, or a late budget; never lent, pledged, used to reward or penalize, or moved elsewhere; held safely and not in assets of anyone the body funds, checks, measures, or decides about; restored before any increase above the floor; balance and draws published; taking from it is interference and an integrity trigger. Self-funded bodies build theirs from operating income. The Public Finance Office draft §11 now applies this rule, holding each paid body's reserve as a separate share of its fund and extending the protection to forums and remedy systems with a §4 floor; the Public Information Office and Public Monetary Office drafts say where their reserves are held. Effect on a rule: new musts for every Independent Body.

**Seven hundred and eighty-sixth move (shortfall and scaling rules for every Independent Body; 2026-11-20; operative change).** Decision (custodian, asked in session, after the reserve became a general rule: "same for sections 12 and 13"). Chapter Eight §11 *Protected funding* gains a **Shortfalls** requirement: whoever pays an Independent Body publishes a shortfall and its cause, draws the body's reserve before cutting its floor, cuts only by a rule published in advance that applies alike to every body paid from the same source, cuts last the functions the designating text names as most urgent, sets an end date for each cut, and restores floors and then reserves first; a shortfall does not excuse the body's deadlines but may support an extension. The §11 **Scaling** paragraph now lets a small adopter merge seats, share administration, or use another adopter's Independent Body by published agreement, and requires that the outside verify-or-authorize seat, separation from bodies it funds, checks, or measures and from any body that funds, checks, or measures it, and protected funding with its reserve all hold. The Public Finance Office draft §12 and §13 now apply these rules (keeping the forum protect-last order and the no-funder-on-a-funded-bench rule), and the Public Information Office §10 and Public Monetary Office §11 cite the scaling rule. Effect on a rule: new musts for every Independent Body and whoever pays it.
