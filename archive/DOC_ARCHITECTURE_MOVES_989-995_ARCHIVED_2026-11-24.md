# Archived doc_architecture move history: moves 989–995

Archived on 2026-11-24 from the move log in `doc_architecture.md`, at base commit `4d2ed5249`. Moves 989–995 were merged into main in pull request #279. Links are relative to this folder.

---

**Nine hundred and eighty-ninth move (Public Information Office and Office of the Census; 2026-11-24; new bodies and duties; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): establish the Public Information Office as an Independent Body and place an Office of the Census within it. [Chapter Seven §4](../core_07.000_independent_bodies.md#4-the-bodies-this-constitution-creates) names the office and its guarantees; [Subcore 7-D](../subcore_07.040_public_information_office.md#subcore-7-d-public-information-office) gives it official statistics and census duties, methods, publication, inclusion, privacy, and separation from rights and electoral rolls. Chapter Seven §3 applies its independence, funding, reserve, privacy, audit, challenge, and scaling safeguards. The Public Policy and Public Stewardship Offices and Chapter Twenty-Seven now point to the new statistics producer. Effect on a rule: every adopter must provide the new Independent Body and its census function, subject to the existing scaling rules. The earlier draft's provisional research and finance assignments are not adopted.

**Nine hundred and ninetieth move (Public Information Office purpose, operating detail, and census protections; 2026-11-24; rules and layout; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): evaluate and resolve the Public Information Office's thin operating text and nonstandard §1. [Subcore 7-D](../subcore_07.040_public_information_office.md#subcore-7-d-public-information-office) now states purpose, role, scope, and out-of-scope boundaries; explains its grounding; and develops its statistics program, methods, equal releases, revisions, privacy, census design and coverage, and public record, audit, and challenge. Effect on a rule: adds duties to publish program and method details, check disclosure risks, assess census coverage, keep a correction history, report on its work, and provide an error-report route; bars using census responses to make or amend an electorate-roll entry. The census stays inside the Public Information Office, and existing Independent Body protections remain in force.

**Nine hundred and ninety-first move (Public Information Office as primary constitutional measurer; 2026-11-24; new role and duties; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): make the Public Information Office the primary measurer of the Constitution and put its mapping of results to principles front and center. The [Preamble §2 Measurements Overview](../core_00.000_preamble.md#2-measurements-overview), [Chapter Seven §4](../core_07.000_independent_bodies.md#4-the-bodies-this-constitution-creates), and [Subcore 7-D](../subcore_07.040_public_information_office.md#subcore-7-d-public-information-office) name the role. Subcore 7-D §4.1 requires a public program mapping observed outcomes and evidence to the Preamble categories, Chapter Three families, and Chapter Two principles, with source, method, uncertainty, gaps, comparisons, and a yearly account. Effect on a rule: new measurement, mapping, disclosure, and reporting duties for the office. Technical Forum standards, forum decisions, other bodies' measurement duties, and the Rights Floor remain in their existing homes.

**Nine hundred and ninety-second move (name data types in the Public Information Office privacy section; 2026-11-24; clarification; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): [Subcore 7-D §5](../subcore_07.040_public_information_office.md#5-information-handling-and-privacy) now points directly to [Subcore 4-A](../subcore_04.050_data_types.md#subcore-4-a-data-types) (*Data Types*) and its most-protective-applicable-protections rule. Effect on a rule: no change; Chapter Seven §3 already applies the data-type rules to every Independent Body.

**Nine hundred and ninety-third move (restore and adapt the nine-rung confidence ladder; 2026-11-24; new rules; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session to bring useful material from the earlier Public Information Office draft into the current text): [Subcore 7-D §§4.2–4.3](../subcore_07.040_public_information_office.md#42-the-confidence-ladder) establishes the nine named rungs and assesses confirmation, quality, and statistical strength, with published criteria, independent method checking, disclosure, correction, and challenge. It adapts the draft for census and other official statistics, forecasts, no-effect claims, nonexperimental evidence, and dependent sources; it does not adopt universal p-value or five-sigma thresholds. The Public Policy Office and Public Stewardship Office now use the same ladder for their own estimates and findings. Effect on a rule: new rating, publication, criteria, and review duties for the three offices; unrated findings remain labeled where suitable published criteria do not exist. Technical Forum standards and decision-makers' own evidentiary rules remain in force.

**Nine hundred and ninety-fourth move (group the confidence ladder application rules; 2026-11-24; layout only; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session): reorganize the long list in [Subcore 7-D §4.3](../subcore_07.040_public_information_office.md#43-applying-the-confidence-ladder) under categories for assessing evidence, kinds of evidence and findings, and checking and correcting ratings. The existing rules and wording are unchanged.

**Nine hundred and ninety-fifth move (archive merged moves 982–988; 2026-11-24; organization only; uncommitted; number provisional under MOVE-NUMBER-01).** Decision (custodian, asked in session to archive and merge): moves 982–988, already merged in pull request #278, move to [archive/DOC_ARCHITECTURE_MOVES_982-988_ARCHIVED_2026-11-24.md](../archive/DOC_ARCHITECTURE_MOVES_982-988_ARCHIVED_2026-11-24.md), with a pointer in the historical-moves line.
