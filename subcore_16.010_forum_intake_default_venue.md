### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 16-A is the constitutional owner of **forum intake and default venue**: what each forum family's intake triage body (first-touch desk) does at filing, the sequence it follows, the default venue table that names the lead forum family for each primary stake, the limits on those default leads, the special routing rules, and the limits on how institutions that share a matter coordinate it. [Chapter Sixteen §4 Default Venue and Primary Stakes](core_16.000_forum.md#4-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) states the duty to keep an intake triage body, the primary-stakes test, and the rules that keep routing honest and timely; this file states how a matter is routed.

*In plain terms: when a case is filed, the first-touch desk works out what the fight is really about and uses the table here to pick which forum family leads. The table is in the Constitution so the starting forum is the same for every adopter and cannot be chosen by whoever has the most power.*

### 2. Why Default Venue Is Fixed in the Constitution

*In plain terms: whoever picks the forum can often pick the result. A fixed, published table takes that choice away from the stronger party.*

Where a case starts shapes how it ends: which experts are heard, which evidence counts, and how fast relief comes. If each adopter, or each party, could choose the starting forum, the powerful would steer cases to friendly venues and the weaker party would lose before the merits were heard. A default venue table that every adopter shares, applied at filing by a desk that cannot decide the merits, makes the starting point predictable and keeps it out of reach of forum shopping.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad): all four legs depend on a fixed default venue:
    - [Participation](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#participation): a sentient can find the forum without a lawyer's map;
    - [Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability): the forum that leads is known in advance;
    - [Oversight](subcore_03.055_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight): the desk that routes a case cannot decide it;
    - [Timeliness](subcore_03.065_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness): a case does not stall while parties argue over where it belongs;
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity), since disputes that reach the right forum early do less lasting harm;
- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§3.1 Fairness](core_02.000_values_principles.md#31-fairness): a fixed default venue is first a matter of fairness;
  - [§18.3 Separation of Duties](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#183-separation-of-duties);
  - [§13.1.5 Least-Restrictive, Time-Bounded, and Reviewable Constraint Principle](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle);
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XIV-B](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy) (*Right to Redress and Remedy*);
  - [Article XXVI-B](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-b-challenge-rights-and-independent-review) (*Challenge Rights and Independent Review*);
  - [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).

<a id="3-intake-triage-body-first-touch-desk"></a>

### 3. Intake Triage Body (First-Touch Desk)

*In plain terms: each forum family has a desk that sorts new cases by what they are really about. It routes cases; it never decides them. Filing costs the claimant nothing.*

Each required forum family must maintain an **intake triage body**, or a functionally equivalent arrangement within that family, as the family's **first-touch desk** at filing. That body:

- **applies** primary-stakes routing under [Chapter Sixteen §4.2.1 The Primary-Stakes Test](core_16.000_forum.md#421-the-primary-stakes-test);
- **sorts** incoming matters for **published** intake treatment consistent with **primary stakes**;
- **flags** preservation, **Rights-Floor**, **cross-family routing**, and **backup-forum** needs (see [Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)); and
- **administers** initial access so that transfer, consolidation, certification, and backup discipline under [Chapter Sixteen §6 Transfer, Consolidation, and Anti-Self-Judging](core_16.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture) and **Chapter Twenty-Three section 4** can take effect inside the **intake target** of the matching tier ([Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)).

That body also stays within three limits. It:

- is **not** a separate constitutional forum family;
- **must not** substitute for a lawfully constituted merits panel on **substantive outcomes** or **collapse** **family** boundaries; and
- **must not** employ funding, fee, administrative preference, or other incentive devices to defeat primary-stakes routing or enable opaque threshold sorting.

**Filing is free to the claimant.** Filing, intake, and routing cost the claimant nothing. No fee, deposit, or cost-shifting order may fall on a claimant for a matter that is heard, in any family or tier. This does not stop a forum from answering a filing shown on the Standing Record to be abusive under its published rules, and it does not stop public or adopter funding of claimant support.

<a id="4-routing-sequence-at-filing"></a>

### 4. Routing Sequence at Filing

*In plain terms: the desk first notes what is already verified, treats accusations as unproven, and then picks the lead forum from the table.*

**Routing map from Chapter Ten §6 (*Verification and Record Requirements*).** Chapter Ten tell forums how to record and *measure* what happened. They do **not** by themselves pick which forum hears the case. At filing, the family's **intake triage body** (first-touch desk) works through this sequence:

1. **Check what is already verified:** Note any verified Chapter Ten **Contribution Axis** or **Violation Axis** material — including the impact slot, severity band, process / response character, and standing effect — when those already exist.
2. **Do not treat accusations as settled facts:** Allegations and provisional labels guide routing and evidence preservation only, until findings exist under **Subcores 3-A to 3-C**.
3. **Pick the lead forum by what the fight is really about:** Use the default venue table in **section 5**: **Sentient**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Integrity**, or **Constitutional**. Apply the special routing rules in **section 7** where they fit.

<a id="5-default-venue-table"></a>

### 5. Default Venue Table

*In plain terms: the table names which forum family leads, by who the parties are and what the main stake is.*

At filing, **default** venue follows these rules unless [Chapter Sixteen §6 Transfer, Consolidation, and Anti-Self-Judging](core_16.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture) transfers or consolidates:

| Family | Party pattern | Primary stake (summary) |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------|
| **Sentient** | Sentient-to-sentient matters, and member, participant, household, neighborhood, association, local, regional, online, or comparable community-governance disputes where no institution is a necessary party and no other family holds the primary stake | Private or community obligations, remedial or restorative harms, local restoration, local or online community norms, community-governance participation, exclusion, restoration, or internal self-governance contests |
| **Technical Forum Domains** | Any party pattern, but **primary** stake is technical-governance procedure, system-alignment specifications, expert-evidence standards, scientific / engineering / medical administration, comparable knowledge-governance within adopted scope, **or** **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) sentience-status determination on indicators / expert evidence / bounded uncertainty | **Technical** standards, system-alignment specifications, measurement and testing methods, expert administrative procedure, evidence stewardship, textbook or curriculum integrity, standards governance, bounded uncertainty-reduction relevant to adjudication, regulation, or alignment certification, **or** sentience-status indicator adjudication under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) |
| **Institutional** | Any **institution** is a **necessary** party **or** the main fight is about what an institution is allowed to do, what it is charged to do, or whether it followed the rules that govern it | What an institution may do, what it is charged to do, the scope it is supervised under, how it is classified, or whether it followed its duties |
| **Environment** | Anyone can be a party, not only systems, and an Environment Forum must also take part in reviewing any system whose alignment materially affects the environment | **Ecological integrity**, **environmental preconditions**, restoration or remediation of shared ecological systems, attributable environmental burdens, lifecycle or systemic ecological harm, environmental-alignment component review for systems with material ecological exposure, or **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure where classification, alignment certification, recertification, or Rights-Floor enforcement depends on that determination |
| **Integrity** | **Integrity** as **the** main issue (including **grave** violation of conflict, procedure, or capture controls), official **constitutional alignment certification** or **ongoing alignment validation** for systems and institutions, **or** a **Chapter Fourteen** ACMD for a Chapter Ten **slot 7, 8, or 9** impact slot as **the** **primary** stake | **Integrity** of office, process, contest pathways, disclosure, conflict rules, anti-capture duties, official system-alignment certification, validation, and recertification using technical-forum inputs and Environment Forum environmental-alignment component determinations where material (**including** **pattern** or **systemic** failure **across** institutions or systems where **Chapter Ten** measurement, a corresponding **Chapter Fourteen** designation, or **Rights-Floor** enforcement depends on that determination) |
| **Constitutional** | **Structural** constitutional validity, **norm** meaning for **all** interpreters, or remedy that **restructures** governance **by constitutional requirement** | **Constitutional** validity or meaning, action **beyond lawful authority**, supremacy, or **class-wide** **structural** remedy |

<a id="6-lead-default-limits"></a>

### 6. Lead-Default Limits

*In plain terms: these rules settle what happens when two default leads seem to apply at once.*

These rules limit how the table's default leads interact. They do not replace [Chapter Sixteen §6 Transfer, Consolidation, and Anti-Self-Judging](core_16.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture) and **Chapter Twenty-Three section 4**, or the mixed-stakes and asymmetry rules in [Chapter Sixteen §4.4 Mixed Stakes and Routing Asymmetry](core_16.000_forum.md#44-mixed-stakes-and-routing-asymmetry).

- **Primary-stakes collision:** If the main fight is about what an institution is allowed to do, what it is charged to do, or whether it followed the rules that govern it, the Chapter Fourteen **Integrity** lead default (set in **section 7**) does not override the **Institutional** row. **Chapter Sixteen sections 4.4 and 6** govern mixed stakes, necessary institutional parties, and lead forum selection.
- **Constitutional questions for ruling:** **Integrity** lead for a Chapter Fourteen designation does not displace Chapter Ten numeric slot measurement, sending a question for ruling, or escalation to **Constitutional** forums under **Chapter Seventeen section 4** where constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy requires determination under the **Constitutional** row or through family-to-family escalation.
- **Anti-self-judging backup:** Where material allegations warrant independent merits review of **Integrity** forum integrity in a Chapter Fourteen designation proceeding concerning a Chapter Ten slot 7–9 record, backup routing under **Chapter Sixteen section 5** and **Chapter Seventeen section 3** applies without narrowing **Chapter Fourteen section 7** or **Article XXI** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).

<a id="7-special-routing-rules"></a>

### 7. Special Routing Rules

*In plain terms: a few kinds of case have their own lead forum: misconduct designations, forum-bias disputes, technical questions, and system-alignment sign-off.*

These rules apply on top of the default venue table in **section 5**. **Section 6** limits how the table's default leads interact.

- **Chapter Fourteen designation:** Where a **Chapter Fourteen** ACMD is the **primary** stake, **Integrity** is the default lead. Keep:
  - the Chapter Ten numeric impact slot;
  - **Constitutional** certification where needed;
  - institutional-party rules; and
  - anti-self-judging backup under [Chapter Sixteen §6 Transfer, Consolidation, and Anti-Self-Judging](core_16.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture) and **Chapter Twenty-Three section 4**.
- **Forum-bias disputes:** If the fight is mainly about a panelist who should have stepped aside, biased panel participation, or a comparable forum-integrity violation — including on a **Constitutional** forum panel under **[Article XXVI-C](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-c-constitutional-forum-independence-and-external-review) (*Constitutional Forum Independence and External Review*)** — route it to **Integrity** forums first. Under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 6**, a forum cannot be the sole final judge of its own bias: a **Constitutional** forum cannot be the only final forum deciding whether its own panelist should have recused. Standing-lock discipline: **[Chapter Twelve §4.5 Special Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks)**. Named misconduct pattern: **[Subcore 14-A §6.3 Forum Recusal Failure and Biased Panel Participation](subcore_14.010_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#63-forum-recusal-failure-and-biased-panel-participation)**.
- **Technical how-to questions:** Specifications, methods, measurement, testing, and expert-evidence questions go to **Technical Forum Domains** when those are the primary stake or component questions for ruling.
- **System-alignment sign-off:**
  - Official **constitutional alignment certification** for new materially impactful systems, and **ongoing alignment validation** for existing ones, default to **Integrity** as lead. So does institutional alignment certification of an Institution, which keeps **Institutional** forums as holders of the institutional mandate and duty-compliance components.
  - Integrity uses technical-forum inputs plus constitutional, institutional, ecological, rights, and anti-capture requirements.
  - The **Environment** forum family's environmental-alignment review (sign-off or objection) comes first wherever [Chapter Seventeen §3.5 Integrity Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#35-integrity-forums) requires it.
  - Keep referrals or sending for ruling to **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Sentient**, and **Constitutional** for component questions those families own.

<a id="8-limits-on-cross-institution-coordination"></a>

### 8. Limits on Cross-Institution Coordination

*In plain terms: when two or more institutions could handle the same matter, they cannot leave it stuck, let a doubtful body break the tie, pass it on without reasons, or let anyone block the independent backup.*

Where institutions share jurisdiction, evidence custody, review, enforcement support, investigation, or forum-adjacent routing on the same matter, each publishes its coordination and escalation plan under [CI-16.210](corpus_institutions/ci_16.210_cross_institution_coordination_escalation.md#ci-16210-cross-institution-coordination-and-escalation) (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*). Whatever the plan says, it must not:

- leave shared jurisdiction without a usable escalation path;
- break a deadlock through the body whose independence is materially contested;
- transfer a matter without a reasoned record; or
- let emergency incapacity, concentration, capture indicators, local enforcement involvement, security-service involvement, private-contractor involvement, or parallel-body involvement defeat independent backup routing under [Chapter Sixteen §6 Transfer, Consolidation, and Anti-Self-Judging](core_16.000_forum.md#6-transfer-consolidation-and-anti-self-judging--continuity-and-anti-capture).
