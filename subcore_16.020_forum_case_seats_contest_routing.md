### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 16-B is the constitutional owner of the **detail of forum cases**: the intake table, who holds each of the four seats for each kind of case and party, the four certification contest paths and who takes each, and the full rules for contesting a Standing Record or an Adoption Transition Record. [Chapter Sixteen](core_16.000_forum.md#chapter-sixteen-standards-routing-and-forum-cases) states each guarantee in one line; where a line there and the text here seem to differ, the reading more protective of the affected sentient controls.

*In plain terms: Chapter Sixteen says how a sentient can bring or contest a case and what the forum may and may not do. This file sets out who sits where and which forum hears which contest, so that every adopter runs forum cases the same way.*

### 2. Why the Detail Is the Same for Every Adopter

*In plain terms: who sits on a case and which forum hears a challenge decide whether the challenge is fair. Those choices cannot be left to the side with the most power.*

A right to contest is only as good as the forum that hears it. If adopters could let the office that made a finding sit on its review, send challenges to captured internal bodies, or turn a Standing-Record contest into a trial of someone's whole reputation, the right would stay on paper while the outcome stayed fixed. One seat table and one set of routing rules, the same for every adopter, mean a sentient knows before filing who will hear the case and that no one will judge their own work.

<a id="3-intake-table"></a>
### 3. Intake Table

*In plain terms: the four ways a case reaches a forum, and how each is routed.*

A case reaches a forum in one of four ways. The intake triage body records the intake kind on the [Subcore 3-J — Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) at filing:

| Intake | What arrives | First step | Routed by |
|--------|--------------|------------|-----------|
| **Direct case** (a [Subcore 3-J — Direct Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#direct-case)) | A dispute that does not contest a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record: sentient-to-sentient, community, institutional, integrity, ecological, or constitutional claims. | Filing with the forum, after the ordinary participation path under [Dispute sequencing](core_16.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing) where one applies. | Primary stakes, under the default venue table in [Subcore 16-A — §5 Default Venue Table](subcore_16.010_forum_intake_default_venue.md#5-default-venue-table). |
| **Certification contest** (a [Subcore 3-J — Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to a System or Institutional Certification Record or one of its certification findings. | The Certification Record's published challenge path, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The four challenge paths, under **section 5**. |
| **Standing-Record contest** (a [Subcore 3-J — Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to a contribution Standing Record or violation Standing Record. | Logged on the Standing Record and set *under challenge* by its custodian, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The real issue in the challenge, under **section 6**. |
| **Adoption Transition Record contest** (a [Subcore 3-J — Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to an adopter's [Subcore 3-K — Adoption Transition Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record): a gate decision, an enforcement-readiness declaration, a ledger entry, interim authority, or a provisional entry. | The challenge route the adopter publishes with the Adoption Transition Record, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The real issue in the challenge, under **section 7**. |

<a id="4-seats-in-a-forum-case"></a>
### 4. Seats in a Forum Case

*In plain terms: who holds each seat for each kind of case, and how the seats change with who is being heard.*

A forum case is a materially binding act, so it has the four seats in [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement), under Chapter Six's names. This section is how Chapter Sixteen applies that requirement, as [Chapter Six §10 Relationship to Later Processes](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#10-relationship-to-later-processes) requires of every later process. It adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Six. Forensic and technical support under **Chapter Seventeen section 6** feeds the verify-or-authorize seat. It is not a fifth seat.

Who holds each seat depends on the intake:

| Seat | Direct case | Certification contest | Standing-Record contest | Adoption Transition Record contest |
|------|-------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **[Subcore 3-J — Initiating Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#initiating-seat)** (brings the case) | The claimant: a sentient, institution, community, or published representative. A sentient needs no institution to file. | The challenger, who is any affected sentient or stakeholder. The operator or proponent when it contests a denial or revocation. | The subject or claimant contesting the Standing Record. | An affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body. |
| **[Subcore 3-J — Verify-or-Authorize Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#verify-or-authorize-seat)** (decides the case) | The merits panel of the lead forum family under [Subcore 16-A — §5 Default Venue Table](subcore_16.010_forum_intake_default_venue.md#5-default-venue-table). | A forum that did not make the challenged finding, chosen under **section 5**. | A forum that did not verify the Standing Record. Never the record custodian or a Backup Record Authority. | A forum independent of the ledger owner and of the party that made the contested decision, chosen under **section 7**. |
| **[Subcore 3-J — Record Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#record-seat)** (keeps the case file) | The office that keeps the [Subcore 3-J — Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). Not the panel and not a party. | The office that keeps the [Subcore 3-J — Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The System or Institutional Certification Record stays with the record holder its Charter Role Map names, and the lead forum coordinates it without keeping it. | The office that keeps the [Subcore 3-J — Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The Standing Record stays with its record custodian. | The office that keeps the [Subcore 3-J — Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The [Subcore 3-K — Adoption Transition Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) stays with the adopter that keeps and publishes it. |
| **[Subcore 3-J — Contest Seat](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contest-seat)** (reviews the forum's own decision) | Appeal or secondary review under [CF-16.420](corpus_forum/cf_16.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-16420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*). A backup family where the forum's own bias or capture is alleged. | The same, with escalation to **Constitutional** forums where constitutional validity is in question. | The same. | The same as a direct case. |
| **Respondents, not seat holders** | None. | The operator, the lead forum, the component authorities, and the Certification Record holder. | The record-opening authority and the record custodian. | The adopter, the ledger owner, the Adoption Transition authority, and the party that made the contested decision or declaration. |

A review case has two layers of seats:

- For the earlier act (the certification, the opening of the Standing Record, or the gate decision or declaration on the Adoption Transition Record), the forum with jurisdiction fills the Contest Seat.
- For the review itself, the forum case is a new act with its own four seats.
- Whoever held the verify-or-authorize seat or the Record Seat on the earlier act is a respondent or witness in the review. That office holds no seat on the review.
- The forum, panel, or office that made the contested finding, decision, or declaration never hears the contest of it. A challenge to a component finding goes to a forum that did not make it, and [CF-16.420](corpus_forum/cf_16.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-16420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*) says when the review must leave the family.

The incompatibilities in [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) apply to each act in a forum case:

- No forum, panel member, or office may hold seats on the same act that Chapter Six prohibits.
- A forum family may hold different seats on different acts. It may not use its family label to merge seats that Chapter Six prohibits on the same act.

Who holds the seats also depends on who is being heard:

- **A system or institution that has a [Subcore 3-K — System Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-charter) or [Subcore 3-K — Institutional Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter):** every seat holder sits outside its Material Control Line. The party cannot choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove the forum or panel that hears the case. A seat that its Charter Role Map should place and leaves unplaced is a defect in the System Charter or Institutional Charter, handled under [Chapter Six §6 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#6-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution).
- **A sentient:** holds the Initiating Seat on its own filing and needs no institution to do so. The forum's published placement under [CF-16.405](corpus_forum/cf_16.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-16405-forum-formation-forum-structure-mapping-and-chamber-structure) (*Forum Formation, Forum-Structure Mapping, and Chamber Structure*) names the other seats, and a holder with an interest in the outcome steps aside under [CF-16.410](corpus_forum/cf_16.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md#cf-16410-panel-formation-disclosure-recusal-and-lawful-bench-constitution) (*Panel Formation, Disclosure, Recusal, and Lawful Bench Constitution*).
- **A community or natural system:** the published representative holds the Initiating Seat ([Chapter Seventeen §3.4 Environment Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#34-environment-forums)). The representative holds no seat in the decision.
- **A forum family as the party (alleged bias, capture, or conflict):** the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in **Chapter Sixteen section 6** assigns the independent lead and the backup. That family holds no seat in the case.

<a id="5-certification-contest-paths"></a>
### 5. Certification Contest Paths

*In plain terms: the four ways to challenge a certification, and which forum takes each.*

A certification contest is a [Subcore 3-J — Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case). A System or Institutional Certification Record names four challenge paths ([Subcore 8-F §5.2 Contestability Paths](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths); [Subcore 9-F §5.2 Contestability Paths](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths)). The paths apply [Dispute sequencing](core_16.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing) under **Chapter Twenty-Two section 4**. They are routes into the forums, not a new forum, and Chapter Twenty-Two decides which forum takes each:

- The **published record challenge path** is the ordinary first step for an ordinary dispute about the Certification Record. A challenge raised there is logged on the Certification Record, and the tier windows under **Chapter Seventeen §5** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from its receipt. Internal operator review, vendor attestation, or technical sign-off is not this path.
- The **component forum path** takes a challenge to one certification finding to the forum family that has jurisdiction over it under the default venue table in [Subcore 16-A — §5 Default Venue Table](subcore_16.010_forum_intake_default_venue.md#5-default-venue-table).
  - Examples:
    - **Technical Forum Domains** for specifications, methods, and evidence standards;
    - **Environment** for the environmental-alignment component; and
    - **Institutional** for the institutional mandate and duty-compliance components of an Institution's certification.
  - The challenge stays with that family for primary-stakes merits.
- The **lead-integrity path** takes a challenge to how the lead forum ran the process.
  - Examples:
    - capture;
    - hidden information;
    - reviewing its own work; or
    - calling certification finished too soon.
  - It goes through **Integrity** routing, with a backup forum so the lead forum does not judge itself, under:
    - the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in **Chapter Sixteen section 6**; and
    - backup routing in **Chapter Seventeen section 4**.
- The **escalation path** moves a dispute to a different or higher forum, where the main stakes belong elsewhere or constitutional validity is in question. It uses one of the moves between forums in **Chapter Twenty-Three section 4** ([Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)):
  - transfer;
  - question for ruling;
  - backup routing; or
  - family-to-family escalation.

While a challenge is pending:

- The **Integrity** forum stays the lead for the System or Institutional Certification Record unless primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or a component assignment places a question elsewhere (**Chapter Seventeen section 3.5**).
- A timely **Environment** forum objection, remediation requirement, or question for ruling that remains unresolved blocks final certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions (**Chapter Seventeen section 3.5**).
- Direct access to a forum path remains available when delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration ([Dispute sequencing](core_16.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing)).
- A credible challenge can reopen review under [Subcore 8-F §4.2 Reopening Triggers](subcore_08.060_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers) and [Subcore 9-F §4.2 Reopening Triggers](subcore_09.060_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers).

<a id="6-standing-record-contests"></a>
### 6. Standing-Record Contests

*In plain terms: how a Standing Record is contested, what a challenge may claim, what the forum may do, and where each kind of challenge goes.*

A Standing-Record contest is a [Subcore 3-J — Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case). A Standing Record may be contested before or after any filing, as follows:

- A challenge raised on a Standing Record before any filing — to the custodian, the record-opening authority, or any office of the institution — is logged on the Standing Record, set *under challenge*, and routed by the custodian under [Subcore 10-B §8.3](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#83-challenge-received-on-the-standing-record) (*Challenge Received on the Standing Record*); the tier windows under **Chapter Twenty-Three §5** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from that receipt, and the **forum case record** opens when the contest reaches a forum.
- Where the record-opening authority was a Backup Record Authority under **CF-16.415 §9** (*Backup Record Authority*), a contest of that Standing Record goes to a forum with jurisdiction and never back to that authority. The Backup Record Authority sits outside the intake triage body and is not a merits panel.
- A sentient, institution, system, community, or other affected subject may ask a forum with jurisdiction to review a **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record** when they claim the Standing Record is:
  - wrong;
  - incomplete;
  - stale;
  - mis-scoped;
  - based on an invalid finding;
  - missing required context;
  - missing a required cross-reference; or
  - being used for a purpose it does not cover.
- The forum's job is to review the challenged record and the way it is being used. It may:
  - confirm the Standing Record;
  - require correction;
  - order a new version;
  - limit or pause reliance on the Standing Record;
  - send an underlying issue to the right forum; or
  - send a constitutional question for ruling.
- The forum must **not** turn a Standing-Record contest into a general reputation trial or merge contribution and violation review into one undifferentiated merits hearing when the challenge targets only one axis-pure record.
- Routing follows the real issue in the challenge:
  - factual or evidentiary defects go to the forum family that can fairly review that material;
  - institutional-use disputes go to **Institutional** forums where the main fight is about what an institution may do or whether it followed the rules that govern it;
  - capture, concealment, retaliation, or process-integrity claims go to **Integrity** forums where integrity is primary;
  - environmental merits go to **Environment** forums where ecological stakes are primary;
  - constitutional validity or meaning goes to **Constitutional** forums through certification or direct routing where Chapter Sixteen allows it.

<a id="7-adoption-transition-record-contests"></a>
### 7. Adoption Transition Record Contests

*In plain terms: what in an adopter's transition can be contested, who hears it, and what the forum may and may not do.*

A contest of an [Subcore 3-K — Adoption Transition Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) is a [Subcore 3-J — Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case). The adopter keeps and publishes the Adoption Transition Record under [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxix-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), and this section does not change that duty. An affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body may contest:

- a gate decision, its criteria, or the evidence it rested on;
- an enforcement-readiness declaration, as premature or as withheld;
- a ledger entry that cannot be independently verified, or a gap presented as closed without verified ledger data;
- interim authority that has outrun its stated limits or been reauthorized without stated reasons; or
- a provisional entry presented as settled, or a closing of the Adoption Transition Record without the hand-over.

Who hears the contest:

- A contest of an enforcement-readiness declaration is decided by a party independent of the ledger owner ([**CI-4.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_04.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-4275-4-article-xxix-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*)).
- Where no forum is yet ready for the enforceability scope in question, the interim route under [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxix-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) and [**CI-4.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_04.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-4275-4-article-xxix-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*) decides the contest, and this section applies from the time a forum is ready.
- The forum is chosen under **section 4**, so no office in the adopter's own line, no ledger owner, no Adoption Transition authority, and no party that made the contested decision or declaration holds a seat on the review.

Routing follows the real issue in the contest:

- what the adopter may do, is charged to do, or whether it followed its own published plan goes to **Institutional** forums;
- capture, concealment, falsified or withheld ledger data, or retaliation goes to **Integrity** forums where integrity is primary;
- ledger measurement methods and evidence standards go to **Technical Forum Domains**;
- a plan or re-baselining that lowers the fixed Rights-Floor target, or an action beyond lawful authority, goes to **Constitutional** forums through certification or direct routing where Chapter Sixteen allows it.

The forum's job is to review the contested decision or entry and the way it is being relied on. It may:

- confirm it;
- require correction or a new entry;
- order a gate reopened or a declaration issued;
- limit reliance on it;
- send an underlying issue to the right forum; or
- send a constitutional question for ruling.

The forum must **not** set the plan, its gate criteria, or its target, lower the fixed Rights-Floor target, or rerun the Adoption Transition. A pending contest does not suspend the restraint duties that bind from the effective date, and the tier windows under **Chapter Seventeen §5** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from receipt of the contest.

**Adoption Charter entries:** A contest of an entry in an adopter's [Subcore 3-K — Adoption Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#adoption-charter), such as its record custodian, its gatekeeper-check contact, or its forum authority assignment, follows this section in the same way, including who hears it and what the forum may and may not do. A contest of a level of trust the adopter extends is not heard under this section; [Chapter Thirteen §3.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#34-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust) governs it.
