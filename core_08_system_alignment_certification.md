*In plain terms: Chapter Eight is split in three — Part A for how to evaluate, Part B for the record and process, Part C for worked examples of the evaluation. Read them as one chapter.*

Read **Part A** first for the process overview and evaluation requirements; **Part B** for the record, forum process and challenge paths, outcomes and reopening, and the standing bridge; **Part C** when you want to see the evaluation applied to concrete Class A, B, and C systems.
Under the **oversight** Tetrad leg, oversight requires auditing; Chapter Eight is one especially large, high-stakes audit process among others (forum-supervised alignment review with recognition outcomes). Auditing floors remain at **Article XVI** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*) and Chapter Five [Auditability](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).
