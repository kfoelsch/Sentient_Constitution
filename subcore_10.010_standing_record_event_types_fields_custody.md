### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 10-A is the constitutional owner of four pieces of Standing Record detail: the **event-type tables** that help name what kind of help or harm a verified Standing Record describes, the **fields** each Standing Record must carry, where a Standing Record goes when a **System Charter or Institutional Charter leaves a seat unnamed**, and how **informal and small-scope work** is verified and held. [Subcore 10-B](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#subcore-10-b-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) states what a Standing Record is, the qualification rules, the separation of duties, and the guarantees; where a line there and the text here seem to differ, the reading more protective of the affected sentient controls.

*In plain terms: Chapter Fourteen says what happened must be verified and written down by sentients who did not do it. This file holds the tables, the field lists, and the step-by-step rules that make that work the same everywhere, from a large operator with a gap in its System Charter to a neighbor who fixes pipes for free.*

### 2. Why the Detail Is the Same for Every Adopter

*In plain terms: a Standing Record follows a sentient wherever they go, so it has to mean the same thing everywhere.*

Standing Records move between adopters, forums, and systems, and effects on standing are drawn from them in Chapter Sixteen. If each adopter chose its own fields, its own event types, or its own answer to a missing seat, the same Standing Record could be complete in one place and void in another, and a Chartered Party could leave a seat empty to keep its own Standing Records in its own hands. One set of fields, one guide to event types, and one route for gaps and informal work, the same for every adopter, keep Standing Records portable, comparable, and out of the subject's control.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad): the legs this file carries:
    - [Subcore 3-D — Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability): every Standing Record names who verified it and who holds it, and no one holds their own;
    - [Subcore 3-G — Oversight](subcore_03.055_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight): every Standing Record lists the materials that make it reviewable and how to challenge it;
    - [Subcore 3-H — Participation](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#participation): unpaid and informal work can be recognized without a group, a System Charter or Institutional Charter, or a formal institution;
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): both aims, which this file serves:
    - [Flourishing](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing): care, teaching, repair, and mutual aid are recognized on equal standards;
    - [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity): ecological and infrastructure help and harm are named among the event types;
- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§18.3 Separation of Duties](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#183-separation-of-duties);
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XVII](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xvii-audit-transparency-and-independent-verification) (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*).

### 3. Event-Type Tables

*In plain terms: tables to help describe what happened once the facts are verified. They do not decide how good or bad it was.*

The qualification rules for each table, and the rule that the tables assign no slot and decide no effect, are in [Subcore 10-B §5 Question 1 Event-Type Guide — What Kind of Help or Harm Occurred?](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#5-question-1-event-type-guide--what-kind-of-help-or-harm-occurred).

| Restoring agency | Someone gained meaningful choices about their own life | **Participation** | **Flourishing** |
| Expanding productive capacity | Tools, infrastructure, or opportunities enabled others to thrive independently | **Participation**, **Oversight** | **Flourishing**, **Continuity** |

<a id="4-standing-record-fields"></a>
### 4. Standing Record Fields

*In plain terms: what every Standing Record must contain, and what each kind adds.*

The verified-input gate, the stage boundary, and the rules on attributable action and privacy that bear on these fields are in [Subcore 10-B §6.1 Minimum Record Contents](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#61-minimum-record-contents).

<a id="41-fields-every-standing-record-must-carry"></a>

#### 4.1 Fields Every Standing Record Must Carry

Every Standing Record must include, at minimum:

- the Standing Record type — **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record**;
- who or what is being assessed and the scope of the assessment, including relevant system boundaries, role boundaries, affected communities, and dependency relationships;
- what conduct or finding is being assessed, including the acts, omissions, duties, impacts, bounded event, pattern within scope, contribution record, or violation finding the Standing Record relies on;
- the record span and current review status — for example, provisional, final for a stated purpose, under challenge, superseded, or due for scheduled re-evaluation;
- any related Standing Records cross-referenced as required under [Subcore 10-B §6.2 Related Standing Record Cross-References](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#62-related-standing-record-cross-references);
- the official records, witnesses, measurements, audits, forum decisions, or other verification materials that make the Standing Record demonstrable, reviewable, and contestable under Subcores 3-A to 3-C;
- the **record-opening authority** that verified the factual basis and authorized entry, and the separate **record custodian** that entered and holds the Standing Record, each named under [Subcore 10-B §7 Record Custody and Opening Authority](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#7-record-custody-and-opening-authority);
- how to challenge the Standing Record, including:
  - which forum or authority reviews it;
  - any disclosure limits; and
  - the conditions for:
    - correction;
    - restoration;
    - expiration; or
    - scheduled review;
- the axis-specific Question 2 measurement fields required below, once measurement occurs.

<a id="42-additional-fields-for-contribution-standing-records"></a>

#### 4.2 Additional Fields for Contribution Standing Records

**Contribution Standing Records** must also state:

- the Question 2 contribution measurement, including:
  - the primary band;
  - slot `s` where assigned (see [Subcore 10-D §6 Slot Grammar and Shared Scaling](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#6-slot-grammar-and-shared-scaling));
  - evidence basis;
  - materiality reasoning; and
  - any adopted LEQU or comparable calibration used for transparent support;
- where contribution is shared, how credit was allocated, including consideration of:
  - time;
  - effort;
  - coordination;
  - maintenance;
  - causal contribution; and
  - less-visible support work;
- the contribution measurement, as recorded here, that any later Chapter Sixteen standing effect must be based on.

<a id="43-additional-fields-for-violation-standing-records"></a>

#### 4.3 Additional Fields for Violation Standing Records

**Violation Standing Records** must also state:

- the Question 2 violation measurement, including:
  - the finding authority or record basis;
  - LEQU impact;
  - slot `s` where assigned (see [Subcore 10-D §6 Slot Grammar and Shared Scaling](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#6-slot-grammar-and-shared-scaling));
  - process / response character where applicable; and
  - any Chapter Twenty designation status;
- where several actors share or divide responsibility for a violation, the verified reasons for any adverse standing recorded against one particular actor, including:
  - causal role;
  - duty;
  - authority;
  - control;
  - foreseeability;
  - benefit;
  - concealment; or
  - feasible prevention capacity;
- where the violation finding involves a forum disclosure omission or intentional recusal-process failure (read [Subcore 10-D §4.2.2 How the Way a Harm Was Caused Is Recorded](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#422-how-the-way-a-harm-was-caused-is-recorded) for measurement discipline):
  - the verified basis for the omission or failure;
  - the unmet disclosure or recusal duty;
  - the actor's knowledge, recklessness, or intent where found; and
  - whether the conduct materially affected:
    - panel lawfulness;
    - independence;
    - recusal challenge;
    - evidence integrity;
    - remedy timing;
    - backup routing; or
    - practical contestability;
- the violation measurement, as recorded here, that any later Chapter Sixteen standing effect must be based on.

<a id="5-unnamed-seats-in-a-system-charter-or-institutional-charter"></a>
### 5. Unnamed Seats in a System Charter or Institutional Charter

*In plain terms: when a System Charter or Institutional Charter leaves a seat empty, it is invalid until it is fixed, and no one fills the seat on the side. The Standing Record goes to the backup offices or a forum.*

The chart of every custody route is in [Subcore 10-B §7.7 When a System Charter or Institutional Charter Leaves a Seat Unnamed](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#77-when-a-system-charter-or-institutional-charter-leaves-a-seat-unnamed).

A System Charter or Institutional Charter that leaves a required record seat or the independent route unnamed:

- **Consequence:** The System Charter or Institutional Charter is invalid, and a change that would do so is not allowed ([Chapter Five §6.7.1 When a Gap Exists and How It Is Logged](core_05.000_charters.md#671-when-a-gap-exists-and-how-it-is-logged)).
- **Chapter Five owns the gap process:** [Chapter Five §6.7 Gaps in the Map for Operator Standing Records](core_05.000_charters.md#67-gaps-in-the-map-for-operator-standing-records) also owns:
  - how a gap is logged;
  - the response and resolution windows (including emergency substitution); and
  - when custody goes back to the named holders.

For Standing Records, this section adds:

- **Who may not act:** While a required seat is vacant and the System Charter or Institutional Charter names no valid replacement, no one may perform that seat's function, except by the route in the next bullet.
- **Where the Standing Record goes:** The Standing Record goes to the Backup Record Authority and the Backup Record Custodian, or to a forum under [Subcore 10-B §4.7 Forum Boundary](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary), as [Subcore 10-B §7.4 No Self-Custody](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#74-no-self-custody) provides, and the lane map does not stand in. Each Standing Record stays whole with one custodian.
- **Standing Records already held:**
  - On the day notice of the gap reaches a forum, a registrar, or the Backup Record Authority, any operator Standing Record held by an office inside the Chartered Party's Material Control Line moves to the Backup Record Custodian with its full trail.
  - Until then that office holds it read-only, enters no version, and gives the Backup Record Custodian a complete copy with its full trail at once.
  - A custodian outside the line keeps the Standing Record and, during the gap, enters each version that the Backup Record Authority or a forum authorizes. If a forum finds that the custodian is inside the line, the Standing Record moves to the Backup Record Custodian with its full trail, as for an office inside the line.
- **Stewards:** A steward who is asked to fill a seat that the System Charter or Institutional Charter does not give them:
  - **Declines the seat:** Having the access or technical ability to enter the Standing Record gives them no authority to enter it.
  - **Names the request and the gap:** They name both in the log.
  - **Sends the Standing Record on:** They send it by the route in *Where the Standing Record goes* above.
  - **Keeps their other duties:** The duties they already have under [Subcore 10-B §7.4 No Self-Custody](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#74-no-self-custody) (*Stewards inside the subject*) continue: they halt what they control, log the reconstructable set, preserve the evidence they hold, notify, and escalate. None of those acts verifies the facts or enters the Standing Record. The log and the preserved evidence go with the Standing Record as inputs for the authority that verifies it; they are not an entry, and they do not decide the facts.

<a id="6-informal-and-small-scope-records"></a>
### 6. Informal and Small-Scope Records

*In plain terms: who verifies and who holds a Standing Record of unpaid, informal, or neighborly work, with worked examples.*

Separation of duties scales with material stake, not with formality, as [Subcore 10-B §7.9 Informal and Small-Scope Records](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#79-informal-and-small-scope-records) states.

- **Work covered:** Local communities run on many kinds of contribution, and much of it is unpaid and informal. This applies to unpaid, peer-organized, mutual-aid, care, maintenance, repair, teaching, and community-stewardship work, which [Chapter Eleven §3 Contribution Consequences](core_11.000_contribution_consequences.md#3-contribution-consequences) requires to be recognized on equal standards.
  - **What it looks like locally:** Neighbors sharing food and tools (mutual aid); looking after a sick or aging neighbor (care); keeping a shared garden or creek in good shape (maintenance and community stewardship); fixing a neighbor's pipes (repair); tutoring or showing someone a skill (teaching). Each is its own kind of contribution.
- **Who fills the verify seat:** For that work, the verify seat is satisfied by **any independent body with published authority to rely on the Standing Record**, which then acts as the verifying authority.
  - **Who that usually is:** Often the body that will rely on the Standing Record: a local group such as a watershed council, an organization that funds the work or keeps it going, the **CI-4.255** (*Commons, cooperatives, mutual aid, and non-market community governance*) records-recognition authority (a body that recognizes records of community work like this), or a forum under [Subcore 10-B §4.7 Forum Boundary](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary).
- **What does not count:** The verify seat is not satisfied by the formality of the verifying authority.

On such a Standing Record:

- a sentient acting alone needs no group, institution, [Subcore 3-K — System Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-charter), or [Subcore 3-K — Institutional Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) to seek a record of their own contribution. In that case:
  - **Who asks:** They hold the initiating seat on their own request, as subject and claimant.
  - **Their own materials:** Their own account, logs, and photos are inputs and never verification.
  - **Verify seat:** Any independent body with published authority to rely on the Standing Record (the verifying authority).
  - **Entry and custody:** A separate record custodian enters and holds the Standing Record.
  - **If someone contests it:** The contest goes to the challenge lane or a forum under [Subcore 10-B §4.7 Forum Boundary](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary).
  - **No relying body available:** Where no such relying body is available, the Standing Record goes to a forum under [Subcore 10-B §4.7 Forum Boundary](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary).
  - **Example, a retired plumber:** A retired plumber who has repaired neighbors' pipes unpaid for years, with no group behind them, can ask a **CI-4.255** (*Commons, cooperatives, mutual aid, and non-market community governance*) records-recognition authority to open a Standing Record:
    - **Requests and claims:** The plumber.
    - **Verifies:** The authority, working from the work logs and the neighbors' attestations.
    - **Enters and holds:** A separate record custodian.
    - **If someone contests it:** The contest goes to a forum, not back to the verifying authority.
- the participants are subjects and, where they seek recognition, claimants. In a collective record under [Subcore 10-B §6.3 Collective and Actor-Specific Records](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#63-collective-and-actor-specific-records):
  - **Co-participants:** Each is a claimant.
  - **A member's statement about another member's share:** It is an input, never verification.
- the materials that support the Standing Record come from two places:
  - **Non-participants:** Beneficiaries, neighbors, and other non-participants supply attestations as verification materials under [Subcore 10-B §6.1 Minimum Record Contents](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#61-minimum-record-contents).
  - **The group's own log:** The sentient who kept it is the recorder and a witness under [Subcore 10-B §8.1 Recorder Statements Are Inputs](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#81-recorder-statements-are-inputs).
- the relying body's record custodian enters and holds what a separate verifying authority has verified:
  - **Who may not hold either seat:** Neither the record custodian nor the verifying authority may be a claimant or in a claimant's [Subcore 3-J — Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line).
  - **One body, two seats:** The same relying body may host both seats under a published lane map with auditable, contestable independence safeguards.
  - **Never the same one:** No authority, custodian, or sentient may perform both duties on the same Standing Record.
- a group's own status does not condition a member's claim:
  - **Informal groups:** A group with no [Subcore 3-K — System Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-charter) or [Subcore 3-K — Institutional Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter), or one that is not an Institution at all, does not block its members.
  - **Each participant:** May seek recognition as an individual claimant for work done in the group or outside it.
  - **What the Standing Record covers:** It concerns the participant's contribution and not the group's standing.
  - **Example, a quilting circle:** A quilting circle that gives quilts to newly arrived refugees is the pattern: each quilter may seek a Standing Record, verified by an independent relying body such as the agency that receives the quilts, and the circle needs no System Charter or Institutional Charter for that.
- a violation Standing Record can arise in the same informal settings, and the seats stay separate there too:
  - **Example, a bar incident:** Someone films another sentient in a bar who becomes drunk, disorderly, and violent, though no one is physically harmed.
    - **No injury needed:** A verified credible threat of violence can support a violation Standing Record even when no one is hurt ([§3.2 Violation Event Types](#32-violation-event-types), coercive agency loss).
    - **The video is an input:** The sentient who filmed is a witness. The footage is an input and never verification by itself, and its existence opens nothing on its own ([Subcore 10-B §4.2 Silence Is the Default](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#42-silence-is-the-default)).
    - **The sentient on the video:** They are the subject. They are told when a Standing Record opens, and they may not fill any seat ([Subcore 10-B §7.4 No Self-Custody](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#74-no-self-custody)).
    - **Verify seat:** An independent body with published authority to rely on the Standing Record, such as a local licensing or safety body, or a forum under [Subcore 10-B §4.7 Forum Boundary](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary). The sentient who filmed and the bar's owner are excluded from it if either is a claimant or in a claimant's [Subcore 3-J — Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line).
    - **Entry, custody, and contest:** A separate record custodian enters and holds the Standing Record, and a contest goes to the challenge lane or a forum under [Subcore 10-B §4.7 Forum Boundary](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary), not back to the verifying body.
- a bar or process that requires verification by a formal institution an informal group cannot reach, where an independent relying body is available, is the [Chapter Eleven §3.2 Competency Bars and Clearances](core_11.000_contribution_consequences.md#32-competency-bars-and-clearances) arbitrary-gatekeeping pattern, not separation of duties.
