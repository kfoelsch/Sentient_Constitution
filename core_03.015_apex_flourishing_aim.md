Chapter Three, Flourishing aim, is the constitutional owner of the **Flourishing** constitutional-aim head definition (O/M/A/C) and hierarchy map. Leaf definitions are in the Chapter Three band files.

*In plain terms: protect and improve how well sentients actually live — truth, safety, trustworthiness, and a real ability to choose and act, kept together — not by chasing one of those as far as it will go while emptying out the others.*

<a id="flourishing-constitutional"></a>
<a id="flourishing"></a>

- **What it is**
  - Protect and improve sentient wellbeing — the constitutional aim this entry names.
  - **In scope:** judging systems against this aim using the Flourishing building blocks and measurement families listed in [Constitutional Aim Decomposition](#constitutional-aim-decomposition).
  - **Depends on:** the building blocks named in [Preamble §1 The Model](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing). Each term's detailed scope and how-to-measure rules live on its own definition; start from [Constitutional Aim Decomposition](#constitutional-aim-decomposition):
    - [Truth (Constitutional Constraint)](core_03.050_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint);
    - [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](core_03.040_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint);
    - [Trustworthiness](core_03.040_band_continuity.md#trustworthiness); and
    - [Meaningful Agency](core_03.055_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
  - **Out of scope:** the leaf-definition details, measurement layers, and implementation mechanics owned by band files and CJS.
<a id="flourishing-constitutional-m"></a>
<a id="flourishing-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** How to tell whether sentient wellbeing is actually protected and improved. This line only points to the measures; the how-to-measure rules sit on each linked definition.
    - **Main outcome measure:** [Wellbeing](core_03.040_band_continuity.md#wellbeing).
    - **Topic map:** [Constitutional Aim Decomposition](#constitutional-aim-decomposition).
    - **Searchable index:** [Chapter Three alphabetical directory](core_03.000_definitions_home.md#definitions-a-z).
  - **Primary assessment:**
    - Judge under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad), scaled to [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake).
    - Tie claimed flourishing results to the [constituent](#constitutional-aim-decomposition) conditions and to outcomes sentients actually experience, under Subcore 3-C and [Chapter Two §19.1.5 Constitutional Outcome Claims Discipline](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#1915-constitutional-outcome-claims-discipline).
    - Always read with [Continuity (Constitutional Aim)](core_03.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-three-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim): short-term gains that foreseeably weaken long-term stability, resilience, or ecological integrity fail **Flourishing** when the two aims are judged together.
<a id="flourishing-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Non-compliant when:
    - one building block is maximized while truth, safety, trustworthiness, or agency is hollowed out;
    - flourishing claims cannot be traced to constitutional outcomes under Subcore 3-C; or
    - **Flourishing** is pursued in a way that materially defeats the [Continuity (Constitutional Aim)](core_03.010_apex_continuity_aim.md#chapter-three-definitions-continuity-constitutional-aim) without clear permission under [Chapter Two §13 Constitutional Collision Resolution Process](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#13-constitutional-collision-resolution-process).
