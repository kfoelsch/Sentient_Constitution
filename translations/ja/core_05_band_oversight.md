<a id="oversight-independent-terms"></a>

### 監督：独立用語

<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions"></a>

#### 敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- あわせて読む： [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [システム捕捉](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： how a system behaves under pressure — when sentients game it, coordinate across accounts, run it at massive scale, or deliberately misuse it.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Operating conditions in which systems or actors behave to maximize advantage in ways that can alter [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), or [システム捕捉](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture), including:
    - システム構造の濫用；
    - インスタンスを横断する調整；
    - high-scale deployment — volume, concurrency, or reach at which behavior or effects can differ from small-scale or ideal-lab use;
    - 反復の相互作用；および
    - 適応的または意図的な誤用。
  - **範囲外：** Ordinary benign use, ideal-lab testing, or one-off misuse anecdotes — those sit outside pressure, scale, cross-instance coordination, or deliberate exploitation.
<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： oversight still works under gaming, scale, coordination, and deliberate misuse — not only under ordinary or ideal-lab conditions.

    **第一次の評価：** Include plausible strategies that could reasonably emerge given system incentives, access, and capabilities, read together with [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) and [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), covering:
    - 誤用；
    - 調整；
    - 拡大；および
    - 適応的応答。
<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** Failure to evaluate these conditions where relevant is non-compliant, especially where it understates [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) or masks [システム捕捉](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture) — including using any of the following as a stand-in for pressure, scale, cross-instance coordination, or deliberate exploitation:
    - 通常の無害な使用；
    - 理想実験室の試験；または
    - 一度きりの誤用の逸話。

<a id="capability-requirement"></a>

#### 能力要件

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- クラスター構成要素： [実質性、影響、リスク、および代替指標の誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- あわせて読む： [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).

</details>

<br>

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A mandatory functionality that a [システム](core_05_band_accountability.md#system) possesses sufficient to achieve a specified outcome, independent of implementation mechanism.
  - **範囲外：** optional features, aspirational roadmap items, or design preferences that are not claimed as a mandatory capability for a specified outcome.
<a id="capability-requirement-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： claimed capability is demonstrated in practice — not only declared in design or documentation.

    **第一次の評価：** Judge capability by what the system actually does — not by:
    - 設計文書；
    - 計画；または
    - 意図された振る舞い。
    That proof must be clear enough for [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) and for outsiders to evaluate [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact).
<a id="capability-requirement-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - The system must demonstrably achieve the specified outcome in practice within auditable [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
  - **第一次の失敗：** a claimed, documented, or planned capability with no demonstrated achievement of the specified outcome under the conditions in which the system is represented, authorized, or used.

<a id="classification-scaled-governance"></a>

#### 分類に応じた統治

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 上流： 原則： [憲法四元](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — [実質的利害](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling.
- クラスター構成要素： [実質性、影響、リスク、および代替指標の誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- あわせて読む： [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), and [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional).
- 下流： [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) — class-scaled whole-system evaluation duty.

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： bigger stake means harder oversight — how closely a system is watched and checked must match how much it actually matters, not a convenient low label.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** How hard [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), verification, and process discipline must be — scaled to [実質的利害](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake), covering:
    - [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency); and
    - [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk).
    The system's class under *[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md)*, CS-3 — システムの分類と取扱い, and related rules sets that intensity.
  - **範囲外：**
    - ordinary uniform process detail among systems that share the same real class under CS-3; or
    - implementation typing and class-assignment mechanics owned by CS-3 —
    それらは各自の本拠に属する; this entry owns stake-scaled intensity, not the typing rules themselves.
<a id="classification-scaled-governance-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： oversight depth and verification burden match the system's real stake, impact, dependency, and risk — not a self-assigned low class.

    **第一次の評価：** Use the real class under the implementation rules, read with [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity). A self-assigned or informal low class is not enough when the system's full effects call for stronger [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional) or governance. Whole-system review under [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) scales by class:
    - **Class A**, **Class B**, and **Class C** — full depth, mandatory;
    - **Class L** — mandatory, but sized to the impact; and
    - **Class P** — encouraged while the system is validly **Class P**.
<a id="classification-scaled-governance-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - one-size-fits-all oversight that ignores real stake differences;
    - a class label that understates the system's real stake, impact, dependency, or risk;
    - running or labeling a system below what its full effects warrant; or
    - skipping class-based duties when [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), or [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) require stronger governance.

<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional"></a>

<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity"></a>
#### 報道とジャーナリズム活動

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- クラスター構成要素： [情報圏](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere)

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： journalism is what sentients do — gathering news, protecting sources, investigating, and publishing — not a badge, title, or press pass. Actions meant to shut that down get heightened scrutiny.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Journalistic activity that functions as journalism — judged by what sentients actually do, not by credentials or institutional status — including:
    - 取材；
    - 情報源の保護；
    - 調査；および
    - 公表。
    Sentients doing that work exercise the [表現](core_05_band_participation.md#expression-constitutional) and [結社](core_05_band_participation.md#assembly-constitutional) 権利の床, with heightened-scrutiny protection under [Article V-H](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-h-expression-assembly-and-press) (*Expression, Assembly, and Press*) against state and operator actions meant to impair that activity.
  - **範囲外：** press credentials, institutional titles, or ordinary commercial speech that is not newsgathering, investigation, or publication functioning as journalism.
<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： journalistic function and press-directed impairment remain visible and contestable — not hidden behind credential, title, or institutional status.

    **第一次の評価：** Ask what the activity actually does — not what badge, title, org form, or institution someone holds — covering:
    - 取材；
    - 情報源の保護；
    - investigation; or
    - publication that serves the information environment.
    Spot actions that impair the press by their real effect, and treat press-directed character as an aggravating factor under **Article XIII-A** (*Security, Intelligence, and Covert-Power Limits*) and **Article V-H** (*Expression, Assembly, and Press*). Apply heightened [必要性](core_05_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [比例性](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality) scrutiny, and keep any limit narrowly tailored.
<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** state or operator actions meant to impair journalistic activity that fail heightened-scrutiny limits analysis;
  - **第二次の失敗：** stretching [善意](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith) or **Article VIII-C** (*Truthful Publication and High-Impact Publication Limits*) so those standards block lawful critical reporting, investigative publication, or dissent;
  - **第三次の失敗：** credentials, institutional status, or ordinary commercial speech used as the journalism test while newsgathering, investigation, or publication that functions as journalism is absent.

<a id="oversight-semi-independent-terms"></a>
### 監督：半独立用語

<a id="materiality-semi-independent"></a>

<a id="materiality-impact-risk-and-proxy-integrity"></a>
#### 実質性、影響、リスク、および代替指標の誠実性

**半独立の文脈**（複合定義は、共同呼び出しの範囲の外でもなお作用してよい）：

- **共同呼び出し：** where materiality classification, material impact or risk, proxy integrity, or material degradation of a protected condition is material.
- **Scope:** where joint invocation is met, the Oversight home for two questions — when do effects matter enough constitutionally, and do the metrics still track reality? Evaluation must rest on real effects — not on manipulable proxies.

**主題群の成員。** この群は次を含む：
- [実質](core_05_band_oversight.md#material) — whether effects are big enough to matter constitutionally.
- [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — the method for classifying that, for systems or events.
- [実質性の誠実性制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) — bars gaming boundaries to shrink the picture.
- [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) — how far, how large, and how severe the effects are.
- [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) — how likely, how fast, and how hard to undo the harm may be.
- [不確実性のもとでの実質性](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-under-uncertainty) — defaults to caution when the answer is unclear.
- [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — flags when a metric no longer matches reality, including when the score is cooked while conditions worsen.
- [実質的劣化](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-degradation) — a real drop in a protected condition such as safety, epistemic integrity, or meaningful agency.

<a id="materiality-operator-phrases"></a>

**運用者フレーズ** (corpus-wide reading aids owned with [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)):

- <a id="materially-implicated"></a>**実質的に関わる** — this duty, measure, or topic is *on the table* for the case because the stake is material.
- <a id="materially-misaligned"></a>**実質的に不整合** / **憲法上の不整合** — conduct or structure is *out of constitutional alignment* at a material scale.

Implication can exist without misalignment. Misalignment always implies material implication.

*測定（ファミリーの経路づけ）：* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on the member definitions below.

<a id="material"></a>

#### 実質

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); 原則： [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (**Materiality** DEC stack; evaluation completeness); [6.1 中核の取引原則](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#61-core-tradeoff-principles) (proportionality stack read with material effects).
- あわせて読む： [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), and [分類に応じた統治](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： big enough to matter — not trivia, cosmetics, or paperwork that changes nothing real.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Conditions that can produce meaningful [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk).
  - **範囲外：** Trivia, cosmetics, or mere paperwork with no meaningful material impact or material risk.
<a id="material-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — threshold input for [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).

    **第一次の評価：** Ask whether the conditions can produce meaningful [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) when you look at the whole system, not just a slice.
  - **第二次の測定：** [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), and [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) — co-measures that can defeat material classification when effects are fragmented or understated.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 ask whether effects that look small one by one would still look small if you put them together — including system-wide [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) pathways.
<a id="material-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** ignoring conditions that [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) would treat as material.
  - **第二次の失敗：** calling individually small effects immaterial when, taken together or system-wide, they would count as material.

<a id="materiality-determination"></a>

#### 実質性の判定

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); 原則： [実質的利害](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling proxy; 第一章 surfaces this definition across **Safety** (**第一章 §8.1**), **Truth** (**第一章 §8.2**), **Trust** (**§12**), **§12** stewardship and openness passages, **§13.1**, **§13.2**, and **第一章 §6.3.2** where **Materiality** appears in D/A/C widgets — see [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) as the principal systems-evaluation home.
- クラスター構成要素： [実質性、影響、リスク、および代替指標の誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- あわせて読む： [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality), and [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： the method for deciding whether an impact or risk is constitutionally material — big enough in stake to change which duties apply — for a system or an event, looking at the connected whole, not just isolated pieces. That includes prospective harm, not only harm that has already happened.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Classification decisions that ask whether [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) is constitutionally material for:
    - a system (including [システム的実質性](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality)); or
    - an event, incident, or episode (including prospective [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) under [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk)),
    when you look at the connected whole rather than isolated pieces.
  - **範囲外：**
    - [実質](core_05_band_oversight.md#material) threshold trivia, cosmetics, or mere paperwork significance with no stake-scaled duty change; or
    - ordinary classification disagreement that still evaluates the connected whole under [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) —
    それらは各自の本拠に属する or remain ordinary disagreement, not a substitute determination.
<a id="materiality-determination-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** Under [実質的利害](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake), ask whether effects are big enough to change which duties apply when you look at the connected whole — for the system or event at issue — covering:
    - [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) (including prospective [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm)); and
    - [システム的実質性](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality) (when system-wide effects are in play).

    **第一次の評価：** Include all kinds of effects that matter for the case, covering:
    - realized impact;
    - prospective harm; and
    - [システム的実質性](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality) where relevant,
    and explain the conclusion for the connected whole, not just a convenient slice.

    For an event, ask whether it is relevant to a sentient's [福祉](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing) — including plausible prospective harm to that wellbeing — not only to:
    - system 指標；
    - process labels; or
    - institutional convenience.
  - **第二次の測定：** [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) — check whether calling something "not material" only works because the pieces were judged separately, while the connected whole would count as material. For an event affecting a sentient, include downstream effects that travel through dependency — not only the immediate hit on that sentient.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 ask whether the classification would change if you looked at the pieces together instead of one by one, including:
    - isolation;
    - aggregation;
    - dependency; and
    - for an event, downstream effects on other sentients or systems that depend on the affected sentient or on what the event disrupted.
  - **第三次の測定：** [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) and [実質性の誠実性制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) — where boundary games, aggregation avoidance, or proxy metrics defeat honest materiality analysis.

    **第三次の評価：** Where operators lean on:
    - boundaries;
    - proxies; or
    - informal low class,
    apply [実質性の誠実性制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) and [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate real materiality analysis from structural manipulation or score gaming.
<a id="materiality-determination-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - A Materiality Determination holds only if the classification is written down, [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and tied to effects that actually matter constitutionally.
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - paper-only or immaterial labels with no real change to which constitutional duties apply — governance, oversight, capability requirements, or other stake-scaled duties for the system or event at issue;
    - determinations that skip material effect types — including prospective harm for an event, or whether the event is relevant to a sentient's [福祉](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing); or
    - determinations that lack a justified conclusion.
  - **第二次の失敗：** calling collectively material effects immaterial by:
    - splitting them up;
    - avoiding aggregation; or
    - ignoring dependency.
  - **第三次の失敗：** determinations that rest on manipulated boundaries or "the metric looks fine" where [実質性の誠実性制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) or [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint"></a>

#### 実質性の誠実性制約

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 原則： [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (aggregation and boundary integrity in evaluation); [§6.2.4 代替指標乖離による無効化](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#624-proxy-divergence-invalidation) (metric/reality gaps that can manipulate materiality).
- あわせて読む： [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [説明責任](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： you may not make a big problem look small by redrawing boxes, splitting pieces, or reclassifying your way out of it.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A ban on shrinking materiality by manipulating [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), aggregation, or classification contrary to [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity).
  - **範囲外：** Ordinary disagreement about materiality, or honest boundary clarification that does not shrink collectively material effects through splitting, aggregation games, or reclassification.
<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — ask whether anyone is making a material problem look smaller by redrawing boxes, splitting pieces, or reclassifying around it.

    **第一次の評価：** Look for structural moves that would defeat honest [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), including:
    - splitting;
    - aggregation avoidance; and
    - boundary games.
  - **第二次の測定：** [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — co-measures that defeat manipulation or proxy gaming of materiality.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 include [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) paths that would hide collectively material effects.
<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** treating collectively material effects as immaterial through structural manipulation.
  - **第二次の失敗：** boundary or aggregation games that defeat [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), or relying on "the metric looks fine" where [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="material-impact"></a>

#### 実質的影響

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); 原則： [実質的利害](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling proxy; [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation); [§9.2 分散した理解](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#92-distributed-understanding) (understanding scales with **Materiality**); [6.1.3 比例](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#613-proportionality) (material impact in the tradeoff index).
- クラスター構成要素： [実質性、影響、リスク、および代替指標の誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- あわせて読む： [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [分類に応じた統治](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), and [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： how far the effects reach, how big they are, and how severe — looking at the whole system, not just the headline or the paperwork.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Effects for [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and constitutional evaluation, covering:
    - how far they reach;
    - how large they are; and
    - how severe they are.
  - **範囲外：**
    - mere attention, visibility, or paperwork significance with no reach, size, or severity stake; or
    - prospective [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) analysis —
    それは自身の本拠に属する.
<a id="material-impact-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — scope-and-severity input for materiality classification.

    **第一次の評価：** Trace how far, how large, and how severe the effects are for the whole system — including:
    - direct 効果；
    - indirect 効果；
    - delayed 効果；
    - cumulative 効果； and
    - cross-system consequences.
  - **第二次の測定：** [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), and [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) — co-measures that can defeat impact assessment when looking only at pieces understates effects.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 include [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and cross-system spread that would change the material classification.
<a id="material-impact-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - prospective-risk talk used in place of actual reach, size, or severity analysis; or
    - material impacts that do not trigger the right constraints under [分類に応じた統治](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), and [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency).
  - **第二次の失敗：** fragmented or dependency-hidden effects labeled immaterial when joint analysis would classify them as material.

<a id="material-risk"></a>

#### 実質的リスク

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); 原則： [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (tail risks, existential risk posture); [6.1.3 比例](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#613-proportionality) (**Risk**, **Irreversible Harm**, **Existential Risk** index).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： how likely harm is, how fast it can arrive, and how hard it is to undo — including rare but severe outcomes.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Prospective [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), covering:
    - how likely it is;
    - how fast it can arrive; and
    - how hard it is to undo.
  - **範囲外：**
    - speculative worry without a plausible harm pathway; or
    - realized [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) already in hand —
    that sits under Harm / [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), not prospective risk alone.
<a id="material-risk-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**

    **第一次の評価：** Looking at the whole system, include:
    - expected outcomes;
    - rare-but-severe (tail) outcomes;
    - how fast [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) can arrive; and
    - how irreversible that harm can be.
  - **第二次の測定：** [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [不可逆的危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — co-measures that can defeat risk classification when tail or systemic pathways are ignored.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 include [不可逆的危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) paths that would change the material-risk classification.
<a id="material-risk-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - realized harm labeled as only future risk; or
    - material risk not addressed and constrained under [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [分類に応じた統治](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance).
  - **第二次の失敗：** tail or irreversible pathways excluded when [不可逆的危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) remains plausible.

<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty"></a>

#### 不確実性のもとでの実質性

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 原則： [6.1.3 比例](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#613-proportionality) (reversibility under uncertainty); [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (precaution against omitted harms).

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： when you are not sure whether something is material, default to the safer (higher) classification — do not wait for certainty or hope for a low label.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** What to do by default when [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) is uncertain.
  - **範囲外：**
    - cases where materiality is already settled with no remaining material uncertainty; or
    - ordinary [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) once uncertainty is not material —
    those sit under settled classification, not this precautionary default.
<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — precautionary default when materiality cannot be excluded.

    **第一次の評価：** Default to precaution when you cannot rule materiality out — including when [不可逆的危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) remains plausible under uncertainty.
  - **第二次の測定：** [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [不可逆的危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm), and [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — co-measures that require conservative classification under uncertainty.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 include [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), and [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) paths that would require a conservative default classification.
<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - waiting for certainty before classifying while material impact or risk remains uncertain;
    - optimistic low classification while material impact or risk remains uncertain; or
    - failure to default to conservative classification under uncertainty where [分類に応じた統治](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) requires it.
  - **第二次の失敗：** optimism or informal low classification where [不可逆的危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) or [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires precaution.

<a id="proxy-divergence"></a>

#### 代替指標の乖離

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： Oversight measurement family (*Truth and epistemic integrity as constitutional measurement*); Accountability measurement family (*Incentive alignment and proxy integrity as constitutional measurement*); 原則： [§6.2.4 代替指標乖離による無効化](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#624-proxy-divergence-invalidation), [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation), and [§10 責務ある管理の規律のもとでの統治](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#10-governance-under-stewardship-discipline).
- クラスター構成要素： [実質性、影響、リスク、および代替指標の誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- あわせて読む： [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [実質性の誠実性制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint), [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [誘因整合](core_05_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： when the score no longer matches reality — a dashboard, class label, or compliance indicator that says one thing while the real condition says another — including cooking the score so it looks fine while conditions stay bad, get worse, or go unmeasured.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A material gap between a proxy and the constitutionally material reality it claims to represent, covering:
    - the gap itself; and
    - conduct, design, reporting, or incentives that improve, preserve, or manipulate the proxy while the underlying reality stays degraded, worsens, or is not measured.
    Proxies include:
    - 指標；
    - model signals;
    - formal class;
    - compliance indicators; and
    - administrative shorthand.
    Claimed realities include rights, risks, burdens, harms, capabilities, and outcomes.
  - **範囲外：** Ordinary measurement noise; a small non-material gap that does not detach the proxy from constitutionally relevant reality; or honest metric improvement that tracks real outcomes.
<a id="proxy-divergence-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) and [説明責任の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability-measurement-family) — *Can sentients see, verify, and rely on what systems represent?* / *報酬構造、市場権力、応答義務は、義務を本物に保つか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： proxies still track constitutionally material reality — and whether reward, reporting, or gating still rests on those proxies after they diverge, including when the score is being gamed or protected.

    **第一次の評価：** Ask whether optimization, reporting, gating, or review still leans on a proxy after evidence shows that proxy no longer tracks the constitutionally material reality. Look for score-cooking moves, including:
    - dashboard optimization;
    - threshold gaming;
    - selective sampling;
    - denominator manipulation;
    - excluded populations;
    - automated score improvement;
    - model reward hacking;
    - compliance theater; and
    - narrative substitution.
    A metric is not reliable when it cannot be traced to observed material outcomes, or when incentives predictably pull it away from those outcomes.
  - **第二次の測定：** [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [実質性の誠実性制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint), and [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) — co-measures that can defeat proxy integrity even when reported indicators look stable.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 look for:
    - selection 効果；
    - boundary manipulation;
    - missing populations;
    - feedback loops;
    - gaming incentives;
    - adversarial adaptation; and
    - aggregate harms hidden by favorable local indicators —
    together with [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).
  - **第三次の測定：** [福祉](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and 権利の床 outcomes — where proxies stand in for what sentients actually experience.

    **第三次の評価：** Where proxies stand in for outcome measures, trace to [福祉](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), or 権利の床 delivery — separate durable constitutional outcomes from proxy throughput.
<a id="proxy-divergence-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - A system satisfies **Proxy Divergence** discipline only if proxies stay aligned with constitutionally material reality under full-system conditions — including when incentives or design would otherwise cook the score.
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - treating a proxy as decisive when the gap is material;
    - claiming compliance from improved metrics while material outcomes stay deficient;
    - designing indicators that can be satisfied without satisfying the underlying duty; or
    - suppressing evidence that indicators have come detached from reality.
  - **第二次の失敗：** using "the metric looks fine" to skip direct evaluation of 権利の床, safety, truth, wellbeing, ecological, or agency outcomes while divergence is reasonably observable.
  - **第三次の失敗：** refusing correction after the gap between reported indicators and what sentients actually experience becomes reasonably observable.

<a id="material-degradation"></a>

#### 実質的劣化

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 原則： [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation); [第一章 §3 交渉不能の原則制約：安全と真理](core_01_a_values_principles.md#3-non-negotiable-constraints-safety-and-truth) (**Safety** / **Truth** degradation paths).
- あわせて読む： [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), and [意味ある行為主体性](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： a real drop in how well a protected condition holds — safety, honest knowledge, or meaningful agency — not a brief blip, planned maintenance, or a worse score on a dashboard that does not track those conditions.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A non-trivial drop in how well a protected constitutional condition holds, covering:
    - [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint);
    - [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity); or
    - [意味ある行為主体性](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
  - **範囲外：** Trivial dips in operational metrics or system throughput, planned maintenance, or temporary blips that do not meaningfully reduce safety, epistemic integrity, or meaningful agency.
<a id="material-degradation-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** 統合的実質性 ([実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — degradation signal for materiality classification.

    **第一次の評価：** Include scaling and system-wide effects under [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) and [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) when judging a non-trivial drop in protected conditions.
  - **第二次の測定：** [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [システム的実質性](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — co-measures that can defeat degradation assessment when proxies hide decline.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 apply [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) where metrics hide material degradation.
<a id="material-degradation-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** material degradation not treated as material under [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).
  - **第二次の失敗：** keeping proxy indicators looking stable while [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), or [意味ある行為主体性](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) materially degrades.

<a id="defo1"></a>
<a id="transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster"></a>

<a id="defo1-transparency-auditability-and-verification"></a>
#### Def.O1 透明性、監査可能性、検証

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- Constitutional frame: **Oversight** leg; **Flourishing** aim (primary); scales with [実質的利害](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) via [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).
- 第一章 basis: 第一章 §8.2, §4, §12.1, §12.2 (see [羅針](core_05__definitions_home.md#chapter-five-compass-and-definition-map) map).
- あわせて読む： Apply [説明責任](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [手続的公正](core_05_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness-constitutional), [保護された通報（内部告発）](core_05_band_accountability.md#protected-reporting-whistleblowing), [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), and 第二から第四章 mechanics where 実質的に関わる.

</details>

<br>


**入場範囲。** この群は、事案が実質的に次に関わるところで適用される：
- transparency of decision-relevant behavior;
- disclosure of evaluated systemic risk to those who need it;
- evidence preservation;
- auditability and records good enough to reconstruct what happened;
- evaluation completeness against plausible failure modes;
- externally detectable indicators usable for audit and independent verification;
- independent verification; or
- whether verification is accessible and independent under scaled or adversarial conditions.
Outside that scope, individual entries may still apply on their own without importing the whole cluster.

Where admission scope is met, this cluster is the joint home for disclosure, audit, verification (including external detectability), and assurance depth when 第二から第四章 require traceable, challengeable evidence of what a system does and whether compliance claims hold.

**監督の枠。** Under the [憲法四元](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) **oversight** leg, oversight requires auditing — being able to reconstruct what happened, verify it independently, and get audit access — owned here and at **Article XV** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*). [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) is one especially large, high-stakes audit process among others (forum-supervised alignment review with recognition outcomes under [第七章](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eight-system-alignment-certification)); it does not swallow this cluster or replace sibling audit modes.

**システム階級と比例。** Transparency, auditability, and verification duties scale with system class under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — システムの分類と取扱い**, and with material impact, dependency, and risk. Higher-class systems (**Class A**, **B**, **C**) need more than lower-class systems (**Class L**, **P**), including:
- より深い保証；
- より細かい記録；
- より強い独立検証；および
- より広い監査範囲。

**読み順。** Start with what must be disclosed ([透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure)); then whether outsiders can reconstruct ([監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) and audit supports); then whether behavior is externally detectable and independently checkable ([検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability)).

**群の成員。** この群は次を含む：

- [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) — decision-relevant clarity about function, limits, and risk;
- [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) — the required public disclosure floor for high-impact systems, including lawful substitutes;
- [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) — communicating evaluated systemic risk to those who need it;
- [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) — whether outsiders can reconstruct and check what a system did, across the material times, states, and contexts that matter;
- [証拠保全](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation) — identify, secure, and keep materially relevant evidence reviewably available;
- [評価完全性の制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#evaluation-completeness-constraint) — cover plausible failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial pathways before claiming compliance; and
- [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — externally detect and measure behavior and effects, and independently validate what was observed under practical access, independence, proportionate burden, and stressed-condition reliability.

**迂回防止。** Within the admission scope, do not split the matter into separate disclosure, preservation, logging, metrics, audit sampling, verification UX, or independence questions in a way that checks a box on one channel while defeating practical reconstruction, challenge, or proportionate assurance depth.

**共同呼び出しと充足** を見よ。

*測定（ファミリーの経路づけ）：* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) below.

<a id="transparency"></a>

##### 透明性

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- クラスター構成要素： [Def.O1 透明性、監査可能性、検証](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- 下流： 原則： [憲法四元](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — **oversight** leg; [§9 責務ある管理と分散理解](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#9-stewardship-and-distributed-understanding), [6.2.1 認識的誠実性の保全](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#621-preservation-of-epistemic-integrity).
- あわせて読む： [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure), [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), and **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — 情報の型と取扱い** (**Type O** — open public oversight baseline disclosure data).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： telling affected sentients enough about how a system works, its limits, and its risks that they can genuinely understand and question it — not burying them in noise.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Giving affected sentients enough information about how a system behaves outwardly — its limits, uncertainties, and what depends on it — that they can understand what it does and what risks it creates. Legitimate safety and security limits still apply, consistent with [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) and [6.2 認識的開示の制約](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#62-epistemic-disclosure-constraints).
    - If a material system, institution, forum process, or similar governed domain can lawfully publish online, its baseline transparency disclosure must be free to the public at the access level its class requires — not behind a paywall or available only to insiders — wherever [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) applies.
    - If publishing the raw material would break lawful protections, the owner must still publish the strongest public substitute reasonably possible online, without gutting accountability or [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — for example:
      - aggregation;
      - de-identification;
      - summary disclosure;
      - delayed disclosure; or
      - qualified audit access.
  - **範囲外：**
    - [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) reconstruction channels alone;
    - [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) Type O floor mechanics as such; or
    - ordinary commercial marketing that is not decision-relevant disclosure to affected parties —
    それらは各自の本拠に属する or ordinary commercial speech.
<a id="transparency-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： affected parties get decision-relevant clarity about function, limits, and risk — not volume, noise, or insider-only access.

    **第一次の評価：** Compare what is disclosed to [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency).
  - **第二次の測定：** [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — co-measures that can defeat transparency even when disclosure volume is high.

    **第二次の評価：** When checking the primary answer:
    - spot volume, obscurity, or formatting standing in for decision-relevant clarity; and
    - apply safety- and security-justified limits consistent with [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).

    Decision-relevant disclosure fails in practice when it cannot be checked — do not treat unverifiable disclosure as decision-relevant clarity. [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) owns those check conditions, including:
    - practical access;
    - independence from operator self-assertion;
    - proportionate burden; and
    - reliability under stress, misuse, and adversarial pressure.
  - **第三次の測定：** [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where volume, formatting, or procedural opacity stands in for decision-relevant clarity.

    **第三次の評価：** Where operators lean on disclosure volume or formatting, apply [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate real transparency from burying sentients in volume.
<a id="transparency-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - a data dump, mountain of documents, paywall, or insider-only access with no real decision-relevant clarity about function and risk for affected sentients; or
    - material omissions or disclosures that foreseeably block informed participation or [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional) where required.
  - **第二次の失敗：** safety- or security-framed limits that defeat materially required clarity without satisfying [必要性](core_05_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [比例性](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
  - **第三次の失敗：** disclosure volume or formatting treated as decisive where [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="public-oversight-baseline-disclosure"></a>

##### 公的監督の基線開示

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- クラスター構成要素： [Def.O1 透明性、監査可能性、検証](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- 下流： **[CS-2 Part A §7](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-2-7-type-o-baseline-for-class-a-b-c-systems)** (*Type O baseline for Class A/B/C systems*); **[CS-2 Part B — Type O](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_b_data_classifications.md#83-type-o-open-public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-data)** (*typing and O/E/G relationship*); [§4](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-data-types-and-handling-evaluation) (*Data Types and Handling Evaluation*); [§11](../../core_08_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#11-system-certification-record) (*Certification record*); [§12](../../core_08_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#12-transparency-auditability-and-contestability) (*Transparency, auditability, and contestability*).
- あわせて読む： [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure), [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional), [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional), [システム分類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional), [システムデータ種類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional), [分類に応じた統治](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), and **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — 情報の型と取扱い** (**Type O**).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： 高影響システムが感知者に理解・監督・異議を可能にするために公表しなければならない最小の公的開示 — 目的、階級、依存、状態、リスク、性能、失敗、統治、影響を受ける側への効果、憲法整合、およびチャーター・分類・データ型付け・認証記録の公的な顔を含む — 生の私的または基線外記録を出せないときは強い公的代替とともに。*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** The required public disclosure floor so sentients can oversee and challenge materially impactful systems — especially **Class A**, **Class B**, and **Class C** under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — システムの分類と取扱い**. Coverage follows the governing [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument), assigned class, and observed [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries). It includes what sentients need to understand:
    - purpose and what the system does;
    - how it is classified;
    - dependency structure and what depends on it;
    - operational status and how it is running;
    - material risks, performance, and failures;
    - how it is governed and material audit outcomes;
    - stakeholder 効果； and
    - how aligned it is with this Constitution.
  - **Class A/B/C Type O instruments:** Where the **Type O** baseline applies for **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C**, that floor includes — at public-baseline fidelity, including lawful substitutes — publication or a usable public view of:
    - the governing [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument);
    - the [システム分類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional);
    - the [システムデータ種類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional); and
    - the [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) produced by [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional), including material governance and audit outcomes required for baseline visibility.
  - **Topic satisfaction:** Baseline visibility into purpose, operational status, material risk, performance, failures, and stakeholder effects may be satisfied through those records, [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) where systemic risk is in scope, and other lawful **Type O** summaries or substitutes — not by omitting the topics.
  - **Lawful substitutes:** Stay in scope where **Type G**, **Type E**, or other source records stay non-public, non-baseline, or otherwise restricted, including:
    - aggregation;
    - de-identification;
    - summary or delayed disclosure; and
    - comparable artifacts.
  - **Type O / Type E / Type G relationship:**
    - This disclosure floor is typed and handled as **Type O**.
    - **Type E** holds survival- and coordination-critical content streams; **Type G** holds audit-accessible governance and operational source that is not public by default.
    - Neither **Type E** nor **Type G** is itself the public floor.
    - Published baseline artifacts drawn from them — including substitutes — are **Type O**; underlying streams may remain under their original type.
    - Systems-layer typing, substitute mechanics, and holding-back limits are owned by **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — 情報の型と取扱い** as **Type O**.
  - **範囲外：**
    - raw **Type G**, **Type E**, or other restricted source records — publishing a public substitute derived from them does not bring those raw records into this disclosure floor;
    - [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) structured or qualified audit access as such — that is not itself the public floor; or
    - [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) of evaluated systemic risk to those who need it —
    それらは各自の本拠に属する.
<a id="public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： the required public disclosure floor is actually published and usable for oversight and challenge — including lawful substitutes where raw records stay restricted.

    **第一次の評価：** Compare published disclosure to certified scope from [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), assigned class, and [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries). Where the **Type O** baseline applies for a **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** system under **[CS-2 Part A §7](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-2-7-type-o-baseline-for-class-a-b-c-systems)**, confirm:
    - a usable public view of the [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [システム分類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional), [システムデータ種類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional), and [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) (or required contents) at public-baseline fidelity;
    - that purpose, dependency structure, operational status, material risks, performance, failures, governance, audit outcomes, stakeholder effects, and constitutional compliance are each covered at baseline depth;
    - that lawful substitutes still support meaningful accountability and [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability); and
    - that free online availability holds where lawful publication infrastructure exists.
  - **第二次の測定：** [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [リスク開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) — co-measures that can defeat the floor even when some public artifacts exist.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 look for:
    - thin summaries;
    - audit-only substitution;
    - coverage gaps against certified scope;
    - missing or non-usable public views of required Charter, classification, data-types, or certification 記録；
    - missing baseline visibility into performance, failures, stakeholder effects, or material risk; and
    - hold-backs that hide systemic behavior, constitutional violations, material risk, dependency, failure, or externalized cost.
<a id="public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** missing or inadequate Public Oversight Baseline Disclosure where the **Type O** baseline for **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** applies under CS-2.
  - **第二次の失敗：**
    - non-public **Type G** audit access, paywalls, or performative summaries used as the public floor;
    - performative summaries that withhold decision-relevant baseline material;
    - paywalled or insider-only distribution of the floor where lawful online publication infrastructure exists for class-appropriate public access;
    - omitting a usable public view of the [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [システム分類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional), [システムデータ種類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional), or [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) where the **Type O** baseline applies for a **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** system under **[CS-2 Part A §7](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-2-7-type-o-baseline-for-class-a-b-c-systems)**; or
    - omitting baseline visibility into purpose, dependency structure, operational status, material risks, performance, failures, governance, audit outcomes, stakeholder effects, or constitutional compliance.
  - **第三次の失敗：** hold-backs that are not narrow, documented, proportionate, auditable, and challengeable under CS-2 and [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency).

##### リスク開示

- あわせて読む： [リスク評価](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), and [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： tell the sentients who need to know about systemic risk — keeping the evaluation in a drawer does not count.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Communicating evaluated systemic [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) to those who need it so they can understand, challenge, and act.
    - **Ongoing duty:** The duty is not limited to certification cycles — it applies whenever [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) or [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires that evaluated risk reach the sentients who need it.
    - **SAC verification:** Where [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs and systemic risk is in scope, this disclosure is part of what certification must verify under [§3.1](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors); findings live on the [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional), not as a separate named risk-disclosure record.
  - **範囲外：**
    - [リスク評価](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation) itself — the duty to evaluate systemic risk;
    - [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) as the broader public baseline floor; or
    - findings placement on the [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) rather than a peer record species beside the [システム分類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional) or [システムデータ種類記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional) —
    それらは各自の本拠に属する.
<a id="risk-disclosure-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： evaluated systemic risk actually reaches the sentients who need it — not only whether an evaluation exists somewhere.

    **第一次の評価：** Ask whether disclosure is timely, decision-relevant, and sized to 実質的利害 under [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency). Read with [リスク評価](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation) — disclosure without evaluation, or evaluation without disclosure, both fail.
  - **第二次の測定：** [リスク評価](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), and [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) — other checks that can show disclosure failed even when some risk wording is on file.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 look for walls of text, buried details, insider-only channels, or late notice that keep the sentients who needed the risk picture from actually understanding it in time.
<a id="risk-disclosure-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - failure to disclose risk where [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) or [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires it; or
    - [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only context in which risk must be disclosed.
  - **第二次の失敗：** an internal-only note, buried appendix, or after-the-fact statement used as disclosure when the sentients who needed the risk picture could not use it in time.

##### 監査可能性

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- クラスター構成要素： [Def.O1 透明性、監査可能性、検証](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- あわせて読む： [憲法四元](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — **oversight** leg; [説明責任](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [証拠保全](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and [評価完全性の制約](core_05_band_oversight.md#evaluation-completeness-constraint).
- あわせて読む： **Article XV** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*); [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) (*one especially large audit process under oversight — not the sole auditing home*); owner-layer mechanics in [core_02_definition_structure.md](core_02_definition_structure.md) 第二から第四章.
- Steward door (non-operative): This definition is the property layer of the audit stack. Binding next-step statement: [Operative steward statement (Article XV)](../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#operative-steward-statement-audit). Three-layer picture: [Article XV](../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#audit-three-layers).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： **auditability** is whether outsiders can actually reconstruct and check what a system did — across the real times, states, and situations that matter, not just one happy-path snapshot. System alignment certification is one especially large way that property gets exercised for high-stakes systems; it is not the only auditing process.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Whether outsiders can systematically examine, reconstruct, and evaluate system behavior through enough [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — including external detectability and independent validation.
    - **Coverage:** Must span the material times, states, and interaction contexts — not only a single snapshot or happy-path sample.
    - **Oversight role:** Under the **oversight** Tetrad leg, oversight requires this property; [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) is one especially large, high-stakes audit process that depends on it, among sibling audit modes.
    - **Operational capability:** Where binding duties require operational capability, auditability includes checking that claimed compliance rests on real, reconstructable capability — not text, assertions, or unimplemented controls alone ([権限スタックと内部階層](core_05_band_integrative.md#authority-stack) supremacy effect).
  - **範囲外：**
    - raw logs or [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) external detectability alone;
    - [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) or [公的監督の基線開示](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) public disclosure alone; or
    - leaf measurement tiers and implementation mechanics owned by [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [証拠保全](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and CJS —
    それらは各自の本拠に属する; they are not themselves reconstructable auditability.
<a id="auditability-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： outsiders can reconstruct and check what the system did across the material times, states, and contexts — including whether claimed compliance rests on real capability, not paper alone.

    **第一次の評価：** It must work across relevant conditions and [影響を受ける側](core_05_band_participation.md#stakeholder), including conditions relevant to [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) and [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional). Look for material failures, [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and 憲法上の不整合 across:
    - [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries);
    - operating 状態； and
    - [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions).
    Where a binding duty requires operational capability, ask whether claimed compliance is backed by verified capability — not:
      - text;
      - assertions;
      - paper controls;
      - emergency practice; or
      - unobservable claims.
  - **第二次の測定：** [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [証拠保全](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — other checks that can show auditability failed even when some audit channel exists on paper.

    **第二次の評価：** When checking the primary answer:
    - where stewardship relies on repeated comparable measures, audit paths should support reviewing variation over time and threshold-triggered follow-through when claimed ([§9.3 制度的発展](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#93-institutional-development); [比例性](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality)); and
    - confirm audit coverage reaches the evaluation criteria, components, dependencies, actors, conditions, and effects that matter — and is available for independent [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) and [監督](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional).
  - **第三次の測定：** [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where sampling, metrics, or ceremonial review stands in for practical reconstruction.

    **第三次の評価：** Where operators lean on check-the-box audit channels or thin sampling, apply [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — ask whether outsiders can really reconstruct what happened, or whether the review is mostly for show.
<a id="auditability-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - audit mechanisms that do not work;
    - failure to enable practical reconstruction and evaluation;
    - a single snapshot, happy-path sample, or narrow window that leaves out material times, states, or interaction contexts;
    - [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only auditing process or as a substitute for this property;
    - paper claims of constitutional compliance that cannot be reconstructed or challenged; or
    - any of the following used as enough compliance where binding duties require verified operational capability:
      - text;
      - assertions;
      - unimplemented controls;
      - support artifacts;
      - private instruments;
      - emergency practice; or
      - unobservable claims.
  - **第二次の失敗：**
    - audit scope that leaves out any of the following when materially relevant:
      - evaluation criteria;
      - system components;
      - dependencies;
      - 行為者；
      - conditions;
      - 効果；
      - 状態；
      - contexts; or
      - [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions);
    - supremacy or compliance claims while suppressing [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) or defeating auditable verification.
  - **第三次の失敗：** audit sampling or "the metric looks fine" treated as decisive where [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="evidence-preservation"></a>

##### 証拠保全

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- 下流： 原則： [第一章 §3 交渉不能の原則制約：安全と真理](core_01_a_values_principles.md#3-non-negotiable-constraints-safety-and-truth), [§6 過程衝突の解決](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#6-process-conflict-resolution), and [6.2.1 認識的誠実性の保全](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#621-preservation-of-epistemic-integrity), where materially relevant evidence must remain available for truth, safety, review, and correction.
- クラスター構成要素： [Def.O1 透明性、監査可能性、検証](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- あわせて読む： [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [説明責任](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [手続的公正](core_05_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness-constitutional), [保護された通報（内部告発）](core_05_band_accountability.md#protected-reporting-whistleblowing), and [救済と是正](core_05_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation-constitutional).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： keep the evidence that matters — identify it, secure it, and make it available for review — once a serious constitutional issue is known or should be known.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A duty to identify, secure, maintain, and make reviewably available materially relevant evidence needed for constitutional review, contestability, accountability, remediation, misconduct routing, or standing assessment, including:
    - 記録；
    - artifacts;
    - logs;
    - provenance data;
    - testimony;
    - system 状態；
    - custody information; and
    - exculpatory or mitigating material.
  - **範囲外：** Ordinary operational retention or backup that does not secure, maintain, and make reviewably available the material needed for review, contest, remedy, or standing measurement.
<a id="evidence-preservation-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： materially relevant evidence remains secured and reviewably available for audit, contest, remedy, and standing measurement.

    **第一次の評価：** Preservation must be prompt, sized to materiality and risk, custody-traceable, tamper-evident where feasible, and protective of privacy, safety, security, and protected reporting. Once a material constitutional issue is reasonably known or should be known, it includes preventing:
    - deletion;
    - alteration;
    - selective retention;
    - access obstruction; or
    - loss of reconstructability.
<a id="evidence-preservation-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** any of the following done to materially relevant evidence in a way that defeats auditability, contestability, accountability, remedy access, protected reporting, due-process review, or standing measurement:
    - destroying;
    - altering;
    - withholding;
    - selectively preserving;
    - making inaccessible; or
    - failing to secure.

<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint"></a>

##### 評価完全性の制約

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

- クラスター構成要素： [Def.O1 透明性、監査可能性、検証](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).

- あわせて読む： [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional), [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), [システムの創出](core_05_band_participation.md#system-creation-constitutional), and [事業の創出](core_05_band_participation.md#business-creation-constitutional).

</details>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： do not claim compliance from a narrow demo suite — evaluation must cover the failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial paths that matter. A Charter states intended scope; certification checks that the hard paths were actually evaluated.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A constraint that evaluation and assurance work must cover materially plausible pathways before compliance claims rest on selectively narrow scenario sets, including:
    - 失敗；
    - 誤用；
    - interaction; and
    - adversarial pathways.
  - **Ongoing duty:** The constraint applies whenever operators, stewards, or reviewers claim constitutional compliance, readiness, or safe reliance from evaluation or assurance work — not only during certification cycles.
  - **Charter and scope process:** Where a governing [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument) exists for a system, institution, business, or comparable stewarded form, evaluation must test chartered purpose and in-scope / out-of-scope limits against observed [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries) and plausible pathways inside and at the edge of that scope. Charter text does not shrink the pathways that must be evaluated.
  - **SAC verification:** Where [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs, this constraint is part of what certification must verify under [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) — including chartered-scope-versus-functional-boundary checks under [§3.1](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors) and class-scaled testing / regression coverage under [CS-5](../../corpus_systems/cs_05_design_testing_verification_deployment.md). Findings live on the [システム認証記録](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional).
  - **範囲外：**
    - [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) text as a scope instrument alone — a Charter states intended scope; it is not itself evaluation coverage;
    - [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) process mechanics as such — certification may verify this constraint; it is not the only context in which the constraint applies; or
    - ordinary unit or feature tests that are not offered as complete constitutional evaluation.
<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： evaluation covers plausible failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial pathways — not only selective demo scenarios.

    **第一次の評価：** Show enough coverage of plausible pathways, sized to system impact, [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), including:
    - 失敗；
    - 誤用；
    - interaction; and
    - [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions) where material.
    Also ask:
      - where a [チャーター](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) exists, whether evaluation tested chartered scope against observed boundaries rather than treating the Charter as a complete scenario list; and
      - where [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs, whether whole-system evaluation and record findings under [§3](../../core_08_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) reflect that coverage.
<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - selective demo scenarios or marketing test suites used as complete evaluation while plausible failure, misuse, interaction, or adversarial pathways remain skipped;
    - incomplete or selectively scoped evaluation that defeats [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), or [システム境界の誠実性](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity); or
    - [システム整合認証](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only context in which evaluation completeness applies.
  - **第二次の失敗：** Charter text, institutional mandate language, business-purpose statements, or a narrow certification demo suite used as complete evaluation while any of the following remain unevaluated:
    - 失敗；
    - 誤用；
    - interaction; or
    - adversarial pathways.

##### 検証可能性

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">追跡</span></strong></summary>

<br>

*平たい言葉で言えば： outsiders can detect and measure what the system does, and someone independent can actually check those observations in practice — not only what operators see inside, take the operator's word for it, or hold a verification right on paper.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Whether system behavior, outputs, and effects can be externally detected, measured, and independently validated for [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability). Constitutive conditions:
    - external detectability before independent validation counts;
    - practical access ([Article XV-C](../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xv-c-verification-accessibility); [実行可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#feasibility));
    - independence from operator self-assertion;
    - proportionate burden under [比例性](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality) and [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact); and
    - reliability under ordinary stress, misuse, and [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions).
  - **範囲外：**
    - internal telemetry or operator-facing instrumentation alone; or
    - [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) reconstruction duties as such —

**入場範囲。** この群は、事案が実質的に次に関わるところで適用される：
- truthful status representation;
- materially misleading communication;
- suppression or distortion of decision-relevant information;
- scientific or empirical integrity for high-impact claims;
- stated methods and 限度；
- uncertainty treatment;
- foreseeable operating conditions;
- adversarial or scaled misuse contexts; or
- splitting truth and integrity duties across disclosure, audit, verification, or publication pathways.
Outside that scope, component definitions may still apply on their own without importing the whole cluster.

入場範囲が成り立つところでは、この群は決定に関わる事実と限度の誠実な表示の本拠であり、証拠、不確実性、開示、異議のための誠実な方法とともにある。方法、不確実性の扱い、または予見可能な条件の分析がなお失敗するとき、開示または公表の箱を一つ点検するだけでは足りない。

**群の成員。** この群は次を含む：

- [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) — honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain in decision-relevant claims;
- [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) — methods and evidence bases stay honest and checkable;
- [予見可能性の勤勉と合理的に予見可能な](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence-and-reasonably-foreseeable) — nested sub-block for foreseeability mechanics read together with **Truth** and **Epistemic Integrity** (not a separate §3 cluster head); and
- [公表と高影響の通信](core_05_band_oversight.md#publication-and-high-impact-communication) — nested sub-block for publication-conduct floors read together with **Truth**, **Epistemic Integrity**, and **Foreseeability** (not a separate §3 cluster head).

**迂回防止。** 入場範囲内で、事案を別個の開示、公表、方法、不確実性、誠実性、検証、または予見可能性の問いに、一つを充足しつつ他を打ち負かす仕方で分節化するな。予見可能な条件の分析、方法の誠実性、または限度と不確実さについての誠実さが実質的に欠けたままでは、開示または公表規則の名目上の遵守では足りない。

<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor"></a>

##### 公表の真実性と無謀の床

*平たい言葉で言えば： if you publish something as fact, do not knowingly lie — and do not recklessly ignore accuracy, uncertainty, context, or material interpretive limits.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Bounds on communicative conduct when publishing or communicating as a factual assertion within the [情報圏](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere).
  - **範囲外：**
    - opinion, commentary, or satire that is not presented as a factual assertion; and
    - good-faith error that is neither knowing falsity nor reckless disregard for accuracy, uncertainty, context, or material interpretive limits.
<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： factual publication stays honest about accuracy, uncertainty, context, and interpretive limits — distinguishing knowing falsity or reckless disregard from good-faith observation.

    **第一次の評価：** Separate knowing falsity, or reckless disregard for:
    - accuracy;
    - uncertainty;
    - context; or
    - material interpretive limits,
    from good-faith observation and bounded interpretation, under [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) and [善意](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith).
<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - Good-faith publication of observations, evidence, and bounded interpretations is governed by 第十一章 **Articles VIII**, **XII**, and **XIII** as applicable and must satisfy this cluster jointly under [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [善意](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith), and [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
  - **第一次の失敗：** knowingly false publication, or publication made with reckless disregard for:
    - accuracy;
    - uncertainty;
    - context; or
    - material interpretive limits.

<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint"></a>

##### 保護されたデータと内部状態の公表制約

*平たい言葉で言えば： do not publish restricted data or expose another sentient's protected inner cognitive or emotional states.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Limits on publishing content that violates restricted-data rules or exposes protected internal states within the [情報圏](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere).
  - **範囲外：** Ordinary public facts, lawfully released information, or publication limited for systemic-harm reasons rather than for protected data or internal-state exposure.
<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： publication respects restricted-data rules and protected internal-state boundaries — not reconstructing or exposing protected cognitive or emotional states.

    **第一次の評価：** Publication must not bypass **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — 情報の型と取扱い**, [プライバシー（情報的）](core_05_band_continuity.md#privacy-informational), [保護された内部状態の境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary-constitutional), or **Type N** handling. Except where this Constitution explicitly permits, publication must not reconstruct, infer, or expose another sentient’s protected internal cognitive or emotional states.
<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** publication that violates restricted-data rules, bypasses CS-2 handling, or exposes protected internal states contrary to [プライバシー（情報的）](core_05_band_continuity.md#privacy-informational) and [保護された内部状態の境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary-constitutional).

<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint"></a>

##### 高影響およびシステム的危害の公表制約

*平たい言葉で言えば： even true publication can be constrained when it foreseeably enables targeted harm, coercion, large-scale harmful coordination, or cascading failure.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Publication that goes out through channels that can broadly affect the shared information environment ([情報圏](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere)), cause [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), or shape how sentients coordinate.
  - **範囲外：** Ordinary true reporting without a foreseeable systemic-harm or harmful-coordination pathway, or ordinary truthfulness disputes under the recklessness floor.
<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： publication's primary or foreseeable effects on coordination, safety, and info-sphere integrity stay within proportional distribution constraints.

    **第一次の評価：** Consider primary and [合理的に予見可能な](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) effects on:
    - 調整；
    - [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint);
    - exploitation of vulnerabilities; and
    - [情報圏](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere) integrity.
<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - Even factually accurate publication is constrained where its primary or [合理的に予見可能な](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) effect is to enable:
    - targeted [危害](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm);
    - [強制と操作](core_05_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation-constitutional);
    - large-scale harmful 調整；
    - [連鎖的失敗](core_05_band_continuity.md#cascading-failure); or
    - patterns that materially degrade [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
  - **Class A** and **Class B** systems under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — システムの分類と取扱い**, trigger heightened proportional constraints on distribution mechanics and safeguards.
  - **第一次の失敗：** publishing without the proportional distribution constraints and safeguards required for these primary or reasonably foreseeable effects, including the heightened constraints for **Class A** and **Class B** systems.

<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance"></a>

##### 安全保障に敏感な開示の均衡

*平たい言葉で言えば： weigh the benefit of disclosure against the risk that it helps someone break safeguards or game the system — give good-faith security researchers (white hats) a real route to publish low-risk findings, and wait to publish exploit-enabling detail until the fix is in place.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Disclosure that can enable exploitation of safeguards, including how good-faith security researchers publish findings.
  - **範囲外：** Blanket secrecy with no exploitation tradeoff; ordinary transparency disclosure that does not enable circumvention of safeguards; or permanent hold-back of low-risk researcher publication after mitigation is complete.
<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： disclosure benefits outweigh foreseeable exploitation, circumvention, or adversarial-manipulation risks.

    **第一次の評価：** Balance disclosure benefits against foreseeable risks under [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), consistent with 第二から第四章 and 第一章, covering:
    - exploitation;
    - circumvention; or
    - adversarial manipulation.
  - **第二次の測定：** Good-faith researcher publication routes and deferred exploit-detail timing — co-measures under **Type S** deferred disclosure and **CJS-3.22** (*constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*).

    **第二次の評価：** Assess whether low-risk findings can be published through a usable route, and whether exploit-enabling detail is deferred until security mitigation is complete, consistent with **Type S** deferred disclosure under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — 情報の型と取扱い**, and **CJS-3.22** (*constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*).
<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - Good-faith security researchers must have a usable, non-retaliatory route to publish low-risk information — including existence notice and non-exploit-enabling detail — without treating researcher publication itself as prohibited disclosure.
  - Exploit-enabling or otherwise high-risk security detail must be deferred until security mitigation is complete, or until **Type S** (or equivalent) restriction justification ends under CS-2 — then disclosed under applicable deferred-disclosure and **Type O** summary rules.
  - **第一次の失敗：** consistent with **第二から第四章**, **第一章**, [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and [敵対的、拡大、搾取された条件](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions):
    - publication that materially enables circumvention of safeguards or adversarial manipulation where those risks outweigh the benefits of disclosure;
    - suppressing low-risk good-faith researcher publication; or
    - indefinite hold-back of discloseable detail after mitigation without a still-justified **Type S** (or equivalent) restriction.

<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint"></a>

##### 真理（憲法上の制約）

*平たい言葉で言えば： when claims matter for decisions, be honest about what is known, unknown, and uncertain — do not deceive, distort, or present things in a misleading structure.*

<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-o"></a>
- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A non-negotiable constraint on how systems internally operate and externally communicate where materially decision-relevant claims, representations, risk communications, or compliance assertions are at stake. It requires:
    - honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain;
    - resistance to deception, distortion, and structurally misleading presentation; and
    - assessment methods sized to the stakes under 第二から第四章.
  - **Depends on:** [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — paired non-negotiable constraint under [第一章 §3 交渉不能の原則制約：安全と真理](core_01_a_values_principles.md#3-non-negotiable-constraints-safety-and-truth); truth duties include honest risk and uncertainty communication where safety is materially at stake.
  - **範囲外：**
    - polite tone or procedural courtesy alone; or
    - market prices or odds as ordinary economic signals outside rights, safety, or governance truth determinations —
    those are not this constraint by themselves.
<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： materially decision-relevant claims stay honest about what is known, unknown, and uncertain — resisting deception, distortion, and structurally misleading presentation.

    **第一次の評価：** Judge the real effect on informed decision-making and auditability — scale scrutiny with [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk); require honest methods, honest uncertainty treatment, and revision when evidence disconfirms prior conclusions.
  - **第二次の測定：** [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [実質性の判定](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — co-measures that can defeat truth even when formal disclosure exists.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 include [認識的誠実性](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) paths that would defeat honest interpretation or verification.
  - **第三次の測定：** [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where metrics, market signals, or institutional assertion stand in for honest constitutional evaluation.

    **第三次の評価：** Where operators lean on proxies, odds, prices, or compliance indicators, apply [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate durable truth from proxy throughput or institutional assertion.
<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：** material degradation of reliable interpretation or auditable integrity through:
    - deception;
    - distortion;
    - suppression;
    - selective disclosure; or
    - structurally misleading presentation —
    including polite tone or procedural form used as a substitute for honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain.
  - **第二次の失敗：** jargon, stacked complexity, or procedural opacity used to defeat [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) or [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) where materially relevant.
  - **第三次の失敗：** prices, odds, or market resolution sources used as enough for rights, safety, or governance truth determinations where 第一章 forbids that substitution, or refusal of correction after [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="epistemic-integrity"></a>

##### 認識的誠実性

*平たい言葉で言えば： the methods and evidence behind an evaluation must stay honest and checkable — prestige, opacity, or paperwork cannot stand in for truth.*

<a id="epistemic-integrity-o"></a>
- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Integrity of methods, evidence bases, and communications for constitutionally relevant evaluation — including clarity about:
    - 限度；
    - uncertainties;
    - conflicts of evidence; and
    - contestable inference steps —
    so [真理（憲法上の制約）](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) cannot be bypassed through procedural form, model opacity, or non-auditable inference.
  - **範囲外：**
    - method documentation alone;
    - institutional prestige rankings; or
    - ordinary stylistic polish without a constitutionally relevant evaluation stake —
    those are not epistemic integrity by themselves.
<a id="epistemic-integrity-a"></a>
- **測定と評価の仕方**
  - **第一次の測定：** [監督の測定ファミリー](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *感知者は、システムが表すものを見、検証し、依拠できるか。* ここで問うのに用いよ： the methods, evidence, and challengeable reasoning stay honest and checkable — so paperwork, black-box opacity, or prestige cannot stand in for truth.

    **第一次の評価：** Require enough openness about methods to match the stakes, within safety and security limits — and watch for any of the following standing in for real integrity:
    - authority;
    - prestige;
    - scale; or
    - complexity.
  - **第二次の測定：** [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [透明性](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — co-measures that can defeat integrity even when methods are formally documented.

    **第二次の評価：** 第一次の測定を点検するとき、 also check [監査可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [検証可能性](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), and [争訟可能性](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) wherever they matter — and include foreseeable misuse and adversarial testing under [予見可能性の勤勉](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
  - **第三次の測定：** [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where authority, prestige, scale, or complexity stands in for demonstrable integrity.

    **第三次の評価：** Where operators lean on authority, model opacity, or unverifiable assertion, apply [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate durable epistemic integrity from procedural form.
<a id="epistemic-integrity-c"></a>
- **成り立たねばならないこと**
  - **第一次の失敗：**
    - a truthful-sounding claim without honest treatment of methods, limits, and contestable inference;
    - evaluation or communication practices that defeat honest uncertainty treatment; or
    - non-auditable inference where audit is materially required.
  - **第二次の失敗：** methodological choices that foreseeably prevent verification or meaningful challenge; integrity claims that rest on unverifiable assertion where observable evidence is reasonably achievable.
  - **第三次の失敗：** formal method documentation or institutional prestige treated as decisive where [代替指標の乖離](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

**Admission scope.** This sub-block applies when the matter materially concerns:
- whether the following were reasonably identifiable before action:
  - outcomes;
  - リスク；
  - 危害；
  - misuse pathways; or
  - operating conditions;
- whether “we could not have foreseen it” claims are justified;
- whether analysis depth, scope, and scaling match [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [実質的リスク](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency); or
- whether evaluation was incomplete in a way that defeats [安全（制約）](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) assessment, or [予見可能性の勤勉](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
Outside that scope, individual entries may still apply on their own without importing the full compound.

Where admission scope is met, this nested sub-block is the joint home for foreseeability mechanics within Def.O2 *Truth and Epistemic Integrity*. All uses of “reasonably foreseeable” in **第二から第四章** are governed exclusively by these definitions and must stay explicitly traceable where invoked ([第二章、§3 — 定義スタックを通じた一貫性](core_02_definition_structure.md#3-consistency-across-the-definition-stack)).

**下位ブロックの成員。** この下位ブロックは次を含む：

- [予見可能性の勤勉](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence) — reasonable analysis sized to impact: required depth, evaluation boundaries, incompleteness failure, and justified non-foreseeability claims; and
- [合理的に予見可能な](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) — outcomes a careful analyst using known methods could identify before acting.

**§3.3 内の迂回防止。** 予見可能性の分析を、孤立した深さ、境界、主張負担、不完全性、または合理的に予見可能な問いに、一つを充足しつつ他を打ち負かす仕方で分節化するな。監査と両立する正当化なしに予見不能を主張すること、または敵対的、拡大、反復、もしくは誤用の文脈が実質的であるところで最良ケース運転だけを評価することは、入場範囲内で不遵守である。

##### 予見可能性の勤勉

*平たい言葉で言えば： do a reasonable analysis sized to the impact — dig deeper when stakes are high, cover the required boundaries (including interactions, dependency, and adversarial use), and if you claim something was unforeseeable, justify that claim. Assertion and checkboxes are not enough.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** A standard of reasonable analysis sized to impact, covering:
    - analysis depth that scales with [実質的影響](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [リスク](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [依存](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) under [比例性](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
    - required evaluation boundaries, including:
      - interactions;
      - dependency;
      - adversarial use; and
      - relevant [システムの境界](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries);
    - the duty to actually perform that evaluation; and
    - responsibility for justifying non-foreseeability claims under [合理的に予見可能な](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable).
  - **範囲外：**
    - outcomes that remain unforeseeable after required diligence;
    - unbounded speculative review of every conceivable outcome — that sits under [合理的に予見可能な](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) exclusions; or
    - flat-maximal analysis demanded for every low-stakes case — depth scales with impact; it is not unbounded maximalism.

##### 合理的に予見可能な

*平たい言葉で言えば： outcomes a careful analyst using known methods could identify before acting — including under adversarial and scaled conditions — not every conceivable outcome or pure hindsight.*

- **それが何か**
  - **範囲内：** Outcomes identifiable using domain knowledge and analytical methods under [予見可能性の勤勉](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
  - **範囲外：** Every conceivable outcome, pure hindsight, or results knowable only with knowledge or methods beyond reasonable diligence.

- **測定と評価の仕方**
