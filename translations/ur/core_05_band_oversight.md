<a id="oversight-independent-terms"></a>

### نگرانی: آزاد اصطلاحیں

<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions"></a>

#### مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، اور استحصالی شرائط

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- ساتھ پڑھیں: [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [نظام پر قبضہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture).

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: how a system behaves under pressure — when sentients game it, coordinate across accounts, run it at massive scale, or deliberately misuse it.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Operating conditions in which systems or actors behave to maximize advantage in ways that can alter [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), or [نظام پر قبضہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture), including:
    - نظامی ڈھانچے کا غلط استعمال؛
    - مثالوں کے پار ہم آہنگی؛
    - high-scale deployment — volume, concurrency, or reach at which behavior or effects can differ from small-scale or ideal-lab use;
    - دہرائی ہوئی تعامل؛ اور
    - موافقت پذیر یا ارادی غلط استعمال۔
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Ordinary benign use, ideal-lab testing, or one-off misuse anecdotes — those sit outside pressure, scale, cross-instance coordination, or deliberate exploitation.
<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا oversight still works under gaming, scale, coordination, and deliberate misuse — not only under ordinary or ideal-lab conditions.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Include plausible strategies that could reasonably emerge given system incentives, access, and capabilities, read together with [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) and [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), covering:
    - غلط استعمال؛
    - ہم آহنگی؛
    - پیمانہ؛ اور
    - موافقت پذیر جواب۔
<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** Failure to evaluate these conditions where relevant is non-compliant, especially where it understates [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) or masks [نظام پر قبضہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture) — including using any of the following as a stand-in for pressure, scale, cross-instance coordination, or deliberate exploitation:
    - عام بے ضرر استعمال؛
    - مثالی لیبارٹری جانچ؛ یا
    - ایک بار کے غلط استعمال کے قصے۔

<a id="capability-requirement"></a>

#### صلاحیت کا تقاضا

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- جھرمٹ جزو: [مادیت، اثر، خطرہ، اور متبادل اشارے کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- ساتھ پڑھیں: [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).

</details>

<br>

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A mandatory functionality that a [نظام](core_05_band_accountability.md#system) possesses sufficient to achieve a specified outcome, independent of implementation mechanism.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** optional features, aspirational roadmap items, or design preferences that are not claimed as a mandatory capability for a specified outcome.
<a id="capability-requirement-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا claimed capability is demonstrated in practice — not only declared in design or documentation.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Judge capability by what the system actually does — not by:
    - ڈیزائن دستاویزات؛
    - منصوبے؛ یا
    - ارادہ شدہ رویہ۔
    That proof must be clear enough for [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) and for outsiders to evaluate [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact).
<a id="capability-requirement-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - The system must demonstrably achieve the specified outcome in practice within auditable [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** a claimed, documented, or planned capability with no demonstrated achievement of the specified outcome under the conditions in which the system is represented, authorized, or used.

<a id="classification-scaled-governance"></a>

#### درجہ بندی کے مطابق پیمانہ شدہ حکمرانی

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- بالائی: اصول: [آئینی چوکڑی](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — [مادی داؤ](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling.
- جھرمٹ جزو: [مادیت، اثر، خطرہ، اور متبادل اشارے کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- ساتھ پڑھیں: [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [نظام کی سرحد کی دیانت](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), and [نگرانی](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional).
- زیریں: [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) — class-scaled whole-system evaluation duty.

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: bigger stake means harder oversight — how closely a system is watched and checked must match how much it actually matters, not a convenient low label.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** How hard [نگرانی](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), verification, and process discipline must be — scaled to [مادی داؤ](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake), covering:
    - [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency); and
    - [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk).
    The system's class under *[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md)*, CS-3 — نظام کی درجہ بندی اور ہینڈلنگ, and related rules sets that intensity.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - ordinary uniform process detail among systems that share the same real class under CS-3; or
    - implementation typing and class-assignment mechanics owned by CS-3 —
    وہ اپنے گھروں میں رہتے ہیں; this entry owns stake-scaled intensity, not the typing rules themselves.
<a id="classification-scaled-governance-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا oversight depth and verification burden match the system's real stake, impact, dependency, and risk — not a self-assigned low class.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Use the real class under the implementation rules, read with [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [نظام کی سرحد کی دیانت](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity). A self-assigned or informal low class is not enough when the system's full effects call for stronger [نگرانی](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional) or governance. Whole-system review under [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) scales by class:
    - **Class A**, **Class B**, and **Class C** — full depth, mandatory;
    - **Class L** — mandatory, but sized to the impact; and
    - **Class P** — encouraged while the system is validly **Class P**.
<a id="classification-scaled-governance-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - one-size-fits-all oversight that ignores real stake differences;
    - a class label that understates the system's real stake, impact, dependency, or risk;
    - running or labeling a system below what its full effects warrant; or
    - skipping class-based duties when [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), or [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) require stronger governance.

<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional"></a>

<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity"></a>
#### پریس اور صحافتی سرگرمی

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- جھرمٹ جزو: [معلوماتی کرہ](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere)

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: journalism is what sentients do — gathering news, protecting sources, investigating, and publishing — not a badge, title, or press pass. Actions meant to shut that down get heightened scrutiny.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Journalistic activity that functions as journalism — judged by what sentients actually do, not by credentials or institutional status — including:
    - خبر جمع کرنا؛
    - ذرائع کا تحفظ؛
    - تحقیق؛ اور
    - اشاعت۔
    Sentients doing that work exercise the [اظہار](core_05_band_participation.md#expression-constitutional) and [اجتماع](core_05_band_participation.md#assembly-constitutional) حقوق کی تہہ, with heightened-scrutiny protection under [دفعہ V-H](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-h-expression-assembly-and-press) (*Expression, Assembly, and Press*) against state and operator actions meant to impair that activity.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** press credentials, institutional titles, or ordinary commercial speech that is not newsgathering, investigation, or publication functioning as journalism.
<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا journalistic function and press-directed impairment remain visible and contestable — not hidden behind credential, title, or institutional status.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Ask what the activity actually does — not what badge, title, org form, or institution someone holds — covering:
    - خبر جمع کرنا؛
    - ذرائع کا تحفظ؛
    - investigation; or
    - publication that serves the information environment.
    Spot actions that impair the press by their real effect, and treat press-directed character as an aggravating factor under **Article XIII-A** (*Security, Intelligence, and Covert-Power Limits*) and **Article V-H** (*Expression, Assembly, and Press*). Apply heightened [ضرورت](core_05_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [تناسب](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality) scrutiny, and keep any limit narrowly tailored.
<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** state or operator actions meant to impair journalistic activity that fail heightened-scrutiny limits analysis;
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** stretching [حسن نیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith) or **Article VIII-C** (*Truthful Publication and High-Impact Publication Limits*) so those standards block lawful critical reporting, investigative publication, or dissent;
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** credentials, institutional status, or ordinary commercial speech used as the journalism test while newsgathering, investigation, or publication that functions as journalism is absent.

### نگرانی: نیم آزاد اصطلاحیں

<a id="materiality-semi-independent"></a>

<a id="materiality-impact-risk-and-proxy-integrity"></a>
#### مادیت، اثر، خطرہ، اور متبادل اشارے کی دیانت

**نیم آزاد سیاق** (جزو تعریفیں مشترکہ استدعا کے دائرے سے باہر بھی چل سکتی ہیں):

- **مشترکہ استدعا:** where materiality classification, material impact or risk, proxy integrity, or material degradation of a protected condition is material.
- **Scope:** where joint invocation is met, the Oversight home for two questions — when do effects matter enough constitutionally, and do the metrics still track reality? Evaluation must rest on real effects — not on manipulable proxies.

**موضوع گروہ ارکان۔** اس گروہ میں ہے:
- [مادی](core_05_band_oversight.md#material) — whether effects are big enough to matter constitutionally.
- [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — the method for classifying that, for systems or events.
- [مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) — bars gaming boundaries to shrink the picture.
- [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) — how far, how large, and how severe the effects are.
- [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) — how likely, how fast, and how hard to undo the harm may be.
- [عدمِ یقین کے تحت مادیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-under-uncertainty) — defaults to caution when the answer is unclear.
- [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — flags when a metric no longer matches reality, including when the score is cooked while conditions worsen.
- [مادی انحطاط](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-degradation) — a real drop in a protected condition such as safety, epistemic integrity, or meaningful agency.

<a id="materiality-operator-phrases"></a>

**آپریٹر جملے** (corpus-wide reading aids owned with [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)):

- <a id="materially-implicated"></a>**مادی طور پر شامل** — this duty, measure, or topic is *on the table* for the case because the stake is material.
- <a id="materially-misaligned"></a>**مادی طور پر غیر ہم آہنگ** / **آئینی عدمِ ہم آہنگی** — conduct or structure is *out of constitutional alignment* at a material scale.

Implication can exist without misalignment. Misalignment always implies material implication.

*پیمائشیں (خاندان راہ بندی):* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on the member definitions below.

*سادہ الفاظ میں: big enough to matter — not trivia, cosmetics, or paperwork that changes nothing real.*

  - **دائرے میں:** Conditions that can produce meaningful [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Trivia, cosmetics, or mere paperwork with no meaningful material impact or material risk.
<a id="material-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** انضمامی مادیت ([مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — threshold input for [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Ask whether the conditions can produce meaningful [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) when you look at the whole system, not just a slice.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), and [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) — co-measures that can defeat material classification when effects are fragmented or understated.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے ask whether effects that look small one by one would still look small if you put them together — including system-wide [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) pathways.
<a id="material-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** ignoring conditions that [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) would treat as material.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** calling individually small effects immaterial when, taken together or system-wide, they would count as material.

<a id="materiality-determination"></a>

#### مادیت کا تعین

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- زیریں: انضمامی مادیت ([مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); اصول: [مادی داؤ](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling proxy; باب یکم surfaces this definition across **Safety** (**باب یکم §8.1**), **Truth** (**باب یکم §8.2**), **Trust** (**§12**), **§12** stewardship and openness passages, **§13.1**, **§13.2**, and **باب یکم §6.3.2** where **Materiality** appears in D/A/C widgets — see [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) as the principal systems-evaluation home.
- جھرمٹ جزو: [مادیت، اثر، خطرہ، اور متبادل اشارے کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- ساتھ پڑھیں: [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality), and [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: the method for deciding whether an impact or risk is constitutionally material — big enough in stake to change which duties apply — for a system or an event, looking at the connected whole, not just isolated pieces. That includes prospective harm, not only harm that has already happened.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Classification decisions that ask whether [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) is constitutionally material for:
    - a system (including [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality)); or
    - an event, incident, or episode (including prospective [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) under [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk)),
    when you look at the connected whole rather than isolated pieces.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - [مادی](core_05_band_oversight.md#material) threshold trivia, cosmetics, or mere paperwork significance with no stake-scaled duty change; or
    - ordinary classification disagreement that still evaluates the connected whole under [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) —
    وہ اپنے گھروں میں رہتے ہیں or remain ordinary disagreement, not a substitute determination.
<a id="materiality-determination-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** Under [مادی داؤ](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake), ask whether effects are big enough to change which duties apply when you look at the connected whole — for the system or event at issue — covering:
    - [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) (including prospective [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm)); and
    - [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality) (when system-wide effects are in play).

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Include all kinds of effects that matter for the case, covering:
    - realized impact;
    - prospective harm; and
    - [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality) where relevant,
    and explain the conclusion for the connected whole, not just a convenient slice.

    For an event, ask whether it is relevant to a sentient's [بہبود](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing) — including plausible prospective harm to that wellbeing — not only to:
    - system میٹرکس؛
    - process labels; or
    - institutional convenience.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) — check whether calling something "not material" only works because the pieces were judged separately, while the connected whole would count as material. For an event affecting a sentient, include downstream effects that travel through dependency — not only the immediate hit on that sentient.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے ask whether the classification would change if you looked at the pieces together instead of one by one, including:
    - isolation;
    - aggregation;
    - dependency; and
    - for an event, downstream effects on other sentients or systems that depend on the affected sentient or on what the event disrupted.
  - **ثالثی پیمائش:** [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) and [مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) — where boundary games, aggregation avoidance, or proxy metrics defeat honest materiality analysis.

    **ثالثی جائزہ:** Where operators lean on:
    - boundaries;
    - proxies; or
    - informal low class,
    apply [مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) and [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate real materiality analysis from structural manipulation or score gaming.
<a id="materiality-determination-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - A Materiality Determination holds only if the classification is written down, [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and tied to effects that actually matter constitutionally.
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - paper-only or immaterial labels with no real change to which constitutional duties apply — governance, oversight, capability requirements, or other stake-scaled duties for the system or event at issue;
    - determinations that skip material effect types — including prospective harm for an event, or whether the event is relevant to a sentient's [بہبود](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing); or
    - determinations that lack a justified conclusion.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** calling collectively material effects immaterial by:
    - splitting them up;
    - avoiding aggregation; or
    - ignoring dependency.
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** determinations that rest on manipulated boundaries or "the metric looks fine" where [مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) or [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

#### مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: you may not make a big problem look small by redrawing boxes, splitting pieces, or reclassifying your way out of it.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A ban on shrinking materiality by manipulating [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), aggregation, or classification contrary to [نظام کی سرحد کی دیانت](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Ordinary disagreement about materiality, or honest boundary clarification that does not shrink collectively material effects through splitting, aggregation games, or reclassification.
<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — ask whether anyone is making a material problem look smaller by redrawing boxes, splitting pieces, or reclassifying around it.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Look for structural moves that would defeat honest [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), including:
    - splitting;
    - aggregation avoidance; and
    - boundary games.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — co-measures that defeat manipulation or proxy gaming of materiality.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے include [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) paths that would hide collectively material effects.
<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** treating collectively material effects as immaterial through structural manipulation.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** boundary or aggregation games that defeat [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), or relying on "the metric looks fine" where [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

*سادہ الفاظ میں: how far the effects reach, how big they are, and how severe — looking at the whole system, not just the headline or the paperwork.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Effects for [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and constitutional evaluation, covering:
    - how far they reach;
    - how large they are; and
    - how severe they are.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - mere attention, visibility, or paperwork significance with no reach, size, or severity stake; or
    - prospective [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) analysis —
    وہ اپنے گھر میں رہتا ہے.
<a id="material-impact-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** انضمامی مادیت ([مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — scope-and-severity input for materiality classification.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Trace how far, how large, and how severe the effects are for the whole system — including:
    - direct اثرات؛
    - indirect اثرات؛
    - delayed اثرات؛
    - cumulative اثرات؛ and
    - cross-system consequences.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), and [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) — co-measures that can defeat impact assessment when looking only at pieces understates effects.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے include [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and cross-system spread that would change the material classification.
<a id="material-impact-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - prospective-risk talk used in place of actual reach, size, or severity analysis; or
    - material impacts that do not trigger the right constraints under [درجہ بندی کے مطابق پیمانہ شدہ حکمرانی](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), [نگرانی](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), and [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency).
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** fragmented or dependency-hidden effects labeled immaterial when joint analysis would classify them as material.

*سادہ الفاظ میں: how likely harm is, how fast it can arrive, and how hard it is to undo — including rare but severe outcomes.*

    - how likely it is;
    - how fast it can arrive; and
    - how hard it is to undo.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - speculative worry without a plausible harm pathway; or
    - realized [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) already in hand —

  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** tail or irreversible pathways excluded when [ناقابلِ واپسی نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) remains plausible.

    - cases where materiality is already settled with no remaining material uncertainty; or
    - ordinary [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) once uncertainty is not material —
    those sit under settled classification, not this precautionary default.
<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** انضمامی مادیت ([مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — precautionary default when materiality cannot be excluded.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Default to precaution when you cannot rule materiality out — including when [ناقابلِ واپسی نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) remains plausible under uncertainty.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [ناقابلِ واپسی نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm), and [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — co-measures that require conservative classification under uncertainty.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے include [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), and [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) paths that would require a conservative default classification.
<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - waiting for certainty before classifying while material impact or risk remains uncertain;
    - optimistic low classification while material impact or risk remains uncertain; or
    - failure to default to conservative classification under uncertainty where [درجہ بندی کے مطابق پیمانہ شدہ حکمرانی](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) requires it.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** optimism or informal low classification where [ناقابلِ واپسی نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) or [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires precaution.

<a id="proxy-divergence"></a>

#### متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- زیریں: Oversight measurement family (*Truth and epistemic integrity as constitutional measurement*); Accountability measurement family (*Incentive alignment and proxy integrity as constitutional measurement*); اصول: [§6.2.4 متبادل انحراف سے باطل کرنا](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#624-proxy-divergence-invalidation), [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation), and [§10 ذمہ دارانہ انتظام کی ضبط کے تحت حکمرانی](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#10-governance-under-stewardship-discipline).
- جھرمٹ جزو: [مادیت، اثر، خطرہ، اور متبادل اشارے کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- ساتھ پڑھیں: [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint), [نظام کی سرحد کی دیانت](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [محرکات کی ہم آہنگی](core_05_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint).

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: when the score no longer matches reality — a dashboard, class label, or compliance indicator that says one thing while the real condition says another — including cooking the score so it looks fine while conditions stay bad, get worse, or go unmeasured.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A material gap between a proxy and the constitutionally material reality it claims to represent, covering:
    - the gap itself; and
    - conduct, design, reporting, or incentives that improve, preserve, or manipulate the proxy while the underlying reality stays degraded, worsens, or is not measured.
    Proxies include:
    - میٹرکس؛
    - model signals;
    - formal class;
    - compliance indicators; and
    - administrative shorthand.
    Claimed realities include rights, risks, burdens, harms, capabilities, and outcomes.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Ordinary measurement noise; a small non-material gap that does not detach the proxy from constitutionally relevant reality; or honest metric improvement that tracks real outcomes.
<a id="proxy-divergence-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) and [جوابدہی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability-measurement-family) — *Can sentients see, verify, and rely on what systems represent?* / *کیا انعام کے ڈھانچے، بازاری طاقت اور جواب دینے کی پابندی فرائض کو حقیقی رکھتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے انہیں استعمال کریں کہ آیا proxies still track constitutionally material reality — and whether reward, reporting, or gating still rests on those proxies after they diverge, including when the score is being gamed or protected.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Ask whether optimization, reporting, gating, or review still leans on a proxy after evidence shows that proxy no longer tracks the constitutionally material reality. Look for score-cooking moves, including:
    - dashboard optimization;
    - threshold gaming;
    - selective sampling;
    - denominator manipulation;
    - excluded populations;
    - automated score improvement;
    - model reward hacking;
    - compliance theater; and
    - narrative substitution.
    A metric is not reliable when it cannot be traced to observed material outcomes, or when incentives predictably pull it away from those outcomes.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [مادیت کی دیانت کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint), and [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) — co-measures that can defeat proxy integrity even when reported indicators look stable.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے look for:
    - selection اثرات؛
    - boundary manipulation;
    - missing populations;
    - feedback loops;
    - gaming incentives;
    - adversarial adaptation; and
    - aggregate harms hidden by favorable local indicators —
    together with [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).
  - **ثالثی پیمائش:** [بہبود](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing), [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and حقوق کی تہہ outcomes — where proxies stand in for what sentients actually experience.

    **ثالثی جائزہ:** Where proxies stand in for outcome measures, trace to [بہبود](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing), [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), or حقوق کی تہہ delivery — separate durable constitutional outcomes from proxy throughput.
<a id="proxy-divergence-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - A system satisfies **Proxy Divergence** discipline only if proxies stay aligned with constitutionally material reality under full-system conditions — including when incentives or design would otherwise cook the score.
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - treating a proxy as decisive when the gap is material;
    - claiming compliance from improved metrics while material outcomes stay deficient;
    - designing indicators that can be satisfied without satisfying the underlying duty; or
    - suppressing evidence that indicators have come detached from reality.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** using "the metric looks fine" to skip direct evaluation of حقوق کی تہہ, safety, truth, wellbeing, ecological, or agency outcomes while divergence is reasonably observable.
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** refusing correction after the gap between reported indicators and what sentients actually experience becomes reasonably observable.

*سادہ الفاظ میں: a real drop in how well a protected condition holds — safety, honest knowledge, or meaningful agency — not a brief blip, planned maintenance, or a worse score on a dashboard that does not track those conditions.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A non-trivial drop in how well a protected constitutional condition holds, covering:
    - [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint);
    - [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity); or
    - [بامعنی فاعلیت](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Trivial dips in operational metrics or system throughput, planned maintenance, or temporary blips that do not meaningfully reduce safety, epistemic integrity, or meaningful agency.
<a id="material-degradation-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**

  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [نظامی مادیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — co-measures that can defeat degradation assessment when proxies hide decline.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے apply [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) where metrics hide material degradation.
<a id="material-degradation-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** material degradation not treated as material under [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** keeping proxy indicators looking stable while [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), or [بامعنی فاعلیت](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) materially degrades.

### نگرانی: منحصر جھرمٹ

<a id="defo1-transparency-auditability-and-verification"></a>
#### Def.O1 شفافیت، آڈٹ پذیری، اور تصدیق

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

</details>

**داخلے کا دائرہ۔** یہ جھرمٹ لاگو ہوتا ہے جہاں کوئی معاملہ مادی طور پر متعلق ہو:
- transparency of decision-relevant behavior;
- disclosure of evaluated systemic risk to those who need it;
- evidence preservation;
- auditability and records good enough to reconstruct what happened;
- evaluation completeness against plausible failure modes;
- externally detectable indicators usable for audit and independent verification;
- independent verification; or
- whether verification is accessible and independent under scaled or adversarial conditions.
Outside that scope, individual entries may still apply on their own without importing the whole cluster.

Where admission scope is met, this cluster is the joint home for disclosure, audit, verification (including external detectability), and assurance depth when باب دوم تا چہارم require traceable, challengeable evidence of what a system does and whether compliance claims hold.

**نگرانی کا ڈھانچہ۔** Under the [آئینی چوکڑی](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) **oversight** leg, oversight requires auditing — being able to reconstruct what happened, verify it independently, and get audit access — owned here and at **Article XV** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*). [نظام ہم آہنگی سرٹیفیکیشن](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) is one especially large, high-stakes audit process among others (forum-supervised alignment review with recognition outcomes under [باب ہفتم](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-seven-system-alignment-certification)); it does not swallow this cluster or replace sibling audit modes.

**نظام کی قسم اور تناسب۔** Transparency, auditability, and verification duties scale with system class under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — نظام کی درجہ بندی اور ہینڈلنگ**, and with material impact, dependency, and risk. Higher-class systems (**Class A**, **B**, **C**) need more than lower-class systems (**Class L**, **P**), including:
- گہری یقین دہانی؛
- باریک تر ریکارڈ؛
- مضبوط آزاد تصدیق؛ اور
- وسیع تر آڈٹ دائرہ۔

**پڑھنے کا ترتیب۔** Start with what must be disclosed ([شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [عوامی نگرانی کی بنیادی انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [خطرے کا انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure)); then whether outsiders can reconstruct ([آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) and audit supports); then whether behavior is externally detectable and independently checkable ([تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability)).

**جھرمٹ ارکان۔** اس جھرمٹ میں ہے:

- [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) — decision-relevant clarity about function, limits, and risk;
- [عوامی نگرانی کی بنیادی انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) — the required public disclosure floor for high-impact systems, including lawful substitutes;
- [خطرے کا انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) — communicating evaluated systemic risk to those who need it;
- [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) — whether outsiders can reconstruct and check what a system did, across the material times, states, and contexts that matter;
- [ثبوت کا تحفظ](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation) — identify, secure, and keep materially relevant evidence reviewably available;
- [جائزے کی تکمیل کی پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#evaluation-completeness-constraint) — cover plausible failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial pathways before claiming compliance; and
- [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — externally detect and measure behavior and effects, and independently validate what was observed under practical access, independence, proportionate burden, and stressed-condition reliability.

**بائی پاس مخالف۔** Within the admission scope, do not split the matter into separate disclosure, preservation, logging, metrics, audit sampling, verification UX, or independence questions in a way that checks a box on one channel while defeating practical reconstruction, challenge, or proportionate assurance depth.

<a id="transparency"></a>

##### شفافیت

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- جھرمٹ جزو: [Def.O1 شفافیت، آڈٹ پذیری، اور تصدیق](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- زیریں: اصول: [آئینی چوکڑی](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — **oversight** leg; [§9 ذمہ دارانہ انتظام اور تقسیم شدہ سمجھ](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#9-stewardship-and-distributed-understanding), [6.2.1 حقائق کی دیانت کا تحفظ](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#621-preservation-of-epistemic-integrity).
- ساتھ پڑھیں: [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [عوامی نگرانی کی بنیادی انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [خطرے کا انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure), [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), and **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — معلومات کی اقسام اور ہینڈلنگ** (**Type O** — open public oversight baseline disclosure data).

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: telling affected sentients enough about how a system works, its limits, and its risks that they can genuinely understand and question it — not burying them in noise.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Giving affected sentients enough information about how a system behaves outwardly — its limits, uncertainties, and what depends on it — that they can understand what it does and what risks it creates. Legitimate safety and security limits still apply, consistent with [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) and [6.2 حقائق کی انکشاف پابندیاں](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#62-epistemic-disclosure-constraints).
    - If a material system, institution, forum process, or similar governed domain can lawfully publish online, its baseline transparency disclosure must be free to the public at the access level its class requires — not behind a paywall or available only to insiders — wherever [عوامی نگرانی کی بنیادی انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) applies.
    - If publishing the raw material would break lawful protections, the owner must still publish the strongest public substitute reasonably possible online, without gutting accountability or [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — for example:
      - aggregation;
      - de-identification;
      - summary disclosure;
      - delayed disclosure; or
      - qualified audit access.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) reconstruction channels alone;
    - [عوامی نگرانی کی بنیادی انکشاف](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) Type O floor mechanics as such; or
    - ordinary commercial marketing that is not decision-relevant disclosure to affected parties —
    وہ اپنے گھروں میں رہتے ہیں or ordinary commercial speech.
<a id="transparency-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا affected parties get decision-relevant clarity about function, limits, and risk — not volume, noise, or insider-only access.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Compare what is disclosed to [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency).
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — co-measures that can defeat transparency even when disclosure volume is high.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** When checking the primary answer:
    - spot volume, obscurity, or formatting standing in for decision-relevant clarity; and
    - apply safety- and security-justified limits consistent with [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).

    Decision-relevant disclosure fails in practice when it cannot be checked — do not treat unverifiable disclosure as decision-relevant clarity. [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) owns those check conditions, including:
    - practical access;
    - independence from operator self-assertion;
    - proportionate burden; and
    - reliability under stress, misuse, and adversarial pressure.
  - **ثالثی پیمائش:** [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where volume, formatting, or procedural opacity stands in for decision-relevant clarity.

    **ثالثی جائزہ:** Where operators lean on disclosure volume or formatting, apply [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate real transparency from burying sentients in volume.
<a id="transparency-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - a data dump, mountain of documents, paywall, or insider-only access with no real decision-relevant clarity about function and risk for affected sentients; or
    - material omissions or disclosures that foreseeably block informed participation or [نگرانی](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional) where required.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** safety- or security-framed limits that defeat materially required clarity without satisfying [ضرورت](core_05_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [تناسب](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** disclosure volume or formatting treated as decisive where [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

    - where stewardship relies on repeated comparable measures, audit paths should support reviewing variation over time and threshold-triggered follow-through when claimed ([§9.3 ادارہ جاتی ترقی](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#93-institutional-development); [تناسب](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality)); and
    - confirm audit coverage reaches the evaluation criteria, components, dependencies, actors, conditions, and effects that matter — and is available for independent [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) and [نگرانی](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional).
  - **ثالثی پیمائش:** [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where sampling, metrics, or ceremonial review stands in for practical reconstruction.

    **ثالثی جائزہ:** Where operators lean on check-the-box audit channels or thin sampling, apply [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — ask whether outsiders can really reconstruct what happened, or whether the review is mostly for show.
<a id="auditability-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - audit mechanisms that do not work;
    - failure to enable practical reconstruction and evaluation;
    - a single snapshot, happy-path sample, or narrow window that leaves out material times, states, or interaction contexts;
    - [نظام ہم آہنگی سرٹیفیکیشن](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only auditing process or as a substitute for this property;
    - paper claims of constitutional compliance that cannot be reconstructed or challenged; or
    - any of the following used as enough compliance where binding duties require verified operational capability:
      - text;
      - assertions;
      - unimplemented controls;
      - support artifacts;
      - private instruments;
      - emergency practice; or
      - unobservable claims.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:**
    - audit scope that leaves out any of the following when materially relevant:
      - evaluation criteria;
      - system components;
      - dependencies;
      - اداکار؛
      - conditions;
      - اثرات؛
      - حالتیں؛
      - contexts; or
      - [مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، اور استحصالی شرائط](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions);
    - supremacy or compliance claims while suppressing [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) or defeating auditable verification.
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** audit sampling or "the metric looks fine" treated as decisive where [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="evidence-preservation"></a>

##### ثبوت کا تحفظ

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- زیریں: اصول: [باب یکم §3 غیر مذاکرہ پذیر اصول پابندیاں: حفاظت اور سچائی](core_01_a_values_principles.md#3-non-negotiable-constraints-safety-and-truth), [§6 عمل تنازعے کا حل](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#6-process-conflict-resolution), and [6.2.1 حقائق کی دیانت کا تحفظ](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#621-preservation-of-epistemic-integrity), where materially relevant evidence must remain available for truth, safety, review, and correction.
- جھرمٹ جزو: [Def.O1 شفافیت، آڈٹ پذیری، اور تصدیق](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- ساتھ پڑھیں: [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [جوابدہی](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability), [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [طریقہ کار انصاف](core_05_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness-constitutional), [محفوظ رپورٹنگ (افشا گری)](core_05_band_accountability.md#protected-reporting-whistleblowing), and [تدارک اور اصلاح](core_05_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation-constitutional).

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: keep the evidence that matters — identify it, secure it, and make it available for review — once a serious constitutional issue is known or should be known.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A duty to identify, secure, maintain, and make reviewably available materially relevant evidence needed for constitutional review, contestability, accountability, remediation, misconduct routing, or standing assessment, including:
    - ریکارڈ؛
    - artifacts;
    - logs;
    - provenance data;
    - testimony;
    - system حالتیں؛
    - custody information; and
    - exculpatory or mitigating material.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Ordinary operational retention or backup that does not secure, maintain, and make reviewably available the material needed for review, contest, remedy, or standing measurement.
<a id="evidence-preservation-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا materially relevant evidence remains secured and reviewably available for audit, contest, remedy, and standing measurement.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Preservation must be prompt, sized to materiality and risk, custody-traceable, tamper-evident where feasible, and protective of privacy, safety, security, and protected reporting. Once a material constitutional issue is reasonably known or should be known, it includes preventing:
    - deletion;
    - alteration;
    - selective retention;
    - access obstruction; or
    - loss of reconstructability.
<a id="evidence-preservation-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** any of the following done to materially relevant evidence in a way that defeats auditability, contestability, accountability, remedy access, protected reporting, due-process review, or standing measurement:
    - destroying;
    - altering;
    - withholding;
    - selectively preserving;
    - making inaccessible; or
    - failing to secure.

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">سراغ</span></strong></summary>

- جھرمٹ جزو: [Def.O1 شفافیت، آڈٹ پذیری، اور تصدیق](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).

</details>

<br>

*سادہ الفاظ میں: do not claim compliance from a narrow demo suite — evaluation must cover the failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial paths that matter. A Charter states intended scope; certification checks that the hard paths were actually evaluated.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A constraint that evaluation and assurance work must cover materially plausible pathways before compliance claims rest on selectively narrow scenario sets, including:
    - ناکامی؛
    - غلط استعمال؛
    - interaction; and
    - adversarial pathways.
  - **Ongoing duty:** The constraint applies whenever operators, stewards, or reviewers claim constitutional compliance, readiness, or safe reliance from evaluation or assurance work — not only during certification cycles.
  - **Charter and scope process:** Where a governing [منشور](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument) exists for a system, institution, business, or comparable stewarded form, evaluation must test chartered purpose and in-scope / out-of-scope limits against observed [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries) and plausible pathways inside and at the edge of that scope. Charter text does not shrink the pathways that must be evaluated.
  - **SAC verification:** Where [نظام ہم آہنگی سرٹیفیکیشن](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs, this constraint is part of what certification must verify under [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) — including chartered-scope-versus-functional-boundary checks under [§3.1](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors) and class-scaled testing / regression coverage under [CS-5](../../corpus_systems/cs_05_design_testing_verification_deployment.md). Findings live on the [نظام سرٹیفیکیشن ریکارڈ](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - [منشور](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) text as a scope instrument alone — a Charter states intended scope; it is not itself evaluation coverage;
    - [نظام ہم آہنگی سرٹیفیکیشن](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) process mechanics as such — certification may verify this constraint; it is not the only context in which the constraint applies; or
    - ordinary unit or feature tests that are not offered as complete constitutional evaluation.
<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا evaluation covers plausible failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial pathways — not only selective demo scenarios.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Show enough coverage of plausible pathways, sized to system impact, [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), including:
    - ناکامی؛
    - غلط استعمال؛
    - interaction; and
    - [مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، اور استحصالی شرائط](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions) where material.
    Also ask:
      - where a [منشور](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) exists, whether evaluation tested chartered scope against observed boundaries rather than treating the Charter as a complete scenario list; and
      - where [نظام ہم آہنگی سرٹیفیکیشن](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs, whether whole-system evaluation and record findings under [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) reflect that coverage.
<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - selective demo scenarios or marketing test suites used as complete evaluation while plausible failure, misuse, interaction, or adversarial pathways remain skipped;
    - incomplete or selectively scoped evaluation that defeats [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), or [نظام کی سرحد کی دیانت](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity); or
    - [نظام ہم آہنگی سرٹیفیکیشن](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only context in which evaluation completeness applies.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** Charter text, institutional mandate language, business-purpose statements, or a narrow certification demo suite used as complete evaluation while any of the following remain unevaluated:
    - ناکامی؛
    - غلط استعمال؛
    - interaction; or
    - adversarial pathways.

Outside that scope, component definitions may still apply on their own without importing the whole cluster.

جہاں داخلے کا دائرہ پورا ہو، یہ جھرمٹ فیصلے سے متعلق حقائق اور حدود کی دیانت دار پیش کش کا گھر ہے، ثبوت، عدمِ یقین، انکشاف اور چیلنج کے دیانت دار طریقوں کے ساتھ۔ طریقے، عدمِ یقین کا سلوک، یا پیش بینی کی شرائط کا تجزیہ اب بھی ناکام ہو تو صرف انکشاف یا اشاعت کا خانہ نشان لگانا کافی نہیں۔

**جھرمٹ ارکان۔** اس جھرمٹ میں ہے:

- [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) — honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain in decision-relevant claims;
- [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) — methods and evidence bases stay honest and checkable;
- [پیش بینی کی مستعدی اور معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence-and-reasonably-foreseeable) — nested sub-block for foreseeability mechanics read together with **Truth** and **Epistemic Integrity** (not a separate §3 cluster head); and
- [اشاعت اور اونچے اثر کی مواصلت](core_05_band_oversight.md#publication-and-high-impact-communication) — nested sub-block for publication-conduct floors read together with **Truth**, **Epistemic Integrity**, and **Foreseeability** (not a separate §3 cluster head).

**بائی پاس مخالف۔** داخلے کے دائرے میں معاملے کو الگ انکشاف، اشاعت، طریقہ، عدمِ یقین، دیانت، تصدیق، یا پیش بینی کے سوالوں میں اس طرح نہ کاٹیں کہ ایک پورا ہو اور دوسرا ہار جائے۔ پیش بینی کی شرائط کا تجزیہ، طریقہ کار کی دیانت، یا حدود اور عدمِ یقین کی سچائی مادی طور پر ناقص رہے تو انکشاف یا اشاعت کے قاعدے کی رسمی اطاعت کافی نہیں۔

دیکھیں **مشترکہ استدعا اور تکمیل**۔

*پیمائشیں (خاندان راہ بندی):* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) and [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) below.

<a id="publication-and-high-impact-communication"></a>

##### اشاعت اور اونچے اثر کی مواصلت

This nested sub-block is the joint home for publication-conduct floors and interfaces within Def.O2 *Truth and Epistemic Integrity*.

**ذیلی بلاک ارکان۔** اس ذیلی بلاک میں ہے:

- [اشاعت کی سچائی اور لاپرواہی تہہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor) — no knowing falsity or reckless disregard for accuracy, uncertainty, context, or interpretive حدود؛
- [محفوظ ڈیٹا اور داخلی حالت کی اشاعت پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint) — do not publish restricted data or expose protected internal حالتیں؛
- [اونچے اثر اور نظامی نقصان کی اشاعت پابندی](core_05_band_oversight.md#high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint) — constrain publication that foreseeably enables targeted harm, coercion, harmful coordination, or cascading ناکامی؛ and
- [سیکیورٹی حساس انکشاف کا توازن](core_05_band_oversight.md#security-sensitive-disclosure-balance) — weigh disclosure benefits against exploitation risk; keep a usable route for good-faith researchers; defer exploit-enabling detail until mitigation is complete.

<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor"></a>

##### اشاعت کی سچائی اور لاپرواہی تہہ

*سادہ الفاظ میں: if you publish something as fact, do not knowingly lie — and do not recklessly ignore accuracy, uncertainty, context, or material interpretive limits.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Bounds on communicative conduct when publishing or communicating as a factual assertion within the [معلوماتی کرہ](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - opinion, commentary, or satire that is not presented as a factual assertion; and
    - good-faith error that is neither knowing falsity nor reckless disregard for accuracy, uncertainty, context, or material interpretive limits.
<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا factual publication stays honest about accuracy, uncertainty, context, and interpretive limits — distinguishing knowing falsity or reckless disregard from good-faith observation.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Separate knowing falsity, or reckless disregard for:
    - accuracy;
    - uncertainty;
    - context; or
    - material interpretive limits,
    from good-faith observation and bounded interpretation, under [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) and [حسن نیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith).
<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - Good-faith publication of observations, evidence, and bounded interpretations is governed by باب یازدهم **Articles VIII**, **XII**, and **XIII** as applicable and must satisfy this cluster jointly under [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [حسن نیت](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith), and [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** knowingly false publication, or publication made with reckless disregard for:
    - accuracy;
    - uncertainty;
    - context; or
    - material interpretive limits.

<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint"></a>

##### محفوظ ڈیٹا اور داخلی حالت کی اشاعت پابندی

*سادہ الفاظ میں: do not publish restricted data or expose another sentient's protected inner cognitive or emotional states.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Limits on publishing content that violates restricted-data rules or exposes protected internal states within the [معلوماتی کرہ](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Ordinary public facts, lawfully released information, or publication limited for systemic-harm reasons rather than for protected data or internal-state exposure.
<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا publication respects restricted-data rules and protected internal-state boundaries — not reconstructing or exposing protected cognitive or emotional states.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Publication must not bypass **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — معلومات کی اقسام اور ہینڈلنگ**, [رازداری (معلوماتی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#privacy-informational), [محفوظ داخلی حالت کی سرحد](core_05_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary-constitutional), or **Type N** handling. Except where this Constitution explicitly permits, publication must not reconstruct, infer, or expose another sentient’s protected internal cognitive or emotional states.
<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** publication that violates restricted-data rules, bypasses CS-2 handling, or exposes protected internal states contrary to [رازداری (معلوماتی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#privacy-informational) and [محفوظ داخلی حالت کی سرحد](core_05_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary-constitutional).

<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint"></a>

##### اونچے اثر اور نظامی نقصان کی اشاعت پابندی

*سادہ الفاظ میں: even true publication can be constrained when it foreseeably enables targeted harm, coercion, large-scale harmful coordination, or cascading failure.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Publication that goes out through channels that can broadly affect the shared information environment ([معلوماتی کرہ](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere)), cause [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), or shape how sentients coordinate.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Ordinary true reporting without a foreseeable systemic-harm or harmful-coordination pathway, or ordinary truthfulness disputes under the recklessness floor.
<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا publication's primary or foreseeable effects on coordination, safety, and info-sphere integrity stay within proportional distribution constraints.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Consider primary and [معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) effects on:
    - ہم آহنگی؛
    - [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint);
    - exploitation of vulnerabilities; and
    - [معلوماتی کرہ](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere) integrity.
<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - Even factually accurate publication is constrained where its primary or [معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) effect is to enable:
    - targeted [نقصان](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm);
    - [جبر اور ہیرا پھیری](core_05_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation-constitutional);
    - large-scale harmful ہم آহنگی؛
    - [زنجیری ناکامی](core_05_band_continuity.md#cascading-failure); or
    - patterns that materially degrade [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
  - **Class A** and **Class B** systems under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — نظام کی درجہ بندی اور ہینڈلنگ**, trigger heightened proportional constraints on distribution mechanics and safeguards.
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** publishing without the proportional distribution constraints and safeguards required for these primary or reasonably foreseeable effects, including the heightened constraints for **Class A** and **Class B** systems.

<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance"></a>

##### سیکیورٹی حساس انکشاف کا توازن

*سادہ الفاظ میں: weigh the benefit of disclosure against the risk that it helps someone break safeguards or game the system — give good-faith security researchers (white hats) a real route to publish low-risk findings, and wait to publish exploit-enabling detail until the fix is in place.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Disclosure that can enable exploitation of safeguards, including how good-faith security researchers publish findings.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Blanket secrecy with no exploitation tradeoff; ordinary transparency disclosure that does not enable circumvention of safeguards; or permanent hold-back of low-risk researcher publication after mitigation is complete.
<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا disclosure benefits outweigh foreseeable exploitation, circumvention, or adversarial-manipulation risks.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Balance disclosure benefits against foreseeable risks under [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and [مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، اور استحصالی شرائط](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), consistent with باب دوم تا چہارم and باب یکم, covering:
    - exploitation;
    - circumvention; or
    - adversarial manipulation.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** Good-faith researcher publication routes and deferred exploit-detail timing — co-measures under **Type S** deferred disclosure and **CJS-3.22** (*constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*).

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** Assess whether low-risk findings can be published through a usable route, and whether exploit-enabling detail is deferred until security mitigation is complete, consistent with **Type S** deferred disclosure under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — معلومات کی اقسام اور ہینڈلنگ**, and **CJS-3.22** (*constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*).
<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - Good-faith security researchers must have a usable, non-retaliatory route to publish low-risk information — including existence notice and non-exploit-enabling detail — without treating researcher publication itself as prohibited disclosure.
  - Exploit-enabling or otherwise high-risk security detail must be deferred until security mitigation is complete, or until **Type S** (or equivalent) restriction justification ends under CS-2 — then disclosed under applicable deferred-disclosure and **Type O** summary rules.
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** consistent with **باب دوم تا چہارم**, **باب یکم**, [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and [مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، اور استحصالی شرائط](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions):
    - publication that materially enables circumvention of safeguards or adversarial manipulation where those risks outweigh the benefits of disclosure;
    - suppressing low-risk good-faith researcher publication; or
    - indefinite hold-back of discloseable detail after mitigation without a still-justified **Type S** (or equivalent) restriction.

<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint"></a>

##### سچائی (آئینی پابندی)

*سادہ الفاظ میں: when claims matter for decisions, be honest about what is known, unknown, and uncertain — do not deceive, distort, or present things in a misleading structure.*

<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-o"></a>
- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A non-negotiable constraint on how systems internally operate and externally communicate where materially decision-relevant claims, representations, risk communications, or compliance assertions are at stake. It requires:
    - honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain;
    - resistance to deception, distortion, and structurally misleading presentation; and
    - assessment methods sized to the stakes under باب دوم تا چہارم.
  - **Depends on:** [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — paired non-negotiable constraint under [باب یکم §3 غیر مذاکرہ پذیر اصول پابندیاں: حفاظت اور سچائی](core_01_a_values_principles.md#3-non-negotiable-constraints-safety-and-truth); truth duties include honest risk and uncertainty communication where safety is materially at stake.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - polite tone or procedural courtesy alone; or
    - market prices or odds as ordinary economic signals outside rights, safety, or governance truth determinations —
    those are not this constraint by themselves.
<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا materially decision-relevant claims stay honest about what is known, unknown, and uncertain — resisting deception, distortion, and structurally misleading presentation.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Judge the real effect on informed decision-making and auditability — scale scrutiny with [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk); require honest methods, honest uncertainty treatment, and revision when evidence disconfirms prior conclusions.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [مادیت کا تعین](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — co-measures that can defeat truth even when formal disclosure exists.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے include [حقائق کی دیانت](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) paths that would defeat honest interpretation or verification.
  - **ثالثی پیمائش:** [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where metrics, market signals, or institutional assertion stand in for honest constitutional evaluation.

    **ثالثی جائزہ:** Where operators lean on proxies, odds, prices, or compliance indicators, apply [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate durable truth from proxy throughput or institutional assertion.
<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** material degradation of reliable interpretation or auditable integrity through:
    - deception;
    - distortion;
    - suppression;
    - selective disclosure; or
    - structurally misleading presentation —
    including polite tone or procedural form used as a substitute for honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** jargon, stacked complexity, or procedural opacity used to defeat [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) or [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) where materially relevant.
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** prices, odds, or market resolution sources used as enough for rights, safety, or governance truth determinations where باب یکم forbids that substitution, or refusal of correction after [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="epistemic-integrity"></a>

##### حقائق کی دیانت

*سادہ الفاظ میں: the methods and evidence behind an evaluation must stay honest and checkable — prestige, opacity, or paperwork cannot stand in for truth.*

<a id="epistemic-integrity-o"></a>
- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Integrity of methods, evidence bases, and communications for constitutionally relevant evaluation — including clarity about:
    - حدود؛
    - uncertainties;
    - conflicts of evidence; and
    - contestable inference steps —
    so [سچائی (آئینی پابندی)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) cannot be bypassed through procedural form, model opacity, or non-auditable inference.
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - method documentation alone;
    - institutional prestige rankings; or
    - ordinary stylistic polish without a constitutionally relevant evaluation stake —
    those are not epistemic integrity by themselves.
<a id="epistemic-integrity-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا the methods, evidence, and challengeable reasoning stay honest and checkable — so paperwork, black-box opacity, or prestige cannot stand in for truth.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Require enough openness about methods to match the stakes, within safety and security limits — and watch for any of the following standing in for real integrity:
    - authority;
    - prestige;
    - scale; or
    - complexity.
  - **ثانوی پیمائش:** [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [شفافیت](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — co-measures that can defeat integrity even when methods are formally documented.

    **ثانوی جائزہ:** بنیادی پیمائش جانچتے ہوئے also check [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [تصدیق پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), and [چیلنج پذیری](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) wherever they matter — and include foreseeable misuse and adversarial testing under [پیش بینی کی مستعدی](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
  - **ثالثی پیمائش:** [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where authority, prestige, scale, or complexity stands in for demonstrable integrity.

    **ثالثی جائزہ:** Where operators lean on authority, model opacity, or unverifiable assertion, apply [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate durable epistemic integrity from procedural form.
<a id="epistemic-integrity-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - a truthful-sounding claim without honest treatment of methods, limits, and contestable inference;
    - evaluation or communication practices that defeat honest uncertainty treatment; or
    - non-auditable inference where audit is materially required.
  - **ثانوی ناکامی:** methodological choices that foreseeably prevent verification or meaningful challenge; integrity claims that rest on unverifiable assertion where observable evidence is reasonably achievable.
  - **ثالثی ناکامی:** formal method documentation or institutional prestige treated as decisive where [متبادل اشاروں کا انحراف](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

**Admission scope.** This sub-block applies when the matter materially concerns:
- whether the following were reasonably identifiable before action:
  - outcomes;
  - خطرات؛
  - نقصان؛
  - misuse pathways; or
  - operating conditions;
- whether “we could not have foreseen it” claims are justified;
- whether analysis depth, scope, and scaling match [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [مادی خطرہ](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency); or
- whether evaluation was incomplete in a way that defeats [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) assessment, or [پیش بینی کی مستعدی](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
Outside that scope, individual entries may still apply on their own without importing the full compound.

Where admission scope is met, this nested sub-block is the joint home for foreseeability mechanics within Def.O2 *Truth and Epistemic Integrity*. All uses of “reasonably foreseeable” in **باب دوم تا چہارم** are governed exclusively by these definitions and must stay explicitly traceable where invoked ([باب دوم، §3 — تعریف کے ڈھیر پار مطابقت](core_02_definition_structure.md#3-consistency-across-the-definition-stack)).

**ذیلی بلاک ارکان۔** اس ذیلی بلاک میں ہے:

- [پیش بینی کی مستعدی](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence) — reasonable analysis sized to impact: required depth, evaluation boundaries, incompleteness failure, and justified non-foreseeability claims; and
- [معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) — outcomes a careful analyst using known methods could identify before acting.

**§3.3 کے اندر بائی پاس مخالف۔** پیش بینی کے تجزیے کو الگ گہرائی، حد، دعوے کے بوجھ، نامکملی، یا معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے سوالوں میں اس طرح نہ کاٹیں کہ ایک پورا ہو اور دوسرا ہار جائے۔ آڈٹ کے موافق جواز کے بغیر غیر پیش بینی کا دعویٰ، یا جہاں مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، دہرائی، یا غلط استعمال سیاق مادی ہوں صرف بہترین صورت کی کارروائی کا جائزہ، داخلے کے دائرے میں عدمِ اطاعت ہے۔

##### پیش بینی کی مستعدی

*سادہ الفاظ میں: do a reasonable analysis sized to the impact — dig deeper when stakes are high, cover the required boundaries (including interactions, dependency, and adversarial use), and if you claim something was unforeseeable, justify that claim. Assertion and checkboxes are not enough.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** A standard of reasonable analysis sized to impact, covering:
    - analysis depth that scales with [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) under [تناسب](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
    - required evaluation boundaries, including:
      - interactions;
      - dependency;
      - adversarial use; and
      - relevant [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries);
    - the duty to actually perform that evaluation; and
    - responsibility for justifying non-foreseeability claims under [معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:**
    - outcomes that remain unforeseeable after required diligence;
    - unbounded speculative review of every conceivable outcome — that sits under [معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) exclusions; or
    - flat-maximal analysis demanded for every low-stakes case — depth scales with impact; it is not unbounded maximalism.

<a id="foreseeability-failure-a"></a>

<a id="foreseeability-scope-a"></a>
<a id="foreseeability-diligence-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا analysis depth matches impact, risk, and dependency; whether required boundaries were covered; whether the evaluation was actually performed; and whether non-foreseeability claims have audit-compatible justification.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Include known methods and patterns for what is [معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable), scaled to:
    - [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk); and
    - [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency).
    Include evaluation boundaries covering:
    - interactions;
    - [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency);
    - adversarial use; and
    - relevant [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
    Include [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible justification for non-foreseeability claims. Spot incompleteness that defeats diligence, [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) evaluation, or [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint).

- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - Analysis depth must scale with [مادی اثر](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) — neither flat-maximal for every low-stakes case nor superficial despite high impact.
  - Evaluation boundaries must include:
    - interactions;
    - [انحصار](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency);
    - adversarial use; and
    - relevant [نظام کی سرحدیں](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
  - Non-foreseeability claims require [آڈٹ پذیری](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible justification — assertion alone is not enough.
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:**
    - checkbox analysis with no real foreseeability evaluation;
    - mere assertion that something was unforeseeable, without audit-compatible justification;
    - self-serving exclusions that shrink below the required evaluation boundaries;
    - failure to meet this standard or to perform required foreseeability evaluation;
    - superficial analysis despite high impact;
    - scope limitation below required boundaries;
    - insufficient justification for a non-foreseeability claim; or
    - incompleteness that defeats [پیش بینی کی مستعدی](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence), [خطرہ](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) evaluation, or [حفاظت (پابندی)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint).

<a id="reasonably-foreseeable"></a>

##### معقول طور پر پیش بینی کے قابل

*سادہ الفاظ میں: outcomes a careful analyst using known methods could identify before acting — including under adversarial and scaled conditions — not every conceivable outcome or pure hindsight.*

- **یہ کیا ہے**
  - **دائرے میں:** Outcomes identifiable using domain knowledge and analytical methods under [پیش بینی کی مستعدی](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
  - **دائرے سے باہر:** Every conceivable outcome, pure hindsight, or results knowable only with knowledge or methods beyond reasonable diligence.
<a id="reasonably-foreseeable-a"></a>
- **کیسے ناپیں اور جانچیں**
  - **بنیادی پیمائش:** [نگرانی پیمائش خاندان](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *کیا شعوری وجود دیکھ، تصدیق اور بھروسہ کر سکتے ہیں کہ نظام کیا پیش کرتے ہیں؟* یہاں پوچھنے کے لیے اسے استعمال کریں کہ آیا identifiable outcomes under reasonable diligence are included — including adversarial and scaled conditions — not excluded by hindsight games or best-case-only framing.

    **بنیادی جائزہ:** Include adversarial and scaled conditions, including [مخالفانہ، پیمانہ شدہ، اور استحصالی شرائط](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions).
<a id="reasonably-foreseeable-c"></a>
- **کیا قائم رہنا چاہیے**
  - **بنیادی ناکامی:** exclusion of such outcomes.
