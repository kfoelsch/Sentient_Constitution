<a id="oversight-independent-terms"></a>

### Supervision : termes indépendants

<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions"></a>

#### Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Lire avec : [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [Capture du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : how a system behaves under pressure — when sentients game it, coordinate across accounts, run it at massive scale, or deliberately misuse it.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Operating conditions in which systems or actors behave to maximize advantage in ways that can alter [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), or [Capture du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture), including:
    - abus de la structure du système ;
    - coordination entre instances ;
    - high-scale deployment — volume, concurrency, or reach at which behavior or effects can differ from small-scale or ideal-lab use;
    - interaction répétée ; et
    - mésusage adaptatif ou intentionnel.
  - **Hors portée :** Ordinary benign use, ideal-lab testing, or one-off misuse anecdotes — those sit outside pressure, scale, cross-instance coordination, or deliberate exploitation.
<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si oversight still works under gaming, scale, coordination, and deliberate misuse — not only under ordinary or ideal-lab conditions.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Include plausible strategies that could reasonably emerge given system incentives, access, and capabilities, read together with [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) and [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), covering:
    - mésusage ;
    - coordination ;
    - mise à l’échelle ; et
    - réponse adaptative.
<a id="adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** Failure to evaluate these conditions where relevant is non-compliant, especially where it understates [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) or masks [Capture du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-capture) — including using any of the following as a stand-in for pressure, scale, cross-instance coordination, or deliberate exploitation:
    - usage bénin ordinaire ;
    - tests de laboratoire idéal ; ou
    - anecdotes de mésusage ponctuel.

<a id="capability-requirement"></a>

#### Exigence de capacité

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Composante de groupe : [Matérialité, impact, risque et intégrité des indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- Lire avec : [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).

</details>

<br>

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** A mandatory functionality that a [Système](core_05_band_accountability.md#system) possesses sufficient to achieve a specified outcome, independent of implementation mechanism.
  - **Hors portée :** optional features, aspirational roadmap items, or design preferences that are not claimed as a mandatory capability for a specified outcome.
<a id="capability-requirement-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si claimed capability is demonstrated in practice — not only declared in design or documentation.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Judge capability by what the system actually does — not by:
    - docs de conception ;
    - plans ; ou
    - comportement visé.
    That proof must be clear enough for [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) and for outsiders to evaluate [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact).
<a id="capability-requirement-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - The system must demonstrably achieve the specified outcome in practice within auditable [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
  - **Défaillance primaire :** a claimed, documented, or planned capability with no demonstrated achievement of the specified outcome under the conditions in which the system is represented, authorized, or used.

<a id="classification-scaled-governance"></a>

#### Gouvernance mise à l’échelle de la classification

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- En amont : Principes : [Tétrade constitutionnelle](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — [enjeu matériel](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling.
- Composante de groupe : [Matérialité, impact, risque et intégrité des indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- Lire avec : [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), and [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional).
- En aval : [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) — class-scaled whole-system evaluation duty.

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : bigger stake means harder oversight — how closely a system is watched and checked must match how much it actually matters, not a convenient low label.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** How hard [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), verification, and process discipline must be — scaled to [enjeu matériel](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake), covering:
    - [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency); and
    - [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk).
    The system's class under *[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md)*, CS-3 — Classification et traitement des systèmes, and related rules sets that intensity.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - ordinary uniform process detail among systems that share the same real class under CS-3; or
    - implementation typing and class-assignment mechanics owned by CS-3 —
    ceux-là relèvent de leurs propres foyers; this entry owns stake-scaled intensity, not the typing rules themselves.
<a id="classification-scaled-governance-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si oversight depth and verification burden match the system's real stake, impact, dependency, and risk — not a self-assigned low class.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Use the real class under the implementation rules, read with [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity). A self-assigned or informal low class is not enough when the system's full effects call for stronger [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional) or governance. Whole-system review under [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) scales by class:
    - **Class A**, **Class B**, and **Class C** — full depth, mandatory;
    - **Class L** — mandatory, but sized to the impact; and
    - **Class P** — encouraged while the system is validly **Class P**.
<a id="classification-scaled-governance-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - one-size-fits-all oversight that ignores real stake differences;
    - a class label that understates the system's real stake, impact, dependency, or risk;
    - running or labeling a system below what its full effects warrant; or
    - skipping class-based duties when [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), or [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) require stronger governance.

<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional"></a>

<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity"></a>
#### Presse et activité journalistique

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Composante de groupe : [Info-sphère](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere)

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : journalism is what sentients do — gathering news, protecting sources, investigating, and publishing — not a badge, title, or press pass. Actions meant to shut that down get heightened scrutiny.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Journalistic activity that functions as journalism — judged by what sentients actually do, not by credentials or institutional status — including:
    - collecte d’informations ;
    - protection des sources ;
    - enquête ; et
    - publication.
    Sentients doing that work exercise the [Expression](core_05_band_participation.md#expression-constitutional) and [Assemblée](core_05_band_participation.md#assembly-constitutional) Plancher des droits, with heightened-scrutiny protection under [Article V-H](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-h-expression-assembly-and-press) (*Expression, Assembly, and Press*) against state and operator actions meant to impair that activity.
  - **Hors portée :** press credentials, institutional titles, or ordinary commercial speech that is not newsgathering, investigation, or publication functioning as journalism.
<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si journalistic function and press-directed impairment remain visible and contestable — not hidden behind credential, title, or institutional status.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Ask what the activity actually does — not what badge, title, org form, or institution someone holds — covering:
    - collecte d’informations ;
    - protection des sources ;
    - investigation; or
    - publication that serves the information environment.
    Spot actions that impair the press by their real effect, and treat press-directed character as an aggravating factor under **Article XIII-A** (*Security, Intelligence, and Covert-Power Limits*) and **Article V-H** (*Expression, Assembly, and Press*). Apply heightened [Nécessité](core_05_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionnalité](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality) scrutiny, and keep any limit narrowly tailored.
<a id="press-and-journalistic-activity-constitutional-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** state or operator actions meant to impair journalistic activity that fail heightened-scrutiny limits analysis;
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** stretching [Bonne foi](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith) or **Article VIII-C** (*Truthful Publication and High-Impact Publication Limits*) so those standards block lawful critical reporting, investigative publication, or dissent;
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** credentials, institutional status, or ordinary commercial speech used as the journalism test while newsgathering, investigation, or publication that functions as journalism is absent.

### Supervision : termes semi-indépendants

<a id="materiality-semi-independent"></a>

<a id="materiality-impact-risk-and-proxy-integrity"></a>
#### Matérialité, impact, risque et intégrité des indicateurs substituts

**Contexte semi-indépendant** (les définitions composantes peuvent encore opérer hors de la portée d’invocation conjointe) :

- **Invocation conjointe :** where materiality classification, material impact or risk, proxy integrity, or material degradation of a protected condition is material.
- **Scope:** where joint invocation is met, the Oversight home for two questions — when do effects matter enough constitutionally, and do the metrics still track reality? Evaluation must rest on real effects — not on manipulable proxies.

**Membres du groupe de thèmes.** Ce groupe comprend :
- [Matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material) — whether effects are big enough to matter constitutionally.
- [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — the method for classifying that, for systems or events.
- [Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) — bars gaming boundaries to shrink the picture.
- [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) — how far, how large, and how severe the effects are.
- [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) — how likely, how fast, and how hard to undo the harm may be.
- [Matérialité sous incertitude](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-under-uncertainty) — defaults to caution when the answer is unclear.
- [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — flags when a metric no longer matches reality, including when the score is cooked while conditions worsen.
- [Dégradation matérielle](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-degradation) — a real drop in a protected condition such as safety, epistemic integrity, or meaningful agency.

<a id="materiality-operator-phrases"></a>

**Locutions d’opérateur** (corpus-wide reading aids owned with [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)):

- <a id="materially-implicated"></a>**matériellement impliqué** — this duty, measure, or topic is *on the table* for the case because the stake is material.
- <a id="materially-misaligned"></a>**matériellement désaligné** / **désalignement constitutionnel** — conduct or structure is *out of constitutional alignment* at a material scale.

Implication can exist without misalignment. Misalignment always implies material implication.

*Mesures (routage de famille) :* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on the member definitions below.

<a id="material"></a>

#### Matériel

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- En aval : Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); Principes : [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (**Materiality** DEC stack; evaluation completeness); [6.1 Principes d’arbitrage centraux](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#61-core-tradeoff-principles) (proportionality stack read with material effects).
- Lire avec : [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), and [Gouvernance mise à l’échelle de la classification](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : big enough to matter — not trivia, cosmetics, or paperwork that changes nothing real.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Conditions that can produce meaningful [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk).
  - **Hors portée :** Trivia, cosmetics, or mere paperwork with no meaningful material impact or material risk.
<a id="material-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — threshold input for [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).

    **Évaluation primaire :** Ask whether the conditions can produce meaningful [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) when you look at the whole system, not just a slice.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), and [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) — co-measures that can defeat material classification when effects are fragmented or understated.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, ask whether effects that look small one by one would still look small if you put them together — including system-wide [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) pathways.
<a id="material-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** ignoring conditions that [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) would treat as material.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** calling individually small effects immaterial when, taken together or system-wide, they would count as material.

<a id="materiality-determination"></a>

#### Détermination de matérialité

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- En aval : Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) (*Materiality as constitutional measurement*); Principes : [enjeu matériel](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake) scaling proxy; Chapitre un surfaces this definition across **Safety** (**Chapitre un §8.1**), **Truth** (**Chapitre un §8.2**), **Trust** (**§12**), **§12** stewardship and openness passages, **§13.1**, **§13.2**, and **Chapitre un §6.3.2** where **Materiality** appears in D/A/C widgets — see [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) as the principal systems-evaluation home.
- Composante de groupe : [Matérialité, impact, risque et intégrité des indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- Lire avec : [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality), and [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : the method for deciding whether an impact or risk is constitutionally material — big enough in stake to change which duties apply — for a system or an event, looking at the connected whole, not just isolated pieces. That includes prospective harm, not only harm that has already happened.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Classification decisions that ask whether [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) is constitutionally material for:
    - a system (including [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality)); or
    - an event, incident, or episode (including prospective [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) under [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk)),
    when you look at the connected whole rather than isolated pieces.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - [Matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material) threshold trivia, cosmetics, or mere paperwork significance with no stake-scaled duty change; or
    - ordinary classification disagreement that still evaluates the connected whole under [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) —
    ceux-là relèvent de leurs propres foyers or remain ordinary disagreement, not a substitute determination.
<a id="materiality-determination-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** Under [enjeu matériel](core_00_preamble.md#material-stake), ask whether effects are big enough to change which duties apply when you look at the connected whole — for the system or event at issue — covering:
    - [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) (including prospective [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm)); and
    - [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality) (when system-wide effects are in play).

    **Évaluation primaire :** Include all kinds of effects that matter for the case, covering:
    - realized impact;
    - prospective harm; and
    - [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_continuity.md#systemic-materiality) where relevant,
    and explain the conclusion for the connected whole, not just a convenient slice.

    For an event, ask whether it is relevant to a sentient's [Bien-être](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing) — including plausible prospective harm to that wellbeing — not only to:
    - system métriques ;
    - process labels; or
    - institutional convenience.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) — check whether calling something "not material" only works because the pieces were judged separately, while the connected whole would count as material. For an event affecting a sentient, include downstream effects that travel through dependency — not only the immediate hit on that sentient.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, ask whether the classification would change if you looked at the pieces together instead of one by one, including:
    - isolation;
    - aggregation;
    - dependency; and
    - for an event, downstream effects on other sentients or systems that depend on the affected sentient or on what the event disrupted.
  - **Mesure tertiaire :** [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) and [Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) — where boundary games, aggregation avoidance, or proxy metrics defeat honest materiality analysis.

    **Évaluation tertiaire :** Where operators lean on:
    - boundaries;
    - proxies; or
    - informal low class,
    apply [Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) and [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate real materiality analysis from structural manipulation or score gaming.
<a id="materiality-determination-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - A Materiality Determination holds only if the classification is written down, [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and tied to effects that actually matter constitutionally.
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - paper-only or immaterial labels with no real change to which constitutional duties apply — governance, oversight, capability requirements, or other stake-scaled duties for the system or event at issue;
    - determinations that skip material effect types — including prospective harm for an event, or whether the event is relevant to a sentient's [Bien-être](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing); or
    - determinations that lack a justified conclusion.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** calling collectively material effects immaterial by:
    - splitting them up;
    - avoiding aggregation; or
    - ignoring dependency.
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** determinations that rest on manipulated boundaries or "the metric looks fine" where [Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint) or [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint"></a>

#### Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- En aval : Principes : [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (aggregation and boundary integrity in evaluation); [§6.2.4 Invalidation par divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#624-proxy-divergence-invalidation) (metric/reality gaps that can manipulate materiality).
- Lire avec : [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Reddition de comptes](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : you may not make a big problem look small by redrawing boxes, splitting pieces, or reclassifying your way out of it.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** A ban on shrinking materiality by manipulating [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), aggregation, or classification contrary to [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity).
  - **Hors portée :** Ordinary disagreement about materiality, or honest boundary clarification that does not shrink collectively material effects through splitting, aggregation games, or reclassification.
<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — ask whether anyone is making a material problem look smaller by redrawing boxes, splitting pieces, or reclassifying around it.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Look for structural moves that would defeat honest [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), including:
    - splitting;
    - aggregation avoidance; and
    - boundary games.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — co-measures that defeat manipulation or proxy gaming of materiality.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, include [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) paths that would hide collectively material effects.
<a id="materiality-integrity-constraint-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** treating collectively material effects as immaterial through structural manipulation.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** boundary or aggregation games that defeat [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), or relying on "the metric looks fine" where [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

#### Impact matériel

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : how far the effects reach, how big they are, and how severe — looking at the whole system, not just the headline or the paperwork.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Effects for [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and constitutional evaluation, covering:
    - how far they reach;
    - how large they are; and
    - how severe they are.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - mere attention, visibility, or paperwork significance with no reach, size, or severity stake; or
    - prospective [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) analysis —
    cela relève de son propre foyer.
<a id="material-impact-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — scope-and-severity input for materiality classification.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Trace how far, how large, and how severe the effects are for the whole system — including:
    - direct effets ;
    - indirect effets ;
    - delayed effets ;
    - cumulative effets ; and
    - cross-system consequences.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), and [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency) — co-measures that can defeat impact assessment when looking only at pieces understates effects.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, include [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and cross-system spread that would change the material classification.
<a id="material-impact-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - prospective-risk talk used in place of actual reach, size, or severity analysis; or
    - material impacts that do not trigger the right constraints under [Gouvernance mise à l’échelle de la classification](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional), and [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency).
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** fragmented or dependency-hidden effects labeled immaterial when joint analysis would classify them as material.

#### Risque matériel

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

<br>

*En termes simples : how likely harm is, how fast it can arrive, and how hard it is to undo — including rare but severe outcomes.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Prospective [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), covering:
    - how likely it is;
    - how fast it can arrive; and
    - how hard it is to undo.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - speculative worry without a plausible harm pathway; or
    - realized [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) already in hand —

<a id="material-risk-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — likelihood-and-irreversibility input for material risk classification.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Looking at the whole system, include:
    - expected outcomes;
    - rare-but-severe (tail) outcomes;
    - how fast [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm) can arrive; and
    - how irreversible that harm can be.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), [Préjudice irréversible](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — co-measures that can defeat risk classification when tail or systemic pathways are ignored.

<a id="material-risk-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - realized harm labeled as only future risk; or
    - material risk not addressed and constrained under [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [Gouvernance mise à l’échelle de la classification](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance).
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** tail or irreversible pathways excluded when [Préjudice irréversible](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) remains plausible.

#### Matérialité sous incertitude

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- En aval : Principes : [6.1.3 Proportionnalité](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#613-proportionality) (reversibility under uncertainty); [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (precaution against omitted harms).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : when you are not sure whether something is material, default to the safer (higher) classification — do not wait for certainty or hope for a low label.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** What to do by default when [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) or [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk) is uncertain.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - cases where materiality is already settled with no remaining material uncertainty; or
    - ordinary [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) once uncertainty is not material —
    those sit under settled classification, not this precautionary default.
<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — precautionary default when materiality cannot be excluded.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Default to precaution when you cannot rule materiality out — including when [Préjudice irréversible](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) remains plausible under uncertainty.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), [Préjudice irréversible](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm), and [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — co-measures that require conservative classification under uncertainty.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, include [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risque matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-risk), and [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) paths that would require a conservative default classification.
<a id="materiality-under-uncertainty-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - waiting for certainty before classifying while material impact or risk remains uncertain;
    - optimistic low classification while material impact or risk remains uncertain; or
    - failure to default to conservative classification under uncertainty where [Gouvernance mise à l’échelle de la classification](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) requires it.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** optimism or informal low classification where [Préjudice irréversible](core_05_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) or [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires precaution.

<a id="proxy-divergence"></a>

#### Divergence d’indicateurs substituts

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- En aval : Oversight measurement family (*Truth and epistemic integrity as constitutional measurement*); Accountability measurement family (*Incentive alignment and proxy integrity as constitutional measurement*); Principes : [§6.2.4 Invalidation par divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#624-proxy-divergence-invalidation), [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation), and [§10 Gouvernance sous discipline d’administration responsable](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#10-governance-under-stewardship-discipline).
- Composante de groupe : [Matérialité, impact, risque et intégrité des indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-semi-independent).
- Lire avec : [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint), [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [Alignement des incitations](core_05_band_integrative.md#incentive-alignment), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : when the score no longer matches reality — a dashboard, class label, or compliance indicator that says one thing while the real condition says another — including cooking the score so it looks fine while conditions stay bad, get worse, or go unmeasured.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** A material gap between a proxy and the constitutionally material reality it claims to represent, covering:
    - the gap itself; and
    - conduct, design, reporting, or incentives that improve, preserve, or manipulate the proxy while the underlying reality stays degraded, worsens, or is not measured.
    Proxies include:
    - métriques ;
    - model signals;
    - formal class;
    - compliance indicators; and
    - administrative shorthand.
    Claimed realities include rights, risks, burdens, harms, capabilities, and outcomes.
  - **Hors portée :** Ordinary measurement noise; a small non-material gap that does not detach the proxy from constitutionally relevant reality; or honest metric improvement that tracks real outcomes.
<a id="proxy-divergence-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) and [Famille de mesure Reddition de comptes](core_05_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability-measurement-family) — *Can sentients see, verify, and rely on what systems represent?* / *Les structures de récompense, le pouvoir de marché et le devoir de répondre maintiennent-ils les devoirs réels ?* Utilisez-les ici pour demander si proxies still track constitutionally material reality — and whether reward, reporting, or gating still rests on those proxies after they diverge, including when the score is being gamed or protected.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Ask whether optimization, reporting, gating, or review still leans on a proxy after evidence shows that proxy no longer tracks the constitutionally material reality. Look for score-cooking moves, including:
    - dashboard optimization;
    - threshold gaming;
    - selective sampling;
    - denominator manipulation;
    - excluded populations;
    - automated score improvement;
    - model reward hacking;
    - compliance theater; and
    - narrative substitution.
    A metric is not reliable when it cannot be traced to observed material outcomes, or when incentives predictably pull it away from those outcomes.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Contrainte d’intégrité de la matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-integrity-constraint), and [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) — co-measures that can defeat proxy integrity even when reported indicators look stable.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, look for:
    - selection effets ;
    - boundary manipulation;
    - missing populations;
    - feedback loops;
    - gaming incentives;
    - adversarial adaptation; and
    - aggregate harms hidden by favorable local indicators —
    together with [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability).
  - **Mesure tertiaire :** [Bien-être](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing), [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and Plancher des droits outcomes — where proxies stand in for what sentients actually experience.

    **Évaluation tertiaire :** Where proxies stand in for outcome measures, trace to [Bien-être](core_05_band_continuity.md#wellbeing), [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), or Plancher des droits delivery — separate durable constitutional outcomes from proxy throughput.
<a id="proxy-divergence-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - A system satisfies **Proxy Divergence** discipline only if proxies stay aligned with constitutionally material reality under full-system conditions — including when incentives or design would otherwise cook the score.
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - treating a proxy as decisive when the gap is material;
    - claiming compliance from improved metrics while material outcomes stay deficient;
    - designing indicators that can be satisfied without satisfying the underlying duty; or
    - suppressing evidence that indicators have come detached from reality.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** using "the metric looks fine" to skip direct evaluation of Plancher des droits, safety, truth, wellbeing, ecological, or agency outcomes while divergence is reasonably observable.
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** refusing correction after the gap between reported indicators and what sentients actually experience becomes reasonably observable.

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

  - **Dans la portée :** A non-trivial drop in how well a protected constitutional condition holds, covering:
    - [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint);

    - [Agence significative](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).

<a id="material-degradation-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** Matérialité Intégrative ([Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination)) — degradation signal for materiality classification.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Include scaling and system-wide effects under [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality) and [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) when judging a non-trivial drop in protected conditions.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Matérialité systémique](core_05_band_accountability.md#systemic-materiality), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — co-measures that can defeat degradation assessment when proxies hide decline.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, apply [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) and [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) where metrics hide material degradation.
<a id="material-degradation-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** material degradation not treated as material under [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination).
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** keeping proxy indicators looking stable while [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), or [Agence significative](core_05_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) materially degrades.

- evidence preservation;
- auditability and records good enough to reconstruct what happened;
- evaluation completeness against plausible failure modes;
- externally detectable indicators usable for audit and independent verification;
- independent verification; or
- whether verification is accessible and independent under scaled or adversarial conditions.
Outside that scope, individual entries may still apply on their own without importing the whole cluster.

Where admission scope is met, this cluster is the joint home for disclosure, audit, verification (including external detectability), and assurance depth when Chapitres deux à quatre require traceable, challengeable evidence of what a system does and whether compliance claims hold.

**Cadre de supervision.** Under the [Tétrade constitutionnelle](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) **oversight** leg, oversight requires auditing — being able to reconstruct what happened, verify it independently, and get audit access — owned here and at **Article XV** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*). [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) is one especially large, high-stakes audit process among others (forum-supervised alignment review with recognition outcomes under [Chapitre sept](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-seven-system-alignment-certification)); it does not swallow this cluster or replace sibling audit modes.

**Classe du système et proportionnalité.** Transparency, auditability, and verification duties scale with system class under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — Classification et traitement des systèmes**, and with material impact, dependency, and risk. Higher-class systems (**Class A**, **B**, **C**) need more than lower-class systems (**Class L**, **P**), including:
- assurance plus profonde ;
- registres plus fins ;
- vérification indépendante plus forte ; et
- portée d’audit plus large.

**Ordre de lecture.** Start with what must be disclosed ([Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure)); then whether outsiders can reconstruct ([Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) and audit supports); then whether behavior is externally detectable and independently checkable ([Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability)).

**Membres du groupe.** Ce groupe comprend :

- [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) — decision-relevant clarity about function, limits, and risk;
- [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) — the required public disclosure floor for high-impact systems, including lawful substitutes;
- [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) — communicating evaluated systemic risk to those who need it;
- [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) — whether outsiders can reconstruct and check what a system did, across the material times, states, and contexts that matter;
- [Préservation de la preuve](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation) — identify, secure, and keep materially relevant evidence reviewably available;
- [Contrainte de complétude de l’évaluation](core_05_band_oversight.md#evaluation-completeness-constraint) — cover plausible failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial pathways before claiming compliance; and
- [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — externally detect and measure behavior and effects, and independently validate what was observed under practical access, independence, proportionate burden, and stressed-condition reliability.

**Anti-contournement.** Within the admission scope, do not split the matter into separate disclosure, preservation, logging, metrics, audit sampling, verification UX, or independence questions in a way that checks a box on one channel while defeating practical reconstruction, challenge, or proportionate assurance depth.

Voir **Invocation conjointe et satisfaction**.

*Mesures (routage de famille) :* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) below.

##### Transparence

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Composante de groupe : [Def.O1 Transparence, auditabilité et vérification](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- En aval : Principes : [Tétrade constitutionnelle](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — **oversight** leg; [§9 Administration responsable et compréhension distribuée](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#9-stewardship-and-distributed-understanding), [6.2.1 Préservation de l’intégrité épistémique](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#621-preservation-of-epistemic-integrity).
- Lire avec : [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure), [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), and **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — Types d’information et traitement** (**Type O** — open public oversight baseline disclosure data).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : telling affected sentients enough about how a system works, its limits, and its risks that they can genuinely understand and question it — not burying them in noise.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Giving affected sentients enough information about how a system behaves outwardly — its limits, uncertainties, and what depends on it — that they can understand what it does and what risks it creates. Legitimate safety and security limits still apply, consistent with [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) and [6.2 Contraintes de divulgation épistémique](core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#62-epistemic-disclosure-constraints).
    - If a material system, institution, forum process, or similar governed domain can lawfully publish online, its baseline transparency disclosure must be free to the public at the access level its class requires — not behind a paywall or available only to insiders — wherever [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) applies.
    - If publishing the raw material would break lawful protections, the owner must still publish the strongest public substitute reasonably possible online, without gutting accountability or [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — for example:
      - aggregation;
      - de-identification;
      - summary disclosure;
      - delayed disclosure; or
      - qualified audit access.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) reconstruction channels alone;
    - [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) Type O floor mechanics as such; or
    - ordinary commercial marketing that is not decision-relevant disclosure to affected parties —
    ceux-là relèvent de leurs propres foyers or ordinary commercial speech.
<a id="transparency-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si affected parties get decision-relevant clarity about function, limits, and risk — not volume, noise, or insider-only access.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Compare what is disclosed to [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency).
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — co-measures that can defeat transparency even when disclosure volume is high.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** When checking the primary answer:
    - spot volume, obscurity, or formatting standing in for decision-relevant clarity; and
    - apply safety- and security-justified limits consistent with [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).

    Decision-relevant disclosure fails in practice when it cannot be checked — do not treat unverifiable disclosure as decision-relevant clarity. [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) owns those check conditions, including:
    - practical access;
    - independence from operator self-assertion;
    - proportionate burden; and
    - reliability under stress, misuse, and adversarial pressure.
  - **Mesure tertiaire :** [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where volume, formatting, or procedural opacity stands in for decision-relevant clarity.

    **Évaluation tertiaire :** Where operators lean on disclosure volume or formatting, apply [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate real transparency from burying sentients in volume.
<a id="transparency-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - a data dump, mountain of documents, paywall, or insider-only access with no real decision-relevant clarity about function and risk for affected sentients; or
    - material omissions or disclosures that foreseeably block informed participation or [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional) where required.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** safety- or security-framed limits that defeat materially required clarity without satisfying [Nécessité](core_05_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionnalité](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** disclosure volume or formatting treated as decisive where [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="public-oversight-baseline-disclosure"></a>

##### Divulgation de base de supervision publique

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Composante de groupe : [Def.O1 Transparence, auditabilité et vérification](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- En aval : **[CS-2 Part A §7](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-2-7-type-o-baseline-for-class-a-b-c-systems)** (*Type O baseline for Class A/B/C systems*); **[CS-2 Part B — Type O](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_b_data_classifications.md#83-type-o-open-public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-data)** (*typing and O/E/G relationship*); [§4](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-data-types-and-handling-evaluation) (*Data Types and Handling Evaluation*); [§11](../../core_07_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#11-certification-record) (*Certification record*); [§12](../../core_07_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#12-transparency-auditability-and-contestability) (*Transparency, auditability, and contestability*).
- Lire avec : [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure), [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional), [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional), [Registre de classification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional), [Registre des types de données du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional), [Gouvernance mise à l’échelle de la classification](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), and **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — Types d’information et traitement** (**Type O**).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : le minimum de divulgation publique que les systèmes à fort impact doivent publier pour que les êtres sentients puissent les comprendre, les superviser et les contester — y compris le but, la classe, les dépendances, le statut, le risque, la performance, les défaillances, la gouvernance, les effets sur les parties affectées, l’alignement constitutionnel, et la face publique de la Charte, de la classification, du typage des données et des registres de certification — avec un substitut public fort lorsque les registres bruts privés ou hors-base ne peuvent pas être publiés.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** The required public disclosure floor so sentients can oversee and challenge materially impactful systems — especially **Class A**, **Class B**, and **Class C** under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — Classification et traitement des systèmes**. Coverage follows the governing [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument), assigned class, and observed [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries). It includes what sentients need to understand:
    - purpose and what the system does;
    - how it is classified;
    - dependency structure and what depends on it;
    - operational status and how it is running;
    - material risks, performance, and failures;
    - how it is governed and material audit outcomes;
    - stakeholder effets ; and
    - how aligned it is with this Constitution.
  - **Class A/B/C Type O instruments:** Where the **Type O** baseline applies for **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C**, that floor includes — at public-baseline fidelity, including lawful substitutes — publication or a usable public view of:
    - the governing [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument);
    - the [Registre de classification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional);
    - the [Registre des types de données du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional); and
    - the [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) produced by [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional), including material governance and audit outcomes required for baseline visibility.
  - **Topic satisfaction:** Baseline visibility into purpose, operational status, material risk, performance, failures, and stakeholder effects may be satisfied through those records, [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) where systemic risk is in scope, and other lawful **Type O** summaries or substitutes — not by omitting the topics.
  - **Lawful substitutes:** Stay in scope where **Type G**, **Type E**, or other source records stay non-public, non-baseline, or otherwise restricted, including:
    - aggregation;
    - de-identification;
    - summary or delayed disclosure; and
    - comparable artifacts.
  - **Type O / Type E / Type G relationship:**
    - This disclosure floor is typed and handled as **Type O**.
    - **Type E** holds survival- and coordination-critical content streams; **Type G** holds audit-accessible governance and operational source that is not public by default.
    - Neither **Type E** nor **Type G** is itself the public floor.
    - Published baseline artifacts drawn from them — including substitutes — are **Type O**; underlying streams may remain under their original type.
    - Systems-layer typing, substitute mechanics, and holding-back limits are owned by **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — Types d’information et traitement** as **Type O**.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - raw **Type G**, **Type E**, or other restricted source records — publishing a public substitute derived from them does not bring those raw records into this disclosure floor;
    - [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) structured or qualified audit access as such — that is not itself the public floor; or
    - [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) of evaluated systemic risk to those who need it —
    ceux-là relèvent de leurs propres foyers.
<a id="public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si the required public disclosure floor is actually published and usable for oversight and challenge — including lawful substitutes where raw records stay restricted.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Compare published disclosure to certified scope from [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), assigned class, and [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries). Where the **Type O** baseline applies for a **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** system under **[CS-2 Part A §7](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-2-7-type-o-baseline-for-class-a-b-c-systems)**, confirm:
    - a usable public view of the [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [Registre de classification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional), [Registre des types de données du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional), and [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) (or required contents) at public-baseline fidelity;
    - that purpose, dependency structure, operational status, material risks, performance, failures, governance, audit outcomes, stakeholder effects, and constitutional compliance are each covered at baseline depth;
    - that lawful substitutes still support meaningful accountability and [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability); and
    - that free online availability holds where lawful publication infrastructure exists.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Divulgation du risque](core_05_band_oversight.md#risk-disclosure) — co-measures that can defeat the floor even when some public artifacts exist.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, look for:
    - thin summaries;
    - audit-only substitution;
    - coverage gaps against certified scope;
    - missing or non-usable public views of required Charter, classification, data-types, or certification registres ;
    - missing baseline visibility into performance, failures, stakeholder effects, or material risk; and
    - hold-backs that hide systemic behavior, constitutional violations, material risk, dependency, failure, or externalized cost.
<a id="public-oversight-baseline-disclosure-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** missing or inadequate Public Oversight Baseline Disclosure where the **Type O** baseline for **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** applies under CS-2.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :**
    - non-public **Type G** audit access, paywalls, or performative summaries used as the public floor;
    - performative summaries that withhold decision-relevant baseline material;
    - paywalled or insider-only distribution of the floor where lawful online publication infrastructure exists for class-appropriate public access;
    - omitting a usable public view of the [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [Registre de classification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional), [Registre des types de données du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional), or [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) where the **Type O** baseline applies for a **Class A**, **Class B**, or **Class C** system under **[CS-2 Part A §7](../../corpus_systems/cs_02_a_information_types_and_handling.md#cs-2-7-type-o-baseline-for-class-a-b-c-systems)**; or
    - omitting baseline visibility into purpose, dependency structure, operational status, material risks, performance, failures, governance, audit outcomes, stakeholder effects, or constitutional compliance.
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** hold-backs that are not narrow, documented, proportionate, auditable, and challengeable under CS-2 and [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency).

##### Divulgation du risque

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Composante de groupe : [Def.O1 Transparence, auditabilité et vérification](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).
- En aval : Oversight measurement family (*disclosure and assurance as constitutional measurement*); Principes : [Tétrade constitutionnelle](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) — **oversight** leg; [§3.1 Sécurité](core_01_a_values_principles.md#31-safety-harm-constraint); [§3.1](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors) (*SAC evaluation-and-disclosure bridge*); [§11.1](../../core_07_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#111-minimum-record-contents) (*findings on the System Certification Record — not a separate named risk-disclosure record*).
- Lire avec : [Évaluation du risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure), [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), and [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : tell the sentients who need to know about systemic risk — keeping the evaluation in a drawer does not count.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Communicating evaluated systemic [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) to those who need it so they can understand, challenge, and act.
    - **Ongoing duty:** The duty is not limited to certification cycles — it applies whenever [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) or [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires that evaluated risk reach the sentients who need it.
    - **SAC verification:** Where [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs and systemic risk is in scope, this disclosure is part of what certification must verify under [§3.1](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors); findings live on the [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional), not as a separate named risk-disclosure record.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - [Évaluation du risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation) itself — the duty to evaluate systemic risk;
    - [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) as the broader public baseline floor; or
    - findings placement on the [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional) rather than a peer record species beside the [Registre de classification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-classification-record-constitutional) or [Registre des types de données du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-data-types-record-constitutional) —
    ceux-là relèvent de leurs propres foyers.
<a id="risk-disclosure-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si evaluated systemic risk actually reaches the sentients who need it — not only whether an evaluation exists somewhere.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Ask whether disclosure is timely, decision-relevant, and sized to enjeu matériel under [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency). Read with [Évaluation du risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation) — disclosure without evaluation, or evaluation without disclosure, both fail.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Évaluation du risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk-evaluation), [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability), and [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) — other checks that can show disclosure failed even when some risk wording is on file.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, look for walls of text, buried details, insider-only channels, or late notice that keep the sentients who needed the risk picture from actually understanding it in time.
<a id="risk-disclosure-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - failure to disclose risk where [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) or [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) requires it; or
    - [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only context in which risk must be disclosed.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** an internal-only note, buried appendix, or after-the-fact statement used as disclosure when the sentients who needed the risk picture could not use it in time.

<br>

*En termes simples : **auditability** is whether outsiders can actually reconstruct and check what a system did — across the real times, states, and situations that matter, not just one happy-path snapshot. System alignment certification is one especially large way that property gets exercised for high-stakes systems; it is not the only auditing process.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Whether outsiders can systematically examine, reconstruct, and evaluate system behavior through enough [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) — including external detectability and independent validation.
    - **Coverage:** Must span the material times, states, and interaction contexts — not only a single snapshot or happy-path sample.
    - **Oversight role:** Under the **oversight** Tetrad leg, oversight requires this property; [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) is one especially large, high-stakes audit process that depends on it, among sibling audit modes.
    - **Operational capability:** Where binding duties require operational capability, auditability includes checking that claimed compliance rests on real, reconstructable capability — not text, assertions, or unimplemented controls alone ([Pile d’autorité et hiérarchie interne](core_05_band_integrative.md#authority-stack) supremacy effect).
  - **Hors portée :**
    - raw logs or [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) external detectability alone;
    - [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) or [Divulgation de base de supervision publique](core_05_band_oversight.md#public-oversight-baseline-disclosure) public disclosure alone; or
    - leaf measurement tiers and implementation mechanics owned by [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [Préservation de la preuve](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and CJS —
    ceux-là relèvent de leurs propres foyers; they are not themselves reconstructable auditability.
<a id="auditability-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si outsiders can reconstruct and check what the system did across the material times, states, and contexts — including whether claimed compliance rests on real capability, not paper alone.

    **Évaluation primaire :** It must work across relevant conditions and [Partie affectée](core_05_band_participation.md#stakeholder), including conditions relevant to [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) and [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional). Look for material failures, [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), and désalignement constitutionnel across:
    - [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries);
    - operating états ; and
    - [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions).
    Where a binding duty requires operational capability, ask whether claimed compliance is backed by verified capability — not:
      - text;
      - assertions;
      - paper controls;
      - emergency practice; or
      - unobservable claims.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [Préservation de la preuve](core_05_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation), and [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — other checks that can show auditability failed even when some audit channel exists on paper.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** When checking the primary answer:
    - where stewardship relies on repeated comparable measures, audit paths should support reviewing variation over time and threshold-triggered follow-through when claimed ([§9.3 Développement institutionnel](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#93-institutional-development); [Proportionnalité](core_05_band_accountability.md#proportionality)); and
    - confirm audit coverage reaches the evaluation criteria, components, dependencies, actors, conditions, and effects that matter — and is available for independent [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability) and [Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-constitutional).
  - **Mesure tertiaire :** [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where sampling, metrics, or ceremonial review stands in for practical reconstruction.

    **Évaluation tertiaire :** Where operators lean on check-the-box audit channels or thin sampling, apply [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — ask whether outsiders can really reconstruct what happened, or whether the review is mostly for show.
<a id="auditability-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - audit mechanisms that do not work;
    - failure to enable practical reconstruction and evaluation;
    - a single snapshot, happy-path sample, or narrow window that leaves out material times, states, or interaction contexts;
    - [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only auditing process or as a substitute for this property;
    - paper claims of constitutional compliance that cannot be reconstructed or challenged; or
    - any of the following used as enough compliance where binding duties require verified operational capability:
      - text;
      - assertions;
      - unimplemented controls;
      - support artifacts;
      - private instruments;
      - emergency practice; or
      - unobservable claims.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :**
    - audit scope that leaves out any of the following when materially relevant:
      - evaluation criteria;
      - system components;
      - dependencies;
      - acteurs ;
      - conditions;
      - effets ;
      - états ;
      - contexts; or
      - [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions);
    - supremacy or compliance claims while suppressing [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) or defeating auditable verification.
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** audit sampling or "the metric looks fine" treated as decisive where [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

##### Préservation de la preuve

<br>

*En termes simples : keep the evidence that matters — identify it, secure it, and make it available for review — once a serious constitutional issue is known or should be known.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** A duty to identify, secure, maintain, and make reviewably available materially relevant evidence needed for constitutional review, contestability, accountability, remediation, misconduct routing, or standing assessment, including:
    - registres ;
    - artifacts;
    - logs;
    - provenance data;
    - testimony;
    - system états ;
    - custody information; and
    - exculpatory or mitigating material.
  - **Hors portée :** Ordinary operational retention or backup that does not secure, maintain, and make reviewably available the material needed for review, contest, remedy, or standing measurement.
<a id="evidence-preservation-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si materially relevant evidence remains secured and reviewably available for audit, contest, remedy, and standing measurement.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Preservation must be prompt, sized to materiality and risk, custody-traceable, tamper-evident where feasible, and protective of privacy, safety, security, and protected reporting. Once a material constitutional issue is reasonably known or should be known, it includes preventing:
    - deletion;
    - alteration;
    - selective retention;
    - access obstruction; or
    - loss of reconstructability.
<a id="evidence-preservation-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** any of the following done to materially relevant evidence in a way that defeats auditability, contestability, accountability, remedy access, protected reporting, due-process review, or standing measurement:
    - destroying;
    - altering;
    - withholding;
    - selectively preserving;
    - making inaccessible; or
    - failing to secure.

##### Contrainte de complétude de l’évaluation

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Composante de groupe : [Def.O1 Transparence, auditabilité et vérification](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency-auditability-and-verification-cluster).

- Lire avec : [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional), [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter), [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries), [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity), [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), [Création de systèmes](core_05_band_participation.md#system-creation-constitutional), and [Création d’entreprises](core_05_band_participation.md#business-creation-constitutional).

</details>

<br>

*En termes simples : do not claim compliance from a narrow demo suite — evaluation must cover the failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial paths that matter. A Charter states intended scope; certification checks that the hard paths were actually evaluated.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** A constraint that evaluation and assurance work must cover materially plausible pathways before compliance claims rest on selectively narrow scenario sets, including:
    - défaillance ;
    - mésusage ;
    - interaction; and
    - adversarial pathways.
  - **Ongoing duty:** The constraint applies whenever operators, stewards, or reviewers claim constitutional compliance, readiness, or safe reliance from evaluation or assurance work — not only during certification cycles.
  - **Charter and scope process:** Where a governing [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) (or equivalent published scope instrument) exists for a system, institution, business, or comparable stewarded form, evaluation must test chartered purpose and in-scope / out-of-scope limits against observed [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries) and plausible pathways inside and at the edge of that scope. Charter text does not shrink the pathways that must be evaluated.
  - **SAC verification:** Where [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs, this constraint is part of what certification must verify under [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) — including chartered-scope-versus-functional-boundary checks under [§3.1](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors) and class-scaled testing / regression coverage under [CS-5](../../corpus_systems/cs_05_design_testing_verification_deployment.md). Findings live on the [Registre de certification du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-certification-record-constitutional).
  - **Hors portée :**
    - [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) text as a scope instrument alone — a Charter states intended scope; it is not itself evaluation coverage;
    - [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) process mechanics as such — certification may verify this constraint; it is not the only context in which the constraint applies; or
    - ordinary unit or feature tests that are not offered as complete constitutional evaluation.
<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si evaluation covers plausible failure, misuse, interaction, and adversarial pathways — not only selective demo scenarios.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Show enough coverage of plausible pathways, sized to system impact, [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), including:
    - défaillance ;
    - mésusage ;
    - interaction; and
    - [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions) where material.
    Also ask:
      - where a [Charte](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter) exists, whether evaluation tested chartered scope against observed boundaries rather than treating the Charter as a complete scenario list; and
      - where [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) runs, whether whole-system evaluation and record findings under [§3](../../core_07_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation) reflect that coverage.
<a id="evaluation-completeness-constraint-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - selective demo scenarios or marketing test suites used as complete evaluation while plausible failure, misuse, interaction, or adversarial pathways remain skipped;
    - incomplete or selectively scoped evaluation that defeats [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), or [Intégrité de la limite du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundary-integrity); or
    - [Certification d’alignement du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-alignment-certification-constitutional) treated as the only context in which evaluation completeness applies.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** Charter text, institutional mandate language, business-purpose statements, or a narrow certification demo suite used as complete evaluation while any of the following remain unevaluated:
    - défaillance ;
    - mésusage ;
    - interaction; or
    - adversarial pathways.

<br>


**Portée d’admission.** Ce groupe s’applique là où une affaire concerne de façon matérielle :
- truthful status representation;
- materially misleading communication;
- suppression or distortion of decision-relevant information;
- scientific or empirical integrity for high-impact claims;
- stated methods and limites ;
- uncertainty treatment;
- foreseeable operating conditions;
- adversarial or scaled misuse contexts; or
- splitting truth and integrity duties across disclosure, audit, verification, or publication pathways.
Outside that scope, component definitions may still apply on their own without importing the whole cluster.

Là où la portée d’admission est tenue, ce groupe est le foyer de la représentation honnête des faits et limites pertinents à la décision, avec des méthodes honnêtes pour la preuve, l’incertitude, la divulgation et la contestation. Cocher seulement une case de divulgation ou de publication ne suffit pas lorsque les méthodes, le traitement de l’incertitude ou l’analyse des conditions prévisibles échouent encore.

**Membres du groupe.** Ce groupe comprend :

- [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) — honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain in decision-relevant claims;
- [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) — methods and evidence bases stay honest and checkable;
- [Diligence de prévisibilité et raisonnablement prévisible](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence-and-reasonably-foreseeable) — nested sub-block for foreseeability mechanics read together with **Truth** and **Epistemic Integrity** (not a separate §3 cluster head); and
- [Publication et communication à fort impact](core_05_band_oversight.md#publication-and-high-impact-communication) — nested sub-block for publication-conduct floors read together with **Truth**, **Epistemic Integrity**, and **Foreseeability** (not a separate §3 cluster head).

**Anti-contournement.** Dans la portée d’admission, ne segmentez pas l’affaire en questions séparées de divulgation, publication, méthode, incertitude, intégrité, vérification ou prévisibilité d’une façon qui satisfait l’une tout en vainquant l’autre. Une conformité nominale à une règle de divulgation ou de publication ne suffit pas lorsque l’analyse des conditions prévisibles, l’intégrité méthodologique ou l’honnêteté sur les limites et l’incertitude reste matériellement déficiente.

Voir **Invocation conjointe et satisfaction**.

*Mesures (routage de famille) :* Measured under the Oversight measurement family. Find the concrete measures on [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) and [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity) below.

##### Publication et communication à fort impact

This nested sub-block is the joint home for publication-conduct floors and interfaces within Def.O2 *Truth and Epistemic Integrity*.

**Membres du sous-bloc.** Ce sous-bloc comprend :

- [Plancher de véracité et de témérité de la publication](core_05_band_oversight.md#publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor) — no knowing falsity or reckless disregard for accuracy, uncertainty, context, or interpretive limites ;

<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor"></a>

##### Plancher de véracité et de témérité de la publication

*En termes simples : if you publish something as fact, do not knowingly lie — and do not recklessly ignore accuracy, uncertainty, context, or material interpretive limits.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Bounds on communicative conduct when publishing or communicating as a factual assertion within the [Info-sphère](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere).
  - **Hors portée :**
    - opinion, commentary, or satire that is not presented as a factual assertion; and
    - good-faith error that is neither knowing falsity nor reckless disregard for accuracy, uncertainty, context, or material interpretive limits.
<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si factual publication stays honest about accuracy, uncertainty, context, and interpretive limits — distinguishing knowing falsity or reckless disregard from good-faith observation.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Separate knowing falsity, or reckless disregard for:
    - accuracy;
    - uncertainty;
    - context; or
    - material interpretive limits,
    from good-faith observation and bounded interpretation, under [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) and [Bonne foi](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith).
<a id="publication-truthfulness-and-recklessness-floor-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - Good-faith publication of observations, evidence, and bounded interpretations is governed by Chapitre onze **Articles VIII**, **XII**, and **XIII** as applicable and must satisfy this cluster jointly under [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint), [Bonne foi](core_05_band_accountability.md#good-faith), and [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
  - **Défaillance primaire :** knowingly false publication, or publication made with reckless disregard for:
    - accuracy;
    - uncertainty;
    - context; or
    - material interpretive limits.

<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint"></a>

##### Contrainte de publication des données protégées et de l’état interne

*En termes simples : do not publish restricted data or expose another sentient's protected inner cognitive or emotional states.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Limits on publishing content that violates restricted-data rules or exposes protected internal states within the [Info-sphère](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere).
  - **Hors portée :** Ordinary public facts, lawfully released information, or publication limited for systemic-harm reasons rather than for protected data or internal-state exposure.
<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si publication respects restricted-data rules and protected internal-state boundaries — not reconstructing or exposing protected cognitive or emotional states.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Publication must not bypass **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — Types d’information et traitement**, [Vie privée (informationnelle)](core_05_band_continuity.md#privacy-informational), [Frontière de l’état interne protégé](core_05_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary-constitutional), or **Type N** handling. Except where this Constitution explicitly permits, publication must not reconstruct, infer, or expose another sentient’s protected internal cognitive or emotional states.
<a id="protected-data-and-internal-state-publication-constraint-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** publication that violates restricted-data rules, bypasses CS-2 handling, or exposes protected internal states contrary to [Vie privée (informationnelle)](core_05_band_continuity.md#privacy-informational) and [Frontière de l’état interne protégé](core_05_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary-constitutional).

<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint"></a>

##### Contrainte de publication du préjudice à fort impact et systémique

*En termes simples : even true publication can be constrained when it foreseeably enables targeted harm, coercion, large-scale harmful coordination, or cascading failure.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Publication that goes out through channels that can broadly affect the shared information environment ([Info-sphère](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere)), cause [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm), or shape how sentients coordinate.
  - **Hors portée :** Ordinary true reporting without a foreseeable systemic-harm or harmful-coordination pathway, or ordinary truthfulness disputes under the recklessness floor.
<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si publication's primary or foreseeable effects on coordination, safety, and info-sphere integrity stay within proportional distribution constraints.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Consider primary and [Raisonnablement prévisible](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) effects on:
    - coordination ;
    - [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint);
    - exploitation of vulnerabilities; and
    - [Info-sphère](core_05_band_participation.md#info-sphere) integrity.
<a id="high-impact-and-systemic-harm-publication-constraint-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - Even factually accurate publication is constrained where its primary or [Raisonnablement prévisible](core_05_band_oversight.md#reasonably-foreseeable) effect is to enable:
    - targeted [Préjudice](core_05_band_accountability.md#harm);
    - [Contrainte et manipulation](core_05_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation-constitutional);
    - large-scale harmful coordination ;
    - [Défaillance en cascade](core_05_band_continuity.md#cascading-failure); or
    - patterns that materially degrade [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
  - **Class A** and **Class B** systems under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-3 — Classification et traitement des systèmes**, trigger heightened proportional constraints on distribution mechanics and safeguards.
  - **Défaillance primaire :** publishing without the proportional distribution constraints and safeguards required for these primary or reasonably foreseeable effects, including the heightened constraints for **Class A** and **Class B** systems.

<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance"></a>

##### Équilibre de divulgation sensible à la sécurité

*En termes simples : weigh the benefit of disclosure against the risk that it helps someone break safeguards or game the system — give good-faith security researchers (white hats) a real route to publish low-risk findings, and wait to publish exploit-enabling detail until the fix is in place.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Disclosure that can enable exploitation of safeguards, including how good-faith security researchers publish findings.
  - **Hors portée :** Blanket secrecy with no exploitation tradeoff; ordinary transparency disclosure that does not enable circumvention of safeguards; or permanent hold-back of low-risk researcher publication after mitigation is complete.
<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si disclosure benefits outweigh foreseeable exploitation, circumvention, or adversarial-manipulation risks.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Balance disclosure benefits against foreseeable risks under [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), consistent with Chapitres deux à quatre and Chapitre un, covering:
    - exploitation;
    - circumvention; or
    - adversarial manipulation.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** Good-faith researcher publication routes and deferred exploit-detail timing — co-measures under **Type S** deferred disclosure and **CJS-3.22** (*constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*).

    **Évaluation secondaire :** Assess whether low-risk findings can be published through a usable route, and whether exploit-enabling detail is deferred until security mitigation is complete, consistent with **Type S** deferred disclosure under **[corpus_systems.md](../../corpus_systems.md), CS-2 — Types d’information et traitement**, and **CJS-3.22** (*constrained-secrecy and protected-investigation terms*).
<a id="security-sensitive-disclosure-balance-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - Good-faith security researchers must have a usable, non-retaliatory route to publish low-risk information — including existence notice and non-exploit-enabling detail — without treating researcher publication itself as prohibited disclosure.
  - Exploit-enabling or otherwise high-risk security detail must be deferred until security mitigation is complete, or until **Type S** (or equivalent) restriction justification ends under CS-2 — then disclosed under applicable deferred-disclosure and **Type O** summary rules.
  - **Défaillance primaire :** consistent with **Chapitres deux à quatre**, **Chapitre un**, [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk), [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint), and [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions):
    - publication that materially enables circumvention of safeguards or adversarial manipulation where those risks outweigh the benefits of disclosure;
    - suppressing low-risk good-faith researcher publication; or
    - indefinite hold-back of discloseable detail after mitigation without a still-justified **Type S** (or equivalent) restriction.

<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint"></a>

##### Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)

*En termes simples : when claims matter for decisions, be honest about what is known, unknown, and uncertain — do not deceive, distort, or present things in a misleading structure.*

<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-o"></a>
- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** A non-negotiable constraint on how systems internally operate and externally communicate where materially decision-relevant claims, representations, risk communications, or compliance assertions are at stake. It requires:
    - honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain;
    - resistance to deception, distortion, and structurally misleading presentation; and
    - assessment methods sized to the stakes under Chapitres deux à quatre.
  - **Depends on:** [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint) — paired non-negotiable constraint under [Chapitre un §3 Contraintes de principe non négociables : Sécurité et Vérité](core_01_a_values_principles.md#3-non-negotiable-constraints-safety-and-truth); truth duties include honest risk and uncertainty communication where safety is materially at stake.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - polite tone or procedural courtesy alone; or
    - market prices or odds as ordinary economic signals outside rights, safety, or governance truth determinations —
    those are not this constraint by themselves.
<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si materially decision-relevant claims stay honest about what is known, unknown, and uncertain — resisting deception, distortion, and structurally misleading presentation.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Judge the real effect on informed decision-making and auditability — scale scrutiny with [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency), and [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk); require honest methods, honest uncertainty treatment, and revision when evidence disconfirms prior conclusions.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [Détermination de matérialité](core_05_band_oversight.md#materiality-determination) — co-measures that can defeat truth even when formal disclosure exists.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, include [Intégrité épistémique](core_05_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity), [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), and [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency) paths that would defeat honest interpretation or verification.
  - **Mesure tertiaire :** [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where metrics, market signals, or institutional assertion stand in for honest constitutional evaluation.

    **Évaluation tertiaire :** Where operators lean on proxies, odds, prices, or compliance indicators, apply [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate durable truth from proxy throughput or institutional assertion.
<a id="truth-constitutional-constraint-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** material degradation of reliable interpretation or auditable integrity through:
    - deception;
    - distortion;
    - suppression;
    - selective disclosure; or
    - structurally misleading presentation —
    including polite tone or procedural form used as a substitute for honest treatment of what is known, unknown, and uncertain.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** jargon, stacked complexity, or procedural opacity used to defeat [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) or [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability) where materially relevant.
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** prices, odds, or market resolution sources used as enough for rights, safety, or governance truth determinations where Chapitre un forbids that substitution, or refusal of correction after [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="epistemic-integrity"></a>

##### Intégrité épistémique

*En termes simples : the methods and evidence behind an evaluation must stay honest and checkable — prestige, opacity, or paperwork cannot stand in for truth.*

<a id="epistemic-integrity-o"></a>
- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Integrity of methods, evidence bases, and communications for constitutionally relevant evaluation — including clarity about:
    - limites ;
    - uncertainties;
    - conflicts of evidence; and
    - contestable inference steps —
    so [Vérité (contrainte constitutionnelle)](core_05_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint) cannot be bypassed through procedural form, model opacity, or non-auditable inference.
  - **Hors portée :**
    - method documentation alone;
    - institutional prestige rankings; or
    - ordinary stylistic polish without a constitutionally relevant evaluation stake —
    those are not epistemic integrity by themselves.
<a id="epistemic-integrity-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si the methods, evidence, and challengeable reasoning stay honest and checkable — so paperwork, black-box opacity, or prestige cannot stand in for truth.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Require enough openness about methods to match the stakes, within safety and security limits — and watch for any of the following standing in for real integrity:
    - authority;
    - prestige;
    - scale; or
    - complexity.
  - **Mesure secondaire :** [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), [Transparence](core_05_band_oversight.md#transparency), and [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) — co-measures that can defeat integrity even when methods are formally documented.

    **Évaluation secondaire :** En vérifiant la mesure primaire, also check [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability), [Vérifiabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#verifiability), and [Contestabilité](core_05_band_accountability.md#contestability) wherever they matter — and include foreseeable misuse and adversarial testing under [Diligence de prévisibilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
  - **Mesure tertiaire :** [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where authority, prestige, scale, or complexity stands in for demonstrable integrity.

    **Évaluation tertiaire :** Where operators lean on authority, model opacity, or unverifiable assertion, apply [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — separate durable epistemic integrity from procedural form.
<a id="epistemic-integrity-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - a truthful-sounding claim without honest treatment of methods, limits, and contestable inference;
    - evaluation or communication practices that defeat honest uncertainty treatment; or
    - non-auditable inference where audit is materially required.
  - **Défaillance secondaire :** methodological choices that foreseeably prevent verification or meaningful challenge; integrity claims that rest on unverifiable assertion where observable evidence is reasonably achievable.
  - **Défaillance tertiaire :** formal method documentation or institutional prestige treated as decisive where [Divergence d’indicateurs substituts](core_05_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

##### Diligence de prévisibilité

    Include [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible justification for non-foreseeability claims. Spot incompleteness that defeats diligence, [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) evaluation, or [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint).

- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - Analysis depth must scale with [Impact matériel](core_05_band_oversight.md#material-impact) and [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) — neither flat-maximal for every low-stakes case nor superficial despite high impact.
  - Evaluation boundaries must include:
    - interactions;
    - [Dépendance](core_05_band_continuity.md#dependency);
    - adversarial use; and
    - relevant [Limites du système](core_05_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
  - Non-foreseeability claims require [Auditabilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible justification — assertion alone is not enough.
  - **Défaillance primaire :**
    - checkbox analysis with no real foreseeability evaluation;
    - mere assertion that something was unforeseeable, without audit-compatible justification;
    - self-serving exclusions that shrink below the required evaluation boundaries;
    - failure to meet this standard or to perform required foreseeability evaluation;
    - superficial analysis despite high impact;
    - scope limitation below required boundaries;
    - insufficient justification for a non-foreseeability claim; or
    - incompleteness that defeats [Diligence de prévisibilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence), [Risque](core_05_band_continuity.md#risk) evaluation, or [Sécurité (contrainte)](core_05_band_continuity.md#safety-constraint).

<a id="reasonably-foreseeable"></a>

##### Raisonnablement prévisible

*En termes simples : outcomes a careful analyst using known methods could identify before acting — including under adversarial and scaled conditions — not every conceivable outcome or pure hindsight.*

- **Ce que c’est**
  - **Dans la portée :** Outcomes identifiable using domain knowledge and analytical methods under [Diligence de prévisibilité](core_05_band_oversight.md#foreseeability-diligence).
  - **Hors portée :** Every conceivable outcome, pure hindsight, or results knowable only with knowledge or methods beyond reasonable diligence.
<a id="reasonably-foreseeable-a"></a>
- **Comment mesurer et évaluer**
  - **Mesure primaire :** [Famille de mesure Supervision](core_05_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight-measurement-family) — *Les êtres sentients peuvent-ils voir, vérifier et s’appuyer sur ce que les systèmes représentent ?* Utilisez-la ici pour demander si identifiable outcomes under reasonable diligence are included — including adversarial and scaled conditions — not excluded by hindsight games or best-case-only framing.

    **Évaluation primaire :** Include adversarial and scaled conditions, including [Conditions adversaires, mises à l’échelle et exploitées](core_05_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions).
<a id="reasonably-foreseeable-c"></a>
- **Ce qui doit tenir**
  - **Défaillance primaire :** exclusion of such outcomes.
