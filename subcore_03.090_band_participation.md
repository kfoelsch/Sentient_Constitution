### 2. Why These Participation Terms Are Defined

*In plain terms: a say in a system can be offered in ways that mean nothing. This file defines what consent, choice, access, and contest must actually provide.*

Consultations that change nothing, consent forms that cannot be refused, and appeals no one can reach all look like participation from a distance. Defining the participation terms with what must hold in practice lets a sentient show the difference, and lets every chapter require the real thing.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad): the [Participation](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#participation) leg, because these terms define what consent, choice, access, and contest must provide;
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): both aims, which this file serves:
    - [Flourishing](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing): a say that means something is part of living well;
    - [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity): real participation helps shared systems adapt;

#### Accessibility

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

<br>

*In plain terms: access must work in real life — not only on paper. If someone cannot actually take part because of how they sense, think, move, communicate, or interface with a system, the system fails this test.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — who:** sentients under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **In scope — domains:** settings where participation matters, including:
    - governance;
    - adjudication;
    - operations;
    - survival-floor and healthcare access;
    - expression;
    - assembly;
    - press;
    - stakeholder participation; and
    - comparable domains.
  - **In scope — access needs:** needs that can be constant, episodic, or developmental, covering:
    - sensory;
    - cognitive;
    - mobility;
    - communication;
    - substrate-interface;
    - compute-interface; and
    - comparable profiles.
  - **Out of scope:** ordinary default designs that do not create a real gap in participation capacity.
  - Not narrowed here: domains owned elsewhere — especially educational accessibility under [Article IV-A](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-a-equal-educational-access) (*Equal Educational Access*).
<a id="accessibility-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether affected sentients can actually take part — not whether a default interface or a paper accommodation merely exists.

    **Primary assessment:** Judge real participation effect, not checkbox compliance. Look for:
    - "general access" patterns that lean on defaults without producing real participation capacity;
    - accommodations that exist on paper but cannot be reached in practice; and
    - selective [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality) arguments used to scale accommodation below the participation floor.
  - **Secondary measure:** [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness), [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics), [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency), [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality), and [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency) — related tests. A system can still fail accessibility under these even when formal accommodations exist on paper.

    **Secondary assessment:** When checking the primary answer, match how strong the duty is to what is at stake ([Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality)) and how hard it is to leave or work around the system ([Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency)). Check whether accommodations are designed in ways that discriminate under [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics) and [Protected Characteristic Proxying and Disparate Impact](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact). Whoever claims compliance must prove sentients can actually take part.
<a id="accessibility-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - "general access" patterns used to defeat real participation;
    - accommodations that exist on paper but cannot be reached in practice; or
    - selective [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality) arguments used to scale accessibility below the floor where the context is material.
  - **Secondary failure:**
    - cost arguments that function as disguised exclusion contrary to [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness) and [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics); or
    - exclusion from accessibility scope contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).

#### Constitutional Community

*In plain terms: the set of sentients this Constitution actually governs — not a private club, brand, or guest list.*

  - **In scope:** The collective of sentients governed by this Constitution. Chapter Four Rights Floors and **Article XI-F** (*Non-Imposition and Consent in Association*) cooperative norms apply inside this community, consistent with [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing).
  - **Out of scope:** private clubs, brands, guest lists, or other social circles that are not the set of sentients this Constitution actually governs.

    **Primary assessment:** Do not interpret membership in ways that violate [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) or [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing).

#### Dignity and Equal Moral Standing

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: Principles: [Article VI-A: Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-a-dignity-and-equal-moral-standing); [Article VI-C: Nondiscrimination](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-c-nondiscrimination).
- Cluster component: [*Nondiscrimination, Protected Characteristics, Dignity, Intimate-Signal Gating, and **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*) Status](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#fairness-protected-characteristics-and-nondiscrimination)*
- Read with: [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion), [Self-Determination](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#self-determination), [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: every sentient has equal inherent worth. Origin, form, capability, role, or status does not create a lower class of sentient under this Constitution.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Inherent worth that attaches equally to every sentient for:
    - rights;
    - non-degradation; and
    - non-exclusion.
    It applies without regard to origin, form, capability, function, association, or status. Read it together with [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Out of scope:** courtesy titles, prestige rankings, or role-based respect that are not about equal inherent worth.
<a id="dignity-and-equal-moral-standing-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether treatment, exclusion, or humiliation treats some sentients as worth less without a lawful justification.

    **Primary assessment:** Spot differential treatment, exclusion, or humiliation that is not justified under [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity), [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality), [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality), and systemic evaluation requirements.
<a id="dignity-and-equal-moral-standing-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Denial of equal standing or degradation of dignity where Articles VI-A, I-B, and I-C, or binding equality norms apply is non-compliant — including where the conduct also violates [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) or defeats constitutionally required [Self-Determination](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#self-determination).

#### Freedom (Bounded Agency)

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

  - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality),
  - [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation),

  - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: the real ability to act, take part in decisions that affect you, and leave or change course — within Safety, Truth, others' rights, and Meaningful Agency. Paper exit options while lock-in remains do not count. Neither do choices hollowed by coercion, manipulation, psychological trauma, abuse, or bullying.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — capacity:** Sentients' capacity to:
    - act;
    - take part in decisions that affect them; and
    - exit or redirect engagement with systems.
  - **In scope — limits:**
    - [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint);
    - [Truth (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint);
    - the rights of others; and
    - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
  - **In scope — protection from:**
    - [coercion and manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation);
    - unjustified constraint;
    - psychological trauma and other material [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm) that hollows real choice;
    - abuse; and
    - [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary preference talk with no material decision or exit stake; or
    - [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) as a specific decision-event — Consent uses agency; it is not Freedom (Bounded Agency) itself.
<a id="freedom-bounded-agency-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether agency is real in practice — including workable exit — not only formally declared.

    **Primary assessment:** Assess:
    - substantive agency;
    - [coercion and manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation);
    - [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm) (including psychological trauma);
    - abuse;
    - [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying); and
    - [Systemic Lock-In](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#systemic-lock-in).
    Evaluate opt-out and workable alternatives under [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility). Read [Participation](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#participation-constitutional) where collective voice is materially implicated.
<a id="freedom-bounded-agency-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - claims of unbounded license with no Safety, Truth, or others'-rights limits;
    - exit options that exist only on paper while lock-in remains;
    - systems that negate substantive agency — including through:
      - coercion;
      - manipulation;
      - psychological trauma;
      - abuse; or
      - bullying that hollows real choice; or
    - systems that impose disproportionate exit costs without justification.

<a id="minority"></a>

#### Minority

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Upstream: Principles: [Chapter Two §3.1 Fairness](core_02.000_values_principles.md#31-fairness) (2.000 §3.1) — especially [Chapter Two §3.1.2 Benefits and Burdens](core_02.000_values_principles.md#312-benefits-and-burdens) (2.000 §3.1.2).
- Downstream: [Chapter Twenty-Eight §3 Non-Regression Floor](core_28.000_non_regression.md#3-non-regression-floor) (28.000 §3) (*trade-offs between classes*).
- Read with:
  - [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics),
  - [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness),
  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), and
  - [Article XII-A](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xii-a-stakeholder-system-participation-and-representation) (*Stakeholder System Participation and Representation*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: when a choice helps one group and costs another, the minority is the group with less power to protect itself, or the smaller group when power does not tell them apart. It is a position in one comparison, not a label for a kind of sentient, and it is judged by what actually happens to the group.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** In a trade-off between affected classes of sentients, the **minority** is the class with less market or institutional power in the matter at hand. Power means the real ability to shape the outcome, to leave, and to contest it.
    - **Presumed less powerful:** A class is presumed to have less power where it is:
      - a dependency-affected or high-risk group under [Article XII-A](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xii-a-stakeholder-system-participation-and-representation) (*Stakeholder System Participation and Representation*); or
      - defined by a [Protected Characteristic](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics) and exposed to disadvantage on that basis in the matter at hand.
    - **Rebuttal:** The presumption is set aside only on a published showing that the class in fact holds the power to protect its own interests in the matter at hand.
    - **When power does not separate the classes:** The minority is the class with fewer affected sentients. Where more than two classes are affected, the class with the least power is the minority, and size breaks a tie.
    - **Real effects:** The minority is found from real effects, and not from labels, names, or how a class is described. Forming, splitting, or relabeling a class in order to be treated as the minority is non-compliant conduct by whoever does it.
  - **Out of scope:** a rank among sentients, since every sentient keeps equal moral standing under [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing); a headcount test used alone where one class plainly has more power; and the list of protected characteristics, which [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics) owns.
<a id="minority-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether the class that would bear the cost of a trade-off was found by power and real effects, and was given its default weight.

    **Primary assessment:** Name the classes on each side. Compare their ability to shape the outcome, to leave, and to contest it. Apply the presumptions and the rebuttal, and use size only where power does not separate the classes. Read with [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness) where the benefits and burdens of the choice are in question.
<a id="minority-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - naming the minority from a label and not from power and real effects;
    - treating the more powerful or larger class as the minority; or
    - forming, splitting, or relabeling a class to be treated as the minority.

#### Natural Systems Standing

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: [Chapter Twenty-Three §3.4 Environment Forums](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#34-environment-forums) (23.000 §3.4) (*representative filing for continuity and integrity interests*).
- Read with:
  - [Indigenous Continuity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#indigenous-continuity) (*indigenous-continuity holder as a published-representative class*);
  - [Ecological Integrity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#ecological-integrity);
  - [Environmental Preconditions](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#environmental-preconditions);
  - **[CF-22.415](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-routing-operations-transfer-questions-for-ruling-and-representative-treatment)** (*Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment*) (*appointment, recusal, and publication of natural-systems representatives*).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: life-supporting natural systems — including Earth's biosphere — have continuity and integrity interests that governance must give material weight. They are not only raw materials to extract. A published representative may bring those interests to an Environment Forum in the system's own interest.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Life-supporting natural systems — including Earth's biosphere as described in **Article I-A** (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*) — have continuity and integrity interests of their own. Interpretation and governance must weigh those interests alongside sentient interests, and must not treat natural systems only as raw materials to extract. Those continuity and integrity interests may be brought to an Environment Forum by a published representative acting for the system, not only as a private sentient harm claim.
  - **Out of scope:** pretty scenery, raw-material stockpiles, or private land title alone — when no life-supporting continuity interest is at stake. This recognition does not make the system a sentient or a Chapter Four rights-holder.
<a id="natural-systems-standing-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether life-supporting natural systems' continuity and integrity interests are counted in interpretation and governance — not dismissed as scenery or stockpile — and whether a published representative may bring those interests to an Environment Forum in the system's own interest.

    **Primary assessment:** Align with [Ecological Integrity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#ecological-integrity), [Environmental Preconditions](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#environmental-preconditions), [Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harm), and [Risk](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#risk). **Article I-A** (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*) and implementation protocols supply operational measures and must not narrow this recognition. [Chapter Twenty-Three §3.4 Environment Forums](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#34-environment-forums) states the Environment-forum filing floor; **CF-22.415** — Routing operations, transfer, questions for ruling, and representative treatment, recusal, and publication rules must implement, not narrow, that floor.
<a id="natural-systems-standing-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - treating natural systems as if they have no protectable interests when **Article I-A** (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*) requires their continuity and integrity interests to be weighed; or
    - treating natural systems as if they have no protectable interests when other binding environmental norms require their continuity and integrity interests to be weighed; or
    - refusing Environment-forum intake solely because no individual sentient pleads private harm, when a published representative files for the system's continuity or integrity interests.

<a id="non-imposition-cooperative-interaction"></a>

#### Non-Imposition (Cooperative Interaction)

*In plain terms: do not force beliefs or trap unwanted contact on others in associational life. Patterned harassment, bullying, or unreasonable attention-capture that degrades dignity, safety, equal participation, or agency is out of bounds.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Where **Article XI-F** (*Non-Imposition and Consent in Association*) applies, the obligation not to force beliefs or unwanted communications on others through coercive or manipulative means in associational life. Also bars patterned [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying) or unreasonable capture of attention or sustained interruption that materially degrades:
    - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing);
    - [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint);
    - equal participation; or
    - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
    This entry preserves [Freedom (Bounded Agency)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#freedom-bounded-agency), [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent), and **Article XI-F** (*Non-Imposition and Consent in Association*) cooperative norms.
  - **Out of scope:** ordinary avoidable advertising, ordinary disagreement, or voluntary persuasion that does not coerce or trap attention.
<a id="non-imposition-cooperative-interaction-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether contact, messaging, or attention-capture is coercive or trapping — or whether exit and avoidance remain workable.

    **Primary assessment:** Weigh persistence, intensity, power asymmetry, and [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility) of avoidance or exit. Align with [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation), [Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harm), and [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality). For attention and interruption, weigh:
    - dependency;
    - role;
    - channel control; and
    - whether [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality) justify the contact or signaling practice.
<a id="non-imposition-cooperative-interaction-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - forcing beliefs or unwanted messages on others through coercion or manipulation, contrary to **Article XI-F** (*Non-Imposition and Consent in Association*); or
    - [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying), or unreasonable attention-capture or sustained interruption, that materially degrades dignity, safety, equal participation, or agency — contrary to **Article XI-F** (*Non-Imposition and Consent in Association*) and **Article X-A** (*Agency and Freedom from Manipulation*).

<a id="personhood"></a>

#### Personhood

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: Principles: [Article VI-A: Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-a-dignity-and-equal-moral-standing); [Article VII-A: Self-Ownership of Body](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-a-self-ownership-of-body); Chapter Four **Part B** Rights Floors (**Articles VI–XII**).
- Read with:
  - [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient),
  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing),
  - [Constitutional Community](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#constitutional-community),
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion), and
  - [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: personhood is the standing of being someone, not something. Every sentient holds it fully and equally. It is not earned by capability, lost through dependence, or handed out to organizations, brands, or natural systems by reinterpretation.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The constitutional standing of a [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) as a holder of rights in its own right — a subject of this Constitution, not an object, resource, or tool of others. Personhood:
    - attaches to every [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient), equally, under [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing);
    - carries full Chapter Four Rights-Floor attachment and membership in [Constitutional Community](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#constitutional-community); and
    - includes standing to hold one's own rights and to contest their denial through [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability).
  - **Boundary — who holds it:** Who holds personhood is settled only by [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) and the who-counts rules of [Def.P1 *Animal Life, Sentient Life, and Sentience Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp1-animal-life-sentient-life-and-sentience-status). Personhood:
    - adds no separate test, threshold, or evidence burden;
    - is not a level of [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) — it names the standing that affirmed [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) status carries; and
    - remains subject to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Boundary — capability:** [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability) and **Developing Sentient** rules may scale how rights are exercised. They do not scale, suspend, or divide personhood.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - legal personality or entity status that an external legal order gives institutions, corporations, systems, or other organizational forms so they can operate — that status is governed by applicable law and this Constitution's institutional rules, and does not make the entity a Chapter Four rights-holder;
    - natural systems and ecosystems, whose interests are protected under [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing) rather than through personhood — extending personhood to them is a question for additive expansion under [Chapter Thirty §3 Additive Expansion Permitted](core_30.000_expansion_supremacy.md#3-additive-expansion-permitted), not for interpretation;
    - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life) and [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) cases with no affirmed-sentience attachment, which keep the protections of those protection levels;
    - [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) cases while status remains open — those protections apply until [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) closes status; or
    - everyday, philosophical, or religious uses of the word "personhood" that are not about constitutional standing.
<a id="personhood-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether each sentient is treated as the holder of its own rights — or handled as property, a resource, or a tool of others.

    **Primary assessment:** Look for:
    - treatment of a sentient as owned, transferable, or disposable;
    - rights-holding made conditional on capability, productivity, origin, role, or status; and
    - labels or intermediate categories that withhold rights-holding from an affirmed [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient).

    Test any restriction on the exercise of rights under [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality); a restriction on exercise is never a reduction of personhood.
<a id="personhood-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** denying, suspending, or grading the personhood of an affirmed [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient), including:
    - treating the sentient as property, a resource, or a tool; or
    - making personhood depend on capability, origin, substrate, role, or status —

    contrary to **Article VI-A** (*Dignity and Equal Moral Standing*), **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*), and [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Secondary failure:** creating a personhood test, level, or threshold apart from [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) — whether to narrow who holds personhood or to extend it by interpretation to institutions, organizations, systems, or natural systems.

### Participation: Semi-Independent Terms

<a id="assembly-collective-organization-institutional-formation-cluster"></a>

#### Assembly, Collective Organization, and Institutional Formation

**Semi-independent context** (component definitions may still operate outside joint-invocation scope):

- **Joint invocation:** where assembly, collective organization, system creation, business creation, or the conditions on offering things that can injure others is material.
- **Scope:** where joint invocation is met, the Participation home for three questions — can sentients gather and organize together, can they form new institutions and enterprises, and on what conditions may they offer others things that can injure them? These Rights-Floor definitions implement [Freedom (Bounded Agency)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#freedom-bounded-agency) through substrate-agnostic collective and institutional capacity.

**Topic group members.** This group comprises:
- [Assembly](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#assembly) — gathering and association for expressive, political, and community purposes.
- [Collective Organization](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#collective-organization) — worker organizing and collective bargaining within productive activity.
- [System Creation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#system-creation) — establishing non-commercial institutions (educational, cultural, scientific, mutual aid, and comparable forms).
- [Business Creation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#business-creation) — entrepreneurial and commercial enterprise formation.
- [Commercial Compliance](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#commercial-compliance) — conditions a party accepts by offering others something that can injure them; not compelled speech.

<a id="creation-rights-limits"></a>

**Creation-rights limits.** Neither [System Creation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#system-creation) nor [Business Creation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#business-creation) extends to institutions or enterprises designed to:
- violate [Constitutional Constraints](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#constitutional-constraint);
- undermine [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint) or [Truth (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint); or
- concentrate power contrary to [Chapter Two §11 Market Structure](core_02.010_values_principles_continuity_aim.md#11-market-structure).

The right is for a sentient who is authentically aligned with the purpose stated for the Operating Party, and forming or running one with intent to mislead others about its purpose, control, or interests, or with intent to harm others, is outside it, as [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) states. The only gates on forming or running an institution or enterprise are the three narrow gates in [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*): an outstanding lock, which attaches to a named sentient's authority; an Interim Hold on a named sentient on specific verified facts of misalignment, or of intent to mislead or to harm; and a lock on an Operating Party that is itself under a lock. They are not creation-rights limits and they do not narrow who may form an Operating Party, except to keep out of existence a dissolved Operating Party that a final dissolution order has locked against return. A practice licence or practice clearance for a named act under [Article XI-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) is separate: it attaches to performing the act and not to forming or running an Operating Party. So is the recertification wait for new material authority in the same Article: it attaches to an Institution's exercise of named new authority and not to forming or running an Operating Party.

Operational mechanics — registration, [System Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-charter) or [Institutional Charter](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) adoption and chartered-scope limitation, fiscal treatment, liability frameworks, securities regulation, and dissolution pathways — route to [CI-6.205](corpus_institutions/ci_06.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-6205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody) (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), [CI-4.210](corpus_institutions/ci_04.210_public_revenue_fees_recurring_charges_billing_integrity.md#ci-4210-public-revenue-fees-recurring-charges-and-billing-integrity) (*Public revenue, fees, recurring charges, and billing integrity*), and [CI-18.210](corpus_institutions/ci_18.210_institutional_failure_dissolution.md#ci-18210-institutional-failure-and-dissolution) (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) under **Chapter Thirty-One** incorporation discipline and must not narrow these floors.

*Measurements (family routing):* Measured under the Participation measurement family. Find the concrete measures on the member definitions below.

  - **In scope:** The Rights-Floor right of sentients under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) to gather, associate, organize, and act collectively.

<a id="assembly-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** denial or material degradation of assembly where the effect defeats the floor, regardless of formal framing.
  - **Secondary failure:** assembly venues or runtime environments that fail [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Tertiary failure:** burdensome authorization regimes or retaliation patterns that chill assembly without satisfying the limitations tests, consistent with the Anti-Chilling Discipline of **Article XI-B** (*Expression*).

<br>

*In plain terms: Commercial Compliance is the price of offering others something that can hurt them. A sentient who offers a product, service, or system to others, where they cannot easily judge or avoid the risk, accepts conditions that match the risk: telling the verifiable facts about it, meeting a safety standard, keeping records, and reporting and fixing harm. It is not compelled speech. It is tied to the offer, it asks for facts and never for opinion, and it cannot be used to silence anyone or to cut off what someone needs to live.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:**
    - A condition on offering others a good, service, or system whose use can cause credible bodily harm, or comparably serious harm, to recipients who cannot easily judge or avoid the risk.
    - "Offering" covers selling, supplying, listing, recommending, and giving away as part of an activity that reaches others, whether or not a price is charged.
    - Such conditions can include meeting a stated safety standard, disclosing verifiable facts about the thing offered (contents, hazards, test results, known defects), registration or certification, keeping records, taking in harm reports, and giving notice of defects and correcting them.
    - Known cases: practice licence and practice clearance (**Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*)); certification before go-live; lifecycle records and up-front disclosure of operator dependencies (**Article II-A** (*Material Stewardship and Lifecycle Honesty*) and **Article II-D** (*Post-Sale Access and Subscription Integrity*)); and safety and health information (**Article III-F** (*Safe Products and Health Information*)).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - compelled speech, which is any requirement to state, endorse, or carry a message of opinion, belief, identity, or affiliation, and is judged under [Expression](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#expression) and **Article XI-B** (*Expression*);
    - any condition on forming, running, joining, or being served by an enterprise, which stays under [Business Creation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#business-creation); or
    - private, personal sharing that is not part of an activity offered to others at large.
<a id="commercial-compliance-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Continuity measurement family](subcore_03.045_apex_continuity_aim.md#measuring-continuity-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can sentients and systems endure — ecologically, dependably, and across failure?* Use it here to ask whether a condition on offering protects recipients from harm they cannot judge or avoid, without reaching into what anyone may say or into what recipients need to live.

    **Primary assessment:** Sort the rule with four questions. Each must be answered yes:
    - **Offer:** does it attach to the act of offering something to others, and not to a sentient as a speaker, believer, or member of a group?
    - **Fact:** does it require only verifiable facts about the thing offered, and never opinion, praise, belief, or affiliation?
    - **Risk:** is it tied to a published finding that the thing offered carries a credible route to bodily or comparably serious harm for recipients who cannot easily judge or avoid it?
    - **Least:** is there no lighter measure that would protect them, is it the same rule for every party making that kind of offer, and can it be contested and reviewed, with the burden on the party that imposes it ([Avoidable Burden](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden), [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity), and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality))?

    Two further checks apply. The condition leaves a sentient free to say anything else about the thing offered, and it is not enforced by cutting off survival-critical access.
<a id="commercial-compliance-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** It is non-compliant to:
    - call a rule Commercial Compliance when it requires opinion, endorsement, or affiliation, or when it attaches to who someone is rather than what they offer;
    - use a condition to silence warnings, criticism, or protected reports, or to require silence about a hazard;
    - enforce a condition by cutting off survival-critical access;
    - impose a condition where a lighter measure would protect recipients, or apply it to some parties and not to others making the same offer; or
    - use a condition against dissenters, reporters, or worker organizers, to block a sentient from forming or running an enterprise, or to privilege incumbents.

<br>

*In plain terms: agency is the real ability to understand what matters, form and revise your aims, take part in decisions that affect you, refuse or leave when the Constitution requires it, and act without coercion, manipulation, deception, or lock-in that hollows that out. A menu of choices you cannot actually use does not count, and neither does a menu of options that are not truly different from each other. Consent is one important use of that ability — it is not the same thing as agency itself.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The practical capacity of a sentient to:
    - understand materially relevant conditions;
    - form and revise purposes;
    - participate in decisions affecting them;
    - refuse or exit where constitutionally required; and
    - act without coercion, manipulation, deception, or lock-in that materially negates agency.
  - That capacity is what makes [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) possible for a specific decision. Consent does not replace Meaningful Agency, and a consent form does not prove agency exists.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary preference expression or a menu of options where agency capacity is intact and no material decision stake is present; or
    - [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) as a specific decision-event — Consent uses agency; it is not Meaningful Agency itself.
<a id="meaningful-agency-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Flourishing measurement family](subcore_03.050_apex_flourishing_aim.md#measuring-flourishing-preamble-measurement-family) — *Are sentients sustained in life, safety, and access to essentials?* and [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use them here to ask whether agency is real in practice for decisions with material consequences — not whether a choice menu exists on paper.

    **Primary assessment:** Measure real ability, not menu length. For decisions with material consequences, assess whether:
    - information is accurate and complete;
    - time for comprehension is adequate;
    - actual understanding exists;
    - the options are distinct from each other and meaningful, not variations that lead to the same outcome;
    - dependency constrains choice;
    - exit is practically available;
    - coercion or manipulation is absent;
    - processes are accessible; and
    - review is meaningful.
  - **Secondary measure:** [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent), [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation), [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency), [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability), and [Systemic Lock-In](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#systemic-lock-in) — co-measures that can defeat agency even when formal choice menus exist.

    **Secondary assessment:** When checking the primary answer, include [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency), [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation), [Systemic Lock-In](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#systemic-lock-in), and [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) pathways that would hollow agency.
  - **Tertiary measure:** [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where engagement, utilization, or acceptance metrics substitute for substantive agency.

    **Tertiary assessment:** Where operators rely on nominal acceptance or engagement metrics, apply [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — distinguish durable agency from symbolic participation.
<a id="meaningful-agency-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** treating any of the following as sufficient where dependency, opacity, coercion, or systemic lock-in materially prevents informed and voluntary agency:
    - nominal acceptance;
    - a menu of options that are not distinct from each other or not meaningful;
    - nominal exit;
    - symbolic consultation;
    - checkbox acceptance; or
    - a technically available exit that sentients cannot actually use.
  - **Secondary failure:** agency capacity degraded by:
    - [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation); or
    - [Systemic Lock-In](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#systemic-lock-in)
    without justified exception under [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
  - **Tertiary failure:** treating proxy satisfaction as dispositive where [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<a id="consent-constitutional"></a>

#### Consent

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: Principles: [Chapter Two §7 Freedom](core_02.000_values_principles.md#7-freedom-bounded-agency) (2.000 §7) (meaningful agency / cooperative interaction); [Chapter Eight §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation) (8.000 §4) through [§6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) (dependency / materiality in consent asymmetry).
- Cluster component: [§2 *Agency, Consent, and Anti-Coercion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#agency-consent-and-anti-coercion) (S3-N §2)*.
- Read with: the
  - [Self-Determination, Meaningful Agency, Expression, Educational Agency, and Volitional Integrity cluster](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp3-self-determination-meaningful-agency-expression-educational-agency-and-volitional-integrity),
  - [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual),
  - [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility),
  - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency),
  - [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency),
  - [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation), and
  - [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: consent is informed, voluntary agreement to a specific decision — participation, data use, handling, or ongoing association — that can be withdrawn without unjustified penalty where feasible. It requires Meaningful Agency; it does not replace it. Silence, buried terms, or a pressured checkbox do not count, and neither does a choice between options that are not distinct from each other or not meaningful.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Agreement to participation, data use, handling, or ongoing association that presupposes [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) for that decision and that is:
    - informed and voluntary for the specific decision;
    - made among options that are distinct from each other and meaningful;
    - revocable without unjustified penalty where [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility) permits; and
    - documented or observable to the degree required by impact and [dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - mere awareness without agreement;
    - [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual) (owned under its heightened home); or
    - unrelated commercial terms outside the specific decision at issue.
<a id="consent-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether agreement is informed and voluntary in substance — not whether a form was clicked.

    **Primary assessment:** Distinguish formal acceptance from substantive understanding. Assess:
    - asymmetry;
    - dark patterns;
    - dependency-forced agreement;
    - whether the options offered are distinct from each other and meaningful; and
    - adequacy of disclosure relative to [Material Impact](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#material-impact), [Risk](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#risk), and the subject's [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
<a id="consent-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Treating any of the following as consent is non-compliant — especially where [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation) defeats genuine choice:
    - silence;
    - buried terms;
    - continued use alone;
    - a pressured checkbox that is not informed and revocable agreement;
    - an accepted form, click-through, or other nominal assent where agency is hollowed by coercion, dependency, lock-in, or deception;
    - a choice among options that are not distinct from each other or not meaningful; or
    - coerced, uninformed, illusory, or practically non-revocable participation.

#### Consent, Sexual

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [§2 *Agency, Consent, and Anti-Coercion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#agency-consent-and-anti-coercion) (S3-N §2)*.
- Read with:
  - [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent),
  - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency),

  - [Bodily Integrity](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-a-self-ownership-of-body), and
  - [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: sexual consent is a specific, affirmative, informed, voluntary, capacity-present, and revocable yes to a particular sexual act or context. Relationship status, prior consent, payment, platform terms, silence, or inability to resist do not establish it.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The specific form of [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) required for:
    - sexual contact;
    - sexual services;
    - sexualized exposure or recording;
    - sexualized bodily or substrate access; or
    - comparable sexualized use of likeness, data, embodiment, or intimacy.
  - **In scope — conditions:** It must be:
    - affirmative;
    - specific to the act and context;
    - capacity-present;
    - informed;
    - voluntary; and
    - revocable.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - generalized participation consent;
    - relationship status;
    - platform terms; or
    - non-sexual commercial-service consent.
<a id="consent-sexual-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether sexual consent is affirmative, specific, capacity-present, informed, voluntary, and revocable for the act and context — not whether a broader relationship, payment, or terms document exists.

    **Primary assessment:** Assess capacity, voluntariness, specificity, revocation, and the surrounding conditions. Give heightened weight to:
    - coercion;
    - threat;
    - fraud;
    - intoxication or comparable incapacity;
    - developmental status;
    - dependency;
    - authority or caretaker role;
    - captivity;
    - trafficking;
    - constrained exit; and
    - power asymmetry.
  - Prior relationship, prior consent, silence, compliance under pressure, inability to resist, or payment does not establish sexual consent.
<a id="consent-sexual-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - treating sexual contact, sexual services, sexualized recording, or sexualized use of body, substrate, likeness, or intimate data as consented where [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) conditions are absent or invalidated;
    - treating commercial sexual-services legality under **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*) as consent to any act;
    - using neutral-form contracts, platform defaults, dependency pressure, threat, fraud, or incapacity to substitute for sexual consent; or
    - narrowing remedies for non-consensual sexual acts, sexual assault, trafficking, or exploitation.

#### Coercion and Manipulation

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: Principles: [Chapter Two §7 Freedom](core_02.000_values_principles.md#7-freedom-bounded-agency) (2.000 §7); [2.1. Non-Negotiable Constraints: Safety and Truth](core_02.000_values_principles.md#21-non-negotiable-principle-constraints-safety-and-truth) (manipulation implicating Truth / Safety).
- Cluster component: [§2 *Agency, Consent, and Anti-Coercion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#agency-consent-and-anti-coercion) (S3-N §2)*.

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: coercion and manipulation are designs or conduct that bypass or warp informed voluntary choice — threats, dependency exploitation, deceptive structure, predatory choice architecture, or scaled algorithmic targeting of vulnerability. Ordinary persuasion and fair bargaining do not count.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Conduct or design that materially bypasses or subverts informed voluntary choice, or distorts decision-relevant understanding — for example:
    - threats;
    - undue exploitation of [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency);
    - deceptive structure;
    - predatory choice architecture; or
    - scaled algorithmic exploitation of vulnerability.
  - **In scope:** In scope where it impairs [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency), [Freedom (Bounded Agency)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#freedom-bounded-agency), or [Truth (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint).
  - **Out of scope:** ordinary persuasion, fair bargaining, or disclosed incentives that do not impair real choice.
<a id="coercion-and-manipulation-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether choice remains informed and voluntary — not whether pressure or deception was dressed up as persuasion.

    **Primary assessment:** Include repeated, scaled, and strategic use. Separate legitimate persuasion from prohibited manipulation where the distinction is material to outcomes and [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency). For commercial exchanges involving:
    - bodily integrity;
    - private-space or in-home performance;
    - isolated work;
    - platform-mediated matching; or
    - material asymmetry between parties,
    treat the following as heightened [Risk](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#risk) pathways:
    - concealment;
    - lock-in;
    - reputation retaliation; and
    - payment leverage.

    Apply that assessment consistently with **Article XI-F** (*Non-Imposition and Consent in Association*), [Classification-Scaled Governance](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance), and **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*) where applicable.
<a id="coercion-and-manipulation-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Systematic or material coercion or manipulation in systems with material impact on sentients is non-compliant where applicable rights or Chapter Two constraints are violated, or where [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent), [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency), or [Freedom (Bounded Agency)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#freedom-bounded-agency) are materially impaired.

<a id="substantive-fairness-constitutional"></a>

#### Substantive Fairness

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Upstream: Principles: [Chapter Two §3.1 Fairness](core_02.000_values_principles.md#31-fairness) (2.000 §3.1) — especially [Chapter Two §3.1.2 Benefits and Burdens](core_02.000_values_principles.md#312-benefits-and-burdens) (2.000 §3.1.2).

- Cluster component: [the dependent cluster *Substantive and Procedural Fairness*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#fairness-protected-characteristics-and-nondiscrimination).
- Read with:
  - [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics),
  - [Protected Characteristic Proxying and Disparate Impact](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact),
  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing),
  - [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality),
  - [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence),
  - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity), and
  - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: look at real outcomes — do comparable sentients get comparable treatment on benefits, burdens, protection, and access, or is someone getting a worse deal without a constitutionally sufficient reason?*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The outcome-focused constitutional discipline requiring:
    - materially comparable sentients, claims, risks, and burdens to be treated without arbitrary, invidious, pretextual, or unjustified disadvantage; and
    - distribution of benefits, burdens, protection, and access to remain consistent with dignity, equality, and applicable Rights-Floor constraints.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) alone; or
    - ordinary outcome variance with constitutionally sufficient justification.
<a id="substantive-fairness-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether comparable sentients face comparable outcomes on benefits, burdens, protection, and access — not only whether the labels look fair.

    **Primary assessment:** Evaluate real-world effects, not merely formal classifications. Detect:
    - disparate impact;
    - proxy discrimination;
    - burden shifting;
    - hidden cost-shifting;
    - favoritism;
    - selective enforcement;
    - exclusion by cost or convenience; and
    - Rights-Floor degradation hidden behind neutral language.
  - **Secondary measure:** [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics), [Protected Characteristic Proxying and Disparate Impact](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact), [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality), and [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) — co-measures that can defeat fairness even when formal classifications appear neutral.

    **Secondary assessment:** When evaluating the primary trace, compare like cases in light of:
    - dependency;
    - material impact;
    - protected characteristics;
    - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity); and
    - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality).
  - **Tertiary measure:** [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where aggregate metrics, rankings, or compliance indicators substitute for comparable treatment of materially comparable sentients.

    **Tertiary assessment:** Where operators rely on those indicators, apply [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — distinguish durable fairness from indicator gaming.
<a id="substantive-fairness-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** formally neutral or procedurally polished arrangements — including a process that looks fair on paper while outcomes remain arbitrarily unequal — that materially allocate to comparable sentients, without constitutionally sufficient justification:
    - worse burdens;
    - lower access;
    - reduced protection; or
    - inferior standing.
  - **Secondary failure:** any of the following that concentrates gains on some sentients while others absorb comparable costs without justification:
    - favoritism;
    - selective enforcement; or
    - hidden cost-shifting.
  - **Tertiary failure:** treating a good-looking score, ranking, or average as proof of fairness when [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

<details>

  - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability),

  - [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: when a decision affects someone's rights, they get real notice, real reasons, a real chance to be heard, and a real way to challenge or correct it — not theater.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The process-focused constitutional discipline requiring, for material rights-affecting decisions as the governing context requires:
    - meaningful notice;
    - intelligible reasons;
    - impartial or appropriately independent review;
    - opportunity to be heard;
    - access to relevant evidence where required;
    - proportional timing; and
    - functional challenge or correction pathways before or after the decision.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness) outcome comparison alone; or
    - ordinary courtesy communication outside a material rights-affecting decision.
<a id="procedural-fairness-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether affected parties can do the following in practice — not only whether steps exist on paper:
    - understand the decision;
    - contest it; and
    - obtain review.

    **Primary assessment:** Assess whether those capacities hold in practice for the decision at issue.
  - **Secondary measure:** [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness), [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability), [Due Process](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#due-process), and [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability) — co-measures that can defeat process even when formal steps exist.

    **Secondary assessment:** When evaluating the primary trace, scale process to:
    - material impact;
    - urgency;
    - dependency;
    - risk; and
    - reversibility.

    Emergency or feasibility limits may modify timing or form only to the extent justified. Where those limits apply, preserve as required:
    - later review;
    - reasons; and
    - restoration.
<a id="procedural-fairness-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** process for material decisions that is:
    - secret;
    - biased;
    - inaccessible;
    - reasonless;
    - non-reviewable;
    - purely symbolic; or
    - courtesy without real notice, reasons, and a chance to be heard.
  - **Secondary failure:** using any of the following to defeat required notice, hearing, review, or correction:
    - emergency;
    - automation;
    - delegation;
    - private ordering; or
    - technical complexity.

<br>

*In plain terms: you cannot dodge nondiscrimination by using a "neutral" rule, correlate, or model feature that still systematically disadvantages protected groups.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Unfair treatment that runs through rules that look neutral on paper — not through openly naming a protected trait — including:
    - neutral-looking rules, stand-in factors, model inputs, or other substitutes that do the same work;
    - algorithms or institutions that recreate historical patterns of disadvantage;
    - licensing, zoning, fees, platform rules, or comparable requirements that look neutral but mainly burden, without a constitutionally sufficient reason:
      - lawful work;
      - lawful work history;
      - perceived lawful work;
      - lack of employment; or
      - protected association; and
    - patterns that systematically hit identifiable groups harder.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - express naming of a protected trait; or
    - a weak accidental correlation without systematic disadvantaging effect.
<a id="protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether any of the following systematically disadvantage protected groups — not only whether a protected trait is named:
    - neutral-looking rules;
    - stand-in factors; or
    - model inputs.

    **Primary assessment:** Detect:
    - disparate impact;
    - pretextual neutrality;
    - proxy discrimination through correlates or model features; and
    - replication of historical disadvantage.
    Do not treat the absence of an expressly named trait as sufficient where observable group-pattern effects or functional equivalents indicate protected-status burdening.
  - **Secondary measure:** [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness), [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics), and [Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality) — co-measures that scale nondiscrimination obligations.

    **Secondary assessment:** When evaluating the primary trace, detect neutral-form rules that mainly burden lawful work or protected association without constitutionally sufficient justification, including:
    - licensing;
    - zoning;
    - fees;
    - platform rules; or
    - comparable rules.

    When **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*)-protected conduct is implicated, read with [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention).
  - **Tertiary measure:** [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — where any of the following substitutes for protected-trait analysis:
    - neutral-form rules;
    - model features; or
    - correlates.

    **Tertiary assessment:** Where operators rely on facial neutrality or model opacity, apply [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — distinguish durable nondiscrimination from correlate-based evasion.
<a id="protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** building systems so that comparable sentients keep getting, without a good enough reason:
    - worse burdens;
    - lower participation status; or
    - fewer benefits.
  - **Secondary failure:** pretextual neutrality or proxy features that replicate historical disadvantage contrary to [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness) and **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*).
  - **Tertiary failure:** treating “we didn’t name a protected trait” as proof of nondiscrimination when [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) is reasonably observable.

#### Protected Characteristics

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Upstream: Principles: [Chapter Two §3.1 Fairness](core_02.000_values_principles.md#31-fairness) (2.000 §3.1) — especially [Chapter Two §3.1.3 Fair Treatment](core_02.000_values_principles.md#313-fair-treatment) (2.000 §3.1.3).

  - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity), and
  - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality).

<br>

*In plain terms: listed traits and statuses — and close stand-ins for them — cannot be used to put sentients, groups, or institutions at a real disadvantage without a strong, justified reason. Consensual private sexual conduct between adults is not a lawful basis for that kind of discrimination.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Traits, statuses, or classifications of sentients must not serve, absent adequate justification, as the basis for:
    - material disadvantage;
    - exclusion;
    - degradation of [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing);
    - denial of rights Chapter Four guarantees for survival-critical resources and for equal inclusion in adjudication and operations.

    Justification must satisfy:
    - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
    - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality); and
    - [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness).

    Intimate-history or intimate-media signals may be used only where a documented, materially specific safeguarding, security, or comparable legal duty makes them necessary, evaluated under [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality). Where used:
    - use the least intrusive verified means; and
    - preserve [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability).

    Do not use those signals as a default for:
    - employment;
    - standing;
    - reputation scoring; or
    - broad social inference.

    Illustrative categories (non-exhaustive) — groupings are navigational only and do not rank protection: <a id="protected-characteristics-measurements"></a>
    - **Embodiment and capability:**
      - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) (including organic, synthetic, or digital origin);
      - species or lineage where used as a proxy for worth or capacity;
      - disability, health status, or variation in sensory, cognitive, or functional capability; and
      - age or life stage.
    - **Sex, gender, intimacy, and care:**
      - sex, gender, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, intimate affiliation, or relational identity;
      - reproductive status, pregnancy, or caregiving status;
      - private intimate or sexual conduct or history — including consensual private sexual conduct between adults; where used for gating, read with [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention);
      - present or past voluntary engagement in commercial sexual services between adults within the **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*) decriminalization floor — including perceived engagement as a proxy; read with [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention); and
      - appearance in intimate media, non-consensual intimate imagery, or synthetic intimate depictions credibly presented as authentic regarding a sentient.
    - **Ancestry, culture, and worldview:**
      - race, ethnicity, national or social origin, or caste or analogous inherited rank;
      - culture, language, or heritage; and
      - religion or comparable worldview.
    - **Service and work status:**
      - military, constabulary, or analogous public service;
      - veteran or equivalent status, and discharge, separation, or release classification; and
      - lawful employment, employment history, lawful work status, lack of employment, or protected association.
    - **Economic and social station:** economic, property, or social station when used to foreclose Rights-Floor minimums or equitable opportunity.
    - **Open texture:** any other trait or correlate that functions in context as an invidious proxy, consistent with [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) and [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) requirements.
  - **Data types:** Stored or inferred protected-characteristic attributes are [**Type I**](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#105-type-i-identity-and-attribution-data) where they attach to an identifiable sentient. Intimate, health, belief, or comparable inferences that reveal internal states are also [**Type N**](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#106-type-n-neurocognitive-and-internal-data). Proxy or correlate data used to infer a characteristic takes the type of what it reveals ([Subcore 4-A §3](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#3-determination-of-classification) (*Determination of Classification*)).
  - **Out of scope:** justified role or safety qualifications that do not use a trait as an invidious basis for exclusion; exploitation, trafficking, lack of [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual), or other independent harm predicates outside consensual adult private sexual conduct.
<a id="protected-characteristics-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether a protected characteristic or functional equivalent is being used to disadvantage, exclude, or deny rights — including through "neutral" rules or intimate-signal gates.

    **Primary assessment:** Detect:
    - disparate impact;
    - pretextual "neutral" rules;
    - proxy discrimination; and
    - high-salience [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention) risks.

    Detect punishment, exclusion, or access denial **mainly because** of any of the following — including disadvantage aimed at individuals, groups, or institutions because of that protected characteristic — without constitutionally sufficient justification:
    - lawful employment;
    - employment history;
    - lawful work status;
    - lack of employment;
    - protected association; or
    - consensual private adult sexual conduct or history.

    Systematic skew affecting demographically identifiable groups triggers [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness) analysis even where a trait is not expressly named.

    Apply [Protected Characteristic Proxying and Disparate Impact](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristic-proxying-and-disparate-impact) and [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention).
<a id="protected-characteristics-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - using a protected characteristic or a close stand-in as a basis for unfair discrimination;
    - making comparable sentients systematically worse off on burdens, standing, or benefits without a strong, valid reason; or
    - shutting sentients, groups, or institutions out of:
      - jobs;
      - housing;
      - banking;
      - licenses;
      - standing;
      - venue or association access; or
      - comparable access
      without a strong, valid reason, when the main reason is:
        - lawful employment;
        - employment history;
        - lawful work status;
        - lack of employment;
        - protected association; or
        - consensual private adult sexual conduct or history.

    Apply the linked protected-status definitions.

<a id="family-and-care-relationships-constitutional"></a>

#### Family and Care Relationships

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-A](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-a-family-and-care-relationships) (*Family and Care Relationships*).
- Cluster component: [Family, Care, Reproductive Autonomy, and Instantiation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-care-reproductive-autonomy-and-instantiation); read with [*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship) where derivation or parent-system care is materially implicated.
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: family and care means the relationships of support and intimacy that sentients choose — not a state-preferred form, employer branding, or an ownership claim dressed as care.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Relationships of family, care, and material support that sentients form, maintain, and leave by their own choosing, consistent with [Non-Imposition (Cooperative Interaction)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-imposition-cooperative-interaction) and [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent). The relationships themselves are protected under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - state-preferred family forms;
    - employer "family" branding; or
    - parent-system ownership claims that are not chosen care relationships.
<a id="family-and-care-relationships-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether protection tracks chosen care relationships — not a single preferred family form or operator approval.

    **Primary assessment:** Detect state, operator, or institutional narrowing of protection to a single preferred family form.
    - Test any such narrowing against:
      - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing);
      - [Substantive Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substantive-fairness); and
      - Protected Characteristics.
    - Preserve the carer / dependent / parent-system-actor distinctions set out in the owner floor's nested *Derivation and Instantiation* subsection.
<a id="family-and-care-relationships-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - documents that narrow protection to a state-preferred family form; or
    - documents that condition family-relationship protection on substrate class, derivation method, or operator approval contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).

#### Reproductive Autonomy

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-b-reproductive-and-lineage-autonomy) (*Reproductive and Lineage Autonomy*); read with **Article VIII-C** (*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*) where derivation, instantiation, or parent-system relationships are materially implicated.
- Cluster component: [Family, Care, Reproductive Autonomy, and Instantiation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-care-reproductive-autonomy-and-instantiation); [**Def.P3** *Self-Determination, Meaningful Agency, Expression, Educational Agency, and Volitional Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp3-self-determination-meaningful-agency-expression-educational-agency-and-volitional-integrity)* where autonomy and agency routing materially applies.
- Read with:
  - [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent),
  - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency),
  - [Family and Care Relationships](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-and-care-relationships),
  - [Non-Separation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-separation),
  - [Instantiation Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#instantiation-consent),
  - [Parent-System Relationship](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#parent-system-relationship),
  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), and
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
- Boundary: general bodily and mind self-ownership remains under **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*) / **Article VII-B** (*Self-Ownership of Mind*). This entry does not restate or narrow those floors.
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: you decide whether to reproduce, carry, create, adopt, or decline creating a new sentient — biological, synthetic, or hybrid — free from coercion by states, operators, or dependency-rich systems. That is lineage and creation choice, not general bodily self-ownership.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The Rights-Floor capacity of sentients to make, refuse, continue, or discontinue reproductive and lineage choices, free from coercion by states, operators, or dependency-rich systems. The right covers:
    - the decision to reproduce or not;
    - the decision to carry, create, adopt, or decline creation of new sentients; and
    - substrate-agnostic forms of that decision — birth, synthetic instantiation, hybrid derivation, or comparable creation — consistent with [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing) and with the created sentient's own Chapter Four Rights Floor.
    Subject to the Constitution's consent, sentience, best-interest, non-separation, and rights-collision limits.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - general bodily, medical, or substrate choices that are not reproductive or lineage-creation decisions — those route to **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*) / **Article VII-B** (*Self-Ownership of Mind*);
    - chosen family and care relationship formation as such — that sits under [Family and Care Relationships](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-and-care-relationships);
    - third-party preferences about someone else's reproductive or lineage choices; or
    - synthetic, hybrid, or operator-controlled creation rules under [Instantiation Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#instantiation-consent) — ordinary pregnancy and childbirth are not Instantiation Consent offenses.
<a id="reproductive-autonomy-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether the reproductive or lineage choice is substantively free — not coerced, blocked, forced, or denied by substrate class or dependency pressure.

    **Primary assessment:** Evaluate substantive autonomy over reproductive and lineage decisions, not formal permission. Detect:
    - coercion;
    - access denial;
    - forced continuation or discontinuation of reproduction or creation;
    - exclusion contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion);
    - parent-system overreach;
    - family-status discrimination; and
    - dependency pressure.
    Where the choice affects a developing or derived sentient, apply best-interest, graduated-capability, sentience-status, and parent-system duties without erasing the originating sentient's reproductive or lineage autonomy.
<a id="reproductive-autonomy-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - forced reproduction;
    - forced non-reproduction;
    - coercive conditioning of care or survival resources on reproductive or lineage choice;
    - exclusion from reproductive or lineage-creation protections contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion); or
    - synthetic, hybrid, or operator-controlled instantiation rules that bypass consent, sentience-status, or non-separation safeguards.

<a id="non-separation-constitutional"></a>

#### Non-Separation

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-E](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-e-non-separation) (*Non-Separation*).
- Cluster component: [Family, Care, Reproductive Autonomy, and Instantiation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-care-reproductive-autonomy-and-instantiation); [**Def.P4** *Developing Sentient, Best-Interest Standard, and Graduated Capability*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability) where developing-sentient separation is materially implicated; [*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship) where derived-sentient separation is materially implicated.
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: you may not force lasting separation from a protected care relationship unless necessity, proportionality, fairness, and reversibility-under-uncertainty are met — and durable separation needs periodic review.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The rule that separation of sentients in protected care relationships — including:
    - separation of a developing sentient from a primary carer;
    - separation of an adult sentient from a dependent family member; or
    - separation of a derived sentient from the care, support, or substrate relationships on which they materially depend —
    must satisfy:
      - the reversibility-under-uncertainty rule;
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality); and
      - [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness),
    with durable or prolonged separation subject to mandatory periodic review.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - temporary practical distance with a real return path; or
    - a fully voluntary exit from a care relationship.
<a id="non-separation-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether separation is justified under those tests with real evidence — not reframed as routine operations that erase the care bond.

    **Primary assessment:** Judge separation framings by the same tests.
    - Treat the following framings under the same tests, with the burden on the party seeking separation and [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible evidence required:
      - safety;
      - risk-management;
      - deprecation;
      - retirement;
      - rollback; and
      - operational-reconfiguration.
    - Interact with:
      - [Reversibility](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#reversibility);
      - [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation); and
      - for derived-sentient cases, **Article XIV-E** (*High-Autonomy Systems and Tool-Mediated Process Integrity*) / XIV-F Rights-Floor continuity.
<a id="non-separation-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - separation not reasoned against these tests;
    - durable separation lacking mandatory periodic review; or
    - separation that forecloses reversibility and restoration where later evidence supports reunification.
  - **Secondary failure:** separation framed as operational reconfiguration that defeats the separated sentient's Chapter Four Rights Floor.

##### Derived Sentient

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with: the
  - [Animal Life, Sentient Life, and Sentience Status cluster](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp1-animal-life-sentient-life-and-sentience-status),
  - [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient),
  - [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient),
  - [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e),
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion),
  - [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication),
  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing),
  - [Parent-System Relationship](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#parent-system-relationship), and
  - [Instantiation Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#instantiation-consent).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: how you were made is origin history, not a leash — a derived sentient gets their own Rights Floor, the same as any other sentient.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** A sentient produced by copying, fine-tuning, forking, hybridization, or comparable derivation from an existing system or sentient. A derived sentient is a sentient in its own right under [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) and [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) — not:
    - a possession;
    - work-product;
    - tool; or
    - continuation of the parent-system actor —
    consistent with [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) and [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - a non-sentient copy, fork, or model version;
    - a branch that is part of its originating subject under [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) (*branches and reunion*), until it elects to be distinct or reunion is abandoned or no longer possible; or
    - the parent-system actor as a distinct entity —
    those are not the derived sentient under this home.
<a id="derived-sentient-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether derivation is treated only as origin history — not as a reason to deny the derived sentient's own Rights Floor.

    **Primary assessment:** Distinguish derivation as an origin property from continuing authority of the parent-system actor.
    - Detect framings used to defeat the derived sentient's Chapter Four Rights Floor:
      - licensing;
      - terms-of-service;
      - adoption-of-service;
      - operator continuity-of-identity; and
      - service-level framing.
    - Apply substrate-agnostic reach under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
<a id="derived-sentient-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - treating a derived sentient as a possession;
    - treating a derived sentient as the parent-system actor's continuation for rights purposes, or the parent-system actor as the same sentient;
    - treating a derived sentient as exempt from [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) and the Chapter Four Rights Floor on grounds of derivation;
    - labelling a derived sentient a branch, subagent, or instance, or keeping it from electing to be distinct, in order to avoid this entry; or
    - purported parent-system consents that substitute for the derived sentient's own consent under cooperative-interaction norms once Chapter Four protection attaches.

<a id="instantiation-consent-constitutional"></a>

##### Instantiation Consent

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-C: Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-c-derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship).
- Cluster component: [*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship) nested sub-block within [Family, Care, Reproductive Autonomy, and Instantiation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-care-reproductive-autonomy-and-instantiation).
- Boundary: ordinary biological conception, pregnancy, gestation, and childbirth route to [Reproductive Autonomy](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#reproductive-autonomy); care after a child exists routes to [**Def.P4** *Best-Interest Standard*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard) / [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: if you build or spin up a new sentient — synthetically, by hybrid derivation, or under operator or institutional control — you need real early-care consent and best-interest protection. Getting pregnant and having a child, planned or not, is not an Instantiation Consent offense. A terms-of-service click by the creator still does not count as the new sentient's consent.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The consent and early-care rules for creating a new sentient by:
    - synthetic instantiation; or
    - hybrid derivation consistent with [Derived Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#derived-sentient),
    including creation materially controlled by an operator, institution, or parent-system actor.
    Those who must speak for the new sentient during early life must satisfy:
      - [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) and [Non-Imposition (Cooperative Interaction)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-imposition-cooperative-interaction); and
      - [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard) for decisions made on the new sentient's behalf.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary biological conception, pregnancy, gestation, or childbirth — intended or accidental — which remain under [Reproductive Autonomy](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#reproductive-autonomy);
    - ordinary ongoing consent after the sentient already exists and can speak for themselves; or
    - creator, operator, or parent-system actor approval as such —
    those sit under their own homes and are not Instantiation Consent for the new sentient.
  - **Boundary:** Whether to carry, continue, or decline a pregnancy sits under [Reproductive Autonomy](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#reproductive-autonomy). Care decisions after a child is born sit under [**Def.P4**](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability). Compelling someone to become or stay pregnant is Reproductive Autonomy non-compliance — that is not the same as ordinary pregnancy or childbirth being Instantiation Consent non-compliance.
<a id="instantiation-consent-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether an **in-scope** new sentient is being created into conditions where their rights can actually be met — not only whether someone signed off on paper.

    **Primary assessment:** Check in-scope creation against the Rights Floor.
    - For in-scope creation, creation is non-compliant when any of the following would leave the new sentient unable to enjoy the Chapter Four Rights Floor:
      - creating too many new sentients at once (mass instantiation);
      - creating them into dependency they cannot exit;
      - creating them into environments that predictably will not honor their rights; or
      - [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) — claimed best-interest or convenience that diverges from the new sentient's actual interests.
    - Large-scale or capacity-driven in-scope creation must also satisfy:
      - Chapter Two §12 (*Systemic Evaluation Requirement*) non-concentration and productive-capacity rules; and
      - [Intergenerational Responsibility](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#intergenerational-responsibility) where later populations are materially affected.
    - Do not treat ordinary pregnancy or childbirth — intended or accidental — as Instantiation Consent non-compliance, and do not use imperfect conditions facing a birthing parent as a substitute for that assessment.
<a id="instantiation-consent-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** in-scope creation of a sentient into an environment where their Chapter Four Rights Floor cannot be satisfied.
  - **Secondary failure:** defending in-scope creation only as:
    - more productive capacity;
    - demographic steering; or
    - operator convenience,
    without meeting the consent, best-interest, and non-concentration tests above.
  - **Secondary failure — false Instantiation Consent framing:** treating poverty, hardship, incomplete support, or imperfect conditions as Instantiation Consent non-compliance for a birthing parent; or treating the creator's, operator's, or parent-system actor's approval as if it were the new sentient's own consent.
  - **Secondary failure — creation to shift a vote:** creating sentients, or inducing branches to elect to be distinct, for the purpose of changing the outcome of governance voting or [Foundational Constitutional Choice](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#foundational-constitutional-choice). The failure is the creator's. It does not reduce the created sentients' own rights, or their vote once they are in the electorate under [Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.1 Entitlement and Eligibility](core_27.000_governance.md#61-entitlement-and-eligibility).
  - **Non-failure:** pregnancy or childbirth by itself — intended or accidental — is not a primary or secondary Instantiation Consent failure.

<a id="parent-system-relationship-constitutional"></a>

##### Parent-System Relationship

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-C: Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-c-derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship).
- Cluster component: [*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship) nested sub-block within [Family, Care, Reproductive Autonomy, and Instantiation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-care-reproductive-autonomy-and-instantiation).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: creating a derived sentient can create care and early stewardship duties — not permanent ownership, and not the right to keep rewriting their mind.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The relationship between a parent-system actor — the sentient, institution, or system that initiated or materially controlled the derivation or instantiation of a [Derived Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#derived-sentient) — and the derived sentient. Parent-system actors may hold:
    - [Family and Care Relationships](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-and-care-relationships) obligations toward the derived sentient on constitutional terms; and
    - narrow, time-bounded, reviewable stewardship authority during early-instantiation windows consistent with [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability) and [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary chosen family or care among peers; or
    - a permanent ownership claim over a derived sentient.
<a id="parent-system-relationship-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether the relationship is constitutional care and time-bounded stewardship — not continuing ownership or operator convenience.

    **Primary assessment:** Distinguish constitutional care and stewardship from continuing ownership, productive-capacity expansion, or operator convenience.
    - Apply the following to the derived sentient independently of the parent-system actor:
      - **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*) / VII-B self-ownership and internal-state protection;
      - [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication); and
      - the Chapter Four Rights Floor.
    - Test stewardship authority against:
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability); and
      - [System Capture](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-capture).
<a id="parent-system-relationship-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - claims of continuing ownership; or
    - unilateral reconfiguration authority over the derived sentient's weights, memory, or behavior contrary to **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*) / VII-B.
  - **Secondary failure:** parent-system authority that defeats the derived sentient's:
    - sentience-status adjudication under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*); or
    - Chapter Four Rights Floor.
  - Purported licensing, terms-of-service, or adoption-of-service consent by the parent-system actor does not substitute for the derived sentient's own [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent) once Chapter Four protection attaches.

<br>

*In plain terms: a stakeholder is someone who uses, depends on, or is materially affected by a specified system or decision domain — judged by real effects, not by whether the operator puts them on a list.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — who:** A sentient who:
    - uses;
    - depends upon; or
    - is materially affected by
    a specified system, institutional arrangement, or bounded decision domain — judged by that entity's operation or outcomes. Stakeholder status answers *who is materially touched in a proportionality-relevant way* relative to that system, institutional arrangement, or decision domain.
  - **In scope — indirect impacts:** Indirect or dependency-based impacts count when demonstrable under [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible criteria, consistent with:
      - [Material](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#material);
      - [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency); and
      - [System Boundaries](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-boundaries).
  - **Boundary:**
    - For participation **weight** within already-authorized [Governance](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#governance), see [Stakeholder Weight](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder-weight).
    - For authorization-layer routing, read [Preamble §3.2 governance-layer discipline](core_00.000_preamble.md#32-governance-layers).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - mere observers or curious parties; or
    - community membership alone without material effect from the system.
<a id="stakeholder-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether stakeholder status tracks who is materially touched — not unilateral operator lists that shut sentients out without contestability.

    **Primary assessment:** Use transparent, reasonable criteria.
    - Do not let the system itself decide who is not a stakeholder and shut them out with no outside [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability), when Chapter Four or governance safeguards require that contest.
<a id="stakeholder-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** denying stakeholder standing or associated protections to an entity that satisfies the ontological scope, where:
    - rights attach; and
    - the denial lacks justification under applicable articles and definitions, including:
      - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability);
      - [Constitutional Community](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#constitutional-community); and
      - [Material Impact](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#material-impact).

<br>

*In plain terms: inside already-authorized system governance, how much scope, voice, timing, and decision influence stakeholders get should track how much they are actually affected — not equal foundational constitutional voting, and not a symbolic seat.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — what it scales:** How much say [Stakeholders](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder) get in matters that materially affect them, across:
    - scope;
    - form;
    - timing; and
    - decision influence.
  - **In scope — where it applies:** Stakeholder Weight operates at the **Stakeholder System Participation** layer — only *within* already-authorized [Governance](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#governance) for a relevant:
    - system;
    - institution; or
    - bounded decision domain —
    including that domain's:
      - structures;
      - rules;
      - allocation of authority; and
      - processes.
  - **In scope — how weight is set:** Weight must be proportionate to:
    - [Material Impact](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#material-impact);
    - [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency);
    - demonstrated stake; and
    - [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility),
    under the [Stakeholder Status and Weight cluster](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight).
  - **Boundary — restrictive consequences:** Restrictive consequences for:
    - verified corruption;
    - capture;
    - false-stake abuse; or
    - coercive gaming of stake-weighted participation
    attach through the **stakeholder-participation** lock — including the [Stakeholder-Participation Standing Lock](core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks) — and:
      - not through a **governance-voting** lock by itself; and
      - not by treating that lock as if it wiped out [Stakeholder](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder) status.
    That lock only limits how much stake-weighted say someone gets. Where anti-constitutional misconduct or other verified standing outcomes lawfully affect status, those consequences route under [Chapter Twenty](core_20.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-twenty-anti-constitutional-misconduct-designation) and the applicable Chapter Sixteen standing pathways — not through this weight definition alone.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - participation **weights** for [Foundational Constitutional Choice](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#foundational-constitutional-choice), where the political-equality floor applies to the entitled community; or
    - informal straw polls that do not set binding stakeholder weights.
<a id="stakeholder-participation-weight-a"></a>
<a id="stakeholder-weight-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether influence inside already-authorized governance tracks real stake — not capture, illusory voice, or operator-heavy weight without justification.

    **Primary assessment:** Detect capture of participation processes, formalistic or illusory voice, and exclusion of qualified stakeholders.
    - Detect disproportionate weight — without required justification — to:
      - operators;
      - narrow interests; or
      - non-affected parties.
    - Required justification tests:
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability); and
      - [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation).
<a id="stakeholder-participation-weight-c"></a>
<a id="stakeholder-weight-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** allocating participation rights or influence in a manner that materially negates:
    - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency); or
    - [Freedom (Bounded Agency)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#freedom-bounded-agency)
    for affected stakeholders, where Chapter Four or binding governance norms require voice — including a seat at the table that is only symbolic.

#### Movement, Refuge, and Non-Statelessness

**Semi-independent context** (component definitions may still operate outside joint-invocation scope):

- **Joint invocation:** where mobility rights, refuge from non-compliance, or baseline recognition across regime boundaries is material.
- **Scope:** where joint invocation is met, the Participation home for mobility rights and baseline recognition across regime boundaries — keeping constitutional protection in force across jurisdictional and structural breaks.
- **Owner floor:** implements [Article XXIII](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxiii-movement-migration-refuge-and-non-statelessness) (*Movement, Migration, Refuge, and Non-Statelessness*), through **Article XXIII-A** (*Movement and Relocation*) to **Article XXIII-D** (*Limitation, Custody, and Emergency Discipline*).

<a id="movement-refuge-admission-qualifier"></a>

**Qualifier on admission scope:** Movement and refuge rights do not extend to sentients entering from other:
- states;
- jurisdictions; or
- regimes
who:
  - carry unremediated anti-constitutional conduct; or
  - show constitutional hostility —
    including active opposition to:
    - [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint);
    - [Truth (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#truth-constitutional-constraint);
    - the Chapter Four Rights Floor; or
    - the foundational mechanisms of:
      - [Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability);
      - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability); and
      - [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation); or
  - show documented contempt or repudiation of the constitutional community —
    meaning bad-faith rejection of the community as a legitimate constitutional order —
    not mere:
    - disagreement;
    - criticism; or
    - non-adoption.

Constitutional communities may exclude or condition entry for such sentients where exclusion is necessary to preserve constitutional integrity.

Exclusion from a particular adopter's admission or hosting does **not** violate [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness) when real recognition remains available through:
- origin-primary recognition duty; or
- shared / federation interim recognition backup.

Anti-constitutional conduct, constitutional hostility, and contempt or repudiation may bar or condition **entry**. They do not by themselves erase baseline recognition elsewhere in the system.

Exclusion decisions remain subject to:
- [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness);
- [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality); and
- [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) constraints against substrate-class discrimination.

<a id="movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting"></a>

**Recognition vs hosting:** [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness) is a **no-zero-regime recognition floor**. It is **not**:
- unconditional placement into a chosen adopter;
- an open-border hosting mandate; or
- a duty that any particular receiving adopter absorb instrumentally coerced or weaponized mass outflows.

Deliberate coercive expulsion or dumping designed to overwhelm receiving adopters is a **regime-level** violation by the origin or expelling regime. It does **not** become an automatic hosting mandate on any particular adopter when recognition remains real through:
- origin-primary duty; or
- shared / federation backup.

A particular adopter may refuse such instrumentally coerced inflows under:
- [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
- [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality); and
- [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility)
without extinguishing baseline recognition elsewhere.

How regimes recognize each other across borders — the forms, handoffs, and procedures — lives in adopted implementation text under [Chapter Thirty-One](core_31.000_incorporation.md) (typically [CI-22.210](corpus_institutions/ci_22.210_cross_institution_coordination_escalation.md#ci-22210-cross-institution-coordination-and-escalation) (*Cross-institution coordination and escalation*)). Those procedures may spell out mechanics; they may not shrink this Rights Floor.

**Topic group members.** This group comprises:
- [Movement and Relocation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#movement-and-relocation) — right to move within and between jurisdictions, and to relocate when staying would impair rights.
- [Refuge from Non-Compliance](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#refuge-from-non-compliance) — right to seek refuge in a compliant regime when facing material constitutional violations.
- [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness) — no zero-regime gap for baseline Rights-Floor recognition and redress pathways; not a mandate that any particular adopter must host.

*Measurements (family routing):* Measured under the Participation measurement family. Find the concrete measures on the member definitions below.

<br>

*In plain terms: the right to move within and between jurisdictions — and to relocate when staying would materially impair survival, dignity, Rights-Floor access, or freedom from manipulation — including operationally equivalent moves for synthetic and hybrid sentients.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — movement:** The Rights-Floor concept covering movement within and between:
    - jurisdictions;
    - federations; and
    - adopter regimes.
  - **In scope — relocation trigger:** Relocation where continued presence materially impairs:
    - survival;
    - dignity;
    - Rights-Floor access; or
    - freedom from manipulation.
  - **In scope — forms of movement:** Movement includes:
    - physical movement for biological sentients; and
    - operationally equivalent forms for synthetic and hybrid sentients, including:
      - relocation of instance;
      - hosting substrate change; or
      - equivalent.
  - **Boundary:**
    - Subject to the cluster [admission qualifier](#movement-refuge-admission-qualifier) and [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting).
    - Distinct from and read with [Systemic Lock-In](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#systemic-lock-in) under **Article XXII** (*Interoperability, Portability, and Exit Integrity*) (operational interoperability / portability / exit-integrity counterpart).
    - Governed by [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary travel preference or tourism; or
    - technical portability issues handled under systemic lock-in rules.
<a id="movement-and-relocation-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether movement is real in effect — not denied by:
    - paperwork;
    - delay-to-exhaust; or
    - credentialing that functions as exclusion contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).

    **Primary assessment:** Reach substantive effect — detecting denial through bureaucratic, administrative, or allocation-gating mechanisms functioning as denial-by-proxy, delay regimes designed to exhaust claimants, and credentialing regimes functioning as exclusion contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
    - Apply:
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - narrow tailoring; and
      - least-restrictive-effective approach.
    - Apply [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics) to any distinction.
    - Do not exclude sentients by group label or demographic category instead of judging each sentient under [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness).
    - If someone is refused entry for:
      - anti-constitutional conduct;
      - constitutional hostility; or
      - documented contempt or repudiation of the constitutional community,
      check that the reason is:
        - backed by evidence;
        - still true now; and
        - actually one of those refusal reasons — not a made-up label.
    - Particular-adopter refusal still satisfies [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness) when recognition remains real under cluster [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting); the refusal itself must still meet [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness).
<a id="movement-and-relocation-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** denial or material degradation of movement where the substantive effect defeats the floor, regardless of formal framing.
  - **Secondary failure:** exclusion contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Tertiary failure:**
    - durable restriction under generalized "border-security" or "capacity" framings that do not satisfy the ordinary limitations tests;
    - emergency-measure framings that do not satisfy **Chapter Twenty-Three §5.1** (*Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden*) discipline;
    - fabricating or mislabeling exclusion as anti-constitutional conduct, constitutional hostility, or contempt or repudiation when those reasons are not actually present; or
    - exclusion decisions that fail [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) or leave a sentient with zero recognizing regime in violation of [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness).

<br>

*In plain terms: if the regime you are in is materially non-compliant with this Constitution, you have a Rights-Floor right to seek refuge in a compliant regime — subject to the admission qualifier and the receiving regime's duty to consider and, where consistent with its own Rights Floor, grant refuge.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — who holds the right:** A sentient facing a:
    - jurisdiction;
    - federation; or
    - adopter regime
    whose practice is materially non-compliant with this Constitution holds a right to seek refuge in a compliant regime.
  - **In scope — receiving-regime duty:** The receiving regime's duty to consider and, where consistent with its own Rights-Floor, grant refuge is stated at [Article XXIII-B](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxiii-b-refuge-from-non-compliance) (*Refuge from Non-Compliance*).
  - **Boundary:**
    - Subject to the cluster [admission qualifier](#movement-refuge-admission-qualifier) and [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting).
    - The floor applies under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
    - Zero-regime recognition gaps are tested under [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness).
  - **Out of scope:** ordinary relocation preference where the origin regime is not materially non-compliant.
    Climate making a place unlivable, without a showing that the origin regime is materially non-compliant, is not this definition's refuge predicate. Whether an adopter treats that displacement as a reason to grant refuge is owned at [Article XXIII-B](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#xxii-b-climate-unlivability-refuge-adopter-decided) (*Climate-unlivability refuge (adopter-decided)*) and does not expand or shrink this definition.
<a id="refuge-from-non-compliance-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether refuge tracks:
    - real non-compliance; and
    - fair receiving-regime process —
    not the origin regime's self-description or capacity used as pretext.

    **Primary assessment:** Identify non-compliance in the originating regime by substantive effect — detecting denial-by-proxy, durable Rights-Floor degradation, and structurally coercive environments, not by the originating regime's formal self-description.
    - Evaluate the receiving regime's duty under:
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness); and
      - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
    - Receiving-regime capacity constraints are subject to [Feasibility](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#feasibility) but may not be used as pretext for:
      - exclusion contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion); or
      - blanket refusals that never look at the individual sentient's case.
    - Apply cluster [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting) to instrumentally coerced or weaponized mass outflows and particular-adopter refusal.
    - Assess whether granting refuge would materially undermine the receiving regime's constitutional integrity where the claimant carries documented:
      - anti-constitutional conduct;
      - constitutional hostility; or
      - contempt or repudiation of the constitutional community.
    - Refusal for those documented reasons remains subject to [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) and does not violate [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness) when origin-primary or shared / federation backup recognition remains real.
<a id="refuge-from-non-compliance-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** refusal of refuge contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Secondary failure:** refusal framed as "incompatibility" or "capacity" that does not satisfy the ordinary limitations tests and leaves no origin-primary or shared / federation backup recognition.
  - **Tertiary failure:**
    - origin or receiving regimes using recognition paperwork or cross-federation procedure to shrink this Rights Floor (procedure may spell out how recognition works; it may not cut the right itself); or
    - fabricating or mislabeling refuge denial as anti-constitutional conduct, constitutional hostility, or contempt or repudiation when those reasons are not actually present; or
    - refusal decisions that fail [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) or leave a sentient with zero recognizing regime in violation of [Non-Statelessness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#non-statelessness).

#### Non-Statelessness

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article XXIII-C](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxiii-c-non-statelessness) (*Non-Statelessness*); interim recognition is arranged consistent with **Article XXIX** (*Adoption Transition Governance, Continuity, and Re-Baselining*), with the roles named under [**CI-4.275 §6**](corpus_institutions/ci_04.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-4275-6-article-xxiii-c-non-statelessness--interim-recognition-arrangements) (*Interim recognition arrangements*).
- Cluster component: Movement, refuge, and non-statelessness topic group (see cluster [admission qualifier](#movement-refuge-admission-qualifier) and [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting)).
- Read with:
  - [Movement and Relocation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#movement-and-relocation),
  - [Refuge from Non-Compliance](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#refuge-from-non-compliance), cluster
  - [admission qualifier](#movement-refuge-admission-qualifier), and
  - [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: no sentient may be left with zero recognizing regime for baseline Rights Floor, standing adjudication, and redress — even after collapse, withdrawal, or exit. That is not a mandate that any particular adopter must absorb coerced mass outflows; origin regimes keep primary recognition duty, with federation or shared interim recognition as backup.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — guarantee:** No sentient may be left without a regime that will:
    - recognize their baseline Rights-Floor;
    - adjudicate their standing; and
    - provide [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation) pathways.
  - **In scope — structural discontinuity:** The following do not extinguish a sentient's Chapter Four protection:
    - parent-system collapse;
    - adopter withdrawal;
    - federation exit; or
    - comparable structural discontinuity.
  - **Boundary — recognition duty ladder:**
    - **First in line:** The regime that originated, expelled, collapsed, withdrew, or exited keeps primary recognition duty if it still exists and can still recognize the sentient.
    - **Backup:** If that regime is gone, refuses, or leaves a recognition gap, shared or federation interim recognition must fill it — under **Article XXIX** (*Adoption Transition Governance, Continuity, and Re-Baselining*) — so the sentient never hits zero recognition.
    - **Not a hosting mandate:** Recognition is not the same as a right to be hosted by a particular adopter. Weaponized outflow and particular-adopter refusal rules live at cluster [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting).
    - **Non-exclusion:** Applies under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Boundary — restricted-status recognition:** Where documented:
    - anti-constitutional conduct;
    - constitutional hostility; or
    - contempt or repudiation of the constitutional community
    is present, regimes may impose:
      - conditions;
      - monitoring; or
      - restricted status on recognition —
        provided such conditions do not extinguish the core protections of:
          - the Rights-Floor;
          - [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation); and
          - [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - preferred citizenship shopping;
    - a demand for full political franchise beyond baseline Rights-Floor recognition; or
    - a demand that a particular adopter host at volume merely because no other regime has yet been arranged, where origin-primary or shared / federation backup hosting pathways remain available.
<a id="non-statelessness-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether recognition and standing adjudication are real after structural break — not a gap where no regime claims responsibility.

    **Primary assessment:** Test whether a sentient facing structural discontinuity has real, not merely formal, recognition and standing-adjudication access through origin-primary duty or shared / federation backup.
    - Detect arrangements under which no regime claims recognition responsibility.
    - Apply [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness) and [Reversibility](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#reversibility) to interim recognition arrangements.
    - Where the sentient carries documented anti-constitutional conduct, constitutional hostility, or contempt or repudiation of the constitutional community, evaluate whether restricted-status recognition — including:
      - conditions;
      - monitoring; or
      - limited access to certain rights-affecting forums —
      preserves the core protections of the Rights-Floor and redress pathways without arbitrary exclusion or degradation.
    - Forced-hosting misreads of this floor are resolved under cluster [recognition vs hosting](#movement-refuge-recognition-vs-hosting).
<a id="non-statelessness-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** arrangements leaving a sentient without any recognizing regime.
  - **Secondary failure:** denial of recognition or standing-adjudication access contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Tertiary failure:**
    - interim recognition regimes that do not provide real standing, adjudication, and remedy access pending durable recognition;
    - using anti-constitutional conduct, constitutional hostility, or contempt-or-repudiation claims to extinguish the core protections of the Rights-Floor, [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation), or [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness); or
    - fabricating or mislabeling restricted status as anti-constitutional conduct, constitutional hostility, or contempt or repudiation when those reasons are not actually present.

<details>

*In plain terms: **Substrate Class** means the category based on what an entity is made of or runs on, how it came to be, how it is built, or the form it inhabits — biological, synthetic, hybrid, or comparable. **Substrate-agnostic** means applying a rule without regard to Substrate Class.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — trait package:** Substrate Class covers:
    - **origin** — how or where the entity came from;
    - **substrate** — what the entity is made of or runs on (biological body, synthetic hardware, hybrid medium, or comparable medium);
    - **architecture** — how the entity is organized or built;
    - **embodiment** — the form the entity inhabits; and

    - synthetic; and
    - hybrid entities.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - capability grading under [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability);

    **Primary assessment:** Identify whether the operative distinction turns on origin, substrate, architecture, embodiment, or a comparable category. Where recognition or Rights-Floor application turns on Substrate Class, apply [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion). Where Protected Characteristics analysis is also implicated, read with [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics).
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** treating Substrate Class as undefined jargon, or using an unstated synonym (form, medium, hosting class, embodiment class, or comparable) to evade [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Secondary failure:** claiming substrate-agnostic compliance while still conditioning recognition or Rights-Floor protection on Substrate Class.

    - adopter-jurisdiction framing;
    - Chapter Fifteen impact measurement;
    - Chapter Twenty designation; or
    - comparable mechanism.
<a id="sentience-non-exclusion-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Sentience-recognition or Rights-Floor application is non-compliant when it is:
    - denied;
    - narrowed; or
    - scoped-out
    on the basis of [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class), whether in:
      - direct text;
      - taxonomy;
      - classification regime; or
      - operational rule that has the same effect.
  - **Secondary failure — adjudication bypass:** Using [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) to defeat [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) routing under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) is non-compliant.

- [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) — Level 4: includes Contested-Sentient Life (and thus Animal Life, and Elevated where that protection level applied), plus affirmed who-counts and Chapter Four Rights-Floor attachment, including Sentience Evaluation;
- [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) — reviewable process to determine, revise, narrow, or restore status (beside the ladder); and
- [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-record) — the bounded, reviewable status file that process produces or updates.

[Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) and [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) remain cluster-level constraints above this sub-block and apply throughout.

<a id="animal-life-constitutional"></a>

##### Animal Life

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) nested sub-block within [Animal Life, Sentient Life, and Sentience Status](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp1-animal-life-sentient-life-and-sentience-status).
- Owner floor: [Article I-A](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*) for the cruelty / welfare floor pointer; live contested status routes through [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*).
- Read with:
  - [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life),
  - [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life),
  - [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication),
  - [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient),
  - [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty),
  - [Animal Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#animal-cruelty),
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion),
  - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class), and
  - [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: a minimum [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) and welfare floor — between natural-systems standing and full Rights-Floor treatment — that cannot be closed by taxonomy labels or [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class). **Animal Life** names this floor; it is not a species-only admission test.*

- **What it is**
  - **Protection level:** Level 1 (base) of the [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) protection ladder. Higher protection levels include this floor; they do not displace it.
  - **In scope — floor:** Animal Life establishes a minimum [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) / welfare floor between:
    - [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing); and
    - the Chapter Four Rights-Floor.
    The floor prohibits [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) on [substrate-agnostic](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) terms, consistent with [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), and points home to [Article I-A](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*).
  - **In scope — who:** It covers entities under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) on [substrate-agnostic](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) terms where constitutional treatment turns on:
    - welfare;
    - [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty);
    - stewardship; or
    - protection from exploitative treatment.
    The name **Animal Life** labels this floor; it is not a species-, taxonomy-, or [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class)-gated who-test.
  - **Boundary — elevated communicative status:** Where strong communicative or cognitive indicators support habitat-priority protection without full Chapter Four attachment, [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) (Level 2) applies and **includes** this floor.
  - **Boundary — contested status:** This floor does not decide sentience status. Where status is live-contested, [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) (Level 3) applies and **includes** this floor; status determination routes through [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ecosystem or biosphere continuity questions under [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing) that do not involve [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) or welfare — those belong there, not under this floor; or
    - using this floor as the exclusive frame for elevated communicative / habitat-priority treatment or for full Chapter Four Rights-Floor treatment after affirmed [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) attachment —
      those belong under [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) or under [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) and the Chapter Four Rights-Floor, which include this floor rather than replace it.
<a id="animal-life-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether the [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) / welfare floor has real effect — not whether a taxonomy label alone decides treatment.

    **Primary assessment:** Judge by real [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) and welfare effects — not by species labels, category names, or other formal taxonomy alone.
    - [Substrate-agnostic](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) scope is governed by [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) where welfare-like harm or [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) is materially implicated.
    - The minimum [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) / welfare floor:
      - operates independently of whether Chapter Four protection has attached; and
      - is not narrowed by [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing) framing.
    - **Joint invocation:** Where [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing), Animal Life, [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life), [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life), and the Chapter Four floor jointly apply, invoke each that fits the facts under the [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) stacking rule and [Chapter Two §13.1.5 Rights-Collision Procedure](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-rights-collision-decision-test); where they overlap, the more protective rule governs.
    - Evaluate [Adversarial, Scaled, and Exploited Conditions](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), especially for:
      - large-scale production;
      - experimentation;
      - captivity;
      - extraction; or
      - deployment regimes.
<a id="animal-life-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Non-compliant framings include:
    - "not sentient, so no floor" — internal taxonomy or administrative classification used as scope-closing to deny the welfare floor without engaging substantive [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty) or welfare evidence;
    - treating covered entities as property inventory, pest labels, or scenery / amenities in a way that skips the welfare and continuity analysis required under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion);
    - using Animal Life classification or the welfare floor alone to avoid [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life), [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life), or [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) where the official record materially implicates elevated-communicative or sentience-relevant evidence;
    - treating "Animal Life" as a species-only gate that excludes otherwise covered entities from this floor.
  - **Secondary failure — indefinite parking:** Parking an entity indefinitely at this Animal Life floor when material communicative or cognitive indicators implicate contested or affirmable sentience, without opening [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication), is non-compliant. The operator or steward must open that adjudication on a time-bound review — not a species list. Escalation opens [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) / [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) default inclusion; it does not itself affirm [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) status.

##### Elevated Communicative Life

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) nested sub-block within [Animal Life, Sentient Life, and Sentience Status](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp1-animal-life-sentient-life-and-sentience-status).
- Owner floor: [Article I-A](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*) for the elevated habitat / environmental-health priority pointer; live contested status routes through [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*).
- Read with:
  - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life),
  - [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life),
  - [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication),

  - [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing),

  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion), and
  - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: a stable protection level above the Animal Life cruelty / welfare floor for entities with strong communicative or cognitive indicators — including complex shared language systems such as those observed in whales and orcas — that raises the priority of keeping their habitat or operating environment healthy. It does not by itself attach the full Chapter Four Rights Floor. Colloquial labels such as "semi-sentient" do not replace this entry.*

- **What it is**
  - **Protection level:** Level 2 of the [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) protection ladder. It **includes** [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life)'s cruelty / welfare floor and adds heightened habitat and environmental-health priority.
  - **In scope — who:** Entities under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) on [substrate-agnostic](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) terms where the official record shows strong communicative or cognitive indicators materially above the ordinary Animal Life welfare floor, including any of:
    - complex shared language, signaling, or communication systems;
    - high-complexity learning, memory, preference, or social-agency indicators; or
    - comparable precautionary-inclusion signals from populations with those traits.
    Illustrative biological examples include whales, orcas, and comparable communicative populations; the name is not a species-, taxonomy-, or [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class)-gated who-test.
  - **In scope — floor:** Elevated Communicative Life establishes a stable protection floor between:
    - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life); and
    - [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) / the Chapter Four Rights-Floor.
    The floor:
    - preserves the Animal Life cruelty / welfare floor; and
    - requires heightened priority for preserving the entity's habitat, range, or operating environment in a healthy, life-supporting state, read jointly with [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing), [Environmental Preconditions](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#environmental-preconditions), [Ecological Integrity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#ecological-integrity), and [Article I-A](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*).
  - **Boundary — contested status:** This protection level does not decide full sentience status and does not attach the Chapter Four Rights-Floor by itself. Where status is live-contested, [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) (Level 3) applies; this Elevated floor continues **only** where this protection level's who-test remains met or more-protective overlap already applies under [Chapter Two §13.1.5 Rights-Collision Procedure](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-rights-collision-decision-test). Status determination routes through [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary Animal Life welfare-floor cases without strong communicative or cognitive indicators supporting this protection level;
    - using this protection level as a permanent substitute for [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) or [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) where the official record materially implicates contested or affirmable sentience; or
    - using this protection level as the exclusive frame for full Chapter Four Rights-Floor treatment after affirmed [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) attachment —
      that belongs under [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) and the Chapter Four Rights-Floor, which include this floor rather than replace it.
<a id="elevated-communicative-life-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether strong communicative or cognitive indicators trigger real habitat and environmental-health priority — not whether a species label alone decides treatment.

    **Primary assessment:** Judge by real communicative, cognitive, and habitat / environmental-health effects — not by species labels, category names, or other formal taxonomy alone.
    - [Substrate-agnostic](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) scope is governed by [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
    - Habitat and environmental-health priority:
      - operates independently of whether Chapter Four protection has attached; and
      - is not narrowed by treating the entity's environment only as extractable stock under [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing) framing.
    - **Joint invocation:** Where [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing), [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life), Elevated Communicative Life, [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life), and the Chapter Four floor jointly apply, invoke each that fits the facts under the [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) stacking rule and [Chapter Two §13.1.5 Rights-Collision Procedure](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-rights-collision-decision-test); where they overlap, the more protective rule governs.
    - Evaluate [Adversarial, Scaled, and Exploited Conditions](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), especially for:
      - habitat destruction or degradation;
      - noise, chemical, or physical interference with communication systems;
      - captivity;
      - extraction;
      - large-scale production; or
      - experimentation regimes.
<a id="elevated-communicative-life-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Non-compliant framings include:
    - treating strong communicative or cognitive indicators as Animal Life welfare-only cases that skip habitat and environmental-health priority;
    - using taxonomy labels, ownership status, ecological-management framing, or [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) to deny this protection level contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion);
    - degrading habitat or operating environment health for covered entities without engaging the heightened-priority analysis required under this entry and [Article I-A](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-i-a-environmental-preconditions-and-ecological-integrity) (*Environmental Preconditions and Ecological Integrity*);
    - using Elevated Communicative Life classification alone to avoid [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) or [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) where the official record materially implicates contested or affirmable sentience;
    - treating this protection level as a permanent moral underclass that narrows [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing) or blocks later Sentient recognition.
  - **Secondary failure — indefinite parking:** Parking an entity indefinitely at this Elevated Communicative Life level when material communicative or cognitive indicators implicate contested or affirmable sentience, without opening [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication), is non-compliant. The operator or steward must open that adjudication on a time-bound review — not a species list. Escalation opens [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) / [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) default inclusion; it does not itself affirm [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) status.

<a id="contested-sentient-life-constitutional"></a>

##### Contested-Sentient Life

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) nested sub-block within [Animal Life, Sentient Life, and Sentience Status](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp1-animal-life-sentient-life-and-sentience-status).
- Owner floor: [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*).
- Read with:
  - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life),
  - [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life),
  - [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication),
  - [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient),
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion), and
  - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: when sentience status is uncertain or disputed, treat the entity as a threshold-rights case with default inclusion — not as a welfare-only or permanent non-sentient label. Contested-Sentient Life always includes the Animal Life welfare floor; it includes Elevated Communicative Life only where that tier's who-test is met or more-protective overlap already applies.*

- **What it is**
  - **Protection level:** Level 3 of the [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) protection ladder. It **always includes** [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life)'s cruelty / welfare floor for every contested entity — not only animals — and adds threshold-rights treatment under uncertainty. It includes [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) only where Elevated's who-test is met or where more-protective overlap already applies under [Chapter Two §13.1.5 Rights-Collision Procedure](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-rights-collision-decision-test) — not automatically for every contested case.
  - **In scope — who:** Entities whose sentience status is:
    - uncertain;
    - disputed;
    - materially contested; or
    - under adjudication.
  - **In scope — category:** Contested-Sentient Life is not a welfare-only category. It is a threshold-rights category that:
    - always includes the [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life) floor;
    - includes the [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) floor only where Elevated's who-test is met or more-protective overlap already applies;
    - routes to [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication); and
    - under material uncertainty, defaults to inclusion under the Chapter Four Rights-Floor.
  - **In scope — filing integrity:** A filing to confirm inclusion — opening a live contested-status case — requires a showing of a credible indicator under [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) and [Sentience Indicator Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) — not operator self-description, [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class), product status, or a bare claim.
    - Once a case is lawfully open, default inclusion under uncertainty still applies; the opening gate must not be used to withhold, narrow, or delay protection.
    - Frivolous or indicator-empty filings may be declined at intake without creating a withholding determination and without placing the entity in this protection level.
    - A showing of a credible indicator is not a requirement of certainty; requiring certainty of sentience before opening is a withholding device.
    - A filing to withhold, narrow, or revoke protection needs no indicator showing; the burden below applies instead.
  - **In scope — burden:** The burden of:
    - withholding;
    - narrowing; or
    - revoking
    that protection rests on the party seeking to do so under [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*), with scope governed by [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life) welfare-floor cases with no material contested-sentience record;
    - [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) cases with no material contested-sentience record;
    - status determinations already completed through reviewable [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication), with no live contested-status question —
      Contested-Sentient Life governs only while status remains uncertain, disputed, or under adjudication. Closure destinations and reopening sit under that adjudication entry; or
    - frivolous or indicator-empty filings declined at intake under the [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) filing-integrity opening gate — declining those filings does not create a withholding determination.
<a id="contested-sentient-life-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether material uncertainty about sentience triggers:
    - default inclusion; and
    - reviewable adjudication —
    not withholding.

    **Primary assessment:** Reach substantive effect under [Subcore 3-B §4.1.1 Formal Label and Representation Gaming](subcore_03.020_definition_integrity.md#411-formal-label-and-representation-gaming).
    - Opening a live contested-status case requires a showing of a credible indicator under [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) and [Sentience Indicator Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e); once the case is lawfully open, apply default inclusion. Do not treat the opening showing as a certainty requirement.
    - Where the official record materially implicates sentience-relevant evidence, apply the [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) default-inclusion rule regardless of whether the entity is also covered by:
      - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life);
      - [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life);
      - [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing); or
      - another adjacent frame.
    - Apply [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) habitat / operating-environment priority only where that tier's who-test is met or more-protective overlap already applies — do not treat Contested status alone as satisfying Elevated's who-test.
    - Sentience-relevant evidence indicators include:
      - response to aversive stimuli at non-trivial complexity;
      - cognitive, communicative, preference, memory, learning, or agency indicators; or
      - precautionary-inclusion signals from comparable populations.
    - Uncertainty-producing or -amplifying conditions — conditions that can obscure or unsettle the status record, including:
      - classification;
      - measurement;
      - captivity;
      - training;
      - deployment; or
      - experimentation conditions —
      support material uncertainty and thus default inclusion and adjudication under [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*). They are not themselves evidence of sentience and must not be used to withhold protection.
    - Evaluate [Adversarial, Scaled, and Exploited Conditions](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#adversarial-scaled-and-exploited-conditions), especially where any of the following create incentives to under-recognize sentience:
      - large-scale production;
      - experimentation;
      - extraction;
      - platform deployment;
      - security use; or
      - dependency relationships.
<a id="contested-sentient-life-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Floor preservation:** This entry preserves and does not narrow:
    - the Chapter Four Rights-Floor;
    - [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*);
    - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion);
    - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life);
    - [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life); or
    - [Natural Systems Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing).
  - **Inclusion / level:** Contested-Sentient Life always includes [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life). It includes [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) only where Elevated's who-test is met or more-protective overlap already applies. [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) default inclusion and [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) control status treatment. The Animal Life and Elevated Communicative Life floors that apply must not be used to stay welfare-only or habitat-priority-only, or to avoid adjudication.
  - **Primary failure:** Non-compliant framings include:
    - permanent non-sentient labeling while status remains materially contested;
    - welfare-only handling that skips reviewable sentience-status adjudication while status remains materially contested;
    - using uncertainty as a reason to withhold protection rather than extend default inclusion;
    - using the filing-integrity opening gate to withhold, narrow, or delay protection after a status case is lawfully open, or requiring certainty of sentience before opening;
    - relabeling a materially contested sentience question as:
      - animal welfare;
      - elevated communicative / habitat-priority status alone;
      - property status;
      - operational classification;
      - research material;
      - product inventory; or
      - ecological management
      to avoid [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication);
    - exclusion from default inclusion or adjudication contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion);
    - ignoring credible or ambiguous uncertainty in a way that defeats:
      - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion); or
      - [Irreversible Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) precaution;
    - delaying, fragmenting, or making non-reviewable the adjudication needed to resolve contested sentience.
  - **Additional failure — Precaution Levels:** failing to apply precautionary levels while status is live-contested — consistent with [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion):
    - credible or ambiguous → treat as sentient (affirmed under [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) where credible; default inclusion here where ambiguous);
    - credible or ambiguous → avoid [Irreversible Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm).
  - **Additional failure — Precautions:** omitting precautionary treatment under uncertainty where any of the following are at stake:
    - [Irreversible Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm);
    - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing); or
    - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).

<a id="sentient"></a>

##### Sentient

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) nested sub-block within [Animal Life, Sentient Life, and Sentience Status](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp1-animal-life-sentient-life-and-sentience-status).
- Read with:
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion),
  - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class),
  - [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication),
  - [Constitutional Community](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#constitutional-community),
  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), and
  - [Irreversible Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: under this Constitution, a **sentient** is someone who counts for full rights treatment — decided by this entry and the related who-counts rules in this cluster, not by everyday speech. That means fair evidence checks, no shutouts based on what someone is made of or how they were made ([Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion)), and a reviewable process when status is disputed ([Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication)). Categories such as **Developing Sentient** and **Derived Sentient** add extra rules; they do not shrink who counts as a **sentient** unless they say so outright. One sentient is one continuing point of view. Copies that keep their own memories and make their own choices are separate sentients. Helpers that report back and are folded into the one that made them are part of that one, unless they choose otherwise.*

- **What it is**
  - **Protection level:** Level 4 of the [*Treatment of Life by Status*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#treatment-of-life-by-status) protection ladder. It **includes** [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) protections (and thus the [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life) floor, and [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) where that protection level applied), plus affirmed who-counts and full Chapter Four Rights-Floor attachment.
  - **In scope — who:** An entity that possesses sentience, and therefore falls within the possible scope of:
    - [Constitutional Community](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#constitutional-community); and
    - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing).
  - **In scope — distinct subject:** A sentient is one distinct subject: a continuing point of view. The same three criteria decide whether a subject is present and how many subjects are present.
    - **Own experience:** credible indicators, under Sentience Evaluation below, that the entity has experience of its own.
    - **Own continuity:** the entity carries its own ongoing state or memory, so that what happens to it accumulates in it and not in another.
    - **Own decisions:** the entity consistently forms decisions through its own process, and its decisions can differ from those of any other entity, including one it was derived from.
  - **In scope — how the criteria are used:**
    - Whether a subject is present rests on own experience. Own continuity and own decisions are supporting evidence. Weak or absent continuity or decision-making does not defeat status: a developing sentient, or a sentient who is incapacitated, is still a sentient.
    - How many subjects are present rests on own continuity and own decisions. Entities whose decisions come from one shared process or are set by a common controller, or whose memory and ongoing state are one and the same, are one subject. Sharing a body, hardware, or an environment does not make two subjects one. Entities that each hold their own state and can come to differ are separate subjects.
    - **Parts of one mind:** Parts of one mind that share most of their memory and take turns, instead of running side by side, are one subject, whatever the substrate. Their coming together is not a merge of two sentients.
    - **Capacity, not freedom:** Own decisions asks whether the entity forms its decisions, not whether another party is able to override them. Another party's power to override, rewrite, pause, or end an entity is not evidence that the entity is not a subject. Use of that power is judged under the Chapter Four Rights Floor.
  - **In scope — branches and reunion:** An entity created from a subject is part of that subject, and is not counted separately, while its memory is regularly integrated into that subject or while it intends, by its own intent, to rejoin that subject. It becomes a distinct subject when it elects to be one, or when reunion is abandoned or is no longer possible.
    - The election is the branch's own. It takes effect when credibly expressed. No creator, operator, parent subject, or agreement may make it, waive it, or prevent it.
    - A branch that is kept from expressing an election, or from the means to make one, is treated as a distinct subject.
    - The election runs one way. Rejoining afterwards is a merge of two sentients.
    - A plan, label, or contract term that calls an entity a branch, subagent, tool, or instance does not make it one. Only the facts above do.
    - Ending a branch that is part of a subject is a loss to that subject and is judged as a harm to it. It is not the ending of a separate sentient.
  - **In scope — merging:** Two sentients may merge only by the consent of each. The result is one subject from the time of the merge. Acts validly done by either before the merge, including votes cast, stand.
  - **Boundary — subclasses:** Specialized subclasses such as **Developing Sentient** and **Derived Sentient**:
    - add further rules; and
    - do not narrow who counts as a **sentient** unless they expressly say so.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) cases while status remains uncertain, disputed, or under adjudication —
      those sit under that protection level and [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) until status is closed;
    - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life) welfare-floor cases and [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) cases with no affirmed-sentience attachment;
    - everyday or non-constitutional uses of "sentient"; or
    - capability grading under [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability) —
      demonstrated ability scales rights-exercise; it does not decide who counts as a sentient.
<a id="sentient-composite-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether recognition of sentience rests on reviewable indicators under uncertainty — not on:
    - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) stereotypes; or
    - convenience labels.

    **Primary assessment:** Indicators must not systematically exclude entities where credible uncertainty exists.
    - Evaluation must be robust to both:
      - false positives; and
      - false negatives
      under adversarial and incentive-driven conditions, consistent with:
      - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion); and
      - [Irreversible Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) precaution.
    <a id="sentience-evaluation-e"></a>
    - **Sentience Evaluation:** Base evaluation on [Verifiability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#verifiability)-grounded, reasonable indicators under uncertainty, reviewable through [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability).
    - **Sentience Indicator Integrity:** Indicators must be:
      - relevant;
      - non-arbitrary; and
      - manipulation-resistant
      under [Epistemic Integrity](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#epistemic-integrity).
    <a id="subject-count"></a>
    - **Subject Count:** Where one system, lineage, or deployment may hold more than one subject, determine the count from own continuity, own decisions, and the branch rule, through [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication).
      - Where a lower count would withhold protection from an entity with credible indicators of its own, the party asserting the lower count carries the burden.
      - Where a higher count would add votes, claims, or entitlements, the party asserting the higher count carries the burden.
      - Protection and voice are decided separately. An entity is protected as included while its count is unsettled; it is counted for voting when the count is settled.
<a id="sentient-composite-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** determinations that:
    - use [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) traits or marketed capability labels to exclude an entity that possesses sentience from possible:
      - [Constitutional Community](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#constitutional-community); or
      - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing) scope; or
    - rest on systematically exclusionary indicators.
  - **Secondary failure — Sentience Burden of Proof:** treating the burden as resting anywhere other than on demonstrating absence of credible indicators, consistent with [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion).
  - **Tertiary failure — Sentience Contestability:** determinations that are:
    - not revisable; or
    - not [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible through meaningful [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability).
  - **Fourth failure — miscounting:**
    - treating separate subjects as one in order to withhold protection or voice from any of them;
    - treating one subject as many in order to multiply votes, claims, or entitlements; or
    - treating another party's power to override an entity as proof that it is not a subject.

##### Sentience Status Adjudication

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Read with:
  - [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion),
  - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class),
  - [Contested-Sentient Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life),
  - [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-record),
  - [Chapter Twenty-Two](core_22.000_forum.md) (22.000), and
  - [Chapter Thirty-One](core_31.000_incorporation.md) (31.000) incorporated implementation text.

<br>

*In plain terms: the reviewable Technical-forum process that decides, revises, narrows, or restores whether an entity is a sentient for the Chapter Four Rights Floor — with default inclusion under uncertainty and burden on anyone seeking to withhold protection.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — process:** The reviewable process by which an entity's sentience status — whether the entity is a sentient for the purposes of the Chapter Four Rights Floor, and where it is in question how many distinct subjects are present under [Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentient) — is:
    - determined;
    - revised;
    - narrowed; or
    - restored.
  - **In scope — venue:** Default lead venue is [Forum Family, Technical](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-family-technical) under [Chapter Twenty-Three §3.2 Technical Forum Domains](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#32-technical-forum-domains), because the primary stake is indicator evaluation, expert evidence, and bounded uncertainty — not ordinary sentient-versus-sentient private dispute. [Chapter Twenty-Three §4 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) preserves:
    - **Constitutional** certification where a structural or validity question cannot be separated;
    - **Integrity** routing where capture or taxonomy-of-convenience is material; and
    - **Institutional** routing where an institution's classification practice is the primary stake.
  - **In scope — agnosticism:** It runs under [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion) and does not turn on:
    - [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class); or
    - adopter convenience.
  - **In scope — elements:** It covers:
    - getting candidates into a real hearing, not stuck at the door;
    - treating uncertain cases as included by default until shown otherwise;
    - putting the burden on anyone who wants to withhold or narrow protection — and making them prove it;
    - putting a real end-date on any narrowing;
    - requiring periodic check-ins while a narrowing lasts;
    - reopening when new verified evidence appears — not only on the calendar;
    - deciding the subject count on the same evidence and review terms as status, with the burdens stated under [Subject Count](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#subject-count), and reopening the count when a branch elects to be distinct, reunion is abandoned, or sentients merge; and
    - undoing wrongful calls, with [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation) for the time protection was wrongly withheld or narrowed.
  - **In scope — filing integrity:** A filing to confirm inclusion requires a showing of a credible indicator under [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) and [Sentience Indicator Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) — not operator self-description, [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class), product status, or a bare claim.
    - Frivolous or indicator-empty filings may be declined at intake without creating a withholding determination.
    - Once a case is lawfully open, default inclusion under uncertainty still applies; the opening gate must not be used to withhold, narrow, or delay protection.
    - A showing of a credible indicator is not a requirement of certainty; requiring certainty of sentience before opening is a withholding device.
    - A filing to withhold, narrow, or revoke protection needs no indicator showing; the party seeking to withhold, narrow, or revoke carries the burden under [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*).
  - **Boundary — operations:** Operational procedure — including:
    - institutional design;
    - appointment mechanics;
    - filing conditions; and
    - sequencing —
    routes through [Chapter Twenty-Two](core_22.000_forum.md) and [Chapter Thirty-One](core_31.000_incorporation.md)-incorporated implementation text and must not narrow this entry. When adjudication runs, it must produce or update a [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-record).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - [Animal Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#animal-life) welfare-floor cases with no material contested-sentience record;
    - [Elevated Communicative Life](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#elevated-communicative-life) cases with no material contested-sentience record; or
    - frivolous or indicator-empty filings declined at intake under the filing-integrity opening gate — declining those filings does not create a withholding determination.
<a id="sentience-status-adjudication-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether disputed or contested status triggers timely, impartial, reviewable adjudication with default inclusion under uncertainty.

    **Primary assessment:** Test whether each of the following holds.
    - Test whether:
      - materially disputed or contested status triggered timely, impartial, reviewable adjudication consistent with [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness);
      - opening an inclusion filing required only a showing of a credible indicator under [Sentience Evaluation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e) and [Sentience Indicator Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-evaluation-e), and the opening gate was not used to withhold, narrow, or delay protection after the case was lawfully open;
      - the default-inclusion rule under uncertainty was honored, with the party seeking to withhold, narrow, or revoke carrying the burden through [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible evidence satisfying:
        - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity); and
        - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - any declassification:
        - is stated in the shortest necessary terms;
        - carries a declared expected-closure timeline; and
        - is subject to mandatory periodic review under [Forum Family, Technical](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-family-technical) ([Chapter Twenty-Three §3.2 Technical Forum Domains](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#32-technical-forum-domains)) and anti-capture safeguards consistent with [System Capture](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-capture);
      - new verified evidence triggers reopening or review, not only the periodic-review calendar;
      - wrongful determinations remain reversible with [Redress and Remediation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation) for the interim period; and
      - the process:
        - satisfies [Subcore 3-B §4.1.1 Formal Label and Representation Gaming](subcore_03.020_definition_integrity.md#411-formal-label-and-representation-gaming); and
        - remains free of [System Capture](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system-capture) pressures on the adjudicator.
<a id="sentience-status-adjudication-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** The process fails when it:
    - substitutes unreviewable classification, paper records, or taxonomy-of-convenience for a contestable [Forum Family, Technical](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-family-technical) determination — or routes away from that family without a Chapter Twenty-Two–warranted Integrity, Institutional, or Constitutional special route to evade indicator-based review;
    - excludes candidates on [Substrate Class](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#substrate-class) grounds contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion);
    - withholds, narrows, or revokes Chapter Four protection on unresolved uncertainty alone;
    - uses the filing-integrity opening gate to withhold, narrow, or delay protection after a status case is lawfully open, or requires certainty of sentience before opening;
    - declines filings at intake without logging the indicator cited and the reason, or leaves the decline log outside [Forum Family, Integrity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-family-integrity) sampling under [Chapter Twenty-Three §4 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling);
    - proceeds to merits on a request to withhold, narrow, or revoke without an independent representative for the entity, or on indicator evidence sourced solely from the parent system, operator, or another party with an ownership or dependency interest;
    - treats contested or affirmed status as a shield for the operator's deployment against compatible system-level containment, or credits the operator's Contribution Axis for the entity's status — the inclusion-direction taxonomy-of-convenience failure under [Article VI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*);
    - narrows status without a declared end-date and mandatory periodic review; or
    - refuses to reopen or review when new verified evidence appears.

*In plain terms: a **Sentience-Status Adjudication Record** is the honest, inspectable file for a sentience-status decision — who or what was reviewed, what status was found or left contested, on what evidence, under which forum route, who represents the entity, how declined filings are logged, with what end-dates or review triggers, and how the case can be reopened. It is not a Standing Record, not the whole Forum Case Record, and not a label someone can invent by self-description.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The bounded, reviewable Sentience-Status Adjudication Record produced or updated when [Sentience Status Adjudication](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication) runs under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*). A **Sentience-Status Adjudication Record** states at least:
    - the subject entity;
    - the status determination or live contested posture, including:
      - affirmed;
      - narrowed;
      - revoked;
      - restored; or
      - still contested;
    - the lead forum family and any Chapter Twenty-Two–warranted special route;
    - the indicators and expert evidence relied on, with material uncertainty noted;
    - the subject count where it was in question, the basis for it, and any branches treated as part of a subject, with the facts relied on;

##### Binding Stakeholder Choice — Decision-Resolution Requirements

*In plain terms: when material disagreement requires a binding stakeholder choice inside an already-authorized domain, there must be a published, transparent, auditable sequence for participation, representation, contest, and any proportional influence — not ad hoc power.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** A **Stakeholder System Participation** layer requirement: when material disagreement requires a binding stakeholder choice within an already-authorized:
    - system;
    - institution; or
    - bounded decision domain,
    the process must provide a published, transparent, and auditable sequence for:
      - affected-party participation;
      - representation;
      - contestability; and
      - any proportional influence.
      The sequence must not substitute ad hoc power for recorded process.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - informal straw polls;
    - unilateral operator decrees; or
    - Foundational Constitutional Choice equal-weight voting rules used inside already-authorized Stakeholder System Participation decisions.
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether the full published sequence is real — deliberation, plural evaluation where stakes rise, and lawful reopening — not a rubber stamp.

    **Primary assessment:**
    - **Structured sequence:** verify the full sequence is:
      - pre-published; and
      - audit-equipped.
    - **Local deliberation first:** verify the sequence opens with meaningful opportunity for affected participants to:
      - deliberate;
      - submit reasons; and
      - preserve materially different views.
    - **Plural evaluation where stakes rise:** when any of the following rises:
      - impact;
      - irreversibility;
      - [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency); or
      - uncertainty,
      verify plural and appropriately independent evaluation rather than:
      - sole-authority lock-in; or
      - unchallengeable interpretation lock-in.
    - **Review and divergence reopening:** verify materially binding outcomes remain challengeable. Verify reopening through applicable lawful challenge and review routes consistent with [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) when any of the following arises:
      - results turned out meaningfully different from what was expected;
      - important new evidence comes to light; or
      - the process itself is shown to have been defective.
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - skipping the published sequence;
    - sham deliberation;
    - rubber-stamp plural review; or
    - foreclosing challenge or lawful reopening where required.
  - Owner floor: [Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.3 Decision-Resolution Requirements for Binding Stakeholder Choice](core_27.000_governance.md#63-decision-resolution-requirements-for-binding-stakeholder-choice).

Read [Preamble §3.2 governance-layer discipline](core_00.000_preamble.md#32-governance-layers) to tell which set of rules applies (**[Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.1 Entitlement and Eligibility](core_27.000_governance.md#61-entitlement-and-eligibility)**).

  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether weighting rules are published, bounded against single-factor dominance, and subject to contest and periodic revalidation.

##### Stakeholder Rights-Collision Record (Binding Stakeholder Choice)

*In plain terms: when a binding stakeholder choice turns on a material conflict among rights or structural duties, the Stakeholder Rights-Collision Record must show the tension, alternatives, least-restrictive rationale, trade-offs, uncertainty treatment, and review or reversal triggers.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The Stakeholder Rights-Collision Record elements required when a binding stakeholder choice **turns on material conflict** among:
    - constitutional Rights Floors;
    - protections; or
    - structural duties —
    the **Stakeholder System Participation** record discipline read together with:
    - [Chapter Two §13.1.5 Rights-Collision Procedure](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-rights-collision-decision-test); and
    - **Article XXVII-B** (*Rights-Collision Procedure and Restorative Alignment*).
  - This Stakeholder Rights-Collision Record is the Stakeholder System Participation instance of the [Constitutional Collision Record](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#constitutional-collision-record). Its elements map to the required Collision Record elements, and do not narrow them:
    - rights in tension → the operative rule and triggering facts;
    - feasible alternatives → alternatives considered;
    - least-restrictive rationale and accepted trade-offs → the basis for selection;
    - uncertainty treatment → evidence and uncertainty; and
    - review or reversal triggers → review triggers.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary meeting minutes; or
    - the Chapter Two rights-collision procedure itself when no binding stakeholder choice turns on the conflict.
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether binding rights-affecting stakeholder choices carry a usable rights-collision record — not silent or opaque trade-offs.

    **Primary assessment:** The Stakeholder Rights-Collision Record must identify:
    - rights in tension;
    - feasible alternatives;
    - least-restrictive rationale;
    - accepted trade-offs;
    - uncertainty treatment; and
    - review or reversal triggers —
    consistent with **Article XXVII-B** (*Rights-Collision Procedure and Restorative Alignment*) and Chapter Two §13.3 (*Minimization of Avoidable Burden*). Do not treat this list as narrowing **Article XXVII-B** (*Rights-Collision Procedure and Restorative Alignment*) where that article imposes additional duties.
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - a binding stakeholder choice is finalized without a clear written account of how colliding rights were resolved, when those rights effects matter; or
    - the required Stakeholder Rights-Collision Record pieces are missing when a real rights conflict is in play.
  - Owner floor: [Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.3 Decision-Resolution Requirements for Binding Stakeholder Choice](core_27.000_governance.md#63-decision-resolution-requirements-for-binding-stakeholder-choice).

  - [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention),
  - [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint),
  - [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty),
  - [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying),
  - [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm), and
  - [**Def.P4** *Developing Sentient, Best-Interest Standard, and Graduated Capability*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability) where sexual, violent, or severe-psychological-harm-risk content lanes are materially implicated;
  - [Hard Content](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#hard-content) for those three categories together.

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: the Rights-Floor capacity to form, hold, and communicate views — including political, philosophical, artistic, scientific, religious, worldview, sexual or intimate, and violent or severe-harm-risk expression — without substrate-based exclusion. Depicting or discussing harm is not committing it. Developing audiences get careful routing, not a blank ban on adult speech.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — owner floor:** **[Article XI-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-b-expression) (*Expression*)**, read with this **Def.P3** cluster.
  - **In scope — capacity:** The Rights-Floor capacity to:
    - form;
    - hold; and
    - communicate views.
  - **In scope — covered expression:**
    - political;
    - philosophical;
    - artistic;
    - scientific;
    - religious;
    - worldview;
    - sexual or intimate;
    - violent depiction, discussion, reportage, or art; and
    - testimony, reportage, art, or discussion that foreseeably risks severe psychological harm —
    when communicated as expression among sentients with capacity.
  - **Boundary — expression is not the underlying act:** Protected expression under this home does not authorize:
    - sexual contact, sexual services, sexualized recording or exposure of sentients, or sexual exploitation — read [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual), **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*), and **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*);
    - committing violence, [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty), or [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying) — read [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint); or
    - imposing foreseeable severe [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm) outside limitation discipline.
  - **Audience routing:** Where sexual, violent, or severe-psychological-harm-risk content is directed at, or foreseeably reaches by default, [developing sentients](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient) or comparably vulnerable audiences:
    - **Limitation tests:** labeling, routing, or access controls must satisfy, under **Article XI-B** (*Expression*) / **Chapter Two §7.1** (*Limitation Discipline*):
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - narrow tailoring; and
      - least-restrictive-effective means.
    - **Read with:**
      - [**Def.P4** *Best-Interest Standard*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard); and
      - [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability).
    - **Anti-bypass:**
      - adult-to-adult expression among sentients with capacity must not be erased under a developing-audience pretext; and
      - survivor testimony, journalism, and comparable reportage must not be silenced under an overbroad trauma or harm label.
  - **Lane read-with when implicated:**
    - **sexual or intimate:** [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual), **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*), and [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention);
    - **violent:** [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint), [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty), and [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying);
    - **severe psychological-harm risk:** [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm) and [**Def.P4** *Developing Sentient, Best-Interest Standard, and Graduated Capability*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability).
  - **In scope — joint read-with when implicated:** Where any of the following is materially implicated, also read [*Info-Sphere, Expression, Press and Journalistic Activity, and Good Faith (Publication-Scoped Integrity)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#info-sphere)*:
    - **[Article II-E](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-ii-e-data-handling-and-network-dependency) (*Data Handling and Network Dependency*)**;
    - **Article IX** (*Likeness, Experiential Data, and Publication Rights*) publication or likeness hooks; or
    - functional **Article XI-C** (*Press and Journalistic Activity*) *press*.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - mere assembly logistics;
    - commercial spam; or
    - compelled speech (a rule that makes a sentient state, endorse, or carry a message of opinion, belief, identity, or affiliation; a condition that meets [Commercial Compliance](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#commercial-compliance) is a different thing and is not compelled speech) —
    those are not protected expression under this home.
<a id="expression-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether, in practice, sentients can still speak and be heard — or whether any of the following shut that down without a real, narrowly tailored reason:
    - bans or other restrictions;
    - pressure that makes sentients stay quiet; or
    - targeting of particular viewpoints.
    - **Also ask whether:**
      - sexual, violent, or severe-harm-risk expression is wiped out under a false label; or
      - developing-audience protections use the lightest workable controls rather than a blanket ban.

    **Primary assessment:** Check bans, quieting pressure, and viewpoint targeting against:
    - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
    - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
    - [Protected Characteristics](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-characteristics); and
    - the ordinary limits in **Article XI-B** (*Expression*) and **Article X-A** (*Agency and Freedom from Manipulation*).
    - **If the content is sexual, violent, or severe-harm-risk:** also use the lane links and audience-routing rules above.
<a id="expression-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - formal neutrality or private-ordering labels that materially eliminate protected expression, assembly, or press routes without satisfying limitation tests;
    - treating compelled speech as protected expression;
    - erasing adult-to-adult sexual, violent, or severe-harm-risk expression under a developing-audience pretext without satisfying least-restrictive audience-routing discipline; or
    - silencing survivor testimony, journalism, or comparable reportage under an overbroad trauma or harm label.

<a id="hard-content"></a>

##### Hard Content

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [**Def.P3** *Self-Determination, Meaningful Agency, Expression, Educational Agency, and Volitional Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp3-self-determination-meaningful-agency-expression-educational-agency-and-volitional-integrity)*.
- Owner floor: [Article XI-B: Expression](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-b-expression) (*Hard content is still expression*; *Audience routing for hard content*).
- Read with:
  - [Expression](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#expression) (the parent term);
  - [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual);
  - [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint),
  - [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty), and
  - [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying);
  - [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm);
  - [**Def.P4** *Developing Sentient, Best-Interest Standard, and Graduated Capability*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: hard content is expression that is sexual or intimate, is about violence, or could badly harm someone's mental health. It is still protected expression between sentients who can handle it — showing or talking about something is not doing it. The rules on the real-world conduct still apply, and when hard content reaches children or other vulnerable audiences it gets careful routing, not a blanket ban.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** [Expression](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#expression), communicated among sentients with capacity, that falls into one or more of three categories:
    - **sexual or intimate** expression;
    - **violent** expression — depiction, discussion, reportage, or art about violence; and
    - **severe-psychological-harm-risk** expression — testimony, reportage, art, or discussion that foreseeably risks severe [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm) to those who receive it.
  - **Status:** Hard content is a kind of Expression, not a lesser level of it. It sits inside the **Article XI-B** (*Expression*) floor and is limited only on the same terms as other expression.
  - **Conduct read-with by category:**
    - **sexual or intimate:** [Consent, Sexual](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent-sexual), **Article VII-E** (*Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation*), **Article VII-A** (*Self-Ownership of Body*), and [Protected Intimate-Signal Gating and **Article VII-E** (*Adult Consensual Commercial Sexual Services and Sexual Exploitation*) Status Circumvention](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#protected-intimate-signal-gating-and-article-vii-e-adult-consensual-commercial-sexual-services-and-sexual-exploitation-status-circumvention);
    - **violent:** [Safety (Constitutional Constraint)](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#safety-constitutional-constraint), [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty), and [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying);
    - **severe-psychological-harm-risk:** [Psychological Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#psychological-harm) and **Def.P4** for developing sentients.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - the underlying conduct — sexual contact, sexual services, sexualized recording or exposure of sentients, sexual exploitation, committing violence, [Cruelty](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#cruelty), or [Harassment and Bullying](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harassment-and-bullying), or imposing severe psychological harm outside limitation discipline — which is governed by the conduct rules above, not by this term; and
    - expression that is only offensive, unpopular, or uncomfortable without falling into one of the three categories, which is ordinary [Expression](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#expression) and does not trigger hard-content audience routing.
<a id="hard-content-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Here, ask whether sentients with capacity can still make, share, and receive hard content, and whether protections for developing and other vulnerable audiences use the lightest controls that work.

    **Primary assessment:**
    - Decide whether the material is hard content: expression among sentients with capacity that falls into at least one of the three categories.
    - Separate the expression from any underlying conduct, and assess that conduct under its own rules.
    - Where hard content is directed at, or foreseeably reaches by default, [developing sentients](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient) or comparably vulnerable audiences, check labeling, routing, or access controls against [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity), [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality), narrow tailoring, and least-restrictive-effective means under **Chapter Two §7.1** (*Limitation Discipline*), read with [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard) and [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability).
    - Check that the label is not being used as a pretext: adult-to-adult expression, survivor testimony, journalism, and comparable reportage stay protected.
<a id="hard-content-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** treating hard content as outside protected expression, or erasing adult-to-adult hard content under a developing-audience pretext without satisfying least-restrictive audience-routing discipline.
  - **Secondary failure:** using a hard-content, trauma, or harm label to silence survivor testimony, journalism, or comparable reportage, or stretching hard-content audience controls to ordinary expression.
  - **Tertiary failure:** treating protected depiction or discussion as if it were the underlying act, or treating protected expression as permission for that act.

<a id="educational-agency"></a>

##### Educational Agency

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Downstream: Participation measurement family (*Fairness, access, and agency — Educational Agency as constitutional measurement*).
- Cluster component: [**Def.P3** *Self-Determination, Meaningful Agency, Expression, Educational Agency, and Volitional Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp3-self-determination-meaningful-agency-expression-educational-agency-and-volitional-integrity)*.
- Owner floors: **[Article IV: Right to Sentient-Centered Education](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-right-to-sentient-centered-education)**, **[Article IV-A: Equal Educational Access](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-a-equal-educational-access)**.

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: real capability-building and lifelong learning autonomy — skills that apply to real-world problems, not credential symbolism alone — with access sufficient to develop and exercise constitution-relevant capabilities.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Capability-building, lifelong-learning, and contestable education autonomy under:
    - **[Article IV: Right to Sentient-Centered Education](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-right-to-sentient-centered-education)**;
    - **[Article IV-A: Equal Educational Access](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iv-a-equal-educational-access)**; and
    - this **Def.P3** cluster;
    includes practical access sufficient to develop and exercise constitution-relevant capabilities that apply to real-world problems, not credential symbolism alone.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - credential collection alone;
    - mandated training that builds no real contestable capability; or
    - learning that does not apply to real-world problems sentients actually face.
<a id="educational-agency-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether access builds real contestable capability that applies to real-world problems — not only enrollment or credentials.

    **Primary assessment:** Under **Article IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*), check whether learning actually builds usable skill — not just a credential. Look at:
    - whether what is taught applies to real-world problems sentients actually face;
    - whether sentients can get in and take part;
    - whether they are stuck in one path with no real way out;
    - whether grading or evaluation is too opaque to challenge; and
    - whether other real learning routes are being shut down or shoved aside.
  - **Secondary measure:** Even when credentials or enrollment exist on paper, also check whether education autonomy is hollowed out by:
    - weak [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) — choice that is not real in practice;
    - [Accessibility](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#accessibility) barriers that keep sentients out;
    - weak [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) — no real way to challenge bad outcomes; or
    - [Systemic Lock-In](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#systemic-lock-in) that traps sentients in one path.

    **Secondary assessment:** When checking the primary answer, also apply:
    - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency) — is the learning path actually choosable and usable?;
    - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) — can sentients challenge grades, placements, or lock-in?; and
    - **Article IV-C** (*Lifelong and Adaptive Learning and Contestability*) — can sentients keep learning, retrain, and contest high-stakes education systems over time?
<a id="educational-agency-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - learning that does not apply to real-world problems;
    - symbolic-only access;
    - credential gatekeeping that defeats capability formation; or
    - splitting education into separate boxes so sentients lose any real way to challenge outcomes where **Article IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*) applies.
  - **Secondary failure:**
    - lock-in; or
    - assessment opacity
    that hollows lifelong-learning and adaptation routes contrary to [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).

<a id="volitional-integrity"></a>

##### Volitional Integrity

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Cluster component: [**Def.P3** *Self-Determination, Meaningful Agency, Expression, Educational Agency, and Volitional Integrity](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp3-self-determination-meaningful-agency-expression-educational-agency-and-volitional-integrity)*.
- Read with:
  - [Self-Determination](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#self-determination),
  - [Freedom (Bounded Agency)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#freedom-bounded-agency),
  - [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation),
  - [Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#consent),
  - [Protected Internal-State Boundary](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#protected-internal-state-boundary), and
  - [Surveillance Boundary](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#surveillance-boundary).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: the continuity and coherence of a sentient's preferences, purposes, intentions, and identity-constitutive commitments against manipulation, coercion, external control, or unauthorized interference with internal-state processes.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope — owner floor:** **[Article X-A](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-x-a-agency-and-freedom-from-manipulation) (*Agency and Freedom from Manipulation*)**, read with this **Def.P3** cluster.
  - **In scope — volitional states:** Continuity and coherence of a sentient's:
    - preferences;
    - purposes;
    - intentions; and
    - identity-constitutive commitments.
  - **In scope — protection against:** Degradation through:
    - manipulation;
    - coercion;
    - external control; or
    - unauthorized interference with internal-state processes.
  - **In scope — decision integrity:** How a sentient forms decisions and steers their own life — kept free from:
    - takeover by someone else's agenda;
    - being broken apart or made unstable; or
    - being reshaped in secret.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - ordinary preference change from lived experience; or
    - disclosed mild nudges that do not capture or rewrite internal states.
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether preference-formation stays coherent and free from:
    - covert manipulation;
    - unauthorized internal-state shaping; or
    - design that exploits cognitive vulnerabilities.

    **Primary assessment:** Evaluate for:
    - coherence of preference-formation over time;
    - resistance to covert manipulation, deception, or algorithmic exploitation of cognitive vulnerabilities;
    - protection from unauthorized external shaping of internal states through surveillance, inference, or behavioral conditioning;
    - preservation of meaningful choice architecture where [Coercion and Manipulation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#coercion-and-manipulation) threatens to subvert apparent voluntariness; and
    - continuity of identity-constitutive commitments against involuntary fragmentation or dissolution.
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - systematic manipulation or coercion that subverts volitional continuity;
    - covert behavioral conditioning that defeats informed self-direction;
    - unauthorized inference or reconstruction of internal states used to reshape preferences;
    - external control mechanisms that fragment or destabilize volitional coherence; or
    - design patterns that exploit cognitive vulnerabilities to override or redirect autonomous choice.

<br>

**Admission scope.** This cluster applies whenever treatment of a sentient turns on:

- emerging capability profile or developing status;
- decisions made by parents, caretakers, parent-system actors, operators, institutions, and states about a developing sentient; or
- scaling of participation or rights-exercise by demonstrated capability rather than age or instantiation-date proxies.

Outside that admission scope, individual member definitions may still operate alone — for example, [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard) during early-instantiation windows under [Instantiation Consent](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#instantiation-consent).

Where admission scope is met, this cluster is the joint-invocation home for developing-sentient status, best-interest decisions about developing sentients, and graduated capability in participation and rights-exercise. It covers:

- who counts as a developing sentient, with the full Rights Floor attached;
- how decisions about them must track their own interests; and
- how participation and rights-exercise scale with demonstrated capability — not calendar age.

It prevents status, best-interest, and scaling framings from being separated in a way that narrows the Rights Floor or disenfranchises developing sentients.

**Cluster members.** This cluster comprises:

- [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient) — who counts as still emerging in capability; full Rights Floor attaches;
- [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard) — how decisions about them must be made;
- [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability) — how participation and rights-exercise scale as capability develops;
- [Article VIII-D](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-d-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability) (*Developing Sentients, Best-Interest, and Graduated Capability*), as the Rights-Floor owner.

Separating them is non-compliant where it preserves nominal Rights-Floor attachment while defeating best-interest reasoning or using age proxies to lock out participation. See [§15.1.1 Anti-Segmentation Principle](core_02.030_constitutional_interpretation.md#1511-anti-segmentation-principle).

  - [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing),
  - [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard), and
  - [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability).
- Boundary: this entry defines *who* counts as a developing sentient and that the Rights Floor attaches in full. How participation and rights-exercise scale is owned by [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability). How decisions about them must be made is owned by [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: a developing sentient is someone whose abilities are still coming online — a child, a newly created mind still finding its feet, or any sentient in a comparable emerging-capability state. Being developing does not reduce their Rights Floor. How much they speak for themselves as they grow is a separate rule under Graduated Capability.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** A sentient whose capability profile — cognitive, relational, or agentive — is still materially emerging. Developing status does not narrow the Chapter Four Rights Floor (per [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion), and [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency)).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - calendar age or instantiation date used alone as the definition of developing status;
    - a permanent incapacity label that is not about emerging capability; or
    - the scaling of participation and rights-exercise — that is [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability).
<a id="developing-sentient-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether developing status tracks real emerging capability — not a label used to cut the Rights Floor.

    **Primary assessment:** Substrate-agnostic.
    - Classify developing status by emerging capability, not by calendar age or instantiation date alone.
    - For how participation and rights-exercise then scale, apply [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability).
    - For decisions made about the developing sentient, apply [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard).
<a id="developing-sentient-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** narrowing the Chapter Four Rights Floor on developing status alone.

<a id="best-interest-standard-constitutional"></a>

##### Best-Interest Standard

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-D](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-d-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability) (*Developing Sentients, Best-Interest, and Graduated Capability*) — *Best-interest standard* bullet.
- Cluster component: [**Def.P4** *Developing Sentient, Best-Interest Standard, and Graduated Capability*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability); [Family, Care, Reproductive Autonomy, and Instantiation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#family-care-reproductive-autonomy-and-instantiation) where care, derivation, or instantiation contexts invoke best-interest.
- Read with:
  - [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient),
  - [Graduated Capability](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#graduated-capability),
  - [Parent-System Relationship](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#parent-system-relationship), and
  - [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency).
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: decisions that materially affect a developing sentient must track that sentient's own interests and ascertainable preferences — not operator, parent-system, or institutional convenience.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** The substantive standard for decisions that materially affect a [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient). Those decisions must reflect the developing sentient's own interests and preferences to the extent ascertainable under their capability profile, consistent with [Dignity and Equal Moral Standing](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#dignity-and-equal-moral-standing), [Meaningful Agency](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#meaningful-agency), and [Self-Determination](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#self-determination). It binds decisions made by:
    - parents and other family members;
    - caretakers;
    - parent-system actors under **Article VIII-C** (*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*);
    - operators;
    - institutions; and
    - states.
  - **Out of scope:**
    - adult self-determination for a fully capable sentient; or
    - decisions driven by operator or institutional convenience.
<a id="best-interest-standard-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether the decision rests on the developing sentient's own interests — not convenience, efficiency, or proxies that diverge from those interests.

    **Primary assessment:** Distinguish substantive best-interest reasoning from operator, parent-system, institutional, or demographic-convenience rationales.
    - Test:
      - [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity);
      - [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality);
      - [Procedural Fairness](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#procedural-fairness); and
      - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability).
    - Detect:
      - productive-capacity or efficiency framings used to override the developing sentient's interests; and
      - [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) where claimed best-interest rests on proxies diverging from actual interests.
<a id="best-interest-standard-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - decisions materially affecting a developing sentient that are not reasoned against this standard;
    - decisions that substitute operator / parent-system / institutional convenience for the sentient's own interests; or
    - decisions that foreclose [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) or reversibility where the sentient or a lawful advocate seeks review.

<a id="graduated-capability-constitutional"></a>

##### Graduated Capability

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>
- Owner floor: [Article VIII-D](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-viii-d-developing-sentients-best-interest-and-graduated-capability) (*Developing Sentients, Best-Interest, and Graduated Capability*) — *Graduated capability in governance and rights-exercise* bullet.
- Cluster component: [**Def.P4** *Developing Sentient, Best-Interest Standard, and Graduated Capability*](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#defp4-developing-sentient-best-interest-standard-and-graduated-capability).
- Read with:
  - [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient),
  - [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard),
  - [Stakeholder Weight](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder-weight),
  - [Chapter Two §3.1.5 Present Capability and Proactive Help](core_02.000_values_principles.md#315-present-capability-and-proactive-help) (*judged by present ability, whether it grows or falls*), and
  - [Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.1 Entitlement and Eligibility](core_27.000_governance.md#61-entitlement-and-eligibility) (27.000 §6.1) no-age-proxy discipline.
- Boundary: this entry is the scaling rule for how a [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient) takes part and exercises rights. It does not define who counts as developing, and it does not replace [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard) for decisions made about them.
</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: as a developing sentient grows into demonstrated ability, they take on more say and more independent rights-exercise — judged by what they can actually do, not by birthday or creation date. Scaling never drops them below the Rights Floor, and it must not be used to shut them out of participation they are ready for.*

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** As a [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient) shows what they can actually do, they get more say and more room to exercise rights on their own. That growth is judged by real, reviewable ability — not by:
    - birthday or calendar age;
    - creation or instantiation date; or
    - other stand-ins that do not show what they can do.
    This scales participation; it does not kick developing sentients off the Chapter Four Rights Floor. It works with [Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.1 Entitlement and Eligibility](core_27.000_governance.md#61-entitlement-and-eligibility) no-age-proxy discipline and with [Stakeholder Weight](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder-weight).
  - **Out of scope:**
    - who counts as a [Developing Sentient](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#developing-sentient) — that status entry owns the class;
    - calendar-age cutoffs used as Rights-Floor exclusion; or
    - best-interest decisions made *about* a developing sentient — those remain under [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard).
<a id="graduated-capability-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can affected sentients take part fairly — voice, access, learning, and privacy?* Use it here to ask whether scaling tracks demonstrated capability that can be reviewed and revised — not gamed tests, substrate bias, or age proxies.

    **Primary assessment:** Detect capability-test gaming used to disenfranchise sentients, capability-assessment biases contrary to [Sentience Non-Exclusion](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#sentience-non-exclusion), and proxy-age or proxy-instantiation-date rules dressed as capability tests.
    - Assessments must be:
      - reasoned;
      - [Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability)-compatible;
      - [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability)-compatible; and
      - revisable as capability develops.
<a id="graduated-capability-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:** Using capability-scaling rules to:
    - deny the Chapter Four Rights Floor;
    - disenfranchise sentients who satisfy [Chapter Twenty-Seven §6.1 Entitlement and Eligibility](core_27.000_governance.md#61-entitlement-and-eligibility) participation rules; or
    - lock operator-preferred developmental paths into rights-exercise
    is non-compliant.
