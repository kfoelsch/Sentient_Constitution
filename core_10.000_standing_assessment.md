# CHAPTER TEN: STANDING ASSESSMENT

> This file is **part of the Sentient Constitution** and is **binding only together** with the other numbered `core_*` files read as one text. It explains the three questions of standing assessment. The detailed rules in Subcores 10-A through 10-F bind with this chapter.

<br>

Chapter Ten is the constitutional owner of **standing assessment**: verified event records, contribution and violation measurement, and integration of those violation findings for the next decision.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

**Purpose:** Answer, in order, **What happened? How good or bad was it? What follows?**

**Role:** This chapter governs standing assessment and its handoff to the separate consequence rules.

**Chapter scope:** Standing is not a rating of inherent worth. Its Standing Record and measurement rules distinguish allegations from verified violation findings and govern what evidence may enter each question.

### 2. Why Standing Assessment Begins with Verified Events

**Grounded in:**
- The event record identifies events, evidence, opening authority, custody, versions, and handling, with applicable verification and challenge rights.
- Allegations remain distinct from verified violation findings.
- Only evidence admitted under the verification rules may enter measurement.

Event types and fields are organized in [Subcore 10-A](subcore_10.010_standing_record_event_types_fields_custody.md); verification and Standing Record rules are in [Subcore 10-B](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md).

### 3. How Good or Bad Was It?

Contribution and violation are measured on separate axes; neither offsets the other. A verified-input gate controls what may be measured. Facts or measurements must not be changed to reach a preferred result. The dimensions, descriptors, route inputs, and tables are in [Subcore 10-C](subcore_10.030_route_descriptor_constitutional_input_tables.md) and [Subcore 10-D](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md). Measurement by itself does not authorize a liberty restriction, and applicable locks control blocked routes.

### 4. What Follows?

Integration begins Question 3; it does not answer every downstream consequence question. The integration record, processing order, and handoff rules are in [Subcore 10-E](subcore_10.050_integration_record_processing_order.md) and [Subcore 10-F](subcore_10.060_standing_integration.md). Consequence rules remain in their own chapters, including [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md) and [Chapter Thirteen](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md).

### 5. Continuity and Review

A manual continuity process and later reconciliation preserve the applicable fields, order, evidence custody, review rights, time limits, and audit trail. The detailed integration provisions govern those steps and any required review.
