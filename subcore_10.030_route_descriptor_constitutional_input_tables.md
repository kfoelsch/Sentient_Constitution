### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 10-C is the constitutional owner of the **tables** Question 2 works from: the catalog of benefit-route descriptors and the catalog of harm-route descriptors, each in five domains, with what each descriptor measures; and the tables that route a measurement to the definitions, principles, and Chapter Four Articles that bear on it. [Subcore 10-D](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#subcore-10-d-standing-measurement--question-2-how-good-or-bad-was-it) states the rules for using them: descriptors stack and rank below the slot, the catalogs are a base and not the full set, every applicable source must be identified, and none of this reopens Question 1 or merges the axes. Where a line there and the text here seem to differ, the reading more protective of the affected sentient controls.

*In plain terms: Chapter Fifteen says how to measure how good or bad something was. This file holds the reference tables used along the way: the named kinds of help and harm, and the constitutional sources to check.*

### 2. Why the Tables Are the Same for Every Adopter

*In plain terms: if each adopter named kinds of help and harm differently, the same act would be measured differently from place to place.*

Standing Records move between adopters, and effects on standing are drawn from them. A shared vocabulary of benefit and harm routes, and a shared map of which constitutional sources to check, let a measurement made in one place be read and contested in another. Adopters may map their own identifiers onto the catalogs, but may not replace them with a taxonomy that forces a Standing Record into one category.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad): the legs this file carries:
    - [Subcore 3-D — Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability): every measurement names the route and the sources it rests on;
    - [Subcore 3-G — Oversight](subcore_03.055_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight): reviewers can check a measurement against the same tables everywhere;
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): both aims, which this file serves:
    - [Flourishing](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing): care, repair, teaching, and agency are named as routes of benefit;
    - [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity): ecology and diligence are a domain of their own on both axes;
- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§13.1 Core Tradeoff Principles](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#131-core-tradeoff-principles);
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XX](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xx-standing-and-participation-status) (*Standing and Participation Status*).

### 3. Contribution Route Descriptors

*In plain terms: the named kinds of verified help, in five domains, and what each one measures in Question 2.*

These tables are used under [Subcore 10-D §5.1 Contribution Route Descriptor Catalog and Measurement Roles](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#51-contribution-route-descriptor-catalog-and-measurement-roles).

<a id="31-bodily-safety-relationships-care-and-agency"></a>

#### 3.1 Bodily Safety, Relationships, Care, and Agency

| **Benefit-route descriptor** | **What it measures in Question 2** |
| --- | --- |
| **Peace of body, mind, and/or community** | Benefit from bodily, psychological, and community calm. It adjusts how much the benefit matters (its materiality). |
| **Reliable Relationships** | Benefit from relationships that can be trusted, including those built on a duty of trust, in settings where the two sides are unequal. |
| **Caretaking and Dependency Support** | Verified care for sentients who depend on others. Informal care counts when it can be shown. |
| **Access and Inclusion** | Benefit from real participation and accessibility. It adjusts how much the benefit matters in settings where one side depends heavily on the other. |

| --- | --- |

| **Resourceful Stewardship** | Careful care of material resources and dependable sharing out (allocation) of them. |

<a id="33-knowledge-education-records-and-discovery"></a>

| **STEM and Discovery Contribution** | Discoveries that others can reproduce, and research that makes things safer. |

<a id="4-violation-route-descriptors"></a>
### 4. Violation Route Descriptors

*In plain terms: the named kinds of verified harm, in the same five domains, and what each one measures in Question 2.*

These tables are used under [Subcore 10-D §5.2 Violation Route Descriptor Catalog and Measurement Roles](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#52-violation-route-descriptor-catalog-and-measurement-roles).

<a id="41-bodily-safety-relationships-care-and-agency"></a>

#### 4.1 Bodily Safety, Relationships, Care, and Agency

| **Harm-route descriptor** | **What it measures in Question 2** |
| --- | --- |
| **Violent Misconduct** | Violence, coercion, or danger to liberty is recorded separately from the LEQU impact slot. It may require heightened safeguards. |
| **Interpersonal / Care Duty Misconduct** | The harm matters more (higher materiality) where one side depends heavily on the other. |
| **Accessibility and Participation-Barrier Misconduct** | The harm matters more (higher materiality) where one side depends heavily on the other, and aggravating features may stack on it. |
| **Privacy and Self-Ownership Misconduct** | Aggravating features apply when concealment or exploitation is verified, and they may stack. |
| **Agency and Manipulation Misconduct** | Coercion, manipulation, or danger to liberty is recorded separately from the impact slot. |

<a id="42-repair-property-and-resources"></a>

#### 4.2 Repair, Property, and Resources

| **Harm-route descriptor** | **What it measures in Question 2** |
| --- | --- |
| **Remedially correctable misconduct** | Records its correction character (whether and how the harm can be put right). The slot follows the total LEQU loss. |
| **Property Misconduct** | The route for loss of property. The slot follows the total LEQU loss. |

<a id="43-knowledge-education-records-and-discovery"></a>

#### 4.3 Knowledge, Education, Records, and Discovery

| **Harm-route descriptor** | **What it measures in Question 2** |
| --- | --- |
| **Info-Sphere Misconduct** | Damage to Safety or Truth. Concealment and deception stay separate facts about how the harm was caused (its character). |
| **Educational Misconduct** | Harm to learning or to sentients' ability to act for themselves (their capabilities). The slot follows the total LEQU loss. |
| **Research and Discovery Misconduct** | Harm to research or discovery. The slot follows the total LEQU loss. |

<a id="44-systems-and-constitutional-operation"></a>

#### 4.4 Systems and Constitutional Operation

| **Harm-route descriptor** | **What it measures in Question 2** |
| --- | --- |
| **System Misconduct** | Diffusion (responsibility spread across actors) and structural enablement are recorded separately. |
| **Accountability Obstruction Misconduct** | Covers obstructing accountability: getting in the way of a harm being answered for. It is recorded as a way the harm happened. How the obstruction was carried out (for example, concealment or negligence) is recorded as a separate fact. When the slot is among the highest (see [Subcore 10-D §6.1 Table 1 — Slot Display Labels](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#61-table-1--slot-display-labels)), the case may also be recorded as **Anti-Constitutional Misconduct** so it can be sent to [Subcore 3-J — Anti-Constitutional Misconduct Review](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#anti-constitutional-misconduct-review) in Chapter Twenty. Being sent there is not the same as being designated for anti-constitutional misconduct: Chapter Twenty decides that separately. |
| **Exit and Lock-In Misconduct** | Carries the features of System Misconduct, plus diffusion or capture when these are structural (built into how the system works). |
| **Lifecycle and Irreversibility Misconduct** | Irreversibility affects the size of the LEQU loss. Concealment or negligence stays separately recorded as character. |
| **Anti-Constitutional Misconduct** (descriptor) | Sends a violation in the highest slots (see [Subcore 10-D §6.1 Table 1 — Slot Display Labels](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#61-table-1--slot-display-labels)) to Chapter Twenty designation review without changing its impact slot. |

<a id="45-ecology-and-diligence"></a>

#### 4.5 Ecology and Diligence

| **Harm-route descriptor** | **What it measures in Question 2** |
| --- | --- |
| **Ecological Misconduct** | The LEQU loss is weighted by the loss of [Subcore 3-K — Ecological Recovery Capacity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#ecological-recovery-capacity) (an ecosystem's ability to regenerate after harm). It may stack with the violent route (Violent Misconduct). |
| **Negligent Misconduct** | Duty, foreseeability, and preventability are recorded separately from the impact slot. |

<a id="5-constitutional-input-tables"></a>
### 5. Constitutional Input Tables

*In plain terms: the definitions, principles, and Articles to check before a slot is chosen, and what each does on each axis.*

These tables are used under [Subcore 10-D §7 Constitutional Inputs to Axis Assignment](subcore_10.040_standing_measurement.md#7-constitutional-inputs-to-axis-assignment). They are non-exhaustive, each linked canonical home controls, and the application limits there apply.

<a id="51-definition-inputs"></a>

#### 5.1 Definition Inputs

| Canonical source | Contribution Axis role | Violation Axis role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Subcore 3-J — Standing State, Contribution, and Violation](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#defa3-standing-state-contribution-and-violation) | Explains what counts as a contribution and which checked facts can be used to measure it. It also makes sure a contribution Standing Record tracks only the good side, never mixed with the bad. | Explains what counts as a violation and how it is recorded. It also sends the most serious cases to Anti-Constitutional Misconduct Review, a separate review for conduct that undermines the Constitution itself. |
| [Subcore 3-D — Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability) | Checks whether a claim of good stewardship or repair really keeps someone answerable. It must still show who did what, who has to answer for it, and how it can be reviewed, made right, and corrected, in proportion to its impact. | Finds cases where it is unclear who did something or who must answer for it. This includes cases where responsibility stops working after the organization or its structure changes. |
| [Subcore 3-J — Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#harm) | Harm that is pushed onto others or hidden can limit or cancel a claimed benefit. Repairing harm can count as a benefit, but only when the repair is verified and independently shown. | Spells out the kinds of damage that count when measuring loss and severity: direct, indirect, delayed, cumulative, spread across systems, and harm to a sentient's mind or emotional wellbeing. |
| [Subcore 3-M — Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#materiality) and [Subcore 3-N — Lifespan Equivalent Unit (LEQU)](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#lifespan-equivalent-unit-lequ) | Set how much a verified benefit counts, based on how widely it reaches, how vulnerable those affected are, and how long it lasts. The result is measured in LEQU, the shared yardstick for a full life's worth of benefit. | Set how much a verified loss or danger counts, based on how widely it reaches, how vulnerable those affected are, how long it lasts, and how hard it is to undo. The result is measured in LEQU, the same yardstick used for a full life's worth of loss. |
| [Subcore 3-J — Contribution Nature](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contribution-nature) | Decides whether something counts as a contribution. It must be good only, never mixed with harm, and backed by a verified contribution record that can be shown. Without both, it cannot get a Contribution Axis assignment. | Has no role here. It does not decide what counts as harm, and it never lowers how serious a violation is. |
| [Subcore 3-J — Violation Nature](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#violation-nature) | Has no role here. A violation is never counted as a negative contribution, and it does not wipe out verified benefit that has nothing to do with it. | Decides whether something counts as a violation. It must be classed as harmful and backed by a verified finding that a violation occurred. Without both, it cannot get a Violation Axis assignment. |
| [Subcore 3-L — Constitutional Constraint](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#constitutional-constraint), [Subcore 3-L — Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#authority-stack-and-internal-hierarchy), and [Subcore 3-M — Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability) | Stop a claimed benefit from counting when it depends on defeating the Constitution, dodging its rules, shrinking the Rights Floor (the minimum protections the Constitution guarantees), or complying on paper only. | Flag what affects how serious a violation really is: breaking a constitutional limit that matters, putting something above the Constitution's authority, evading the rules, or making them impossible to enforce. |
| [Subcore 3-M — Auditability](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#auditability) and [Subcore 3-J — Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) | Require that a claimed benefit can be shown and checked, and that anyone who disagrees has a working way to challenge it. | Require findings that can be checked and challenged. An accusation, or a label with no explanation, cannot set how serious a violation is. |

<a id="52-principle-inputs"></a>

#### 5.2 Principle Inputs

| Principle source | Axis-assignment role |
| --- | --- |
| [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad), [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims), and [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake) | Set the four working disciplines (participation, oversight, accountability, and timeliness), the two outcomes the Constitution aims for (Flourishing and Continuity), and the frame that sizes every assignment to what is at stake. |
| [Wellbeing](core_02.000_values_principles.md#3-foundational-objective-wellbeing-flourishing-aim) and [Recognition, Reinforcement, and Aspiration](core_02.000_values_principles.md#32-recognition-reinforcement-and-aspiration) | Anchor benefit that respects rights, and recognition that fits it, on the Contribution Axis. Prestige or rewards are never treated as proof of a contribution. |
| [Safety](core_02.000_values_principles.md#4-safety-harm-constraint) and [Truth](core_02.000_values_principles.md#5-truth-epistemic-integrity-constraint) | Set binding limits on both axes. Benefit gained through harm or deception does not count in full, and a verified failure of safety or truth can show how large, or what kind, of violation occurred. |
| [Stewardship in Depth](core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#16-stewardship-in-depth) and [Governance under Stewardship Discipline](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#18-governance-under-stewardship-discipline) | Guide how to weigh lasting contribution, duties that come with a role, institutional repair, supervision, and responsibility, whenever the verified facts call for them. |
| [Core Tradeoff Principles](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#131-core-tradeoff-principles) and [Constitutional Floors, Safety, and Anti-Degrading Process](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1314-constitutional-floors-safety-and-anti-degrading-process) | Apply when the conduct being measured involves necessity, keeping harm as small as possible, proportionality, a clash between rights, or a restriction. They never authorize a consequence on their own. |

<a id="53-rights-floor-and-article-inputs"></a>

#### 5.3 Rights-Floor and Article Inputs

| Article source | Axis-assignment role or boundary |
| --- | --- |
| The factually applicable [Chapter Four Rights Floor](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#chapter-four-foundational-rights), including every Article materially implicated by the verified Standing Record | Sets the basic protections, interests, duties, benefits, or violations at stake. No Article is left out just because it isn't listed below. |
| [Article XX — Standing and Participation Status](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xx-standing-and-participation-status) | Keeps these separate: a sentient's standing, dignity, the minimum protections of the Rights Floor, stakeholder status, reputation, and any later change to participation status. |
| [Article XIV-A — Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-a-reliability-and-trustworthiness-baseline) and [Article XIV-B — Right to Redress and Remedy](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy), [Article XVI-B — Transparency, Auditability, and Contestability](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xvi-b-transparency-auditability-and-contestability), and [Article XVII — Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xvii-audit-transparency-and-independent-verification) | Apply when the verified benefit or violation involves keeping honest records, sharing information, checking the facts, being able to challenge a decision, or fixing mistakes. |
| [Article XXV — Root Cause Analysis and Adaptive Response](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxv-root-cause-analysis-and-adaptive-response) | Applies when it matters what caused the conduct, whether it was corrected, whether it keeps happening, or whether the response adapted, because that shows how the conduct stands under the Constitution. |
| [Article XXI — Justice After Verified Violation](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxi-justice-after-verified-violation) and [Article XXVII-C — Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) | Apply to any process or delay the facts actually involve. **Article XXI** (*Justice After Verified Violation*) mainly limits what happens after a violation is measured (Question 3). It must not be used to raise or lower the Question 2 slot just to justify a response someone already prefers. |
