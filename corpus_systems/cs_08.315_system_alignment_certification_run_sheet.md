Core home: 08

This file is the systems implementation home for **CS-8.315** (*System alignment certification run sheet*).

*In plain terms: **CS-8.315** (*System alignment certification run sheet*) is a checklist for certification, from classifying the system to monitoring it once it is live. Each step points to its constitutional source. The checklist adds no duty and changes none.*

<a id="cs-8315-1-purpose-role-and-scope"></a>
**Purpose and role:** This procedural aid follows System Alignment Certification from framing through operation. [Chapter Eight](../core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eight-system-alignment-certification--evaluation) owns evaluation; [Chapter Nine](../core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes) owns forum review and outcomes; and [Chapter Ten](../core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-ten-system-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing) owns the System Certification Record, recertification, challenge, and standing bridge. [Chapters Eleven through Thirteen](../core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eleven-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation) govern Institutional Alignment Certification. The linked source controls if a summary differs.

The six phases are **I Frame**, **II Evaluate**, **III Review**, **IV Decide**, **V Record**, and **VI Operate**. The process chart in [Chapter Eight §2.2 Certification at a Glance](../core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#22-certification-at-a-glance) shows the same flow.

| 9 | Publish the completed System Certification Record and open its challenge path (already open for an operating system). Before a first go-live or material expansion, satisfy the notice interval, independently verified prerequisites, and applicable stays or reliance limits. | [Chapter Nine §4.1 Go-Live Gate](../core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#41-go-live-gate), [§5 Challenging a Certification](../core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-challenging-a-certification), [§5.1 When the Path Opens](../core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#51-when-the-path-opens), [§5.2 Contestability Paths](../core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths) |
| 10 | Monitor outcomes and respond to incidents and challenges; protect affected sentients, correct defects, and obtain independent verification before authorization, fulfillment of or release from a certification condition. Recertify on the system class and status cadence or reopen on a material trigger; each certification pass opens a new linked System Certification Record. | [Chapter Ten §4 Recertification and Reopening](../core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#4-recertification-and-reopening), [§4.4 Non-Evasion](../core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#44-non-evasion) |

| **For an Institution** | | |
| 12 | Run the same phases for the Institution: classify it by what it decides and holds, evaluate it on the eight institutional items (including the standing and authenticity of the holders of its covered offices), cite each System's System Certification Record without redoing it, and carry a failed or lapsed System certification into its status by the cascade rule. On a material governance violation (a determination that its governance failed, a denial or revocation on a governance ground, or an institutional cause of a System's misalignment), reopen the review of each of its Systems. Where it is found misaligned, assess willingness and capacity to correct it and apply the Institutional denial and revocation rules. No step is a licence to form or run the Institution. | [Chapters Eleven to Thirteen, Institutional Alignment Certification](../core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eleven-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation) |
