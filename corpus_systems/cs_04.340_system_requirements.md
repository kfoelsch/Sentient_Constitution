Core home: 04

This file is the systems implementation home for **CS-4.340** (*System requirements*). Design, testing, verification, and deployment are in **CS-4.345** (*System lifecycle*), in [cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md](cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md).

*In plain terms: **CS-4.340** (*System requirements*) is a pointer catalog of what an in-scope system must let sentients do or inspect — identity control, export, Type O inspect, challenge entry, comprehensibility, digital self-service when entry was already digital — without turning those duties into a product list, and without taking standing, forum, voting, or remedy away from their owners.*

<a id="cs-4340-1-purpose-role-and-scope"></a>
**Purpose and role:** This file catalogs the capabilities that in-scope systems must make reachable and points readers to the rules that govern each capability. It does not prescribe particular applications.

**What this file owns:**

- the systems-layer catalog of **user-reachable capability surfaces** for in-scope systems — who can do or inspect a named act, and which owner already states the duty;
- the channel-class rule in [§3 Analog, Digital, and Channel Class](#cs-4340-3-analog-digital-and-channel-class): analog remains lawful unless the cited owner already requires a digital or machine-usable channel;
- the negative duties in [§4 What This File Does Not Own](#cs-4340-4-what-this-file-does-not-own): do not invent a standing portal, a federated Type O directory, or a unified subject-access product that defeats Chapter Fourteen silence.

**What this file does not own:**

- lifecycle engineering in **[CS-4.345](cs_04.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md#cs-4345-system-lifecycle) (*System lifecycle*)** as a whole;
- information typing and identity / export mechanics in **CS-4.305 (*Information types and handling*) and CS-4.310 (*Data Classifications*) — Data classifications**;
- classification disclosure and challenge in **CS-8.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-8.310 (*System Impact Classifications*) — System impact classifications**;
- inspectable attributable action and chokepoint continuity in **CS-26.305 — Critical system stewardship**;
- comprehensibility and complexity stewardship in **CS-4.315** (*Comprehensibility and Complexity Stewardship*);
- shared digital self-service and exit-integrity terms in **CJS-3.130 §17** (*Continuity: Interoperability, Portability, and Exit-Integrity Terms*) and institutional supervision in **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*);
- the challenge, review, and redress floor in **Article XIV-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*);
- Standing Records, forum dockets, collective choice, and remedy offices — those remain in Chapters Eight through Twenty-Six, **CF**, and **CI**.

This file implements the **CS-4.345** (*System lifecycle*) rule that the constitution defines required capabilities and outcomes, and that specific technical implementations may evolve, provided they remain auditable. It is a catalog of surfaces, not a software list and not a second home for **CF-22.465** (*Standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts*) or **CJS-3.130 §17** (*Continuity: Interoperability, Portability, and Exit-Integrity Terms*).

Where this file is silent, Sentient Constitution Chapter Three, including its subcore, governs. Where this file and the joint-structure family conflict, the applicable requirement with the [Fullest Protective Effect](../subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#fullest-protective-effect) governs.

## CS-4.340 §3 Analog, Digital, and Channel Class

*In plain terms: paper, hearing, notice, or manual continuity can satisfy unless the owner already requires a digital or machine-usable channel — and digital self-service must not make exit harder than the channel that was actually used.*

This constitution defines required capabilities and outcomes. Analog forms — paper, hearing, published notice, and manual continuity — satisfy a catalog row unless the cited owner already requires a digital or machine-usable channel for that act.

**CJS-3.130 §17** (*Continuity: Interoperability, Portability, and Exit-Integrity Terms*) and **CI-4.205 §3** (*Digital Self-Service Pathway Integrity*) apply when entry was already digital. They require meaningful management and exit in the same channel class where feasible, subject to the shared rule’s narrow documented exceptions and billing protections where applicable. They do **not** force a digital channel onto a system that never used one.

**CS-4.330 §5** (*Offline Audit Integrity and Reconciliation*) requires tamper-evident offline chains with integrity proofs and reconciliation where that section applies. Schema validators and other machine-checkable forms in `implementation/` instantiate owners already in this catalog; they are process support and cannot narrow the owner.

Do not treat “user-facing” as “must be an app.”


## CS-4.340 §4 What This File Does Not Own

*In plain terms: standing, forum dockets, voting, and remedy stay where they already live; do not build a portal that quietly opens a Standing Record or pretends to be a second forms book.*

**Constitutional-operating surfaces** — Standing Records, forum intake and standard records, collective choice, seat catalogs, and remedy offices — are **not** owned here. They remain with Chapters Eight through Twenty-Six, **CF**, and **CI**. Integrator inventory of those surfaces (process support; cannot narrow; not a second **CF-22.465** (*Standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts*)): [`evidence/2026-09-10/cs_user_facing_software_audit.md`](../evidence/2026-09-10/cs_user_facing_software_audit.md).

**Silence default.** Do not invent a standing portal for everyone. [Chapter Fourteen §4.2 Silence Is the Default](../core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#42-silence-is-the-default) — silence is the default; most sentients never have a Standing Record. A filed case is not standing by itself. Tools under [Chapter Fourteen §6.5 Implementation Tools](../core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#65-implementation-tools) are optional and must not bury required fields in a score.

**No unified subject-access product.** Integrators may compose existing surfaces (identity control, inspect-if-open Standing Records, **Article IX-B** (*Experiential and Derived Data Rights*) experiential and derived data). Composition must **not** defeat the Chapter Fourteen silence default.

**Not a software list.** This family catalogs user-reachable in-scope surfaces and nothing more. Constitutional-operating surfaces stay with their existing owners. Do not extend it into a list of required software.
