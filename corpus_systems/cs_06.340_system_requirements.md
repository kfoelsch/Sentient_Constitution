Core home: 06

This file is the systems implementation home for **CS-6.340** (*System requirements*). Design, testing, verification, and deployment are in **CS-6.345** (*System lifecycle*), in [cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md](cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md).

*In plain terms: **CS-6.340** (*System requirements*) is a pointer catalog of what an in-scope system must let sentients do or inspect — identity control, export, Type O inspect, challenge entry, comprehensibility, digital self-service when entry was already digital — without turning those duties into a product list, and without taking standing, forum, voting, or remedy away from their owners.*

## CS-6.340 §1 Purpose and role

*In plain terms: this file names the reachable surfaces an in-scope system must expose, then points at the homes that already own the rules; it does not invent apps.*

**What this file owns**

- the systems-layer catalog of **user-reachable capability surfaces** for in-scope systems — who can do or inspect a named act, and which owner already states the duty;
- the channel-class rule in [§3 Analog, digital, and channel class](#cs-6340-3-analog-digital-and-channel-class): analog remains lawful unless the cited owner already requires a digital or machine-usable channel;
- the negative duties in [§4 What this file does not own](#cs-6340-4-what-this-file-does-not-own): do not invent a standing portal, a federated Type O directory, or a unified subject-access product that defeats Chapter Ten silence.

**What this file does not own**

- lifecycle engineering in **[CS-6.345](cs_06.345_design_testing_verification_deployment.md) (*System lifecycle*)** as a whole;
- information typing and identity / export mechanics in **CS-6.305 (*Information types and handling*) and CS-6.310 — Information types and handling**;
- classification disclosure and challenge in **CS-9.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-9.310 — System classification and handling**;
- inspectable attributable action and chokepoint continuity in **CS-14.305 — Critical system stewardship**;
- comprehensibility and complexity stewardship in **CS-6.315** (*Comprehensibility and complexity stewardship*);
- shared digital self-service and exit-integrity terms in **CJS-5.130 §17** (*Continuity: interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*) and institutional supervision in **CI-6.205 §3** (*Digital self-service pathway integrity*);
- the challenge, review, and redress floor in **Article XIII-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*);
- standing records, forum dockets, collective choice, and remedy offices — those remain in Chapters Nine through Fourteen, **CF**, and **CI**.

This file implements the **CS-6.345 §1** (*Purpose and role*) rule that the constitution defines required capabilities and outcomes, and that specific technical implementations may evolve, provided they remain auditable. It is a catalog of surfaces, not a software list and not a second home for **CF-13.465** (*Standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts*) or **CJS-5.130 §17** (*Continuity: interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*).

Where this file is silent, Sentient Constitution Chapters Two through Five govern. Where this file and `corpus_joint_structure.md` conflict, the applicable requirement with the [Fullest Protective Effect](../core_05.045_band_integrative.md#fullest-protective-effect) governs.

## CS-6.340 §3 Analog, digital, and channel class

*In plain terms: paper, hearing, notice, or manual continuity can satisfy unless the owner already requires a digital or machine-usable channel — and digital self-service must not make exit harder than the channel that was actually used.*

This constitution defines required capabilities and outcomes. Analog forms — paper, hearing, published notice, and manual continuity — satisfy a catalog row unless the cited owner already requires a digital or machine-usable channel for that act.

**CJS-5.130 §17** (*Continuity: interoperability, portability, and exit-integrity terms*) and **CI-6.205 §3** (*Digital self-service pathway integrity*) apply when entry was already digital. They require same-channel-class self-service and charge-exit on that channel. They do **not** force a digital channel onto a system that never used one.

**CS-6.330 §5** (*Offline audit integrity and reconciliation*) requires tamper-evident offline chains where that section applies. Schema validators and other machine-checkable forms in `implementation/` instantiate owners already in this catalog; they are process support and cannot narrow the owner.

Do not treat “user-facing” as “must be an app.”


## CS-6.340 §4 What this file does not own

*In plain terms: standing, forum dockets, voting, and remedy stay where they already live; do not build a portal that quietly opens a standing record or pretends to be a second forms book.*

**Constitutional-operating surfaces** — standing records, forum intake and standard records, collective choice, seat catalogs, and remedy offices — are **not** owned here. They remain with Chapters Nine through Fourteen, **CF**, and **CI**. Integrator inventory of those surfaces (process support; cannot narrow; not a second **CF-13.465** (*Standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts*)): [`evidence/2026-09-10/cs_user_facing_software_audit.md`](../evidence/2026-09-10/cs_user_facing_software_audit.md).

**Silence default.** Do not invent a standing portal for everyone. [Chapter Ten §2.1 Silence is the default](../core_10.000_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default) — silence is the default; most sentients never have a standing record. A filed case is not standing by itself. Tools under [Chapter Ten §3.5 Implementation tools](../core_10.000_standing_assessment.md#35-implementation-tools) are optional and must not bury required fields in a score.

**No unified subject-access product.** Integrators may compose existing surfaces (identity control, inspect-if-open standing records, **Article IX-B** (*Experiential and Derived Data Rights*) experiential and derived data). Composition must **not** defeat the Chapter Ten silence default.

**Not a software list.** This family catalogs user-reachable in-scope surfaces and nothing more. Constitutional-operating surfaces stay with their existing owners. Do not extend it into a list of required software.
