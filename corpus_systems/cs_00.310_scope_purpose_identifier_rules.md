Core home: 00

This file is the systems implementation home for **CS-0.310** (*Scope, purpose, and boundary interface*).

*In plain terms: **CS** is the systems-and-data rulebook — typing, classification, stewardship tiers, and the CS-6.345 (*System lifecycle*)–CS-6.340 (*System requirements*) engineering families.*

**What this layer owns**

- information typing and handling (**CS-6.305 (*Information types and handling*) and CS-6.310 — Information types and handling**);
- system classification and dependency typing (**CS-9.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-9.310 — System classification and handling**);
- critical system stewardship tiers (**CS-14.305 — Critical system stewardship**), scaled from **CS-9.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-9.310** (*System impact classifications*) impact class and dependency type;
- **CS-6.345** (*System lifecycle*) through **CS-6.330** (*Decentralized continuity and partition resilience*) families for design and verification, comprehensibility, justice safeguards, sustainability, funding, transition, subversion response, and partition resilience, and **CS-6.340** (*System requirements*) for user-reachable in-scope surfaces.

**What this layer does not own**

- constitutional term meaning and O/M/A/C definition mechanics — those remain in the Sentient Constitution (Chapters Two through Five);
- joint operational definitions, and joint cross-layer interfaces that apply when two or more of **CJS**, **CS**, **CI**, and **CF** must be satisfied together — those belong to **CJS** (reusable joint operational definitions in **CJS-5.105 (*operational cluster library (oDef)*), CJS-5.110, CJS-5.115, CJS-5.120, CJS-5.125, CJS-5.130, and CJS-5.135** (*operational cluster library*));
- Rights Floors — those remain in Chapter Six;
- institutional formation, authorization, oversight, correction, and dissolution — those belong to **CI**;
- forum-family operational doctrine under Chapter Thirteen — those belong to **CF**.

**Authority stack.** This file does not create a separate authority stack. Use the constitutional [Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](../core_05.045_band_integrative.md#authority-stack-and-internal-hierarchy) in Chapter Five, read with [Chapter Eighteen](../core_18.000_incorporation.md#chapter-eighteen-incorporation-bridge) for incorporation, edition custody, and anti-drift rules. Domain owners: [Preamble — constitutional owner register](../core_00.000_preamble.md#4-principles-definitions-and-rights).

*In plain terms: this file is the front door to the systems-and-data book. The Constitution says what must be true of systems; this layer tells you how to type data, classify systems, scale stewardship, and run the engineering families without rewriting those rules.*

**Role in the implementation corpus.** Adopted CS text adds systems-and-data how-to detail within adoption scope; it must satisfy the Sentient Constitution and must not weaken constitutional requirements or Rights Floors.

This file (**CS-0.310** (*Scope, purpose, and boundary interface*)) is the **binding gateway** for the systems layer. Use it when you need the layer boundary — what CS owns, what it does not, and how CS families co-apply. Joint-layer purpose, identifier homes, and the shared contract live in **CJS-0.110 §0** (*Purpose of this layer*), **CJS-0.110 §1** (*Section identifiers and article references*), and **CJS-0.110 §3** (*Shared implementation-corpus preamble contract*). Combined satisfaction and the default **CJS → CS → CI → CF** reading stack live in [Chapter One §15.4.4 Combined satisfaction of jointly applicable incorporated obligations](../core_01.010_interaction_interpretation.md#1544-combined-satisfaction-of-jointly-applicable-incorporated-obligations). This file does **not** replace [Chapter One §1 Purpose and Role](../core_01.005_values_principles.md#1-purpose-and-role) — that section states why the Constitution itself exists.

**How CS families co-apply.** CS families work together — typing, classification, stewardship, and the CS-6.345 (*System lifecycle*)–CS-6.340 (*System requirements*) engineering families. Apply them so systems and data are judged by what they do, not by siloed labels. Family-specific duties remain in **CS-6.305 (*Information types and handling*) and CS-6.310** (*Data classifications*) through **CS-6.330** (*Decentralized continuity and partition resilience*). Cross-layer topic routing remains in **CJS-0.105 §1** (*Cross-file routing*).

**Steward screens (process support; cannot narrow this layer or core text).** This file remains the **binding gateway** for the systems layer. It is not replaced by operator pointers or by a markdown declaration. For high-pressure next steps, human and AI stewards use the same pointer index: [`implementation/STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md`](../implementation/STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md) (including the [shared refusal and logging](../implementation/STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#shared-refusal-and-logging) pointer that points at **CS-14.305** (*Critical system stewardship*) **§10** (*Inspectable attributable action*)). For a fitness screen and an own-conduct declaration, use [`evaluation/self_application/`](../evaluation/self_application/) (design: [`implementation/adoption/SELF_APPLICATION_GATEWAY.md`](../implementation/adoption/SELF_APPLICATION_GATEWAY.md)). For two parties or a first qualifying body, the fill-in document and non-adoption banner live in [`implementation/adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](../implementation/adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md). For institution foundation under that path, the labeled-not-live Chapter Five Institutional Charter fill-in lives in [`implementation/adoption/INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](../implementation/adoption/INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). Those sittings and templates are **not** [Chapter Seventeen §10 Ratification and Adoption](../core_17.000_amendment_ratification.md#10-ratification-and-adoption) adoption, **not** [Chapter Eighteen §4](../core_18.000_incorporation.md#4-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) incorporation, and **not** **CS-6.350** (*Transition constitution and migration governance*) phase entry or advancement.
