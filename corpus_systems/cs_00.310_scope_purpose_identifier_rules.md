Core home: 00

This file is the systems implementation home for **CS-0.310** (*Scope, purpose, and boundary interface*).

*In plain terms: **CS** is the systems-and-data rulebook — data handling, system classification, stewardship levels, and engineering protocols.*

**What this layer owns**

- information typing and handling (**CS-4.305 (*Information types and handling*) and CS-4.310 (*Data Classifications*) — Data classifications**);
- system classification and dependency typing (**CS-8.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-8.310 (*System Impact Classifications*) — System impact classifications**);
- critical system stewardship levels (**CS-26.305 — Critical system stewardship**), scaled from **CS-8.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-8.310** (*System Impact Classifications*) impact class and dependency type;
- engineering protocols for design and verification, comprehensibility, justice safeguards, sustainability, funding, Adoption Transition, subversion response, partition resilience, and user-reachable in-scope surfaces, listed in the [Systems registry](cs_00.305_registry_and_reading_rules.md#systems-registry-stable-section-families).

**What this layer does not own**

- constitutional term meaning and O/M/A/C definition mechanics — those remain in the Sentient Constitution (Chapter Three, including its subcore);
- joint operational definitions, and joint cross-layer interfaces that apply when two or more of **CJS**, **CS**, **CI**, and **CF** must be satisfied together — those belong to **CJS** (reusable joint operational definitions in **CJS-3.105 (*Operational cluster library (oDef)*) through CJS-3.135 (*Integrative cross-leg operations (oDef.22–oDef.23)*)**);
- Rights Floors — those remain in Chapter Four;
- institutional formation, authorization, oversight, correction, and dissolution — those belong to **CI**;
- Forum operations under Chapters Twenty-Two and Twenty-Three — those belong to **CF**.

**Authority stack.** This file does not create a separate authority stack. Use the constitutional [Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](../subcore_03.045_band_integrative.md#authority-stack-and-internal-hierarchy) in Chapter Three, read with [Chapter Thirty](../core_30.000_incorporation.md#chapter-thirty-incorporation-bridge) for incorporation, edition custody, and anti-drift rules. Domain owners: [Chapter One — constitutional owner register](../core_01.000_constitutional_map.md#4-principles-definitions-and-rights).

*In plain terms: this file is the front door to the systems-and-data book. The Constitution says what must be true of systems; this layer tells you how to type data, classify systems, scale stewardship, and run the engineering families without rewriting those rules.*

**Role in the implementation corpus.** Adopted CS text adds systems-and-data how-to detail within adoption scope; it must satisfy the Sentient Constitution and must not weaken constitutional requirements or Rights Floors.

This file (**CS-0.310** (*Scope, purpose, and boundary interface*)) is the **binding gateway** for the systems layer. Use it when you need the layer boundary — what CS owns, what it does not, and how CS families co-apply. Joint-layer purpose, identifier homes, and the shared contract live in **CJS-0.110 §0** (*Purpose, Role, and Scope*), **CJS-0.110 §1** (*Section Identifiers and Article References*), and **CJS-0.110 §3** (*Shared Implementation-Corpus Preamble Contract*). Combined satisfaction and the default **CJS → CS → CI → CF** reading stack live in [Chapter Two §15.4.4 Combined Satisfaction of Jointly Applicable Incorporated Obligations](../core_02.030_constitutional_interpretation.md#1544-combined-satisfaction-of-jointly-applicable-incorporated-obligations). This file does **not** replace [Chapter Two §1 Purpose, Role, and Scope](../core_02.000_values_principles.md#1-purpose-role-and-scope) — that section states why the Constitution itself exists.

**How CS families co-apply.** CS families work together — typing, classification, stewardship, and the engineering protocols. Apply them so systems and data are judged by what they do, not by siloed labels. Family-specific duties remain in the files listed in the [Systems registry](cs_00.305_registry_and_reading_rules.md#systems-registry-stable-section-families). Cross-layer topic routing remains in **CJS-0.105 §1** (*Cross-File Routing*).

**Steward screens (process support; cannot narrow this layer or core text).** This file remains the **binding gateway** for the systems layer. It is not replaced by operator pointers or by a markdown declaration. For high-pressure next steps, human and AI stewards use the same pointer index: [`implementation/STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md`](../implementation/STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md) (including the [shared refusal and logging](../implementation/STEWARD_ENTRY_DOORS.md#shared-refusal-and-logging) pointer that points at **CS-26.305** (*Critical system stewardship*) **§10** (*Inspectable Attributable Action*)). For a fitness screen and an own-conduct declaration, use [`evaluation/self_application/`](../evaluation/self_application/) (design: [`implementation/adoption/SELF_APPLICATION_GATEWAY.md`](../implementation/adoption/SELF_APPLICATION_GATEWAY.md)). For two parties or a first qualifying body, the fill-in document and non-adoption banner live in [`implementation/adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md`](../implementation/adoption/FIRST_ADOPTER_KIT.md). For institution foundation under that path, the labeled-not-live Chapter Three Institutional Charter fill-in lives in [`implementation/adoption/INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md`](../implementation/adoption/INSTITUTION_FOUNDATION_KIT.md). Those sittings and templates are **not** [Chapter Twenty-Eight §12 Ratification and Adoption](../core_28.000_amendment_ratification.md#12-ratification-and-adoption) adoption, **not** [Chapter Thirty §5 Adoption Framing and Scope of Authority](../core_30.000_incorporation.md#5-adoption-framing-and-scope-of-authority) incorporation, and **not** **CS-4.350** (*Adoption Transition constitution and migration governance*) phase entry or advancement. Informal “we kind of follow it” practice, and a self-application declaration, are not enough to claim constitutional protection while dodging matching obligations.
