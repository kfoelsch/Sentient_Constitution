### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Chapter Three, Continuity aim, is the constitutional owner of the **Continuity** constitutional-aim head definition (O/M/A/C) and hierarchy map. Leaf definitions are in the [Continuity band](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md).

*In plain terms: keep sentients and the living systems they depend on going for the long run — stable, sustainable, able to bounce back, and ecologically sound — not just looking fine for now.*

### 2. Why Continuity Is a Constitutional Aim

*In plain terms: a good life for sentients now is not enough if the systems and the living world it rests on are used up. This file says what it means for them to last.*

Flourishing depends on things that can be worn down quietly: ecosystems, shared infrastructure, institutions, and the knowledge to run them. Damage to these often shows up late and cannot always be undone. Making continuity an aim of its own keeps every system answerable for what it leaves behind, not only for what it delivers today.

### Continuity: Head Definition

- **What it is**
  - **In scope:** Judging systems against this aim using the Continuity building blocks and measurement families listed in [Constitutional Aim Decomposition](#constitutional-aim-decomposition). The aim is long-horizon stability, sustainability, resilience, and ecological wellbeing for sentients and the life-supporting systems they depend on.
  - **Depends on:** the themes named in [Preamble §1 The Model](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity). Each term's detailed scope and how-to-measure rules live on its own definition; start from [Constitutional Aim Decomposition](#constitutional-aim-decomposition):
    - [Stewardship](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#stewardship);
    - [Self-Healing](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#self-healing);
    - [Sustainability](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#sustainability);
    - [Ecological Integrity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#ecological-integrity);
    - [Environmental Preconditions](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#environmental-preconditions);
    - [Intergenerational Responsibility](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#intergenerational-responsibility);
    - [Dependency](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#dependency); and
    - [Proportionate Cross-System Support](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#proportionate-cross-system-support).
  - **Out of scope:** [Indigenous Continuity](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#indigenous-continuity) unless the text expressly brings it in; the leaf-definition details, measurement layers, and implementation mechanics owned by band files and CJS.
<a id="continuity-aim-constitutional-m"></a>
<a id="continuity-aim-constitutional-a"></a>
- **How to measure and assess**
  - **Primary measure:** [Continuity measurement family](#measuring-continuity-preamble-measurement-family) — *Can sentients and systems endure — ecologically, dependably, and across failure?* See also [Constitutional Aim Decomposition](#constitutional-aim-decomposition). This line only points to the measures; the how-to-measure rules sit on each linked definition.
  - **Primary assessment:** Judge under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad), scaled to [material stake](core_00.000_preamble.md#material-stake). Ask whether systems keep lasting capacity to repair, adapt, face risk honestly, and stay within ecological limits — not whether today's numbers look calm. Tie claimed continuity results to the [constituent](#constitutional-aim-decomposition) conditions, under Subcore 3-C and [Chapter Two §19.1.5 Constitutional Outcome Claims Discipline](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#1915-constitutional-outcome-claims-discipline).
<a id="continuity-aim-constitutional-c"></a>
- **What must hold**
  - **Primary failure:**
    - using **Continuity** to forever put off fixes;
    - treating "stability" as compliant when it rests on hidden risk dumping, irreversible harm, or shifting ecological burdens onto others;
    - using ordinary "keep the lights on" talk to block [Contestability](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#contestability) or [Timely Resolution](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#timely-resolution); or
    - pursuing **Continuity** in a way that materially defeats [Flourishing](subcore_03.050_apex_flourishing_aim.md#flourishing-constitutional) without clear permission under [Chapter Two §13 Constitutional Collision Resolution Process](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#13-constitutional-collision-resolution-process).
