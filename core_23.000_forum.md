*In plain terms: this section assigns constitutional **participation** and **oversight** through forum families that **supervise** two tracks — **System Alignment Certification** (Chapter Nine certification and review) and the **standing pipeline** (Chapters Fifteen–Twenty-Two disputes and record effects) — then states accountability requirements for published threshold placement. Ordinary disputes inside already-authorized systems use the published Stakeholder System Participation challenge path first; this chapter takes over when that path is still contested, missing, captured, or cannot grant relief. Detailed hearing rules live elsewhere and may not quietly shrink what this chapter guarantees.*

**Purpose:** Under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) and [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims), Chapter Twenty-Three answers the question that follows any dispute under the certification and standing chapters: who hears it, and can the sentient affected get a fair and timely hearing? The chapter supplies:

- forums that are independent and contestable;
- a clear starting point for each kind of dispute; and
- an accessible way to challenge decisions that affect sentients, backed by forensic and technical support and by the timeliness floor in **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).

What it produces is the set of forum families and the routing that sends each dispute to one of them.

**Role:** Where this chapter sits:

- It supervises two tracks: system and institutional alignment certification ([Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--evaluation) through [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-fourteen-institutional-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing)), and the standing pipeline ([Chapter Fifteen](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fifteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) through [Chapter Twenty-Two](core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#chapter-twenty-two-anti-constitutional-misconduct--pattern-applications-and-criteria-routing)).
- Ordinary disputes inside an already-authorized system use the published Stakeholder System Participation challenge path first. This chapter takes over when that path leaves the dispute contested, is missing, is captured, or cannot grant the relief needed.
- Forum eligibility, contested selection, and democratic minimums are set in [Chapter Twenty-Seven](core_27.000_governance.md#chapter-twenty-seven-constitutional-contract-legitimacy-authorization-and-stewardship), **section 2.2**.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers the following:

- forum family supervision and standards (**section 2**);
- default venue and primary stakes (**section 3**);
- forum cases: intakes, seats, records, and contests (**section 4**); and
- transfer, consolidation, and anti-self-judging (**section 5**).

Forum family definitions, escalation and questions for ruling, timely resolution, and forum support are in [Chapter Twenty-Four](core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#chapter-twenty-four-forum-families-escalation-timely-resolution-and-support).

The chapter leaves the certification rules to the certification chapters, the standing rules to the standing pipeline chapters, and procedure details to adopted implementation text. Forum families do **not** run a second, separate decision track beside the standing pipeline.

*In plain terms: forums are the main way this Constitution governs, and this section says what they oversee. That is the certification of systems and institutions, and the standing pipeline: how Standing Records are opened, corrected, and acted on, including remedies and findings of anti-constitutional misconduct. It also sets the rules every forum must follow. A forum must be independent, open to challenge, and able to show how it reached its decision, and the roles in a case (filing, decision, record-keeping, and review of the forum's own decision) must sit in separate seats, as section 4 sets out. It may not offer rewards or shortcuts that let a case skip its proper route or avoid a real decision on the merits. And it works alongside the detailed implementation files without taking over another forum's job.*

<a id="21-what-forum-families-supervise"></a>

#### 2.1 What Forum Families Supervise

This chapter states which **forum families** **supervise** which primary questions through:

- **participation** (sentient-accessible challenge, representative treatment, and proportionate access); and
- **oversight** (independence, forensic support, and traceable merits review).

It does **not** specify docket rules, staffing, budgets, granular procedure, or operational escalation mechanics. Those details belong in adopted implementation text.

**Forum families supervise two tracks:**

1. **Certification** ([Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--evaluation))
   - System Alignment Certification and Institutional Alignment Certification outcomes, from conditional certification through recertification and withdrawal, under [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-ten-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes), [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-eleven-system-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing), and [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-twelve-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation). Component roles are set in [Chapter Ten §2.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles).
   - A certification does **not** replace standing measurement ([Chapter Eleven §5 Relationship to Standing](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing)).

2. **Standing pipeline** (Chapters Fifteen–Twenty-Two, applying Chapters One through Six and designated [corpus](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#corpus) implementation norms in disputes)
   - Primary-stakes routing, merits review where assigned, transfer, consolidation, certification coordination, and remedy sequencing for standing-related disputes.
   - Standing Records, effects, and anti-constitutional-misconduct designation (ACMD) stay under Chapters Fifteen, Sixteen, Seventeen, and Twenty-One. A filed case is not standing by itself, and forum narrative must not substitute for Chapter Sixteen standing measurement ([Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary)).

**Forum families** are the primary institutions through which this Constitution **supervises** those tracks. They are distinct from routine executive administration of enacted rules.

#### 2.2 Standards Every Forum Family Meets

Every **forum family** meets the standards below, whichever primary stake brings a matter to it.

<a id="221-independence-contestability-and-tracing"></a>

##### 2.2.1 Independence, Contestability, and Tracing

Forum families:

- carry a proactive governance role, in alignment with **Chapter One** principles including **Safety**, **Truth**, **Wellbeing**, **Responsiveness**, and **Stewardship in Depth**, that includes:
  - problem resolution, root-cause analysis, and prevention;
  - remediation sequencing; and
  - learning from patterns of failure.
- must satisfy **independence**, **contestability**, and **tracing** expectations in:
  - **Chapters Two through Four**;
  - **Article XIII-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), where challenge and remediation are implicated; and
  - **Article XX** (*Justice After Verified Violation*), where justice constraints govern.
- are subject to this Constitution's most stringent expressed procedural and integrity-accountability requirements, including:
  - public justification and traceability;
  - recusal and panel discipline; and
  - forensic and analytical support where this chapter assigns them.
- require anti-self-judging backup routing, which sends a matter to an independent backup forum when the lead forum cannot judge it ([Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)). Those requirements must not substitute political control for judicial independence.
- hold the four seats in a forum case in the way [§4.2 Seats in a Forum Case](#42-seats-in-a-forum-case) sets out.
- **Integrity**, **Constitutional**, and **Environment** forums, and **Technical Forum Domains** when they hear sentience-status adjudication, require:
  - **published**, **contested**, **rotatable** appointment or an equivalent independence check under [Chapter Twenty-Seven §2.2 Eligibility, Contested Selection, and Democratic Minimums](core_27.000_governance.md#22-eligibility-contested-selection-and-democratic-minimums); and
  - adequate appointment and funding, because under-appointment or underfunding of those benches is a [Chapter Twenty §4 Enforcement Realism and Remedy Systems](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-enforcement-realism-and-remedy-systems) failure.

<a id="222-incentives-and-non-substitution"></a>

##### 2.2.2 Incentives and Non-Substitution

Each **forum family** bears primary responsibility for incentive structures primarily within its sphere. This includes:

- adopting documents and designated implementation layers;
- reviewing cost, sanction, contest and reporting pathways; and
- remediating sequencing hooks, and comparable adjudication-adjacent alignment.

**System-wide** budgets, cross-cutting research funding, and governance-scale programs remain subject to [Chapter Twenty-Seven — Governance](core_27.000_governance.md#chapter-twenty-seven-constitutional-contract-legitimacy-authorization-and-stewardship), **[CS-6.335](corpus_systems/cs_06.335_resource_allocation_funding_stewardship.md#cs-6335-resource-allocation-and-funding-stewardship)** (*Resource allocation and funding stewardship*), and other supremacy rules where applicable. Payoffs, cost rules, and similar incentive tools must **not** take the place of:

- proper forum routing;
- a real merits decision by a lawfully constituted panel;
- Chapter Sixteen impact-slot measurement;
- any matching **Chapter Twenty-One** designation; and
- the justice constraints in **Article XX** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).

<a id="31-dispute-sequencing"></a>

#### 3.1 Dispute Sequencing

<a id="dispute-sequencing"></a>
Inside a system, institution, or decision area that is already authorized, an ordinary dispute goes in two steps:

- It first uses the published [Stakeholder System Participation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) path for challenges and due process.
- This chapter sends the matter to the forum that fits its primary stake, under **section 3**, when that path:
  - leaves the result still contested;
  - does not exist;
  - has been captured; or
  - cannot lawfully give the relief needed.

Three rules keep this sequence from becoming a way to delay:

- **Integrity**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Environment**, and **Constitutional** forums lead by default when their area is the primary stake. That is the normal order, not an exception that skips the sequence.
- An unfinished internal process, an "exhaustion" label, or a claim that internal review is not done must not stall those leads, push aside their decision on the merits, or use up the time limits in **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).
- Direct access stays open when waiting would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or a practical way to restore what was lost.

<a id="32-primary-stakes-routing-and-intake"></a>

#### 3.2 Primary-Stakes Routing and Intake

<a id="321-the-primary-stakes-test"></a>

##### 3.2.1 The Primary-Stakes Test

**Primary-stakes routing** assigns a matter to the forum family responsible for the main legal, remedial, coercive-safeguard, constitutional-floor, or practical stake in the **claim** or **defense**. It follows what the dispute is really about, not the caption or the party's preferred outcome.

<a id="322-intake-triage-body-first-touch-desk"></a>

##### 3.2.2 Intake Triage Body (First-Touch Desk)

Each required forum family must maintain an **intake triage body**, or a functionally equivalent arrangement within that family, as the family's **first-touch desk** at filing. That body:
- **applies** primary-stakes routing under this section;
- **sorts** incoming matters for **published** intake treatment consistent with **primary stakes**;
- **flags** preservation, **Rights-Floor**, **cross-family routing**, and **backup-forum** needs (see [Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)); and
- **administers** initial access so that transfer, consolidation, certification, and backup discipline under **section 5** and **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3** can take effect inside the **intake target** of the matching tier ([Chapter Twenty-Four §4 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#4-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)).

That body also stays within three limits. It:

- is **not** a separate constitutional forum family;
- **must not** substitute for a lawfully constituted merits panel on **substantive outcomes** or **collapse** **family** boundaries; and
- **must not** employ funding, fee, administrative preference, or other incentive devices to defeat primary-stakes routing or enable opaque threshold sorting.

<a id="323-good-faith-characterization-and-anti-gaming"></a>

##### 3.2.3 Good-Faith Characterization and Anti-Gaming

<a id="good-faith-characterization-and-anti-gaming"></a>
Two rules keep routing honest:

- Forum characterization must be **good-faith** and **warranted** by **primary stakes**. **Knowing** mischaracterization **may** trigger **costs**, **dismissal**, or **referral** under adopting law. Where it is verified conduct in Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9, it may also be anti-constitutional misconduct (ACM) under [Chapter Twenty-One §5 Violation Axis Slot 7–9 Anti-Constitutional Designation](core_21.000_misconduct_designation.md#5-violation-axis-slot-79-anti-constitutional-designation).
- Incentive devices must not be structured or applied to reward:
  - forum shopping;
  - opaque threshold sorting; or
  - routing inconsistent with primary-stakes discipline in **this section** and **section 5**.

<a id="324-initial-access-and-timing"></a>

##### 3.2.4 Initial Access and Timing

Operational requirements — including **published intake classes**, attributable intake records, independence and **contest** expectations, and review of **contested** routing inside the **intake target** — are stated in **CF-23.415** (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*).

**Initial access** to each forum family must come inside the **intake target** of the matching tier ([CF-23.445 §3.1](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*)) and be contestable enough that:
- primary-stakes routing under this section remains meaningful;
- transfer, consolidation, certification, and backup discipline under **section 5** and **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3** are not nullified by delay or by opaque threshold sorting.

Time expectations for forum access must:
- be **sentient-accessible**;
- be calibrated to harm urgency and matter complexity under **Chapter Twenty-Four section 4** and **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*);
- favor real threshold access and lawful interim relief under **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3** (*Interim Protection*) over administrative convenience.

The **intake triage body** under this section, together with **CF-23.415** (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*), satisfies this obligation and must implement **Chapter Twenty-Four section 4** tier-default windows within incorporation scope.

<a id="33-default-venue"></a>

#### 3.3 Default Venue

<a id="331-routing-sequence-at-filing"></a>

##### 3.3.1 Routing Sequence at Filing

**Routing map from Chapter Fifteen §5 (*Verification and Record Requirements*).** Chapters Fifteen and Sixteen tell forums how to record and *measure* what happened. They do **not** by themselves pick which forum hears the case. At filing, the family's **intake triage body** (first-touch desk) works through this sequence:

1. **Check what is already verified:** Note any verified Chapter Sixteen **Contribution Axis** or **Violation Axis** material — including the impact slot, severity band, process / response character, and standing effect — when those already exist.
2. **Do not treat accusations as settled facts:** Allegations and provisional labels guide routing and evidence preservation only, until findings exist under **Chapters Two through Four**.
3. **Pick the lead forum by what the fight is really about:** Use the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**: **Sentient**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Integrity**, or **Constitutional**. Apply the special routing rules in **section 3.3.4** where they fit.

<a id="332-default-venue-table"></a>

##### 3.3.2 Default Venue Table

At filing, **default** venue follows these rules unless **section 5** transfers or consolidates:

| Family | Party pattern | Primary stake (summary) |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------|
| **Sentient** | Sentient-to-sentient matters, and member, participant, household, neighborhood, association, local, regional, online, or comparable community-governance disputes where no institution is a necessary party and no other family holds the primary stake | Private or community obligations, remedial or restorative harms, local restoration, local or online community norms, community-governance participation, exclusion, restoration, or internal self-governance contests |
| **Technical Forum Domains** | Any party pattern, but **primary** stake is technical-governance procedure, system-alignment specifications, expert-evidence standards, scientific / engineering / medical administration, comparable knowledge-governance within adopted scope, **or** **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) sentience-status determination on indicators / expert evidence / bounded uncertainty | **Technical** standards, system-alignment specifications, measurement and testing methods, expert administrative procedure, evidence stewardship, textbook or curriculum integrity, standards governance, bounded uncertainty-reduction relevant to adjudication, regulation, or alignment certification, **or** sentience-status indicator adjudication under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) |
| **Institutional** | Any **institution** is a **necessary** party **or** the main fight is about what an institution is allowed to do, what it is charged to do, or whether it followed the rules that govern it | What an institution may do, what it is charged to do, the scope it is supervised under, how it is classified, or whether it followed its duties |
| **Environment** | Anyone can be a party, not only systems, and an Environment Forum must also take part in reviewing any system whose alignment materially affects the environment | **Ecological integrity**, **environmental preconditions**, restoration or remediation of shared ecological systems, attributable environmental burdens, lifecycle or systemic ecological harm, environmental-alignment component review for systems with material ecological exposure, or **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure where classification, alignment certification, recertification, or Rights-Floor enforcement depends on that determination |
| **Integrity** | **Integrity** as **the** main issue (including **grave** violation of conflict, procedure, or capture controls), official **constitutional alignment certification** or **ongoing alignment validation** for systems and institutions, **or** a **Chapter Twenty-One** ACMD for a Chapter Sixteen **slot 7, 8, or 9** impact slot as **the** **primary** stake | **Integrity** of office, process, contest pathways, disclosure, conflict rules, anti-capture duties, official system-alignment certification, validation, and recertification using technical-forum inputs and Environment Forum environmental-alignment component determinations where material (**including** **pattern** or **systemic** failure **across** institutions or systems where **Chapter Sixteen** measurement, a corresponding **Chapter Twenty-One** designation, or **Rights-Floor** enforcement depends on that determination) |
| **Constitutional** | **Structural** constitutional validity, **norm** meaning for **all** interpreters, or remedy that **restructures** governance **by constitutional requirement** | **Constitutional** validity or meaning, action **beyond lawful authority**, supremacy, or **class-wide** **structural** remedy |

<a id="333-lead-default-limits"></a>

##### 3.3.3 Lead-Default Limits

These rules limit how the table's default leads interact. They do not replace **section 5** and **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3**, or the mixed-stakes and asymmetry rules in **section 3.4**.

- **Primary-stakes collision:** If the main fight is about what an institution is allowed to do, what it is charged to do, or whether it followed the rules that govern it, the Chapter Twenty-One **Integrity** lead default (set in **section 3.3.4**) does not override the **Institutional** row. **Section 3.4** and **section 5** govern mixed stakes, necessary institutional parties, and lead forum selection.
- **Constitutional questions for ruling:** **Integrity** lead for a Chapter Twenty-One designation does not displace Chapter Sixteen numeric slot measurement, sending a question for ruling, or escalation to **Constitutional** forums under **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3** where constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy requires determination under the **Constitutional** row or through family-to-family escalation.
- **Anti-self-judging backup:** Where material allegations warrant independent merits review of **Integrity** forum integrity in a Chapter Twenty-One designation proceeding concerning a Chapter Sixteen slot 7–9 record, backup routing under **section 4** and **Chapter Twenty-Four section 2** applies without narrowing **Chapter Twenty-One section 6** or **Article XX** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).

<a id="334-special-routing-rules"></a>

##### 3.3.4 Special Routing Rules

These rules apply on top of the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**. **Section 3.3.3** limits how the table's default leads interact.

- **Chapter Twenty-One designation:** Where a **Chapter Twenty-One** ACMD is the **primary** stake, **Integrity** is the default lead. Keep:
  - the Chapter Sixteen numeric impact slot;
  - **Constitutional** certification where needed;
  - institutional-party rules; and
  - anti-self-judging backup under **sections 3, 5, and 7**.
- **Forum-bias disputes:** If the fight is mainly about a panelist who should have stepped aside, biased panel participation, or a comparable forum-integrity violation — including on a **Constitutional** forum panel under **[Article XXV-C](core_06.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxv-c-constitutional-forum-independence-and-external-review) (*Constitutional Forum Independence and External Review*)** — route it to **Integrity** forums first. Under **section 5**, a forum cannot be the sole final judge of its own bias: a **Constitutional** forum cannot be the only final forum deciding whether its own panelist should have recused. Standing-lock discipline: **[Chapter Nineteen §3.5 Special Locks](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#35-special-locks)**. Named misconduct pattern: **[Chapter Twenty-Two §5.3 Forum Recusal Failure and Biased Panel Participation](core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#53-forum-recusal-failure-and-biased-panel-participation)**.
- **Technical how-to questions:** Specifications, methods, measurement, testing, and expert-evidence questions go to **Technical Forum Domains** when those are the primary stake or component questions for ruling.
- **System-alignment sign-off:**
  - Official **constitutional alignment certification** for new materially impactful systems, and **ongoing alignment validation** for existing ones, default to **Integrity** as lead. So does institutional alignment certification of an Institution, which keeps **Institutional** forums as holders of the institutional mandate and duty-compliance components.
  - Integrity uses technical-forum inputs plus constitutional, institutional, ecological, rights, and anti-capture requirements.
  - Where the system has material ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk, the **Environment** forum family must complete its environmental-alignment review (sign-off or objection) before certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions may become final.
  - Keep referrals or sending for ruling to **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Sentient**, and **Constitutional** for component questions those families own.

<a id="34-mixed-stakes-and-routing-asymmetry"></a>

#### 3.4 Mixed Stakes and Routing Asymmetry

<a id="341-one-case-record-one-lead"></a>

##### 3.4.1 One Case Record, One Lead

One Forum Case Record and one lead family ordinarily hear interdependent claims. Secondary issues may be certified, stayed, or resolved through issue preclusion as adopting documents provide, consistent with **Article XIII-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*), **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), and **Chapter Fifteen** joint-assessment and non-substitution discipline.

<a id="342-asymmetry"></a>

##### 3.4.2 Asymmetry

Three asymmetry rules keep routing available where the table assigns the stake:

- Where **dependency**, **measurement**, or an **institutional** **monopoly** on a **necessary** **input** materially disadvantages a **sentient** party, **Institutional** or **Integrity** routing **must** be **available** when the table assigns **institutional** or **integrity** stakes.
- Where **material** **ecological** stakes are **primary**, **Environment** routing **must** be **available** as the table assigns.
- **Sentient** forums **must not** be the **sole** **mandatory** forum when the **primary** stakes are **institutional**, **integrity**, or **ecological** under the table in this section.

*In plain terms: a case reaches a forum either as a direct case or as a review case that contests another process's Certification Record, Standing Record, or Adoption Transition Record. Who sits in each seat depends on how the case arrived and who is being heard, and no one who handled the earlier act may sit on its review. A forum keeps its own file on a dispute, a certification has its own Certification Record, and standing has its own Standing Record, and none of them changes anyone's standing by itself. A certification can be challenged along four paths, and this section says which forum takes each. A Standing Record can be contested too, and the forum reviews the Standing Record and how it is being used, not a sentient's whole reputation. An adopter's Adoption Transition Record can be contested, and the forum reviews the contested gate decision or entry, not the whole transition.*

<a id="41-intakes"></a>

#### 4.1 Intakes

A case reaches a forum in one of four ways. The intake triage body records the intake kind on the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) at filing:

| Intake | What arrives | First step | Routed by |
|--------|--------------|------------|-----------|
| **Direct case** (a [Direct Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#direct-case)) | A dispute that does not contest a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record: sentient-to-sentient, community, institutional, integrity, ecological, or constitutional claims. | Filing with the forum, after the ordinary participation path under [Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing) where one applies. | Primary stakes, under the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**. |
| **Certification contest** (a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to a System or Institutional Certification Record or one of its certification findings. | The Certification Record's published challenge path, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The four challenge paths, under **section 4.4**. |
| **Standing-Record contest** (a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to a contribution Standing Record or violation Standing Record. | Logged on the Standing Record and set *under challenge* by its custodian, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The real issue in the challenge, under **section 4.5**. |
| **Adoption Transition Record contest** (a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to an adopter's [Adoption Transition Record](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record): a gate decision, an enforcement-readiness declaration, a ledger entry, interim authority, or a provisional entry. | The challenge route the adopter publishes with the Adoption Transition Record, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The real issue in the challenge, under **section 4.6**. |

These rules apply to every intake:

- A claim is routed by what it contests in substance, not by how it is filed. A claim that in substance contests a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record is a review case.
- A transfer, consolidation, or referral between forums continues the same case. The case keeps its intake kind and its [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record), and only the lead changes (**section 5**). A move that changes the lead is one automated step that updates the docket portal and sends alerts ([CF-23.415 §10 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-10-transfer-procedure)).
- A direct case may end in a verified violation finding that opens or corrects a Standing Record. The forum then acts as record-opening authority on the terms in [Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary), and a contest of that Standing Record is a new review case heard by a forum that did not make the finding.

<a id="42-seats-in-a-forum-case"></a>

#### 4.2 Seats in a Forum Case

A forum case is a materially binding act, so it has the four seats in [Chapter Eight §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement), under Chapter Eight's names. This subsection is how this chapter applies that requirement, as [Chapter Eight §10 Relationship to Later Processes](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#10-relationship-to-later-processes) requires of every later process. It adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Eight. Forensic and technical support under **Chapter Twenty-Four section 5** feeds the verify-or-authorize seat. It is not a fifth seat.

Who holds each seat depends on the intake:

| Seat | Direct case | Certification contest | Standing-Record contest | Adoption Transition Record contest |
|------|-------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **[Initiating Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#initiating-seat)** (brings the case) | The claimant: a sentient, institution, community, or published representative. A sentient needs no institution to file. | The challenger, who is any affected sentient or stakeholder. The operator or proponent when it contests a denial or revocation. | The subject or claimant contesting the Standing Record. | An affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body. |
| **[Verify-or-Authorize Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#verify-or-authorize-seat)** (decides the case) | The merits panel of the lead forum family under **section 3.3.2**. | A forum that did not make the challenged finding, chosen under **section 4.4**. | A forum that did not verify the Standing Record. Never the record custodian or a Backup Record Authority. | A forum independent of the ledger owner and of the party that made the contested decision, chosen under **section 4.6**. |
| **[Record Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#record-seat)** (keeps the case file) | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). Not the panel and not a party. | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The System or Institutional Certification Record stays with the record holder its Charter Role Map names, and the lead forum coordinates it without keeping it. | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The Standing Record stays with its record custodian. | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The [Adoption Transition Record](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) stays with the adopter that keeps and publishes it. |
| **[Contest Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#contest-seat)** (reviews the forum's own decision) | Appeal or secondary review under [CF-23.420](corpus_forum/cf_23.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-23420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*). A backup family where the forum's own bias or capture is alleged. | The same, with escalation to **Constitutional** forums where constitutional validity is in question. | The same. | The same as a direct case. |
| **Respondents, not seat holders** | None. | The operator, the lead forum, the component authorities, and the Certification Record holder. | The record-opening authority and the record custodian. | The adopter, the ledger owner, the Adoption Transition authority, and the party that made the contested decision or declaration. |

A review case has two layers of seats:

- For the earlier act (the certification, the opening of the Standing Record, or the gate decision or declaration on the Adoption Transition Record), the forum with jurisdiction fills the Contest Seat.
- For the review itself, the forum case is a new act with its own four seats.
- Whoever held the verify-or-authorize seat or the Record Seat on the earlier act is a respondent or witness in the review. That office holds no seat on the review.
- The forum, panel, or office that made the contested finding, decision, or declaration never hears the contest of it. A challenge to a component finding goes to a forum that did not make it, and [CF-23.420](corpus_forum/cf_23.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-23420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*) says when the review must leave the family.

The incompatibilities in [Chapter Eight §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) apply to each act in a forum case:

- No forum, panel member, or office may hold seats on the same act that Chapter Eight prohibits.
- A forum family may hold different seats on different acts. It may not use its family label to merge seats that Chapter Eight prohibits on the same act.

Who holds the seats also depends on who is being heard:

- **A system or institution that has a [System Charter](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-charter) or [Institutional Charter](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter):** every seat holder sits outside its Material Control Line. The party cannot choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove the forum or panel that hears the case. A seat that its Charter Role Map should place and leaves unplaced is a defect in the System Charter or Institutional Charter, handled under [Chapter Eight §6 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#6-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution).
- **A sentient:** holds the Initiating Seat on its own filing and needs no institution to do so. The forum's published placement under [CF-23.405](corpus_forum/cf_23.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-23405-forum-formation-forum-structure-mapping-and-chamber-structure) (*Forum Formation, Forum-Structure Mapping, and Chamber Structure*) names the other seats, and a holder with an interest in the outcome steps aside under [CF-23.410](corpus_forum/cf_23.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md#cf-23410-panel-formation-disclosure-recusal-and-lawful-bench-constitution) (*Panel Formation, Disclosure, Recusal, and Lawful Bench Constitution*).
- **A community or natural system:** the published representative holds the Initiating Seat ([Chapter Twenty-Four §2.4 Environment Forums](core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#24-environment-forums)). The representative holds no seat in the decision.
- **A forum family as the party (alleged bias, capture, or conflict):** the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in **section 5** assigns the independent lead and the backup. That family holds no seat in the case.

<a id="43-kinds-of-records"></a>

#### 4.3 Kinds of Records

A forum works with four kinds of record, and none of them by itself changes anyone's standing:

- A [**forum case record**](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) is the forum's file on the dispute before it. That Forum Case Record tracks:
  - the claims;
  - evidence;
  - routing choices;
  - temporary orders;
  - questions for ruling; and
  - final findings in that case.
- A **System or Institutional Certification Record** states a certification outcome and its limits on reliance. It is kept under [Chapter Eleven §2 System Certification Record](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#2-system-certification-record) and [Chapter Fourteen §2 Institutional Certification Record](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#2-institutional-certification-record).
- A **Standing Record** is a Chapter Fifteen **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record**, as required by [Chapter Fifteen](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#3-question-1--what-happened) (see [Standing Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#standing-record)).
- An **Adoption Transition Record** is an adopter's published, running record of its Adoption Transition: its gates, Rights-Floor ledger, and interim authority. The adopter keeps it under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*). A forum reviews it only on a contest under **section 4.6**; the forum does not keep it.

These two rules keep the kinds of record apart:

- A forum case record is **not** a Standing Record. None of the following by itself changes anyone's standing, role eligibility, trust status, recognition, or final standing effect:
  - filing a case;
  - assigning it to a forum family;
  - making intake notes;
  - issuing a temporary order;
  - recording an unresolved allegation;
  - discussing settlement; or
  - using a provisional routing label.
- A certification does **not** replace standing measurement. Its findings may count as **verified inputs** when Chapter Sixteen measures standing, but it does **not**, by itself, assign anyone a standing slot ([Chapter Eleven §5 Relationship to Standing](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing); [Chapter Fourteen §5 Relationship to Standing](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing)).

These rules cover how forum work reaches Standing Records:

- A forum decision may become part of a Chapter Fifteen **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record** only when both of these hold:
  - it produces one of:
    - a verified contribution record;
    - a verified violation finding;
    - a **System or Institutional Certification Record**; or
    - another finding; and
  - that output is:
    - bounded;
    - traceable;
    - contestable; and
    - verified under Chapters Two through Four and any required review safeguards.
  - [Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary) owns how verified contribution records and verified violation findings enter Standing Records.
- When one lead forum handles a mixed-stakes case, it must keep the **forum case record** clear enough that separate Chapter Sixteen contribution and violation measurements, any Chapter Twenty-One designation, and Chapter Seventeen standing effects can still be audited separately and recorded in axis-pure Standing Records.

<a id="44-contests-of-certification-records"></a>

#### 4.4 Contests of Certification Records

A certification contest is a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case). A System or Institutional Certification Record names four challenge paths ([Chapter Eleven §4.2 Contestability Paths](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-contestability-paths); [Chapter Fourteen §4.2 Contestability Paths](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-contestability-paths)). The paths apply [Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing) under **section 3**. They are routes into the forums, not a new forum, and this chapter decides which forum takes each:

- The **published record challenge path** is the ordinary first step for an ordinary dispute about the Certification Record. A challenge raised there is logged on the Certification Record, and the tier windows under **Chapter Twenty-Four §4** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from its receipt. Internal operator review, vendor attestation, or technical sign-off is not this path.
- The **component forum path** takes a challenge to one certification finding to the forum family that has jurisdiction over it under the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**.
  - Examples:
    - **Technical Forum Domains** for specifications, methods, and evidence standards;
    - **Environment** for the environmental-alignment component; and
    - **Institutional** for the institutional mandate and duty-compliance components of an Institution's certification.
  - The challenge stays with that family for primary-stakes merits.
- The **lead-integrity path** takes a challenge to how the lead forum ran the process.
  - Examples:
    - capture;
    - hidden information;
    - reviewing its own work; or
    - calling certification finished too soon.
  - It goes through **Integrity** routing, with a backup forum so the lead forum does not judge itself, under:
    - the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in **section 5**; and
    - backup routing in **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3**.
- The **escalation path** moves a dispute to a different or higher forum, where the main stakes belong elsewhere or constitutional validity is in question. It uses one of the moves between forums in **Chapter Twenty-Four section 3** ([Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](core_24.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)):
  - transfer;
  - question for ruling;
  - backup routing; or
  - family-to-family escalation.

While a challenge is pending:

- The **Integrity** forum stays the lead for the System or Institutional Certification Record unless primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or a component assignment places a question elsewhere (**Chapter Twenty-Four section 2.5**).
- A timely **Environment** forum objection, remediation requirement, or question for ruling that remains unresolved blocks final certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions (**Chapter Twenty-Four section 2.5**).
- Direct access to a forum path remains available when delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration ([Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing)).
- A credible challenge can reopen review under [Chapter Eleven §3.2 Reopening Triggers](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#32-reopening-triggers) and [Chapter Fourteen §3.2 Reopening Triggers](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#32-reopening-triggers).

<a id="45-contests-of-standing-records"></a>

#### 4.5 Contests of Standing Records

A Standing-Record contest is a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case). A Standing Record may be contested before or after any filing, as follows:

- A challenge raised on a Standing Record before any filing — to the custodian, the record-opening authority, or any office of the institution — is logged on the Standing Record, set *under challenge*, and routed by the custodian under [Chapter Fifteen §7.3](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#73-challenge-received-on-the-standing-record) (*Challenge Received on the Standing Record*); the tier windows under **Chapter Twenty-Four §4** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from that receipt, and the **forum case record** opens when the contest reaches a forum.
- Where the record-opening authority was a Backup Record Authority under **CF-23.415 §7** (*Backup Record Authority*), a contest of that Standing Record goes to a forum with jurisdiction and never back to that authority. The Backup Record Authority sits outside the intake triage body and is not a merits panel.
- A sentient, institution, system, community, or other affected subject may ask a forum with jurisdiction to review a **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record** when they claim the Standing Record is:
  - wrong;
  - incomplete;
  - stale;
  - mis-scoped;
  - based on an invalid finding;
  - missing required context;
  - missing a required cross-reference; or
  - being used for a purpose it does not cover.
- The forum's job is to review the challenged record and the way it is being used. It may:
  - confirm the Standing Record;
  - require correction;
  - order a new version;
  - limit or pause reliance on the Standing Record;
  - send an underlying issue to the right forum; or
  - send a constitutional question for ruling.
- The forum must **not** turn a Standing-Record contest into a general reputation trial or merge contribution and violation review into one undifferentiated merits hearing when the challenge targets only one axis-pure record.
- Routing follows the real issue in the challenge:
  - factual or evidentiary defects go to the forum family that can fairly review that material;
  - institutional-use disputes go to **Institutional** forums where the main fight is about what an institution may do or whether it followed the rules that govern it;
  - capture, concealment, retaliation, or process-integrity claims go to **Integrity** forums where integrity is primary;
  - environmental merits go to **Environment** forums where ecological stakes are primary;
  - constitutional validity or meaning goes to **Constitutional** forums through certification or direct routing where this chapter allows it.

<a id="46-contests-of-adoption-transition-records"></a>

#### 4.6 Contests of Adoption Transition Records

A contest of an [Adoption Transition Record](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) is a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case). The adopter keeps and publishes the Adoption Transition Record under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), and this subsection does not change that duty. An affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body may contest:

- a gate decision, its criteria, or the evidence it rested on;
- an enforcement-readiness declaration, as premature or as withheld;
- a ledger entry that cannot be independently verified, or a gap presented as closed without verified ledger data;
- interim authority that has outrun its stated limits or been reauthorized without stated reasons; or
- a provisional entry presented as settled, or a closing of the Adoption Transition Record without the hand-over.

Who hears the contest:

- A contest of an enforcement-readiness declaration is decided by a party independent of the ledger owner ([**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*)).
- Where no forum is yet ready for the enforceability scope in question, the interim route under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) and [**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*) decides the contest, and this subsection applies from the time a forum is ready.
- The forum is chosen under **section 4.2**, so no office in the adopter's own line, no ledger owner, no Adoption Transition authority, and no party that made the contested decision or declaration holds a seat on the review.

Routing follows the real issue in the contest:

- what the adopter may do, is charged to do, or whether it followed its own published plan goes to **Institutional** forums;
- capture, concealment, falsified or withheld ledger data, or retaliation goes to **Integrity** forums where integrity is primary;
- ledger measurement methods and evidence standards go to **Technical Forum Domains**;
- a plan or re-baselining that lowers the fixed Rights-Floor target, or an action beyond lawful authority, goes to **Constitutional** forums through certification or direct routing where this chapter allows it.

The forum's job is to review the contested decision or entry and the way it is being relied on. It may:

- confirm it;
- require correction or a new entry;
- order a gate reopened or a declaration issued;
- limit reliance on it;
- send an underlying issue to the right forum; or
- send a constitutional question for ruling.

The forum must **not** set the plan, its gate criteria, or its target, lower the fixed Rights-Floor target, or rerun the Adoption Transition. A pending contest does not suspend the restraint duties that bind from the effective date, and the tier windows under **Chapter Twenty-Four §4** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from receipt of the contest.

**Adoption Charter entries:** A contest of an entry in an adopter's [Adoption Charter](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-charter), such as its record custodian, its gatekeeper-check contact, or its forum authority assignment, follows this subsection in the same way, including who hears it and what the forum may and may not do. A contest of a level of trust the adopter extends is not heard under this subsection; [Chapter Twenty §2.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#24-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust) governs it.

<a id="47-keeping-forum-case-records"></a>

#### 4.7 Keeping Forum Case Records

Forum Case Records are kept where the sentients who need them can reach them and where no single failure can erase them.

- The public part of a Forum Case Record is kept in the [Constitutional Library](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#constitutional-library). The restricted part is kept whole in the [Constitutional Archive](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#constitutional-archive), and every sealed item leaves a marker in the Library.
- A custodian independent of the forum that made the entries keeps the Library and the Archive.
- When a matter closes, each non-routine entry gets a physical backup, held offline and apart from the forum's own systems. Routine entries do not need one, and doubt resolves as non-routine.
- An [Essential Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#essential-record) keeps its physical backup in at least three separate places in different regions. Standing Records at slot 5 and above are Essential Records ([Chapter Fifteen §7.5 Physical Backup and Certified Copies](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#75-physical-backup-and-certified-copies)).
- Mechanics are in [CF-23.465 §5.1 Retention Classes and Physical Backup](corpus_forum/cf_23.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-23465-51-retention-classes-and-physical-backup) and [CF-23.465 §5.2 Constitutional Library and Constitutional Archive](corpus_forum/cf_23.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-23465-52-constitutional-library-and-constitutional-archive).

*In plain terms: when many **affected** **parties** share the same underlying harm pattern, forums may widen the case fairly; and no forum family gets to be the only final word when the accusation is essentially that this same family is rigged, conflicted, or hiding the ball — the rules send that to a different lead track with written backups.*

<a id="51-scope-expansion-and-representative-treatment"></a>

#### 5.1 Scope Expansion and Representative Treatment

When one sentient files a case, the forum **may** widen the proceeding to cover an affected **class**, **subclass**, or other group in the same situation.
- Expansion is available when the Forum Case Record shows any of the following:
  - a shared injury that matters;
  - the same unlawful practice;
  - the same decision rule;
  - shared dependence on the same conduct, system, or institutional choice; or
  - a **systems-alignment** issue with material effects upstream or downstream.
- The forum **should** expand when refusing to expand would predictably leave similarly situated **sentients** with no practical remedy.
- It **should** also expand when refusing to expand would predictably produce conflicting rulings, or would block relief that has to work at a **structural** level.
- Expanding the case does not take away the original claimant's **standing**. It also does not erase individual issues that still need person-by-person proof or remedy.

<a id="52-a-transfer-is-one-step-and-keeps-the-case"></a>

#### 5.2 A Transfer Is One Step and Keeps the Case

When a lawful order or published rule sends a matter to another forum, the move is one automated step: "go here."
- The case keeps its Forum Case Record, intake kind, tier, and the time already counted. Only the lead changes. Nothing is re-filed, re-opened, or re-proved.
- The same step updates the docket portal and alerts the parties, the representatives, and both forums. No one has to carry the matter across by hand.
- The order or published rule decides where the matter goes. The step does not decide that, and it cannot be used to steer a matter. The mechanics are in [CF-23.415 §10 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-10-transfer-procedure).

<a id="53-cross-forum-anti-self-judging-rule"></a>

#### 5.3 Cross-Forum Anti-Self-Judging Rule

A **forum** family **must not** be the **sole** **final** **merits** forum for a claim whose **primary** issue is that same family's own **bias**, **capture**, **conflict**, **recusal failure**, **concealment**, **process abuse**, or comparable **integrity** violation.
- Primary-stakes routing still governs. The **independent** lead family for such claims is assigned as follows unless a more specific constitutional rule controls:
  - against **Constitutional** forums: **Integrity** first and **Institutional** as backup
  - against **Institutional** forums: **Constitutional** first and **Integrity** as backup
  - against **Integrity** forums: **Institutional** first and **Constitutional** as backup
  - against **Environment** forums: **Institutional** first and **Integrity** as backup
- This rule does **not** convert every case naming a forum into a special venue rule. It applies only where **anti-self-judging** protection is materially necessary to preserve **independence**, **contestability**, or **public** **trust**.

<a id="54-family-level-capture"></a>

#### 5.4 Family-Level Capture

Where credible evidence indicates **capture**, compromise, coercive control, coordinated obstruction, or structural dependency affecting a **forum family** as a whole, ordinary intra-family recusal, appeal, or continuity process is not sufficient by itself.
- The forum system must activate the following, sufficient to restore lawful, contestable merits adjudication:
  - family-level backup routing;
  - independent preservation of records; and
  - time-bound external review.
- The captured or compromised family must not control the activation record, restoration review, or final determination of its own restored independence.
- Backup authority remains limited to what is necessary for:
  - lawful merits adjudication;
  - emergency relief;
  - record custody; and
  - restoration.
- It does not permanently absorb the captured family's jurisdiction or displace **Constitutional** certification where structural remedy or constitutional meaning is materially at issue.

<a id="55-capacity-failure-is-heard-outside-the-starved-body"></a>

#### 5.5 Capacity Failure Is Heard Outside the Starved Body

A claim that a forum family, remedy system, or standing-integration function is under capacity — designed backlog, inaccessible intake, chronic underfunding, chronic milestone failure, or a tripped [Chapter Nineteen §2.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#24-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) remedy-parity tripwire — is an anti-self-judging matter. The body whose capacity is in question must not be the sole finder of fact on its own capacity.
- The **Integrity** family is the default lead for capacity-failure claims, with the **section 5** backup pairs applying where Integrity is itself the body in question.
- Any affected party, protected reporter, or self-organized group under [Chapter One §17.4 Aligned Self-Organization](core_01.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#174-aligned-self-organization) may file a capacity-failure claim. It is heard on a Tier B window under **Chapter Twenty-Four section 4** unless the Forum Case Record shows Tier A stakes.
- The body in question must supply its published [Chapter Nineteen §2.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#24-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) and **Chapter Twenty-Four section 4** backlog figures, and may give evidence, but must not control the capacity-failure finding or the corrective order.
- A capacity-failure finding is a [Chapter Twenty §4.2 Remedy-System Durability](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#42-remedy-system-durability) durability failure. It routes corrective orders to the [primary-stakes](#3-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) family responsible for staffing and funding and, where the pattern is chronic or concealed, opens the ordinary Chapter Fifteen path.
