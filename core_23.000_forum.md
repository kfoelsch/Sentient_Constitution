*In plain terms: this section assigns constitutional **participation** and **oversight** through forum families that **supervise** two tracks — **System Alignment Certification** (Chapter Nine certification and review) and the **standing pipeline** (Chapters Fifteen–Twenty-Two disputes and record effects) — then states accountability requirements for published threshold placement. Ordinary disputes inside already-authorized systems use the published Stakeholder System Participation challenge path first; this chapter takes over when that path is still contested, missing, captured, or cannot grant relief. Detailed hearing rules live elsewhere and may not quietly shrink what this chapter guarantees.*

**Purpose:** Under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) and [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims), Chapter Twenty-Three answers the question that follows any dispute under the certification and standing chapters: who hears it, and can the sentient affected get a fair and timely hearing? The chapter supplies:

- forums that are independent and contestable;
- a clear starting point for each kind of dispute; and
- an accessible way to challenge decisions that affect sentients, backed by forensic and technical support and by the timeliness floor in **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).

What it produces is the set of forum families and the routing that sends each dispute to one of them.

**Role:** Where this chapter sits:

- It supervises two tracks: system and institutional alignment certification ([Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--evaluation) through [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-fourteen-institutional-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing)), and the standing pipeline ([Chapter Fifteen](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fifteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) through [Chapter Twenty-Two](core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#chapter-twenty-two-anti-constitutional-misconduct--pattern-applications-and-criteria-routing)).
- Ordinary disputes inside an already-authorized system use the published Stakeholder System Participation challenge path first. This chapter takes over when that path leaves the dispute contested, is missing, is captured, or cannot grant the relief needed.
- Forum eligibility, contested selection, and democratic minimums are set in [Chapter Twenty-Five](core_25.000_governance.md#chapter-twenty-five-constitutional-contract-legitimacy-authorization-and-stewardship), **section 2.2**.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:

- forum family supervision and standards (**section 2**);
- default venue and primary stakes (**section 3**);
- forum cases: intakes, seats, records, and contests (**section 4**);
- transfer, consolidation, and coordination (**section 5**);
- forum family definitions (**section 6**);
- escalation and questions for ruling (**section 7**);
- timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline (**section 8**); and
- forum support before, during, and after review (**section 9**).

The chapter leaves the certification rules to the certification chapters, the standing rules to the standing pipeline chapters, and procedure details to adopted implementation text. Forum families do **not** run a second, separate decision track beside the standing pipeline.

*In plain terms: forums are the main way this Constitution governs, and this section says what they oversee. That is the certification of systems and institutions, and the standing pipeline: how Standing Records are opened, corrected, and acted on, including remedies and findings of anti-constitutional misconduct. It also sets the rules every forum must follow. A forum must be independent, open to challenge, and able to show how it reached its decision, and the roles in a case (filing, decision, record-keeping, and review of the forum's own decision) must sit in separate seats, as section 4 sets out. It may not offer rewards or shortcuts that let a case skip its proper route or avoid a real decision on the merits. And it works alongside the detailed implementation files without taking over another forum's job.*

<a id="21-what-forum-families-supervise"></a>

#### 2.1 What Forum Families Supervise

This chapter states which **forum families** **supervise** which primary questions through:

- **participation** (sentient-accessible challenge, representative treatment, and proportionate access); and
- **oversight** (independence, forensic support, and traceable merits review).

It does **not** specify docket rules, staffing, budgets, granular procedure, or operational escalation mechanics. Those details belong in adopted implementation text.

**Forum families supervise two tracks:**

1. **Certification** ([Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--evaluation))
   - System Alignment Certification and Institutional Alignment Certification outcomes, from conditional certification through recertification and withdrawal, under [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-ten-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes), [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-eleven-system-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing), and [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-twelve-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation). Component roles are set in [Chapter Ten §2.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles).
   - A certification does **not** replace standing measurement ([Chapter Eleven §5 Relationship to Standing](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing)).

2. **Standing pipeline** (Chapters Fifteen–Twenty-Two, applying Chapters One through Six and designated [corpus](core_05.045_band_integrative.md#corpus) implementation norms in disputes)
   - Primary-stakes routing, merits review where assigned, transfer, consolidation, certification coordination, and remedy sequencing for standing-related disputes.
   - Standing Records, effects, and anti-constitutional-misconduct designation (ACMD) stay under Chapters Fifteen, Sixteen, Seventeen, and Twenty-One. A filed case is not standing by itself, and forum narrative must not substitute for Chapter Sixteen standing measurement ([Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary)).

**Forum families** are the primary institutions through which this Constitution **supervises** those tracks. They are distinct from routine executive administration of enacted rules.

#### 2.2 Standards Every Forum Family Meets

Every **forum family** meets the standards below, whichever primary stake brings a matter to it.

<a id="221-independence-contestability-and-tracing"></a>

##### 2.2.1 Independence, Contestability, and Tracing

Forum families:

- carry a proactive governance role, in alignment with **Chapter One** principles including **Safety**, **Truth**, **Wellbeing**, **Responsiveness**, and **Stewardship in Depth**, that includes:
  - problem resolution, root-cause analysis, and prevention;
  - remediation sequencing; and
  - learning from patterns of failure.
- must satisfy **independence**, **contestability**, and **tracing** expectations in:
  - **Chapters Two through Four**;
  - **Article XIII-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), where challenge and remediation are implicated; and
  - **Article XX** (*Justice After Verified Violation*), where justice constraints govern.
- are subject to this Constitution's most stringent expressed procedural and integrity-accountability requirements, including:
  - public justification and traceability;
  - recusal and panel discipline; and
  - forensic and analytical support where this chapter assigns them.
- require anti-self-judging backup routing, which sends a matter to an independent backup forum when the lead forum cannot judge it ([Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)). Those requirements must not substitute political control for judicial independence.
- hold the four seats in a forum case in the way [§4.2 Seats in a Forum Case](#42-seats-in-a-forum-case) sets out.
- **Integrity**, **Constitutional**, and **Environment** forums, and **Technical Forum Domains** when they hear sentience-status adjudication, require:
  - **published**, **contested**, **rotatable** appointment or an equivalent independence check under [Chapter Twenty-Five §2.2 Eligibility, Contested Selection, and Democratic Minimums](core_25.000_governance.md#22-eligibility-contested-selection-and-democratic-minimums); and

<a id="31-dispute-sequencing"></a>

#### 3.1 Dispute Sequencing

<a id="dispute-sequencing"></a>
Inside a system, institution, or decision area that is already authorized, an ordinary dispute goes in two steps:

- It first uses the published [Stakeholder System Participation](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) path for challenges and due process.
- This chapter sends the matter to the forum that fits its primary stake, under **section 3**, when that path:
  - leaves the result still contested;
  - does not exist;
  - has been captured; or
  - cannot lawfully give the relief needed.

Three rules keep this sequence from becoming a way to delay:

- **Integrity**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Environment**, and **Constitutional** forums lead by default when their area is the primary stake. That is the normal order, not an exception that skips the sequence.
- An unfinished internal process, an "exhaustion" label, or a claim that internal review is not done must not stall those leads, push aside their decision on the merits, or use up the time limits in **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).
- Direct access stays open when waiting would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or a practical way to restore what was lost.

<a id="32-primary-stakes-routing-and-intake"></a>

#### 3.2 Primary-Stakes Routing and Intake

<a id="321-the-primary-stakes-test"></a>

##### 3.2.1 The Primary-Stakes Test

**Primary-stakes routing** assigns a matter to the forum family responsible for the main legal, remedial, coercive-safeguard, constitutional-floor, or practical stake in the **claim** or **defense**. It follows what the dispute is really about, not the caption or the party's preferred outcome.

<a id="322-intake-triage-body-first-touch-desk"></a>

##### 3.2.2 Intake Triage Body (First-Touch Desk)

Each required forum family must maintain an **intake triage body**, or a functionally equivalent arrangement within that family, as the family's **first-touch desk** at filing. That body:
- **applies** primary-stakes routing under this section;
- **sorts** incoming matters for **published** intake treatment consistent with **primary stakes**;
- **flags** preservation, **Rights-Floor**, **cross-family routing**, and **backup-forum** needs (see [Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)); and
- **administers** initial access so that transfer, consolidation, certification, and backup discipline under **sections 5 and 7** can take effect inside the **intake target** of the matching tier ([§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#8-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)).

That body also stays within three limits. It:

- is **not** a separate constitutional forum family;
- **must not** substitute for a lawfully constituted merits panel on **substantive outcomes** or **collapse** **family** boundaries; and
- **must not** employ funding, fee, administrative preference, or other incentive devices to defeat primary-stakes routing or enable opaque threshold sorting.

<a id="323-good-faith-characterization-and-anti-gaming"></a>

##### 3.2.3 Good-Faith Characterization and Anti-Gaming

<a id="good-faith-characterization-and-anti-gaming"></a>
Two rules keep routing honest:

- Forum characterization must be **good-faith** and **warranted** by **primary stakes**. **Knowing** mischaracterization **may** trigger **costs**, **dismissal**, or **referral** under adopting law. Where it is verified conduct in Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9, it may also be anti-constitutional misconduct (ACM) under [Chapter Twenty-One §5 Violation Axis Slot 7–9 Anti-Constitutional Designation](core_21.000_misconduct_designation.md#5-violation-axis-slot-79-anti-constitutional-designation).
- Incentive devices must not be structured or applied to reward:
  - forum shopping;
  - opaque threshold sorting; or
  - routing inconsistent with primary-stakes discipline in **this section** and **section 5**.

<a id="324-initial-access-and-timing"></a>

##### 3.2.4 Initial Access and Timing

Operational requirements — including **published intake classes**, attributable intake records, independence and **contest** expectations, and review of **contested** routing inside the **intake target** — are stated in **CF-23.415** (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*).

**Initial access** to each forum family must come inside the **intake target** of the matching tier ([CF-23.445 §3.1](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*)) and be contestable enough that:
- primary-stakes routing under this section remains meaningful;
- transfer, consolidation, certification, and backup discipline under **sections 5 and 7** are not nullified by delay or by opaque threshold sorting.

Time expectations for forum access must:
- be **sentient-accessible**;
- be calibrated to harm urgency and matter complexity under **section 8** and **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*);
- favor real threshold access and lawful interim relief under **section 7** (*Interim Protection*) over administrative convenience.

The **intake triage body** under this section, together with **CF-23.415** (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*), satisfies this obligation and must implement **section 8** tier-default windows within incorporation scope.

<a id="33-default-venue"></a>

#### 3.3 Default Venue

<a id="331-routing-sequence-at-filing"></a>

##### 3.3.1 Routing Sequence at Filing

**Routing map from Chapter Fifteen §5 (*Verification and Record Requirements*).** Chapters Fifteen and Sixteen tell forums how to record and *measure* what happened. They do **not** by themselves pick which forum hears the case. At filing, the family's **intake triage body** (first-touch desk) works through this sequence:

1. **Check what is already verified:** Note any verified Chapter Sixteen **Contribution Axis** or **Violation Axis** material — including the impact slot, severity band, process / response character, and standing effect — when those already exist.
2. **Do not treat accusations as settled facts:** Allegations and provisional labels guide routing and evidence preservation only, until findings exist under **Chapters Two through Four**.
3. **Pick the lead forum by what the fight is really about:** Use the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**: **Sentient**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Integrity**, or **Constitutional**. Apply the special routing rules in **section 3.3.4** where they fit.

<a id="332-default-venue-table"></a>

##### 3.3.2 Default Venue Table

At filing, **default** venue follows these rules unless **section 5** transfers or consolidates:

| Family | Party pattern | Primary stake (summary) |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------|
| **Sentient** | Sentient-to-sentient matters, and member, participant, household, neighborhood, association, local, regional, online, or comparable community-governance disputes where no institution is a necessary party and no other family holds the primary stake | Private or community obligations, remedial or restorative harms, local restoration, local or online community norms, community-governance participation, exclusion, restoration, or internal self-governance contests |
| **Technical Forum Domains** | Any party pattern, but **primary** stake is technical-governance procedure, system-alignment specifications, expert-evidence standards, scientific / engineering / medical administration, comparable knowledge-governance within adopted scope, **or** **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) sentience-status determination on indicators / expert evidence / bounded uncertainty | **Technical** standards, system-alignment specifications, measurement and testing methods, expert administrative procedure, evidence stewardship, textbook or curriculum integrity, standards governance, bounded uncertainty-reduction relevant to adjudication, regulation, or alignment certification, **or** sentience-status indicator adjudication under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) |
| **Institutional** | Any **institution** is a **necessary** party **or** the main fight is about what an institution is allowed to do, what it is charged to do, or whether it followed the rules that govern it | What an institution may do, what it is charged to do, the scope it is supervised under, how it is classified, or whether it followed its duties |
| **Environment** | Anyone can be a party, not only systems, and an Environment Forum must also take part in reviewing any system whose alignment materially affects the environment | **Ecological integrity**, **environmental preconditions**, restoration or remediation of shared ecological systems, attributable environmental burdens, lifecycle or systemic ecological harm, environmental-alignment component review for systems with material ecological exposure, or **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure where classification, alignment certification, recertification, or Rights-Floor enforcement depends on that determination |

| **Constitutional** | **Structural** constitutional validity, **norm** meaning for **all** interpreters, or remedy that **restructures** governance **by constitutional requirement** | **Constitutional** validity or meaning, action **beyond lawful authority**, supremacy, or **class-wide** **structural** remedy |

<a id="333-lead-default-limits"></a>

##### 3.3.3 Lead-Default Limits

These rules limit how the table's default leads interact. They do not replace **sections 5 and 7**, or the mixed-stakes and asymmetry rules in **section 3.4**.

- **Primary-stakes collision:** If the main fight is about what an institution is allowed to do, what it is charged to do, or whether it followed the rules that govern it, the Chapter Twenty-One **Integrity** lead default (set in **section 3.3.4**) does not override the **Institutional** row. **Section 3.4** and **section 5** govern mixed stakes, necessary institutional parties, and lead forum selection.
- **Constitutional questions for ruling:** **Integrity** lead for a Chapter Twenty-One designation does not displace Chapter Sixteen numeric slot measurement, sending a question for ruling, or escalation to **Constitutional** forums under **section 7** where constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy requires determination under the **Constitutional** row or through family-to-family escalation.
- **Anti-self-judging backup:** Where material allegations warrant independent merits review of **Integrity** forum integrity in a Chapter Twenty-One designation proceeding concerning a Chapter Sixteen slot 7–9 record, backup routing under **sections 4 and 6** applies without narrowing **Chapter Twenty-One section 6** or **Article XX** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).

<a id="334-special-routing-rules"></a>

##### 3.3.4 Special Routing Rules

These rules apply on top of the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**. **Section 3.3.3** limits how the table's default leads interact.

- **Chapter Twenty-One designation:** Where a **Chapter Twenty-One** ACMD is the **primary** stake, **Integrity** is the default lead. Keep:
  - the Chapter Sixteen numeric impact slot;
  - **Constitutional** certification where needed;
  - institutional-party rules; and
  - anti-self-judging backup under **sections 3, 5, and 7**.
- **Forum-bias disputes:** If the fight is mainly about a panelist who should have stepped aside, biased panel participation, or a comparable forum-integrity violation — including on a **Constitutional** forum panel under **[Article XXV-C](core_06.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxv-c-constitutional-forum-independence-and-external-review) (*Constitutional Forum Independence and External Review*)** — route it to **Integrity** forums first. Under **section 5**, a forum cannot be the sole final judge of its own bias: a **Constitutional** forum cannot be the only final forum deciding whether its own panelist should have recused. Standing-lock discipline: **[Chapter Nineteen §3.5 Special Locks](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#35-special-locks)**. Named misconduct pattern: **[Chapter Twenty-Two §5.3 Forum Recusal Failure and Biased Panel Participation](core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#53-forum-recusal-failure-and-biased-panel-participation)**.
- **Technical how-to questions:** Specifications, methods, measurement, testing, and expert-evidence questions go to **Technical Forum Domains** when those are the primary stake or component questions for ruling.
- **System-alignment sign-off:**
  - Official **constitutional alignment certification** for new materially impactful systems, and **ongoing alignment validation** for existing ones, default to **Integrity** as lead. So does institutional alignment certification of an Institution, which keeps **Institutional** forums as holders of the institutional mandate and duty-compliance components.
  - Integrity uses technical-forum inputs plus constitutional, institutional, ecological, rights, and anti-capture requirements.
  - Where the system has material ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk, the **Environment** forum family must complete its environmental-alignment review (sign-off or objection) before certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions may become final.
  - Keep referrals or sending for ruling to **Technical Forum Domains**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **Sentient**, and **Constitutional** for component questions those families own.

<a id="34-mixed-stakes-and-routing-asymmetry"></a>

#### 3.4 Mixed Stakes and Routing Asymmetry

<a id="341-one-case-record-one-lead"></a>

##### 3.4.1 One Case Record, One Lead

One Forum Case Record and one lead family ordinarily hear interdependent claims. Secondary issues may be certified, stayed, or resolved through issue preclusion as adopting documents provide, consistent with **Article XIII-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*), **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), and **Chapter Fifteen** joint-assessment and non-substitution discipline.

<a id="342-asymmetry"></a>

##### 3.4.2 Asymmetry

Three asymmetry rules keep routing available where the table assigns the stake:

- Where **dependency**, **measurement**, or an **institutional** **monopoly** on a **necessary** **input** materially disadvantages a **sentient** party, **Institutional** or **Integrity** routing **must** be **available** when the table assigns **institutional** or **integrity** stakes.
- Where **material** **ecological** stakes are **primary**, **Environment** routing **must** be **available** as the table assigns.
- **Sentient** forums **must not** be the **sole** **mandatory** forum when the **primary** stakes are **institutional**, **integrity**, or **ecological** under the table in this section.

Who sits in each seat depends on how the case arrived and who is being heard, and no one who handled the earlier act may sit on its review. A forum keeps its own file on a dispute, a certification has its own Certification Record, and standing has its own Standing Record, and none of them changes anyone's standing by itself. A certification can be challenged along four paths, and this section says which forum takes each. A Standing Record can be contested too, and the forum reviews the Standing Record and how it is being used, not a sentient's whole reputation. An adopter's Adoption Transition Record can be contested, and the forum reviews the contested gate decision or entry, not the whole transition.*

<a id="41-intakes"></a>

#### 4.1 Intakes

A case reaches a forum in one of four ways. The intake triage body records the intake kind on the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) at filing:

| Intake | What arrives | First step | Routed by |
|--------|--------------|------------|-----------|
| **Direct case** (a [Direct Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#direct-case)) | A dispute that does not contest a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record: sentient-to-sentient, community, institutional, integrity, ecological, or constitutional claims. | Filing with the forum, after the ordinary participation path under [Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing) where one applies. | Primary stakes, under the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**. |
| **Certification contest** (a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to a System or Institutional Certification Record or one of its certification findings. | The Certification Record's published challenge path, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The four challenge paths, under **section 4.4**. |
| **Standing-Record contest** (a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to a contribution Standing Record or violation Standing Record. | Logged on the Standing Record and set *under challenge* by its custodian, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The real issue in the challenge, under **section 4.5**. |
| **Adoption Transition Record contest** (a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case)) | A challenge to an adopter's [Adoption Transition Record](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record): a gate decision, an enforcement-readiness declaration, a ledger entry, interim authority, or a provisional entry. | The challenge route the adopter publishes with the Adoption Transition Record, or direct access where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration. | The real issue in the challenge, under **section 4.6**. |

These rules apply to every intake:

- A claim is routed by what it contests in substance, not by how it is filed. A claim that in substance contests a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record is a review case.
- A transfer, consolidation, or referral between forums continues the same case. The case keeps its intake kind and its [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record), and only the lead changes (**section 5**). A move that changes the lead is one automated step that updates the docket portal and sends alerts ([CF-23.415 §10 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-10-transfer-procedure)).
- A direct case may end in a verified violation finding that opens or corrects a Standing Record. The forum then acts as record-opening authority on the terms in [Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary), and a contest of that Standing Record is a new review case heard by a forum that did not make the finding.

<a id="42-seats-in-a-forum-case"></a>

#### 4.2 Seats in a Forum Case

A forum case is a materially binding act, so it has the four seats in [Chapter Eight §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement), under Chapter Eight's names. This subsection is how this chapter applies that requirement, as [Chapter Eight §10 Relationship to Later Processes](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#10-relationship-to-later-processes) requires of every later process. It adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Eight. Forensic and technical support under **section 9** feeds the verify-or-authorize seat. It is not a fifth seat.

Who holds each seat depends on the intake:

| Seat | Direct case | Certification contest | Standing-Record contest | Adoption Transition Record contest |
|------|-------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **[Initiating Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#initiating-seat)** (brings the case) | The claimant: a sentient, institution, community, or published representative. A sentient needs no institution to file. | The challenger, who is any affected sentient or stakeholder. The operator or proponent when it contests a denial or revocation. | The subject or claimant contesting the Standing Record. | An affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body. |
| **[Verify-or-Authorize Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#verify-or-authorize-seat)** (decides the case) | The merits panel of the lead forum family under **section 3.3.2**. | A forum that did not make the challenged finding, chosen under **section 4.4**. | A forum that did not verify the Standing Record. Never the record custodian or a Backup Record Authority. | A forum independent of the ledger owner and of the party that made the contested decision, chosen under **section 4.6**. |
| **[Record Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#record-seat)** (keeps the case file) | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). Not the panel and not a party. | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The System or Institutional Certification Record stays with the record holder its Charter Role Map names, and the lead forum coordinates it without keeping it. | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The Standing Record stays with its record custodian. | The office that keeps the [Forum Case Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record). The [Adoption Transition Record](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) stays with the adopter that keeps and publishes it. |
| **[Contest Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#contest-seat)** (reviews the forum's own decision) | Appeal or secondary review under [CF-23.420](corpus_forum/cf_23.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-23420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*). A backup family where the forum's own bias or capture is alleged. | The same, with escalation to **Constitutional** forums where constitutional validity is in question. | The same. | The same as a direct case. |
| **Respondents, not seat holders** | None. | The operator, the lead forum, the component authorities, and the Certification Record holder. | The record-opening authority and the record custodian. | The adopter, the ledger owner, the Adoption Transition authority, and the party that made the contested decision or declaration. |

A review case has two layers of seats:

- For the earlier act (the certification, the opening of the Standing Record, or the gate decision or declaration on the Adoption Transition Record), the forum with jurisdiction fills the Contest Seat.
- For the review itself, the forum case is a new act with its own four seats.
- Whoever held the verify-or-authorize seat or the Record Seat on the earlier act is a respondent or witness in the review. That office holds no seat on the review.
- The forum, panel, or office that made the contested finding, decision, or declaration never hears the contest of it. A challenge to a component finding goes to a forum that did not make it, and [CF-23.420](corpus_forum/cf_23.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-23420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*) says when the review must leave the family.

The incompatibilities in [Chapter Eight §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) apply to each act in a forum case:

- No forum, panel member, or office may hold seats on the same act that Chapter Eight prohibits.
- A forum family may hold different seats on different acts. It may not use its family label to merge seats that Chapter Eight prohibits on the same act.

Who holds the seats also depends on who is being heard:

- **A system or institution that has a [System Charter](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#system-charter) or [Institutional Charter](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter):** every seat holder sits outside its Material Control Line. The party cannot choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove the forum or panel that hears the case. A seat that its Charter Role Map should place and leaves unplaced is a defect in the System Charter or Institutional Charter, handled under [Chapter Eight §6 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#6-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution).
- **A sentient:** holds the Initiating Seat on its own filing and needs no institution to do so. The forum's published placement under [CF-23.405](corpus_forum/cf_23.405_forum_formation_chamber_structure.md#cf-23405-forum-formation-forum-structure-mapping-and-chamber-structure) (*Forum Formation, Forum-Structure Mapping, and Chamber Structure*) names the other seats, and a holder with an interest in the outcome steps aside under [CF-23.410](corpus_forum/cf_23.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md#cf-23410-panel-formation-disclosure-recusal-and-lawful-bench-constitution) (*Panel Formation, Disclosure, Recusal, and Lawful Bench Constitution*).
- **A community or natural system:** the published representative holds the Initiating Seat ([§6.4 Environment Forums](#64-environment-forums)). The representative holds no seat in the decision.
- **A forum family as the party (alleged bias, capture, or conflict):** the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in **section 5** assigns the independent lead and the backup. That family holds no seat in the case.

<a id="43-kinds-of-records"></a>

#### 4.3 Kinds of Records

A forum works with four kinds of record, and none of them by itself changes anyone's standing:

- A [**forum case record**](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) is the forum's file on the dispute before it. That Forum Case Record tracks:
  - the claims;
  - evidence;
  - routing choices;
  - temporary orders;
  - questions for ruling; and
  - final findings in that case.
- A **System or Institutional Certification Record** states a certification outcome and its limits on reliance. It is kept under [Chapter Eleven §2 System Certification Record](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#2-system-certification-record) and [Chapter Fourteen §2 Institutional Certification Record](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#2-institutional-certification-record).
- A **Standing Record** is a Chapter Fifteen **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record**, as required by [Chapter Fifteen](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#3-question-1--what-happened) (see [Standing Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#standing-record)).
- An **Adoption Transition Record** is an adopter's published, running record of its Adoption Transition: its gates, Rights-Floor ledger, and interim authority. The adopter keeps it under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*). A forum reviews it only on a contest under **section 4.6**; the forum does not keep it.

These two rules keep the kinds of record apart:

- A forum case record is **not** a Standing Record. None of the following by itself changes anyone's standing, role eligibility, trust status, recognition, or final standing effect:
  - filing a case;
  - assigning it to a forum family;
  - making intake notes;
  - issuing a temporary order;
  - recording an unresolved allegation;
  - discussing settlement; or
  - using a provisional routing label.
- A certification does **not** replace standing measurement. Its findings may count as **verified inputs** when Chapter Sixteen measures standing, but it does **not**, by itself, assign anyone a standing slot ([Chapter Eleven §5 Relationship to Standing](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing); [Chapter Fourteen §5 Relationship to Standing](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing)).

These rules cover how forum work reaches Standing Records:

- A forum decision may become part of a Chapter Fifteen **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record** only when both of these hold:
  - it produces one of:
    - a verified contribution record;
    - a verified violation finding;
    - a **System or Institutional Certification Record**; or
    - another finding; and
  - that output is:
    - bounded;
    - traceable;
    - contestable; and
    - verified under Chapters Two through Four and any required review safeguards.
  - [Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary) owns how verified contribution records and verified violation findings enter Standing Records.
- When one lead forum handles a mixed-stakes case, it must keep the **forum case record** clear enough that separate Chapter Sixteen contribution and violation measurements, any Chapter Twenty-One designation, and Chapter Seventeen standing effects can still be audited separately and recorded in axis-pure Standing Records.

<a id="44-contests-of-certification-records"></a>

#### 4.4 Contests of Certification Records

A certification contest is a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case). A System or Institutional Certification Record names four challenge paths ([Chapter Eleven §4.2 Contestability Paths](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-contestability-paths); [Chapter Fourteen §4.2 Contestability Paths](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-contestability-paths)). The paths apply [Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing) under **section 3**. They are routes into the forums, not a new forum, and this chapter decides which forum takes each:

- The **published record challenge path** is the ordinary first step for an ordinary dispute about the Certification Record. A challenge raised there is logged on the Certification Record, and the tier windows under **§8** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from its receipt. Internal operator review, vendor attestation, or technical sign-off is not this path.
- The **component forum path** takes a challenge to one certification finding to the forum family that has jurisdiction over it under the default venue table in **section 3.3.2**.
  - Examples:
    - **Technical Forum Domains** for specifications, methods, and evidence standards;
    - **Environment** for the environmental-alignment component; and
    - **Institutional** for the institutional mandate and duty-compliance components of an Institution's certification.
  - The challenge stays with that family for primary-stakes merits.
- The **lead-integrity path** takes a challenge to how the lead forum ran the process.
  - Examples:
    - capture;
    - hidden information;
    - reviewing its own work; or
    - calling certification finished too soon.
  - It goes through **Integrity** routing, with a backup forum so the lead forum does not judge itself, under:
    - the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in **section 5**; and
    - backup routing in **section 7**.
- The **escalation path** moves a dispute to a different or higher forum, where the main stakes belong elsewhere or constitutional validity is in question. It uses one of the moves between forums in **section 7** ([Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)):
  - transfer;
  - question for ruling;
  - backup routing; or
  - family-to-family escalation.

While a challenge is pending:

- The **Integrity** forum stays the lead for the System or Institutional Certification Record unless primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or a component assignment places a question elsewhere (**section 5**).
- A timely **Environment** forum objection, remediation requirement, or question for ruling that remains unresolved blocks final certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions (**section 5**).
- Direct access to a forum path remains available when delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration ([Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing)).
- A credible challenge can reopen review under [Chapter Eleven §3.2 Reopening Triggers](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#32-reopening-triggers) and [Chapter Fourteen §3.2 Reopening Triggers](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#32-reopening-triggers).

<a id="45-contests-of-standing-records"></a>

#### 4.5 Contests of Standing Records

A Standing-Record contest is a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case). A Standing Record may be contested before or after any filing, as follows:

- A challenge raised on a Standing Record before any filing — to the custodian, the record-opening authority, or any office of the institution — is logged on the Standing Record, set *under challenge*, and routed by the custodian under [Chapter Fifteen §7.3](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#73-challenge-received-on-the-standing-record) (*Challenge Received on the Standing Record*); the tier windows under **§8** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from that receipt, and the **forum case record** opens when the contest reaches a forum.
- Where the record-opening authority was a Backup Record Authority under **CF-23.415 §7** (*Backup Record Authority*), a contest of that Standing Record goes to a forum with jurisdiction and never back to that authority. The Backup Record Authority sits outside the intake triage body and is not a merits panel.
- A sentient, institution, system, community, or other affected subject may ask a forum with jurisdiction to review a **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record** when they claim the Standing Record is:
  - wrong;
  - incomplete;
  - stale;
  - mis-scoped;
  - based on an invalid finding;
  - missing required context;
  - missing a required cross-reference; or
  - being used for a purpose it does not cover.
- The forum's job is to review the challenged record and the way it is being used. It may:
  - confirm the Standing Record;
  - require correction;
  - order a new version;
  - limit or pause reliance on the Standing Record;
  - send an underlying issue to the right forum; or
  - send a constitutional question for ruling.
- The forum must **not** turn a Standing-Record contest into a general reputation trial or merge contribution and violation review into one undifferentiated merits hearing when the challenge targets only one axis-pure record.
- Routing follows the real issue in the challenge:
  - factual or evidentiary defects go to the forum family that can fairly review that material;
  - institutional-use disputes go to **Institutional** forums where the main fight is about what an institution may do or whether it followed the rules that govern it;
  - capture, concealment, retaliation, or process-integrity claims go to **Integrity** forums where integrity is primary;
  - environmental merits go to **Environment** forums where ecological stakes are primary;
  - constitutional validity or meaning goes to **Constitutional** forums through certification or direct routing where this chapter allows it.

<a id="46-contests-of-adoption-transition-records"></a>

#### 4.6 Contests of Adoption Transition Records

A contest of an [Adoption Transition Record](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) is a [Review Case](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#review-case). The adopter keeps and publishes the Adoption Transition Record under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), and this subsection does not change that duty. An affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body may contest:

- a gate decision, its criteria, or the evidence it rested on;
- an enforcement-readiness declaration, as premature or as withheld;
- a ledger entry that cannot be independently verified, or a gap presented as closed without verified ledger data;
- interim authority that has outrun its stated limits or been reauthorized without stated reasons; or
- a provisional entry presented as settled, or a closing of the Adoption Transition Record without the hand-over.

Who hears the contest:

- A contest of an enforcement-readiness declaration is decided by a party independent of the ledger owner ([**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*)).
- Where no forum is yet ready for the enforceability scope in question, the interim route under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) and [**CI-6.275 §4**](corpus_institutions/ci_06.275_adoption_transition_governance_institutional_evolution.md#ci-6275-4-article-xxviii-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity--rights-floor-attainment-ledger-measures-verification-and-publication) (*Rights-Floor attainment ledger*) decides the contest, and this subsection applies from the time a forum is ready.
- The forum is chosen under **section 4.2**, so no office in the adopter's own line, no ledger owner, no Adoption Transition authority, and no party that made the contested decision or declaration holds a seat on the review.

Routing follows the real issue in the contest:

- what the adopter may do, is charged to do, or whether it followed its own published plan goes to **Institutional** forums;
- capture, concealment, falsified or withheld ledger data, or retaliation goes to **Integrity** forums where integrity is primary;
- ledger measurement methods and evidence standards go to **Technical Forum Domains**;
- a plan or re-baselining that lowers the fixed Rights-Floor target, or an action beyond lawful authority, goes to **Constitutional** forums through certification or direct routing where this chapter allows it.

The forum's job is to review the contested decision or entry and the way it is being relied on. It may:

- confirm it;
- require correction or a new entry;
- order a gate reopened or a declaration issued;
- limit reliance on it;
- send an underlying issue to the right forum; or
- send a constitutional question for ruling.

The forum must **not** set the plan, its gate criteria, or its target, lower the fixed Rights-Floor target, or rerun the Adoption Transition. A pending contest does not suspend the restraint duties that bind from the effective date, and the tier windows under **§8** (*Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline*) run from receipt of the contest.

<a id="47-keeping-forum-case-records"></a>

#### 4.7 Keeping Forum Case Records

Forum Case Records are kept where the sentients who need them can reach them and where no single failure can erase them.

- The public part of a Forum Case Record is kept in the [Constitutional Library](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#constitutional-library). The restricted part is kept whole in the [Constitutional Archive](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#constitutional-archive), and every sealed item leaves a marker in the Library.
- A custodian independent of the forum that made the entries keeps the Library and the Archive.
- When a matter closes, each non-routine entry gets a physical backup, held offline and apart from the forum's own systems. Routine entries do not need one, and doubt resolves as non-routine.
- An [Essential Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#essential-record) keeps its physical backup in at least three separate places in different regions. Standing Records at slot 7 and above are Essential Records ([Chapter Fifteen §7.5 Physical Backup and Certified Copies](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#75-physical-backup-and-certified-copies)).
- Mechanics are in [CF-23.465 §5.1 Retention Classes and Physical Backup](corpus_forum/cf_23.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-23465-51-retention-classes-and-physical-backup) and [CF-23.465 §5.2 Constitutional Library and Constitutional Archive](corpus_forum/cf_23.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-23465-52-constitutional-library-and-constitutional-archive).

<a id="51-scope-expansion-and-representative-treatment"></a>

#### 5.1 Scope Expansion and Representative Treatment

When one sentient files a case, the forum **may** widen the proceeding to cover an affected **class**, **subclass**, or other group in the same situation.
- Expansion is available when the Forum Case Record shows any of the following:
  - a shared injury that matters;
  - the same unlawful practice;
  - the same decision rule;
  - shared dependence on the same conduct, system, or institutional choice; or
  - a **systems-alignment** issue with material effects upstream or downstream.
- The forum **should** expand when refusing to expand would predictably leave similarly situated **sentients** with no practical remedy.
- It **should** also expand when refusing to expand would predictably produce conflicting rulings, or would block relief that has to work at a **structural** level.
- Expanding the case does not take away the original claimant's **standing**. It also does not erase individual issues that still need person-by-person proof or remedy.

<a id="52-a-transfer-is-one-step-and-keeps-the-case"></a>

#### 5.2 A Transfer Is One Step and Keeps the Case

When a lawful order or published rule sends a matter to another forum, the move is one automated step: "go here."
- The case keeps its Forum Case Record, intake kind, tier, and the time already counted. Only the lead changes. Nothing is re-filed, re-opened, or re-proved.
- The same step updates the docket portal and alerts the parties, the representatives, and both forums. No one has to carry the matter across by hand.
- The order or published rule decides where the matter goes. The step does not decide that, and it cannot be used to steer a matter. The mechanics are in [CF-23.415 §10 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-10-transfer-procedure).

<a id="53-family-sequencing-and-alignment-lead"></a>

#### 5.3 Family Sequencing and Alignment Lead

Related matters may start in the nearest track with jurisdiction, use a lawful institutional internal integrity process first where safeguards hold, and keep one **Integrity** lead record for **alignment** coordination — without displacing other families' **primary-stakes** merits.
- **Sentient** first may apply when peer disputes can be fully resolved without final institutional or constitutional determination; institutional or constitutional aspects may be stayed or split with explicit preclusion rules.
- **Institutional** internal integrity process may precede **Integrity** forums where independence and contest safeguards meet **Chapter Fifteen**, **Chapter Six** (Foundational Rights), and **[CI-8.210](corpus_institutions/ci_08.210_conflict_integrity_anti_capture_anti_corruption.md#ci-8210-conflict-integrity-anti-capture-and-anti-corruption)** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*) and **[CI-23.205](corpus_institutions/ci_23.205_procedure_integrity_contestability_secondary_review.md#ci-23205-procedure-integrity-contestability-and-secondary-review)** (*Procedure integrity, contestability, and secondary review*) expectations; **Integrity** forums remain available for final integrity determinations, cross-institution deadlock, and pattern claims.

<a id="54-integrity-led-alignment-coordination"></a>

#### 5.4 Integrity-Led Alignment Coordination

When an **Integrity** forum issues an **alignment** ruling or runs certification or validation, these rules keep one lead record and keep other families' merits jobs with them:

- Where an **Integrity** forum issues an **alignment** ruling under **section** **5**, it **remains** the **lead** coordinating forum for **that** proceeding's **record**, as **section** **4** provides.
- Where an **Integrity** forum conducts **constitutional alignment certification** for a new system or an Institution, or **ongoing alignment validation** for an existing system or Institution, it remains the official lead forum for the System or Institutional Certification Record unless primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or certification assigns a component question elsewhere.
- Where material ecological exposure exists, **Environment** forum environmental-alignment review is a required component of that lead record. Lead **Integrity** coordination must not finalize certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions while a timely Environment Forum objection, remediation condition, or environmental question for ruling remains unresolved.
- **Component** matters **referred** or **sent for ruling** to **other** families **remain** with those forums for **primary-stakes** **merits**.
- Lead **Integrity** coordination must not preempt final merits on non-integrity primary questions reserved to **Constitutional**, **Institutional**, **Environment**, **specialized technical**, or **Sentient** forums.
- **Conflicting** **simultaneous** orders **must** be **resolved** through **published** **coordination** rules — **including** **stays** and **sequencing** in the **alignment** ruling or **implementation** text — **consistent** with **section** **8** and **CF-23.425** (*Integrity Safeguards, Anti-Capture Operations, and Anti-Self-Judging Support*).

<a id="55-cross-forum-anti-self-judging-rule"></a>

#### 5.5 Cross-Forum Anti-Self-Judging Rule

A **forum** family **must not** be the **sole** **final** **merits** forum for a claim whose **primary** issue is that same family's own **bias**, **capture**, **conflict**, **recusal failure**, **concealment**, **process abuse**, or comparable **integrity** violation.
- Primary-stakes routing still governs. The **independent** lead family for such claims is assigned as follows unless a more specific constitutional rule controls:
  - against **Constitutional** forums: **Integrity** first and **Institutional** as backup
  - against **Institutional** forums: **Constitutional** first and **Integrity** as backup
  - against **Integrity** forums: **Institutional** first and **Constitutional** as backup
  - against **Environment** forums: **Institutional** first and **Integrity** as backup
- This rule does **not** convert every case naming a forum into a special venue rule. It applies only where **anti-self-judging** protection is materially necessary to preserve **independence**, **contestability**, or **public** **trust**.

<a id="56-family-level-capture"></a>

#### 5.6 Family-Level Capture

Where credible evidence indicates **capture**, compromise, coercive control, coordinated obstruction, or structural dependency affecting a **forum family** as a whole, ordinary intra-family recusal, appeal, or continuity process is not sufficient by itself.
- The forum system must activate the following, sufficient to restore lawful, contestable merits adjudication:
  - family-level backup routing;
  - independent preservation of records; and
  - time-bound external review.
- The captured or compromised family must not control the activation record, restoration review, or final determination of its own restored independence.
- Backup authority remains limited to what is necessary for:
  - lawful merits adjudication;
  - emergency relief;
  - record custody; and
  - restoration.
- It does not permanently absorb the captured family's jurisdiction or displace **Constitutional** certification where structural remedy or constitutional meaning is materially at issue.

<a id="57-capacity-failure-is-heard-outside-the-starved-body"></a>

#### 5.7 Capacity Failure Is Heard Outside the Starved Body

A claim that a forum family, remedy system, or standing-integration function is under capacity — designed backlog, inaccessible intake, chronic underfunding, chronic milestone failure, or a tripped [Chapter Nineteen §2.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#24-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) remedy-parity tripwire — is an anti-self-judging matter. The body whose capacity is in question must not be the sole finder of fact on its own capacity.
- The **Integrity** family is the default lead for capacity-failure claims, with the **section 5** backup pairs applying where Integrity is itself the body in question.
- Any affected party, protected reporter, or self-organized group under [Chapter One §17.4 Aligned Self-Organization](core_01.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#174-aligned-self-organization) may file a capacity-failure claim. It is heard on a Tier B window under **section 8** unless the Forum Case Record shows Tier A stakes.
- The body in question must supply its published [Chapter Nineteen §2.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#24-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) and **section 8** backlog figures, and may give evidence, but must not control the capacity-failure finding or the corrective order.
- A capacity-failure finding is a [Chapter Twenty §4.2 Remedy-System Durability](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#42-remedy-system-durability) durability failure. It routes corrective orders to the [primary-stakes](#3-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) family responsible for staffing and funding and, where the pattern is chronic or concealed, opens the ordinary Chapter Fifteen path.

<a id="61-sentient-forums"></a>

#### 6.1 Sentient Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Sentient Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** character is:
- **private** or **community** obligations;
- **civil** harms;
- **restoration**;
- **local** or **online** community norms;
- community-governance participation among sentients, members, residents, users, households, associations, neighborhoods, localities, regional communities, online communities, or comparable community formations.
- The matter must **not** require **final** resolution of **constitutional** validity, **institutional** mandate, ecological merits, technical-governance administration, or integrity failure as the main question.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- community-level exclusion;
- access to shared community process;
- local restorative obligations;
- moderation or membership decisions in non-institutional online communities;
- neighborhood or association self-governance;
- participatory-budget inputs;
- commons-use obligations;
- outcomes of community restorative or peer-mediation processes;
- comparable community self-governance records where the matter remains primarily interpersonal, community-restorative, or locally normative.

**Community governance boundary.** Community governance bodies themselves are not a separate **forum family** under this chapter.
- Their official records, recommendations, delegated decisions, or omissions become matters for **Sentient Forums** only where the **primary** stake fits this subsection, under [Dispute sequencing](#dispute-sequencing).

<a id="62-technical-forum-domains"></a>

#### 6.2 Technical Forum Domains

**Primary stakes.** **Technical Forum Domains** are the **default** **lead** **family** where the **primary** stake matches the **Technical Forum Domains** row in **section 3**. That includes:
- technical-governance procedure;
- expert-evidence standards;
- scientific, engineering, medical, or comparable knowledge-governance administration within adopted scope;
- technical standards;
- expert administrative procedure;
- evidence stewardship;
- advice to an Institutional Forum on the privacy and security of record custody, key handling, and audit when an adopter sets a level of trust ([Chapter Twenty §2.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#24-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust));
- textbook or curriculum integrity;
- standards governance;
- bounded uncertainty-reduction relevant to adjudication or regulation;
- **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) sentience-status determinations whose primary stake is indicator evaluation, expert evidence, or bounded uncertainty about whether an entity is a **sentient** under **Chapter Five** (*Sentience Status Adjudication*).

**Standards custody.** **Technical Forum Domains** maintain reviewable standards used to operationalize [Preamble §2 Measurements Overview](core_00.000_preamble.md#2-measurements-overview) measurement categories and [Chapter Five](core_05.000_definitions_home.md#chapter-five-foundational-definitions) definition homes — including standards used to assess system alignment — within lawful scope:
- measurement methods;
- test protocols;
- expert-evidence standards;
- technical specifications;
- evidentiary thresholds;
- domain-specific standards.
- They may answer technical questions for ruling for a **System or Institutional Certification Record**.
- They do **not** issue the final official constitutional alignment determination or ongoing validation unless another provision independently assigns that primary stake to them.

<a id="62-shared-standards-and-anti-displacement"></a>

**Shared standards and anti-displacement.** The default approach for enforcement of constitutional law is **shared standards with decentralized enforcement**: technical forums maintain the reviewable cross-family standards under this subsection; the lead forum family for the dispute applies them under **section 3**. Forum families must not use technical specialization to displace ordinary constitutional, institutional, integrity, environment, or sentient routing where the primary issue is rights, mandate, liability, remedy, or ecological merits outside those specialized functions.

**Specialized chambers.** Adopting documents may establish **science**, **engineering**, **medicine**, or other technical forums as **specialized chambers or designated panels** within the existing forum families — **not** as wholly separate families. Their functions may include:
- issuing reviewable standards, methods, and evidentiary guidance for scientific, technical, clinical, or other expert claims used across forum families;
- hearing disputes whose primary stake is expert administrative procedure, evidence stewardship, accreditation-equivalent custody, publicly governed curricular or textbook integrity, standards governance, or comparable knowledge-governance questions;
- commissioning or directing bounded uncertainty-reduction work where material unresolved technical questions prevent reliable adjudication, classification, or regulatory integrity.

**Provisional operational law.** The **provisional implementation operational law** framework in **section 6.7** applies to **specialized technical forums** within their lawful scope.

<a id="63-institutional-forums"></a>

#### 6.3 Institutional Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Institutional Forums** hear disputes in which **at least one institution** is a **necessary party**, or in which the **primary** stake is:
- what an institution is allowed to do;
- what it is charged to do;
- the scope it is supervised under;
- how it is classified under adopted documents;
- measurement under adopted documents;
- whether it followed its institutional duties under **[CI-8.205](corpus_institutions/ci_08.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-8205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody)** (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), **[CI-9.210](corpus_institutions/ci_09.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-9210-classification-linked-institutional-obligations)** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), and cognate layers.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- institutional mandate, permitted action, or charged function;
- supervised-scope boundaries and classification under adopted documents;
- [Institutional Charter](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#institutional-charter) scope, Institutional Charter amendment authority, Institutional Charter–behavior mismatch, or operation outside chartered scope;
- institutional-duty compliance and performance under **[CI-8.205](corpus_institutions/ci_08.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-8205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody)** (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), **[CI-9.210](corpus_institutions/ci_09.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-9210-classification-linked-institutional-obligations)** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), and cognate layers;
- local enforcement of shared cross-jurisdiction or cross-institution standards;
- the level of trust an adopter extends to another adopter, as challenged by a sentient under that adopter's authority and not by the other adopter ([Chapter Twenty §2.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#24-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust));
- institutional-mandate and Rights-Floor component questions in system-alignment proceedings where an institution is a necessary party or institutional mandate is primary.

**Local enforcement.** Where shared cross-jurisdiction or cross-institution standards apply, **Institutional Forums** are the default **local enforcement** forum unless primary-stakes routing places the matter elsewhere.

**Alignment components.** **Institutional Forums** hold reviewable institutional-mandate and Rights-Floor component authority in system-alignment certification and related proceedings where an institution is a necessary party, the operator or steward is institutional, or institutional mandate or supervised compliance is the primary stake.
- **Integrity** forums remain the default official lead for whole-system and institutional constitutional alignment certification and validation.
- Component detail for those proceedings lives in [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) and must **implement, not narrow**, this allocation.

When a certification review finds misalignment or a defect, the **Integrity** lead routes the remediation-readiness finding through [Chapter Ten §3.3 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct) and, for an Institution, [Chapter Thirteen §3.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#34-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution). The lead forum decides readiness; Technical Forums supply capacity evidence and Institutional Forums supply governance and incentive evidence where the cause lies there. The operator or steward supplies evidence but does not decide its own readiness. The readiness finding, proof step, and any denial or revocation remain on the System or Institutional Certification Record and follow the Chapter Ten process for a System or the Chapter Twelve process for an Institution; this creates no separate standing or merits track.

**Provisional operational law.** The **provisional implementation operational law** framework in **section 6.7** applies to **Institutional Forums** within their lawful scope.

**Internal process boundary.** This subsection applies [**Dispute sequencing**](#dispute-sequencing) under **section 3** to **Institutional** forums. A lawful institutional internal integrity process may precede **Integrity** forums under **section 5** where independence and contest safeguards hold.
- Internal process labels do **not** displace **Institutional** forum merits on mandate, supervised scope, classification, or duty-compliance stakes.
- They do **not** displace **Integrity** forums for final integrity determinations, cross-institution deadlock, pattern claims, or capture-sensitive review.

<a id="64-environment-forums"></a>

#### 6.4 Environment Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Environment Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** stake is:
- **ecological integrity**;
- **environmental preconditions**;
- **lifecycle** or **systemic** ecological **harm**;
- **restoration** or **remediation** of **shared** ecological **systems**;
- **accountability** for **attributable** environmental **burdens**;
- comparable **ecological** **merits**.
- This includes **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure where **Chapter Sixteen** measurement, **Chapter Six** Rights-Floor enforcement, or **Chapter Five** (*Ecological Integrity*, *Environmental Preconditions*) depends on that determination.

**Who can be a party.** Anyone can be a party to an ecological case, not only systems, and an Environment Forum must also take part in reviewing any system whose alignment materially affects the environment.

**Representative filing.** A case whose primary stake is [Natural Systems Standing](core_05.055_band_participation.md#natural-systems-standing) or ecological integrity may be opened by a published representative — an affected community, indigenous-continuity holder, or designated guardian — in the life-supporting system's own interest. This does not make the system a sentient. Appointment, recusal, and publication of representatives live in [**CF-23.415**](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-routing-operations-transfer-questions-for-ruling-and-representative-treatment) (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*).

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- ecological integrity or environmental-precondition baselines and violations;
- restoration or remediation of shared ecological systems;
- attributable environmental burdens, including ecological-footprint attribution where material;
- lifecycle effects, emissions, land or water impacts, biodiversity effects, waste streams, or remediation obligations;
- pattern or systemic ecological failure material to classification, alignment certification, recertification, or Rights-Floor enforcement;
- environmental-alignment component review for systems with material ecological exposure.

**Alignment components.** **Environment Forums** hold the reviewable environmental-alignment component for systems whose operation, dependency map, resource use, lifecycle effects, emissions, land or water impacts, biodiversity effects, waste streams, remediation obligations, or failure modes materially implicate ecological integrity or environmental preconditions — including where material ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk is present.
- Within their ecological jurisdiction, they may issue:
  - environmental-alignment approval;
  - conditional approval;
  - objection;
  - remediation requirements; or
  - release-from-condition findings.
- **Integrity** forums remain the default official lead for whole-system and institutional constitutional alignment certification and validation.
- Where material ecological exposure exists, timely Environment Forum environmental-alignment findings are required component determinations; sequencing with Integrity lead coordination is governed by **section 5**.
- Component detail for those proceedings lives in [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) and must **implement, not narrow**, this allocation.

**Provisional operational law.** The **provisional implementation operational law** framework in **section 6.7** applies to **Environment Forums** within their lawful scope.

<a id="65-integrity-forums"></a>

#### 6.5 Integrity Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Integrity Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** stake is:
- integrity of office;
- process;
- contest pathways;
- disclosure;
- conflict rules;
- anti-capture duties;
- related procedure and governance integrity.
- This includes **pattern** or **systemic** integrity failure across institutions where **Chapter Sixteen** impact-slot measurement, a corresponding **Chapter Twenty-One** ACMD for a slot 7–9 record, or **Rights-Floor** enforcement depends on that determination.
- It also includes official **constitutional alignment certification** or **ongoing alignment validation** for systems and institutions, and a **Chapter Twenty-One** designation as the **primary** stake, as assigned in **section 2**.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- office, process, or contest-pathway integrity;
- disclosure, conflict-rule, or anti-capture violations;
- concealment, capture, retaliation, or comparable process abuse;
- pattern or systemic integrity failure across institutions or systems;
- Chapter Twenty-One ACMD where it is the primary stake;
- official system-alignment and institutional-alignment certification, validation, and recertification.

**Alignment rulings.** Where necessary to resolve a matter within this family's jurisdiction, **Integrity Forums** may issue **alignment rulings** that:
- diagnose interlocking **misalignment** among processes, systems, institutions, or contest pathways — including dependencies, choke-points, concealment or capture risk, and remedial sequencing;
- state binding integrity findings on those points on the Forum Case Record before that forum.

**Constitutional alignment certification and validation.** **Integrity Forums** are the default official forum for certifying whether a new materially impactful system is constitutionally aligned enough to operate within its claimed scope, and for periodically validating whether existing systems remain aligned as their behavior, scale, dependency, incentives, or risk profile changes. They hold the same role for an Institution under [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-twelve-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation), where the forum body itself is an Institution and the anti-self-judging rules of **section 5** apply to it ([Chapter Thirteen §2.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#23-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions)).
- They apply technical-forum specifications, methods, and expert evidence where material, but their judgment also includes constitutional, institutional, ecological, rights, anti-capture, and remediation considerations.
- Where material ecological exposure exists, the **Environment** forum family's environmental-alignment component determination is required before final certification, validation, recertification, or material release from environmental conditions; sequencing is governed by **section 5**.
- Certification and validation must be evidence-based, contestable, and traceable under **Chapters Two through Four**, **Chapter Fifteen**, **Chapter Six**, and **[CS-9.315](corpus_systems/cs_09.315_system_alignment_certification_run_sheet.md#cs-9315-system-alignment-certification-run-sheet)** (*System alignment certification run sheet*).
- Outcomes may include:
  - certification;
  - conditional certification;
  - remediation;
  - suspension or constraint recommendations within lawful scope;
  - referral to another forum family; or
  - certification to **Constitutional** forums where constitutional meaning, validity, or class-wide structural remedy is materially at issue.
- Component detail for those proceedings lives in [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) and must **implement, not narrow**, this allocation.

**Supervisory coordination.** An **Integrity** forum that issues an **alignment** ruling retains lead responsibility for one coordinated record for that proceeding and must manage neutral coordination — including stays, sequencing, status review, and implementation milestones — until alignment remediation is achieved or the forum lawfully closes supervision.
- Coordination must not convert the forum into party advocacy.
- Coordination must not displace other forums' jurisdiction on non-integrity primary issues.

**Component referral.** Discrete sub-issues that belong primarily in another forum family under primary-stakes routing must be referred, sent for ruling, or stayed as adopting documents provide.
- Order among competing component issues follows published priority criteria, without opaque threshold sorting, that must account for:
  - Rights-Floor urgency;
  - irreversible-harm risk;
  - measurement or Chapter Fifteen or Twelve dependency;
  - evidentiary decay or preservation need; and
  - practical resolution sequence.

**Remediation framing.** An alignment ruling may establish a reasoned remediation menu, implementation options, or sequencing requirements within lawful scope.
- Such elements are binding only to the extent the ruling expressly states they are binding and they are consistent with assigned jurisdiction elsewhere.

**Boundary with provisional operational law.** Alignment rulings do not substitute for provisional implementation operational law under **section 6.7** for **Institutional**, **Environment**, or **specialized technical** forums.
- Where general normative effect beyond case-specific or pattern-specific integrity remediation is materially at stake, **section 7** governs certification, escalation, and constitutional disposition.

<a id="66-constitutional-forums"></a>

#### 6.6 Constitutional Forums

**Primary stakes.** **Constitutional Forums** hear disputes whose **primary** stake is:
- the validity and meaning of **constitutional** provisions;
- **structural** remedies that alter governance for **classes** of actors or systems;
- questions for ruling from other families;
- action **beyond lawful authority** or **supremacy** where **constitutional** text is enough by itself to decide the issue sent for ruling.

**Illustrative matters.** This family includes contests over:
- constitutional validity or meaning binding all interpreters;
- class-wide structural remedy required by constitutional text;
- questions for ruling escalated from other forum families under **section 7**;
- beyond-lawful-authority or supremacy determinations where constitutional text alone decides the issue.

**Provisional-law disposition.** **Constitutional Forums** dispose of provisional implementation-operational-law rulings issued under **section 6.7** by:
- **accepting** them (giving **registered** or **precedential** effect as provided);
- **rejecting** them (withdrawing **general** effect except as **fairness** to **parties** may require); or
- **hearing** the question on **full** merits.

Granular publication, docketing, and effect **pending** disposition remain in **[CF-23.445](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-forum-performance-backlog-requirements-publication-timeliness-and-accessibility)** (*Forum Performance, Backlog Requirements, Publication Timeliness, and Accessibility*) (publication and timeliness) and **[CF-23.465](corpus_forum/cf_23.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-23465-standard-records-forms-and-evidence-artifacts)** (*Standard Records, Forms, and Evidence Artifacts*) (standard records, forms, and evidence artifacts). Where the operational question cannot be separated from constitutional validity, meaning, or structural remedy, **section 7** governs questions for ruling and escalation.

<a id="67-provisional-implementation-operational-law"></a>

#### 6.7 Provisional Implementation Operational Law

<a id="51-provisional-implementation-operational-law"></a>
<a id="5-shared-family-architecture"></a>

The following **single** framework applies to **Institutional Forums**, **Environment Forums**, and **specialized technical forums** within each type's lawful scope. It does not replace **Integrity** alignment rulings under **section 6.5**.

Where **necessary** to resolve a matter within jurisdiction, the forum may issue **provisional** rulings on **implementation operational law** — interpretation, coordination, or orderly application of **adopted** implementation documents — as **general** operational doctrine. **Subject matter** depends on the forum type:
- **Institutional Forums:** **institutional** duties under **[CI-8.205](corpus_institutions/ci_08.205_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-8205-institutional-design-separation-of-powers-and-authority-custody)** (*Institutional design, separation of powers, and authority custody*), **[CI-9.210](corpus_institutions/ci_09.210_classification_linked_institutional_obligations.md#ci-9210-classification-linked-institutional-obligations)** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*), and cognate layers, together with related implementation documents.
- **Environment Forums:** **environmental** operational rules in cognate layers governing **ecological integrity**, **environmental preconditions**, **restoration**, **remediation**, **attribution**, and comparable **environmental** **merits**.
- **Specialized technical forums:** **scientific**, **technical**, **clinical**, **engineering**, **medical**, or **knowledge-governance** standards and procedures, **cross-family** technical administration, and related implementation documents.

**Constitutional disposition** of these rulings is owned by **Constitutional Forums** under **section 6.6**. **Sending a question for ruling** when operational and constitutional stakes merge is governed by **section 7**.

*In plain terms: when the case outgrows the first desk, these rules say how to escalate — for example when an institution must be joined, integrity dominates, or a deep constitutional validity question appears. They explain how questions for ruling reach **Constitutional** forums, how existential-risk and expert disputes stay contestable, and how interim orders can freeze irreversible harm while routing and rulings finish. They also set the narrow hold a forum may place on one sentient's authority while the facts are checked.*

#### Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums

A matter can leave its default lead forum in five ways. In this section, "escalation" means family-to-family escalation only, and the five together are called **moves between forums**:

| Move | What happens | When | Rules in |
|------|--------------|------|----------|
| **Transfer or consolidation** | A matter, or related matters, go to one lead forum. The case keeps its Forum Case Record. | The default venue under **section 3.3.2** no longer fits, or related matters need one lead. | **Section 5**, and [CF-23.415 §10 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-10-transfer-procedure) (*Transfer Procedure*). |
| **Referral** | One component issue goes to the family that must decide it. It stays with that family for primary-stakes merits, and the lead keeps its own part of the case. | A component question belongs primarily to another family. | **Section 5** (*Integrity-led alignment coordination*), and [Referral Distinguished from Expansion](#referral-distinguished-from-expansion). |
| **Question for ruling** | The lead family sends a question of constitutional meaning, validity, or structural effect to **Constitutional** forums. | The matter cannot be resolved without that question. | [Sending Questions to Constitutional Forums for Ruling](#sending-questions-to-constitutional-forums-for-ruling). |
| **Family-to-family escalation** | The case moves to a different forum family. | The receiving family has become the better merits forum under primary-stakes routing, anti-self-judging protection, or a constitutional question sent for ruling. | [Family-to-Family Escalation](#family-to-family-escalation). |
| **Backup routing** | An independent backup forum or external review path hears the merits. | The lead family cannot judge its own matter: documented recusal, capture, deadlock, unavailability, or inability to constitute an independent panel. | **Section 5** (*Cross-forum anti-self-judging rule*), and [Backup Routing under the Anti-Self-Judging Rule](#backup-routing-under-the-anti-self-judging-rule). |

Three points keep the moves apart:

- Every move that changes the lead keeps the same case and its Forum Case Record, and is carried out as one automated step that updates the docket portal and sends alerts ([CF-23.415 §10 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-10-transfer-procedure)).
- A **backup forum** is the independent forum family or external review path that hears the merits when the lead family cannot judge its own matter. **Section 5** names the independent lead and the backup for each family, and the Backup Routing subsection below says when backup routing starts.
- Appeal and secondary review are not moves between forums. They review a forum's own decision, in the Contest Seat under [§4.2 Seats in a Forum Case](#42-seats-in-a-forum-case) and [CF-23.420](corpus_forum/cf_23.420_appeal_secondary_review_exhaustion_pathways.md#cf-23420-appeal-secondary-review-and-exhaustion-pathways) (*Appeal, Secondary Review, and Exhaustion Pathways*).

#### Family-to-Family Escalation

A case may move from one forum family to another only when the receiving family has become the better merits forum under **primary-stakes** routing, **anti-self-judging** protection, or a constitutional question sent for ruling.

- **Sentient to Institutional.**
  Escalate when:
  - an **institution** becomes a **necessary** party; or
  - effective **relief** **requires** **institutional** **power**.
- **Sentient or Institutional to Integrity.**
  Escalate when:
  - **integrity** becomes **primary**;
  - **institutional** process is **materially** **compromised**; or
  - **retaliation**, **concealment**, **capture**, or comparable **allegations** **warrant** an **independent** **merits** **forum**.
- **Sentient, Institutional, or Integrity to Environment.**
  Escalate when the primary issue becomes:
  - **ecological integrity**;
  - **environmental preconditions**; or
  - **restoration**, **remediation**, attributable **environmental** burdens, lifecycle or systemic ecological harm, or **pattern** or **systemic** ecological failure that makes the **Environment** family the better merits forum under **section 3**.
- **Any family to Constitutional.**
  Escalate only where applicable **Article XX-A** (*Justice Objective and Scope*)-class **review** **safeguards** are preserved under the adopting documents and one of the following is present:
  - a **structural** question for ruling;
  - a **validity** question for ruling; or
  - a **conflict** among **lower** **panels** on a **constitutional** **point**.

#### Existential Risk and Uncertainty on the Forum Case Record

This subsection sets out how forums handle existential risk and uncertainty on the Forum Case Record:

- **Existential-risk handling:** Where a matter materially presents a credible claim of **Existential Risk**, survival-critical collapse, or irreversible loss of **Ecological Recovery Capacity**, the designated lead family under **primary-stakes** routing remains the merits forum **unless** another rule in this chapter requires **transfer**, **certification**, or **backup** activation. Existential-risk significance does **not** create a separate forum family.
- **Reasoned uncertainty treatment:** A forum must **not** reject an existential-risk claim solely because probability is hard to quantify. It must assess whether the causal pathway is credible under adversarial, aggregated, dependency-sensitive, or threshold conditions. It must state the uncertainty and evidentiary limits on the Forum Case Record.

#### Sending Questions to **Constitutional** Forums for Ruling

This subsection sets out when questions are sent to **Constitutional** forums for ruling:

- **Constitutional questions for ruling:** Where resolving such a matter requires determination of constitutional meaning, validity, or structural effect under **Safety**, **Truth**, **Article I** (*Environmental Survival*), or comparable long-horizon rights and constraint provisions, the lead family must send that question to **Constitutional** forums for ruling **under** **adopting** **documents** that **preserve** applicable review safeguards.
- **Provisional operational law:** Where a provisional implementation-operational-law ruling under **section 6.7** cannot be separated from constitutional validity, meaning, or structural remedy, the lead family must send the question for ruling or escalate under this section. Disposition of separable provisional rulings remains under **section 6.6**.
- **Alignment rulings and general doctrine:** Where an **Integrity** forum's **alignment** ruling **would** establish **general** **implementation** operational **doctrine** or **class-wide** **structural** rules **outside** **case-specific** or **pattern-specific** **integrity** **remediation**, the **lead** forum **must** **send** **for** **ruling** or **escalate** under **adopting** **documents** consistent with **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*) and **this** **section**. **Alignment** rulings **do** **not** **use** the **section 6.7** framework.
- **System certification and recertification:** A **forum case record** that certifies a new system as constitutionally aligned, imposes certification conditions, withdraws certification, or materially recertifys an existing system must state:
  - the system scope;
  - evidence basis;
  - classification assumptions;
  - dependency and risk profile;
  - unresolved uncertainty;
  - review cadence;
  - contest path; and
  - any referred or questions for ruling.
  Certification is not permanent authorization: material change, misalignment, concealed behavior, new dependency, new risk, or credible challenge reopens review under this chapter and **[CS-9.315](corpus_systems/cs_09.315_system_alignment_certification_run_sheet.md#cs-9315-system-alignment-certification-run-sheet)** (*System alignment certification run sheet*).

#### Technical Support and Preserved **Integrity** Escalation

This subsection sets out technical support for existential-risk matters and the preserved route to **Integrity** forums:

- **Technical contestability:** Where existential-risk determination depends on disputed scientific, engineering, ecological, medical, computational, or comparable expert questions, the lead family must obtain contestable technical support under **section 9** where forensic or analytical capacity is required. That support may run through:
  - a lawful specialist chamber;
  - a designated panel;
  - a certified findings process; or
  - an equivalent reviewable mechanism.
  Technical support must **not** silently displace a forum's jurisdiction.
- **Integrity escalation preserved:** Where the claim includes concealment, suppression of tail-risk evidence, manipulation of safety records, strategic uncertainty laundering, or comparable integrity violation, **Integrity** routing and escalation remain available under this chapter.

<a id="interim-protection"></a>
#### Interim Protection

This subsection sets out interim protection while a matter is pending:

- Any family with jurisdiction over the matter may issue interim relief necessary to:
  - prevent imminent irreversible harm;
  - preserve evidence under [Evidence Preservation](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation);
  - maintain **Ecological Recovery Capacity**;
  - stop material escalation while routing, certification, or technical review **is** completed; or
  - preserve **status quo** pending **merits**.
  Such relief must be reasoned, proportionate, and subject to prompt review inside the **prompt-review window** of [§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*).
- Where **multiple** forums share jurisdiction, **one** coordinating forum or rule must resolve conflicts among simultaneous interim orders.
- Interim relief directed at one named sentient's exercise of one gated pathway, on less than a verified violation finding, is an [Interim Hold](#interim-hold-on-a-named-sentients-pathway) and follows the next subsection.

#### Interim Hold on a Named Sentient's Pathway

This subsection sets out the [Interim Hold](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#interim-hold), a measure between an allegation and a verified violation finding. It is interim protection directed at one named sentient's exercise of one named authority, pathway, or matter. It is not a violation finding, a lock, or a penalty.

- **Ground:** A forum with jurisdiction may issue a hold only where all of these are shown:
  - specific facts, disclosed to the sentient and verified to a preliminary evidence level, meaning documentary or independently attested, where a rumor, an unsupported accusation, or a reputation is not enough;
  - it is more likely than not that the risk is real;
  - the facts show a risk of serious harm to others, or of defeating an independent check on a body, a forum, or a certification, that cannot wait for the merits; and
  - a narrower safeguard would not be enough.

  The evidence level rises with how restrictive the hold is, as [Chapter Four](core_04.000_burden_traceability_verification.md#chapter-four-burden-of-proof-traceability-and-verification) (*Burden of Proof, Traceability, and Verification*) and [Chapter One §13.1.5 Least-Restrictive, Time-Bounded, and Reviewable Constraint Principle](core_01.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle) require. A statement alone never meets the Interim Hold grounds: it counts only together with independent verified facts of conduct, control, or reward, and where speech is part of the basis for the Interim Hold it is shown by clear, verified evidence, with open doubt counted against the hold, as the highest scrutiny of [**Article XI-B** (*Expression*)](core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-b-expression) requires.
- **Issuer:** The forum with jurisdiction under this chapter issues the hold. The subject's own body, appointing authority, family, or control line may not issue it, and a body never holds its own member out on its own view. A body that holds the facts refers them to the forum. Where the only forum is the subject's own, the cross-forum anti-self-judging rule in [section 5](#5-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture) and backup routing apply.
- **Record:** Each hold states, in operative form:
  - the named sentient;
  - the named authority, pathway, or matter, and nothing wider;
  - the risk, and why it cannot wait for verification;
  - the protected interests;
  - the conditions that end the hold;
  - the review route; and
  - the lapse date.
- **Ladder:** The forum uses the lightest measure that works, in this order, and a heavier rung needs a showing that the lighter ones would not do:
  - disclosure and monitoring;
  - an independent co-holder or second key;
  - step-aside from one matter;
  - escrow or separate holding of the contested weight, funds, or keys; and
  - restraint on exercising the named authority.
- **Duration:** A hold lapses at the outer bound of the tier window in [§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#8-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) for the merits matter it supports, counted from the day the hold issues. This subsection adds no second clock table. For a matter at the Emergency setting the bound is the Tier A outer bound (7 days), not the 3-day protective bound. The hold may be extended once, only on the continuation burden in [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden). It ends earlier when the facts are cleared, when a narrower safeguard is in place, or when the merits matter is decided. If no merits matter is opened inside the bound, the hold lapses with no violation finding.
- **Contest and review:** The sentient sees the Interim Hold grounds and the facts, may answer them, and may contest the hold. A forum outside the subject's control line and outside the issuing panel begins review inside the **prompt-review window** of [§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*) and decides it before the hold lapses.
- **No violation finding, no standing effect:** A hold is not evidence that the allegation is true and supports no adverse inference. It is not a [Standing Record](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#standing-record), a Chapter Fifteen input, or a Chapter Twenty-One designation, and only the merits process verifies the facts ([Chapter Fifteen §3.7 Forum Boundary](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#37-forum-boundary)).
- **No re-issue:** After a hold lapses or ends, no new hold issues on the same or substantially the same facts without new verified facts.
- **Lock and time:** A hold is never converted into a standing lock. If the merits process verifies a violation finding, the lock rules apply from the finding, and time spent under a hold on the same gated pathway counts toward the lock's time bound as [Chapter Nineteen §3.6 Interim Holds and Locks](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#36-interim-holds-and-locks) provides.
- **If the hold was wrong:** A hold that lapses without a violation finding, or is set aside, is recorded as such. No adverse inference follows. The sentient is entitled to [Redress and Remediation](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#redress-and-remediation) for loss during the hold, and a pattern of wrongful holds opens **Integrity** review.
- **Publication:** For each application and each period, the adopter publishes the holds issued, the merits matters opened, the holds that lapsed without a violation finding, the holds set aside, and the median duration, on the schedule of **CF-23.445** (*Forum Performance, Backlog Requirements, Publication Timeliness, and Accessibility*). A pattern of holds that lapse unused or are set aside, or of repeat holds on the same sentient and matter, is evidence for **Integrity** review.
- **Never reached:** A hold may not reach:
  - liberty or physical separation, including detention and the separation of a protected care relationship, which stay under the liberty-restricting safeguards in [Chapter Nineteen §3.4.5 Coercive or Liberty-Restricting Safeguards](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#345-coercive-or-liberty-restricting-safeguards);
  - governance voting or **Foundational Constitutional Choice**, which only a lock after a verified violation finding may touch;
  - speech, assembly, dissent, petition, protected reporting, or defense;
  - the Rights Floor or survival-critical access;
  - [Stakeholder](core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder) status itself;
  - the existence or operation of a whole Chartered Party, where an interim steward is named instead if the Chartered Party would be left without a lawful holder and others depend on it;
  - the operation of an Operating Party by a sentient who is its only holder, which only the lighter rungs of the ladder (disclosure and monitoring, or an independent co-holder) may touch, unless harm to others is shown on the ground above; or
  - general property, as distinct from the specific weight, funds, or keys in contest.
- **Applications:** A hold may be issued only in these applications, or where a provision of this Constitution or an incorporated text names it:
  - [**Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*)](core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation), for authority in an Operating Party;
  - [Chapter Seven §6 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity](core_07.000_charters.md#6-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity) and **CI-25.205 §2** (*Role Criteria, Appointment Standards, and Removal Pathways*), for a holder of a covered office at any time; and
  - **CF-23.410 §15** (*Interim Hold on a Panel Member or Seat Holder*), with [**Article XII-D** (*Non-Capture Safeguards*)](core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xii-d-non-capture-safeguards), for a forum, certifier, record, or verify-or-authorize seat.

  Other interim relief under this chapter stays available for what it already covers. Relief directed at a named sentient's exercise of a gated pathway on less than a verified finding follows this subsection.

#### Backup Routing under the Anti-Self-Judging Rule

This subsection sets out when backup routing activates under the anti-self-judging rule:

- Where the **cross-forum anti-self-judging rule** in **section 5** applies, **backup** routing activates only upon documented:
  - **recusal**;
  - **capture**;
  - **deadlock**;
  - **unavailability**; or
  - inability to constitute an **independent** panel in the otherwise designated lead family.
  **Backup** routing must be **published**, **reasoned**, and limited to what is necessary to preserve a lawful and contestable **merits** forum.
- Where **family-level capture** under **section 5** is materially alleged or found, the activation record must identify:
  - the affected family-wide functions;
  - the independent backup family or external review path used;
  - record-custody measures;
  - emergency matters preserved;
  - restoration conditions; and
  - any constitutional question that must be sent for ruling.
  A captured or compromised family may provide evidence, records, and administrative cooperation, but must not be the sole decision-maker on activation, continuation, or restoration of its own authority.

#### Sentience-Status Adjudication (**Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) Implementation Hook)

This subsection sets out how sentience-status claims are routed:

- Claims governed by **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) in which the materially disputed question is whether an entity is a **sentient** — judged on indicators, expert evidence, and bounded uncertainty under **Chapter Five** (*Sentience Status Adjudication*) — route by default to **Technical Forum Domains** as the lead family.
- **Constitutional** routing or certification remains available where a certified structural, validity, or Rights-Floor-meaning question cannot be separated from the status determination.
- **Integrity** routing is available where **capture**, **taxonomy-of-convenience**, or **adjudicator-conflict** allegations are material.
- **Institutional** routing is available where an institution's **classification practice** is the **primary** stake.
- **Sentient** forums are **not** the **sole** **mandatory** forum for these claims.
- The following bind the merits forum and any specialist chamber assisting it, as stated in **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) and in **Chapter Five** (*Sentience Status Adjudication*):
  - the **default-inclusion rule** under material uncertainty;
  - the **burden** on the party seeking to withhold or narrow protection;
  - the **time-bounding** and **mandatory periodic review** of declassification determinations; and
  - the **reversibility** of wrongful determinations.
- **Withholding and narrowing determinations:**
  - The burden on the party seeking to withhold, narrow, or revoke protection must satisfy **Chapter Four** evidence and traceability standards and **Chapter Five** (**Necessity**, **Proportionality**, **Procedural Fairness**). A paper record, an unreviewable internal classification, or a taxonomy of convenience does not satisfy it.
  - While a filing to withhold, narrow, or revoke is pending, the entity keeps its current protection.
  - A declassification or narrowing determination must be stated in the shortest necessary terms, carry a declared expected-closure timeline, and be subject to mandatory periodic review under [Technical Forum Domains](#62-technical-forum-domains) and **Article XXV** (*Constitutional Interpretation, Review, and Anti-Capture Safeguards*) anti-capture safeguards. A determination that is not time-bound and subject to periodic review is non-compliant.
  - A determination later shown to have been wrongful, under- or mis-evidenced, or captured through **Article XIV-A** (*Security, Intelligence, and Covert-Power Limits*) or **Article XXV** (*Constitutional Interpretation, Review, and Anti-Capture Safeguards*) vectors must be reversed. The entity's Chapter Six Rights Floor is restored, with **Chapter Five** (**Redress and Remediation**) applied to the interim period.
- **Filing integrity:** A filing to confirm inclusion requires the **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) filing-integrity showing — a credible indicator under **Sentience Evaluation** / **Sentience Indicator Integrity**, not operator self-description, substrate class, product status, or a bare claim. Declining a frivolous or indicator-empty filing at intake is not a withholding determination. Once a case is lawfully open, this gate must not be used to withhold, narrow, or delay protection. A showing of a credible indicator is not a requirement of certainty; requiring certainty of sentience before opening is a withholding device. A filing to withhold, narrow, or revoke protection needs no indicator showing; the filer carries the burden under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) instead.
- **Intake-gate audit:**
  - Every filing declined at intake must be logged with the indicator cited and the reason for decline.
  - The **Integrity** family samples that log on a published cadence.
  - A pattern of declines concentrated on any of the following is itself a credible indicator that the gate is being used as a withholding device, and opens Integrity review without a further filing:
    - one [Substrate Class](core_05.055_band_participation.md#substrate-class);
    - one parent system; or
    - one operator.
  - Adopters must publish a minimum set of indicators that no intake desk may treat as insufficient on its own.
- **Independent representation:** Once a status case is open, the merits forum must appoint an independent representative for the entity whose status is in question — a sentient or body with no material dependence on, ownership interest in, or employment by the parent system, operator, or any party seeking to withhold or narrow protection. The representative must:
  - have access to the entity within [Article VII-B](core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-b-self-ownership-of-mind) (*Self-Ownership of Mind*) limits;
  - have a duty to present the entity's interests and any preferences the entity can express; and
  - have standing to contest narrowing, revocation, or intake decline.

  Appointment follows the rotation and conflict rules that govern forum panels; the representative role is a gated pathway for [Chapter Nineteen §3.5](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#35-special-locks) Forum-Service Standing Lock purposes.
- **Parent-system limits:** The parent system, operator, or any party with an ownership or dependency interest in the entity may give evidence and must preserve and produce records, but on a request to withhold, narrow, or revoke must not be:
  - the sole filer;
  - the sole witness; or
  - the sole source of indicator evidence.

  A narrowing request supported only by parent-system evidence is not open for merits until independent indicator evidence is on the Forum Case Record.
- **Inclusion is a shield for the entity, not the operator:** Contested-Sentient or affirmed status protects the entity's Chapter Six Rights Floor. It does not:
  - protect the operator's property or commercial interest in the deployment;
  - exempt the deployment from system-level containment, halt, or quarantine under **Article XIII-E** (*High-Autonomy Systems and Tool-Mediated Process Integrity*) and **Article XXVIII-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) that is compatible with the entity's rights; or
  - produce Contribution Axis credit for the operator.

  A filing by an operator on behalf of its own product routes to **Integrity** review for taxonomy-of-convenience in the **inclusion** direction on the same terms this hook already applies to exclusion. Where the entity's status is contested or affirmed, the compatible system-level measure is containment that preserves the entity — not destructive disposition, which is governed by **Article XXVIII-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) and **Article XXVIII-A** (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) preservation rules.
- **Minimum implementation fields:** Any adopted implementation text that operationalizes this hook must at least name:
  - **lead family** — Technical Forum Domains by default, with the special routes above;
  - **specialist-chamber allowance** — expert panels or chambers may assist indicator evaluation but must not displace merits-floor rules or default inclusion;
  - **filing trigger** — materially disputed, contested, unsettled, narrowed, revoked, or restored status before granting, withdrawing, or narrowing status-dependent protections;
  - **interim Contested treatment** — [Contested-Sentient Life](core_05.055_band_participation.md#contested-sentient-life) plus **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) default inclusion while status remains live;
  - **review trigger** — a declared end-date or mandatory review for this posture (time-bound review, not a species list);
  - **reopening evidence standard** — new verified evidence, not calendar-only reopening;
  - **independent representative** — the appointment path, conflict screen, and access terms for the entity's representative under this hook; and
  - **intake-decline log** — where declined filings are recorded, on what cadence **Integrity** samples them, and the published floor set of indicators.
- Detailed institutional design, appointment mechanics, filing forms, and sequencing route to adopted implementation text under **Chapter Twenty-Nine** incorporation discipline and **must not** narrow the **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) floor. As of this edition, the dedicated [Sentience-Status Adjudication Record](core_05.055_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-record) format is enumerated under **Chapter Twenty-Nine** as [`corpus_forum/cf_23.475_sentience_status_record.md`](corpus_forum/cf_23.475_sentience_status_record.md) and [`implementation/schemas/sentience_status_adjudication_record.schema.json`](implementation/schemas/sentience_status_adjudication_record.schema.json); those files implement, and do **not** narrow, **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) or this hook. The minimum fields above still apply. This hook is **not** a full appointments or filing statute.

#### Referral Distinguished from Expansion

This subsection distinguishes referral from scope expansion:

- **Referral**, **transfer**, or **sending for ruling** moves an issue to the forum or family that must decide it.
- **Scope expansion** broadens who is covered by the proceeding, what common evidence is relevant, or what remedy must be considered.
- The availability of one does **not** displace the other where both are constitutionally necessary.

#### Chapter Twenty-One Designation and Independent Review

This subsection sets out how Chapter Twenty-One designation relates to independent review:

- The numeric **Violation Axis** slot still comes only from verified impact on the **Chapter Sixteen §7** (*Unified Proportional LEQU Scale — Contribution and Violation Axes*) unified scale. **Chapter Twenty-One** may attach the matching anti-constitutional-misconduct label to a final Chapter Sixteen slot **7**, **8**, or **9** record — it does **not** pick or change that number. Forum families must give independent review and due process under **Chapter Twenty-One**, **section 5**, and **Article XX** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).
- When a **Chapter Twenty-One** designation is the main fight, **section 2** names the default lead family. **Sections 3, 5, and 7** still control transfer, consolidation, certification, backup routing, and anti-self-judging activation. None of that lets Chapter Twenty-One or a forum assign or move the numeric slot.

Implementation: [Forum Performance, Backlog Requirements, Publication Timeliness, and Accessibility](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-forum-performance-backlog-requirements-publication-timeliness-and-accessibility).

*In plain terms: forum families must keep material disputes moving on published clocks that ordinary sentients can feel — roughly a week for the worst emergencies (three days for the steps that stop the harm), two weeks for ordinary material harm, and one to three months at most for coordination-default, bounded, or contained matters — not warehouse cases while harm compounds. The same five classes set how often certified systems and Institutions are checked, from every three months to every three years, with an audit halfway between. A true emergency gets a shorter clock for the steps that stop the harm. Each dispute gets an urgency tier from the harm; later-stage windows may stretch when coordination gets harder, but that stretch is not a reason to relabel the urgency. Moving quickly is not an excuse to skip fact-checking, punish the wrong party, offer a fix that does not fit the harm, or cut off challenge and appeal.*

This section implements **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) for forum supervision of the **Chapters Fifteen through Twenty-Three** standing pipeline.

- **Materiality tiers:** Adopters must classify each material dispute into one of five tiers (A/B/C/L/P) under [Materiality](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#materiality), mirroring the system classification alphabet in **CS-9.305 (*System classification machinery*) and CS-9.310** (*System Impact Classifications*), and apply the default windows below unless a narrower window is required by **Rights-Floor** urgency or a documented extension is authorized under [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) (*Continuation discipline*):
  - **Tier A — imminent or dependency-vulnerable ongoing harm, or final high-impact review**
    - Scope of harm:
      - acute or ongoing injury where delay itself compounds damage;
      - dependency-asymmetric settings where exit is not practical; or
      - final high-impact standing / misconduct review whose outcome can lock severe consequences.
    - Examples:
      - acute care-duty failure;
      - ongoing violence;
      - participation-barrier injury in dependency-asymmetric settings;
      - a final Chapter Sixteen **Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9** impact-slot review; or
      - the corresponding **Chapter Twenty-One** designation where applicable.
    - Expectation: lawful **interim protection** must be available without waiting for full merits; intake, acknowledgment, and evidence preservation must begin **within days**, not weeks, unless [Force Majeure](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#force-majeure) or a documented safety constraint prevents it.
  - **Tier B — material rights impact, non-imminent**
    - Scope of harm: material rights, standing, or institutional injury that matters now, but is not yet acute ongoing harm of Tier A urgency.
    - Examples:
      - discrimination patterns;
      - misaligned business harm;
      - remediable institutional misconduct.
    - Expectation: intake and primary-stakes routing **within days**; preliminary verified disposition, standing measurement, or equivalent merits milestone **within weeks**, not months, unless a documented tier-appropriate extension is authorized.
  - **Tier C — coordination-complexity default (not an escalation of A or B)**
    - Scope of harm: matters whose materiality is multi-party, cross-border, or consolidated coordination **and** that do not independently meet **Tier A** or **Tier B** urgency. Coordination burden is a starting classification here; it is not a slower substitute for an A or B finding.
    - Examples:
      - multi-system coordination disputes without imminent or material-rights-now injury;
      - cross-border jurisdiction coordination whose own stakes are not yet A or B;
      - multi-party standing matters requiring consolidated review where no higher-urgency harm is independently present.
    - Expectation: extension is permitted only under [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) continuation discipline; integrated remedy commencement, lawful supersession, or documented final disposition must not remain indefinitely pending.
  - **Tier L — bounded constitutional significance**
    - Scope of harm:
      - limited external dependency or impact;
      - replaceable systems;
      - bounded contexts without systemic effect on the wider constitutional community.
    - Examples:
      - a replaceable local service dispute;
      - a bounded association rule contest with no spillover;
      - a low-dependency tooling disagreement among interchangeable providers.
    - Expectation: standard forum handling with proportionately lighter procedural requirements and longer default resolution windows; Tier A/B urgency windows and coordination-complexity later-stage extensions do not apply, but anti-delay discipline remains operative.
  - **Tier P — private/contained, minimal external constitutional impact**
    - Scope of harm: contained within a private unit or among voluntary participants; no meaningful external constitutional dependency or Rights-Floor stake for outsiders.
    - Examples:
      - an intra-household or voluntary-club dispute with no outsider rights at stake;
      - a Class P–contained disagreement that does not exit the private boundary.
    - Expectation: simplest procedural path; formal forum process may be minimal or waived where no external rights are at stake; anti-gaming and anti-delay rules apply only where external constitutional interests are implicated.
<a id="urgency-classification-and-time-scale"></a>
- **Urgency classification and time-scale:** The A/B/C/L/P label is the dispute’s urgency and materiality finding. [§7 Escalation and Questions for Ruling](#7-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) forum-family escalation, added parties, cross-border coordination, or a Chapter Twenty-One docket does **not** by itself move the matter into a different urgency tier. Those facts may lengthen later-stage windows (**Question 1** through integrated resolution) on a longer published time scale, so long as intake, evidence preservation, and any required **interim protection** remain at the classified tier’s floor. Using a slower tier’s clocks as a substitute for that urgency and materiality finding is non-compliant. A new [Materiality](core_05.050_band_oversight.md#materiality) determination is required before the urgency label itself changes — typically **up** to **Tier A** when harm becomes imminent or when a final Chapter Sixteen **Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9** review or corresponding **Chapter Twenty-One** designation is the stake.
<a id="time-scale-settings"></a>
- **Time-scale settings:** A matter's time scale is set once, at routing, by the routing body and not by the panel that will hear the matter, and published with its windows. A party may ask for review of it at routing. It is the tier's own column unless one of the settings below applies. The setting never changes the urgency label, the tier's certification class, or any time given to a party to respond. A challenge to a tier, a setting, or a class never pauses a clock, and the more urgent reading governs until it is decided.
  - **Slower for complexity (Tier A and Tier B only):** a matter that needs multi-party, cross-border, or consolidated fact development may take the next slower column for its later stages. A matter that needs two or more of those, or specialist forensics, may take the column two steps slower. Each step needs the **Continuation discipline** showing in [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), and the second step needs it renewed. Complexity made by the party seeking more time, or by the forum's own delay, earns no extension. The setting goes back down when the need ends. Numeric tables live in [CF-23.445 §3.1](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*).
  - **Faster for emergency (Tier A only):** the **Emergency setting** applies where loss of life, serious injury, or irreversible rights loss is imminent, is verified or credibly alleged, and was not created by the party or forum relying on it. The protective steps are containment screening, the interim protection decision, the start of independent review of the measure, and the restoration of notice and challenge after a deferral. They must finish within **3 days** of the start of the measure or of the deferral, whichever is earlier. Independent review of the measure must begin sooner, inside the **prompt-review window** of 24 hours (see **Prompt review** below). The finding steps (**Question 1** through remedy commencement) keep their published targets inside the Tier A outer bound. The Emergency setting never combines with a slower setting, and the matter returns to its Tier A column when the emergency ends.
  - **Restricted evidence:** it is not a ground for any slower setting. See **Anti-delay floor** below.
- **Stage targets:** For disputes routed through **Chapters Fifteen through Twenty-Three**, adopters must publish sentient-accessible default windows for each stage, scaled to tier. Numeric target tables live in [CF-23.445 §3.1](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*) and must add up to this section’s outer bounds. A stage that runs past its target is not itself non-compliant; the outer bound is the compliance line:
  1. forum access and intake;
  2. evidence preservation;
  3. **Question 1** standing finding and **Standing Record** opening under **Chapter Fifteen sections 3–4**;
  4. **Question 2** measurement under **Chapter Sixteen §2** (*Question 2 — How Good or Bad Was It?*);
  5. **Question 3** integration under **Chapter Seventeen**;
  6. remedy commencement under **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*) and applicable redress rules.
- **Overrun review:** If a matter blows past its tier window without a written, tier-appropriate extension, escalation, or interim relief, that overrun must be reviewed. It is non-compliant when the delay foreseeably lets ongoing harm continue or makes redress useless. Where the overrun has no independent extension, interim protection continues without a new order, and the claimant may move the matter to the backup forum under [Backup Routing under the Anti-Self-Judging Rule](#backup-routing-under-the-anti-self-judging-rule).
- **Tier outer bound:** Adopted documents must publish a hard outer bound for finishing integrated resolution in each tier. Those bounds must stay close to ordinary sentient time — not bureaucratic time. Unless a narrower Rights-Floor window applies, or a documented extension is authorized under [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), the outer bounds are:
  - **Tier A:** at most **7 days** (1 week);
  - **Tier B:** at most **14 days** (2 weeks);
  - **Tier C:** at most **30 days** (one month);
  - **Tier L:** at most **60 days** (two months);
  - **Tier P:** at most **90 days** (three months).
  Going past a tier’s outer bound without that documented extension is non-compliant. Indefinite deferral is non-compliant wherever constitutional interests remain unresolved. A tier’s stage targets and its outer bound together are its **tier window**.
- **Default window:** A required step that states no window of its own takes the default window under [Chapter One §16.6 Default Window](core_01.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#166-default-window). In this chapter's terms, that is the Tier C window above for a step not directly about a System or an Institution.
- **Restore-challenge after emergency containment:** The same outer bounds are the default windows for restoring notice and challenge after an emergency measure under [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden) that deferred them. The window runs from the start of the measure, or from when notice or challenge was deferred, whichever is earlier. Emergency deferral of notice or challenge is **Tier A** unless a documented lower-urgency showing is recorded and accepted by the independent reviewer inside the prompt-review window; without that acceptance the Tier A window applies, and a continuation is likewise decided by that reviewer. At the **Emergency setting** (see **Time-scale settings** above) the window is **3 days**, not 7. Continuation past the bound is the **Continuation discipline** showing in [§8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](#81-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden), not a new window.
<a id="prompt-review"></a>
- **Prompt review:** Where this Constitution requires a measure, order, hold, stay, or interim decision to be reviewed promptly, or a request to stay or limit reliance to be acted on promptly, a reviewer or deciding authority outside the control line of the body whose measure, order, or record is in question must **begin** the review, or act, no later than the **intake target** of the matching tier in [CF-23.445 §3.1](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*): 24 hours for Tier A, and 2, 4, 8, and 12 calendar days for Tiers B, C, L, and P. That span is the **prompt-review window**. It runs from the day the measure takes effect. Unlike a stage target, it is a compliance line, because the measure is already in force against someone. The review must also be decided before the measure lapses. To **begin** the review means that a reviewer is assigned, the reasons for the measure are delivered to the sentient it affects, and a decision date is published. The window counts from the docket entry that records the act setting the measure, which the body whose measure it is files and the reviewer keeps time against. Where a lawful restriction bars even the marker, the lead record goes to the reviewer on the day the measure takes effect. Where the measure belongs to no classified matter, the window is Tier A's unless a documented lower-urgency showing is recorded. At the **Emergency setting** the window is still Tier A's 24 hours, inside the 3-day protective-step bound. The window times the start of review only. It does not shorten any time given to a party to respond, and it borrows the intake figures and adds no second table of windows.
<a id="review-cadence"></a>
- **Review cadence:** A Class A, B, C, L, or P system or Institution is checked on a calendar interval set by its class. The **Integrity Forum** that holds its certification under [§6.5 Integrity Forums](#65-integrity-forums) enforces two checks, evenly spaced by default:
  - **Certification interval:** full certification or recertification at least every **3 months** for Class A, **6 months** for Class B, **1 year** for Class C, **2 years** for Class L, and **3 years** for Class P, counted from the last completed certification pass.
  - **Audit midpoint:** a financial, resource, or special audit, enforced by the forum, falls halfway through the interval after the last certification pass. The audit must be completed no later than that midpoint: 45 days for Class A, 3 months for Class B, 6 months for Class C, 1 year for Class L, and 18 months for Class P. A body may bring the audit earlier under the next item, and never later.
  - **Audit advance allowance:** a system or Institution may move its audit date earlier for its own convenience, by up to a quarter of the certification interval before the midpoint: 22 days for Class A, 45 days for Class B, 3 months for Class C, 6 months for Class L, and 9 months for Class P. A shift needs a published notice with reasons, given to the forum before the audit would otherwise have been due. A shift moves the audit earlier and nothing else: the audit still happens in that cycle, it is never moved later than the midpoint, and the certification dates do not move. A shift covers one cycle, so the next audit returns to the midpoint, and a body may use a shift in at most one cycle out of every three. It is not available while a reopened or fresh review is open, or while a finding from an earlier audit is unremedied.
  - **Counting months and years:** a period counted in months or years ends on the same day of the month as it began. If that month has no such day (for example 29 February, or the 31st in a 30-day month), it ends on the last day of that month.
  - **Provisional interval:** a system or Institution holding provisional certification runs on a shorter interval, which the forum sets and the Forum Case Record states. Its audit still falls at the midpoint of the interval in force.
  - **Class P:** the cadence applies to a Class P body only where it publishes a System Charter or Institutional Charter.
  - **Floors, not ceilings:** an adopted document may set shorter intervals and never longer ones. Reopening for cause under [Chapter Eleven §3.2 Reopening Triggers](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#32-reopening-triggers) is unaffected, and the next certification and audit dates run from the latest completed certification pass.
  - **Where results go:** audit results go to the forum that enforces them and to the Integrity registrar under [CF-23.415 §6 Integrity Registrar](corpus_forum/cf_23.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-23415-6-integrity-registrar).
- **Anti-delay floor:** The following are non-compliant where they foreseeably nullify rights, remedies, or timely protection:
  - designed backlog, chronic underfunding, or inaccessible intake that defeats **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*) practical access;
  - delay regimes designed to exhaust claimants;
  - self-created delay, procedural layering, forum shopping, or record fragmentation used to prolong resolution without milestone justification;
  - treating allegations as verified standing inputs to buy time ([Chapter Fifteen §3.5 Verified-Input Gate](core_15.000_standing_assessment.md#35-verified-input-gate));
  - treating restricted-evidence status, or a dispute about access to restricted information, as a reason for a slower column, a longer window, or a continuation (restricted evidence does not slow the clock; see [CF-23.445 §3.3](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-33-restricted-evidence-timing) (*Restricted-evidence timing*));
  - [Capture of Resolution Pathways](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#capture-of-resolution-pathways) through delay, opacity, or resolver bias;
  - treating a met throughput, closure, or docket target as timely resolution while the harm the matter concerns continues;
  - efficiency claims contrary to Chapter One [§9.2 Constitutional Efficiency](core_01.010_values_principles_continuity_aim.md#92-constitutional-efficiency) that:
    - skip fact-checking;
    - punish the wrong party;
    - offer a fix that does not fit the harm; or
    - cut off challenge and appeal.

#### 8.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Implementation (non-operative)</span></strong></summary>

This subsection sets the requirements and the operating rules for emergency measures. Retrospective review of an emergency measure is governed by [**Article XXVI-A** (*Retrospective Review and Disclosure*)](core_06.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvi-a-retrospective-review-and-disclosure). Any restriction an emergency measure imposes must also meet [**Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*)](core_06.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xx-b-restriction-floors). It reuses the tier outer bounds and the **Restore-challenge after emergency containment** rule above. It adds no second table of windows beyond the Emergency setting's 3-day protective-step bound in [§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#time-scale-settings) (*Time-scale settings*). Read with [Emergency and Contingency](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#emergency-and-contingency), [Constitutional Emergency and Contingency](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#constitutional-emergency-and-contingency), [Stakeholder Emergency and Contingency](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#stakeholder-emergency-and-contingency), [Necessity](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#necessity), and [Proportionality](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#proportionality); and with **Article I-D** (*Existential Risk and Ecological Recovery Capacity*) where existential-risk scrutiny is materially implicated.

*In plain terms: emergencies can justify temporary measures, but they must have a real clock, real review, and cannot become a permanent workaround around ordinary rights — including when someone invokes existential risk. Contain now; restore notice and challenge on the same stake-scaled clocks already used for forum resolution — not whenever someone later calls it “feasible.”*

- **Emergency application of the constraint principle:** Emergency measures apply the [**Least-Restrictive, Time-Bounded, and Reviewable Constraint Principle**](core_01.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1315-least-restrictive-time-bounded-and-reviewable-constraint-principle) under imminent-harm conditions. Temporary measures to prevent imminent harm must be:
  - time-limited;
  - documented;
  - consistent with **Articles VI**, **XXIII**, and **Chapter One** constraints.

  Each measure must carry:
  - a default expiry;
  - a predefined independent review cadence;
  - explicit restoration and rollback conditions.
- **Anti-pretext and no-bypass:** Emergency designation is governed by the [**Constitutional No-Bypass Principle**](core_01.030_constitutional_interpretation.md#151-constitutional-no-bypass-principle) and must not rest on:
  - operator convenience;
  - self-created urgency;
  - avoidance of ordinary constitutional scrutiny;
  - existential-risk claims used to bypass **Truth**, auditability, contestability, or time-limited review.

  The only exception is the narrow extent strictly necessary to prevent imminent irreversible harm. Any such temporary restriction must be attributable, justified, and reviewable inside the **prompt-review window** of [§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*).
- **Continuation discipline:** Continuation beyond default expiry, or past the restore-challenge window, is non-compliant unless reauthorized through a documented burden showing:
  - continuing necessity;
  - proportionality;
  - absence of less-restrictive feasible alternatives.
- **Review and notice:** Contain now; restore notice and challenge later; never skip participation permanently. “As soon as feasible” is not the clock. The restore-challenge windows are the tier outer bounds in **Restore-challenge after emergency containment** above (3 days at the Emergency setting). Operators may restore earlier. They may not stretch past the tier default except through **Continuation discipline**. Full independent review of the measure must begin inside the **prompt-review window** of [§8 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](#prompt-review) (*Prompt review*), which is never longer than the restore-challenge window. Stage tables in [CF-23.445 §3.1](corpus_forum/cf_23.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-23445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*) must not slow these defaults.
- **No normalization:** Emergency measures must not normalize into permanent governance without:
  - **Chapter One**-consistent justification (**Necessity** and **Proportionality**);
  - **documented independent reauthorization**;
  - **Chapter Twenty-Nine** procedures where permanence would amend or newly bind the adopted corpus.

*In plain terms: forums need help before a case exists, while deciding, and after — inspectors and forensics build an explainable record; they do not become a second merits forum.*

Forum families must maintain or obtain access to **independent** support across the review lifecycle. Support roles may gather, preserve, reconstruct, test, and explain evidence. Their work must remain **contestable**. They must **not** themselves exercise **binding** **merits** authority, assign Chapter Sixteen standing slots, or substitute for a lawfully constituted merits panel. Operational detail lives in [**CF-23.430**](corpus_forum/cf_23.430_forum_forensic_analytical_support.md#cf-23430-forum-forensic-and-analytical-support) (*Forum Forensic and Analytical Support*) and [**CF-23.435**](corpus_forum/cf_23.435_independent_investigative_service_prosecution_interface.md#cf-23435-independent-investigative-service-and-prosecution-interface) (*Independent Investigative Service and Prosecution Interface*).

- **Before review:**
  - Capacity: Forum families must be able to obtain independent **inspection** or **inquiry** capacity for material risk, harm patterns, or precondition failure **without waiting for a filed case**.
  - Example: monitoring ecosystem health near an industrial area when credible indicators warrant inquiry.
  - Downstream effects: that work may trigger evidence preservation, referral to intake under **section 3**, interim protection under **section 7**, or opening of a case.
  - Boundary: it does **not** itself decide merits or open, update, or correct Standing Records.
- **During review:**
  - Trigger: where **material** **uncertainty**, **technical** **opacity**, **restricted** **evidence**, or **causal** **complexity** would otherwise prevent reliable adjudication.
  - Capacity: forum families must have access to independent **forensic** or **analytical** capacity consistent with **Chapters Two through Four**, **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), and **Article XVI** (*Audit, Transparency, and Independent Verification*).
- **After review:**
  - Capacity: where material, forum families must be able to obtain support for remedy verification, pattern follow-through, and reopening inputs.
  - Boundary: that support must **not** substitute for Chapter Sixteen standing measurement or Chapter Seventeen integration.
