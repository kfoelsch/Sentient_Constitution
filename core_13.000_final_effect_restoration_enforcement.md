Chapter Thirteen is the constitutional owner of the **final standing effect** for each applicable gated pathway, of how that effect is restored or reassessed, and of the check that it can be enforced.
### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: when the earlier steps are done, the system must state, one gated pathway at a time, what is open, limited, or blocked. That statement is not a dignity rank or a merged score. A lock that is not permanent must have a real way back.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Thirteen states one final standing effect for each applicable gated pathway (**section 3**). It then sets restoration and reassessment (**section 4**) and confirms that standing consequences and redress can be delivered by real institutions (**section 5**), applying the **Chapter Two section 6.1** (*Correction and Remedy*) principle.

**Role:** The chapter is where the two tracks of Question 3 meet:

- It takes the contribution result from Chapter Eleven and the violation result from Chapter Twelve as fixed, in the order **Chapter Ten section 4.4** sets.
- Gated-pathway effects are never aggregated (**section 3.1**). A gated pathway a lock blocks stays closed, whatever the contribution track found.
- Restoration is not automatic forgiveness, record erasure, or letting past help cancel verified harm.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:

- the final effect (**section 3**);
- restoration and reassessment (**section 4**); and
- enforcement realism and remedy systems (**section 5**).

The chapter leaves:

- the decision order to [Subcore 10-F](subcore_10.060_standing_integration.md#subcore-10-f-standing-integration--question-3-what-follows);
- how contribution and violation results are reached to Chapters [Seventeen](core_11.000_contribution_consequences.md#chapter-eleven-contribution-consequences) and [Eighteen](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#chapter-twelve-violation-consequences--remedy-correction-and-locks); and
- day-to-day staffing, funding, backlog, and succession rules to adopted implementation text.

### 2. Why Final Effects and Restoration Exist

*In plain terms: a sentient must be able to see exactly what is open and closed to them, and how to get back what was closed. This chapter exists so no consequence becomes a hidden, permanent mark.*

After the earlier steps, the results have to land somewhere concrete. If they become a general label, they follow a sentient everywhere and cannot be answered. This chapter states the effect one gated pathway at a time, never as a dignity rank or merged score, and gives every lock that is not permanent a real way back. It also requires that the effects and the redress can be delivered by real institutions.

### 3. Final Standing Effect

*In plain terms: when the earlier steps are done, the system must state — one gated pathway at a time — what is open, limited, or blocked. That statement is not a dignity rank or a merged score.*

The final standing effect is governed by the following rule groups:

- **Automatic statement:**
  - The standing system must **automatically** state the final standing effect for each applicable gated pathway once the earlier steps in [Subcore 10-F §4.4 Processing Order](subcore_10.060_standing_integration.md#44-processing-order) are complete.
  - That statement must list every open clearance and every gated pathway under a lock or hold.
- **Review and approval:**
  - No additional manual approval is required when verified inputs and published rules produce a determinate result.
  - Contested, uncertain, novel, materially discretionary, or high-impact questions still assemble and route automatically, then go to authorized review under [Subcore 10-F §4 Automatic Integration, Review, and Continuity](subcore_10.060_standing_integration.md#4-automatic-integration-review-and-continuity).
- **Separate roles:**
  - Automatic integration may use verified Standing Records, remedies, correction duties, locks, bars, clearances, and descriptors together.
  - Each still has its own job, and none may do the work of another.
- **Limits on restriction:**
  - Closer or automated scrutiny is not, by itself, a reason to impose a harsher restriction.
  - Any restriction must be necessary, matched to the verified facts, backed by evidence, open to review, and consistent with **Article XXI** (*Justice After Verified Violation*).

The integration record must state the final effect separately for each affected gated pathway:

- **open**—competency clearance is in force against the published bar and no lock blocks the gated pathway;
- **open with safeguards**—readiness is established but named, proportionate safeguards remain required;
- **limited**—only a stated scope or level may open;
- **blocked**—a standing lock controls;
- **restoration-only**—contribution may support repair credibility or supervised lower-risk service but not ordinary gated pathway access; or
- **no effect**—the verified Standing Records do not justify a change.

The integration record must show which of the following produced the effect:

- immutable inputs;
- descriptors;
- remedies;
- locks;
- currentness findings;
- competency bars; and
- clearances.

It must also expose the inputs, weights, rules, and review routes used. The final effect is not a dignity rank, merged merit score, universal reputation label, or substitute for the underlying Standing Records.

<a id="31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects"></a>
#### 3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects

*In plain terms: this chapter says what standing is not. This subsection stops anyone from building the thing it is not. Effects are stated one gated pathway at a time and shown only to whoever gates that gated pathway. A gatekeeper, such as an institution hiring into a gated role, may ask whether an effect applies to that role and gets an answer about that role alone; it may not pass the answer on, and no one may make the subject show it. Stitching them together into a profile, a rank, or a public display constructs the merged score this chapter forbids — and doing so is itself a violation.*

This subsection sets out who may see gated-pathway effects, what a gatekeeper may ask and do with the answer, and what aggregation is prohibited:

- **Disclosure scope:** The final effect for a gated pathway is disclosed to the subject, to the gatekeeper of that gated pathway, and to authorized reviewers. It is not disclosed to gatekeepers of other gated pathways as a matter of course.
- **Gatekeeper check:** A **gatekeeper** is a party that decides who takes or keeps a role or access on a gated pathway, including an institution that hires or appoints into one. When it decides whether the subject takes or keeps that role or access, the gatekeeper may ask the record custodian whether a standing lock or other standing effect applies to **that gated pathway** for the subject, and is entitled to an answer.
  - The answer states only whether an effect applies to the gated pathway the gatekeeper gates, what it opens, limits, or blocks there, and when it expires or is next reviewed. It does not state the facts, the findings, the slot, the descriptors, the evidence, or any effect on another gated pathway.
  - Where no effect applies to that gated pathway, the answer says only that none does. It does not say whether the subject has a Standing Record or an effect elsewhere.
  - A lock reaches a gatekeeper only through the gated pathway the lock closes or limits, as the lock record names it. A gatekeeper whose gated pathway the lock does not reach gets the answer that none applies.
  - A role or access that is not a gated pathway has no gatekeeper under this subsection, and whoever fills it gets no answer.
  - Each request is recorded on the subject's Standing Record: who asked, for which gated pathway, and when. The subject may see the entry.
  - **Answer window:** The record custodian answers inside the **check-answer window**, which is four calendar days from the day the request reaches the custodian or, for a request from another adopter, the office-level contact named under [§3.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](#34-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust). An answer that the requester is not a gatekeeper of a gated pathway is also an answer, and it too comes inside the window.
  - **Silence:** If the window ends with no answer, that is a failure of the answering custodian. It is never the answer that no effect applies, and it is never an adverse fact about the subject. The gatekeeper may wait, or may decide that gated pathway without the answer, as the subject chooses. Neither choice is an adverse fact about the subject.
- **No passing on:** The gatekeeper may use the answer only to decide that gated pathway. It may share the answer only with the sentients who decide it, and keep it only as long as the decision and any challenge to it need. It must not pass the answer on to another institution, another gated pathway, a data service, or the public. It may give the answer to the subject, to an authorized reviewer, or to a forum in a proceeding about that decision.
- **No requiring the subject to show it:** Outside the gatekeeper check, no one may require a subject to show, attest to, or consent to the release of a standing effect or any part of a Standing Record. A request counts as a requirement when refusing it is treated as an adverse fact or costs the subject the role or access.
- **Violation conduct:** Passing an answer on, keeping it beyond the point above, and requiring a subject to show an effect or record outside the gatekeeper check are each independently measurable violation conduct under [Subcore 10-B](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#subcore-10-b-standing-records--question-1-what-happened), read with [Article VII-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-b-self-ownership-of-mind) (*Self-Ownership of Mind*).
- **Prohibited aggregation:** The following construct a merged score and are independently measurable violation conduct under [Subcore 10-B](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#subcore-10-b-standing-records--question-1-what-happened), read with [Article VII-B](core_04.010_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-b-self-ownership-of-mind) (*Self-Ownership of Mind*), whose aggregation discipline this subsection mirrors for standing:
  - assembling final effects across gated pathways into:
    - a profile;
    - a composite;
    - a ranking;
    - a grade;
    - a badge;
    - a public display; or
    - a comparable artifact;
  - transferring an effect to a gated pathway that did not produce it; or
  - inferring general trustworthiness, worth, or risk from the set of a subject's effects.
- **Bounded exceptions:** [Integrity Forums](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#35-integrity-forums) examining capture or pattern claims, [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-fourteen-anti-constitutional-misconduct-designation) designation review, and [§5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](#54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority) look-through may view cross-pathway records, under recusal rules and the [Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) clocks, for that proceeding only. [Subcore 10-B §6.2 Related Standing Record Cross-References](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#62-related-standing-record-cross-references) cross-references between linked Standing Records remain; they are record links, not a composite.
- **Public LEQU display:** Violation Axis and Contribution Axis slots are not published as a general attribute of a sentient. They appear in the Standing Record, in forum proceedings, in the disclosure to the gated-pathway gatekeeper where the slot is a lawful input to that gated pathway, and, for a violation at slot 5 or higher, in a public notice about that event under [§3.3 Public Notice of Violation Findings](#33-public-notice-of-violation-findings).

<a id="32-plain-statement-of-effect-and-burden"></a>
#### 3.2 Plain Statement of Effect and Burden

*In plain terms: no one should learn what a standing effect does to them from its consequences. The integration record must tell the subject, in plain language, exactly which gated pathways are affected, what they must do, how long it lasts, and where to contest it — and the adopter must measure whether sentients are experiencing standing as a score, because the rule that it is not one is worthless if that is how it lands.*

This subsection sets out what the subject must be told when an effect attaches:

- **Plain-language statement to the subject:** When a final effect other than **no effect** attaches, the subject must receive a plain-language statement. The statement must satisfy [Chapter Two §5.2 Plain-Language Accessibility (Participation and Stewardship Duty)](core_02.000_values_principles.md#52-plain-language-accessibility-participation-and-stewardship-duty) and **Article XXIV-A** (*Proportional Comprehensibility Right*), and must name:
  - each affected gated pathway;
  - what the subject may and may not do while the effect holds;
  - the corrective conditions and reassessment point;
  - the expected duration or review cadence; and
  - the challenge route under **Article XIV-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*); and
  - where a violation at slot 5 or higher is involved, that a public notice will be published under [§3.3 Public Notice of Violation Findings](#33-public-notice-of-violation-findings), what it will say, and when it comes down.
- **Honest cost:** A standing lock closes real doors and an inspectable-action duty is a real burden on the steward who carries it. Adopters must state those costs plainly in the material that describes standing to affected sentients, alongside what standing is not. Describing only the limits of standing while omitting its weight is a [Truth](core_02.000_values_principles.md#5-truth-epistemic-integrity-constraint) defect.
- **Felt-burden measure:** Adopters must measure, under the [Participation measurement family](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family), whether affected sentients experience standing effects as a general worth rank, reputation score, or permanent status rather than as bounded gated-pathway conditions. Material divergence between the legal category and the lived one is [Proxy Divergence](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#proxy-divergence) under [Chapter Two §13.2.4 Proxy-Divergence Invalidation](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1324-proxy-divergence-invalidation): it must be documented, escalated, and corrected through design, disclosure, or [§3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects) enforcement — not explained away.
  - <a id="felt-burden-independent-verification"></a>**Independent verification:** The measure is run, or audited, by a verifying authority independent of the adopter and of any party that holds or uses its Standing Records, under the audit process in [CJS-3.115 §3](corpus_joint_structure/cjs_03.115_audit_process.md#cjs-3115-3-audit-process-home-odef3) (*Audit process home*), on a published cadence. The method and aggregate results are published; no result identifies a sentient. Any affected sentient may ask the verifying authority to review a divergence. Adopters too small to support their own verifying authority may share one, provided it stays independent of each of them.

<a id="33-public-notice-of-violation-findings"></a>
#### 3.3 Public Notice of Violation Findings

*In plain terms: a verified violation at slot 5 or higher is published as a notice about the event, whoever the subject is, once review has ended and for as long as the violation record is active. Violations at slots 1 to 4 stay private. Contribution stays private unless the contributor chooses to publish it. The sentient harmed is not named unless that sentient chooses. Publishing a notice never licenses turning published notices into a score or screening product, and a notice carries no contact, location, or activity data about the subject.*

This subsection sets out what is public, what stays private, and how a public notice is limited:

- **Which are published:** The record custodian publishes a public notice for each verified violation finding at Violation Axis slot **5 or higher**, whether the subject is a sentient, an institution, or a system.
  - Violation findings at slots **1 to 4** are private. They appear in the Standing Record, in forum proceedings, to the sentients harmed, and in the gatekeeper answer under [§3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects).
  - Contribution is private at every slot. A contributor may choose to publish their own contribution record and may withdraw the choice. No one may require or press a contributor to publish, and not publishing is never an adverse fact ([Subcore 10-B §4.2 Silence Is the Default](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#42-silence-is-the-default)).
- **When it is published:** Only once the violation finding is final: the time to review or challenge it under [Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) has run, or the review has ended with the violation finding standing.
  - A hold, an allegation, or a violation finding still under review is never published.
  - If a published violation finding is later set aside or corrected, the custodian publishes the correction in the same place with the same prominence and takes the original down.
- **What the notice says:** the event or pattern; the subject found responsible; the duty violated; what was found; the Violation Axis slot for that event; the remedy ordered and whether it has been delivered; and the name of any lock imposed and the gated pathway it closes.
  - The slot is a statement about the event. It is not a label of the subject.
  - The notice is kept current. When a lock lifts or the remedy is delivered, the notice says so.
  - The notice states no other standing effect, and it is not a gatekeeper answer under [§3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects).
- **How long it stays up:** while the violation Standing Record is active. It comes down when that violation Standing Record is archived under [§4.3 Rest State and Archival](#43-rest-state-and-archival). A violation at slot 7, 8, or 9 stays active, so its notice stays up while it does. Taking a notice down does not erase the Standing Record.
- **Sentients harmed and others protected:** The notice does not identify the sentient harmed, or any protected reporter or witness, unless that sentient chooses to be identified.
  - The choice must be informed and free of pressure from the subject, the forum, or any party, and it may be withdrawn for later publication.
  - Where the sentient harmed cannot decide, their independent representative decides in their best interest under the [Best-Interest Standard](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#best-interest-standard).
  - A sentient who chooses to be identified is named as the sentient harmed.
- **Developing sentients:** A violation finding against a developing sentient is not published. The notice for the adult, institution, or system responsible is.
- **No scoring or screening from notices:** Publishing a notice does not license anyone to use published notices to build a score, ranking, grade, badge, or screening product about a subject that is used to decide access or treatment, or to infer general trustworthiness, worth, or risk from them. That is the aggregation [§3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects) bars, and doing it is itself violation conduct.
  - Reporting, research, journalism, and oversight work that draws on published notices is not barred.
  - A gatekeeper uses the gatekeeper check in [§3.1 Anti-Aggregation of Gated-Pathway Effects](#31-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects), so no such product is ever needed.
- **What the notice leaves out:** The notice carries no contact or location information and no activity history about the subject beyond the event itself. A published notice does not license anyone to compile contact, location, or activity data about the subject. That data stays Type I or Type H and falls under the onward-transfer rule in [Subcore 4-A §6.0 Access-posture bands](subcore_04.050_data_types.md#onward-transfer-of-type-h-and-type-i-data).
- **Accuracy before publication:** Before a notice is published, the subject may point out errors of fact, which the custodian must correct where verified. This corrects errors of fact and does not reopen the violation finding.

<a id="34-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust"></a>
#### 3.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust

*In plain terms: a gatekeeper at one adopter may send the same narrow question to another adopter's record custodian that it could send at home, and gets an answer as far as the answering adopter trusts the asker. The level of trust changes who may ask and what kind of answer is given. It never changes how much of a Standing Record is disclosed. Each adopter decides the level of trust it extends for itself, and the adopter on the other side cannot contest that decision. Each adopter keeps its own Standing Records and its own keys, and the door stays open for adopters to merge later.*

The full rules are in [Subcore 13-A — §3 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters](subcore_13.010_cross_adopter_checks_remedy_durability.md#3-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters). Their guarantees are:

- **Same narrow question:** a gatekeeper at one adopter asks another adopter's record custodian, through the office-level contact in its Adoption Charter or Adoption Transition Record, the same narrow gatekeeper check it could ask at home.
- **Four levels of trust, public by default:** each adopter decides the level of trust it extends to each other adopter (public, notice, query, or joint), and the default is the public level. A level of trust is one-way unless both agree otherwise.
- <a id="34-lock-floor"></a>**Lock floor:** whatever the level of trust, every adopter answers a lock check from a gatekeeper at any adopter with a published Adoption Charter or Adoption Transition Record, with only whether a lock applies to that gated pathway, so a verified lock follows the sentient without the Standing Record behind it.
- **What no level of trust does:** no level of trust widens what a gatekeeper check may answer, makes private data public, lets anyone require the subject to show a Standing Record, or ends a sentient's right to direct disclosure of their own Standing Record.
- **Who decides, and how:** the adopter's Institutional Forum decides, separate from the record custodian seat, on published technical advice. A departure from that advice meets [Heightened Scrutiny](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#heightened-scrutiny), and the decision is published in the Adoption Charter.
- **Lowering with notice:** a level of trust is lowered on published reasons and notice to the other adopter, at once if urgent and otherwise after the **lowering notice period** of fourteen calendar days, and lowering never ends a lock already in force.
- **Own decision, own checks:** the other adopter cannot contest a level of trust, but a sentient under the deciding adopter's authority may challenge it in that adopter's own forums.
- **Own keys, no forced merging:** each adopter keeps its own data and keys below the joint level, and the joint level is a deliberate transfer, made only after the sentient is told and with a reversible exit.
- **No relationship, no escape:** a verified lock, remedy, safeguard, or review obligation binds across the boundary under [§5.3 Cross-Jurisdiction Continuity](#53-cross-jurisdiction-continuity) even where no lookup route exists.

<a id="4-restoration-and-reassessment"></a>
### 4. Restoration and Reassessment

*In plain terms: a lock that is not permanent must have a real way back. Restoration is not automatic forgiveness, record erasure, or letting past help cancel verified harm.*

Every non-permanent lock must provide a real route to review. Restoration may require completed correction, restitution, remedy, evidence preservation, safeguard implementation, monitoring, requalification, supervised lower-risk service, or another verified reduction in recurrence risk. Historical status alone cannot justify indefinite exclusion.

The subsections below cover how reassessment works, when a lock may be permanent, and when a resolved violation record leaves active integration.

<a id="41-reassessment-and-restoration-outcomes"></a>
#### 4.1 Reassessment and Restoration Outcomes

*In plain terms: reassessment asks whether the repair was really delivered and the risk really went down. Restoration can be partial, and it is not forgiveness or erasure of the violation record.*

Reassessment begins when the integration record's stated trigger is met or when material new verified facts require review. It asks:

- **Delivery:**
  - whether remedy and correction obligations were actually completed;
  - whether affected sentients and interests received practical redress;
- **Risk and readiness:**
  - whether recurrence, evasion, dependency, and gated pathway risks materially declined;
  - whether current competence and reliability satisfy any applicable bar and support clearance;
- **Continued restriction and renewed access:**
  - whether continued restriction remains needed and proportionate; and
  - whether renewed access is compatible with the Rights Floor and the protected constitutional interests.

Restoration is not automatic forgiveness, record erasure, or contribution offset. Partial completion may justify a narrower lock only when the remaining protection is sufficient. Completed remedy does not end the need for prevention. If the same harm or pattern is verified to repeat after the remedy was otherwise completed, or verified recurrence risk otherwise remains live, a general standing lock under [§3.2 Prevention — General Standing Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#32-prevention--general-standing-locks) may be set or continued (a recurrence-persistence lock). It is not a separate lock type, and it must still meet [§4 Lock Design and Enforcement](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#4-lock-design-and-enforcement). The special restoration rules for Forum-Service, Stakeholder-Participation, Institutional Authority, and Anti-Constitutional Trust Locks control over this general rule. The Dissolution Reformation Lock follows the permanence rule in [§4.2 Permanent Locks](#42-permanent-locks).

<a id="42-permanent-locks"></a>
#### 4.2 Permanent Locks

*In plain terms: a lock is permanent only when the forum records one of two specific grounds, and even then it stays open to review for error.*

A lock is permanent only where the forum that sets it records, in the lock record and on the verified violation findings, one of two permanent-lock grounds:

- **Pervasive anti-constitutional conduct:** a final [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-fourteen-anti-constitutional-misconduct-designation) designation, with a finding that the conduct runs through the subject's control, operation, or purpose, so that no part of the subject can be separated from it and trusted; or
- **Unremedied Irreversible Harm:** verified [Irreversible Harm](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#irreversible-harm) for which the remedy owed has not been delivered and cannot be delivered by restitution or lawful substitute restoration. Insolvency of the subject is not, by itself, a remedy that cannot be delivered, and this Unremedied Irreversible Harm ground is recorded or confirmed at the final accounting, when the outcome of the liquidation is known, and not at the order.

- **Which locks can be permanent:**
  - Only the Dissolution Reformation Lock is defined here as able to be permanent.
  - No other special lock is, and the Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock keeps its exceptional restoration route.
- **Ending a permanent lock:**
  - Passage of time, apology, prior contribution, capital, or a change of holders, name, or form cannot lift a permanent lock.
  - A permanent lock stays open to review for error: a set-aside of the violation finding it rests on, or material new verified facts, can end it.
  - Where later verified facts show that the remedy was in fact delivered, a lock set on the Unremedied Irreversible Harm ground is reassessed as a non-permanent lock.

<a id="43-rest-state-and-archival"></a>
#### 4.3 Rest State and Archival

*In plain terms: once restoration is complete and nothing remains open, most violation records leave active use and are archived, not deleted; the most serious findings stay active.*

Contribution has a currentness half-life under [§3.1 Recency and Currentness](core_11.000_contribution_consequences.md#31-recency-and-currentness); a violation record must also have a rest state, or a resolved finding follows a sentient indefinitely and becomes the permanent status [Subcore 10-B §4.1 Standing Records: Kinds and Subjects](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#41-standing-records-kinds-and-subjects) forbids. Once restoration is complete and no lock, remedy duty, or correction duty remains open, the violation record leaves active integration:

- slot **1**, **2**, or **3** findings are archived on completed restoration;
- slot **4**, **5**, or **6** findings are archived after a published period that may not exceed the [§3.1 Recency and Currentness](core_11.000_contribution_consequences.md#31-recency-and-currentness) contribution half-life;
- slot **7**, **8**, or **9** findings remain active indefinitely, subject to [§4.5 Special Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#45-special-locks) restoration rules and Chapter Twenty.

A public notice under [§3.3 Public Notice of Violation Findings](#33-public-notice-of-violation-findings) comes down when its violation Standing Record is archived.

Archival is not deletion. [Evidence Preservation](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#evidence-preservation) continues; the archived record remains reachable to a forum on a documented showing that it bears on recurrence risk in a live matter; and archival is recorded in the version history under [Subcore 10-B §6.4 Versioning](subcore_10.020_standing_assessment.md#64-versioning). An archived record produces no gated-pathway effect, is not disclosed to gated-pathway gatekeepers, and is not an input to competency bars, except where a published bar for a specific high-sensitivity gated pathway states a longer look-back and that statement itself satisfies [Necessity](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#necessity) and [Proportionality](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#proportionality).

<a id="5-enforcement-realism-and-remedy-systems"></a>
### 5. Enforcement Realism and Remedy Systems

*In plain terms: standing consequences and redress count only when real institutions can deliver them. This section applies the [Chapter Two §6.1](core_02.000_values_principles.md#61-correction-and-remedy) (*Correction and Remedy*) principle to that machinery. Day-to-day staffing, funding, backlog, and succession rules live in the institutions layer.*

Consequences under this chapter only count if real institutions can carry them out. This applies [Chapter Two §6.1](core_02.000_values_principles.md#61-correction-and-remedy) (*Correction and Remedy*) to standing effects, including the redress that goes with them.

- **What a Remedy System is:** a [Remedy System](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#remedy-system) is lasting, working capacity, not a remedy that exists only on paper. It must be able to:
  - let sentients challenge a standing effect and receive their cases;
  - preserve records and evidence;
  - review cases;
  - repair harm;
  - monitor follow-through;
  - enforce safeguards; and
  - review locks.
- **Where the details live:** [CI-19.210](corpus_institutions/ci_19.210_remedy_systems_institutional_redress_capacity.md#ci-19210-remedy-systems-and-institutional-redress-capacity) (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*) owns how a Remedy System is put into practice.
- **Read with:** **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), and [Chapter Sixteen](core_16.000_forum.md#chapter-sixteen-standards-routing-and-forum-cases) for forum supervision.

Standing integration fails if consequences exist only on paper. Adopters must keep the practical capacity, and keep it timely, to:

- carry out corrections;
- fund remedies;
- review restrictions;
- preserve records;
- enforce safeguards; and
- stop anyone from escaping through cost, delay, borders, restructuring, or a change of formal identity.

<a id="51-remediation-capacity-and-funding"></a>
#### 5.1 Remediation Capacity and Funding

Correction, repair, compensation, monitoring, safeguards, and recurrence prevention require real:

- institutional capacity;
- technical capacity; and
- financial capacity.

Expense, insolvency, inconvenience, receivership, dissolution, asset transfer, or restructuring does not by itself defeat a verified obligation. Successors, estates, receivers, and comparable transferees inherit proportionate duties — whether the responsible actor is an individual, an institution, or a system — unless a less harmful lawful transfer is demonstrated.

Costs must not be externalized onto:

- affected sentients;
- communities;
- ecological subjects; or
- public remedy systems

where the responsible actors under a verified violation finding, and their successors, estates, receivers, and comparable transferees, can lawfully bear them.

<a id="52-remedy-system-durability"></a>
#### 5.2 Remedy-System Durability

The full rules are in [Subcore 13-A — §4 Remedy-System Durability and Parity](subcore_13.010_cross_adopter_checks_remedy_durability.md#4-remedy-system-durability-and-parity). Their guarantees are:

- **Durable bodies:** forums, oversight bodies, complaint offices, technical review bodies, and rights-sensitive administrative systems have enough staffing, funding, independence, records capacity, accessibility, and succession planning, scaled to risk, case volume, vulnerability, and rights sensitivity.
- **High-impact systems:** child protection, environmental enforcement, benefits administration, detention oversight, public-health response, and comparable systems have remedy systems able to take in, preserve, review, and enforce on time.
- **Clear roles:** timing follows [Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), Technical Forums set capacity and measurement standards without commanding remedy systems, and the primary-stakes forum family answers for performance.
- **Hollow capacity is a violation:** designed backlog, inaccessible intake, chronic underfunding, dependence on one fragile office, chronic milestone failure, and under-appointment of the benches named in the subcore text violate this rule where they foreseeably nullify challenge or remedy.
- <a id="52-remedy-parity-funding-minimum"></a>**Remedy-parity funding minimum:** for each system class, capacity funded for remedy, restoration, and reassessment is no less than capacity funded for locks, enforcement, and safeguards on the same class; the ratio is published, a departure is reasoned and shown not to delay remedy, and a starting reserve is held when lock machinery is first stood up.

<a id="53-cross-jurisdiction-continuity"></a>
#### 5.3 Cross-Jurisdiction Continuity

Verified remedy, lock, safeguard, and review obligations remain actionable across boundaries wherever applicable law permits recognition, cooperation, enforcement, or fallback review. Flight, relocation, forum shopping, cross-border structure, or distributed operation does not by itself nullify them.

<a id="54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority"></a>
#### 5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority

Forums and implementation systems must be able to look through formal structure to functional control, benefit, continuity, and responsibility. Reincorporation, shells, contractual layering, nominal ownership transfer, asset stripping, record fragmentation, successor relabeling, forum shopping, misuse of confidentiality, and transfer of custody do not erase verified duties. Look-through decisions must remain evidence-based, actor-specific, contestable, proportionate, and reviewable. Where the verified facts warrant, consequences may include suspension, receivership, structural separation, dissolution, and successor-control restrictions. The Institutional Authority Standing Lock on a sentient who used the institution to carry out a violation is separate from these institution-level consequences and does not replace them.

<a id="55-timely-implementation"></a>
#### 5.5 Timely Implementation

Remedy commencement, correction milestones, lock review, and reassessment must satisfy [Timeliness](subcore_03.065_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness-constitutional), [Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), and **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*). Delay that compounds harm, conceals non-performance, or defeats review is a new verified fact for Question 3 and may justify escalated safeguards without changing the original Chapter Fifteen slot.

Rights and standing consequences are real only when sentients can invoke them, verify them, fund practical repair, obtain timely review, and enforce obligations against the actors functionally responsible.
