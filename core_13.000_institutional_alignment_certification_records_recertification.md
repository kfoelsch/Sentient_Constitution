Chapter Thirteen is the constitutional owner of **Institutional Certification Records, recertification, challenge, and standing consequences of institutional alignment certification**.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: the outcome is kept on an Institutional Certification Record held by an office the institution does not control. The certification is re-checked on a schedule and when something material changes, and it can be challenged. It may feed standing, but it is a separate thing from standing.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Thirteen states what happens after an institutional outcome:

- what the Institutional Certification Record must contain, how open it is, and how it is audited (**section 3**);
- monitoring and recertification with reopening triggers, provisional and full certification, non-evasion, the cascade from System certifications, and the reopening of the Institution's Systems (**section 4**);
- the challenge path (**section 5**); and
- how an institutional certification relates to standing (**section 6**).

If one of an Institution's Systems loses its certification, the Institution answers for what that says about the Institution, and no more. If the Institution is found to have governed badly, its Systems are looked at again.

**Role:** The chapter is the Institution's counterpart to [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-ten-system-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing):

- It takes these as fixed:
  - the outcome from [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-twelve-institutional-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes); and
  - the evaluation and class framing from [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eleven-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation).
- The figures:
  - [Chapter Eleven §2 Why Institutional Alignment Certification Exists](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#2-why-institutional-alignment-certification-exists) shows how an Institutional Certification Record links to the System Certification Records it cites; and
  - [§4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority) has the figure for the wait on new material authority.
- It cites System Certification Records by link and does not copy or re-decide them.
- [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fourteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) accepts verified facts from this chapter only at the verified-input gate (**section 6.3**), and what they do to standing is decided there and in [Chapter Nineteen](core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#chapter-nineteen-final-standing-effect-restoration-and-enforcement).

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:

The chapter leaves:

### 2. Why Institutional Records and Recertification Exist

*In plain terms: an institution's certification must stay true as the institution changes. This chapter exists so the Institutional Certification Record is held by someone else and re-checked on time.*

Institutions merge, split, change owners, and change leaders. A certification that does not follow those changes would let an institution keep a good result after the facts behind it are gone. This chapter keeps the outcome on an Institutional Certification Record held by an office the institution does not control, sets when the institution is checked again, including when something material changes, and keeps the certification apart from standing.

*In plain terms: the stated outcome is kept on an Institutional Certification Record held by an office the institution does not control, and the Institutional Certification Record can be challenged.*

This section is **Phase V, Record** for an Institution.

An **Institutional Certification Record** is bounded and reviewable, and is about one Institution, one Institutional Charter version, one certified scope, one record span, and one decision context. It satisfies and extends the [Materially Binding Act Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act-record) and [Chapter Six §9 Act Records and Attributable Handoffs](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#9-act-records-and-attributable-handoffs). The record-keeping rules of [Chapter Ten §3 System Certification Record](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-system-certification-record) apply to it (Record Versions, no change in place to a completed Institutional Certification Record, change requests logged, and a challenge that does not freeze the Institutional Certification Record), with the Institutional Charter version in place of the System Version.

<a id="31-minimum-record-contents"></a>

#### 3.1 Minimum Record Contents

The Institutional Certification Record must identify, at minimum:

- **Institution identity, scope, and status:** the Institution, its governing authority, its Institutional Charter version (or the System Charter that serves as one), and its review status;
- **Prior Institutional Certification Record:** the completed Institutional Certification Record this certification pass follows, if any;
- **Institution classification:** the class under [§3.1 Institution Class](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-institution-class), with reasons;
- **Portfolio and cited System records:** each System in the portfolio, its System Charter version, and the System Certification Record cited, with its version, status, expiry, and the regression-testing results it carries. A System with no System Certification Record in force is listed with the reason;
- **Data types and handling:** for data the Institution handles outside any System, the data types materially in scope and how each is handled under the most restrictive applicable classification, which stands in the Institutional Certification Record in place of a System Data Types Record. It is re-evaluated on the schedule of [Chapter Eight §4.4.4 Scheduled data-type re-evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#444-scheduled-data-type-re-evaluation). Data handled inside a System is found once, in that System's record, and cited;
- **Institutional findings:** the findings on the eight items in [§4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), on the two further parts of Chapter Eight §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation that apply to the Institution (systemic risk, and data types and handling), and on any evaluation made under [§5 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated) or [§6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations), each with its evidence;
- **Charter Role Map version in force** and the placement of each of the four seats for this certification pass, with the independent route;
- **Forum supervision and functional-independence record:** the lead forum and each component authority, and how the seats were kept apart;
- **Challenge paths:** the published record challenge path, its receiver, how to file, and its clocks, as [Chapter Ten §5.2 Contestability Paths](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths) provides;
- **Concerns raised in review and decision;**
- **Review submissions log:** the log kept by the record seat holder under [Chapter Nine §3.2.4 Participation during Review](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#324-participation-during-review), published with the Institutional Certification Record;
- **Cause finding** for any misalignment, as [Chapter Ten §4.3.3 Cause of Misalignment](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#433-cause-of-misalignment) states, including whether the cause lies in the Institution or in a System;
- **Remediation readiness:** where misalignment was found, the findings and evidence required by [Chapter Ten §3.1 Minimum Record Contents](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#31-minimum-record-contents), stated for the Institution as [§4.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#44-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) provides;
- **Outcome, status, and reliance limits** (in a one-System evaluation, a line for the System and a line for the Institution);
- **Systems notified:** each System notified under [§4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems](#46-reopening-the-institutions-systems) and the date of the notice; and
- **Operating plan:** owners, clocks, monitoring and indicators, certification conditions, and verification criteria.

An Institutional Certification Record is incomplete if it omits the findings of any evaluation that applied, or any concern, objection, or dissent raised in review or in reaching the outcome, as [Chapter Ten §3.1.10 Completeness](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3110-completeness) states.

A System Certification Record alone, or an Institution's own report or attestation, offered in place of the Institutional Certification Record does not satisfy this section.

#### 4.1 Monitoring, Recertification, and Status

[Chapter Ten §4 Recertification and Reopening](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#4-recertification-and-reopening) (*monitoring and response; new record for each certification pass; validation*) and [§4.3 Provisional and Full Certification](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#43-provisional-and-full-certification) apply to an Institutional Certification Record with these substitutions:

<a id="42-reopening-triggers"></a>

#### 4.2 Reopening Triggers

An Institutional Certification Record is recertified on the cadence set for the Institution's class in [Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#review-cadence) (*Review cadence*), and the Institution is audited at the midpoint of that cadence. Review also reopens when any of the following is material:

- **Chapter Ten triggers,** as [Chapter Ten §4.2 Reopening Triggers](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers) lists them, applied to the Institution:
  - material change in its activity or context;
  - concealed behavior;
  - dependency growth, including a significant fall in what it puts back into shared foundations;
  - new risk;
  - incident history;
  - stakeholder evidence;
  - an ecological objection;
  - a credible challenge;
  - misclassification or overdue reassessment of its class;
  - data-type or handling misalignment, or an overdue or skipped re-evaluation of its data types; and
  - misalignment or a defect found under any evaluation that applied;
- **Institutional Charter events:**
  - an Institutional Charter amendment;
  - a mismatch between the Institutional Charter and observed operation;
  - operation materially outside chartered scope; or
  - an overdue scheduled Institutional Charter review;
- **Portfolio change:**
  - a System added, retired, merged, split, or transferred to or from the Institution; or
  - a change in who controls one;
- **New material authority:** the Institution takes on authority over others that is material under [Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) and that its Institutional Charter and current Institutional Certification Record do not cover (defined in [§4.2.1 What Counts as New Material Authority](#421-what-counts-as-new-material-authority); the recertification it requires is in [§4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](#422-recertification-for-new-material-authority));
- **Change of ownership, control, or structure,** including any change that moves authority outside the line that was evaluated ([Chapter Two §19.6 Keeping Responsibility When Ownership or Structure Changes](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#196-keeping-responsibility-when-ownership-or-structure-changes));
- **Reward and incentive change:** a material change to the Institution's pay, promotion, target, or reward structure;
- **Role Map or custody defect:**
  - a seat unplaced;
  - an office vacant without substitution; or
  - a custodian brought inside the Institution's control line;
- **Office-holder events:**
  - a change of holder in a covered office;
  - a change in who controls one;
  - a verified standing lock or an [Interim Hold](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) on a holder; or
  - a finding that the formation check statement was false or incomplete;
- **Readiness events,** under [Chapter Nine §4.3 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#43-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct):
  - a missed proof step or certification condition deadline; or
  - false readiness evidence; and
- **Cited System events:** a cited System Certification Record failed, withdrawn, or expired, which also triggers [§4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](#45-cascade-from-system-certifications); or reopened for a reason other than [§4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems](#46-reopening-the-institutions-systems). A reopening under §4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems follows from the Institution's own determination and does not reopen the Institution's review again.

A material and timely trigger requires timely forum review and does not wait for the next scheduled pass. The tier windows and materiality tiers of [Chapter Ten §4.3.4 Tier Windows](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#434-tier-windows) apply, with the tier presumptively matching the institution class.

<a id="421-what-counts-as-new-material-authority"></a>

##### 4.2.1 What Counts as New Material Authority

What counts as new material authority is set out in full in [Subcore 13-A §3.1 What Counts as New Material Authority](subcore_13.010_new_material_authority_system_reopening.md#31-what-counts-as-new-material-authority). Its guarantees are:

- **Both tests:** authority is new material authority only when it is material to others under [Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) and neither the Institutional Charter nor the current Institutional Certification Record covers it.
- **Counted together, pilots included:** reach that is material only when counted with Operating Parties under common control, or with others on the same shared system, counts; and a pilot that is itself material waits like the full authority.
- **No gate on forming or growing:** authority stated in the first Institutional Charter, growth within the scope its Institutional Certification Record already evaluated, and a new System going live on its own certification are not new material authority.
- **No splitting:** splitting or staging an authority into parts does not avoid the wait, and where it is unclear the Institution gives notice and the lead forum decides.

<a id="422-recertification-for-new-material-authority"></a>

##### 4.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority

An Institution that decides to take on new material authority must be recertified, and it must wait for the result before it exercises that authority. This is the wait that **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) states. The procedure is set out in full in [Subcore 13-A §3.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](subcore_13.010_new_material_authority_system_reopening.md#32-recertification-for-new-material-authority). Its guarantees are:

- **Notice opens it:** notice of the Institutional Charter amendment to the lead forum opens the recertification, or, for an Institution with no Institutional Certification Record, a first certification limited to the new authority.
- **Every class waits, nothing else stops:** no class is exempt and no emergency lifts the wait; what the Institution already does continues; and urgent protection of affected sentients never waits.
- **Presumed cleared:** the lead forum declines to clear only on a blocker in [§4.2.3 Blockers to Clearing New Material Authority](#423-blockers-to-clearing-new-material-authority) shown on evidence, and where certification conditions could contain the blocker, it clears with them.
- **A screen clears, it never refuses:** an automated screen may clear, but only a sentient reviewer in the lead forum may state a blocker; in Classes A, B, and C a sentient reviewer confirms every clearance.
- **The forum's delay is not the Institution's:** if no official response comes by the intake target, or a stated blocker is not decided inside the tier window, the authority is cleared for the time being with interim protection, and the overrun is a forum defect.
- **Published and open to challenge:** each clearance is published with the challenge path of [§5 Challenging a Certification](#5-challenging-a-certification) open, and an affected sentient may ask for a stay or interim protection.
- **Refusal is narrow:** not clearing is not by itself a denial or revocation of the status the Institution holds, and it may apply again.
- **Acting early is a finding:** exercising the authority without notice, or before it is cleared, is practice outside the Institutional Charter and reopens review.

<a id="423-blockers-to-clearing-new-material-authority"></a>

##### 4.2.3 Blockers to Clearing New Material Authority

The blockers are stated in full in [Subcore 13-A §3.3 Blockers to Clearing New Material Authority](subcore_13.010_new_material_authority_system_reopening.md#33-blockers-to-clearing-new-material-authority). Their guarantees are:

- **Five blockers only:** misaligned authority that no certification condition could contain, no accountability for the authority, unresolved misalignment with no readiness to correct it, concealment, and concentration of power.
- **Never a blocker:** the Institution's size, age, or wealth, or that it has no earlier Institutional Certification Record; competition or protection of an incumbent; the forum's cost or workload; or a good-faith dispute made through the published route.
- **Judgment stays with sentients:** a blocker that calls for judgment may be stated only by a sentient reviewer, on evidence stated on the Institutional Certification Record together with what would clear it.
- **A hearing first:** the Institution is told the blocker and the evidence and may answer inside the tier window, and the decision states its reasons.
- **Holds bind the holder:** an Interim Hold or standing lock bars the named sentient, not the Institution, so the authority can still be cleared for another holder.

<a id="43-provisional-and-full-certification"></a>

#### 4.3 Provisional and Full Certification

*In plain terms: an institution earns trust in steps, as a system does. A new institution, or one that has failed a review, starts on a short leash and is re-checked often. Only an institution with nothing wrong now, and nothing heading that way, graduates to full certification and a longer interval. Failing a review sends it back to the short leash.*

The status rules of [Chapter Ten §4.3 Provisional and Full Certification](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#43-provisional-and-full-certification) apply to an Institutional Certification Record, with the substitutions in [§4.1 Monitoring, Recertification, and Status](#41-monitoring-recertification-and-status). This subsection summarizes them and sets out what is specific to an Institution.

<a id="431-the-two-statuses"></a>

##### 4.3.1 The Two Statuses

[Chapter Ten §4.3.1 The Two Statuses](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#431-the-two-statuses) applies, reading Institution for System and institution class for system class. In short:

- **Provisional certification** is the status of an Institution's first certification, including a new Institution ([§2.3 Window for the First Certification](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#23-window-for-the-first-certification)), and of an Institution certified after a failed recertification or after a reopened review that found misalignment. It runs on a provisional interval, shorter than the full certification interval for the same institution class, with reliance limits stated on the Institutional Certification Record ([§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](#31-minimum-record-contents), *Outcome, status, and reliance limits*).
- **Full certification** is earned only at a recertification of an Institution holding provisional certification, and only where it has completed the required number of consecutive clean provisional recertifications (at least one, and more for higher institution classes, as the adopted document sets), no misalignment is current (the Institutional Certification Record shows no open misalignment, unremedied defect, or overdue check), and no monitoring indicator points to impending misalignment. It runs on the class-scaled certification interval, remains time-bound, and is not a permanent approval.
- **Clean:** a recertification is clean when it meets the last two of those requirements.

**Regression testing** in the no-current-misalignment requirement means the regression testing of the Systems the Institutional Certification Record cites, whose results each System Certification Record carries ([Chapter Ten §3.1 Minimum Record Contents](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#31-minimum-record-contents)). The Institution has no separate regression test of its own. A cited System's failed or missing regression testing is treated as the event in [§4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](#45-cascade-from-system-certifications), rule 1, and so is an indicator: it keeps the Institution from full certification and from a clean recertification. It is current misalignment of the Institution only where rule 2 of §4.5 Cascade from System certifications places the cause in the Institution.

<a id="432-failure-and-step-down"></a>

##### 4.3.2 Failure and Step-Down

[Chapter Ten §4.3.2 Failure and Step-Down](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#432-failure-and-step-down) applies, reading Institution for System. In short, what a recertification or a reopened review finds decides what happens to the Institution's status:

- **Current misalignment:** a recertification or a reopened review that finds it is a **failed** recertification. The consequences in [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) apply, as [§4 Certification Outcomes](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) states for an Institution. The next certification the Institution can earn is provisional certification, however clean the next check is, and its count of clean recertifications restarts at zero.
- **An indicator only:** a recertification that finds no current misalignment but shows an indicator of impending misalignment is not a failure and is not clean. An Institution holding provisional certification stays provisional and its count does not advance. One holding full certification steps down to provisional certification.

Specific to an Institution, a failed, withdrawn, or expired System certification that the Institution cites is not a recertification of the Institution, and it does not fail the Institution by itself. [§4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](#45-cascade-from-system-certifications) sets its effect in two steps:

- It is always an indicator of impending misalignment of the Institution, with the effect in the bullet above.
- It counts as a failure of the Institution itself (current misalignment, so a failed recertification) only if the review of the System found that the Institution caused the problem: through how it is governed, the incentives it sets, how it resources the System, its Institutional Charter, or the portfolio of Systems it runs. If the problem came from somewhere else, the Institution is not failed on that account, and only the warning sign in the first step applies.

<a id="433-cause-of-misalignment"></a>

##### 4.3.3 Cause of Misalignment

[Chapter Ten §4.3.3 Cause of Misalignment](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#433-cause-of-misalignment) applies, reading Institution for System. In short:

- **The cause:** where an evaluation, a recertification, or a reopened review finds misalignment of the Institution, the Institutional Certification Record states its cause as **design-caused** (built so that it produces misaligned results even when run as intended), **operating-caused** (sound on its face, but the way the Institution is run, resourced, or maintained makes its results misaligned), or **not yet determined** (the remedies for both stay open until the forum finds the cause).
- **The limits:** an Institution that keeps producing misaligned results is misaligned, even if its design looks sound on paper. A design that would produce misaligned results is misalignment before any such result appears. The cause finding sets the remedy sought and the scope of any reopened review, and never decides whether the Institution is misaligned.
- **If design-caused:** the count of clean recertifications stays at zero until the redesign is in place and has been verified. A component evaluation made before then does not count toward the clean total, however clean it is, and a change to how the Institution is run does not meet this step. This adds to the step-down rules in [§4.3.2 Failure and Step-Down](#432-failure-and-step-down) and does not replace them.

Specific to an Institution:

- **Who verifies:** the redesign is verified by the forum that found the cause, or by the next forum assigned under [§3.2 Forum Roles and Sequence](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-forum-roles-and-sequence), inside the matching intake target; it may be verified at the first provisional recertification. A component evaluation is one component's part of a certification pass, as [Chapter Nine §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) defines it.
- **Review of the cause:** the Institution or any affected sentient may ask the forum to review the cause finding, and if the cause is changed, these rules stop applying.

<a id="434-tier-windows"></a>

##### 4.3.4 Tier Windows

[Chapter Ten §4.3.4 Tier Windows](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#434-tier-windows) applies. A matter in which an initial evaluation, a recertification, or a reopened review finds misalignment of the Institution is presumptively placed in the materiality tier that matches the Institution's class (a misaligned Class A Institution is presumptively Tier A), and its intake, evidence preservation, interim protection, and decision run on that tier's windows and outer bounds under [Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline). An overrun is reviewed as that section requires.

<a id="435-class-a-reliance"></a>

##### 4.3.5 Class A Reliance

[Chapter Ten §4.3.5 Class A Reliance](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#435-class-a-reliance) applies, reading Institution for System. Provisional certification means a shorter certification interval and reliance limits stated on the Institutional Certification Record ([§4.3.1 The Two Statuses](#431-the-two-statuses); [§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](#31-minimum-record-contents)), and by itself it does not require interrupting an Institution whose interruption would cut off survival essentials before substitutes arrive. Interim protection and certification conditions under [Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#4-certification-outcomes) handle that case. The same holds while the count is held after a finding of design-caused misalignment: the hold never by itself interrupts an Institution that sentients depend on, and for as long as it lasts the forum states the interim protection, the certification conditions on reliance, and the notice to dependents.

Specific to an Institution, provisional certification never bars an Institution from operating ([§2.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#22-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)).

<a id="436-verification-not-assertion"></a>

##### 4.3.6 Verification, Not Assertion

[Chapter Ten §4.3.6 Verification, Not Assertion](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#436-verification-not-assertion) applies, reading Institution for System. An Institution's status, its count of clean recertifications, and its monitoring indicators must be verified, and the verification must be open to independent review. The Institution's say-so is not enough. Indicators may not be chosen or set so that misalignment could never show, and a redesign is verified the same way, on evidence that others can check. Full certification does not turn off any reason to reopen review: every trigger in [§4.2 Reopening Triggers](#42-reopening-triggers) still applies, and evasion is treated under [§4.4 Non-Evasion](#44-non-evasion).

<a id="44-non-evasion"></a>

#### 4.4 Non-Evasion

*In plain terms: no one gets out of this Chapter by dividing, renaming, or reshaping an institution.*

Evasion of this Chapter is governed by Subcore 3-B ([Subcore 3-B §3 Definition Integrity and Anti-Evasion Constraints](subcore_03.020_definition_integrity.md#3-definition-integrity-and-anti-evasion-constraints), including scope-and-boundary evasion under [§4.1 Common Evasion Patterns](subcore_03.020_definition_integrity.md#41-common-evasion-patterns)) and by [Chapter Ten §4.4 Non-Evasion](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#44-non-evasion). Without limiting those, these are evasions of this Chapter:

- dividing an Institution, its portfolio, or its authority so that no part reaches the class that requires certification;
- moving a System, an office, or a System Certification Record or Institutional Certification Record between Operating Parties under common control to avoid a finding or a trigger;
- placing the record seat or the contest seat, or the independent route, inside the Institution's own control line;
- placing a nominee or proxy in a covered office, or hiding who controls one, so that the formation check or the office-holder item passes, including seating through a nominee a sentient who is under an Institutional Authority Standing Lock or an Interim Hold;
- choosing or paying a reviewer through a channel the Institution controls; and
- using this Chapter, in either direction, as a licence or a shield: as a threshold that excludes a founder or protects an incumbent, or as a certificate offered in place of a System's own certification.

<a id="45-cascade-from-system-certifications"></a>

#### 4.5 Cascade from System Certifications

*In plain terms: a failed system certification is a warning light for the institution, and sometimes an alarm. It is an alarm only if the institution's own governance caused it.*

When a System Certification Record the Institution cites **fails** (a recertification, reopened review, or fresh review finds current misalignment), is **withdrawn**, or **expires**, the effect on the Institution is as follows.

1. **Indicator, always:** The event is an indicator of impending misalignment of the Institution. An Institution holding provisional certification stays provisional and its count of clean recertifications does not advance. An Institution holding full certification steps down to provisional certification. The Institution is not otherwise penalized for the event alone.
2. **Current misalignment, where the cause is institutional:** If the cause finding for the System ([Chapter Ten §4.3.3 Cause of Misalignment](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#433-cause-of-misalignment)) traces the misalignment to the Institution's own governance, incentives, resourcing, Institutional Charter, or portfolio, the cause is an **institutional cause**. The Institution then has current misalignment, and the certification pass is a failed one with the consequences of Chapter Nine §4 Certification Outcomes. The lead forum cites the System's cause finding. It does not make a new one about the System, and it decides only whether the cause lies in the Institution.
3. **Expiry without a reason:** A cited certification that has expired, with no recorded reason and no Adoption Transition window running, is an overdue check of the Institution under [§4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation) (item 6), and counts as an indicator under rule 1.
4. **No re-judging the System:** The cascade from System certifications runs one way. It never changes the System's outcome, status, certification conditions, or clocks, and a finding that the cause is not institutional leaves the Institution's status as rule 1 sets it. The reopening in [§4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems](#46-reopening-the-institutions-systems) changes none of those either. It only opens the System's own review.
5. **Record:** The Institutional Certification Record states the cited event, the System's cause finding by link, and the lead forum's finding on where the cause lies.

<a id="46-reopening-the-institutions-systems"></a>

#### 4.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems

*In plain terms: when an institution is found to have governed badly in a way that matters, each of its systems is reopened for a fresh look as soon as notice is given, unless that system's own review is already under way. That is a fresh look and not an outcome. Each system keeps its status until its own review decides.*

When an Institution has a **governance violation**, review of each System in its portfolio reopens. What counts as one, and how each reopening runs, are set out in full in [Subcore 13-A §4 Reopening the Institution's Systems](subcore_13.010_new_material_authority_system_reopening.md#4-reopening-the-institutions-systems). Its guarantees are:

- **What reopens the Systems:** a material determination that the Institution's own governance failed, a denial or revocation that rests on such a failure or on the conduct of its governing authority, or an institutional cause found under rule 2 of [§4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](#45-cascade-from-system-certifications).
- **Material only, and once:** only a material determination reopens, and each determination reopens each System once.
- **Notice at once:** the Institution's governing authority gives notice at once to the lead forum of each System's certification pass; if it does not, the forum that made the determination gives it inside the matching intake target, and the failure is a defect of accountable authority.
- **A review, not an outcome:** reopening changes no System's outcome, status, or certification condition; neither the Institution nor any System is stopped; and urgent protection of affected sentients does not wait.
- **Written down:** the Institutional Certification Record names each System notified and the date of the notice.

### 5. Challenging a Certification

*In plain terms: an Institutional Certification Record only works if sentients can push back when it is wrong. A challenge has to be real: able to reopen review, pause reliance, and reach the forum with authority over the point in dispute. A complaints box that changes nothing does not count.*

This section is **Phase VI, Operate** for an Institution. It covers how affected parties challenge an Institutional Certification Record. [Chapter Ten §5 Challenging a Certification](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-challenging-a-certification), with its [§5.1 When the Path Opens](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#51-when-the-path-opens) and [§5.2 Contestability Paths](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths), applies to the Institutional Certification Record, reading Institution for system and the Institution's governing authority for operator. This section keeps what is specific to an Institution and summarizes the rest.

In short, as Chapter Ten §5 Challenging a Certification states it:

- **Real ways to challenge:** the Institutional Certification Record must state the contestability paths of [§5.2 Contestability Paths](#52-contestability-paths), enough to contest its content, scope, classification assumptions, evidence reliance, supervisory integrity, outcome, and continued reliance. The paths must work and be easy to use, and not be ceremonial.
- **What a challenge can do:** it must be able to reopen review, [stay](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#stay) or limit reliance where material error or integrity risk is credibly shown, and route to the forum family with lawful jurisdiction over the challenged point.
- **Scale:** these duties scale with institution class and material impact, and they do not go away because of how the Institution or its activity is labeled, where material impact is present.

<a id="51-when-the-path-opens"></a>

#### 5.1 When the Path Opens

*In plain terms: the challenge path has to be open before anyone is asked to rely on a certification. For an Institution that already has stakeholders, it is already open, or opens when the Institutional Certification Record opens. For an Institution with no stakeholders yet, it opens with the first outcome.*

[Chapter Ten §5.1 When the Path Opens](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#51-when-the-path-opens) applies, reading Institution for system. When the path opens, and how long it stays open:

- **An Institution that already has stakeholders:** its path is already open, or it opens when its Institutional Certification Record opens. The rule reaches an Institution in an Adoption Transition window under [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#xxviii-a-existing-instantiations-adoption-transition-window) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), and one in a [later certification pass](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#certification-pass).
- **An Institution with no stakeholders yet:** the published record challenge path opens when its first certification outcome is published, whatever the outcome. That lets a challenge be filed, and reliance stayed, from the start. Until then the Institutional Certification Record still names the path ([§3.2 Forum Roles and Sequence](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-forum-roles-and-sequence)), and the other paths in [§5.2 Contestability Paths](#52-contestability-paths) are available throughout review.

These two bullets replace the bullets of Chapter Ten §5.1 When the path opens that depend on a System's go-live. The rest is as Chapter Ten §5.1 When the path opens states it. Stakeholders are the sentients the Institution materially affects or who materially rely on it, as found in evaluation and not as the Institution identifies them. The path stays open while the Institution has stakeholders, and an Institution that gains stakeholders after certification reopens review under [§4 Recertification and Reopening](#4-recertification-and-reopening). A claim that an Institution is aligned is unsupported while it has stakeholders but no open path for challenging the Institutional Certification Record.

<a id="52-contestability-paths"></a>

#### 5.2 Contestability Paths

*In plain terms: an Institutional Certification Record has to say where to push back. There is one ordinary first step, then routes to the forum that owns a disputed finding, to a backup when the lead forum itself is in question, and to escalation. Where waiting would do harm, a challenger can go straight to a forum.*

An Institutional Certification Record must name the four challenge paths of [Chapter Ten §5.2 Contestability Paths](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-contestability-paths), so anyone affected knows where to take a dispute. The paths apply Chapter Twenty-Two's [Dispute sequencing](core_22.000_forum.md#dispute-sequencing) to Institutional Certification Records. They are routes into the forums, not a new forum, and Chapter Twenty-Two still decides which forum has authority. The order they run in is in [Chapter Nine §3.3 Contestability Chain](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-contestability-chain). In short:

- **Published record challenge path** — the ordinary first step for an ordinary dispute about the Institutional Certification Record. The Institutional Certification Record names the contest seat ([§3.1 Four Seats in an Institutional Certification](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-four-seats-in-an-institutional-certification)), which sits outside the Institution's own control line, with who receives the challenge, how to file it, and that windows under [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) run from receipt. An internal review by the Institution, or its own report or attestation, is not this path.
- **Component forum path** — a challenge to one finding goes to the Chapter Twenty-Two forum family that has authority over it.
- **Lead-integrity path** — a challenge to how the lead forum ran the process, such as capture, hidden information, reviewing its own work, or calling certification finished too soon, goes through Integrity routing, with a backup forum so the lead forum does not judge itself.
- **Escalation path** — a dispute that needs to move to a different or a higher forum under Chapter Twenty-Two goes by transfer, a question for ruling, backup routing, or escalation between forum families. It applies where the main stakes lie in another forum family, constitutional validity is in question, a structural fix is needed, two families are deadlocked, or a forum would be judging itself.

Direct access to a forum path remains available when delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration, and unfinished use of the published record challenge path may not stall it or use up [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) windows.

Two points are specific to an Institution:

- A challenge to a System's own certification goes to that System's record. It is not heard in the Institution's certification pass.
- A challenge to the Institution's class goes by the Challenge route in [Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality). Challenges on related points are heard together as one matter.

*In plain terms: certification can feed standing, but it is not standing. An Institutional Certification Record, good or bad and whatever the outcome, may supply verified facts to Chapter Fourteen, but only through a strict gate. Chapters Fourteen and Nineteen alone decide Standing Records and their effects.*

This section is **Phase VI, Operate** for an Institution. It is the standing bridge. [Chapter Ten §6 Relationship to Standing](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing) applies to an Institutional Certification Record, with the Institution as the actor to whom facts are attributed. This section keeps what is specific to an Institution and summarizes the rest.

<a id="61-what-may-pass-to-standing"></a>

#### 6.1 What May Pass to Standing

Only the [Institutional Certification Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#institutional-certification-record) can pass facts on to standing, and which side they land on follows the [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims). Nothing counts until it clears Chapter Fourteen's verified-input gate ([Chapter Fourteen §4.5 Verified-Input Gate](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#45-verified-input-gate)), as [§6.3 Verified-Input Gate](#63-verified-input-gate) below sets out:

- An Institutional Certification Record showing that the Institution's own conduct really lines up with the Rights Floor can supply verified **Flourishing**-side input, for a **contribution Standing Record** ([§6.4 Contribution Standing Record](#64-contribution-standing-record)).
- An Institutional Certification Record documenting that the Institution was misclassified, hid defects, or operated in ways that defeat the Rights Floor can supply verified **Accountability**-side input, for a **violation Standing Record** ([§6.5 Violation Standing Record](#65-violation-standing-record)).

This bridge covers only what the Institutional Certification Record can pass on. Any other verified input about an Institution, such as a standing finding made in a forum case, reaches standing by its own route under Chapter Fourteen. Chapter Fourteen decides which other inputs it accepts, and this bridge neither adds to them nor limits them.

**What the bridge carries, and what it does not:** it carries facts from the Institutional Certification Record and nothing else. An operation that passes any other information on to the standing process, whether the Institution runs it directly or through a System, is not covered by this bridge or by the Institutional Certification Record. It is its own [System](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#system), because a System is identified by what it actually does and not by the label or the certification next to it, and it is evaluated, classified, and certified as a System on its own, as [Chapter Ten §6.1 What a System Certification Record Can Pass on to Standing](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#61-what-a-system-certification-record-can-pass-on-to-standing) states. What it passes on still has to clear the verified-input gate in [§6.3 Verified-Input Gate](#63-verified-input-gate).

<a id="62-every-certification-outcome"></a>

#### 6.2 Every Certification Outcome

[Chapter Ten §6.2 Every Certification Outcome](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#62-every-certification-outcome) applies to the Institutional Certification Record, with the Institution as the actor. In short:

- **Every outcome counts:** certification (provisional or full, with or without certification conditions), conditional or deferred certification, denial, withdrawal, expiration, or any other result supplies standing input, including for an Institution in its first certification and one that has never held certification, on the same terms. Loss of certification does not exclude verified facts that arose while the Institution held it. All such inputs remain subject to the verified-input gate below.
- **Not yet aligned is not a violation:** a certification finding that an Institution is not yet aligned is not a violation by itself, because a certification pass is where problems are meant to be found and fixed. Where the Institution makes consistent, honest efforts and is not negligent, a violation finding is unlikely. Standing input needs facts that support an adverse classification and attribution to the Institution as the actor ([§6.5 Violation Standing Record](#65-violation-standing-record)), and a readiness finding that the Institution was willing but not able to correct a problem supplies none ([Chapter Nine §4.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#45-effects-of-denial-and-revocation)). None of this covers negligence, concealment, or false evidence.

<a id="63-verified-input-gate"></a>

#### 6.3 Verified-Input Gate

An Institutional Certification Record may feed Chapter Fourteen standing only when it is bounded, traceable, contestable (through the challenge paths in [§5 Challenging a Certification](#5-challenging-a-certification)), and verified under all of these:

- the institution class evaluation ([§3 Institution Class Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-institution-class-evaluation)), which settles what institution class the Institution is in, the whole-Institution evaluation ([§4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation)), and the data types and handling evaluation ([Chapter Eight §4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation)), which settles what data the Institution handles and how;
- every evaluation in [§5 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated) and [§6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) that applies to the Institution's own conduct, meaning each one whose trigger is met;
- the Institutional Certification Record's own transparency and auditability rules ([§3.2 Transparency and Auditability of the Institutional Certification Record](#32-transparency-and-auditability-of-the-institutional-certification-record)) and the forum review rules ([§3 Forum Process](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#3-forum-process)); and
- Subcores 3-A to 3-C, and whichever Chapter Twenty-Two process applies.

<a id="64-contribution-standing-record"></a>

#### 6.4 Contribution Standing Record

On the terms of [Chapter Ten §6.4 Contribution Standing Record](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#64-contribution-standing-record), positive certification, validation, recertification, remediation completion, or fulfillment of or release from a certification condition may supply verified input for a **contribution Standing Record** where it demonstrates constitutional benefit, competent stewardship, safe delegation readiness, system optimization, diligence, or constitutional alignment.

<a id="65-violation-standing-record"></a>

#### 6.5 Violation Standing Record

These items, in the Institutional Certification Record, may supply verified input for a **violation Standing Record** where the facts support adverse classification and attribution to the Institution as the actor:

- withdrawal of certification;
- concealed misclassification;
- failed recertification;
- failed or missing regression testing of a cited System on recertification, where required and where [§4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](#45-cascade-from-system-certifications), rule 2, places the cause in the Institution;
- fraudulent certification evidence, including a formation check statement that hides who controls a covered office;
- material violation of a certification condition;
- verified operation outside certified scope;
- infrastructure fragility inappropriate for the institution class; and
- a material defect found under an evaluation that was required of the Institution's own conduct under [§4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), [§5 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated), or [§6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations), including each kind of defect that [Chapter Ten §6.5 Violation Standing Record](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#65-violation-standing-record) lists: data handling, risk evaluation or risk disclosure where systemic risk was in scope, ecological-footprint misrepresentation where disclosure was required, nondiscrimination or proxy discrimination, accessibility or substantive participation, educational capability, and trustworthiness.
