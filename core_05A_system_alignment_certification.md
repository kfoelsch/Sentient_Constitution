## CHAPTER 5A: SYSTEM ALIGNMENT CERTIFICATION AND RECOGNITION

*Non-operative subtitle:* Forum-supervised certification records before standing.

### 1. Purpose and Role

*In plain terms: Chapter 5A is the certification bridge. Chapter Five defines the vocabulary. Chapter 5A turns system evidence into forum-supervised, contestable alignment records. Chapter Six may then use those records as verified inputs for standing, but only through Chapter Six's standing-record gate. A certification is not a reputation score, permanent permission slip, or shortcut around rights review. Instead, it is formal documentation that, to the best of everyone's knowledge, the system is currently aligned with constitutional outcomes. System alignment certifications are never permanent. They are required to be renewed on a frequency based on their system class, with Class A systems requiring the most frequent recertification.*

This chapter states the constitutional status of **system alignment certification and recognition records**. It does not replace Chapter Five definitions, Chapter Six standing classification, Chapter Seven standing effects, Chapter Nine forum supervision, or incorporated system and forum implementation duties.

System alignment certification exists to determine whether a system, within a stated scope and review cadence, has demonstrated constitutional alignment sufficient for recognition, conditional recognition, validation, revalidation, continued reliance, deployment, or material release from conditions.

### 2. Certification Record

A **System Alignment Certification Record** is a bounded, reviewable record concerning a particular system, version, operator or steward, scope, time window, material-impact profile, and decision context. It must identify, at minimum:

- **System identity, scope, and status:** the system, version, operator or steward, scope, affected communities, dependencies, and review status;
- **Classification and material-impact assumptions:** the system classification and material-impact assumptions used;
- **Technical evidence and assurance:** the technical standards, tests, measurements, uncertainty, safety margins, failure modes, and evidence basis materially relied on;
- **Stakeholder, environmental, and integrity review:**
  - stakeholder, Rights-Floor, accessibility, contestability, and dependency considerations;
  - ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, and environmental-alignment component review where material;
  - capture, self-review, incentive-alignment, evidence-control, and contest-pathway risks;
- **Forum supervision:** the forum family or authority issuing, adopting, referring, certifying, staying, or reviewing each component;
- **Certification outcome, contestability, and reliance limits:**
  - the certification outcome, including recognition, conditional recognition, deferred recognition, non-recognition, withdrawal, expiration, revalidation, or release from conditions; and
  - the challenge path, revalidation cadence, reopening triggers, and any limits on reliance.

The record must be attributable, auditable, and contestable under Chapters Two through Four, Chapter Nine, and any applicable Chapter Ten rights.

### 3. Forum Supervision and Component Roles

Official constitutional alignment recognition, validation, revalidation, withdrawal, and non-recognition for materially impactful systems require forum-supervised process under Chapter Nine.

Technical forums maintain and apply technical specifications, measurement methods, testing protocols, expert-evidence standards, uncertainty-reduction methods, and technical adequacy findings within their lawful scope. Those technical outputs may be component findings for a certification record.

Integrity forums are the default lead for official constitutional alignment recognition and ongoing alignment validation unless Chapter Nine assigns a different primary stake. Integrity forums must use technical-forum standards where materially applicable and must refer, certify, or stay component questions assigned to other forum families.

Environment forums hold the environmental-alignment component role where material ecological exposure, environmental-precondition dependency, lifecycle burden, restoration duty, or reasonably foreseeable ecological risk is present.

Sentient, Institutional, Constitutional, and other regular forum families retain their assigned component authority for rights, stakeholder, institutional, sentience-status, constitutional-validity, structural-remedy, or other primary-stakes questions. A certification process must not let technical expertise or Integrity coordination displace those assigned merits roles.

### 4. Relationship to Standing

A certification record may feed Chapter Six standing only when it is bounded, traceable, contestable, and verified under this chapter, Chapters Two through Four, and the applicable Chapter Nine process.

Positive certification, recognition, validation, revalidation, remediation completion, or condition release may supply verified input for a **contribution standing record** where it demonstrates constitutional benefit, competent stewardship, safe delegation readiness, system optimization, diligence, or constitutional alignment.

Non-recognition, withdrawal, concealed misclassification, failed revalidation, fraudulent certification evidence, material condition violation, or verified operation outside recognized scope may supply verified input for a **violation standing record** where the facts support adverse classification and actor-specific attribution.

Certification records do not themselves apply standing effects. Chapter Six controls standing records and axis-pure classification; Chapter Seven controls standing effects, competency gates, standing locks, restoration, and integration. No certification record may merge contribution and violation into a single score, convert technical adequacy into final constitutional alignment by itself, or treat recognition as permanent authorization.

### 5. Reopening, Drift, and Non-Evasion

Recognition is scope-bound and time-bound. Material change, drift, concealed behavior, dependency growth, new risk, incident history, credible challenge, ecological objection, stakeholder evidence, or classification drift reopens review under Chapter Nine and incorporated system implementation duties.

System owners, operators, stewards, institutions, and forums must not fragment a system, relabel its scope, suppress evidence, shift work across entities, or claim experimental, personal, isolated, or technical-only status to evade certification, revalidation, contestability, or Chapter Six standing consequences where material impact is present.
