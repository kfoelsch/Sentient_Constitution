# Floor term inventory (2026-10-22)

> **Status after the Hundred and seventh move (2026-10-22).** The first version of this inventory counted 2,161 mentions, with 286 in Group C. The Group C renames were then applied (see below), and this file was regenerated from the corpus afterwards, so the counts here are the current ones. Group C now holds only the phrases deliberately kept or not yet decided.

**What the Hundred and seventh move changed (about 215 replacements in 53 files, plus matching anchors):**
- Time: "Stage targets and timing floors" (CF-13.445 §3.1) became "Stage targets and outer bounds" (anchor `#cf-13445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds`); "adopter floor" became "adopters may only shorten them"; "Tier B floor" became "Tier B intake target"; "Tier A/B urgency floors" became "urgency levels"; "Numeric tier floors" became "Numeric tier targets"; "cadence floors" became "certification intervals"; "intake timing floors" became "intake targets"; "notification urgency floor" became "notification urgency levels".
- Joint-structure headings (CJS-5.105 to 5.135 and their CI/CF/CS cross-cites): "X floor" became "X minimum" for about 35 phrases (cluster, compliance, auditability, class-scaled template, external assurance trigger, dissolution-review trigger and the rest), with the `<a id>` anchors and link fragments updated.
- Process titles: Composition minimum (CJS-18.110 §1.1), Lived-condition minimums (CS-6.320 §11), Remedy-parity funding minimum (Chapter Eleven §9.2), Funding minimum and capacity minimum (CI-11.205), clean-recertification minimum, classification minimum, Binding minimums (Minimum Viable Adopter).
- Chapter Fourteen and CI-14.205 §1: integrity, verifiability, incentive-integrity, no-single-point-of-failure and competency floors became "requirement".
- Chapter Nine Part D: "portfolio floor" became "portfolio rule".

**Kept on purpose (Rights Floor family or Chapter Fifteen):** political-equality, durable political-voice, constitutional choice equality and Stakeholder System Participation floors; the public floor and Type O floor; destructive-disposition, imprisonment and anti-discrimination floors under Article XX-B; substantive non-regression and Chapter Fifteen floors; the recovery, containment and redress floors in Articles XIII-A and XIII-F; essential-access and creation floors; default-inclusion; the operating-environment floor; "thin public floor", "organize floor" and "business-creation floor" in the easy-entry guides.

**Still undecided:** the Chapter One §11 "Market Structure floor" (3 mentions), the "governing floor" wording in CJS-5.130, and the 1,116 bare "the floor" uses, which each need a look in context.

Purpose: list every use of "floor" that is not the **Rights Floor** itself, so the custodian can decide which stay and which are renamed. Scope: live corpus text (core, corpus_*, guides, implementation, README and similar); excludes translations, evidence, archive, generated files, evaluation runs, `project/` and `doc_architecture.md`. Counts are mentions, grouped by the words before "floor"; "Rights Floor" mentions are not counted.

**Totals:** 1,866 mentions of other "floor" phrases in 188 distinct phrases. 761 mentions in 143 phrases fall in the Rights Floor family (Group A: named in the list, or found only in Chapters Zero, One, Five and Six). 1036 are bare "the floor", "this floor" or "that floor" (last section). The other 69 mentions, in 44 phrases, are Group C: kept on purpose or undecided (see the status note at the top).
