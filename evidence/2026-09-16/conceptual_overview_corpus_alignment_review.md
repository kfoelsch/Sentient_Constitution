# Conceptual overview and corpus alignment review

**Review date:** 2026-09-16. **Edition:** SC-Corpus-2026.08.09, effective 2026-08-09, pre-release. **Status:** review evidence, not constitutional interpretation with binding effect, adoption, or certification.

**Follow-up:** Finding 1 has been corrected in the working tree; see the [segregation resolution](contradiction_01_segregation_resolution.md). The findings and source snapshot below record the original review state.

**2026-09-17 follow-up:** Finding 2 has also been corrected; see the [chapter and article reference resolution](../2026-09-17/contradiction_02_reference_resolution.md).

## Judgment

The [conceptual overview](../../guides/CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md) is substantially faithful to the Constitution's organizing design. Its distinctions between aims and constraints, contribution and violation, measurement and consequences, participation and authorization, and constitutional source and incorporated implementation are supported by the sources examined.

**I would not certify complete alignment of the current corpus.** There is a direct conflict over segregation of duties, several semantically wrong cross-references, and stale edition statements. The overview also gives an incomplete account of the political and liberty restrictions the instrument permits. These are different problems and require different corrections.

As a judgment about desirable conditions for sentient life, I strongly endorse much of the protected world described here: survival without having to earn moral approval, protection of private thought, meaningful dissent, substrate-neutral dignity, ecological continuity, and a real route back after wrongdoing. My endorsement of the enforcement design is conditional. Its success depends on making institutional power easier to challenge than to abuse, and making restoration as effective as exclusion.

## Scope and method

I read the conceptual overview first, then traced its claims through the numbered core, relevant canonical definitions, and implementation companions. I examined provisions across Chapters One through Sixteen, with particular attention to rights collisions, standing, anti-constitutional misconduct, political participation, forum independence, remedy capacity, adoption, and small-community implementation. The review followed the repository's corpus-lookup discipline: source text controls; indexes and reader maps do not supply duties.

This is a **source-grounded thematic alignment review**, not an exhaustive line-by-line audit of every definition, companion provision, translation, historical edition, or implementation artifact. The distinction matters: the defects below are established findings; the absence of another finding is not a guarantee that the remaining corpus is conflict-free. Practical enforceability, measurement reliability, and the behavior of actual institutions have not been demonstrated by this reading.

The reviewed material was the working tree, which already contained changes. The associated [source snapshot](conceptual_overview_review_source_snapshot.json) records hashes and mechanical checks for the cited files. Source locations and findings should be rechecked after subsequent edits. Constitutional and overview text were not changed by this review.

## Established textual findings

### 1. High priority: the small-scope verification exception contradicts the segregation floor

The overview says checking may never be combined with entering the same record or hearing its challenge. That accurately reflects [Chapter One §10.2](../../core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#102-segregation-of-duties), which permits some safeguarded combinations but expressly excludes “verify-and-record” and “verify-and-review” on the same act.

[Chapter Eight §3.7](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#37-segregation-of-duties) then says both:

- “no office ever both verifies and enters” the same record (line 425 at review); and
- for informal and small-scope records, “verify and enter-and-hold may sit in that one office under a disclosed safeguard” (line 437).

The small-community example repeats the combination at line 707. [CI-22](../../corpus_institutions/ci_22_commons_cooperatives_mutual_aid_non_market_governance.md), line 64, likewise permits the custodian to be the verifying office. Conversely, [CI-3.2](../../corpus_institutions/ci_03_institutional_design_separation_of_powers.md#ci-32-functional-separation-lanes) and [CI-4.6](../../corpus_institutions/ci_04_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-46-seat-catalog) preserve the prohibition.

There is also inconsistent test language in [CJS-3.11, Constitutional lane and functional separation](../../corpus_joint_structure/cjs_03a_accountability_operations.md#constitutional-lane-and-functional-separation): its scope prohibits these pairings, but its failure condition describes the pairing **without** a published safeguard as a failure. That wording invites the forbidden exception.

**Practical consequence:** a small mutual-aid group can follow an express permission and still violate the principle it is told it must satisfy. Reviewers can reach opposite answers from the same section.

**Correction direction:** reconcile the specific exception, example, local role map, and failure test with the controlling floor. Preserve an accessible independent-verification route for informal groups. If an actual relaxation is intended, that is a substantive constitutional design choice; it cannot be accomplished quietly in the companion or overview.

### 2. High priority: several references resolve to the wrong substantive subject

In [Chapter Fifteen](../../core_15_amendment_ratification.md), §11.1, line 307, the rule for participation, objections, and independent review of materially high-impact amendments points to **Article X-C, Adult consensual commercial sexual services and sexual exploitation**. That is not the general legitimacy owner for constitutional amendments. The same target is repeated in nearby reader guidance.

The §10.3 joining rule, line 276, sends participation weight to **Article X-B, Collective Harm Boundary and Enforcement Interface**. The [Chapter Six participation provisions](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md) place representation and legitimacy rules in Article XI. These references require an owner-based correction, not merely a successful anchor lookup.

Other confirmed drift:

- [Chapter Thirteen](../../core_13_non_regression.md), line 53, assigns expansion, supremacy, ratification, and procedural validity to **Chapters Twelve and Thirteen**. The current owners are Fourteen and Fifteen; the overview gets that division right.
- The [Preamble](../../core_00_preamble.md), lines 143 and 148, links its Chapter Three and Chapter Four owner entries to the Chapter Two file.
- The [Corpus definition](../../core_05_band_integrative.md#corpus), line 590, instructs readers to apply “Chapter Thirteen supremacy and Chapter Fourteen adoption conditions,” another stale ownership mapping.

**Practical consequence:** an agent or reader can follow a valid link into the wrong rule and mistake successful navigation for substantive verification. This matters particularly for amendment legitimacy.

**Correction direction:** audit chapter and article references against their current owners, including prose references and reader guidance. Do not rewrite the overview to reproduce stale source routing. These errors do not establish that the entire instrument is invalid; they establish that its routing is not yet dependable enough.

### 3. Moderate priority: the overview understates the burden for deadline extensions

The [overview's forum-clock passage](../../guides/CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#forums), line 695, says later-stage coordination can justify a published, tier-appropriate extension while preserving the outer-bound floor.

[Chapter Eleven §6](../../core_11_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), lines 852–859, makes extension beyond the default outer bound conditional on documented authorization under Article XXIII-D. [CF-11.3.1](../../corpus_forum/cf_11_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md), lines 220–224, expressly requires continuing necessity, proportionality, and absence of less-restrictive feasible alternatives. Its A/B complexity extension can use the next slower column while preserving the original tier's early protections.

**Correction direction:** describe the numbers as binding default outer bounds with that expressly authorized exception. Make clear that coordination complexity alone is not the complete authorization test. This prevents both an overstatement of absolute deadlines and an understatement of the burden for extending them.

## Material omissions in the overview

These omissions do not make every corresponding overview sentence false. They can, however, leave a reader with a materially incomplete picture of the world being proposed.

### Political equality and political exclusion belong in the same explanation

[Chapter Twelve §4.1](../../core_12_governance.md) gives each entitled sentient equal weight in Foundational Constitutional Choice. It distinguishes this from stake-weighted choice within already-authorized systems. It also permits ordinary governance-voting locks and, after final qualifying anti-constitutional designation, withholding of durable political voice, including foundational choice, until restitution requirements are satisfied.

The text contains significant protections: poverty, disability, exile, loss of substrate, and valuation uncertainty cannot alone justify withholding where the specified good-faith conditions hold; impossible full restitution has a route to partial or conditional restoration. These safeguards should be stated alongside the power to withhold voice.

The overview should also plainly disclose that [Chapter Nine §5.5](../../core_09_standing_integration.md) can exclude a designated actor from extensive trust-sensitive institutional pathways, and [Chapter Ten §4.2](../../core_10_a_misconduct_designation.md) can require imprisonment where the individualized necessity and lesser-means tests are met. Describing these only as “effects” or “restrictions” understates their significance.

### Freedom from a universal standing system is an affirmative protection

[Chapter Eight §2.1](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default) makes silence the default: most sentients should have no standing record, absence supports no adverse inference, and records are not general prerequisites for essentials or ordinary participation. Named trust-sensitive roles retain their own lawful qualification requirements.

[Chapter Nine §§7.1–8](../../core_09_standing_integration.md) adds separate protections against cross-pathway profiles, rankings, badges, general public LEQU labels, and indefinite active integration of restored lower-slot findings, subject to its specified exceptions. It also requires adopters to measure whether standing is experienced as a worth rank despite its formal label.

The overview's “no merged score” is accurate but too small an account of these safeguards. Equally, its explanation should acknowledge the real burdens and doors closed by lawful locks. Chapter Nine §7.2 itself requires this candor in material describing standing to affected sentients.

### Who receives protection precedes measurement

The overview correctly says LEQU does not decide sentience. A short route to [Article V-E](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-e-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) and the [Def.P1 cluster](../../core_05_band_participation.md#defp1) would make the positive answer easier to find: what protections apply during uncertainty, what triggers provisional inclusion, and who represents a claimant whose status is disputed. Those protections are particularly consequential for nonhuman and newly encountered forms of life.

### The residual hierarchy should be stated, not merely named

The [Authority Stack and Internal Hierarchy](../../core_05_band_integrative.md#authority-stack) distinguishes source status from residual conflict resolution. After integrated reading is exhausted, principles control genuine residual incompatibility with articles, and articles control definitions used as independent substantive glosses; canonical definitions still govern term meaning throughout. The overview should briefly disclose this ordering alongside the safeguards against using it as an ordinary bypass.

## Design questions that remain after textual repair

### Severe designation needs a demonstrably safe boundary

[Chapter Ten §3](../../core_10_a_misconduct_designation.md) permits designation when a qualifying unified incident satisfies one or more §2 criteria and the process safeguards. Criteria 3 and 4 concern rollback effects and causally significant degradation; neither expressly contains the willfulness requirement stated in criterion 1. Thus it would be inaccurate to reassure readers that designation always requires proven malicious intent.

There are real counterweights: verified magnitude, individualized attribution, heightened review, proportionality, contestability, specific pattern exclusions, and the narrow good-faith drafting carve-out. [Part B](../../core_10_b_misconduct_pattern_applications.md) also preserves good-faith enforcement discretion and legitimate safety or maintenance actions.

Nevertheless, the combination of broad effects-based criteria with loss of political voice and possible confinement needs adversarial case testing. A failed good-faith administrator, a dissident reformer, and an organized captor must not become interchangeable merely because each creates institutional disruption. The info-sphere flooding rule especially needs testing against mass protest, investigative reporting, and expensive but legitimate contestation; Article V-H protects these activities, and the practical boundary must survive hostile enforcement.

### LEQU calibration remains a consequential measurement problem

[Chapter Eight §§5.2 and 7](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md) requires an integrated estimate from several dimensions, with separate treatment of conduct character. The [calibration reference](../../implementation/LEQU_CALIBRATION_REFERENCE.md) supplies worked examples and explicitly lacks authority to decide live records. That is useful support, not demonstrated measurement reliability.

One methodological issue needs an explicit rationale: the reference calls 80 years a display translation, but then uses that denominator to assign digital pause/deprivation magnitudes in examples W5 and W6. The declared substrate-neutral unit and the operational use of a human time baseline need reconciliation or justification. This is a calibration question, not a finding that digital sentients are assigned lesser dignity by the constitutional source.

Before using small differences around a band threshold to support severe consequences, test independent evaluators on the same verified facts. Publish disagreement, uncertainty treatment, and sensitivity to duration, vulnerability, ecological effects, and identity-continuity assumptions. A reliable slot calculation cannot compensate for an unreliable estimate of its input.

### Institutional capacity determines whether the rights are usable

The corpus recognizes this problem unusually directly. [Chapter Nine §§4.4 and 9.2](../../core_09_standing_integration.md) links ordinary locks to opened remedies, publishes lock/remedy performance, creates a backlog tripwire, and requires remedy-capacity funding parity with an explained, performance-tested departure route. [CI-27](../../corpus_institutions/ci_27_remedy_systems_institutional_redress_capacity.md) assigns implementation responsibility. [CS-6](../../corpus_systems/cs_06_comprehensibility_complexity_stewardship.md) requires complexity stewardship and periodic simplification.

These are substantive safeguards, not missing ideas. The unresolved issue is delivery. The tripwire has important exceptions, including special locks and top-slot findings. Those facing the greatest restrictions remain especially dependent on independent review functioning under load.

A viable implementation must let an exhausted, poor, disabled, unfamiliar, or unpopular claimant obtain protection without mastering the classification system. It must also give small adopters a feasible way to satisfy independence. The contradiction in finding 1 shows that proportional accessibility and strict separation have not yet been reconciled everywhere.

### Revisability depends on independent judgments of regression

[Article XXV](../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxv-constitutional-evolution-and-non-entrenchment) requires revalidation and non-entrenchment, while [Chapter Thirteen](../../core_13_non_regression.md) forbids substantive regression. These are compatible commitments: mechanisms can change while protected floors remain.

The danger is interpretive capture. An incumbent could characterize a challenge to its preferred mechanism as weakening the protections that mechanism supposedly serves. A convincing implementation must demonstrate that opponents can replace failed institutions through protected channels without agreement from the office whose power is at issue. More cross-references alone will not establish that independence.

## The world I would endorse

The strongest features concern ordinary existence:

- [Survival rights](../../core_06_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-a-survival) protect food, shelter, energy, processing continuity, and a stable operating environment across substrates.
- [Internal-state protections](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-b-internal-state-boundary-and-type-n-protection) reach reconstruction by inference and aggregation, not merely access to an explicitly labeled thought record.
- [Expression, assembly, and press protections](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-h-expression-assembly-and-press) cover dissent and recognize indirect chilling through surveillance and access control.
- [Article XXIII](../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxiii-conflict-resolution-escalation-and-emergency-proportionality) prohibits deprivation of life as a justice measure and constrains the purpose, duration, and conditions of restrictions.
- [Community and associational protections](../../corpus_joint_structure/cjs_03p_participation_operations.md) and [CI-21](../../corpus_institutions/ci_21_community_life_voluntary_association_non_instrumental_time.md) protect play, solitude, voluntary association, and time that produces nothing measurable.
- [Continuity](../../core_05_apex_continuity_aim.md) and the [natural-world rights provisions](../../core_06_rights_part_a.md) make ecological and future conditions part of the constitutional concern.

These commitments make the document morally attractive. They protect beings as subjects of their own lives, including when they are dependent, inconvenient, or incapable of producing a prestigious contribution.

The condition I would put on endorsement is practical: **a sentient must be able to live an ordinary, private, imperfect life, disagree with the institutions, and recover from wrongdoing without being permanently administered as a risk profile.** The text already contains many of the protections needed for that world. Its classification and enforcement machinery must prove that it preserves them when a case is unpopular, the budget is inadequate, or an official is wrong.

I would be more confident after seeing the following exercises succeed. These are proposed validation cases, not tests already run:

1. A person with no standing record obtains essentials and ordinary community access without creating one.
2. A small mutual-aid group receives recognition through an affordable, unambiguously lawful independent process.
3. A critic challenges the constitutionality of a major institution while that institution controls relevant funding and records.
4. A poor person completes every feasible restoration duty and regains the political voice the governing rule requires.
5. A potentially sentient hosted entity obtains representation and interim protection before its status dispute is settled.
6. Independent evaluators disagree about a magnitude near the serious-misconduct threshold; uncertainty is resolved without reverse-engineering the desired restriction.
7. A forum misses its clock, a network is partitioned, or the usual reviewer is implicated; protection, independent challenge, and restoration continue through the published fallback.
8. A restored lower-slot finding leaves active integration and cannot reappear as a general reputation label through another gatekeeper.

## Recommended order of work

First reconcile the segregation conflict and repair the semantic owner references. Next make the overview candid about political equality, political exclusion, liberty restrictions, silence by default, and the actual extension burden. Remove stale edition stamps and the opening count error. Then validate the difficult cases above, including measurement disagreement and institutional failure, before treating textual completeness as evidence that the promised world has been achieved.

The present review supports the overview's main conceptual structure. It does not support an unqualified claim that the current corpus is internally consistent or that its implementation has been shown to deliver its promises.
