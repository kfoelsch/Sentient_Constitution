# Remaining textual issues — resolution

**Date:** 2026-09-17. **Edition:** SC-Corpus-2026.08.09, pre-release. **Status:** working-tree correction, not adoption or publication. Resolves findings 1–4 of the [follow-up quality check](alignment_followup_quality_check.md). Finding 5, the broader audit backlog, remains open.

## Corrections

1. **Record-entry separation.** [Chapter Eight §2.1](../../core_09_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default) now assigns authorization to the named record-opening authority and entry to a separate custodian, matching §§3.1 and 3.7. The [overview](../../guides/CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#ordinary-life-privacy-and-the-way-back) uses the same division. This repairs an inconsistent sentence; it does not create a new separation rule.
2. **Restoration and archived records.** The overview now distinguishes a forum's access on a documented recurrence-risk showing in a live matter from the separate exception for gatekeeper disclosure, competency bars, and named-pathway effects. The latter requires a published longer look-back for a specific high-sensitivity pathway satisfying Necessity and Proportionality. The overview also preserves the specific trust-sensitive competency-bar exception to ordinary access without a standing record and states the source's archival periods. Owners: [Chapter Nine §8.1](../../core_10_standing_integration.md#81-rest-state-and-archival), [§6.2](../../core_10_standing_integration.md#62-competency-bars-and-clearances), and Chapter Eight §2.1.
3. **Extensions and clocks.** The [forum summary](../../guides/CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#forums) distinguishes extendable default integrated-resolution bounds from the classified tier's intake, preservation, and interim-protection floors. Extensions require the Article XXIII-D continuation showing and remain subject to narrower Rights-Floor windows. Restore-challenge time runs from the original measure or deferral, whichever is earlier; continuation beyond its default bound requires that showing and does not reset the clock. Checked against [Chapter Eleven §6](../../core_12_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), [CF-11.3.1](../../corpus_forum/cf_11_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-1131-target-windows-and-timing-floors), and [Article XXIII-D](../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#xxiii-d-restore-challenge-clocks).
4. **Residual hierarchy.** The [overview's hierarchy paragraph](../../guides/CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#authority-stack-and-internal-hierarchy) now explicitly states principles over articles, and articles over definitions used as independent substantive glosses, only for genuine residual incompatibility after integrated reading. Canonical definitions still govern term meaning throughout. The [canonical definition](../../core_05_band_integrative.md#authority-stack) remains the owner.
