## Findings

1. **High — record-entry ownership remains contradictory.** [Chapter Eight §2.1](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-silence-is-the-default), line 136, still says a record is “entered by a named record-opening authority.” Sections 3.1 and 3.7 assign verification and authorization to that authority and entry to a separate custodian; §3.7 prohibits the same office from verifying and entering. The earlier correction missed this sentence. Align §2.1 with the separate authorization and entry roles; the segregation item is not fully closed.

2. **High — archival summary conflates forum access with gatekeeper disclosure.** [Overview](../../CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#ordinary-life-privacy-and-the-way-back), line 614, allows gatekeeper disclosure on a documented, proportionate recurrence-risk showing. [Chapter Nine §8.1](../../core_09_standing_integration.md#81-rest-state-and-archival) allows a *forum* to reach an archived record on a documented recurrence-risk showing in a live matter. Its separate exception for pathway effects, gatekeeper disclosure, and competency bars requires a published longer look-back for a specific high-sensitivity pathway, satisfying Necessity and Proportionality. The overview's broader exception could undermine restoration. State these two access rules separately. Also preserve §2.1's specific trust-sensitive competency-bar exception when summarizing ordinary access without a record.

3. **Medium — extension language still leaves the original ambiguity.** [Overview](../../CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#forums), line 709, correctly adds the continuation burden but says restore-challenge clocks and the outer-bound floor “remain in force” without explaining that these are default outer bounds which can themselves be exceeded under documented Article XXIII-D continuation. [Chapter Eleven §6](../../core_11_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), lines 852–860, and [CF-11.3.1](../../corpus_forum/cf_11_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-1131-target-windows-and-timing-floors), lines 220–224, state that exception. Distinguish preserved early-stage protection, extendable default outer bounds, and the original restore-challenge clock start, which cannot be reset.

4. **Medium — residual hierarchy is still not disclosed.** [Overview](../../CONCEPTUAL_OVERVIEW.md#authority-stack-and-internal-hierarchy), line 790, explains when to use Internal Hierarchy but still omits its substantive order. The [canonical definition](../../core_05_band_integrative.md#authority-stack), lines 625–631, says principles control over articles for genuine residual incompatibility, and articles over definitions read as independent substantive glosses; canonical definitions continue to govern term meaning. The accepted TODO requested this ordering, not only the sequence of reading steps.

5. **Medium — audit closure exceeds the evidence.** The checked “Resolve remaining audit findings” task in [TODO](../../TODO.md) covers the outstanding failures in both earlier resolution notes. All four checks cited in the new closure note pass, but seven other previously failing targets still fail. Reopen the broader item and retain the successful citation/label repairs as partial completion. Audit diagnostics need individual review; a failing formatting heuristic is not by itself a substantive constitutional defect.
