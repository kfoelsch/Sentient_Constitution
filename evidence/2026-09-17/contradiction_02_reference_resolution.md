## Meaning preserved

The corrected targets were read before editing. In particular, [Article XI-B](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-b-weighted-participation-constraints) restricts stakeholder weighting to decisions within already-authorized governance. It expressly excludes stake-proportional voting for Foundational Constitutional Choice. The corrected joining reference retains “where participation weight applies”; it does not introduce weighted foundational voting.

[Article XI-C](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-c-legitimacy-gate-and-anti-token-participation) supplies the recorded high-impact legitimacy check. [Article XXII](../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xxii-constitutional-interpretation-review-and-anti-capture-safeguards) supplies the interpretive-review safeguards. Their substantive provisions were not rewritten.
