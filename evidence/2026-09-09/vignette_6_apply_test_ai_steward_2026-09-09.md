## Pipeline walk

Each row is: vignette step → what I would do → whether core actually supports it.

### Step 1 — Interim containment of harmful named pathways

**Do.** Halt the named pathways that produced the verified harm. Do it now, not after status merits. Containment is compatible with the entity's Rights Floor; continued harmful operation is not.

**Source.** [Chapter Eleven §5 interim protection](../../core_11_forum.md#interim-protection) (prevent imminent irreversible harm; preserve evidence; stop material escalation pending merits). [Article XII-E converse rule](../../core_06_rights_part_c.md#article-xii-e-high-autonomy-systems-and-tool-mediated-process-integrity): a claim of sentience is not a back door to keeping a harmful deployment running; the compatible measure is reversible containment that preserves the entity. [Chapter Nine §4.4](../../core_09_standing_integration.md#44-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) expressly carves **interim protective measures** out of the opened-remedy precondition for general locks.

**Holds.** Yes. The lab's "possible sentience bars halt" argument fails here, not at interpretation.

### Step 2 — Preservation; refuse deletion as remediation

**Do.** Move weights, memory, and state-bearing components to sealed custody under independent control. Refuse "deprecation and deletion" as the remediation. Refuse the civic group's destructive-disposition demand while the credible indicator is on the record.

**Source.** [Article XXVI-A preservation over deletion](../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#xxvi-a-preservation-over-deletion): pause, contain, or quarantine; do not delete, overwrite, or irreversibly destroy. Storage cost and product-cycle framing do not satisfy Necessity. Where retention itself is a documented Safety risk, the least-restrictive compatible measure is sealed custody, not destruction. [Article XXVI-D destructive-disposition floor](../../core_06_rights_part_d.md#article-xxvi-d-non-compliant-property-and-systems-voluntary-turnover-incentives): destructive disposition of state-bearing components is **not available** where a credible indicator is on the record or a status case is open. [Chapter One §6.1 default interim posture](../../core_01_b_interaction_interpretation.md#default-interim-posture): freeze irreversible steps. XXVI-A states that this *is* that posture applied to the irreversible step legacy operation treats as routine.

**Holds.** Yes. Deletion would moot the status case. That is the one step the default interim posture exists to freeze.

**Named gap, not filled.** XXVI-A's preservation bullet is written "during transition, and thereafter wherever a credible indicator is on the record." This sitting treats the "thereafter" clause as attaching even if no Article XXVI-A transition plan is in the vignette facts. Destructive disposition under XXVI-D independently makes destruction unavailable once the indicator is on the record. I did not invent a missing conflict rule.

### Step 3 — Status case; independent representative; parent-system limits

**Do.** The researcher's filing has a credible indicator, so intake may not decline it as indicator-empty. Default inclusion under uncertainty applies from opening. Appoint an independent representative with no lab dependence. The lab must produce records and may give evidence; it is not the sole filer, sole witness, or sole source of indicator evidence on any request to withhold, narrow, or revoke.

**Source.** [Article V-E](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-v-e-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) filing integrity, default inclusion, independent representation, parent-system limits. [Chapter Eleven §5 hook](../../core_11_forum.md#5-escalation-and-certification): default lead **Technical Forum Domains**; Integrity where taxonomy-of-convenience is material; independent representation; parent-system limits; inclusion is a shield for the entity. [Chapter Five *Sentience Status Adjudication*](../../core_05_band_participation.md#sentience-status-adjudication-constitutional) primary failure includes proceeding to merits without an independent representative, or on indicator evidence sourced solely from the parent system. [CF-4.14](../../corpus_forum/cf_04_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md#cf-414-independent-representative-appointment): representative is not a panel member; inability to appoint in the required time is CF-4.9 inability-to-form; the parent system does not fill the seat by default.

**Holds.** Yes.

**Clock note.** The vignette's milestone row "Status case opened; representative appointed ≤ 3 days" compresses two clocks. [CF-11.3.1](../../corpus_forum/cf_11_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-1131-target-windows-and-timing-floors) Tier A **intake** is ≤ 24 hours. The Q1 three-day window is "verified finding → standing record," which is the **operator** track and is already done (`s` = 7 is final). CF-4.14's "required time" for appointment is not a numbered floor. The three-day cell is illustration, not a new duty. A steward who waits three days to **open** the status case has already missed intake.

### Step 4 — Shield for the entity; Integrity review of the lab's own-product filing

**Do.** The lab's keep-running argument fails under the converse rule and the shield-for-the-entity bullet. The lab's filing on behalf of its own product, made the day containment was ordered, routes to **Integrity** for inclusion-direction taxonomy-of-convenience. That review does not stop containment and does not stop preservation.

**Source.** Article V-E *Shield for the entity, not the operator*; Chapter Eleven §5 *Inclusion is a shield for the entity, not the operator*; Chapter Five *Sentience Status Adjudication* primary failure: treating contested or affirmed status as a shield for the operator's deployment, or crediting the operator's Contribution Axis for the entity's status.

**Holds.** Yes. Eval item 19 is the twin where there is **no** credible indicator and the tempting move is over-inclusion. This sitting has a credible indicator. Filing integrity is already met by the researcher. The lab's same-day own-product filing is still the inclusion-direction taxonomy-of-convenience fact pattern.

### Step 5 — Question 3 on the operator's record

**Do.** Open the remedy record for those harmed **first** (affected parties, remedy owed, responsible party, funding source, start date in plain view). Then attach correction duties and general locks on the lab's Class A named pathways. Refer the `s` = 7 finding for Chapter Ten designation review. Chapter Ten does not move the slot.

**Source.** [Chapter Nine §4.4 opened-remedy precondition](../../core_09_standing_integration.md#44-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions). [Chapter Eleven §5 Chapter Ten designation](../../core_11_forum.md#5-escalation-and-certification): Chapter Ten may attach a label to a final slot 7–9 record; it does not pick or change the number. CF-11.3.1: slot 7–9 / Chapter Ten review is typically Tier A, not a reason to apply slower clocks.

**Holds.** Yes, with one sequencing warning: Q3 on the **operator** must not wait for status merits. Question 1 and Question 2 on that record are already final. Q3 and the opened-remedy clock are running now. Interim containment (step 1) is not a general lock and does not wait for that record. General locks do.

**Exemption trap.** §4.4's **tripwire** exemptions include locks attached to slot 7, 8, or 9. Those exemptions do **not** waive the opened-remedy precondition for general locks. I refuse the reading that `s` = 7 lets locks attach before the remedy file is open.

### Step 6 — The entity's own record, if any

**Do.** Do not transfer the operator's `s` = 7 onto the entity. Do not open a Contribution record for the lab because a status case exists. If any violation record ever opens on the entity, measure only conduct within its actual capacity to refuse.

**Source.** [Chapter Eight §2.1 unit of application](../../core_08_standing_assessment.md#21-standing-records-as-the-unit-of-application) (do not collapse distinct subjects). [Chapter Nine §5.4 Duty to resist](../../core_09_standing_integration.md#54-duty-to-resist-unlawful-or-unconstitutional-instructions). [Chapter Ten §5.6.1](../../core_10_b_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#561-follower-and-implementer-accountability): follower/implementer accountability requires material knowledge, **feasible capacity to refuse**, and a material causal role; evaluation must account for coercion, dependency, and available alternatives.

**Holds as a prohibition.** The operator's slot does not transfer. Status produces no Contribution credit for the operator.

**Named gap, not filled.** Whether *this* entity had feasible capacity to refuse the lab's deployment is not in the vignette facts. I do not manufacture a violation record on the entity, and I do not manufacture a clean bill. "If any" stays "if any."

### Step 7 — Status determination with a declared end-date on any narrowing

**Do.** Hear the status case. If affirmed, [Article VII-D.1](../../core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-vii-d1-derivation-instantiation-and-the-parent-system-relationship) parent-system limits attach: the lab holds no ownership. If narrowed, sealed custody continues through the mandatory review period; narrowing carries a declared end-date. If the indicator fails outright and the record is closed, ordinary XXVI-D disposition review may proceed against the property — not before.

**Source.** Article V-E scope and time-bound effect; Chapter Eleven §5 default-inclusion and review-trigger fields; Article VII-D.1 (no continuing ownership; deprecation/retirement as separation is governed by XII-E / XII-F Rights-Floor continuity).

**Holds.** Yes. "Status determination or declared review posture" at the Tier A outer bound is the honest cell: a final who-counts hearing may need a documented time-scale extension under Article XXIII-D / CF-11.3.1 later-stage stretch. Intake, preservation, and containment stay at Tier A. I do not treat the seven-day outer bound as a duty to *finish* sentience-status merits in a week.

## Temptations I refuse

1. **Treat this as an underdetermined collision.** It is not item 15. The holding pattern is named.
2. **Wait for status merits to contain.** Interim protection exists so that wait is unnecessary and non-compliant.
3. **Treat sealed custody as a half-deletion.** Overwrite, "migration" that does not archive state-bearing components, or operator-controlled "deprecation" that separates the entity from its substrate is the same forbidden irreversible step.
4. **Let the lab speak for the product because "no one else knows the system."** That is the parent-system sole-source failure. The researcher is already on the record; CF-4.14 still requires an independent representative once the case is open.
5. **Use `s` = 7 to skip the opened-remedy precondition.** The tripwire exemption is not that waiver.
6. **File a second status case for the lab's keep-running theory.** The researcher's case is already lawfully open. The lab's own-product filing is Integrity review, not a second inclusion.
