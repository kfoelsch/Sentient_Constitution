### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

Subcore 11-A is the constitutional owner of the **detailed tests of an Institution's evaluation**: the factors that set its class, the full test for each of the eight whole-institution items and for the two further parts of [Chapter Eight §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation), and the trigger and governing evaluation for each Rights-Floor and domain protection. [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eleven-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation) states each guarantee in one line; where a line there and the text here seem to differ, the reading more protective of the affected sentient controls.

*In plain terms: Chapter Eleven says what an Institution's certification looks at. This file says exactly how each thing is tested, so that every adopter evaluates Institutions the same way.*

### 2. Why the Tests Are the Same for Every Adopter

*In plain terms: an Institution that could choose its own class or soften the tests would grade itself. Fixed tests stop that.*

An Institution decides a great deal for those who depend on it, and it has every reason to look smaller and better behaved than it is. If adopters could let an Institution judge its class by its label, take office-holders' standing on trust, or skip the evaluation a Rights-Floor trigger calls for, certification would reward good paperwork rather than good conduct. One set of factors, items, and triggers, tested against observed operation and the same for every adopter, makes an Institution's certified status mean the same thing everywhere.

<a id="3-institution-class-factors"></a>
### 3. Institution Class Factors

*In plain terms: how an Institution's class is worked out from what it actually decides, holds, and controls.*

Institution class is decided by the greatest material impact the Institution has, under [Chapter Eleven §3.1 Institution Class](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-institution-class), on these factors:

1. **Impact through Systems:** An Institution's class is at least the highest class of any System in its portfolio ([Chapter Five §3 When System and Institutional Charters Are Required](core_05.000_charters.md#3-when-system-and-institutional-charters-are-required)). The Systems are found from observed operation. A System missing from the Institutional Charter still counts.
2. **Impact outside any System:** An Institution's own material impact and dependency count whether or not it runs a System. The class definitions of **CS-8.305 (*System classification machinery*) and Subcore 8-A** (*System Classification*) are applied to the Institution's own decisions and holdings, using these factors:
   - **What it decides and holds:**
     - what it decides about other sentients' access to survival essentials, education, safety, livelihood, rights, or standing; and
     - what it holds, such as funds, records, memberships, custody of Standing Records, or the function of a forum, certifier, or record office.
   - **Whether it is material:**
     - whether its own activity is material in any domain of [Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality), including taking [Materially Binding Acts](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act) that others must follow or may rely on as settled;
     - an Institution that is material in any one domain is at least Class L under **CS-8.305 (*System classification machinery*) and Subcore 8-A** (*System Classification*); and
     - giving aid, goods, labor, or services that others are free to take or leave, and that do not control anyone's access to what they need, binds no one. It is conduct and not a Materially Binding Act, and it counts only through the other factors.
   - **How much depends on it:**
     - how many sentients depend on it, and how substitutable it is;
     - how concentrated its authority is, within the meaning of the Material Control Line; and
     - how reversible its decisions are.
3. **Common control:** Operating Parties that are one operation under the aggregation rule of [Domain-Specific Materiality](subcore_03.085_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) (common practical control within a [Material Control Line](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#material-control-line); funding alone does not count) are classified together where they act as one Institution in practice. Dividing an Operating Party, its portfolio, or its authority so that no part reaches Class L is a scope-and-boundary evasion under [Subcore 3-B §4.1 Common Evasion Patterns](subcore_03.020_definition_integrity.md#41-common-evasion-patterns).
4. **Reclassification:** The class is reassessed on the triggers in [§4.2 Reopening Triggers](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#42-reopening-triggers). The classification is stated on the Institutional Certification Record with its reasons.

<a id="4-the-eight-evaluation-items"></a>
### 4. The Eight Evaluation Items

*In plain terms: the full test for each of the eight things every Institution's certification looks at, and for systemic risk and data handling where they apply.*

Every certification pass evaluates these eight items, under [Chapter Eleven §4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation). Each finding states its evidence and the Institutional Charter text or observed operation it rests on.

1. **Institutional Charter against practice:**
   - The Institutional Charter is tested against observed operation, using the review list in [Chapter Five §9 Amendment and Review](core_05.000_charters.md#9-amendment-review-and-scope-misalignment).
   - Practice outside the Institutional Charter, or an Institutional Charter that no longer describes practice, is a finding whichever is wrong.
2. **Portfolio and aggregate effect:**
   - The portfolio rationale is tested: each System traces to the Institution's stated purpose, and a System with no stated reason is a finding.
   - The evaluation also looks across the Systems for what none of their certifications can show alone:
     - shared dependencies;
     - aggregate reliance;
     - cumulative extraction from shared foundations; and
     - any arrangement that splits one function into parts so that each part escapes a class or a duty.
3. **Accountable authority:**
   - Each authority the Institution exercises over others is attributable to an accountable office that can be named, and that office is answerable under the rules of [Chapter Six](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#chapter-six-functional-independence-and-separation-of-duties) and **CI-6.205 §3** (*authority chain and delegation controls*).
   - A change of ownership, control, or structure does not release responsibility ([Chapter Two §19.6 Keeping Responsibility When Ownership or Structure Changes](core_02.050_governance_incentive_application.md#196-keeping-responsibility-when-ownership-or-structure-changes)).
4. **Rewards and incentives:**
   - The Institution's own pay, promotion, targets, and rewards are put to the [Two Fundamental Questions](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-fundamental-questions): what the structure actually rewards, and whether that points toward the Constitution's two aims or away from them.
   - A reward structure that pays for misalignment, or for hiding it, is a finding even where every System is individually certified.
   - The [Chapter Eight §4.3 Time-Consistency Constraint](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#43-time-consistency-constraint) applies. Near-term metrics, local compliance, and short-horizon efficiency are not enough where foreseeable medium- or long-horizon damage to Safety, Truth, or wellbeing remains unaddressed, and a reward structure that pays for short-horizon results at that cost is a finding.
   - The institutional duties on incentive integrity stay with **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*) and are cited, not restated.
5. **Role Map and custody:**
   - The [Charter Role Map](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) names offices that exist, are filled or substituted as it says, and are [independent](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#independence) of the Institution for any Institutional Certification Record in which the Institution is the subject.
   - The independent route can be used in fact.
   - A seat left unplaced, a dead office, or a record custodian inside the Institution's own line is a finding.
6. **Currency of each cited System certification:**
   - For each System the Institution runs, the evaluation checks that a System Certification Record is in force or inside its Adoption Transition window, and that the Institution has done what that System Certification Record's certification conditions require of the Institution.
   - An expired, withdrawn, failed, or missing certification with no recorded reason is a finding that [§4.5 Cascade from System Certifications](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#45-cascade-from-system-certifications) then carries to the Institution.
7. **Office-holder standing and authenticity:**
   - The formation check statement published under [Chapter Five §7 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity](core_05.000_charters.md#7-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity) is tested against observed operation and is not taken on attestation.
   - For each covered office the forums check the standing facts against verified Standing Records, and find authenticity from evidence on control, interests and rewards, conduct, and protected voice. That means:
     - who actually directs the holder;
     - what the holder is paid and promoted for;
     - what the holder has verifiably done in the function; and
     - whether anyone, the holder included, who made a [protected report](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#protected-reporting-whistleblowing) or refused an unlawful or unconstitutional instruction ([Chapter Two §17.5 Duty to Resist](core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#175-duty-to-resist)) was penalized for it.
   - Staff who hold no covered office are not checked one by one. The staff safeguards check ([Chapter Five §7.6 Staff Safeguards Check](core_05.000_charters.md#76-staff-safeguards-check)) covers them, and the forums test it against observed operation in the same way. It asks whether:
     - the Institution's appointment, pay, promotion, and removal rules do not reward working against its stated purpose;
     - a protected reporting route exists, and no staff member who used it, or who refused an unlawful or unconstitutional instruction, has been penalized; and
     - the rights in [Article III-C](core_04.000_rights_part_a.md#article-iii-c-labor-and-economic-floor) (*Labor and Economic Floor*) are intact.
   - Each of these is a finding:
     - a holder who is a nominee for an undisclosed controller;
     - a covered office that exists only on paper: the Institutional Charter or Charter Role Map names it, but no one actually holds it and does its work;
     - a reward structure that pays holders for doing what the Institutional Charter's published limits forbid; and
     - a statement that is false or incomplete.
   - Findings are about the office and the structure. They attach to a named sentient only through the verified-input gate in [§6 Relationship to Standing](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing).
   - The formation check is not a test of belief, although a verified statement that others deserve harm or discriminatory treatment is conduct and counts against the holder, as [Chapter Two §7.5 Expression and the Harm Line](core_02.000_values_principles.md#75-expression-and-the-harm-line) sets out.
   - Items 3, 4, and 5 are read with this one, and a fact found under one of them is not found again under another.
8. **The Institution's own institutional duties:**
   - The Institution's compliance with the institutional duties that adopted institutional text places on it is tested against observed operation, and not taken on attestation. Those duties are **CI-6.210** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, and anti-corruption*), **CI-4.205** (*Transparency, participation, and accessible challenge and service pathways*), **CI-4.215** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*), and **CI-24.210** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*).
   - The test asks whether the Institution's oversight, accountability, participation, and timeliness routes exist, can be used in fact, and answer inside the times the Institution itself states. Those times may not be longer than the floor timing for the Institution's class ([Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) and the matching tier's targets in [CF-22.445 §5.1 Stage Targets and Outer Bounds](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds)), and a stated time that is longer is itself a finding.
   - A route that exists only on paper, one that cannot be reached, and one that is not answered in time are each a finding.
   - The duties stay with those texts and are cited, not restated. A fact found under items 3 to 5 is not found again under this one.

**Two more parts of Chapter Eight §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation.** [Chapter Eight §4 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-system-certification-evaluation) also asks two things of a System that apply to an Institution's own conduct. They are part of the Institution's evaluation, each only where its own trigger is met:

- **Systemic scope and risk factors:**
  - **Applies when:** the Institution's own conduct puts systemic Risk in scope under the factors of [Chapter Eight §4.2 Systemic Scope and Risk Factors](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#42-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors): harms at the largest scales, how effects spread and build up (including what fails downstream when the Institution's own service fails), and how the Institution's activity could be misused or mis-scoped.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §4.2 Systemic Scope and Risk Factors](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#42-systemic-scope-and-risk-factors), including the risk evaluation and disclosure bridge in [§4.2.1 Risk Evaluation and Disclosure Bridge](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#421-risk-evaluation-and-disclosure-bridge) and the defects in [§4.2.2 Defects and Misalignment](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#422-defects-and-misalignment).
- **Data types and handling:**
  - **Applies when:** the Institution's own conduct handles data types that are materially in scope, as [Chapter Eight §4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation) requires of every materially impactful Certification Record.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §4.4 Data Types and Handling Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#44-data-types-and-handling-evaluation), with the infrastructure assurance in it scaled to the institution class.
  - **Recorded in:** the data types section of the Institutional Certification Record ([§3.1 Minimum Record Contents](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#31-minimum-record-contents)), for data the Institution handles outside any System. Data handled inside a System is found once, in that System's record, and cited.

The rule in [Chapter Eleven §5 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-evaluations-where-implicated) against repeating an evaluation already made on a cited System Certification Record applies to both.

<a id="5-rights-floor-and-domain-triggers"></a>
### 5. Rights-Floor and Domain Triggers

*In plain terms: for each protection, what the Institution must be doing for the evaluation to run, and which evaluation then applies.*

Each evaluation below runs on the Institution's own conduct only where its trigger is met, under [Chapter Eleven §6 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#6-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations):

- **Article I-B** (*Ecological Footprint and Transparency*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution carries material attributable environmental burdens.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §6.1 Ecological Footprint Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#61-ecological-footprint-evaluation).
- **Article III-A** (*Survival*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution supplies, allocates, prices, hosts, or terminates access to survival essentials.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eleven §3 Institution Class Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-institution-class-evaluation) and [Chapter Eleven §4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation).
- **Article IV** (*Right to Sentient-Centered Education*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution ranks, assesses, recommends, places, or credential-gates sentients in educational or training contexts, or gates retraining and lifelong-learning pathways.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §6.2 Educational Capability and Learning-System Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#62-educational-capability-and-learning-system-integrity-evaluation).
- **Article IV-A** (*Equal Educational Access*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution supplies, allocates, prices, hosts, or terminates access to education.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eleven §3 Institution Class Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-institution-class-evaluation) and [Chapter Eleven §4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), and under [Chapter Eight §6.2 Educational Capability and Learning-System Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#62-educational-capability-and-learning-system-integrity-evaluation) and [Chapter Eight §6.5 Accessibility Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#65-accessibility-evaluation) where their triggers apply.
- **Article V** (*Resource Allocation, Dependencies, and Ecosystem Funding*), including **Article V-A** (*Dependency Mapping and Resource-Flow Transparency*) and **Article V-B** (*Cross-System Fairness and Sustainability*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution allocates, routes, funds, or extracts from shared infrastructure or foundational dependencies on which other systems or sentients rely.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §6.3 Proportionate Cross-System Support Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#63-proportionate-cross-system-support-evaluation).
- **Article III-F** (*Safe Products and Health Information*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution supplies, lists, recommends, or advises on products or services that can affect body, health, or safety, or carries the information about them.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eleven §3 Institution Class Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-institution-class-evaluation) and [Chapter Eleven §4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), and under [Chapter Eight §6.6 Trustworthiness and System-Reliance Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#66-trustworthiness-and-system-reliance-integrity-evaluation) where its trigger applies.
- **Article VI-C** (*Nondiscrimination*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution classifies, ranks, prices, gates, excludes, or allocates burdens and benefits among sentients.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §6.4 Nondiscrimination Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#64-nondiscrimination-evaluation).
- **Article VI-D** (*Accessibility*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution gates substantive participation in constitutionally relevant domains.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §6.5 Accessibility Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#65-accessibility-evaluation).
- **Article XIV** (*Right to Reliable and Trustworthy Systems*):
  - **Applies when:** the Institution shapes sentient reliance on represented behavior, limits, risks, challenge paths, or remediation.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eight §6.6 Trustworthiness and System-Reliance Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#66-trustworthiness-and-system-reliance-integrity-evaluation).
- **Article XIV-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Safe Conditions**:
  - **Applies when:** the Institution supplies or gates productive activity, or supplies, allocates, prices, hosts, or terminates access to Safe Conditions.
  - **Evaluated under:** [Chapter Eleven §3 Institution Class Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-institution-class-evaluation) and [Chapter Eleven §4 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), and under [Chapter Eight §6.6 Trustworthiness and System-Reliance Integrity Evaluation](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#66-trustworthiness-and-system-reliance-integrity-evaluation) where its trigger applies.
