*In plain terms: this section assigns constitutional **participation** and **oversight** through forum families that **supervise** two tracks — **System Alignment Certification** (Chapter Eight certification and review) and the **standing pipeline** (Chapters Fourteen–Twenty-One disputes and record effects) — then states accountability requirements for published threshold placement. Ordinary disputes inside already-authorized systems use the published Stakeholder System Participation challenge path first; this chapter takes over when that path is still contested, missing, captured, or cannot grant relief. Detailed hearing rules live elsewhere and may not quietly shrink what this chapter guarantees.*

**Purpose:** Under the [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad) and [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims), Chapter Twenty-Two answers the question that follows any dispute under the certification and standing chapters: who hears it, and can the sentient affected get a fair and timely hearing? The chapter supplies:

- forums that are independent and contestable;
- a clear starting point for each kind of dispute; and
- an accessible way to challenge decisions that affect sentients, backed by forensic and technical support and by the timeliness floor in **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).

What it produces is the set of forum families and the routing that sends each dispute to one of them.

**Role:** Where this chapter sits:

- It supervises two tracks: system and institutional alignment certification ([Chapter Eight](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eight-system-alignment-certification--evaluation) through [Chapter Thirteen](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-thirteen-institutional-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing)), and the standing pipeline ([Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#chapter-fourteen-standing-records--question-1-what-happened) through [Chapter Twenty-One](core_21.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#chapter-twenty-one-anti-constitutional-misconduct--pattern-applications-and-criteria-routing)).
- Ordinary disputes inside an already-authorized system use the published Stakeholder System Participation challenge path first. This chapter takes over when that path leaves the dispute contested, is missing, is captured, or cannot grant the relief needed.
- Forum eligibility, contested selection, and democratic minimums are set in [Chapter Twenty-Six](core_26.000_governance.md#chapter-twenty-six-constitutional-contract-legitimacy-authorization-and-stewardship), **section 3.2**.

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers the following:

- forum family supervision and standards (**section 3**);
- default venue and primary stakes (**section 4**);
- forum cases: intakes, seats, records, and contests (**section 5**); and
- transfer, consolidation, and anti-self-judging (**section 6**).

Forum family definitions, escalation and questions for ruling, timely resolution, and forum support are in [Chapter Twenty-Three](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#chapter-twenty-three-forum-families-escalation-timely-resolution-and-support).

The chapter leaves the certification rules to the certification chapters, the standing rules to the standing pipeline chapters, and procedure details to adopted implementation text. Forum families do **not** run a second, separate decision track beside the standing pipeline.

<a id="2-why-forums-exist"></a>
### 2. Why Forums Exist

*In plain terms: every right needs a place where a sentient can be heard when it is denied. This chapter exists so that place is fair, independent, timely, and actually reachable.*

A right that cannot be brought before someone independent is only a hope. Disputes also go wrong in predictable ways: the powerful choose a friendly venue, a case is passed between offices until it lapses, or the body being challenged hears the challenge. This chapter supervises the forums, decides which forum hears a matter by its primary stake, and keeps any body from judging its own case.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad): all four legs meet in a forum:
    - [Participation](subcore_03.060_apex_participation_leg.md#participation): the sentient affected must be heard;
    - [Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability): someone must answer for the decision;
    - [Oversight](subcore_03.055_apex_oversight_leg.md#oversight): an independent body checks what was done;
    - [Timeliness](subcore_03.065_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness): relief that comes too late is no relief;
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity), since disputes left open wear down the shared systems sentients depend on;

*In plain terms: forums are the main way this Constitution governs, and this section says what they oversee. That is the certification of systems and institutions, and the standing pipeline: how Standing Records are opened, corrected, and acted on, including remedies and findings of anti-constitutional misconduct. <a id="31-what-forum-families-supervise"></a>

#### 3.1 What Forum Families Supervise

This chapter states which **forum families** **supervise** which primary questions through:

- **participation** (sentient-accessible challenge, representative treatment, and proportionate access); and
- **oversight** (independence, forensic support, and traceable merits review).

It does **not** specify docket rules, staffing, budgets, granular procedure, or operational escalation mechanics. Those details belong in adopted implementation text.

**Forum families supervise two tracks:**

1. **Certification** ([Chapter Eight](core_08.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eight-system-alignment-certification--evaluation))
   - System Alignment Certification and Institutional Alignment Certification outcomes, from conditional certification through recertification and withdrawal, under [Chapter Nine](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes), [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-ten-system-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing), and [Chapter Eleven](core_11.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-eleven-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation). Component roles are set in [Chapter Nine §3.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-forum-supervision-and-component-roles).
   - A certification does **not** replace standing measurement ([Chapter Ten §6 Relationship to Standing](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing)).

2. **Standing pipeline** (Chapters Fourteen–Twenty-One, applying Chapters Two through Four and designated [corpus](subcore_03.080_band_integrative.md#corpus) implementation norms in disputes)
   - Primary-stakes routing, merits review where assigned, transfer, consolidation, certification coordination, and remedy sequencing for standing-related disputes.
   - Standing Records, effects, and anti-constitutional-misconduct designation (ACMD) stay under Chapters Fourteen, Fifteen, Sixteen, and Twenty. A filed case is not standing by itself, and forum narrative must not substitute for Chapter Fifteen standing measurement ([Chapter Fourteen §4.7 Forum Boundary](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary)).

**Forum families** are the primary institutions through which this Constitution **supervises** those tracks. They are distinct from routine executive administration of enacted rules.

#### 3.2 Standards Every Forum Family Meets

<a id="321-independence-contestability-and-tracing"></a>

##### 3.2.1 Independence, Contestability, and Tracing

Forum families:

- carry a proactive governance role, in alignment with **Chapter Two** principles including **Safety**, **Truth**, **Wellbeing**, **Responsiveness**, and **Stewardship in Depth**, that includes:
  - problem resolution, root-cause analysis, and prevention;
  - remediation sequencing; and
  - learning from patterns of failure.
- must satisfy **independence**, **contestability**, and **tracing** expectations in:
  - **Subcores 3-A to 3-C**;
  - **Article XIV-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), where challenge and remediation are implicated; and
  - **Article XXI** (*Justice After Verified Violation*), where justice constraints govern.
- are subject to this Constitution's most stringent expressed procedural and integrity-accountability requirements, including:
  - public justification and traceability;
  - recusal and panel discipline; and
  - forensic and analytical support where this chapter assigns them.
- require anti-self-judging backup routing, which sends a matter to an independent backup forum when the lead forum cannot judge it ([Ways a Matter Moves Between Forums](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#ways-a-matter-moves-between-forums)). Those requirements must not substitute political control for judicial independence.
- hold the four seats in a forum case in the way [§5.2 Seats in a Forum Case](#52-seats-in-a-forum-case) sets out.
- **Integrity**, **Constitutional**, and **Environment** forums, and **Technical Forum Domains** when they hear sentience-status adjudication, require:
  - **published**, **contested**, **rotatable** appointment or an equivalent independence check under [Chapter Twenty-Six §3.2.4 Forum Appointment Independence Requirement](core_26.000_governance.md#324-forum-appointment-independence-requirement); and

##### 3.2.2 Incentives and Non-Substitution

Each **forum family** bears primary responsibility for incentive structures primarily within its sphere. This includes:

- adopting documents and designated implementation layers;

<a id="41-dispute-sequencing"></a>

#### 4.1 Dispute Sequencing

<a id="dispute-sequencing"></a>
Inside a system, institution, or decision area that is already authorized, an ordinary dispute goes in two steps:

- It first uses the published [Stakeholder System Participation](subcore_03.090_band_participation.md#stakeholder-status-and-weight) path for challenges and due process.
- This chapter sends the matter to the forum that fits its primary stake, under **section 4**, when that path:
  - leaves the result still contested;
  - does not exist;
  - has been captured; or
  - cannot lawfully give the relief needed.

Three rules keep this sequence from becoming a way to delay:

- **Integrity**, **Technical Forum Domains**, **Environment**, and **Constitutional** forums lead by default when their area is the primary stake. That is the normal order, not an exception that skips the sequence.
- An unfinished internal process, an "exhaustion" label, or a claim that internal review is not done must not stall those leads, push aside their decision on the merits, or use up the time limits in **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).
- Direct access stays open when waiting would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or a practical way to restore what was lost.

<a id="42-primary-stakes-routing-and-intake"></a>

#### 4.2 Primary-Stakes Routing and Intake

<a id="421-the-primary-stakes-test"></a>

##### 4.2.1 The Primary-Stakes Test

**Primary-stakes routing** assigns a matter to the forum family responsible for the main legal, remedial, coercive-safeguard, constitutional-floor, or practical stake in the **claim** or **defense**. It follows what the dispute is really about, not the caption or the party's preferred outcome.

<a id="422-intake-triage-body-first-touch-desk"></a>

##### 4.2.2 Intake Triage Body (First-Touch Desk)

Each required forum family must maintain an **intake triage body**, or a functionally equivalent arrangement within that family, as the family's **first-touch desk** at filing. What it does, and the limits that keep it from deciding the merits or collapsing family boundaries, are in [Subcore 22-A §3 Intake Triage Body (First-Touch Desk)](subcore_22.010_forum_intake_default_venue.md#3-intake-triage-body-first-touch-desk).

<a id="423-good-faith-characterization-and-anti-gaming"></a>

##### 4.2.3 Good-Faith Characterization and Anti-Gaming

<a id="good-faith-characterization-and-anti-gaming"></a>
Two rules keep routing honest:

- Forum characterization must be **good-faith** and **warranted** by **primary stakes**. **Knowing** mischaracterization **may** trigger **costs**, **dismissal**, or **referral** under adopting law. Where it is verified conduct in Violation Axis slot 7, 8, or 9, it may also be anti-constitutional misconduct (ACM) under [Chapter Twenty §6 Violation Axis Slot 7–9 Anti-Constitutional Designation](core_20.000_misconduct_designation.md#6-violation-axis-slot-79-anti-constitutional-designation).
- Incentive devices must not be structured or applied to reward:
  - forum shopping;
  - opaque threshold sorting; or
  - routing inconsistent with primary-stakes discipline in **this section** and **section 6**.

<a id="424-initial-access-and-timing"></a>

##### 4.2.4 Initial Access and Timing

Operational requirements — including **published intake classes**, attributable intake records, independence and **contest** expectations, and review of **contested** routing inside the **intake target** — are stated in **CF-22.415** (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*).

**Initial access** to each forum family must come inside the **intake target** of the matching tier ([CF-22.445 §5.1](corpus_forum/cf_22.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-22445-51-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*Stage targets and outer bounds*)) and be contestable enough that:
- primary-stakes routing under this section remains meaningful;
- transfer, consolidation, certification, and backup discipline under **section 6** and **Chapter Twenty-Three section 4** are not nullified by delay or by opaque threshold sorting.

Time expectations for forum access must:
- be **sentient-accessible**;
- be calibrated to harm urgency and matter complexity under **Chapter Twenty-Three section 5** and **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*);
- favor real threshold access and lawful interim relief under **Chapter Twenty-Three section 4** (*Interim Protection*) over administrative convenience.

The **intake triage body** under this section, together with **CF-22.415** (*Routing Operations, Transfer, Questions for Ruling, and Representative Treatment*), satisfies this obligation and must implement **Chapter Twenty-Three section 5** tier-default windows within incorporation scope.

<a id="44-mixed-stakes-and-routing-asymmetry"></a>

#### 4.4 Mixed Stakes and Routing Asymmetry

<a id="441-one-case-record-one-lead"></a>

##### 4.4.1 One Case Record, One Lead

One Forum Case Record and one lead family ordinarily hear interdependent claims. Secondary issues may be certified, stayed, or resolved through issue preclusion as adopting documents provide, consistent with **Article XIV-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*), **Article XIV-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*), and **Chapter Fourteen** joint-assessment and non-substitution discipline.

<a id="442-asymmetry"></a>

##### 4.4.2 Asymmetry

Three asymmetry rules keep routing available where the table assigns the stake:

- Where **dependency**, **measurement**, or an **institutional** **monopoly** on a **necessary** **input** materially disadvantages a **sentient** party, **Institutional** or **Integrity** routing **must** be **available** when the table assigns **institutional** or **integrity** stakes.
- Where **material** **ecological** stakes are **primary**, **Environment** routing **must** be **available** as the table assigns.
- **Sentient** forums **must not** be the **sole** **mandatory** forum when the **primary** stakes are **institutional**, **integrity**, or **ecological** under the table in this section.

A certification can be challenged along four paths, and this section says which forum takes each. A Standing Record can be contested too, and the forum reviews the Standing Record and how it is being used, not a sentient's whole reputation. An adopter's Adoption Transition Record can be contested, and the forum reviews the contested gate decision or entry, not the whole transition.*

<a id="51-intakes"></a>

#### 5.1 Intakes

A case reaches a forum in one of four ways: as a **direct case** (a [Direct Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#direct-case)), or as a [Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case) that contests a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record. The intake triage body records the intake kind on the [Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) at filing. What arrives, the first step, and how each kind is routed are in [Subcore 22-B §3 Intake Table](subcore_22.020_forum_case_seats_contest_routing.md#3-intake-table). Where delay would materially endanger rights, evidence, independence, or practical restoration, a review case has direct access to a forum.

These rules apply to every intake:

- A claim is routed by what it contests in substance, not by how it is filed. A claim that in substance contests a Certification Record, a Standing Record, or an Adoption Transition Record is a review case.
- A transfer, consolidation, or referral between forums continues the same case. The case keeps its intake kind and its [Forum Case Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record), and only the lead changes (**section 6**). A move that changes the lead is one automated step that updates the docket portal and sends alerts ([CF-22.415 §12 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-12-transfer-procedure)).
- A direct case may end in a verified violation finding that opens or corrects a Standing Record. The forum then acts as record-opening authority on the terms in [Chapter Fourteen §4.7 Forum Boundary](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary), and a contest of that Standing Record is a new review case heard by a forum that did not make the finding.

<a id="52-seats-in-a-forum-case"></a>

#### 5.2 Seats in a Forum Case

The full seat table and the rules for each kind of party are in [Subcore 22-B §4 Seats in a Forum Case](subcore_22.020_forum_case_seats_contest_routing.md#4-seats-in-a-forum-case). Their guarantees are:

- **Four seats, no fifth:** a forum case is a materially binding act with the four seats of [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement); this chapter adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Six, and forensic and technical support is not a fifth seat.
- **No one reviews their own act:** the forum, panel, or office that made a contested finding, decision, or declaration never hears the contest of it, and whoever held a seat on the earlier act is a respondent or witness on the review.
- **No merging by label:** Chapter Six's incompatibilities apply to each act, and a forum family may not use its family label to merge seats Chapter Six prohibits on the same act.
- **Chartered parties do not pick their judges:** where the party has a System Charter or Institutional Charter, every seat holder sits outside its Material Control Line, and the party cannot choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove the forum or panel.
- **A sentient files alone:** a sentient holds the Initiating Seat on its own filing and needs no institution to do so; a community's representative holds no seat in the decision; and a forum family accused of bias, capture, or conflict holds no seat in the case.

<a id="53-kinds-of-records"></a>

#### 5.3 Kinds of Records

A forum works with four kinds of record, and none of them by itself changes anyone's standing:

- A [**forum case record**](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#forum-case-record) is the forum's file on the dispute before it. That Forum Case Record tracks:
  - the claims;
  - evidence;
  - routing choices;
  - temporary orders;
  - questions for ruling; and
  - final findings in that case.
- A **System or Institutional Certification Record** states a certification outcome and its limits on reliance. It is kept under [Chapter Ten §3 System Certification Record](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-system-certification-record) and [Chapter Thirteen §3 Institutional Certification Record](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-institutional-certification-record).
- A **Standing Record** is a Chapter Fourteen **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record**, as required by [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#4-question-1--what-happened) (see [Standing Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#standing-record)).
- An **Adoption Transition Record** is an adopter's published, running record of its Adoption Transition: its gates, Rights-Floor ledger, and interim authority. The adopter keeps it under [Article XXIX-A](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxix-a-phased-adoption-and-rights-floor-continuity) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*). A forum reviews it only on a contest under **section 5.6**; the forum does not keep it.

These two rules keep the kinds of record apart:

- A forum case record is **not** a Standing Record. None of the following by itself changes anyone's standing, role eligibility, trust status, recognition, or final standing effect:
  - filing a case;
  - assigning it to a forum family;
  - making intake notes;
  - issuing a temporary order;
  - recording an unresolved allegation;
  - discussing settlement; or
  - using a provisional routing label.
- A certification does **not** replace standing measurement. Its findings may count as **verified inputs** when Chapter Fifteen measures standing, but it does **not**, by itself, assign anyone a standing slot ([Chapter Ten §6 Relationship to Standing](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing); [Chapter Thirteen §6 Relationship to Standing](core_13.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#6-relationship-to-standing)).

These rules cover how forum work reaches Standing Records:

- A forum decision may become part of a Chapter Fourteen **contribution Standing Record** or **violation Standing Record** only when both of these hold:
  - it produces one of:
    - a verified contribution record;
    - a verified violation finding;
    - a **System or Institutional Certification Record**; or
    - another finding; and
  - that output is:
    - bounded;
    - traceable;
    - contestable; and
    - verified under Subcores 3-A to 3-C and any required review safeguards.
  - [Chapter Fourteen §4.7 Forum Boundary](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#47-forum-boundary) owns how verified contribution records and verified violation findings enter Standing Records.
- When one lead forum handles a mixed-stakes case, it must keep the **forum case record** clear enough that separate Chapter Fifteen contribution and violation measurements, any Chapter Twenty designation, and Chapter Sixteen standing effects can still be audited separately and recorded in axis-pure Standing Records.

<a id="54-contests-of-certification-records"></a>

#### 5.4 Contests of Certification Records

A certification contest is a [Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case). The paths and who takes each are in full in [Subcore 22-B §5 Certification Contest Paths](subcore_22.020_forum_case_seats_contest_routing.md#5-certification-contest-paths). Their guarantees are:

- **Four paths into the forums:** the published record challenge path, the component forum path, the lead-integrity path, and the escalation path, which are routes into the forums and not a new forum. Internal operator review, vendor attestation, or technical sign-off is not the published path.
- **The clock runs from receipt:** a challenge is logged on the Certification Record, and the tier windows under [Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_23.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) run from its receipt.
- **The lead never judges itself:** a challenge to how the lead forum ran the process goes through Integrity routing with a backup forum.
- **While it is pending:** the Integrity Forum stays lead unless a question is placed elsewhere; an unresolved timely Environment objection blocks final certification; direct access stays open where delay would endanger rights; and a credible challenge can reopen review.

<a id="55-contests-of-standing-records"></a>

#### 5.5 Contests of Standing Records

A Standing-Record contest is a [Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case). The full rules are in [Subcore 22-B §6 Standing-Record Contests](subcore_22.020_forum_case_seats_contest_routing.md#6-standing-record-contests). Their guarantees are:

- **Any time, from receipt:** a Standing Record may be contested before or after any filing; the challenge is logged and the Standing Record set *under challenge*, and the tier windows run from receipt.
- **Never back to its author:** a contest of a Standing Record opened by a Backup Record Authority goes to a forum with jurisdiction, never back to that authority.
- **Broad grounds:** a Standing Record may be contested as wrong, incomplete, stale, mis-scoped, based on an invalid finding, missing required context or a cross-reference, or used for a purpose it does not cover.
- **What the forum may do:** confirm it, require correction, order a new version, limit or pause reliance on it, send an underlying issue to the right forum, or send a constitutional question for ruling.
- **No reputation trial:** the forum reviews the Standing Record and how it is used, never the sentient's whole reputation, and does not merge contribution and violation review when only one axis-pure Standing Record is challenged.
- **Routed by the real issue:** the challenge goes to the forum family its real issue belongs to.

<a id="56-contests-of-adoption-transition-records"></a>

#### 5.6 Contests of Adoption Transition Records

A contest of an [Adoption Transition Record](subcore_03.075_band_continuity.md#adoption-transition-record) is a [Review Case](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#review-case). The full rules are in [Subcore 22-B §7 Adoption Transition Record Contests](subcore_22.020_forum_case_seats_contest_routing.md#7-adoption-transition-record-contests). Their guarantees are:

- **What may be contested:** an affected sentient, institution, community, or oversight body may contest a gate decision, an enforcement-readiness declaration, an unverifiable ledger entry, interim authority beyond its limits, or a provisional entry presented as settled.
- **Independent hearing:** no office in the adopter's own line, no ledger owner, no Adoption Transition authority, and no party that made the contested decision holds a seat on the review.
- **What the forum may and may not do:** it may confirm, correct, reopen a gate, order a declaration, limit reliance, refer, or send a question for ruling. It may not set the plan, its gate criteria, or its target, lower the fixed Rights-Floor target, or rerun the Adoption Transition.
- **Restraint continues:** a pending contest does not suspend the restraint duties that bind from the effective date, and the tier windows run from receipt.
- **Adoption Charter entries:** a contest of an entry in an adopter's Adoption Charter follows the same rules; a contest of a level of trust is governed by [Chapter Nineteen §3.4 Gatekeeper Checks Across Adopters and Levels of Trust](core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#34-gatekeeper-checks-across-adopters-and-levels-of-trust).

<a id="57-keeping-forum-case-records"></a>

#### 5.7 Keeping Forum Case Records

Forum Case Records are kept where the sentients who need them can reach them and where no single failure can erase them.

- The public part of a Forum Case Record is kept in the [Constitutional Library](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#constitutional-library). The restricted part is kept whole in the [Constitutional Archive](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#constitutional-archive), and every sealed item leaves a marker in the Library.
- A custodian independent of the forum that made the entries keeps the Library and the Archive.
- When a matter closes, each non-routine entry gets a physical backup, held offline and apart from the forum's own systems. Routine entries do not need one, and doubt resolves as non-routine.
- An [Essential Record](subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#essential-record) keeps its physical backup in at least three separate places in different regions. Standing Records at slot 5 and above are Essential Records ([Chapter Fourteen §8.5 Physical Backup and Certified Copies](core_14.000_standing_assessment.md#85-physical-backup-and-certified-copies)).
- Mechanics are in [CF-22.465 §7.1 Retention Classes and Physical Backup](corpus_forum/cf_22.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-22465-71-retention-classes-and-physical-backup) and [CF-22.465 §7.2 Constitutional Library and Constitutional Archive](corpus_forum/cf_22.465_standard_records_forms_evidence_artifacts.md#cf-22465-72-constitutional-library-and-constitutional-archive).

*In plain terms: when many **affected** **parties** share the same underlying harm pattern, forums may widen the case fairly; and no forum family gets to be the only final word when the accusation is essentially that this same family is rigged, conflicted, or hiding the ball — the rules send that to a different lead track with written backups.*

<a id="61-scope-expansion-and-representative-treatment"></a>

#### 6.1 Scope Expansion and Representative Treatment

When one sentient files a case, the forum **may** widen the proceeding to cover an affected **class**, **subclass**, or other group in the same situation.
- Expansion is available when the Forum Case Record shows any of the following:
  - a shared injury that matters;
  - the same unlawful practice;
  - the same decision rule;
  - shared dependence on the same conduct, system, or institutional choice; or
  - a **systems-alignment** issue with material effects upstream or downstream.
- The forum **should** expand when refusing to expand would predictably leave similarly situated **sentients** with no practical remedy.
- It **should** also expand when refusing to expand would predictably produce conflicting rulings, or would block relief that has to work at a **structural** level.
- Expanding the case does not take away the original claimant's **standing**. It also does not erase individual issues that still need person-by-person proof or remedy.

<a id="62-a-transfer-is-one-step-and-keeps-the-case"></a>

#### 6.2 A Transfer Is One Step and Keeps the Case

When a lawful order or published rule sends a matter to another forum, the move is one automated step: "go here."
- The case keeps its Forum Case Record, intake kind, tier, and the time already counted. Only the lead changes. Nothing is re-filed, re-opened, or re-proved.
- The same step updates the docket portal and alerts the parties, the representatives, and both forums. No one has to carry the matter across by hand.
- The order or published rule decides where the matter goes. The step does not decide that, and it cannot be used to steer a matter. The mechanics are in [CF-22.415 §12 Transfer Procedure](corpus_forum/cf_22.415_routing_operations_transfer_certification_representative_treatment.md#cf-22415-12-transfer-procedure).

<a id="63-cross-forum-anti-self-judging-rule"></a>

#### 6.3 Cross-Forum Anti-Self-Judging Rule

A **forum** family **must not** be the **sole** **final** **merits** forum for a claim whose **primary** issue is that same family's own **bias**, **capture**, **conflict**, **recusal failure**, **concealment**, **process abuse**, or comparable **integrity** violation.
- Primary-stakes routing still governs. The **independent** lead family for such claims is assigned as follows unless a more specific constitutional rule controls:
  - against **Constitutional** forums: **Integrity** first and **Institutional** as backup
  - against **Institutional** forums: **Constitutional** first and **Integrity** as backup
  - against **Integrity** forums: **Institutional** first and **Constitutional** as backup
  - against **Environment** forums: **Institutional** first and **Integrity** as backup
- This rule does **not** convert every case naming a forum into a special venue rule. It applies only where **anti-self-judging** protection is materially necessary to preserve **independence**, **contestability**, or **public** **trust**.

<a id="64-family-level-capture"></a>

#### 6.4 Family-Level Capture

Where credible evidence indicates **capture**, compromise, coercive control, coordinated obstruction, or structural dependency affecting a **forum family** as a whole, ordinary intra-family recusal, appeal, or continuity process is not sufficient by itself.
- The forum system must activate the following, sufficient to restore lawful, contestable merits adjudication:
  - family-level backup routing;
  - independent preservation of records; and
  - time-bound external review.
- The captured or compromised family must not control the activation record, restoration review, or final determination of its own restored independence.
- Backup authority remains limited to what is necessary for:
  - lawful merits adjudication;
  - emergency relief;
  - record custody; and
  - restoration.
- It does not permanently absorb the captured family's jurisdiction or displace **Constitutional** certification where structural remedy or constitutional meaning is materially at issue.

<a id="65-capacity-failure-is-heard-outside-the-starved-body"></a>

#### 6.5 Capacity Failure Is Heard Outside the Starved Body

A claim that a forum family, remedy system, or standing-integration function is under capacity — designed backlog, inaccessible intake, chronic underfunding, chronic milestone failure, or a tripped [Chapter Eighteen §3.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#34-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) remedy-parity tripwire — is an anti-self-judging matter. The body whose capacity is in question must not be the sole finder of fact on its own capacity.
- The **Integrity** family is the default lead for capacity-failure claims, with the **section 6** backup pairs applying where Integrity is itself the body in question.
- Any affected party, protected reporter, or self-organized group under [Chapter Two §17.4 Aligned Self-Organization](core_02.040_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#174-aligned-self-organization) may file a capacity-failure claim. It is heard on a Tier B window under **Chapter Twenty-Three section 5** unless the Forum Case Record shows Tier A stakes.
- The body in question must supply its published [Chapter Eighteen §3.4 Remedy Parity and Lock Preconditions](core_18.000_violation_consequences.md#34-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) and **Chapter Twenty-Three section 5** backlog figures, and may give evidence, but must not control the capacity-failure finding or the corrective order.
- A capacity-failure finding is a [Chapter Nineteen §5.2 Remedy-System Durability](core_19.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#52-remedy-system-durability) durability failure. It routes corrective orders to the [primary-stakes](#4-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) family responsible for staffing and funding and, where the pattern is chronic or concealed, opens the ordinary Chapter Fourteen path.
