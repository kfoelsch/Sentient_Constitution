Chapter Nineteen is the constitutional owner of **how outcomes are carried out**: who may do it, within what time, and the safeguards that keep it from being avoided or delayed.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: earlier chapters decide what happened and what follows, and the forum chapter says who rules on disputes. This chapter is about what comes next: making sure the outcome is carried out, on time, by someone, and that it cannot be dodged.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Nineteen states how the outcomes of the standing pipeline and of forum review are carried out, so that an outcome counts only when it is actually delivered.

**Role:** Where this chapter sits:

- It takes every outcome of the standing pipeline and of forum review as fixed. It does not reopen them and it adds no new kind of consequence.
- It applies the [Chapter Two §6.1](core_02.000_values_principles.md#61-correction-and-remedy) (*Correction and Remedy*) principle to the moment of delivery. [Chapter Thirteen §5 Enforcement Realism and Remedy Systems](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#5-enforcement-realism-and-remedy-systems) asks whether real institutions can deliver standing effects and redress; this chapter states what delivery itself requires.
- Carrying an outcome out is not a second judgment. Whoever carries it out acts within the outcome and is bound by the separation of duties in [Chapter Six](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#chapter-six-functional-independence-and-separation-of-duties).
- Refusal, delay, and evasion are matters the forums can hear under [Chapter Sixteen](core_16.000_forum.md#chapter-sixteen-standards-routing-and-forum-cases), and failing to enforce can itself be misconduct under [Subcore 14-A §6 Shielding Misconduct and Failing to Enforce](subcore_14.010_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#6-shielding-misconduct-and-failing-to-enforce).

**Chapter scope:** The chapter covers:

- who carries outcomes out;
- what counts as an outcome that must be carried out, and from what moment;
- time limits for carrying out, and what follows when they are missed;
- refusal, stalling, and non-compliance, including by the institution that is meant to carry the outcome out;
- evasion by cost, delay, borders, restructuring, or a change of formal identity, read together with the [Chapter Twenty-Two §5 Anti-Evasion Clause](core_22.000_non_regression.md#5-anti-evasion-clause); and
- limits on the carrying out itself, so that an outcome is delivered as found and no further; and
- routing to special enforcement for money and seizure, potential violence, and root-cause evaluation.

How outcomes are reached stays with the chapters that produce them. Staffing, funding, backlog, and procedure stay with adopted implementation text.

No enforcement text has moved here from Chapters Eighteen, Nineteen, or Twenty-Two: the sections below cite those rules where they stand. Money and seizure, potential violence, and root-cause evaluation are routed to [Chapter Twenty](core_20.000_special_enforcement.md#chapter-twenty-special-enforcement) under [§8 Routing to Special Enforcement](#8-routing-to-special-enforcement).

<a id="2-why-enforcement-exists"></a>
### 2. Why Enforcement Exists

*In plain terms: a remedy that is ordered but never delivered teaches everyone that harm pays. This chapter exists so that an outcome is real only when it has actually happened.*

A system gets more of whatever it rewards ([What Systems Reward](core_00.000_preamble.md#what-systems-reward)). If an outcome can be ignored, delayed until it no longer matters, or dodged by moving money, offices, or borders, then the system rewards ignoring, delaying, and dodging, whatever the outcome says. The chapters before this one decide what happened and what follows. This chapter carries those decisions to the point where they change something, and stops there: it adds no new kind of consequence, and it never lets carrying out an outcome become a second punishment.

**Grounded in:**

- Preamble:
  - [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00.000_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad): the legs this chapter carries:
    - [Accountability](subcore_03.040_apex_accountability_leg.md#accountability): an outcome counts only when it has actually happened;
    - [Timeliness](subcore_03.065_apex_timeliness_leg.md#timeliness): ordered remedies are delivered without open-ended delay;
  - [Two Constitutional Aims](core_00.000_preamble.md#two-constitutional-aims): both aims, which this chapter serves:
    - [Flourishing](core_00.000_preamble.md#flourishing): sentients receive the remedies they are owed;
    - [Continuity](core_00.000_preamble.md#continuity): harm stops paying, which protects shared systems;
- Chapter Two principles:
  - [§6.1 Correction and Remedy](core_02.000_values_principles.md#61-correction-and-remedy);
  - [§13.1.3 Proportionality](core_02.020_interaction_interpretation.md#1313-proportionality);
- Chapter Four Articles:
  - [Article XIV-B](core_04.020_rights_part_c.md#article-xiv-b-right-to-redress-and-remedy) (*Right to Redress and Remedy*);
  - [Article XXI-B](core_04.030_rights_part_d.md#article-xxi-b-restriction-floors) (*Restriction Floors*);
  - [Article XXVII-C](core_04.040_rights_part_e.md#article-xxvii-c-timely-resolution-and-anti-delay-floor) (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*).

<a id="3-what-must-be-carried-out-and-from-when"></a>
### 3. What Must Be Carried Out, and From When

*In plain terms: anything a forum or the standing pipeline orders someone to do must be done, starting from a stated moment. Asking for review does not pause it unless a forum says so.*

This section says which outcomes must be carried out and from what moment they bind.

- **What counts as an outcome:** an outcome is any of the following that requires someone to act or to stop acting:
  - a final standing effect ([Chapter Thirteen §3 Final Standing Effect](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#3-final-standing-effect));
  - a remedy or correction order ([Chapter Twelve §3.1 Remedy and Correction](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#31-remedy-and-correction));
  - a standing lock or special lock ([Chapter Twelve §4 Lock Design and Enforcement](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#4-lock-design-and-enforcement));
  - a forum order, including an interim or emergency measure ([Chapter Seventeen §5.1 Emergency Measures and Continuation Burden](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#51-emergency-measures-and-continuation-burden)); and
  - a certification outcome that requires a system or institution to act.
- **From when:** an interim or emergency measure binds when it is issued. Any other outcome binds from the date it states, or, where it states none, from the date it becomes final.
- **Review does not pause it:** a pending challenge, appeal, or secondary review does not suspend an outcome unless the forum hearing it orders a stay, with stated reasons and an end date.

<a id="4-who-carries-outcomes-out"></a>
### 4. Who Carries Outcomes Out

*In plain terms: every outcome names who must act and by when. The one who carries it out is not the one who decided it, and nobody may hide behind not having been named.*

This section says who carries an outcome out and how that seat stays separate from the one that decided it.

- **Named in the outcome:** every outcome names the actor bound by it and the enforcement body that carries it out or verifies that it was carried out, with the time limit under [§5 Time Limits and Missed Deadlines](#5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines).
- **When no one is named:** where an outcome names no enforcement body to carry it out, the forum that issued it names one on the request of any party, inside the intake target of the matter's tier ([Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline)). The gap does not excuse anyone bound in the meantime.
- **Separate seats:** the enforcement body that carries an outcome out acts within the outcome. It is not the body that decided it, and it does not judge a challenge to its own performance ([Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement)).
- **Reaching the bound actor:** where the bound actor has a System Charter or Institutional Charter, the outcome is served through the enforcement contact that System Charter or Institutional Charter publishes ([Chapter Five §5.1 What a System Charter States](core_05.000_charters.md#51-what-a-system-charter-states), item 10), and serving it there counts as serving the bound actor.
- **Institutional detail:** how institutions are sanctioned and supervised when they are the bound actor is set out in [CI-19.205](corpus_institutions/ci_19.205_institutional_accountability_sanctions.md#ci-19205-institutional-accountability-and-sanctions) (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*); remedy-system capacity is set out in [CI-19.210](corpus_institutions/ci_19.210_remedy_systems_institutional_redress_capacity.md#ci-19210-remedy-systems-and-institutional-redress-capacity) (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*).

<a id="5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines"></a>
### 5. Time Limits and Missed Deadlines

*In plain terms: outcomes run on the same published clocks as everything else, and a missed deadline is itself something a forum can act on.*

This section says which clocks apply to carrying an outcome out and what follows when one is missed.

- **The clocks:** remedy commencement, correction milestones, lock review, and reassessment follow [Chapter Thirteen §5.5 Timely Implementation](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#55-timely-implementation), the tier windows of [Chapter Seventeen §5 Timely Resolution, Materiality Tiers, and Anti-Delay Discipline](core_17.000_forum_families_escalation_timeliness_support.md#5-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline), and **Article XXVII-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*). This chapter adds no clock table.
- **A missed time limit is non-performance:** any sentient affected by the outcome, or the body that verifies it, may bring the missed limit to the forum that issued the outcome. The forum may:
  - order completion by a new date;
  - order another enforcement body to carry the outcome out, at the cost of the actor bound by it where that actor can lawfully bear it ([Chapter Thirteen §5.1 Remediation Capacity and Funding](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#51-remediation-capacity-and-funding)); and
  - refer the failure for a violation finding, and, where an authority refuses or suppresses enforcement, under [Subcore 14-A §6.2 Enforcement Refusal, Suppression, or Non-Prosecution Conduct](subcore_14.010_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#62-enforcement-refusal-suppression-or-non-prosecution-conduct).
- **Not carried by the sentient harmed:** delay in carrying an outcome out is never counted against the sentient it protects, and does not shorten any window that sentient has.

<a id="6-refusal-stalling-and-evasion"></a>
### 6. Refusal, Stalling, and Evasion

*In plain terms: you cannot escape an outcome by refusing, stalling, going broke, moving, or changing your name. The rules that stop this already exist; this section gathers them.*

This section gathers the rules that stop an outcome from being refused, stalled, or escaped.

- **Non-performance by anyone:** refusing or stalling an outcome is non-performance under [§5 Time Limits and Missed Deadlines](#5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines), whoever does it, including the institution meant to carry the outcome out. Where an enforcement, oversight, or referral authority does it knowingly, recklessly, corruptly, selectively, or by pretext, it may be anti-constitutional misconduct under [Subcore 14-A §6.2 Enforcement Refusal, Suppression, or Non-Prosecution Conduct](subcore_14.010_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#62-enforcement-refusal-suppression-or-non-prosecution-conduct).
- **Cost and insolvency:** expense, insolvency, restructuring, or asset transfer does not by itself defeat a verified obligation, and successors inherit proportionate duties ([Chapter Thirteen §5.1 Remediation Capacity and Funding](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#51-remediation-capacity-and-funding)).
- **Borders:** obligations stay actionable across boundaries wherever applicable law permits ([Chapter Thirteen §5.3 Cross-Jurisdiction Continuity](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#53-cross-jurisdiction-continuity)).
- **Change of form or identity:** forums look through formal structure to functional control ([Chapter Thirteen §5.4 Anti-Evasion and Look-through Authority](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#54-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority)), read with [Chapter Twenty-Two §5 Anti-Evasion Clause](core_22.000_non_regression.md#5-anti-evasion-clause), which this chapter does not narrow.
- **Capacity to deliver:** adopters keep the capacity to deliver outcomes, including remedy parity ([Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Remedy-System Durability](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#52-remedy-system-durability)).

<a id="7-delivered-as-found-and-no-further"></a>
### 7. Delivered as Found, and No Further

*In plain terms: an outcome is carried out exactly as decided. Carrying it out harder, wider, or longer than decided is itself a wrong that can be challenged and repaired.*

This section sets the limits on carrying an outcome out.

- **No wider than the outcome:** carrying an outcome out may not reach further, last longer, or cost the bound actor more than the outcome requires.
- **No reach into body or mind:** no outcome is carried out by intruding into a body or a mind, the limit [Chapter Twelve §4.4.7 Capacity Locks](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#447-capacity-locks) and the Care and Stewardship Lock already set for those locks. A suspension or a health-crisis intervention follows its own floor under **Article XXI-B** (*Restriction Floors*) or **Article VII-C** (*Health Crisis and Involuntary-Intervention Floor*), not this section.
- <a id="mal-enforcement"></a>**Mal-enforcement can be challenged:** carrying an outcome out further, longer, or more harshly than it requires, or outside the limits this chapter and [Chapter Twenty](core_20.000_special_enforcement.md#chapter-twenty-special-enforcement) set on how it is carried out, is mal-enforcement. The bound actor, or any sentient affected, may challenge it before the forum that issued the outcome, and mal-enforcement found on that challenge is remedied under [Chapter Twelve §3.1 Remedy and Correction](core_12.000_violation_consequences.md#31-remedy-and-correction) on the same terms as any other harm.
- **The outcome ends when it ends:** when an outcome is complete, or a lock ends or is lifted, carrying it out stops at once, and the Forum Case Record says so ([Chapter Thirteen §4 Restoration and Reassessment](core_13.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-restoration-and-reassessment)).

<a id="8-routing-to-special-enforcement"></a>
### 8. Routing to Special Enforcement

*In plain terms: freezing or seizing money or property, anything that could turn violent, and fixing the root causes of enforcement failure need rules of their own. They sit in Chapter Twenty. This section says which matters go there.*

This section says which steps go to special enforcement under [Chapter Twenty](core_20.000_special_enforcement.md#chapter-twenty-special-enforcement).

- **What special enforcement covers:**
  - freezing, seizing, taking, or recovering money, funds, or other assets;
  - any use or threat of physical force, and any carrying out of an outcome where violence is reasonably foreseeable; and
  - evaluating the root causes of enforcement failure, so that they can be removed rather than repeated.
- **Routing:**
  - A step that carrying out an outcome requires is routed to special enforcement when it involves money or assets, or force, as listed above. The rest of the outcome stays under this chapter.
  - Where the same kind of outcome keeps missing its time limits, or refusals or stalling recur at the same body or across the same class of systems or institutions, the forum that sees the pattern refers it for root-cause evaluation. Individual cases continue under [§5 Time Limits and Missed Deadlines](#5-time-limits-and-missed-deadlines) meanwhile.
  - Where it is unclear whether a step belongs to special enforcement, it is treated as belonging there.
  - A routed step starts from an [Enforcement Record](core_20.000_special_enforcement.md#enforcement-record) that the issuing forum issues under Chapter Twenty-Six §3.1 Whether to Act.
- **Both apply:** special-enforcement rules add to sections 3 to 7 and never lower them.
- **Special-enforcement rules:** a step routed to Chapter Twenty-Six is taken under its [§3 Shared Floors](core_20.000_special_enforcement.md#3-shared-floors) and the section for its kind of step.
