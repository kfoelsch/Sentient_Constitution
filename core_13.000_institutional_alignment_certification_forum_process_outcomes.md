Chapter Thirteen is the constitutional owner of **forum process and outcomes of institutional alignment certification**.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: the same independent forums, in the same order, review the institution, and the institution never chooses its own reviewer. A published outcome never decides whether the institution may exist or operate.*

**Purpose.** Chapter Thirteen states how forums supervise institutional alignment certification: the four seats in an institutional certification, forum roles and sequence, and forums, certifiers, and record offices treated as institutions (**section 2**). It then states the outcomes: go-live for an Institution, defects and correction, remediation readiness, denial and revocation, and their effects (**section 3**). An outcome decides what the Institution may say about itself, who is warned, and how much the outcome counts when the Institution's Systems are reviewed. The forums can also limit the specific practice or authority the review found at issue.

**Role.** Institutional certification and System Alignment Certification are two certifications joined by a link, not one inside the other (**Chapter Twelve section 1**):

- It takes the evaluation from [Chapter Twelve](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-twelve-institutional-alignment-certification--evaluation) as fixed.
- It runs the same forum process as [Chapter Ten](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#chapter-ten-system-alignment-certification--forum-process-and-outcomes), applied to an Institution.
- It cites System Certification Records by link and does not redo them.
- [Chapter Fourteen](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#chapter-fourteen-institutional-alignment-certification--records-recertification-challenge-and-standing) takes over from here, with the Institutional Certification Record, recertification, challenge, and the standing bridge.

**Chapter scope.** The chapter covers:

- the forum process (**section 2**); and
- the outcomes (**section 3**).

The chapter leaves:

- evaluation to Chapter Twelve;
- the Institutional Certification Record, recertification, challenge, and standing bridge to Chapter Fourteen; and
- every System-level decision to [Chapters Nine through Eleven](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#chapter-nine-system-alignment-certification--evaluation).

<a id="21-four-seats-in-an-institutional-certification"></a>

#### 2.1 Four Seats in an Institutional Certification

This subsection is how this Chapter applies the [Chapter Eight §2 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#2-four-seat-constitutional-requirement), and it is the statement [Chapter Eight §9 Relationship to Later Processes](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#9-relationship-to-later-processes) requires of this process. An institutional certification is a [Materially Binding Act](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), so every certification pass has the four seats. This Chapter adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Eight. [Chapter Ten §2.1.1 Four Seats in a Certification](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#211-four-seats-in-a-certification) applies with these substitutions:

- **[Initiating Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#initiating-seat):** the Institution's governing authority.
  - **Role:** it requests certification or is the proponent who must supply the evidence.
  - **A certification pass opened on a trigger:** it is opened by the lead forum's order, and the governing authority remains the proponent.
- **[Verify-or-Authorize Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#verify-or-authorize-seat):** the lead forum for the outcome, and each component authority for its own finding, assigned under [§2.2 Forum Roles and Sequence](#22-forum-roles-and-sequence) and Chapter Twenty-Three.
  - **Seat placement:** the [Charter Role Map](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) does not place this seat.
  - **What the Institution cannot do:** choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove its own verifying authority.
  - **Who may hold it:** no one who works for the Institution, is owned by it, or answers to it may hold this seat for its certification, and everyone who holds it must have no interest that the outcome decides.
  - **Systems:** the same rule applies to a system certification ([Chapter Ten §2.1.1 Four Seats in a Certification](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#211-four-seats-in-a-certification), *Outside the operator*).
- **[Record Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#record-seat):** the office the Institutional Charter's Charter Role Map places.
  - **Role:** it opens, amends, preserves, and publishes the Institutional Certification Record.
  - **Position:** it sits outside the Institution's [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line).
- **[Contest Seat](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#contest-seat):** the published record challenge path with the competent review or backup forum.
  - **Position:** it is placed the same way and outside the same line.

**Self-placement.** The Institution publishes the map that places the record seat and the contest seat for its own certification.

- **Invalid placement:** a placement of either seat inside the Institution's own Material Control Line is not a valid placement for an Institutional Certification Record in which the Institution is the subject, whatever the map says ([Chapter Eight §3 Independence, Conflict, and Control Lines](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-independence-conflict-and-control-lines)).
- **Routing:** the certification pass then routes as [Chapter Eight §5 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#5-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution) provides. The seat's work goes to the independent route the Charter Role Map names, and the seat does not pass to the Institution or to anyone in its control line.
- **Finding:** the defective placement of the seat is itself a finding under [§3 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-institution-certification-evaluation).

**Merged hosting.** [Chapter Eight §6 Proportional Scaling and Merged Hosting](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#6-proportional-scaling-and-merged-hosting) applies unchanged. This Chapter adds no merged hosting and removes none.

<a id="22-forum-roles-and-sequence"></a>

#### 2.2 Forum Roles and Sequence

The supervisory sequence in [Chapter Ten §2.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#22-supervisory-sequence) applies, from the challenge path first through the hand-off to Phases IV and V, with these changes. [Chapter Ten §2.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles) applies as well, except where this section changes it:

- **Lead:** An **Integrity** forum leads each certification pass.
  - **Exception:** Chapter Twenty-Three may assign a different lawful lead for the primary stake.
  - **Record seat:** the lead forum directs the record seat holder and does not hold the record seat on the same act.
- **Institutional Forums hold the core components:**
  - **Necessary party:** an Institution is always a necessary party to its own certification.
  - **What a component is:** a component is one part of the certification, and a forum that holds **component authority** over it checks the evidence and authority on that part against the governing standard and issues its own finding, within that authority. That forum's part of the certification pass is a **component evaluation** ([Chapter Ten §2.1 Forum Supervision and Component Roles](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#21-forum-supervision-and-component-roles)).
  - **Held by Institutional Forums:** Institutional Forums hold component authority for institutional mandate, supervised scope, institution class, institutional-duty compliance ([§3 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), item 8), and the Rights Floor component for the Institution's own conduct ([Chapter Twenty-Three §4.3 Institutional Forums](core_23.000_forum.md#43-institutional-forums), [Chapter Ten §2.1.6 Institutional Forums](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#216-institutional-forums)).
- **Other components:**
  - **Technical Forums:** supply evidence standards and any measurement the incentive or aggregate findings need.
  - **Environment Forums:** supply a component finding where the Institution's aggregate ecological exposure across its Systems is material.
  - **Sentient Forums:** supply the community-governance finding where the Institution's own conduct materially affects how a community governs itself.
  - **Affected members and communities:** submit evidence through the assigned forums.
  - **Constitutional Forums:** keep the meaning, validity, and structural-remedy questions.
- **Systems are referred, not decided:**
  - **Where it goes:** a component question that belongs to one System goes to that System's own certification pass, and not to the Institution's.
  - **What the lead forum may do:** it may refer the question, send it for ruling, or [stay](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#stay) it, as [Chapter Ten §2.2 Supervisory Sequence](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#22-supervisory-sequence) provides.
    - **Refer:** hand the question to the forum family it belongs to.
    - **Send for ruling:** send it to another forum as a question for ruling and wait for the answer. The matter stays with the lead forum.
    - **Stay:** pause it.
  - **What it may not do:** decide a System's component question in the Institution's certification pass. That question is decided in the System's own certification pass.
- **Participation, urgent protection, and contest:** Chapter Ten applies in full to an Institution's certification pass, with the System Certification Record read as the Institutional Certification Record.
  - **Participation during review** ([Chapter Ten §2.2.4 Participation during Review](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#224-participation-during-review)): the assigned forums give affected sentients practicable, accessible ways to submit evidence and concerns during evaluation and forum review. Every submission goes into the review submissions log kept by the record seat holder.
  - **Who can submit:** the Institution's affected members, workers, users, and communities, current and reasonably foreseeable, are among the affected sentients who must have an accessible way to submit evidence. Chapter Ten identifies them by actual and reasonably foreseeable effects and dependency, including before deployment.
  - **Staff and confidentiality:** Staff are the likeliest to know of a problem in an Institution. A submitter, staff included, may ask to stay anonymous or confidential. Until the lead forum decides, the submitter's identity is withheld from everyone, the Institution included, as [Chapter Ten §2.2.4 Participation during Review](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#224-participation-during-review) provides.
  - **Urgent protection** ([Chapter Ten §2.2.3 Urgent Protection](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#223-urgent-protection)): a stay, interim protection, or reliance limit may be ordered before the sequence finishes. The sequence still runs, and the measure is entered on the Institutional Certification Record.
  - **Contest** ([Chapter Ten §2.3 Contestability Chain](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#23-contestability-chain)): affected parties can challenge the Institutional Certification Record, and a challenge goes to the level where the dispute actually lives.
  - **Anti-bypass** ([Chapter Ten §2.4 Anti-Bypass](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#24-anti-bypass)): the supervisory sequence and the contestability chain may not be defeated by hidden sub-records, by routing challenges only to captured internal bodies, by letting a challenge end at the lowest administrative layer, or by treating a component's silence as approval.

<a id="23-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions"></a>

#### 2.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions

*In plain terms: the bodies that do the certifying are institutions too, so they cannot certify each other in a circle. A body that is itself Not Certified gives up its seats in other certifications.*

A forum host, a certifying body, an Institutional Certification Record office, a verifying authority, or any other body that takes part in certifications is an [Institution](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#institution) where it meets that definition, and this Chapter applies to it as to any other Institution of its class.

- **No self-judging:** Its own pass is led and reviewed by a forum outside it and outside its [Material Control Line](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#material-control-line). Where the question is the forum family's own bias, capture, conflict, or process abuse, the backup routing of the [Chapter Twenty-Three §3 Transfer, consolidation, and coordination](core_23.000_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture) cross-forum anti-self-judging rule and **CF-23.435 §6** (*no self-investigation*) applies.
- **No circle:** A body does not hold a seat in the certification of a body that holds a seat in its own, directly or through a chain of bodies that returns to the first within one recertification cycle. A certification pass that would close a circle routes to a body outside it.
- **Seats stay apart:** Taking part in another body's certification, including by holding a seat in it, gives a body no advantage of its own.
  - **No hold over the other body:** it gives no power over how that body responds to its certification, such as its answer to findings, certification conditions, or challenges.
  - **No preference in its own certification:** it gives no favorable treatment in the body's own certification.
- **No seat while Not Certified:** While an Institution is Not Certified ([§3 Certification Outcomes](#3-certification-outcomes)), it may not hold the verify-or-authorize, record, or contest seat in another body's certification, and it may not host a forum or certifying body. Any seat placements it holds are moved elsewhere in a new, dated version of the Role Map.

*In plain terms: a certification review ends with a published outcome for the Institution. The outcome never decides whether the Institution may exist or operate. It decides what the Institution may say about itself, who is warned, and how much the outcome counts when the Institution's Systems are reviewed. The forums can also limit the specific practice or authority the review found wrong, and a Not Certified Institution may not hold a seat in another body's certification. They cannot bar the Institution from operating or from forming Systems.*

This section is **Phase IV, Decide** for an Institution: the forums reach the outcome here, after the review in [§2 Forum Process](#2-forum-process).

An Institution can receive the same outcomes a System can. [Chapter Ten §3 Certification Outcomes](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#3-certification-outcomes) defines them:

- provisional certification and full certification, which are the two **statuses** (a provisional certification is reviewed again sooner);
- conditional certification (certification that carries certification conditions);
- deferred certification (the decision is put off);
- non-certification, which is a **denial** (certification is refused);
- withdrawal, which is a **revocation** (a certification already held is taken back);
- expiration (the certification runs out);
- fulfillment of certification conditions (the certification conditions have been met); and
- release from certification conditions (the certification conditions no longer apply, so they are lifted without having been met).

An Institution that holds neither provisional nor full certification is **Not Certified**. The rules for statuses and certification conditions are those of Chapter Ten §3 Certification Outcomes and [§3.3 Provisional and Full Certification](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#33-provisional-and-full-certification). Under them, a certification condition ends, by fulfillment or by release, only after independent verification.

<a id="31-go-live-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 3.1 Go-Live for an Institution

*In plain terms: a System cannot go live until it is certified. An Institution can form and run without a certificate, but it waits for its recertification before it exercises new material authority. An outcome for an Institution does three narrower things: it sets the Institution's public status, it attaches certification conditions to the specific problem found, and it counts as evidence when the Institution's Systems are reviewed.*

[Chapter Ten §3.1 Go-Live Gate](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-go-live-gate) says a System does not go live until it is certified, the outcome has been published with the challenge path open, the notice interval has run, and every required certification condition has been independently verified.

An Institution has no such gate on forming or running ([§1.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#12-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)). It forms, publishes its Institutional Charter, begins to exercise authority, and keeps operating without holding a certification, and no outcome grants or withholds that right. New material authority is the one exception. The Institution waits for its recertification to clear that authority before it exercises it ([§3.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#322-recertification-for-new-material-authority)). Everything else it does continues, and any certification conditions required before the new authority is exercised are verified first ([§3.1.1 Certification Conditions](#311-certification-conditions)).

**Go-live still gates the Systems.** A new System, or a material expansion of one, goes live only through its own certification, whatever the Institution's status. The Institution's status can inform that decision, but it can never make it:

- **Evidence, not an outcome for the System:** When one of the Institution's Systems is up for certification, the Institution's status is one piece of evidence about how that System is run, what drives it, and whether sentients can challenge it ([Chapter Nine §3.1 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline)). On its own it never certifies a System and never blocks one.
- **A higher bar after a denial or revocation:** If the Institution has been denied or has lost its certification, anyone asking to launch a new System or make a major expansion that depends on it has more to prove. They have to show that what they are relying on about the Institution is still solid enough, even with that finding on record ([§3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution), effect item 3).

<a id="311-certification-conditions"></a>

##### 3.1.1 Certification Conditions

A conditional certification of an Institution is written as [Chapter Ten §3.1 Go-Live Gate](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-go-live-gate) (*Certification conditions and bounded authorization*) requires, with these limits:

- **Tied to the problem found:** A certification condition or reliance limit attaches to the authority, practice, or structure the finding is about.
- **Fully specified:** Every certification condition spells out five things:
  - Who is responsible for it.
  - When it is due, or how often it repeats if it is ongoing.
  - What proof will show it was met.
  - Who independently checks that proof.
  - What happens if it is missed.
- **No reaching past the problem:** It cannot require the Institution to stop forming Systems, to ask leave to keep operating, or to change anything the findings do not touch.
- **Certification conditions before new authority:** The certification conditions that [Chapter Ten §3.1 Go-Live Gate](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#31-go-live-gate) requires before go-live apply to an Institution only for new material authority. They must be independently verified before the Institution exercises that authority. They never make its existing operation wait.
- **How it ends:** A certification condition ends by fulfillment or by release, as Chapter Ten §3.1 Go-Live Gate (*How a certification condition ends*) sets out, and only after independent verification.

<a id="312-other-effects-of-an-outcome"></a>

##### 3.1.2 Other Effects of an Outcome

Apart from the Systems and the certification conditions, an outcome has only these effects:

- **Status on the Institutional Certification Record:** The Institution's status is published, and anyone affected can challenge it. An Institution that is Not Certified may not describe itself as certified.
- **A warning to those who rely on the Institution:** Where a finding means their reliance is not safe, they are told.
- **How soon the next review comes:** A provisional certification has a shorter certification interval than a full certification.
- **Denial and revocation:** The effects are in [§3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution).
- **Reopening and the cascade:** A review can be reopened, and a failed System certification can affect the Institution. See [§3 Recertification and Reopening](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#3-recertification-and-reopening).
- **Standing:** An outcome can touch a sentient's standing only through [§5 Relationship to Standing](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing).

Sanctions, dissolution, and institutional-failure consequences stay with **CI-19.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) and **CI-19.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*). This Chapter creates none of them.

<a id="32-certification-defects-and-correction"></a>

#### 3.2 Certification Defects and Correction

*In plain terms: a certification defect is a real problem the evaluation turns up, not a paperwork slip. When one is found in an Institution, the repair rules are the same as for a System.*

[Chapter Ten §3.2 Certification Defects and Correction](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#32-certification-defects-and-correction) applies when a certification pass finds a defect in an Institution. The findings under [§2 Institution Class Evaluation](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#2-institution-class-evaluation), [§3 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), [§4 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-evaluations-where-implicated), and [§5 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations) take the place of the Chapter Nine evaluations that Chapter Ten lists. Chapter Ten §3.2 Certification Defects and Correction sets:

- the choices open to the forums;
- the statement of what is affected, the interim protection and reliance limits, and the corrective work required; and
- the rule that the correction is independently verified before it supports fulfillment of certification conditions.

Two points are specific to an Institution:

- **No reaching past the defect:** A certification condition cannot reach beyond the defect ([§3.1 Go-Live for an Institution](#31-go-live-for-an-institution)).
- **No ban on operating:** The last paragraph of Chapter Ten §3.2 Certification Defects and Correction says a System that is Not Certified cannot begin new operation. That is a rule for Systems. It does not apply to an Institution ([§1.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#12-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)).

<a id="33-remediation-readiness-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 3.3 Remediation Readiness for an Institution

*In plain terms: when a review finds a problem in an Institution, the forums ask the same question they ask about a System: is it both willing and able to fix the problem? If the honest answer is no, its certification is refused or taken back. By itself, that cannot stop the Institution from existing or operating. It takes away the Institution's claim to be certified, warns those who rely on it, weakens the support its Systems can draw from its good status, and keeps it out of the seats that certify others.*

[Chapter Ten §3.3 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#33-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct) applies to an Institution with these changes:

- **Who does what:**
  - **The Institution's governing authority** is the proponent: the party that carries the case that the Institution is ready. It supplies the evidence. It does not judge its own readiness.
  - **The lead Integrity Forum** decides the finding: whether the Institution is both willing and able to fix the problem ([Chapter Ten §3.3.4 Findings](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#334-findings) lists the possible findings).
  - **Institutional Forums** hold the evidence on governance, authority, and incentives, and bring it to the lead forum.
  - **Technical Forums** hold any capacity evidence that needs measuring, and bring it to the lead forum ([§2.2 Forum Roles and Sequence](#22-forum-roles-and-sequence)).
- **Willingness:** In addition to the factors in [Chapter Ten §3.3.1 Willingness](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#331-willingness), the forums look at whether the Institution is:
  - changing its own reward structure, where that structure caused the finding;
  - replacing or disclosing an office-holder found not authentic (for example, a holder who fronts for an undisclosed controller) under [§3 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-institution-certification-evaluation) (item 7);
  - restoring an independent seat placement on its [Charter Role Map](core_05.040_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map);
  - correcting its Institutional Charter or its portfolio of Systems; and
  - answering for any System whose cause finding traced back to the Institution ([§3.5 Cascade from System Certifications](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#35-cascade-from-system-certifications)).
- **Capacity:** In addition to the factors in [Chapter Ten §3.3.2 Capacity](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#332-capacity), the forums look at whether:
  - the offices that must act have the authority to do so (**CI-8.205 §3** (*Authority Chain and Delegation Controls*));
  - the Institution has the funds, staff, and succession plan for the work (**CI-24.205 §1** (*Shared Staffing and Competency Requirement*); **CI-20.205** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*)); and
  - an independent verifying authority can test the correction, with the access and the time it is actually given ([Chapter Ten §3.3.2 Capacity](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#332-capacity), *Verifiability*). A correction nobody can verify is not capacity.
- **One System:** An Institution that runs exactly one System gets one readiness check, in the one-System evaluation. The readiness check states its denial grounds separately for the System and for the Institution, as [§3.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#34-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) requires.

<a id="34-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 3.4 Denial and Revocation for an Institution

*In plain terms: denial means certification is refused, and revocation means a certification already held is taken back. Both are real consequences. Neither shuts the Institution down.*

[Chapter Ten §3.4 Denial and Revocation](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#34-denial-and-revocation) applies to an Institution with these changes:

- **Grounds and process:** Chapter Ten §3.4 Denial and Revocation supplies these, unchanged:
  - **Grounds** ([Chapter Ten §3.4.2 When Denial or Revocation Is Required](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#342-when-denial-or-revocation-is-required)):
    - **Denial,** where no certification is held: the readiness check finds the Institution unwilling, or neither willing nor able; or willing but not able, with no narrower certified scope, support, or handover that closes the gap in time; or the one deferral has run and readiness is still not shown.
    - **Revocation,** where certification is held: the same readiness findings, after notice and a chance to answer. Also a certification condition missed twice or materially violated, evidence shown to be false, fabricated, or concealed, or a required proof step not done in its window.
    - **No conditional certification instead:** when one of these denial or revocation grounds is met, the forum must deny or revoke. It may not answer the denial or revocation ground by granting or continuing certification with certification conditions attached.
  - **Protection first** ([Chapter Ten §3.4.3 Protection First](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#343-protection-first)):
    - **What it acts on:** denial and revocation act on the certification status. They do not by themselves shut the Institution down.
    - **Before either takes effect:** the forum states the interim protection and the reliance limits.
  - **Process** ([Chapter Ten §3.4.4 Process](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#344-process)):
    - **Given beforehand:** before a denial or revocation, the Institution is given notice of the finding and the evidence, a short window to answer or to show the proof step, and the chance to contest the finding through the contestability chain.
    - **Reasons:** the decision states its reasons on the Institutional Certification Record.
    - **Urgent protection** ([Chapter Ten §2.2.3 Urgent Protection](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#223-urgent-protection)): a stay, interim protection, or reliance limit may be ordered at once, without waiting for the notice, the answer, or the decision. The process still runs afterwards, and the measure is entered on the Institutional Certification Record.
  - **Not a permanent bar:** a denied or revoked operator may apply again.
- **Who stands in:**
  - **Operator:** read the Institution's governing authority wherever Chapter Ten §3.4 Denial and Revocation says "operator".
  - **System findings:** read the institutional findings wherever it says "system findings".
- **Effects:** the effects are in [§3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution), not those in [Chapter Ten §3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation](core_10.000_system_alignment_certification_forum_process_outcomes.md#35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation).
- **One System:** An Institution that runs exactly one System has a one-System evaluation ([§1.1 Who Is Certified](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#11-who-is-certified)). Each outcome follows its own denial or revocation ground, so that an Institution with one System is not treated worse than one with several:
  - **The System:** it is denied or revoked under Chapter Ten §3.4 Denial and Revocation only on a System ground, one that rests on the System's own findings. An institutional ground, one that rests only on the institutional items (items 1 to 5, 7, and 8 of [§3 Whole-Institution Certification Evaluation](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-institution-certification-evaluation), and the Institution's own evaluations under [§4 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-evaluations-where-implicated) and [§5 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations)) does not deny or revoke the System.
  - **The Institution:** it is published as Not Certified, with the effects in [§3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution), only on an institutional ground, one that rests on the institutional findings. A System ground leaves the Institution's status as it stood, unless the cause finding places the cause in the Institution ([Chapter Eleven §3.3.3 Cause of Misalignment](core_11.000_system_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#333-cause-of-misalignment)). The test is the one in [§3.5 Cascade from System Certifications](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#35-cascade-from-system-certifications), rule 2, which calls such a cause an *institutional cause*. Rules 1 and 2 of Chapter Fourteen §3.5 Cascade from System certifications apply to a one-System evaluation as this section states; the rest of Chapter Fourteen §3.5 Cascade from System certifications does not.
  - **Both:** a joint ground, one that rests on both, produces both outcomes. A denial or revocation ground is joint where it is the conduct of the Institution's governing authority (unwillingness, evidence shown to be false, fabricated, or concealed, a certification condition missed twice or materially violated, or a required proof step not done), or where it rests on a fact that [Chapter Nine §3.1 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline) attributes to the System itself, and not a fact the System takes by link from the Institution's record. The Institutional Certification Record states which denial or revocation ground supports which outcome.
  - **Two status lines:** The one Institutional Certification Record carries a status line for the System and a status line for the Institution. Where the Institution holds no certification yet and the only denial ground is a System ground, the Institution's pass ends on its own institutional findings, as [§3.3.1 The Two Statuses](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#331-the-two-statuses) provides (provisional certification, with reliance limits stated on the Institutional Certification Record). It is not Not Certified, and the seat bar of [§2.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions](#23-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions) and the effects of [§3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution](#35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) do not apply to it. The System's denial or revocation is an indicator for the Institution under rule 1 of Chapter Fourteen §3.5 Cascade from System certifications, as it is for an Institution with several Systems.

<a id="35-effects-of-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 3.5 Effects of Denial and Revocation for an Institution

*In plain terms: a denial or revocation changes what the Institution may say about itself, who is told, how much its status counts in its Systems' reviews, and where the matter goes next. By itself it does not shut the Institution down.*

**Effects:** Because there is no gate ([§1.2 No Permission and No Gate on Forming or Running](core_12.000_institutional_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#12-no-permission-and-no-gate-on-forming-or-running)), a denial or revocation has only these effects:

1. **Status:** The Institution is published as Not Certified and may not describe itself as certified.
2. **Notice:** Those who rely on the Institution are told, and so are the forums of each System in its portfolio. The lead forum that publishes the outcome sees that this is done. Where [§3.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#36-reopening-the-institutions-systems) applies, the same notice carries the request to reopen and runs on the giver, clock, and defaults of Chapter Fourteen §3.6 Reopening the Institution's Systems, and the *Systems notified* entry of the Institutional Certification Record covers both.
3. **Weight in its Systems' certifications:**
   - The status is adverse evidence under [Chapter Nine §3.1 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](core_09.000_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#31-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline) when each System in its portfolio is certified.
   - For a new System's go-live or a material expansion, the proponent must show that the institutional facts it relies on are enough despite the finding.
   - The status does not certify or block any System by itself. The System's own certification pass decides.
4. **Seats in other bodies' certifications:** The Institution gives them up while it is Not Certified, as [§2.3 Forums, Certifiers, and Record Offices as Institutions](#23-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions) provides.
5. **Referral:** The lead forum refers the matter to the forum that has jurisdiction over the type of certification problem identified. There are three routes:
   - **To the forum that decides failure and sanctions:** The lead forum refers the matter to the forum with jurisdiction under **CI-19.210** (*Institutional failure and dissolution*) and **CI-19.215** (*Institutional accountability and sanctions*) where:
     - the finding is able and not willing;
     - a revocation rests on concealment or fraudulent evidence; or
     - a failed formation check was not resolved inside the resolution window in [Chapter Seven §7 When a Holder Fails the Formation Check](core_07.000_charters.md#7-when-a-holder-fails-the-formation-check) (*Resolution window*).

     That forum alone decides any suspension, receivership, structural separation, or dissolution. This Chapter creates none of them.
   - **To the forum over a named sentient:** Where the certification finding rests on a holder who is not authentic, or on a sentient's use of the Institution to carry out a violation, the lead forum also refers the facts to the forum with jurisdiction over that sentient.
   - **To a lock or an Interim Hold:** For a verified finding of violation, the matter goes to the Institutional Authority Standing Lock under [Chapter Nineteen §3.5 Special Locks](core_19.000_violation_consequences.md#35-special-locks). Where only the grounds for an [Interim Hold](core_05.035_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) are met, it goes to an Interim Hold under [Chapter Twenty-Three §5 Interim Hold on a Named Sentient's Pathway](core_23.000_forum.md#interim-hold-on-a-named-sentients-pathway). The hold rests on that sentient's authority, never on the Institution.
6. **Standing:** Only through [§5 Relationship to Standing](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#5-relationship-to-standing). This holds for a denial of a first certification too: the findings behind it may supply input under [§5.2 Every Certification Outcome](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#52-every-certification-outcome), on the same terms as any other.

An Institution that is Not Certified may continue to exist, publish its Institutional Charter, operate, and apply again on the terms of Chapter Ten §3.4 (*Not a Permanent Bar*). It may not exercise new material authority until a recertification clears that authority ([§3.2.2 Recertification for New Material Authority](core_14.000_institutional_alignment_certification_records_recertification.md#322-recertification-for-new-material-authority)).

Not Certified status does not open any of the three narrow gates in **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*):

- a lock on a named sentient's authority;
- an Interim Hold on a named sentient's authority; and
- a lock on an Operating Party itself. This binds an Institution only where a lock actually sits on it, such as the Dissolution Reformation Lock under **CI-19.210 §4** (*Dissolution*). Not Certified status is not such a lock.

Whether an Operating Party falls outside the right to form, under the Limits in [Article XI-E](core_06.010_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*), is for the forums to decide on that Article's terms. This Chapter does not decide it.
