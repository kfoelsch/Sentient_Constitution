Chapter Nine, **Part D**, is the constitutional owner of **institutional alignment certification**. It extends the process of Parts A to C from a System to an Institution:

- **Phase I, Frame** and **Phase II, Evaluate:** the institution class and the institutional evaluation ([§12 Institution Class and Institutional Evaluation](#12-institution-class-and-institutional-evaluation));
- **Phase III, Review**, **Phase IV, Decide**, and **Phase V, Record:** the forum roles, the outcome, the Institutional Certification Record, and remediation readiness with denial and revocation ([§13 Review, Decision, and Record](#13-review-decision-and-record)); and
- **Phase VI, Operate:** recertification, reopening, the cascade from System certifications, and the forums that certify ([§14 Operating, Reopening, and the Cascade](#14-operating-reopening-and-the-cascade)), with the standing bridge ([§15 Standing Bridge and Non-Evasion](#15-standing-bridge-and-non-evasion)).

*In plain terms: Parts A to C ask whether a **system** is aligned. Part D asks the same of the **institution** that runs systems, or that holds authority without running one. An institution can be aligned while one of its systems is not, and the reverse. So the institution is checked on what only it controls: whether it does what its Institutional Charter says, who answers for its authority, what it rewards in its own members, and whether the checks on it can actually be used. It relies on each system's certification and does not redo it. It is not a licence: nobody needs its permission to start or run an institution.*

*In plain terms: a system's certification belongs to the system. An institution's certification cites those certifications and adds what only the institution can answer for. One system can appear under several institutions, or under none. Nothing here lets a certification stand in the way of starting an institution.*

<a id="111-relation-to-system-alignment-certification"></a>

#### 11.1 Relation to System Alignment Certification

Institutional alignment certification and System Alignment Certification are two certifications joined by a link. The relation is **composition by link**, not containment:

- **The System Certification Record belongs to the System:** An Institutional Certification Record cites it by link and names its version. Part D never re-runs a System evaluation, never re-decides a System's class, and never changes a System's outcome, status, or conditions.
- **One System Certification Record may be cited by several Institutions:** A System can be operated, stewarded, or materially controlled by more than one Institution under [Chapter Seven §1 When System and Institutional Charters Are Required](core_07_charters.md#1-when-system-and-institutional-charters-are-required). Each cites the same record.
- **A System need not sit under any Institution:** A single sentient is not an Institution, so a System may have none. Its certification is complete without Part D.
- **The Institution's certification cannot lift a System's:** A System that is Not Certified stays Not Certified whatever the Institution's status. A System that holds certification gains no protection from an Institution's finding.
- **Institutional facts are found once:** Where [Part A §3.3 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#33-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline) asks about the operator's governance and incentives, a current Institutional Certification Record for the operator may supply those facts by link. The System's forums still decide whether those facts are enough for that System. They may not find the same institutional fact differently without saying why on the System Certification Record.

<a id="112-who-is-certified"></a>

#### 11.2 Who is certified

An Institution of Class L or higher is within this Part, with the class decided under [§12.1 Institution class](#121-institution-class). That includes an Institution that runs no System that needs a System Charter, which has no certification of any kind under Parts A to C.

- **Class A, B, C, and L:** the process applies, scaled by class under [§12.4 Scaling by class](#124-scaling-by-class).
- **Class P:** the process is encouraged and may be used voluntarily. It becomes mandatory on reclassification to Class L or higher, or where the Institution voluntarily asserts constitutional compliance. This mirrors [Part A §1 Purpose and Role](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#1-purpose-and-role).
- **One System:** an Institution that runs exactly one System that needs a System Charter has one pass and one record. The System Certification Record carries an *institutional findings block* with the items in [§13.4 Institutional Certification Record](#134-institutional-certification-record), and it serves as the Institutional Certification Record. Forums, seats, and clocks are those of the System pass, with the institutional items added. The cascade in [§14.3 Cascade from System certifications](#143-cascade-from-system-certifications) does not apply, since the System is the whole portfolio.
- **Several Systems:** an Institutional Certification Record, linking each System Certification Record.
- **No System:** an Institutional Certification Record alone.

<a id="113-no-permission-and-no-gate"></a>

#### 11.3 No permission and no gate

Institutional alignment certification is an independent check. It is not permission.

- **No gate on forming or operating:** No Institution needs an institutional certification to be formed, to publish its Institutional Charter, to begin to exercise authority, or to keep operating. The go-live gate in [Part B §7 Certification Outcomes](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes) stays with Systems. A new System, or a material expansion of one, goes live only through its own certification, whatever the Institution's status. The only gates in the area are the two narrow gates on a named sentient's authority in **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) (an outstanding lock, and an Interim Hold on probable cause of misalignment). They attach to the sentient and never to the Institution, and this Part supplies facts to them without being one.
- **No back door:** Nothing in this Part may be used, by a forum, an adopter, or an incumbent, as an authorization threshold that shuts out founders or protects incumbents. That would defeat [Article XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*). [Avoidable Burden](core_05_band_continuity.md#avoidable-burden) applies to the cost of the process, and [Classification-Scaled Governance](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) to its depth.
- **A window for the first certification:** A first Institutional Certification Record is due within a window the adopting instrument sets for the Institution's class. For a new Institution, the window runs from the day it begins to exercise authority over others, and its first certification is provisional. For one already operating, the window is the transition clock under [Article XXVIII-A](core_06_rights_part_e.md#xxviii-a-existing-instantiations-transition-clock) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*), consistent with the default in [Chapter Seven §1.4 When it is due](core_07_charters.md#14-when-it-is-due). An Institution that lets the window run out without a first pass has a default to log and correct. The default opens a pass. It does not stop the Institution.

*In plain terms: first work out how much the institution matters, by what it actually does and holds. This section is **Phase I, Frame** and **Phase II, Evaluate** for an Institution.

<a id="121-institution-class"></a>

#### 12.1 Institution class

**Institution class** is decided by what the Institution actually decides, holds, and controls, and not by its label, legal form, or the list in its Institutional Charter. The class governs the depth of the evaluation and the clocks, as [Part A §2.1 How system class scales every evaluation](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#21-how-system-class-scales-every-evaluation) does for a System.

1. **Portfolio floor:** An Institution takes the highest class of any System it operates, stewards, or materially controls ([Chapter Seven §1 When System and Institutional Charters Are Required](core_07_charters.md#1-when-system-and-institutional-charters-are-required)). The Systems are found from observed operation. A System missing from the Institutional Charter still counts.
2. **Own impact:** An Institution that runs no such System is classified by its own material impact and dependency. The class definitions of **CS-3** (*System classification machinery*) are applied to the Institution's own decisions and holdings, using these factors:
   - what it decides about other sentients' access to survival essentials, education, safety, livelihood, rights, or standing;
   - what it holds, such as funds, records, memberships, custody of standing records, or the function of a forum, certifier, or record office;
   - whether its own activity is material in any domain of [Domain-Specific Materiality](core_05_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality), including taking [Materially Binding Acts](core_05_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act) that others must follow or may rely on as settled: an Institution that is material in any one domain is at least Class L under **CS-3** (*System classification machinery*), and giving aid, goods, labor, or services that no one is required to accept, and that gate no one's access, is conduct and not such an act;
   - how many sentients depend on it, and how substitutable it is;
   - how concentrated its authority is, within the meaning of the Material Control Line; and
   - how reversible its decisions are.
3. **Own impact above the portfolio:** Where an Institution's own decisions and holdings fall in a higher class than its Systems, the higher class governs the items in [§12.2 Institutional evaluation, always](#122-institutional-evaluation-always) that concern the Institution alone. The portfolio floor still sets the Institution's class for Chapter Seven.
4. **Common control:** Operating Parties that are one operation under the aggregation rule of [Domain-Specific Materiality](core_05_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) (common practical control within a [Material Control Line](core_05_band_accountability.md#material-control-line); funding alone does not count) are classified together where they act as one Institution in practice. Dividing an Operating Party, its portfolio, or its authority so that no part reaches Class L is a scope-and-boundary evasion under [Chapter Three §2.1 Common Evasion Patterns](core_03_definition_integrity.md#21-common-evasion-patterns).
5. **Reclassification:** The class is reassessed on the triggers in [§14.2 Reopening triggers](#142-reopening-triggers). The classification is stated on the Institutional Certification Record with its reasons.

<a id="122-institutional-evaluation-always"></a>

#### 12.2 Institutional evaluation, always

Every pass evaluates these seven items, which are the institutional counterpart of [Part A §3 Whole-System Certification Evaluation](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#3-whole-system-certification-evaluation). Each finding states its evidence and the Institutional Charter text or observed operation it rests on.

1. **Institutional Charter against practice:** The Institutional Charter is tested against observed operation, using the review list in [Chapter Seven §7 Amendment and Review](core_07_charters.md#7-amendment-review-and-scope-misalignment). Practice outside the Institutional Charter, or an Institutional Charter that no longer describes practice, is a finding whichever is wrong.
2. **Portfolio and aggregate effect:** The portfolio rationale is tested: each System traces to the Institution's stated purpose, and a System with no stated reason is a finding. The evaluation also looks across the Systems for what none of their certifications can show alone: shared dependencies, aggregate reliance, cumulative extraction from shared foundations, and any arrangement that splits one function into parts so that each part escapes a class or a duty.
3. **Accountable authority:** Each authority the Institution exercises over others is attributable to an accountable office that can be named, and that office is answerable under the rules of [Chapter Eight](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#chapter-eight-functional-independence-and-segregation-of-duties) and **CI-3.3** (*authority chain and delegation controls*). A change of ownership, control, or structure does not release responsibility ([Chapter One §19.6 Keeping Responsibility When Ownership or Structure Changes](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#196-keeping-responsibility-when-ownership-or-structure-changes)).
4. **Rewards and incentives:** The Institution's own pay, promotion, targets, and rewards are put to the [Two Fundamental Questions](core_00_preamble.md#two-fundamental-questions): what the structure actually rewards, and whether that points toward the Constitution's two aims or away from them. A reward structure that pays for misalignment, or for hiding it, is a finding even where every System is individually certified. The institutional duties on incentive integrity stay with **CI-11** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*) and are cited, not restated.
5. **Role Map and custody:** The [Charter Role Map](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) names offices that exist, are filled or substituted as it says, and sit outside the Institution's [Material Control Line](core_05_band_accountability.md#material-control-line) where the Institution is the subject. The independent route can be used in fact. A seat left unplaced, a dead office, or a record custodian inside the Institution's own line is a finding.
6. **Currency of each cited System certification:** For each System the Institution runs, the evaluation checks that a System Certification Record is in force or inside its transition clock, and that the Institution has done what that record's conditions require of the Institution. An expired, withdrawn, failed, or missing certification with no recorded reason is a finding that [§14.3 Cascade from System certifications](#143-cascade-from-system-certifications) then carries to the Institution.
7. **Office-holder standing and authenticity:** The formation check statement published under [Chapter Seven §5 Formation Check: Office-Holder Standing and Authenticity](core_07_charters.md#5-formation-check-office-holder-standing-and-authenticity) is tested against observed operation and is not taken on attestation. For each covered office the forums check the standing facts against verified records, and find authenticity from evidence on control, interests and rewards, conduct, and protected voice. That means who actually directs the holder, what the holder is paid and promoted for, what the holder has verifiably done in the function, and whether anyone who raised a concern or refused an instruction was penalized. The structural limb for other staff is tested the same way. Each of these is a finding: a holder who is a nominee for an undisclosed controller, a covered office that exists only on paper, a reward structure that pays holders for crossing the Institutional Charter's limits, and a statement that is false or incomplete. Findings are about the office and the structure. They attach to a named sentient only through the verified-input gate in [§15 Standing Bridge and Non-Evasion](#15-standing-bridge-and-non-evasion). The check is not a test of belief, although a verified statement that others deserve harm or discriminatory treatment is conduct and counts against the holder, as [Chapter One §7.5 Expression and the Harm Line](core_01_a_values_principles.md#75-expression-and-the-harm-line) sets out. Items 3, 4, and 5 are read with this one, and a fact found under one of them is not found again under another.

<a id="123-evaluations-where-implicated"></a>

#### 12.3 Evaluations where implicated

Where an Institution's own conduct implicates a Rights Floor or a domain covered by [Part A §4 Evaluations Where Implicated](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#4-evaluations-where-implicated) or [§5 Rights-Floor and Domain Evaluations](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#5-rights-floor-and-domain-evaluations), the matching evaluation runs on the Institution's own conduct, with the same triggers. The institutional duties themselves are owned by [corpus_institutions](corpus_institutions.md), including **CI-5** (*Conflict integrity, anti-capture, anti-corruption*), **CI-8** (*Transparency, participation, and accessible challenge and service pathways*), **CI-11** (*Resource stewardship and incentive integrity*), and **CI-27** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*). Part D cites them and does not restate them. An evaluation already made on a cited System Certification Record is not made again.

<a id="124-scaling-by-class"></a>

#### 12.4 Scaling by class

The institution class scales the depth of evidence, the independence of the review, the default recertification cadence, and the disclosure on the record, in the way [Part A §2.1 How system class scales every evaluation](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#21-how-system-class-scales-every-evaluation) does. The cadence floors are in [Chapter Thirteen §6 Timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline](core_13_forum.md#review-cadence) (*Review cadence*); the adopting instrument may set shorter intervals and sets the number of clean provisional recertifications.

- **Class A, B, and C:** all seven items in [§12.2 Institutional evaluation, always](#122-institutional-evaluation-always), with evidence and independence in proportion to the class.
- **Class L:** the same seven items, simplified, with simpler records and a lower default cadence where [Classification-Scaled Governance](core_05_band_oversight.md#classification-scaled-governance) allows. The core of a Class L pass is the Institutional Charter against practice, the Role Map, the formation check statement, and the currency of the cited System certifications. No item may be omitted, and an item with nothing to find is stated in a line.
- **Class P:** voluntary, as [§11.2 Who is certified](#112-who-is-certified) states.

**A host in a joint professional practice.** Where an Institution is a host ([**CI-4.7**](corpus_institutions/ci_04_appointment_competency_rotation_removal.md#ci-47-practice-licence-practice-clearance-and-joint-professional-practice) (*Practice licence, practice clearance, and joint professional practice*)), its certification also samples members' acts against the seats the host carries and reads the members' adoption notices against what the host does. A finding about one member's act is a finding about that member, and it reaches the host only where a seat the host carries failed.

<a id="131-four-seats-in-an-institutional-certification"></a>

#### 13.1 Four seats in an institutional certification

This subsection is how Part D applies the [Chapter Eight §2 Four-Seat Constitutional Floor](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#2-four-seat-constitutional-floor), and it is the statement [Chapter Eight §9 Relationship to Later Processes](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#9-relationship-to-later-processes) requires of this process. An institutional certification is a [Materially Binding Act](core_05_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act), so every pass has the four seats. This Part adds no seat and relaxes no rule of Chapter Eight. [Part B §6.1.1 Four seats in a certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#611-four-seats-in-a-certification) applies with these substitutions:

- **[Initiating Seat](core_05_band_accountability.md#initiating-seat):** the Institution's governing authority, which requests certification or is the proponent who must supply the evidence. A pass opened on a trigger is opened by the lead forum's order, and the governing authority remains the proponent.
- **[Verify-or-Authorize Seat](core_05_band_accountability.md#verify-or-authorize-seat):** the lead forum for the outcome, and each component authority for its own finding, assigned under [§13.2 Forum roles and sequence](#132-forum-roles-and-sequence) and Chapter Thirteen. The [Charter Role Map](core_05_band_continuity.md#charter-role-map) does not place this seat. The Institution cannot choose, appoint, fund through a channel it controls, or remove its own verifier.
- **[Record Seat](core_05_band_accountability.md#record-seat):** the office the Institutional Charter's Charter Role Map places, which opens, versions, preserves, and publishes the Institutional Certification Record. It sits outside the Institution's [Material Control Line](core_05_band_accountability.md#material-control-line).
- **[Contest Seat](core_05_band_accountability.md#contest-seat):** the published record challenge path with the competent review or backup forum, placed the same way and outside the same line.

**Self-placement.** The Institution publishes the map that places the record and contest seats for its own certification. A placement inside its own Material Control Line is not a valid placement for a record in which it is the subject, whatever the map says ([Chapter Eight §3 Independence, Conflict, and Control Lines](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#3-independence-conflict-and-control-lines)). The pass then routes as [Chapter Eight §5 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#5-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution) provides, using the independent route the Charter Role Map names. The defective placement is itself a finding under [§12.2 Institutional evaluation, always](#122-institutional-evaluation-always).

**Merged hosting.** [Chapter Eight §6 Proportional Scaling and Merged Hosting](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#6-proportional-scaling-and-merged-hosting) applies unchanged. This Part adds no merged hosting and removes none.

<a id="132-forum-roles-and-sequence"></a>

#### 13.2 Forum roles and sequence

The supervisory sequence in [Part B §6.2 Supervisory sequence](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#62-supervisory-sequence) applies, from the challenge path first through the hand-off to Phases IV and V, with these changes:

- **Lead:** An **Integrity** forum leads each pass, unless Chapter Thirteen assigns a different lawful lead for the primary stake. The lead forum directs the record seat holder and does not hold the record seat on the same act.
- **Institutional forums hold the core components:** An Institution is always a necessary party to its own certification. Institutional forums hold component authority for institutional mandate, supervised scope, institution class, institutional-duty compliance, and the Rights Floor component for the Institution's own conduct ([Chapter Thirteen §4.3 Institutional Forums](core_13_forum.md#43-institutional-forums), [Part B §6.1.6 Institutional forums](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#616-institutional-forums)).
- **Other components:** Technical forums supply evidence standards and any measurement the incentive or aggregate findings need. Environment forums supply a component finding where the Institution's aggregate ecological exposure across its Systems is material. Sentient forums receive affected-stakeholder participation and hold components only where Chapter Thirteen keeps the stake in Sentient forums. Constitutional forums keep the meaning, validity, and structural-remedy questions.
- **Systems are referred, not decided:** A component question that belongs to one System goes to that System's own pass. The lead forum may refer, certify, or [stay](core_05_band_accountability.md#stay) it, but it does not decide it in the Institution's pass.
- **Participation, urgent protection, and contest:** [Part B §6.2](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#62-supervisory-sequence) (*participation during review; urgent protection*), [§6.3 Contestability chain](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#63-contestability-chain), and [§6.4 Anti-bypass](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#64-anti-bypass) apply in full. Affected members, workers, users, and communities of the Institution are among the affected sentients who must have an accessible way to submit evidence.

<a id="133-outcomes-and-effect"></a>

#### 13.3 Outcomes and effect

The outcomes are those of [Part B §7 Certification Outcomes](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#7-certification-outcomes): provisional certification, full certification, conditional certification, deferred certification, non-certification, withdrawal, expiration, and release from conditions. The statuses, the conditions rules, and the requirement of independent verification before release are those of Part B §7 Certification Outcomes and [§9.2 Provisional and full certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#92-provisional-and-full-certification).

Because there is no gate ([§11.3 No permission and no gate](#113-no-permission-and-no-gate)), the **effect** of an outcome is limited to these:

- **The status on the record:** The Institution's status is stated, published, and open to challenge. An Institution that is Not Certified may not describe itself as certified.
- **Conditions and reliance limits tied to the defect:** A condition or limit attaches to the authority, practice, or structure the finding concerns, and it states its owner, deadline, evidence, independent verifier, and consequence, as Part B §7 Certification Outcomes requires. A condition cannot reach beyond the defect. It cannot require the Institution to stop forming Systems, to seek leave to operate, or to change matters the findings do not touch.
- **Notice to those who rely on the Institution** where a finding means their reliance is not safe.
- **Cadence:** A shorter recertification cadence under provisional status.
- **Denial and revocation:** The effects in [§13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution).
- **Reopening and the cascade:** The effects in [§14 Operating, Reopening, and the Cascade](#14-operating-reopening-and-the-cascade).
- **The standing bridge:** Only through [§15 Standing Bridge and Non-Evasion](#15-standing-bridge-and-non-evasion).

The Institution's status is evidence in the certification of each System it runs, through [Part A §3.3 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#33-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline). It does not by itself certify or block any System. A denied or revoked status carries the added weight stated in [§13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution). Sanctions, dissolution, and institutional-failure consequences stay with **CI-13** (*Institutional failure, sanctions, dissolution, and accountability*). This Part creates none.

<a id="134-institutional-certification-record"></a>

#### 13.4 Institutional Certification Record

An **Institutional Certification Record** is a bounded, reviewable record about one Institution, one Institutional Charter version, one scope, one time window, and one decision context. It satisfies and extends the [Materially Binding Act Record](core_05_band_accountability.md#materially-binding-act-record) and [Chapter Eight §8 Act Records and Attributable Handoffs](core_08_functional_independence_segregation_of_duties.md#8-act-records-and-attributable-handoffs), and the integrity rules of [Part B §8.2 Record integrity: transparency and auditability](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#82-record-integrity-transparency-and-auditability) apply to it. It is opened by the record seat holder at step 1 of the sequence and completed with the outcome. No second record is opened for the decision.

The record must identify, at minimum:

- **Institution identity, scope, and status:** the Institution, its governing authority, its Institutional Charter version (or the System Charter that serves as one), and its review status;
- **Prior record:** the completed Institutional Certification Record this pass follows, if any;
- **Institution classification:** the class under [§12.1 Institution class](#121-institution-class), with reasons, and any higher own-impact class that governs institutional-only items;
- **Portfolio and cited System records:** each System the Institution operates, stewards, or materially controls, its System Charter version, and the System Certification Record cited, with its version, status, and expiry. A System with no record in force is listed with the reason;
- **Institutional findings:** the findings on the seven items in [§12.2 Institutional evaluation, always](#122-institutional-evaluation-always), and on any evaluation run under [§12.3 Evaluations where implicated](#123-evaluations-where-implicated), each with its evidence;
- **Charter Role Map version in force** and the placement of each of the four seats for this pass, with the independent route;
- **Forum supervision and functional-independence record:** the lead forum and each component authority, and how the seats were kept apart;
- **Challenge paths:** the published record challenge path, its receiver, how to file, and its clocks, as [Part B §9.1.1 Contestability paths](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#911-contestability-paths) provides;
- **Concerns raised in review and decision;**
- **Cause finding** for any misalignment, as [Part B §9.2 Provisional and full certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#92-provisional-and-full-certification) states, including whether the cause lies in the Institution or in a System;
- **Remediation readiness:** where misalignment was found, the findings and evidence required by [Part B §8.1 Minimum record contents](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#81-minimum-record-contents), stated for the Institution as [§13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution](#135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution) provides;
- **Outcome, status, and reliance limits;** and
- **Operating plan:** owners, clocks, monitoring and indicators, conditions, and verification criteria.

A System Certification Record, or an Institution's own report or attestation, offered in place of this record does not satisfy this section.

<a id="135-remediation-readiness-denial-and-revocation-for-an-institution"></a>

#### 13.5 Remediation readiness, denial, and revocation for an Institution

*In plain terms: when a review finds a problem in an institution, the same question is asked of it as of a system: does it want to fix the problem, and can it? If not, its certification is denied or taken back. That cannot stop the institution from existing or operating. It takes away its claim to be certified, warns those who rely on it, weakens what its systems can borrow from its standing, and keeps it out of the seats that certify others.*

[Part B §7.1 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#71-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct) and [§7.2 Denial and Revocation](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#72-denial-and-revocation) apply to an Institution with these substitutions.

- **Who is asked:** The Institution's governing authority is the proponent and supplies the evidence. The lead Integrity forum decides the finding. Institutional forums hold the governance, authority, and incentive evidence, and Technical forums hold any capacity evidence that needs measurement ([§13.2 Forum roles and sequence](#132-forum-roles-and-sequence)).
- **Willingness, for an Institution,** includes whether it is changing its own reward structure where that structure caused the finding; replacing or disclosing a holder found not authentic under [§12.2 Institutional evaluation, always](#122-institutional-evaluation-always) (item 7); restoring an independent Role Map placement; correcting its Institutional Charter or its portfolio; and answering for a System whose cause finding traced to it under [§14.3 Cascade from System certifications](#143-cascade-from-system-certifications).
- **Capacity, for an Institution,** includes whether the offices that must act hold the authority to do so under **CI-3.3** (*Authority chain and delegation controls*); whether it has the funds, staffing, and succession for the work (**CI-4.1** (*Shared staffing and competency floor*); **CI-27** (*Remedy systems and institutional redress capacity*)); and whether the checks on it can in fact be used.
- **One System:** An Institution that runs exactly one System has one readiness check on the one pass. A denial or revocation then applies to the System under Part B §7.2 Denial and Revocation and to the Institution's status under this subsection.
- **Grounds and process:** The grounds for denial and for revocation, the protection first rule, the process, and the bar on a permanent exclusion are those of Part B §7.2 Denial and Revocation, with the Institution's governing authority and the institutional findings in place of the operator and the system.

**Effect:** Because there is no gate ([§11.3 No permission and no gate](#113-no-permission-and-no-gate)), the effect of an institutional denial or revocation is limited to these:

1. **Status:** The Institution is published as Not Certified and may not describe itself as certified.
2. **Notice** to those who rely on the Institution and to the forums of each System that cites its record.
3. **Weight in its Systems' certifications:** The status is adverse evidence under [Part A §3.3 Governance, Incentive, and Contestability Discipline](core_09_a_system_alignment_certification_evaluation.md#33-governance-incentive-and-contestability-discipline) in the certification of each System the Institution operates, stewards, or materially controls. For a new System's go-live or a material expansion, the proponent must show that the institutional facts relied on are enough despite the finding. This does not certify or block any System by itself. The System's own pass decides.
4. **Seats in others' certifications:** While it is Not Certified the Institution may not hold the verify-or-authorize, record, or contest seat in another body's certification, or host a forum or certifying body ([§14.5 Forums, certifiers, and record offices as institutions](#145-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions)). Seat placements it holds are re-placed by dated Role Map version.
5. **Referral:** Where the finding is able and not willing, or a revocation rests on concealment or fraudulent evidence, or a failed formation check was not cured inside the clock in [Chapter Seven §6 When a Holder Fails the Check](core_07_charters.md#6-when-a-holder-fails-the-check) (*Cure clock*), the lead forum refers the matter to the forum with jurisdiction under **CI-13** (*Institutional failure, sanctions, dissolution, and accountability*). That forum alone decides any suspension, receivership, structural separation, or dissolution. This Part creates none of them. Where the finding rests on a holder who is not authentic, or on a sentient's use of the Institution as a vehicle for a violation, the lead forum also refers the facts to the forum with jurisdiction over that sentient. For a verified finding of violation the matter goes to the Institutional Authority Standing Lock under [Chapter Eleven §5.5 Special locks](core_11_standing_integration.md#55-special-locks). For probable cause only, it goes to an [Interim Hold](core_05_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) under [Chapter Thirteen §5 Interim hold on a named sentient's pathway](core_13_forum.md#interim-hold-on-a-named-sentients-pathway) on that sentient's authority, never on the Institution.
6. **Standing:** Only through [§15 Standing Bridge and Non-Evasion](#15-standing-bridge-and-non-evasion). A denial of a first certification, the Institution having held none, supplies no standing input.

An Institution that is Not Certified may continue to exist, publish its Institutional Charter, operate, and apply again on the terms of Part B §7.2 (*Not a permanent bar*). The two narrow gates in **Article XI-E** (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) bind a named sentient's authority and not the Institution. Whether an Operating Party falls outside the right to form under the Limits in [Article XI-E](core_06_rights_part_b.md#article-xi-e-institutional-formation-and-business-creation) (*Institutional Formation and Business Creation*) is for the forums on that Article's terms. This Part does not decide it.

### 14. Operating, Reopening, and the Cascade

*In plain terms: an institution's certification is checked again on a schedule and whenever something material changes. If one of its systems loses its certification, the institution answers for what that says about the institution, and no more than that. And the bodies that do the certifying are institutions too, so they cannot certify each other in a circle.*

This section is **Phase VI, Operate** for an Institution.

<a id="141-monitoring-recertification-and-status"></a>

#### 14.1 Monitoring, recertification, and status

[Part B §9 Recertification and Reopening](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#9-recertification-and-reopening) (*monitoring and response; new record for each pass; validation*) and [§9.2 Provisional and full certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#92-provisional-and-full-certification) apply to an Institutional Certification Record with these substitutions: the Institution's governing authority is the proponent, the cadence is scaled to the institution class, and each pass opens a new record linked to the one it follows. The completed record is preserved as it stood and stays open to challenge.

A first Institutional Certification Record is provisional. Full certification is earned only at a recertification of an Institution holding provisional certification, on the three conditions of Part B §9.2 Provisional and full certification: the required number of consecutive clean provisional recertifications, no current misalignment, and no monitoring indicator of impending misalignment. Failure and step-down work as that section states.

<a id="142-reopening-triggers"></a>

#### 14.2 Reopening triggers

An Institutional Certification Record is recertified on the cadence set for the Institution's class in [Chapter Thirteen §6 Timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline](core_13_forum.md#review-cadence) (*Review cadence*), and the Institution is audited at the midpoint of that cadence. Review also reopens when any of the following is material:

- **General triggers,** as [Part B §9 Recertification and Reopening](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#9-recertification-and-reopening) lists them, applied to the Institution: material change in its activity or context, concealed behavior, dependency growth, new risk, incident history, stakeholder evidence, a credible challenge, and misclassification or overdue reassessment of its class;
- **Institutional Charter events:** an Institutional Charter amendment, a mismatch between the Institutional Charter and observed operation, operation materially outside chartered scope, or an overdue periodic Institutional Charter review;
- **Portfolio change:** a System added, retired, merged, split, or transferred to or from the Institution, or a change in who controls one;
- **Change of ownership, control, or structure,** including any change that moves authority outside the line that was evaluated ([Chapter One §19.6 Keeping Responsibility When Ownership or Structure Changes](core_01_c_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#196-keeping-responsibility-when-ownership-or-structure-changes));
- **Reward and incentive change:** a material change to the Institution's pay, promotion, target, or reward structure;
- **Role Map or custody defect:** a seat unplaced, an office vacant without substitution, or a custodian brought inside the Institution's control line;
- **Office-holder events:** a change of holder in a covered office, a change in who controls one, a verified standing lock or an [Interim Hold](core_05_band_accountability.md#interim-hold) on a holder, or a finding that the formation check statement was false or incomplete;
- **Readiness events:** a missed proof step or condition deadline, or false readiness evidence, under [Part B §7.1 Remediation Readiness: Willingness and Capacity to Correct](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#71-remediation-readiness-willingness-and-capacity-to-correct); and
- **Cited System events:** a cited System Certification Record failed, withdrawn, expired, or reopened, which also triggers [§14.3 Cascade from System certifications](#143-cascade-from-system-certifications).

A material and timely trigger requires timely forum review and does not wait for the next scheduled pass. The response clocks and materiality tiers of [Part B §9.2 Provisional and full certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#92-provisional-and-full-certification) apply, with the tier presumptively matching the institution class.

<a id="143-cascade-from-system-certifications"></a>

#### 14.3 Cascade from System certifications

*In plain terms: a failed system certification is a warning light for the institution, and sometimes an alarm. It is an alarm only if the institution's own governance caused it.*

When a System Certification Record the Institution cites **fails** (a recertification or reopened review finds current misalignment), is **withdrawn**, or **expires**, the effect on the Institution is as follows.

1. **Indicator, always:** The event is an indicator of impending misalignment of the Institution. An Institution holding provisional certification stays provisional and its count of clean recertifications does not advance. An Institution holding full certification steps down to provisional certification. The Institution is not otherwise penalized for the event alone.
2. **Current misalignment, where the cause is institutional:** If the cause finding for the System ([Part B §9.2 Provisional and full certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#92-provisional-and-full-certification)) traces the misalignment to the Institution's own governance, incentives, resourcing, Institutional Charter, or portfolio, then the Institution has current misalignment, and the pass is a failed one with the consequences of Part B §7 Certification Outcomes. The lead forum cites the System's cause finding. It does not make a new one about the System, and it decides only whether the cause lies in the Institution.
3. **Expiry without a reason:** A cited certification that has expired, with no recorded reason and no transition clock running, is an overdue check of the Institution under [§12.2 Institutional evaluation, always](#122-institutional-evaluation-always) (item 6), and counts as an indicator under rule 1.
4. **No re-judging the System:** The cascade runs one way. It never changes the System's outcome, status, conditions, or clocks, and a finding that the cause is not institutional leaves the Institution's status as rule 1 sets it.
5. **Record:** The record states the cited event, the System's cause finding by link, and the lead forum's finding on where the cause lies.

<a id="144-challenge-path"></a>

#### 14.4 Challenge path

[Part B §9.1 Challenging a Certification](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#91-challenging-a-certification) and [§9.1.1 Contestability paths](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#911-contestability-paths) apply to the Institutional Certification Record. The path opens when the outcome is published. Where the Institution is already operating, it is already open. A challenge about a System's certification goes to that System's record and is not heard in the Institution's pass. A challenge to the Institution's class under [Domain-Specific Materiality](core_05_band_oversight.md#domain-specific-materiality) follows that entry's Challenge route, and related challenges are heard as one matter.

<a id="145-forums-certifiers-and-record-offices-as-institutions"></a>

#### 14.5 Forums, certifiers, and record offices as institutions

A forum host, a certifying body, a record office, a verifier, or any other body that takes part in certifications is an [Institution](core_05_band_continuity.md#institution) where it meets that definition, and Part D applies to it as to any other Institution of its class.

- **No self-judging:** Its own pass is led and reviewed by a forum outside it and outside its [Material Control Line](core_05_band_accountability.md#material-control-line). Where the question is the forum family's own bias, capture, conflict, or process abuse, the backup routing of the [Chapter Thirteen §3 Transfer, consolidation, and coordination](core_13_forum.md#3-transfer-consolidation-and-coordination--continuity-and-anti-capture) cross-forum anti-self-judging rule and **CF-9.6** (*no self-investigation*) applies.
- **No circle:** A body does not hold a seat in the certification of a body that holds a seat in its own, directly or through a chain of bodies that returns to the first within one recertification cycle. A pass that would close a circle routes to a body outside it.
- **Seats stay apart:** Taking part in another body's certification gives no hold over that body's response and no preference in its own.

<a id="15-standing-bridge-and-non-evasion"></a>

### 15. Standing Bridge and Non-Evasion

<details>
<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

- Upstream: [§13](#13-review-decision-and-record) and [§14](#14-operating-reopening-and-the-cascade); [Part B §10 Relationship to Standing](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#10-relationship-to-standing); [Verified Inputs for Standing](core_05_band_accountability.md#verified-inputs-for-standing); [Chapter Three §1 Definition Integrity and Anti-Evasion Constraints](core_03_definition_integrity.md#1-definition-integrity-and-anti-evasion-constraints) and [§2.1 Common Evasion Patterns](core_03_definition_integrity.md#21-common-evasion-patterns).
- Downstream: [Chapter Ten](core_10_standing_assessment.md#chapter-ten-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) (*standing records and verified-input gate*); [Chapter Eleven](core_11_standing_integration.md#chapter-eleven-standing-effects-and-integration) (*standing effects and integration*).
- Read with: **Article III-A** (*Survival*); [Constitutional Tetrad](core_00_preamble.md#constitutional-tetrad).

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: like a system's certification, an institution's can feed standing through a strict gate and never replaces standing. And no one gets out of this Part by dividing, renaming, or reshaping an institution.*

**What may pass to standing.** [Part B §10 Relationship to Standing](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#10-relationship-to-standing) applies to an Institutional Certification Record, with the Institution as the actor to whom facts are attributed. Only the record of a certified Institution may supply input, and only through Chapter Ten's verified-input gate, and in one of two cases: it holds certification, or the facts arose while it held certification. The record must be bounded, traceable, contestable, and verified under [§13 Review, Decision, and Record](#13-review-decision-and-record). Positive certification, recertification, and condition release may supply verified input for a contribution standing record. Withdrawal, concealed misclassification, failed recertification, fraudulent evidence (including a formation check statement that hides who controls a covered office), material condition violation, and verified operation outside certified scope may supply verified input for a violation standing record where the facts support adverse classification and actor-specific attribution. This bridge does not decide what other verified inputs Chapter Ten accepts about an Institution.

**Boundary.** An Institutional Certification Record does not apply standing effects. [Chapter Ten](core_10_standing_assessment.md#chapter-ten-contribution-violation-and-standing-model--measurement) owns standing records and axis-pure measurement. [Chapter Eleven](core_11_standing_integration.md#chapter-eleven-standing-effects-and-integration) owns standing effects. This Part does not absorb or relocate them. It is also not a sentience-status determination.

**Non-evasion.** Evasion of this Part is governed by Chapter Three ([Chapter Three §1 Definition Integrity and Anti-Evasion Constraints](core_03_definition_integrity.md#1-definition-integrity-and-anti-evasion-constraints), including scope-and-boundary evasion under [§2.1 Common Evasion Patterns](core_03_definition_integrity.md#21-common-evasion-patterns)) and by [Part B §9.3 Non-evasion](core_09_b_system_alignment_certification_record_process.md#93-non-evasion). Without limiting those, these are evasions of this Part:

- dividing an Institution, its portfolio, or its authority so that no part reaches the class that requires certification;
- moving a System, an office, or a record between Operating Parties under common control to avoid a finding or a trigger;
- placing the record or contest seat, or the independent route, inside the Institution's own control line;
- placing a nominee or proxy in a covered office, or hiding who controls one, so that the formation check or the office-holder item passes, including seating through a nominee a sentient who is under an Institutional Authority Standing Lock or an Interim Hold;
- choosing or paying a reviewer through a channel the Institution controls; and
- using this Part, in either direction, as a licence or a shield: as a threshold that excludes a founder or protects an incumbent, or as a certificate offered in place of a System's own certification.
