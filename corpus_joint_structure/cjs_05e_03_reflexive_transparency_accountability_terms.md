## CJS-5E.3 Cross-implementation reflexive transparency and accountability terms
Use this rule when legitimacy depends on authorities holding themselves to equal or stronger standards than they apply to others. Read it with **PROT3 — Reflexive Transparency and Accountability**, **PRIM4 — Transparency and Disclosure**, **PRIM9 — Auditability**, **PRIM10 — Tiered Transparency and Audit Access**, **PRIM11 — Independent Verification and Integrity of Claims**, **PRIM15 — Evolution, Revalidation, and Non-Entrenchment**, **PROT1 — Distributed and Proportional Authority**, **PROT4 — Burden of Justification and Constraint**, **PROT5 — Constrained Secrecy and Protected Investigations**, and **PROT6 — Procedural Integrity and Adjudication**.

Cross-implementation reflexive transparency and accountability terms
- OP-O: Authorities must be transparent, auditable, realistic about enforcement capacity, open to challenge, and protected against corruption.
- OP-E: Reviewers must apply all components together.
- OP-C: Reflexive accountability is non-compliant when authorities use weaker standards for themselves than for subjects.

Parity and non-exemption floor
- OP-O: Oversight, governance, adjudication, and enforcement actors must meet standards equal to or stricter than those they impose.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify that authorities are not exempt from verification, challenge, or accountability.
- OP-C: Self-rules weaker than imposed rules are non-compliant.

Transparency, audit, and verification-integrity duties
- OP-O: Enforcement, investigation, and governance actions and methods must be documented, auditable, proportionate, and independently reviewable.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify that records can reconstruct decisions and actions.
- OP-C: Opaque methods or selective disclosure are non-compliant when they undermine accountability.

Opaque-enforcement limits and constrained-disclosure handling
- OP-O: Non-auditable enforcement is disallowed when feasible alternatives exist. If lawful limits prevent full disclosure, authorities must still provide the most transparency feasible and document the reason for the limit.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify that constrained disclosure remains independently auditable and reviewable.
- OP-C: Secrecy used as a practical bypass of safeguards is non-compliant.

Ongoing accountability and revalidation continuity
- OP-O: Governance and enforcement systems must remain under ongoing audit, challenge, revalidation, and independent verification.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify that accountability channels continue over time and under stress.
- OP-C: Lapsed, symbolic, or check-the-box accountability is non-compliant.

Enforcement-capacity realism requirements
- OP-O: Accountability must include real remedies and real enforcement capacity, including funding, staffing, tools, and coordination.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify that capacity matches harm risk, dependency, claimant scale, and cross-jurisdiction needs.
- OP-C: Hollow enforcement claims are non-compliant.

Misrepresentation and violation signaling
- OP-O: Misstating compliance, audit status, validation level, or system behavior is an integrity breach.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify mechanisms to detect, record, and correct misrepresentation by authorities.
- OP-C: Untracked or unremedied misrepresentation is non-compliant.

Pluralistic validation and contestability protections
- OP-O: No single verification authority may monopolize truth determination where plural validation is required.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify paths for challenge, refinement, and re-evaluation.
- OP-C: Suppressing dissenting or minority verification outcomes is non-compliant.

Anti-corruption and undue-influence auditability controls
- OP-O: Material benefits and influence channels that could distort high-stakes decisions must be recorded and disclosed in proportion to impact.
- OP-E: Reviewers must verify escalation routes for improper influence and alignment with anti-capture controls.
- OP-C: Concealed side benefits or decision paths concentrated around private benefit are non-compliant.
