This file is the joint-structure implementation home for **CJS-6.105 §9** (*Boundary between Support Roles and Merits Decisions*), **CJS-6.105 §10** (*Institution-Hosted or Forum-Adjacent Operations*), and **CJS-6.105 §11** (*Classification Alignment for Supervised Scope*).

*In plain terms: monitors and forensic helpers must not quietly decide who wins; hosting a forum's building or software does not let the host control its decisions; and published system classifications must match actual operation.*

## Purpose, Role, and Scope

**Purpose:** Keep support, hosting, and supervision arrangements aligned with the independence and classification rules that apply to them.

**Role:** Section 9 distinguishes support roles from merits decisions; section 10 addresses institution-hosted or forum-adjacent operations; and section 11 addresses classification alignment for supervised systems.

## CJS-6.105 §9: Boundary between Support Roles and Merits Decisions

<summary><strong><span style="color: #2563eb;">Trace</span></strong></summary>

</details>

<br>

*In plain terms: sentients who monitor performance, give forensic help, or support investigations help the process — they do not decide who wins, unless a separate lawful document clearly says they may.*

Roles that monitor, review how challenge or review routes perform, provide forensic help, or support investigations must not make binding merits determinations within the meaning of [Merits Determination](../subcore_03.070_band_accountability.md#merits-determination) under [Chapter Sixteen](../core_16.000_forum.md#chapter-sixteen-standards-routing-and-forum-cases) and **Adjudication and Dispute Resolution**. The only exception is when a separate lawful document clearly gives a named role limited authority to decide merits issues.

Institutions must publish what each role may do and what it may **not** decide — including who the role reports to, how often it reports, and how it connects to assurance work. Oversight and support roles must not quietly take over merits decisions assigned to a lawfully constituted forum family under **Chapter Twenty-Two**.

A separate lawful grant of limited decision-making authority remains subject to [Chapter Six §3 Four-Seat Constitutional Requirement](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#3-four-seat-constitutional-requirement) and its independence, substitution, and hosting rules: the role may not verify its own act, hear its own challenge, or acquire a prohibited seat combination. Its [Act Record](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#9-act-records-and-attributable-handoffs) must identify that authority and the seats used. A support-role title or delegation does not waive those requirements.

See **CI-6.215 §3** (*Contest-Integrity Monitoring (Class A and Class B)*), **CF-16.430** (*Forum forensic and analytical support*), **CF-16.435** (*Independent investigative service and prosecution interface*), and **CJS-3.125 §13** (*Accountability: Procedural Integrity and Adjudication Terms*).

## CJS-6.105 §10: Institution-Hosted or Forum-Adjacent Operations


*In plain terms: hosting the building, budget, or software for a forum does not let the host control the forum's decisions.*

When an institution hosts, funds, administers, or technically runs forum infrastructure, it must make forum independence and contestability work in practice — including budget, staffing, records, security, procurement, clerking, digital systems, and personnel systems. Independence on paper is not enough.

Assess those arrangements for the particular act under [Chapter Six §4 Independence, Conflict, and Control Lines](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#4-independence-conflict-and-control-lines). Shared infrastructure alone does not establish a Material Control Line; practical power over the verification, evidence, credentials, funding, removal, or record does. If the holder is absent or conflicted, use the published substitute or independent route under [Chapter Six §5 Wrong-Seat Routing](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#5-wrong-seat-routing) and [§6 Published Placement, Vacancy, and Substitution](../core_06.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#6-published-placement-vacancy-and-substitution), preserving evidence and logging the handoff. Hosting does not let the institution fill a missing independent seat with an interested operator.

## CJS-6.105 §11: Classification Alignment for Supervised Scope


*In plain terms: supervision papers must use the system classification that actual operation warrants, so a lower label cannot reduce the duties that apply.*

Apply **CJS-3.125 §11** (*Accountability: Distributed and Proportional Authority Terms*) **Classification-scaled governance burden** for the shared class- and stewardship-level scaling rule.

When an institution supervises systems under [**CS-8.305**](../corpus_systems/cs_08.305_system_classification_machinery.md#cs-8305-system-classification-machinery) (*System classification machinery*) or **CS-21.305 — Critical system stewardship**, its published maps under **CI-8.210 §2** (*Published Industry and Domain Mapping*) and related **CI-8.210** (*Classification-linked institutional obligations*) material must match the operative systems classification profile, including when reclassification is required.
