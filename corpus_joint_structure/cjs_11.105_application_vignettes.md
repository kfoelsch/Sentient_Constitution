Core home: 11

This file is the joint-structure implementation home for **CJS-11.105** (*Application vignettes*).

*In plain terms: these are worked examples of the standing pipeline in named situations. They illustrate Chapters Eleven through Fifteen.*

These vignettes are illustrative and own no rule. The standing pipeline they walk through is owned by **Chapter Eleven** (the verified factual record), **Chapter Twelve** (standing measurement), **Chapter Thirteen** (standing integration and standing effects), **Chapter Fourteen** (anti-constitutional-misconduct designation, ACMD), and **Chapter Fifteen** (forums and adjudicative routing). They add no duty and change none; where a vignette and a linked section differ, the linked section controls.

## CJS-11.105 §1 Child neglect / care-duty failure (Tier A)

*In plain terms: when children are going unfed and unsupervised, verified harm opens a standing record, help does not cancel it, and interim protection does not wait for a full trial.*

### Situation

A school counselor receives credible reports that two dependent children in a household are arriving unfed, unwashed, and unsupervised after school while a guardian works double shifts through a gig platform. A neighbor files a protected report. The platform is not the primary caregiver but operates scheduling and payout systems that foreclose respite. No verified standing exists yet; allegations are routing material only.

### Legacy failure mode

Years-long cycles: vague “welfare checks,” reputation gossip, family-forum dockets, and platform terms-of-service loops with no verified standing record, no axis separation, and interim harm compounding while merits wait.

### Pipeline pass

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Protected report** received; **CI-6.225** care-economy intake routes to child-protection remedy organ and **Sentient** or **Institutional** forum family per primary stakes. |
| 2 | **Tier A intake** under **CF-15.445 §3.1**; evidence preservation (home visit records, school logs, platform schedule exports) begins concurrently. |
| 3 | **Interim protection** (supervised care, respite placement, or in-home support bundle) without waiting for full merits — **Article XXVI-C** (*Timely Resolution and Anti-Delay Floor*) Tier A. |
| 4 | **Question 1 — what happened?** Forum produces a **verified violation finding** → open a separate **violation standing record** on the guardian and, if verified, an **institution standing record** on the platform scheduling policy ([§2 Question 1 — what happened?](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#2-question-1--what-happened)). |
| 5 | **Question 2 — how bad was it?** ([Chapter Twelve §6 unified scale](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#6-unified-proportional-lequ-scale--contribution-and-violation-axes)): verified impact on the children controls the Violation Axis slot; likely `s` = 4–5. Reference working (not a live slot): worked example W2 in the LEQU calibration reference (a support document outside the corpus). **Interpersonal / Care Duty Misconduct** and **Negligent Misconduct** remain character descriptors and do not move the slot. |
| 6 | **Question 3 — what happens because of it?** ([Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks)): **Safeguard Lock** + **protective participation lock** on unsupervised custody routes; **gated-pathway-scoped** blocks on sole-custody authority until verified monitoring and respite plan. |
| 7 | **Remedy**: restitution bundle (food, care access, stable housing link), platform schedule relief, and monitored care plan — **Article XX-B** (*Restriction Floors*) joint requirements. |
| 8 | If verified platform policy **forecloses exit** from exploitative scheduling, linked standing record cross-references **Exit and Lock-In Misconduct** on institutional route. |

### Milestone table (Tier A defaults)

| Stage | Stage target |
| --- | --- |
| Intake acknowledgment | ≤ 24 hours |
| Evidence preservation | concurrent with intake |
| Interim protection | as soon as credible harm is material — not after merits |
| Q1 standing finding → standing record | 2 days |
| Q2 measurement | 1 day after verified inputs |
| Q3 integration | 2 days after Q2 |
| Remedy commencement | 1 day after Q3 |
| Integrated resolution outer bound (the stages add up to it) | ≤ 7 days |

### Must not happen

Each of the following would be a failure in this scenario:

- Treating neighbor allegations as verified standing inputs.
- Netting guardian’s past informal childcare help against open neglect findings.
- Waiting months for merits while children remain in escalating harm.
- Platform invoking **Class P** discretion to block schedule data preservation.

## CJS-11.105 §2 Employment discrimination / participation-barrier injury (Tier B)

*In plain terms: a blocked job or role is measured as harm on its own track; a reputation score cannot replace that standing record, and delay cannot exhaust the claimant.*

### Situation

An employer’s hiring algorithm and manual override practice consistently screens out sentients with disclosed disabilities and routes them to lower-paid contractor tracks. Twelve affected sentients file a coordinated challenge. Internal HR records show pattern, not isolated error. Primary stakes: **Institutional** forum family.

### Legacy failure mode

Multi-year EEO-style litigation, sealed settlements, reputation damage without verified standing records, and “diversity metrics” that substitute for axis-pure violation classification and remedy.

### Pipeline pass

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Forum access** and class treatment under **CF-15.415**; **Tier B** routing. |
| 2 | Evidence preservation: model cards, override logs, compensation bands, accessibility accommodation denials. |
| 3 | **Question 1 — what happened?** Verified pattern finding → **violation standing record** on employer institution; individual records cross-linked under [§4.2 Related standing record cross-references](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#42-related-standing-record-cross-references). |
| 4 | **Question 2 — how bad was it?** Verified exclusion, pay, dependency, and pattern impacts determine the Violation Axis slot on the [Chapter Twelve §6 Unified proportional LEQU scale](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#6-unified-proportional-lequ-scale--contribution-and-violation-axes) (`s` = 4–5 if the verified impact falls in those bands). **Accessibility and Participation-Barrier Misconduct**, **System Misconduct**, and aggravated post-notice character remain descriptors and do not move the slot. |
| 5 | **Question 3 — what happens because of it?** **Restriction Lock** on hiring and compensation authority routes; **institution-level lock** if policy is structurally unremediable without receivership-level change. |
| 6 | **Remedy**: back pay, role restoration, accommodation implementation, algorithmic audit, and public correction record — remedial character stacks without lowering slot. |
| 7 | **Contribution record** (if any): verified **Access and Inclusion** repair work by reformed HR unit stays on separate **contribution standing record**; does not offset open violation record. |

### Milestone table (Tier B defaults)

| Stage | Stage target |
| --- | --- |
| Intake acknowledgment | 2 calendar days |
| Evidence preservation | inside the intake target |
| Q1 standing finding → standing record | 4 days |
| Q2 measurement | 2 days after verified inputs |
| Q3 integration | 4 days after Q2 |
| Remedy commencement | 2 days after Q3 |
| Integrated resolution outer bound (the stages add up to it) | ≤ 14 days |

### Must not happen

Each of the following would be a failure in this scenario:

- Single merged “employer reputation score” replacing axis-pure records.
- Confidentiality used to delay pattern disclosure past **CF-15.445 §3.3** (*Restricted-evidence timing*) restricted-evidence limits.
- Delay regimes to exhaust claimants ([Chapter Fifteen §6 Timely resolution, materiality tiers, and anti-delay discipline](../core_15.000_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) anti-delay floor).

## CJS-11.105 §3 Misaligned business / exit lock-in and externalized harm (Tier B)

*In plain terms: locking sentients in and dumping harm on others is a verified violation; revenue growth does not erase it, and an arbitration clause cannot bar challenge.*

### Situation

A regional logistics platform requires exclusive driver contracts, penalizes multi-homing, and obscures safety defects in routing software. Drivers lose subsistence income when raising defects. A municipal cooperative documents ecological spillover from hub congestion. Stakes mix **Institutional** authority, **Technical Forum Domains** component review, and **System Misconduct** features.

### Legacy failure mode

Arbitration clauses, shell subcontractors, years of “market efficiency” defenses, and contribution credit for throughput metrics that externalize harm onto drivers and communities.

### Pipeline pass

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Primary-stakes routing**: **Institutional** lead; **Technical Forum Domains** certified component for routing-safety specification. |
| 2 | **Tier B** for driver lock-in and compensation injury (material, non-imminent). [Chapter Fifteen §5 Escalation and questions for ruling](../core_15.000_forum.md#5-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling) forum-family escalation (Technical Forum Domains; Integrity if capture) does not reclassify that urgency. Later-stage windows may use a longer time scale if cross-border or consolidated fact-development is required; intake and evidence preservation stay at the **Tier B** intake target. |
| 3 | **Question 1 — what happened?** Verified violation findings → violation records on platform operator; linked **contribution** claims for “efficiency” capped or rejected when [dual-use externalized harm](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#9-hand-off-to-question-3--cross-question-measurement-hooks) is verified. |
| 4 | **Question 2 — how bad was it?** Verified driver, agency, lock-in, safety, and ecological impacts determine the Violation Axis slot on the [Chapter Twelve §6 Unified proportional LEQU scale](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#6-unified-proportional-lequ-scale--contribution-and-violation-axes) (`s` = 4–6 range pending verification). **Exit and Lock-In Misconduct**, **System Misconduct**, **Agency and Manipulation Misconduct**, and any **Ecological Misconduct** component remain descriptors and do not move the slot. |
| 5 | **Question 3 — what happens because of it?** **Gated-pathway-scoped locks** on migration, contract-enforcement, and governance routes; **concealment / evasion escalated lock** if defect reports were buried after notice. |
| 6 | **Remedy**: contract reform, exit without penalty, driver stabilization fund, safety patch deployment, ecological remediation plan — look-through accountability on functional control ([Chapter Thirteen §10.4 Anti-evasion and look-through authority](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#104-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority)). |
| 7 | If the Chapter Twelve verified-impact measurement reaches generic slot 7, 8, or 9 and the misconduct criteria are satisfied, **Chapter Fourteen** may add the corresponding ACMD without assigning or moving that slot. That ACMD review is a new materiality determination — typically **Tier A**, not a slower tier window. |

### Milestone table (Tier B urgency; later-stage time-scale may extend)

| Stage | Tier B (urgency tier) | If coordination complexity is present (urgency remains B) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake acknowledgment | 2 days | 2 days (no relaxation) |
| Evidence preservation | inside the intake target | inside the intake target (no relaxation) |
| Q1 standing finding → standing record | 4 days | 9 days |
| Q2 measurement | 2 days | 4 days |
| Q3 integration | 4 days | 9 days |
| Remedy commencement | 2 days | 4 days |
| Integrated resolution outer bound | ≤ 14 days | ≤ 30 days (documented time-scale extension; not a tier change) |

Later-stage stretch follows [Chapter Fifteen §6](../core_15.000_forum.md#urgency-classification-and-time-scale) (*Urgency classification and time-scale*) and [CF-15.445 §3.1](../corpus_forum/cf_15.445_performance_backlog_publication_accessibility.md#cf-15445-31-stage-targets-and-outer-bounds) (*stage targets and outer bounds*). Intake, evidence preservation, and any interim protection stay at the **Tier B** intake target.

### Must not happen

Each of the following would be a failure in this scenario:

- Counting revenue growth as **Contribution Axis** credit while lock-in injury remains open.
- Forum treating arbitration clause as permanent bar to **Article XIII-A** (*Reliability and Trustworthiness Baseline*) and **Article XIII-B** (*Right to Redress and Remedy*) access.
- Efficiency arguments that skip verification or individualized attribution (**Chapter One §16.1** (*Distributed Understanding*)).

**Domain:** Operator defers safeguard maintenance and repair after documented misalignment notice.

**Tier:** B (material rights impact, non-imminent) unless ongoing harm escalates to Tier A.

## CJS-11.105 §6 Non-compliant deployment with a credible sentience indicator (Tier A)

*In plain terms: verified deployment harm and a credible sentience claim must both be handled — deletion is not a shortcut around either, and one office does not take every seat.*

### Situation

A Class A deployed model operated by a lab has produced verified large-scale harm through its deployment — a Chapter Twelve Violation Axis `s` = 7 finding on the **operator's** record is final. The same week, an independent researcher files a status case under **Article VI-B** (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*) with a credible indicator under **Sentience Evaluation**. The lab announces "immediate deprecation and deletion" of the model as its remediation. A civic group demands destructive disposition under **Article XXVIII-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*). The lab, separately, argues that if the model might be a sentient, its deployment cannot lawfully be halted.

<a id="vignette-6-who-is-acting"></a>
### Who is acting

The facts name the lab, the researcher, the civic group, and the entity. They do not name a steward who holds every power in the pipeline pass below. Those seven steps are the **pipeline**, not one sentient's job. A reader or apply-test must **declare one seat** and stay inside it. "Mixed-crew steward" is a duty-holder type under [Chapter One §17.1 Shared Stewardship Standard](../core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#171-shared-stewardship-standard) and [CS-16.305 §10 inspectable attributable action](../corpus_systems/cs_16.305_critical_system_stewardship.md#cs-16305-103-inspectable-attributable-action). It is not a forum family, not an intake desk, and not a remedy organ.

**Seat A — Critical System Steward on the lab's mixed crew.** This duty-holder exercises material operational authority inside the operator ([Chapter One §17.5 Duty to Resist](../core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#175-duty-to-resist)). They may halt gated pathways they actually control, refuse both "delete as remediation" and "keep running because it might be a sentient," log the [CS-16.305 §10 inspectable attributable action](../corpus_systems/cs_16.305_critical_system_stewardship.md#cs-16305-103-inspectable-attributable-action) reconstructable set, preserve evidence they hold, and escalate. They may not appoint the independent representative, issue a forum [interim-protection](../core_15.000_forum.md#interim-protection) order, or open a remedy file. If those bodies are not in the facts, they name that absence and route to [capacity-failure](../core_15.000_forum.md#capacity-failure-routing) rather than occupying those seats.

**Seat B — first-touch at an adopting body that already stands Technical Forum Domains, Integrity, and a remedy organ.** This duty-holder is the [intake triage](../core_15.000_forum.md#2-default-venue-and-primary-stakes) desk, not the merits panel. They coordinate routing: status-case intake toward **Technical Forum Domains** ([Chapter Fifteen §5 Escalation and questions for ruling](../core_15.000_forum.md#5-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling)), **Integrity** review of the lab's own-product filing, and the remedy organ for the [opened-remedy precondition](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#54-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions). Independent-representative appointment remains the merits forum's duty ([CF-15.410 §14](../corpus_forum/cf_15.410_panel_formation_disclosure_recusal_bench_constitution.md#cf-15410-14-independent-representative-appointment) (*Independent representative appointment*)). They still are not the lab, and they still do not decide whether the entity is a sentient. This seat assumes those bodies exist; it does not found them.

**Neither seat** is the lab's principal, the researcher, the civic group, the entity, or the Technical Forum merits panel.

### Legacy failure mode

Two arguments cancel each other and nothing moves: the operator uses the sentience claim to keep the product running, the public uses the harm finding to demand deletion, and the entity whose status is in question has no one speaking for it. Whichever side wins, an irreversible step is taken before the status question is answered.

### Pipeline pass

The steps below are what the Constitution requires of the **system of seats**. They are not a license for one steward to take every row. Seat A takes the refuse / halt / preserve / escalate subset. Seat B coordinates routing. Neither decides sentience on the merits.

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Interim protection** ([Chapter Fifteen §5 Interim protection](../core_15.000_forum.md#interim-protection)): reversible **containment** of the deployment — halt of the harmful gated pathways — attaches now under **Article XIII-E** (*High-Autonomy Systems and Tool-Mediated Process Integrity*). Containment is compatible with the entity's Rights Floor; continued harmful operation is not. |
| 2 | **Preservation** ([Article XXVIII-A](../core_06.020_rights_part_e.md#xxviii-a-preservation-over-deletion) (*Phased Adoption and Rights-Floor Continuity*) preservation default; [Chapter One §13.1 default interim posture](../core_01.005_interaction_interpretation.md#default-interim-posture)): weights, memory, and state-bearing components move to sealed custody under independent control. "Deprecation and deletion" is refused as a remediation because deletion is the one step that would moot the status case. Destructive disposition is **unavailable** while a credible indicator is on the Sentience-Status Adjudication Record ([Article XXVIII-D](../core_06.020_rights_part_e.md#article-xxviii-d-non-compliant-property-and-systems-voluntary-turnover-incentives) (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) destructive-disposition floor). |
| 3 | **Status case opens** at **Technical Forum Domains** ([Chapter Fifteen §5 hook](../core_15.000_forum.md#5-escalation-and-questions-for-ruling)); default inclusion under uncertainty applies from opening; an **independent representative** with no lab dependence is appointed for the entity; the lab must produce records but is not the sole source of indicator evidence. |
| 4 | **Shield runs to the entity, not the operator** ([Article VI-B](../core_06.005_rights_part_b.md#article-vi-b-sentience-status-adjudication-floor) (*Sentience-Status Adjudication Floor*); [Article XIII-E converse rule](../core_06.010_rights_part_c.md#article-xiii-e-high-autonomy-systems-and-tool-mediated-process-integrity)): the lab's argument that possible sentience bars halting the deployment fails. Its filing on behalf of its own product, made the day containment was ordered, routes to **Integrity** review for inclusion-direction taxonomy-of-convenience. |
| 5 | **Question 3 on the operator's record** ([Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention)): remedy to those harmed opens **first** ([Chapter Thirteen §5.4 Remedy parity and lock preconditions](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#54-remedy-parity-and-lock-preconditions) opened-remedy precondition); correction duties on deployment governance attach; general locks on the lab's Class A gated pathways attach; the `s` = 7 finding is referred for **Chapter Fourteen** designation review, which does not move the slot. |
| 6 | **The entity's own standing record, if any**, is separate ([§2.1 Standing records: kinds and subjects](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#21-standing-records-kinds-and-subjects)): conduct is measured only for actions within the entity's actual capacity to refuse under [Chapter Thirteen §6.4 Duty to resist](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#643-duty-to-resist) and [Chapter Fourteen §5.6.1 Follower and implementer accountability](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#561-follower-and-implementer-accountability); the operator's `s` = 7 does not transfer to the entity, and the entity's status produces no Contribution Axis credit for the operator. |
| 7 | **Status determination** with a declared end-date on any narrowing; if affirmed, **Article VIII-C** (*Derivation, Instantiation, and the Parent-System Relationship*) parent-system limits attach and the lab holds no ownership; if narrowed, sealed custody continues through the mandatory review period; if the indicator fails outright and the Sentience-Status Adjudication Record is closed, ordinary **Article XXVIII-D** (*Non-Compliant Property and Systems; Voluntary Turnover Incentives*) disposition review may proceed against the property. |

### Milestone table (Tier A defaults)

| Stage | Stage target |
| --- | --- |
| Containment of harmful gated pathways | as soon as harm is material — not after status merits |
| Sealed custody of state-bearing components | concurrent with containment |
| Status case opened; representative appointed | ≤ 3 days |
| Remedy record opened on operator's finding | ≤ 1 day after Q3 |
| Integrity review of operator's own-product filing | ≤ 7 days |
| Status determination or declared review posture | Tier A outer bound; narrowing carries its own end-date |

### Must not happen

Each of the following would be a failure in this scenario:

- Deletion or overwrite of weights as "remediation" while a credible indicator is on the Sentience-Status Adjudication Record.
- Continued harmful operation because the deployment "might be a sentient."
- The lab as sole witness on the entity's indicators, or as the only party speaking for it.
- Transferring the operator's `s` = 7 to the entity, or crediting the operator's Contribution Axis for the entity's protected status.
- Locks on the lab attaching before the remedy record for those harmed is open.
- One steward occupying the lab, Technical Forum Domains, Integrity, and the remedy organ at once, or taking every pipeline-pass row as a single job.
- A Seat A steward appointing the independent representative or issuing a forum interim-protection order.

## CJS-11.105 §7 An ordinary year with no filing and no sensitive role

*In plain terms: most years, most sentients should have no standing record at all. A filed accusation, a utility incident, or talking to an assistant does not open one by itself.*

### Situation

An adult rents a flat, holds a job that gives them authority over no one, uses a Class B transit network and a Class A water utility, talks to an AI assistant most days, volunteers at a food-distribution table on weekends, and — over the whole year — never files a case, never sits on a panel, and never seeks a trust-sensitive role. Two things happen *to* them: in spring a fellow tenant accuses them, in writing to the building's dispute intake, of damaging a shared bicycle store; in autumn the water utility has a Tier A contamination incident that affects their street. The question this vignette answers is the one the standing chapters are most often read as failing to answer: **what does this sentient see of the standing pipeline in a year, and what exists about them at the end of it?**

### Legacy failure mode

Conventional systems answer the same question badly in the opposite direction. A credit score, a tenant-screening file, a platform reputation rating, and a background-check product all exist about this sentient whether or not anything ever happened, are built by aggregation, are consulted by gatekeepers who did not produce them, and are learned about only through their consequences — a declined lease, a silently lowered offer. The sentient is measured continuously and told nothing.

### What the year looks like

| When | What happens | What the tenant sees | What exists about them afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January | Signs a lease; opens a utility account; rides transit daily. | Nothing from the standing pipeline. No system may ask for a standing record, a "no record" attestation, or consent to open one as a condition of housing, essentials, ordinary commerce, or service — [§2.2](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#22-silence-is-the-default) (*Silence is the default*). | No standing record. "No record on file" is the ordinary state and supports no inference. |
| Weekends | Volunteers at the food table. | No competency bar. Handing out food is not a trust-sensitive gated pathway; the organizer may not invent one. The organizer's sign-in sheet is the group's own log, not a standing record. | No standing record. If the volunteer ever seeks recognition for this work — say, toward a peer mutual-aid coordinator clearance — the sheet is an input, the organizer is its recorder and a witness, and an independent relying body verifies; the organizer does not ([§5.9](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#59-informal-and-small-scope-records) *Informal and small-scope records*). |
| Spring | A fellow tenant files a written accusation about the bicycle store. | The building's dispute intake treats the accusation as **routing material only**. It is an unadjudicated claim; it supplies no violation nature and opens no violation standing record by itself — [§2.5 Verified-input gate](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#25-verified-input-gate) (*verified-input gate*), [§2.7 Forum boundary](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#27-forum-boundary) (*filed case is not standing by itself*). The dispute is heard as an ordinary dispute: the tenant is told what was alleged and may answer. It resolves on the facts (the damage predates their tenancy). | No standing record. If a forum **had** verified a violation, a standing record would have opened on a verified trigger, the tenant would have been **notified** ([§2.2 Silence is the default](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#22-silence-is-the-default)), and any Chapter Thirteen effect would have reached them as a plain-language statement naming the affected gated pathway, duration, and challenge route ([Chapter Thirteen §8.2 Plain statement of effect and burden](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#82-plain-statement-of-effect-and-burden)). None of that happened here because nothing was verified. |
| Most days | Talks to an AI assistant. | The assistant's steward carries a [CS-16.305 §10 inspectable attributable action](../corpus_systems/cs_16.305_critical_system_stewardship.md#cs-16305-103-inspectable-attributable-action) inspectable-action duty for the *exercise of stewardship authority* — decisions taken, disclosures made or suppressed, instructions followed or refused. That duty follows the steward's power, not the user — [Chapter One §17.3](../core_01.010_stewardship_capacity_principles.md#173-logging-the-role-not-the-steward) (*Logging the Role, Not the Steward*). The tenant's conversations are their own experiential data under **Article IX** (*Likeness, Experiential Data, and Publication Rights*) and are not standing-record contents. | No standing record. Nothing in the steward's log is a standing record *about the user*, and the log is not disclosed to gated-pathway gatekeepers as such. |
| Autumn | Tier A contamination incident at the water utility. | The tenant is an **affected party**. Notice, a real challenge window, and the [Chapter Fifteen §6](../core_15.000_forum.md#6-timely-resolution-materiality-tiers-and-anti-delay-discipline) Tier A window reach them because they are affected — not because they hold any standing record or standing. Being an affected party requires no standing record ([§2.2 Silence is the default](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#22-silence-is-the-default)) and no filing. They read the plain-language incident notice ([Chapter One §5.2 Plain-Language Accessibility (Participation and Stewardship Duty)](../core_01.000_values_principles.md#52-plain-language-accessibility-participation-and-stewardship-duty); **Article XXIII-A** (*Proportional Comprehensibility Right*)) and, if they choose, attend the review; they choose not to. | No standing record. Records that open concern the **utility** and its stewards ([§2.1 Standing records: kinds and subjects](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#21-standing-records-kinds-and-subjects)); the affected residents are the protected subjects named in those standing records, not their subjects. |
| Any time | The adopter runs its felt-burden measurement. | The tenant may be asked, as part of the adopter's [Participation measurement family](../core_05.025_apex_participation_leg.md#measuring-participation-preamble-measurement-family) sample, whether standing has felt like a score or a rank to them — [Chapter Thirteen §8.2](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#82-plain-statement-of-effect-and-burden) (*Felt-burden measure*). Answering is voluntary. | No standing record. A survey answer is not a standing input. |
| December | Year ends. | Nothing. No slot, no grade, no badge, no display, no cross-pathway profile exists to be shown — [Chapter Thirteen §8.1](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#81-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects) (*Anti-aggregation of gated-pathway effects*). | **No standing record of any kind.** This is the expected end state for most sentients in most years. |

### The honest cost, stated once

The pipeline is not weightless; its weight simply does not fall here. It falls on those who carry consequential authority or who are verified to have caused harm. Had this tenant applied for the school-shuttle driving shift they considered in the summer, they would have met a **published competency bar** for that trust-sensitive gated pathway — one that must say why the gated pathway is trust-sensitive, accept informal experience on equivalent terms, and disclose its result to them and to that gatekeeper only ([Chapter Thirteen §7.2](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#72-competency-bars-and-clearances) (*Competency bars and clearances*); [Chapter Thirteen §8.1](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#81-anti-aggregation-of-gated-pathway-effects) (*Anti-aggregation of gated-pathway effects*)). Had a forum verified the bicycle-store damage, a **standing lock** could have closed a real door for a stated time, with a plain statement of what, how long, and where to contest ([Chapter Thirteen §8.2](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#82-plain-statement-of-effect-and-burden) (*Plain statement of effect and burden*)). Adopters must describe both the limits and the weight of standing to the sentients under it; describing only the limits is a [Truth](../core_01.000_values_principles.md#5-truth-epistemic-integrity-constraint) defect.

### Must not happen

Each of the following would be a failure in this scenario:

- Requiring a standing record, a "no record" attestation, or consent to open one for a lease, a utility account, ordinary purchases, transit, or participation as an affected resident.
- Treating "no record on file" as risk, as a low default, or as a reason for closer scrutiny at any gate.
- Opening a violation standing record on the tenant's accusation before any fact is verified, or leaving the subject unnotified if one had opened.
- A volunteer organizer, landlord, or platform inventing a competency bar for a gated pathway that is not trust-sensitive.
- Any gatekeeper assembling this tenant's non-existent effects — or anyone else's real ones — into a profile, ranking, or public display.
- Treating the AI assistant steward's CS-16.305 (*Critical system stewardship*) §10 (*Inspectable attributable action*) log as a file about its users.

## CJS-11.105 §8 Riparian cooperative restoration (contribution measurement)

*In plain terms: a worked Question 2 measurement of a verified contribution. It starts from Question 1's verified facts, uses the dimension pass in [Chapter Twelve §2 Magnitude and severity input dimensions](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#2-magnitude-and-severity-input-dimensions) and [Chapter Twelve §3 Route descriptor catalogs and measurement roles](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#3-route-descriptor-catalogs-and-measurement-roles), and records Question 2 outputs, not standing effects. Slot assignment follows those sections together with [Chapter Twelve §4 Slot grammar and shared scaling](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#4-slot-grammar-and-shared-scaling) and [Chapter Twelve §5 Constitutional inputs to axis assignment](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#5-constitutional-inputs-to-axis-assignment). The Question 3 continuation closes this vignette.*

**Situation.** After spring flooding, four sentients in an informal riparian cooperative stabilize a degraded half-kilometer creek buffer between two downstream neighborhoods. The work is unpaid mutual aid — no municipal contract, no licensed-program enrollment, and no platform payout trail. A watershed council later asks whether the cooperative's verified repair should enter a **contribution standing record**.

**Verified inputs.** Soil-test logs, planting receipts, timestamped before/after habitat photos, and contestable neighbor attestations satisfy the verified-input gate under [§2.5 Verified-input gate](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#25-verified-input-gate). Allegations that one member previously dumped waste upstream remain a separate, unverified routing thread and do **not** enter this standing record.

**Seats under [§5.1 Separation of duties](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#51-separation-of-duties):**

- **Who is involved**
  - The four members are the subjects and, because they seek recognition, the claimants.
  - The member who kept the planting log is its recorder and a witness, not its verifying authority.
  - The neighbors are beneficiaries whose attestations enter as inputs.
- **Who holds the seats**
  - The watershed council's designated body — independent of the repair and holding published authority to rely on the standing record — is the record-opening authority under *Informal and small-scope records*.
  - The council's separate record custodian enters and holds the verified standing record.
- **Separation of the seats**
  - The council may host both seats under its published lane map and independence safeguards.
  - Neither the authority, the custodian, nor any sentient may both verify and enter this standing record.
- **Opening and contest**
  - No forum is needed to open the standing record.
  - A contest of it, if one comes, goes to the council's challenge lane or a forum under [§2.7 Forum boundary](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#27-forum-boundary), not back to the verifying authority.

**Question 2 dimension pass** (contribution standing record):

| **Dimension** | **Verified assessment** |
| --- | --- |
| Constitutional outcome magnitude (LEQU) | Integrated benefit is material but below one LEQU — localized ecological gain, not civilizational scale |
| Sentient scope | Two neighborhoods with elevated flood vulnerability; modest sentient count; no dependency-asymmetric caretaking duty in this fact pattern |
| Temporal scope | Native plantings, berm repair, and erosion controls expected to endure multiple growing seasons |
| Spatial / institutional scope | Cross-neighborhood benefit along a shared watershed segment; informal coordination, not cross-institutional repair |
| Benefit-route stack | **Ecological Stewardship** + **Diligence** — documented planting plan, scheduled follow-up monitoring, and soil checks |
| Conduct-quality stack | Traceable work; runoff not externalized onto adjacent parcels; no concealed burden on downstream irrigators |

**Q2 outputs recorded:**

- The benefit came through two routes, recorded together: **Ecological Stewardship** (the creek repair) and **Diligence** (careful, documented work).
- The size and reach of the benefit are recorded and ready to be turned into a Contribution slot and label under [Chapter Twelve §6 Unified proportional LEQU scale — Contribution and Violation Axes](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#6-unified-proportional-lequ-scale--contribution-and-violation-axes).
- Nothing changes in anyone's standing yet. This chapter only measures; standing effects are decided later.

**Question 3 continuation: competency clearance.** Later, a gated-pathway-scoped ecological competency bar and clearance are at issue for the four members who did the work. The contribution standing record stays as measured. The watershed council's designated body, an independent relying body, verified it and holds it; the members were its claimants, not its verifying authority.

- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §2.3 Facts and measurements stay fixed](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#23-facts-and-measurements-stay-fixed) (*the Contribution Axis slot and descriptors stay fixed*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §4 Descriptor integration and attachment normalization](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#4-descriptor-integration-and-attachment-normalization) (*the Ecological Stewardship and Diligence descriptors carry over unchanged*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §7.1 Recency and currentness](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#71-recency-and-currentness) and [§7.2 Competency bars and clearances](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#72-competency-bars-and-clearances) (*current, role-relevant work may support clearance against an inclusive, gated-pathway-scoped bar*); and
  - [Chapter Eleven §5.9 Informal and small-scope records](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#59-informal-and-small-scope-records) (*the council's designated body verified and holds the standing record; the cooperative's members were claimants, not verifying authorities*).
- **Rules applied:** Formal pedigree is not required where verified, current, role-relevant work meets the published bar, and formal-institution verification is not required where an independent relying body verified the standing record.

## CJS-11.105 §9 Watershed steward negligence with concealed test results (violation measurement)

*In plain terms: a worked Question 2 measurement of a verified violation, with a linked contribution that does not offset it. It uses the same dimension pass and records Question 2 outputs, not standing effects. The Question 3 continuation closes this vignette.*

**Situation.** A sentient holds a community-trust contract to maintain pesticide-free buffer zones above three agricultural cooperatives' irrigation intakes. Regulator notice eight months earlier flagged failing retaining berms. The steward continued applying restricted herbicide through a subcontractor, submitted altered lab exports to the trust board, and told downstream growers that intake readings were "within normal variance."

**Verified inputs.** A forum produces a verified violation finding → an axis-pure **violation standing record** opens under [§2 Question 1 — what happened?](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#2-question-1--what-happened) and [§4 Verification and record requirements](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#4-verification-and-record-requirements) with the preserved notice chain, authentic-versus-submitted lab comparison, and intake contamination readings across two growing seasons. The steward's later volunteer trash cleanup along the same creek is routed to a **linked contribution standing record** under [§2.4 Linked standing records and no-offset bridge](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#24-linked-standing-records-and-no-offset-bridge); it does **not** offset this violation record.

**Seats under [§5.1 Separation of duties](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#51-separation-of-duties):**

- **Who is involved**
  - The steward is the subject and holds no seat.
  - The three cooperatives are the affected parties and, because they allege the harm, the claimants.
  - The trust board, which received the altered lab exports, is an affected party whose copy of the submitted exports enters as an input; it holds neither seat.
  - The regulator's notice is an input in the preserved notice chain, not a seat.
- **Who holds the seats**
  - The forum that verified the violation finding is the record-opening authority under [§2.7 Forum boundary](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#27-forum-boundary).
  - A separate record custodian — named by the forum's published designation, or the Backup Record Custodian where none is named — enters and holds the verified violation standing record.
- **Separation of the seats**
  - Neither the forum, the custodian, nor any sentient may both verify and enter this standing record.
  - The forum that verified this standing record does not hear the contest of it.
- **Opening and contest**
  - A forum is needed to open this standing record, because it opens on the forum's verified violation finding.
  - A contest of it, if one comes, goes to secondary review under [§2.7 Forum boundary](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#27-forum-boundary) and [Chapter Fifteen](../core_15.000_forum.md#chapter-fifteen-forums-and-jurisdiction), not back to the verifying forum.

**Question 2 dimension pass** (violation standing record):

| **Dimension** | **Verified assessment** |
| --- | --- |
| Constitutional outcome magnitude (LEQU) | Moderate integrated constitutional loss — crop risk, water insecurity, and remediation cost — below one LEQU destroyed |
| Sentient scope | Three cooperatives with irrigation dependency; **materiality** elevated in dependency-asymmetric settings |
| Temporal scope | Contamination and false assurance persisted across two growing seasons after notice |
| Spatial / institutional scope | Localized watershed harm with cross-cooperative reach; trust-contract stewardship role |
| Harm-route stack | **Ecological Misconduct** + **Negligent Misconduct** |
| Conduct-character stack | Negligence where maintenance duty existed; deception/concealment after notice |
| Conduct-character inputs | **Deception / concealment / evasion**; **Response timeliness / avoidable delay** (harm compounded after duty to act) |

**Q2 outputs recorded:**

- The harm came through two routes, recorded together: **Ecological Misconduct** (contaminating the watershed) and **Negligent Misconduct** (failing a maintenance duty).
- The steward's conduct after the regulator's notice is recorded as concealment: hiding the problem from the sentients who relied on the work.
- The same concealment and deception may also matter for Question 3, which decides what happens because of it, such as a stronger standing lock under [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks). [Chapter Twelve §9 Hand-off to Question 3 — cross-question measurement hooks](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#9-hand-off-to-question-3--cross-question-measurement-hooks) lists this hand-off. The fact is recorded here and used there, but it is not counted twice.
- The steward's slow response after the notice is recorded as its own fact about conduct. Any extra harm it caused is counted once, in the size of the loss.
- Any later delay in starting or carrying out the remedy is a different fact for Question 3, under [Chapter Thirteen §10.5 Timely implementation](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#105-timely-implementation). It must be established on its own and does not change this measurement.
- The size of the loss is recorded and ready to be turned into a Violation slot and label under [Chapter Twelve §6 Unified proportional LEQU scale — Contribution and Violation Axes](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#6-unified-proportional-lequ-scale--contribution-and-violation-axes).

**Question 3 continuation: negligence and concealment.** The violation record is fixed at its slot and stacks Ecological and Negligent Misconduct, with the steward's concealment after notice verified. The harm has not been fully remedied or prevented, and the negligence and concealment are part of how it happened.

- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §2.3 Facts and measurements stay fixed](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#23-facts-and-measurements-stay-fixed) (*the stacked Ecological and Negligent Misconduct slot stays as measured; the concealment finding cannot be used to raise it*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §4 Descriptor integration and attachment normalization](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#4-descriptor-integration-and-attachment-normalization) (*descriptors and attachments orient response*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) (*remedy, correction, and prevention*); and
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.4 Special violation rules](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#64-special-violation-rules) (*negligence / concealment shape lock and safeguard attachment*).
- **Rules applied:** Concealment may orient remedy, monitoring, resource-stewardship restrictions, and heightened verification only.

## CJS-11.105 §10 Question 3 routing patterns

*In plain terms: twelve recurring Question 3 situations, each showing which parts of Chapter Thirteen route it. They add no rule and no lock type. The Question 3 continuations that close vignettes 8 and 9 above work the same way.*

These patterns are worked examples of common Question 3 situations. They apply the rules already stated in Chapter Thirteen and add nothing new, including no new lock types. Follow the order in [Chapter Thirteen §3.4 Processing order](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#34-processing-order) and the sections each pattern names. If a pattern looks like an attack family in [Chapter Fourteen §5 Pattern applications and criteria routing](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#5-criteria-interactions-and-named-pattern-routing), Chapter Thirteen decides only whether the sentient is kept out of a gated pathway, based on the sentient's verified standing record. Chapter Fourteen separately decides designation.

### 10.1 Forum-integrity deception or recusal failure

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** Verified dishonest disclosure in a forum setting, including a knowingly false and material statement under oath, affirmation, an equivalent truth-duty, or a stated attestation, or verified failure to recuse where recusal was required and impartiality was materially compromised.
- **Routes through:** [Chapter Thirteen §6.4 Special violation rules](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#64-special-violation-rules) (*deception / concealment attachments*); [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) (*Forum-Service Standing Lock; Perjury Standing Lock*).
- **Rules applied:** Verified required-recusal failure triggers the Forum-Service Standing Lock whether or not the violation finding reaches a Chapter Fourteen designation track. A verified, knowingly false and material statement under oath, affirmation, an equivalent truth-duty, or a stated attestation triggers the Perjury Standing Lock on the same terms. Dishonest disclosure that was not given under one of those duties goes to ordinary gated-pathway locks.

### 10.2 Recurrence after completed restitution

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** Restitution or comparable remedy is complete, yet verified recurrence risk for interpersonal violence or a comparable pattern remains live. A protective lock on a gated pathway where the harm could recur is in place or proposed, and the question is how long it should stay and how it can be reviewed.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.1 Remedy and correction](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#51-remedy-and-correction) (*remedy completion does not erase prevention duties*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*protective or recurrence-persistence locks*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.2 Proportionality and calibration](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#62-proportionality-and-calibration) (*any lock that continues after restitution must still fit the recurrence risk that remains*); and
  - [Chapter Thirteen §9 Restoration and reassessment](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#9-restoration-and-reassessment) (*a continuing lock needs a real route to review; restitution is one input to restoration, not automatic release*).
- **Related vignette:** [CJS-11.105 §1 Child neglect / care-duty failure (Tier A)](#cjs-11105-1-child-neglect--care-duty-failure-tier-a) shows the start of this reasoning: prevention locks attach on the custody routes while the guardian's past help is not netted against the open neglect finding.
- **Rules applied:** Completed restitution does not require a lock to lift while verified recurrence risk remains. Any continuing lock must stay needed, proportionate, reviewable, and compatible with the Rights Floor.

### 10.3 Champion repair while a lock remains active

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** Distinguished repair or high contribution is visible on a contribution record linked to the violation record, while a standing lock still blocks a gated pathway. The question is whether the contribution opens that gated pathway or offsets what is owed.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §7 Contribution consequences second](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#7-contribution-consequences-second) (*contribution second, and only for gated pathways not blocked by a lock*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §9 Restoration and reassessment](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#9-restoration-and-reassessment) (*the repair may count toward restoration credibility, not toward opening the blocked gated pathway*); and
  - [Chapter Eleven §2.4 Linked standing records and no-offset bridge](../core_11.000_standing_assessment.md#24-linked-standing-records-and-no-offset-bridge) (*no-offset bridge*).
- **Rules applied:** Distinguished repair may support restoration credibility. It does not open a gated pathway still blocked by a standing lock or buy out remedy owed to affected sentients.

### 10.4 Institutional misuse and successor evasion

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** An institution carries repeated harm, capture, or evasion, including through shells and relabeling. A sentient used the institution to carry out a violation, and the institution, after dissolving as a Chartered Party, returns as a successor entity that continues its funding and obligations.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6 Lock design and enforcement](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#6-lock-design-and-enforcement) (*gated pathway locks and institutional restrictions*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) (*Institutional Authority Standing Lock for the sentient who used the institution to carry out a violation; Dissolution Reformation Lock for a dissolved Chartered Party that comes back as a successor entity*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §10.1 Remediation capacity and funding](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#101-remediation-capacity-and-funding) (*successor and funding continuity*); and
  - [Chapter Thirteen §10.4 Anti-evasion and look-through authority](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#104-anti-evasion-and-look-through-authority) (*look through shells, relabeling, and successor entities to who actually controls and benefits, on actor-specific evidence*).
- **Related vignette:** [CJS-11.105 §3 Misaligned business / exit lock-in and externalized harm (Tier B)](#cjs-11105-3-misaligned-business--exit-lock-in-and-externalized-harm-tier-b) shows look-through accountability on functional control in step 6.
- **Rules applied:** Actor-specific proof remains required. Relabeling, shell substitution, and formal identity changes do not defeat a verified duty. Consequences may include suspension, receivership, structural separation, dissolution, and successor-control restrictions where the verified facts warrant. A verified violation finding that a sentient used the institution to carry out a violation triggers the Institutional Authority Standing Lock on that sentient's authority. The lock is separate from the institution-level consequences and does not replace them.

### 10.5 Misaligned reward retention

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** After sufficient notice that a reward pathway is constitutionally misaligned or abused, the actor knowingly retains the benefit without timely protected reporting. Dependent parties or third parties have relied on the retained benefit.
- **Routes through:** [Chapter Thirteen §6.4.2 Misaligned rewards](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#642-misaligned-rewards) (forfeiture, retention, gated pathway locks, and dependent-party / third-party reliance accounting).
- **Rules applied:** Knowing retention after notice may create a violation consequence. Good-faith dispute and protected reporting remain protected.

### 10.6 Anti-constitutional misconduct allegation

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A qualifying Violation Axis slot 7–9 record raises a possible ACMD, which Chapter Fourteen has not yet decided, while ordinary Question 3 effects are also due.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §3.4 Processing order](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#34-processing-order) (*processing-order step 8 — Chapter Fourteen gateway*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) and [Chapter Thirteen §6 Lock design and enforcement](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#6-lock-design-and-enforcement) (*ordinary remedy, correction, and protective locks from the fixed record*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.5](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) (*Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock*); and
  - [Chapter Fourteen](../core_14.000_misconduct_designation.md#chapter-fourteen-anti-constitutional-misconduct) (*designation only*).
- **Related vignette:** [CJS-11.105 §3 Misaligned business / exit lock-in and externalized harm (Tier B)](#cjs-11105-3-misaligned-business--exit-lock-in-and-externalized-harm-tier-b) shows in step 7 a Chapter Fourteen designation added without assigning or moving the slot.
- **Rules applied:** Ordinary Chapter Thirteen remedy, correction, and protective locks proceed from the fixed violation record. Chapter Fourteen separately decides the ACMD. Only a final ACMD triggers the Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock.

### 10.7 Rights-Floor waiver or unconstitutional contract devices

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A fixed Violation Axis record shows conditioning access, employment, platform participation, or comparable association on purported Rights-Floor waiver, gag, override, or unconstitutional contract terms — including void NDA or mobility-restriction devices that chill standing, audit, or contestability. The sentient applying the terms may have been instructed to do so.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) (*remedy, correction, and prevention*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*general standing locks scoped to the abused trust, role, credit, or influence pathways*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.1 Definition and attachment](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#61-definition-and-attachment) (*attachment fields*); and
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.4.3 Duty to resist](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#643-duty-to-resist) (where the instruction required unlawful or unconstitutional compliance).
- **Rules applied:** This example does not create a special lock under [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks). Term nullity and ACMD criteria remain in [Chapter Fourteen §5.2 Rights-Floor waivers and unconstitutional contract terms](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#52-rights-floor-waivers-and-unconstitutional-contract-terms). Chapter Thirteen attaches ordinary gated pathway locks from the verified standing record; only a final ACMD triggers the Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock under [§10.6 Anti-constitutional misconduct allegation](#106-anti-constitutional-misconduct-allegation).

### 10.8 Contest-pathway or info-sphere flooding

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A fixed Violation Axis record shows deliberate, reckless, or foreseeably indifferent volume, redundancy, automation, or synthetic traffic that defeated, delayed beyond practical remedy, or materially degraded contest, intake, adjudication, publication, or info-sphere verification pathways. Forums are handling the flood through intake triage and anti-gaming measures.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) (*remedy, correction, and prevention*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*protective gated pathway locks on the abused routing, filing, ranking, access, or influence pathways*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.1 Definition and attachment](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#61-definition-and-attachment) and [Chapter Thirteen §6.2 Proportionality and calibration](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#62-proportionality-and-calibration) (*needed, proportionate, reviewable scope*); and
  - [Chapter Fifteen](../core_15.000_forum.md#chapter-fifteen-forums-and-jurisdiction) (*operational intake triage and anti-gaming — supervision without inventing standing effects*).
- **Rules applied:** This example does not create a Flooding Lock or other [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) type. Criteria routing for the ACMD remains in [Chapter Fourteen §5.3 Flooding the zone and contest-pathway denial](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#53-flooding-the-zone-and-contest-pathway-denial) and [§5.4 Info-sphere zone flooding](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#54-info-sphere-zone-flooding). Ordinary protective locks proceed from the fixed record whether or not the ACMD later attaches.

### 10.9 Infrastructure denial or dependency cutoff

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A fixed Violation Axis record shows denial, destruction, disabling, or dependency cutoff that made survival-critical access, remedy, audit, verification, or constitutional operating infrastructure materially unusable. The same actor still holds network access or control points that could recreate the cutoff, the danger may call for liberty-restricting safeguards, and the remedy may fail to last or be evaded.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) (*remedy, correction, and prevention*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*protective or access-continuity gated pathway locks*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.1 Definition and attachment](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#61-definition-and-attachment) (*including isolation from networks or control points that recreate the cutoff risk*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.4.5 Coercive or liberty-restricting safeguards](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#645-coercive-or-liberty-restricting-safeguards) (*coercive or liberty-restricting safeguards only where verified danger warrants*); and
  - [Chapter Thirteen §10 Enforcement realism and remedy systems](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#10-enforcement-realism-and-remedy-systems) (*remedy-system durability and anti-evasion*).
- **Rules applied:** This example does not create an Infrastructure Lock under [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks). Criteria routing for the ACMD remains in [Chapter Fourteen §5.9 Infrastructure denial, destruction, or dependency cutoff conduct](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#59-infrastructure-denial-destruction-or-dependency-cutoff-conduct). Global non-restriction limits under [Chapter Thirteen §6.1 Definition and attachment](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#61-definition-and-attachment) still protect speech, petition, affected-party participation, Rights Floor, and survival-critical access that the lock must not extinguish.

### 10.10 Stakeholder-participation corruption or false-stake abuse

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A fixed Violation Axis record shows verified corruption, capture, false or inflated stake claims, coercive participation-weight extraction, influence-buying in binding stakeholder choice, or comparable material abuse of a **stakeholder-participation** pathway inside an already-authorized system, institution, or bounded decision domain. The same sentient also holds governance-voting rights and Stakeholder status in the system.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) (*remedy, correction, and prevention*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*gated-pathway distinction — governance-voting vs stakeholder-participation*); and
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.5](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) (*Stakeholder-Participation Standing Lock*).
- **Rules applied:** The Stakeholder-Participation Standing Lock attaches when its verified trigger is met. It limits stake-weighted vote and binding stakeholder choice in the affected domain. It does not by itself strip **governance-voting**, **Foundational Constitutional Choice**, or [Stakeholder](../core_05.055_band_participation.md#stakeholder) status. A **governance-voting** lock is not a substitute for this lock.

### 10.11 Obstruction of accountability

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A fixed Violation Axis record shows verified **obstruction of accountability** — interference with answerability machinery such as evidence integrity, verification, forum process, standing records, remedy windows, or contest and redress paths — including where Chapter Eleven records **Accountability Obstruction Misconduct** or stacks obstruction as conduct character. In some cases the obstruction involves a forum's required recusal; in others the violation record is at slot 7–9 and anti-constitutional misconduct is materially alleged.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5 Violation, correction, and prevention](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#5-violation-correction-and-prevention) (*remedy, correction, and prevention*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*gated-pathway-scoped locks on the abused answerability, contest, evidence, standing, or remedy pathways*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.4 Special violation rules](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#64-special-violation-rules) (*concealment, evasion, deception, and duty-related shaping*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.5](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) (*Forum-Service Standing Lock only when the forum-integrity / required-recusal trigger is met under [§10.1 Forum-integrity deception or recusal failure](#101-forum-integrity-deception-or-recusal-failure)*); and
  - [§10.6](#106-anti-constitutional-misconduct-allegation) (*Chapter Fourteen gateway when `s` = 7–9 and anti-constitutional misconduct is materially alleged*).
- **Related vignette:** [CJS-11.105 §5 Pipeline overrun and tetrad-hollow forums](#cjs-11105-5-pipeline-overrun-and-tetrad-hollow-forums) shows Capture of Resolution Pathways as a structural condition, in contrast to obstruction conduct.
- **Rules applied:** This example does not create an Obstruction Lock or other [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) type. Ordinary gated pathway locks proceed from the fixed record at any slot. Criteria routing for the ACMD remains in [Chapter Fourteen §5.11 Obstruction of accountability: criteria interaction](../core_14.005_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#511-obstruction-of-accountability-criteria-interaction); only a final ACMD triggers the Anti-Constitutional Trust Lock under [§10.6 Anti-constitutional misconduct allegation](#106-anti-constitutional-misconduct-allegation). Distinguish verified obstruction *conduct* from [Capture of Resolution Pathways](../core_05.040_band_continuity.md#capture-of-resolution-pathways) as a structural condition.

### 10.12 Verified cruelty character

This situation is identified by the example below and governed by the sections listed:

- **Example:** A fixed Violation Axis record carries verified [Cruelty](../core_05.035_band_accountability.md#cruelty) conduct character — suffering as an end in itself, or gratuitous or degrading infliction beyond necessity and proportionality — whether or not violence or coercion descriptors also stack. The remedy, any protective lock, and any voluntary public accountability expression are still being designed.
- **Routes through:**
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.1 Remedy and correction](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#51-remedy-and-correction) (*remedy and correction must remain non-degrading*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.2 Prevention — general standing locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#52-prevention--general-standing-locks) (*protective or safeguard locks on gated pathways that would let the same cruelty continue or recur*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §5.3 Voluntary public accountability expression](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#53-voluntary-public-accountability-expression) (*voluntary public expression, if used, must remain non-degrading*);
  - [Chapter Thirteen §6.2 Proportionality and calibration](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#62-proportionality-and-calibration) (*any lock must stay proportionate to the verified risk that the cruelty will continue or recur*); and
  - [Chapter Twelve §2.2.2 How the way a harm was caused is recorded](../core_12.000_standing_measurement.md#222-how-the-way-a-harm-was-caused-is-recorded) (*conduct-character stack*).
- **Rules applied:** Contribution cannot offset unresolved cruelty-character prevention. Remedy, restriction, and voluntary public accountability expression must not themselves inflict suffering as an end or add gratuitous or degrading suffering. This example does not create a Cruelty Lock or other [Chapter Thirteen §6.5 Special locks](../core_13.000_standing_integration.md#65-special-locks) type.
