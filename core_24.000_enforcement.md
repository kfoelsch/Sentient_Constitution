Chapter Twenty-Four is the constitutional owner of **how outcomes are carried out**: who may do it, within what time, and the safeguards that keep it from being avoided or delayed.

### 1. Purpose, Role, and Scope

*In plain terms: earlier chapters decide what happened and what follows, and the forum chapter says who rules on disputes. This chapter is about what comes next: making sure the outcome is carried out, on time, by someone, and that it cannot be dodged.*

**Purpose:** Chapter Twenty-Four states how the outcomes of the standing pipeline and of forum review are carried out, so that an outcome counts only when it is actually delivered.

**Role:** Where this chapter sits:

- It takes every outcome of the standing pipeline and of forum review as fixed. It does not reopen them and it adds no new kind of consequence.
- It applies the [Chapter One §6.1](core_01.000_values_principles.md#61-correction-and-remedy) (*Correction and Remedy*) principle to the moment of delivery. [Chapter Twenty §4 Enforcement Realism and Remedy Systems](core_20.000_final_effect_restoration_enforcement.md#4-enforcement-realism-and-remedy-systems) asks whether real institutions can deliver standing effects and redress; this chapter states what delivery itself requires.
- Carrying an outcome out is not a second judgment. Whoever carries it out acts within the outcome and is bound by the separation of duties in [Chapter Eight](core_08.000_functional_independence_separation_of_duties.md#chapter-eight-functional-independence-and-separation-of-duties).
- Refusal, delay, and evasion are matters the forums can hear under [Chapter Twenty-Three](core_23.000_forum.md#chapter-twenty-three-part-a-standards-routing-and-forum-cases), and failing to enforce can itself be misconduct under [Chapter Twenty-Two §5 Shielding Misconduct and Failing to Enforce](core_22.000_misconduct_pattern_applications.md#5-shielding-misconduct-and-failing-to-enforce).

**Chapter scope:** The chapter will cover:

- who carries outcomes out;
- what counts as an outcome that must be carried out, and from what moment;
- time limits for carrying out, and what follows when they are missed;
- refusal, stalling, and non-compliance, including by the institution that is meant to carry the outcome out;
- evasion by cost, delay, borders, restructuring, or a change of formal identity, read together with the [Chapter Twenty-Six §4 Anti-Evasion Clause](core_26.000_non_regression.md#4-anti-evasion-clause); and
- limits on the carrying out itself, so that an outcome is delivered as found and no further.

How outcomes are reached stays with the chapters that produce them. Staffing, funding, backlog, and procedure stay with adopted implementation text.
